逆、秩与零空间Inverse, Rank & Null Space

矩阵把空间变了形，我们能不能把这部电影倒着放？信息没丢的时候可以，这就是逆。平面被压成一条线时就不行了：有些输入被压到同一点（被压掉的方向叫零空间），有些输出再也够不到（够得到的部分叫列空间）。冗余机械臂正是靠这份“多余的自由”，在手纹丝不动的同时把手肘摆来摆去。 A matrix reshapes space. Can we play the movie backwards? Yes, as long as no information was lost: that is the inverse. When the plane gets squashed onto a line, rewinding fails: some inputs land on the same point (the crushed directions form the null space) and some outputs can never be reached (the reachable ones form the column space). A redundant robot arm uses exactly this spare freedom to swing its elbow while the hand stays perfectly still.

    1为什么机器人需要它Why robots need this

    机器人每天都在解“反问题”。我想让手以 0.1 m/s 向前走，每个关节该转多快？写成方程就是 \(J\,\dot{\mathbf q} = \dot{\mathbf x}\)：矩阵 \(J\)（雅可比，3.3 节）和想要的手速 \(\dot{\mathbf x}\) 已知，要求关节速度 \(\dot{\mathbf q}\)。又比如：激光雷达看到了这些点，到底是哪个位姿产生了这些测量？

    这类问题的答案有三种可能：恰好一个，一个也没有，或者无穷多个。六关节机械臂在一般姿态下，每个手速恰好对应一组关节速度。手想去的方向被关节锁死时，没有答案。而七关节机械臂有个有趣的现象：手可以纹丝不动，手肘却还能绕着转，于是有无穷多组关节速度给出同一个手速。这份多出来的自由叫零空间，机器人用它躲障碍、避开关节限位。

    这一节回答三个问题：什么时候能“倒放”（逆）？不能倒放时，到底丢了什么（列空间与零空间）？答案有多少个（解集）？

    Robots solve "inverse problems" all day. I want the hand to move forward at 0.1 m/s: how fast should each joint turn? As an equation, \(J\,\dot{\mathbf q} = \dot{\mathbf x}\): the matrix \(J\) (the Jacobian, Lesson 3.3) and the wanted hand velocity \(\dot{\mathbf x}\) are known, and we need the joint velocities \(\dot{\mathbf q}\). Or: a lidar sees these points, so which pose produced these measurements?

    Such problems have three possible kinds of answer: exactly one, none at all, or infinitely many. A six-joint arm in a generic pose has exactly one set of joint velocities for each hand velocity. If the wanted direction is locked by the joints, there is none. And a seven-joint arm shows something curious: the hand can stay perfectly still while the elbow keeps swinging, so infinitely many joint velocities give the same hand velocity. That spare freedom is the null space, and robots use it to dodge obstacles and stay away from joint limits.

    This lesson answers three questions: when can we "rewind" (the inverse)? When we cannot, what exactly got lost (column space and null space)? And how many answers are there (the solution set)?

    2直觉：倒放电影Intuition: play the movie backwards

    把矩阵 \(A\) 想成一部电影：每个点从原位置出发，滑到新位置。逆 \(A^{-1}\) 就是倒放这部电影，让每个点滑回去。要能倒放，有一个条件：不能有信息丢失。只要每个终点都能唯一地告诉你“我是从哪里来的”，倒放就成立。

    剪切、旋转、拉伸都满足这个条件。但如果电影把整个平面压到一条线上（行列式 \(=0\)，1.5 节），不同的起点就会挤到同一个终点，倒放时你根本不知道该把它送回哪里。下面选一种变换，把滑块拖到 1 看正放，再拖到 2 看倒放。

    Think of a matrix \(A\) as a movie: every point slides from its old position to a new one. The inverse \(A^{-1}\) plays that movie backwards and slides every point home. Rewinding works under one condition: no information may be lost. As long as every end point can tell you uniquely where it came from, you can send it back.

    Shears, rotations and stretches all qualify. But if the movie squashes the whole plane onto a line (determinant \(=0\), Lesson 1.5), different start points pile up on the same end point, and when you rewind there is no way to know where to send it. Pick a transformation below, drag the slider to 1 to see it play forward, then on to 2 to rewind.

    核心洞见Key insight

    能不能求逆，问的是有没有信息被丢掉。丢掉的那部分有两个名字：被压成零的输入方向叫零空间；输出里真正够得到的那部分叫列空间，够不到的输出就是“信息缺口”。没有方向被压掉、所有输出都够得到，\(A\) 才可逆。

    Whether an inverse exists asks: was any information thrown away? The lost part has two names. The input directions crushed to zero form the null space; the outputs that can be reached at all form the column space. A is invertible exactly when nothing is crushed and everything is reachable.

    5第三个视角：三维里的秩与零空间Third view: rank and null space in 3D

    在三维里，一个 \(3\times3\) 矩阵把单位立方体变成平行六面体。如果有信息丢失，六面体会塌掉：秩 3 是实心的，秩 2 压成一片平行四边形，秩 1 压成一根线，秩 0 压成一个点。能到达的地方（列空间）有多大，取决于秩；被压掉的方向（零空间）有多大，取决于“还剩多少自由”。两者的维数之和永远等于 3：

    In 3D, a \(3\times3\) matrix turns the unit cube into a parallelepiped. If information is lost, that solid collapses: rank 3 stays solid, rank 2 flattens into a parallelogram, rank 1 into a line segment, rank 0 into a point. How much space is reachable (the column space) depends on the rank; how much is crushed (the null space) depends on how much freedom is left over. Their dimensions always add up to 3:

    $$ \underbrace{\dim C(A)}_{\text{rank}} \;+\; \underbrace{\dim N(A)}_{\text{nullity}} \;=\; 3. $$

    转动视角，点预设，或者直接编辑矩阵。立方体的红、绿、蓝三类边是 \(A\) 的第 1、2、3 列。

    6再看数学The math

    提示：把鼠标悬停在公式中带底色的符号上，FIG 2 和 FIG 4 里对应的元素会被加粗。Tip: hover the highlighted symbols in the formulas; the matching elements in FIG 2 and FIG 4 get thicker.

    逆The inverse

    如果存在矩阵 \(A^{-1}\)，使得

    If there is a matrix \(A^{-1}\) such that

    $$ A^{-1}A = A A^{-1} = I, $$

    就说 \(A\) 可逆，\(A^{-1}\) 是它的逆。\(A^{-1}A = I\) 的意思是：先做 \(A\)，再做 \(A^{-1}\)，等于什么都没做（回忆 1.4 节：右边的先作用）。对 \(2\times2\) 矩阵，逆有一个封闭公式：

    we say \(A\) is invertible and \(A^{-1}\) is its inverse. \(A^{-1}A = I\) says: do \(A\), then \(A^{-1}\), and nothing has happened (recall Lesson 1.4: the right-hand factor acts first). For a \(2\times2\) matrix the inverse has a closed form:

    $$ A = \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix} \;\Longrightarrow\; A^{-1} = \frac{1}{ad - bc}\begin{bmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{bmatrix}. $$

    不必死记，验证一下就会了。把两个矩阵乘起来：

    No need to memorise it: check it. Multiply the two matrices:

    $$ \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} ad - bc & -ab + ba \\ cd - dc & -cb + da \end{bmatrix} = (ad - bc)\, I. $$

    除以 \(ad - bc\)（就是行列式）后得到 \(I\)。例如 \(A = \begin{bmatrix}2&1\\4&3\end{bmatrix}\)，\(\det A = 6 - 4 = 2\)，所以 \(A^{-1} = \tfrac12\begin{bmatrix}3&-1\\-4&2\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}1.5&-0.5\\-2&1\end{bmatrix}\)。把两者相乘，确实得到 \(I\)。

    Dividing by \(ad - bc\) (the determinant) gives \(I\). For example \(A = \begin{bmatrix}2&1\\4&3\end{bmatrix}\) has \(\det A = 6 - 4 = 2\), so \(A^{-1} = \tfrac12\begin{bmatrix}3&-1\\-4&2\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}1.5&-0.5\\-2&1\end{bmatrix}\). Multiply the two and you do get \(I\).

    这个公式怎么读How to read the formula

    除以行列式，是在抵消 \(A\) 对面积的缩放（1.5 节）。\(\det A \to 0\) 时分母趋于零，\(A^{-1}\) 的元素爆炸：这正是 FIG 2 里“逆有多大”条变红的原因。\(\det A = 0\) 时没有逆。

    Dividing by the determinant cancels \(A\)'s scaling of area (Lesson 1.5). As \(\det A \to 0\) the denominator vanishes and the entries of \(A^{-1}\) blow up: that is why the "how big is the inverse" bar in FIG 2 turns red. At \(\det A = 0\) there is no inverse.

    乘积的逆：先穿袜子，后穿鞋The inverse of a product: socks and shoes

    \(AB\) 的意思是“先 \(B\) 后 \(A\)”（1.4 节）。撤销一串动作，要倒着撤：先脱鞋（撤销 \(A\)），再脱袜子（撤销 \(B\)）：

    \(AB\) means "\(B\) first, then \(A\)" (Lesson 1.4). To undo a sequence you must undo it in reverse order: shoes off first (undo \(A\)), then socks (undo \(B\)):

    $$ (AB)^{-1} = B^{-1}A^{-1}. $$

    验证：\((AB)(B^{-1}A^{-1}) = A(BB^{-1})A^{-1} = AA^{-1} = I\)。数值例子：\(A = \begin{bmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{bmatrix}\)，\(B = \begin{bmatrix}2&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}\)，则 \(AB = \begin{bmatrix}2&1\\0&1\end{bmatrix}\)，\((AB)^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix}0.5&-0.5\\0&1\end{bmatrix}\)，恰好等于 \(B^{-1}A^{-1}\)，而 \(A^{-1}B^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix}0.5&-1\\0&1\end{bmatrix}\) 不对。

    Check: \((AB)(B^{-1}A^{-1}) = A(BB^{-1})A^{-1} = AA^{-1} = I\). A numeric example: \(A = \begin{bmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{bmatrix}\), \(B = \begin{bmatrix}2&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}\), so \(AB = \begin{bmatrix}2&1\\0&1\end{bmatrix}\) and \((AB)^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix}0.5&-0.5\\0&1\end{bmatrix}\), which equals \(B^{-1}A^{-1}\), while \(A^{-1}B^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix}0.5&-1\\0&1\end{bmatrix}\) is wrong.

    列空间与秩Column space and rank

    \(A\mathbf x\) 是 \(A\) 各列的线性组合，系数是 \(\mathbf x\) 的各个分量（1.3 节的列视角）。所以所有能够作为输出的向量，恰好是各列的张成（1.2 节）。这个集合叫 \(A\) 的列空间：

    \(A\mathbf x\) is a combination of the columns of \(A\), weighted by the entries of \(\mathbf x\) (the column view from Lesson 1.3). So the set of all possible outputs is exactly the span of the columns (Lesson 1.2). It is called the column space:

    $$ \hl{col}{C(A)} = \{\, A\mathbf x : \mathbf x \in \R^n \,\} = \operatorname{span}\{\text{columns of } A\}. $$

    它的维数叫秩（rank）：\(\operatorname{rank}A = \dim C(A)\)，也就是线性无关的列的个数。一个 \(2\times2\) 矩阵的秩是 2（列空间是整个平面）、1（一条线）或 0（只有原点）。同一个矩阵，线性无关的行也同样多（这是个定理，证明见 Strang）。

    Its dimension is the rank: \(\operatorname{rank}A = \dim C(A)\), the number of linearly independent columns. A \(2\times2\) matrix has rank 2 (the column space is the whole plane), 1 (a line) or 0 (just the origin). The same matrix also has exactly that many independent rows (a theorem; see Strang for the proof).

    零空间与秩–零化度定理Null space and the rank–nullity theorem

    被 \(A\) 压成零向量的所有输入，叫 \(A\) 的零空间：

    The inputs that \(A\) crushes to the zero vector form its null space:

    $$ \hl{null}{N(A)} = \{\, \mathbf x : A\mathbf x = \mathbf 0 \,\}. $$

    它总是包含 \(\mathbf 0\)，而且是子空间：若 \(A\mathbf u = A\mathbf w = \mathbf 0\)，则 \(A(\mathbf u + s\mathbf w) = \mathbf 0\)。它的维数叫零化度（nullity）。例如 \(A = \begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{bmatrix}\)：第二列是第一列的 2 倍，列空间是 \((1,2)\) 方向的直线；\(A\mathbf x = \mathbf 0\) 等价于 \(x + 2y = 0\)，零空间是 \((-2, 1)\) 方向的直线。

    It always contains \(\mathbf 0\), and it is a subspace: if \(A\mathbf u = A\mathbf w = \mathbf 0\) then \(A(\mathbf u + s\mathbf w) = \mathbf 0\). Its dimension is the nullity. Example: \(A = \begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{bmatrix}\). The second column is twice the first, so the column space is the line along \((1,2)\); \(A\mathbf x = \mathbf 0\) means \(x + 2y = 0\), so the null space is the line along \((-2, 1)\).

    每个输入方向，要么“活下来”（贡献一维列空间），要么“被压掉”（贡献一维零空间），没有第三种命运。这就是秩–零化度定理：对有 \(n\) 列的矩阵，

    Every input direction either "survives" (and contributes one dimension of column space) or "is crushed" (and contributes one dimension of null space); there is no third fate. This is the rank–nullity theorem: for a matrix with \(n\) columns,

    $$ \operatorname{rank} A \;+\; \operatorname{nullity} A \;=\; n. $$

    FIG 5 里 \(n = 3\)：秩 3 → 零化度 0；秩 2 → 1；秩 1 → 2；秩 0 → 3。

    In FIG 5, \(n = 3\): rank 3 gives nullity 0; rank 2 gives 1; rank 1 gives 2; rank 0 gives 3.

    \(A\mathbf x = \mathbf b\) 的解集The solution set of \(A\mathbf x = \mathbf b\)

    $$ A\,\hl{x}{\mathbf x} = \hl{b}{\mathbf b} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \text{solutions} = \hl{xp}{\mathbf x_p} + \hl{null}{N(A)}. $$

    有没有解：当且仅当 \(\mathbf b \in C(A)\)。有几个解：只要有一个特解 \(\mathbf x_p\)（\(A\mathbf x_p = \mathbf b\)），再加上零空间里任意向量 \(\mathbf n\)，\(A(\mathbf x_p + \mathbf n) = \mathbf b + \mathbf 0 = \mathbf b\) 仍然成立。反过来，两个解的差 \(\mathbf x - \mathbf x_p\) 被 \(A\) 送到 \(\mathbf 0\)，所以落在零空间里。因此解集就是零空间平移到 \(\mathbf x_p\)，维数等于零化度：零空间只有 \(\{\mathbf 0\}\) 时解唯一，否则无穷多。例：\(A = \begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{bmatrix}\)，\(\mathbf b = (3, 6)\)。\(\mathbf x_p = (3, 0)\) 是一个特解，通解是 \((3,0) + t(-2, 1)\)；取 \(t = 1\) 得 \((1,1)\)，确有 \(A(1,1) = (3,6)\)。而 \(\mathbf b = (3,5)\) 不是 \((1,2)\) 的倍数，无解。

    Is there a solution? If and only if \(\mathbf b \in C(A)\). How many? Once you have one particular solution \(\mathbf x_p\) (\(A\mathbf x_p = \mathbf b\)), adding any \(\mathbf n\) from the null space still works: \(A(\mathbf x_p + \mathbf n) = \mathbf b + \mathbf 0 = \mathbf b\). Conversely, two solutions differ by a vector \(\mathbf x - \mathbf x_p\) that \(A\) sends to \(\mathbf 0\), so the difference lies in the null space. The solution set is therefore the null space shifted to \(\mathbf x_p\), with dimension equal to the nullity: if the null space is just \(\{\mathbf 0\}\) the solution is unique, otherwise there are infinitely many. Example: \(A = \begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{bmatrix}\), \(\mathbf b = (3, 6)\). One particular solution is \(\mathbf x_p = (3, 0)\) and the general solution is \((3,0) + t(-2, 1)\); with \(t = 1\) we get \((1,1)\), and indeed \(A(1,1) = (3,6)\). But \(\mathbf b = (3,5)\) is not a multiple of \((1,2)\), so there is no solution.

    方阵：五句话说的是同一件事Square matrices: five statements, one fact

    对 \(n\times n\) 方阵 \(A\)，下面几条互相等价：

    For an \(n\times n\) matrix \(A\), the following are equivalent:

    $$ A \text{ invertible} \;\Leftrightarrow\; \det A \ne 0 \;\Leftrightarrow\; \operatorname{rank}A = n \;\Leftrightarrow\; N(A) = \{\mathbf 0\} \;\Leftrightarrow\; C(A) = \R^n. $$

    几何读法：没有方向被压掉（零空间只有 \(\mathbf 0\)），所以也没有输出够不到（列空间是全空间），所以每个 \(\mathbf b\) 恰好有一个解 \(\mathbf x = A^{-1}\mathbf b\)。“压扁”这件事在 \(\det A = 0\) 时发生，它同时意味着零空间变大、列空间变小。对非方阵（\(m \ne n\)）没有逆，\(m < n\) 时必有非零的零空间，\(m > n\) 时一般有够不到的 \(\mathbf b\)，处理办法见 1.13 节。

    Geometric reading: no direction is crushed (the null space is just \(\mathbf 0\)), hence no output is unreachable (the column space is everything), hence every \(\mathbf b\) has exactly one solution \(\mathbf x = A^{-1}\mathbf b\). "Squashing" happens exactly when \(\det A = 0\), and it makes the null space bigger and the column space smaller at the same time. Non-square matrices (\(m \ne n\)) have no inverse: if \(m < n\) there is always a non-zero null space, and if \(m > n\) some \(\mathbf b\) are generally unreachable. Lesson 1.13 shows how to deal with that.

    实际怎么算How it is computed in practice

    教科书里 \(\mathbf x = A^{-1}\mathbf b\) 很漂亮，代码里很少这么写。库函数（np.linalg.solve、Eigen 的 A.lu().solve(b)）先把 \(A\) 做 LU 分解（高斯消元的矩阵形式），再回代，既快又准。秩也不是靠行列式判断的：np.linalg.matrix_rank 会计算奇异值（1.12 节），把小于阈值的当作零。

    \(\mathbf x = A^{-1}\mathbf b\) is lovely on paper and rarely written in code. Library routines (np.linalg.solve, Eigen's A.lu().solve(b)) factor \(A\) with an LU decomposition (the matrix form of Gaussian elimination) and back-substitute, which is both faster and more accurate. Rank is not found from the determinant either: np.linalg.matrix_rank computes the singular values (Lesson 1.12) and counts those above a threshold.

    7机器人中的它：手不动，肘在动In robotics: the hand stays, the elbow moves

    考虑一个平面三连杆机械臂：3 个关节 \(\mathbf q = (q_1,q_2,q_3)\)，而手的位置只有 2 个坐标 \(\mathbf x = (x, y)\)。手速和关节速度之间是一个 \(2\times3\) 的雅可比矩阵：\(\dot{\mathbf x} = J(\mathbf q)\,\dot{\mathbf q}\)（3.3 节）。第 \(i\) 列是“只转第 \(i\) 个关节时手怎么动”：手相对于关节 \(i\) 的位置向量逆时针转 90°，即 \((-(y_h - y_i),\, x_h - x_i)\)。

    \(J\) 有 3 列、2 行，秩至多是 2。由秩–零化度定理，零空间至少是 \(3 - 2 = 1\) 维：存在一个关节速度方向 \(\mathbf n\)，使 \(J\mathbf n = \mathbf 0\)。沿着它转动关节，手一动也不动，但手肘和手腕都在动。这个方向可以直接算出来：\(J\) 的两行都是 \(\mathbb R^3\) 里的向量，\(\mathbf n\) 与两行都垂直，所以 \(\mathbf n\) 平行于两行的叉积（1.8 节）。

    下面是这个想法的完整演示。拖动黄色的手，机械臂用阻尼伪逆迭代自己找关节角（伪逆见 1.13 节，这里是页内自带的简单实现）。拖动白色的手肘或下方的“自运动”滑块，关节沿着零空间方向 \(\mathbf n(\mathbf q)\) 积分：\(\dot{\mathbf q} = \alpha\,\mathbf n(\mathbf q)\)。注意积分只在一阶意义上保持手不动，所以每一步都要小步前进、重新归一化，再用牛顿修正把手拉回原位。紫色小箭头是 \(\mathbf n\) 引起的各关节点的速度：手上的那个是零。

    Consider a planar three-link arm: three joints \(\mathbf q = (q_1,q_2,q_3)\), but the hand position has only two coordinates \(\mathbf x = (x, y)\). The hand velocity and the joint velocities are related by a \(2\times3\) Jacobian: \(\dot{\mathbf x} = J(\mathbf q)\,\dot{\mathbf q}\) (Lesson 3.3). Column \(i\) says "how the hand moves when only joint \(i\) turns": the hand's position relative to joint \(i\), rotated 90° counter-clockwise, \((-(y_h - y_i),\, x_h - x_i)\).

    \(J\) has 3 columns and 2 rows, so its rank is at most 2. By the rank–nullity theorem the null space is at least \(3 - 2 = 1\) dimensional: there is a joint-velocity direction \(\mathbf n\) with \(J\mathbf n = \mathbf 0\). Turn the joints along it and the hand does not move at all, yet the elbow and wrist do. We can even compute it directly: the two rows of \(J\) are vectors in \(\mathbb R^3\), and \(\mathbf n\) is perpendicular to both, so \(\mathbf n\) is parallel to their cross product (Lesson 1.8).

    Here is the whole idea in action. Drag the amber hand and the arm finds joint angles itself with damped pseudo-inverse iterations (the pseudo-inverse is Lesson 1.13; this page uses a small local implementation). Drag the white elbow or the "self-motion" slider below and the joints integrate along the null direction \(\mathbf n(\mathbf q)\): \(\dot{\mathbf q} = \alpha\,\mathbf n(\mathbf q)\). The integration keeps the hand still only to first order, so every step is small, renormalised, and followed by a Newton correction that pulls the hand back to its place. The small purple arrows are the velocities of the joints under \(\mathbf n\): the one on the hand is zero.

    真实系统里In real systems

    Franka Panda、KUKA iiwa 这类 7 关节机械臂，手的位姿是 6 维（3 个位置加 3 个姿态），\(J\) 是 \(6\times7\)，一般姿态下秩为 6，零空间正好 1 维：手肘可以绕着“肩–腕连线”转一圈，手完全不动。控制器常写成 \(\dot{\mathbf q} = J^{+}\dot{\mathbf x} + (I - J^{+}J)\,\mathbf z\)：第一项完成手的任务，第二项把任意想要的关节运动 \(\mathbf z\)（离关节限位远一点、离障碍物远一点）投影进零空间，保证不影响手。伪逆 \(J^{+}\) 在 1.13 节展开。

    For 7-joint arms such as the Franka Panda or KUKA iiwa, the hand pose is 6-dimensional (3 position plus 3 orientation), so \(J\) is \(6\times7\) with rank 6 in a generic pose and a null space of exactly 1 dimension: the elbow can circle around the shoulder-to-wrist line while the hand stays put. Controllers often write \(\dot{\mathbf q} = J^{+}\dot{\mathbf x} + (I - J^{+}J)\,\mathbf z\): the first term does the hand's job, and the second projects any wanted joint motion \(\mathbf z\) (stay away from joint limits, stay away from obstacles) into the null space so that the hand is unaffected. The pseudo-inverse \(J^{+}\) is the subject of Lesson 1.13.

    8常见误区Common pitfalls

    误区 1：用 inv(A) @ b 来解 Ax = bPitfall 1: computing inv(A) @ b to solve Ax = b

    先显式求逆再乘，比直接解方程慢，数值上也更不准，还会白白算出你根本不需要的整个矩阵。写 np.linalg.solve(A, b)。只有当你真的需要 \(A^{-1}\) 本身（比如要它的元素）时才去求逆。

    Forming the inverse and multiplying is slower than solving directly, less accurate numerically, and computes a whole matrix you never needed. Write np.linalg.solve(A, b). Invert only when you truly need \(A^{-1}\) itself (its entries, say).

    误区 2：“只要 det ≠ 0 就没问题”Pitfall 2: "as long as det ≠ 0 we are fine"

    几乎奇异的矩阵会把噪声放大，放大倍数约和 \(1/|\det|\) 同量级。取 \(A = \begin{bmatrix}1&2\\0.5&1.1\end{bmatrix}\)，\(\det A = 0.1\)，\(A^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix}11&-20\\-5&10\end{bmatrix}\)。若 \(\mathbf b = (3, 1.6)\)，解是 \((1, 1)\)；传感器只让 \(b_2\) 变了 0.01，变成 \(\mathbf b = (3, 1.61)\)，解就变成 \((0.8, 1.1)\)。输入误差 0.01，输出误差 0.22：约 20 倍。矩阵越接近奇异，这个倍数越大（1.12 节的条件数）。

    A nearly singular matrix amplifies noise, by a factor of roughly \(1/|\det|\). Take \(A = \begin{bmatrix}1&2\\0.5&1.1\end{bmatrix}\), \(\det A = 0.1\), \(A^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix}11&-20\\-5&10\end{bmatrix}\). For \(\mathbf b = (3, 1.6)\) the solution is \((1, 1)\). If the sensor shifts only \(b_2\) by 0.01, giving \(\mathbf b = (3, 1.61)\), the solution jumps to \((0.8, 1.1)\). An input error of 0.01 became an output error of 0.22: about 20 times. The closer to singular, the bigger the factor (the condition number of Lesson 1.12).

    误区 3：给非方阵求逆Pitfall 3: inverting a non-square matrix

    \(2\times3\) 的雅可比没有逆：列比行多，必有零空间，不可能“一一对应”。需要的是伪逆（最小范数解或最小二乘解），见 1.13 节。

    A \(2\times3\) Jacobian has no inverse: with more columns than rows there is always a null space, so no one-to-one correspondence. What you need is the pseudo-inverse (the minimum-norm or least-squares solution); see Lesson 1.13.

    误区 4：\((AB)^{-1} = A^{-1}B^{-1}\)Pitfall 4: \((AB)^{-1} = A^{-1}B^{-1}\)

    错，顺序要反过来：\((AB)^{-1} = B^{-1}A^{-1}\)。穿袜子再穿鞋，脱的时候必须先脱鞋。上一节的数值例子里，\(A^{-1}B^{-1}\) 和正确答案差一个元素。机器人里常见的情形：\(({}^{A}T_{B}\,{}^{B}T_{C})^{-1} = ({}^{B}T_{C})^{-1}({}^{A}T_{B})^{-1}\)。

    Wrong: the order reverses, \((AB)^{-1} = B^{-1}A^{-1}\). Socks, then shoes; off comes the shoe first. In the numeric example above, \(A^{-1}B^{-1}\) differs from the right answer in one entry. A frequent robotics instance: \(({}^{A}T_{B}\,{}^{B}T_{C})^{-1} = ({}^{B}T_{C})^{-1}({}^{A}T_{B})^{-1}\).

    误区 5：浮点数里“秩”是个阈值问题Pitfall 5: in floating point, rank is a question of thresholds

    数学上秩 1 的矩阵，经过浮点运算常常变成“秩 2，但第二个奇异值是 \(10^{-17}\)”。比如 0.1*3 - 0.3 的结果是 \(5.55\times10^{-17}\)，不是 0。所以“\(\det = 0\)”这种判断在代码里几乎永远不成立；要看奇异值（1.12 节），并和一个相对阈值比较。

    A matrix of rank 1 in exact arithmetic often turns into "rank 2, but the second singular value is \(10^{-17}\)" after floating-point operations. For instance 0.1*3 - 0.3 evaluates to \(5.55\times10^{-17}\), not 0. A test like "\(\det = 0\)" therefore almost never fires in code; look at the singular values (Lesson 1.12) and compare them with a relative threshold.

    9小练习Check yourself

    1. 矩阵 \(A = \begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{bmatrix}\)，下面哪个向量在它的零空间里？1. For \(A = \begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{bmatrix}\), which vector lies in its null space?
    \(A(2,-1) = (2-2,\ 4-4) = (0,0)\)。其余三个：\(A(1,2) = (5,10)\)，\(A(2,1) = (4,8)\)，\(A(1,-2) = (-3,-6)\)，都不是零。零空间就是方程 \(x + 2y = 0\) 的解，即 \((-2,1)\) 方向的直线。\(A(2,-1) = (2-2,\ 4-4) = (0,0)\). The others: \(A(1,2) = (5,10)\), \(A(2,1) = (4,8)\), \(A(1,-2) = (-3,-6)\), none zero. The null space is the solution set of \(x + 2y = 0\), the line along \((-2,1)\).
    2. 一个 7 关节机械臂要跟踪 6 维的手部位姿，雅可比 \(J\) 是 \(6\times7\)，在某个姿态下秩为 6。它的零空间有几维？2. A 7-joint arm tracks a 6-D hand pose; its Jacobian \(J\) is \(6\times7\) and has rank 6 in some pose. How many dimensions does its null space have?
    秩–零化度：\(\operatorname{rank} + \operatorname{nullity} = n = 7\)（列数，也就是关节数），所以零化度 \(= 7 - 6 = 1\)。这 1 维自由就是“手不动，肘在动”的自运动。Rank–nullity: \(\operatorname{rank} + \operatorname{nullity} = n = 7\) (the number of columns, i.e. joints), so nullity \(= 7 - 6 = 1\). That one dimension of freedom is the self-motion: hand fixed, elbow moving.
    3. 你对点云先做 \(B\)（缩放），再做 \(A\)（剪切）。要把点云恢复原状，应该用哪个矩阵？3. You apply \(B\) (a scaling) to a point cloud and then \(A\) (a shear). Which matrix restores the original cloud?
    做过的是 \(AB\)（右边的先作用）。要撤销，先撤销最后做的 \(A\)，再撤销 \(B\)：\(B^{-1}\) 在 \(A^{-1}\) 的左边，\(B^{-1}A^{-1}\)。检验：\(B^{-1}A^{-1}AB = B^{-1}B = I\)。What you applied is \(AB\) (the right-hand factor acts first). To undo it, undo the last step \(A\) first and then \(B\): \(B^{-1}\) goes to the left of \(A^{-1}\), giving \(B^{-1}A^{-1}\). Check: \(B^{-1}A^{-1}AB = B^{-1}B = I\).
    4. 还是 \(A = \begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{bmatrix}\)，方程 \(A\mathbf x = (3, 5)\) 有几个解？4. With the same \(A = \begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{bmatrix}\), how many solutions does \(A\mathbf x = (3, 5)\) have?
    列空间是 \((1,2)\) 方向的直线，输出只能是 \((t, 2t)\)。\((3,5)\) 不在这条线上（\(3\) 的两倍是 \(6\) 而不是 \(5\)），所以无解。如果换成 \(\mathbf b = (3,6)\)，则有一整条直线的解，见上一节的例子。The column space is the line along \((1,2)\), so every output has the form \((t, 2t)\). \((3,5)\) is not on it (twice 3 is 6, not 5), so there is no solution. With \(\mathbf b = (3,6)\) instead, the solutions would fill a whole line, as in the example in the math section.
    5. 你要在代码里反复解 \(A\mathbf x = \mathbf b\)，\(A\) 是一个 \(50\times50\) 的良态方阵。最合适的做法是？5. In code you must solve \(A\mathbf x = \mathbf b\) for a well-conditioned \(50\times50\) matrix \(A\). What is the best approach?
    直接分解后回代，更快也更准；1/det 公式在高维里运算量爆炸，且数值上很差。显式求逆只有在真的需要 \(A^{-1}\) 的元素时才值得。Factorizing and back-substituting is faster and more accurate; the \(1/\det\) formula explodes in cost in high dimensions and is numerically poor. Explicit inversion is worth it only when you actually need the entries of \(A^{-1}\).
