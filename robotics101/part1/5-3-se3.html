齐次变换与 SE(3)Homogeneous Transforms & SE(3)

旋转加平移不是线性变换，没法直接写成一个矩阵乘法。给坐标多添一个 1，升高一维，"旋转加平移"就变成了一个 4×4 矩阵，位姿的连乘、求逆都变成了普通的矩阵运算。这样的矩阵构成的集合就是 SE(3)，它是李群的第一个主角。 Rotation plus translation is not a linear map, so it cannot be a single matrix product. Append a 1 to the coordinates, go up one dimension, and "rotate then translate" becomes one 4×4 matrix; chaining and inverting poses turn into ordinary matrix algebra. The set of such matrices is SE(3), the first leading actor among Lie groups.

    1为什么机器人需要它Why robots need this

    机械臂有六七个关节，每个关节处的坐标系相对前一个都是"转一下再平移一段"。末端在底座里的位姿，是这一长串"旋转 + 平移"的连乘。用 5.1 节的写法 \((R, \mathbf t)\)，每乘一次要算 \(R_1R_2\) 和 \(R_1\mathbf t_2 + \mathbf t_1\) 两个式子，求逆还要记 \(-R\T\mathbf t\)。关节多了，推导和代码都很容易写错。

    齐次坐标的好处是：把"旋转 + 平移"装进同一个矩阵，连乘就是矩阵乘法，求逆有固定的分块公式。相机投影 \(P = K\,[R\,|\,\mathbf t]\)、机械臂的正运动学、SLAM 里的位姿图，都靠这一个小技巧。更重要的是，这样得到的矩阵集合 SE(3) 本身是一个群，下一节会看到，它也是一个弯曲的光滑流形，这正是李群理论登场的地方。

    A robot arm has six or seven joints, and each joint frame sits relative to the previous one by "turn, then slide". The end effector's pose in the base frame is a long chain of such "rotation + translation" steps. With the \((R, \mathbf t)\) notation of Lesson 5.1, every product needs two formulas, \(R_1R_2\) and \(R_1\mathbf t_2 + \mathbf t_1\), and inversion adds \(-R\T\mathbf t\). With many joints, both derivations and code are easy to get wrong.

    The beauty of homogeneous coordinates is that they put "rotation + translation" in one matrix: chaining is matrix multiplication and inversion has a fixed block formula. Camera projection \(P = K\,[R\,|\,\mathbf t]\), forward kinematics and SLAM pose graphs all rely on this one trick. More importantly, the set SE(3) of such matrices is itself a group, and, as the next lesson shows, a curved smooth manifold: exactly where Lie-group theory enters.

    2直觉：升一维，把平移变成"剪切"Intuition: go up one dimension and translation becomes a shear

    线性变换必须把原点留在原点（1.3 节），所以平移不是线性的。但看一个小把戏：把平面上的点 \((x, y)\) 放到三维空间的高度 \(w = 1\) 的那张平面上，变成 \((x, y, 1)\)。现在对三维空间做一个剪切：每个点沿 \(xy\) 方向平移的距离，和它的高度 \(w\) 成正比，\((x, y, w) \mapsto (x + t_x w,\ y + t_y w,\ w)\)。高度为 1 的那张平面上，每个点恰好平移了 \((t_x, t_y)\)；这个剪切是三维里的线性变换，因此可以写成矩阵乘法。

    还有一个意外的好处：高度 \(w = 0\) 的点，剪切对它们没有任何影响。所以 \(w=0\) 的"点"可以用来表示方向向量，它们只会被旋转，不会被平移。下面的实验里，拖动 \(t_x, t_y, \theta\)：琥珀色的点（\(w=1\)）会被平移，紫色的箭头（\(w=0\)）不会。

    A linear map must keep the origin fixed (Lesson 1.3), so translation is not linear. Now watch a small trick: put the plane point \((x, y)\) at the plane of height \(w = 1\) in 3D, as \((x, y, 1)\). Then shear 3D space: every point slides in the \(xy\) direction by an amount proportional to its height \(w\), \((x, y, w) \mapsto (x + t_x w,\ y + t_y w,\ w)\). On the plane \(w = 1\) every point moves by exactly \((t_x, t_y)\); and this shear is a linear map in 3D, hence a matrix product.

    There is a pleasant side effect: points with height \(w = 0\) are untouched by the shear. So "points" with \(w=0\) can represent direction vectors: they get rotated but never translated. In the lab below, drag \(t_x, t_y, \theta\): the amber point (\(w=1\)) is translated while the purple arrow (\(w=0\)) is not.

    核心洞见Key insight

    矩阵的最后一列是平移，因为 \(\hat w\)（即"原点"）被送到了 \((\mathbf t, 1)\)。矩阵的左上角是旋转，右下角的 1 保证平面 \(w=1\) 被送回 \(w=1\)。对点 \((\mathbf p, 1)\) 做乘法，得到 \((R\mathbf p + \mathbf t,\ 1)\)，恰好就是"先转后移"。

    The last column of the matrix is the translation, because \(\hat w\) (the "origin") is sent to \((\mathbf t, 1)\). The upper-left block is the rotation, and the 1 in the lower-right corner keeps the plane \(w=1\) mapped onto \(w=1\). Multiplying the point \((\mathbf p, 1)\) yields \((R\mathbf p + \mathbf t,\ 1)\): exactly "rotate then translate".

    3动手玩：SE(2) 与求逆Play: SE(2) and the inverse

    平面里的刚体运动构成 SE(2)，位姿是 \((x, y, \theta)\)，3×3 齐次矩阵是

    Rigid motions of the plane form SE(2); a pose is \((x, y, \theta)\) and its 3×3 homogeneous matrix is

    $$ T = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta & x \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta & y \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} R & \mathbf t \\ \mathbf 0\T & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad T^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} R\T & -R\T\mathbf t \\ \mathbf 0\T & 1 \end{bmatrix}. $$

    \(T\) 把机器人系的读数变成世界系的读数，\(T^{-1}\) 反过来。直观上，\(T^{-1}\) 就是"站在机器人身上看世界"：机器人变成原点，朝向变成 \(+x\)，世界系被放到了 \(T^{-1}\) 所描述的位置。左边是世界视角，右边是机器人视角。拖动任何一边的点，另一边同步更新。

    \(T\) turns robot-frame readings into world-frame readings, and \(T^{-1}\) does the opposite. Intuitively, \(T^{-1}\) is "seeing the world from the robot's seat": the robot becomes the origin, its heading becomes \(+x\), and the world frame is placed where \(T^{-1}\) says. The left panel is the world view and the right one the robot view. Drag the point in either panel and the other follows.

    三维：SE(3) 编辑器In 3D: the SE(3) editor

    4再看数学The math

    提示：把鼠标悬停在公式中带底色的符号上，FIG 3、FIG 4 或 FIG 5 中对应的元素会被高亮。Tip: hover the highlighted symbols in the formulas; the matching element in FIG 3, FIG 4 or FIG 5 lights up.

    齐次坐标：点和方向Homogeneous coordinates: points and directions

    给 \(\mathbf p \in \R^3\) 添一个分量 1，得到齐次坐标 \(\tilde{\mathbf p} = (\mathbf p, 1) \in \R^4\)。方向（自由向量）则用 \((\mathbf d, 0)\)。位姿 \(T\) 作用在它们上：

    Append a 1 to \(\mathbf p \in \R^3\) to get the homogeneous coordinates \(\tilde{\mathbf p} = (\mathbf p, 1) \in \R^4\). Directions (free vectors) use \((\mathbf d, 0)\). A pose \(T\) acts on them by

    $$ T\tilde{\mathbf p} = \begin{bmatrix} \hl{R}{R} & \hl{t}{\mathbf t} \\ \mathbf 0\T & 1 \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} \mathbf p \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} R\mathbf p + \mathbf t \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad T\begin{bmatrix} \mathbf d \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} R\mathbf d \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}. $$

    点 − 点 = 方向（\(w\) 分量 \(1 - 1 = 0\)），点 + 方向 = 点（\(1 + 0 = 1\)），点 + 点没有意义（\(w = 2\)）。这和直觉一致：两个位置相减得到位移，两个位置相加没有物理意义。

    Point − point = direction (the \(w\) component is \(1 - 1 = 0\)), point + direction = point (\(1 + 0 = 1\)), and point + point makes no sense (\(w = 2\)). That matches intuition: subtracting positions gives a displacement, adding positions has no physical meaning.

    SE(3) 的定义The definition of SE(3)

    $$ \SE = \left\{ T = \begin{bmatrix} R & \mathbf t \\ \mathbf 0\T & 1 \end{bmatrix} \;\middle|\; R \in \SO,\ \mathbf t \in \R^3 \right\}, \qquad \mathrm{SE}(2) \text{ is the same with } R \in \mathrm{SO}(2),\ \mathbf t\in\R^2. $$

    "S"是 special（\(\det R = +1\)，没有镜像），"E"是 Euclidean（保持距离）。它有 \(3 + 3 = 6\) 个自由度（SE(2) 有 \(1 + 2 = 3\) 个）。注意 \(T\) 本身不是正交矩阵：\(T\T T \ne I\)，所以 \(T^{-1} \ne T\T\)。

    "S" is special (\(\det R = +1\), no reflections) and "E" is Euclidean (distances are preserved). It has \(3 + 3 = 6\) degrees of freedom (SE(2) has \(1 + 2 = 3\)). Note that \(T\) itself is not orthogonal: \(T\T T \ne I\), so \(T^{-1} \ne T\T\).

    连乘与求逆Composition and inverse

    分块矩阵相乘，正好重现了 5.1 节的两个公式：

    Block multiplication reproduces the two formulas of Lesson 5.1:

    $$ \begin{bmatrix} R_1 & \mathbf t_1 \\ \mathbf 0\T & 1 \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} R_2 & \mathbf t_2 \\ \mathbf 0\T & 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} R_1R_2 & R_1\mathbf t_2 + \mathbf t_1 \\ \mathbf 0\T & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad T^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} R\T & -R\T\mathbf t \\ \mathbf 0\T & 1 \end{bmatrix}. $$

    验证逆：\(\begin{bmatrix} R & \mathbf t \\ \mathbf 0\T & 1 \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} R\T & -R\T\mathbf t \\ \mathbf 0\T & 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} RR\T & -RR\T\mathbf t + \mathbf t \\ \mathbf 0\T & 1 \end{bmatrix} = I\)。求逆不需要解线性方程组，只要一次转置和一次矩阵乘向量。而且 \(\det T = \det R = 1\)，\(T^{-1}\) 一定存在。

    Check the inverse: \(\begin{bmatrix} R & \mathbf t \\ \mathbf 0\T & 1 \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} R\T & -R\T\mathbf t \\ \mathbf 0\T & 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} RR\T & -RR\T\mathbf t + \mathbf t \\ \mathbf 0\T & 1 \end{bmatrix} = I\). Inversion needs no linear solve, only a transpose and one matrix-vector product, and since \(\det T = \det R = 1\) the inverse always exists.

    它是一个群It is a group

    上面的公式说明 SE(3) 满足群的四条要求：封闭（两个 \(T\) 相乘还是 \(T\)）、结合律（矩阵乘法天然满足）、有单位元 \(I\)、每个元素有逆。但它不可交换：\(T_1T_2 \ne T_2T_1\)（5.1 节的左乘右乘之别，以及 FIG 4 里"先转后移"和"先移后转"的区别）。群的定义是下一节（5.4）的主题。

    The formulas show that SE(3) satisfies the four requirements of a group: closure (the product of two \(T\)'s is a \(T\)), associativity (free from matrix multiplication), an identity \(I\), and an inverse for every element. It is not commutative: \(T_1T_2 \ne T_2T_1\) (the left/right distinction of Lesson 5.1, and the difference between "turn then move" and "move then turn" in FIG 4). Lesson 5.4 takes up the definition of a group.

    平面三连杆的正运动学Forward kinematics of the planar arm

    $$ \hl{k1}{T_{01}} = \mathrm{Rot}(q_1),\quad \hl{k2}{T_{12}} = \mathrm{Trans}(L_1)\,\mathrm{Rot}(q_2),\quad \hl{k3}{T_{23}} = \mathrm{Trans}(L_2)\,\mathrm{Rot}(q_3),\quad T_{3E} = \mathrm{Trans}(L_3), $$ $$ T_{0E} = T_{01}\,T_{12}\,T_{23}\,T_{3E}, \qquad \mathrm{Trans}(L) = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & L \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}. $$

    读最后一列就得到末端位置，左上角的 \(2\times2\) 块给出末端朝向 \(\theta = q_1 + q_2 + q_3\)（平面里的角度直接相加）。三维关节用的是同样的思路，5.10 节会把它改写成指数积公式。

    The last column gives the tip position and the upper-left \(2\times2\) block gives the tip orientation \(\theta = q_1 + q_2 + q_3\) (planar angles simply add). The same idea carries to 3D joints, and Lesson 5.10 rewrites it as the product of exponentials.

    真实系统里In real systems

    机械臂控制器内部把每个连杆的位姿存成 4×4 矩阵（或旋转 + 平移的对），正运动学就是一连串乘法。相机的投影矩阵 \(P = K[R\,|\,\mathbf t]\) 里的 \([R\,|\,\mathbf t]\) 就是 \(T_{CW}\) 去掉最后一行。点云配准（ICP）输出的是一个 4×4 的 \(T\)。SLAM 的位姿图每条边都是一个相对位姿 \(T_{ij}\)。数值上，连乘几千次后 \(R\) 会慢慢漂离旋转矩阵，需要定期重新正交化（1.10 节）。

    Robot arm controllers store each link's pose as a 4×4 matrix (or a rotation + translation pair), and forward kinematics is a string of multiplications. In a camera projection matrix \(P = K[R\,|\,\mathbf t]\), the \([R\,|\,\mathbf t]\) is \(T_{CW}\) without its last row. Point-cloud registration (ICP) returns a 4×4 \(T\). In SLAM pose graphs every edge is a relative pose \(T_{ij}\). Numerically, after thousands of products \(R\) slowly drifts off the rotation matrices and must be re-orthonormalised periodically (Lesson 1.10).

    6常见误区Common pitfalls

    误区 1：用转置求 4×4 位姿的逆Pitfall 1: inverting a 4×4 pose by transposing it

    只有左上角的 \(R\) 满足 \(R^{-1} = R\T\)。整个 \(T\) 的逆是 \(\begin{bmatrix}R\T & -R\T\mathbf t\\ \mathbf 0\T & 1\end{bmatrix}\)，而 \(T\T = \begin{bmatrix}R\T & \mathbf 0\\ \mathbf t\T & 1\end{bmatrix}\) 完全是另一个东西。如果平移为零，两者碰巧一样，所以这个 bug 在原点附近测试时不会暴露。

    Only the upper-left \(R\) satisfies \(R^{-1} = R\T\). The inverse of the whole \(T\) is \(\begin{bmatrix}R\T & -R\T\mathbf t\\ \mathbf 0\T & 1\end{bmatrix}\), while \(T\T = \begin{bmatrix}R\T & \mathbf 0\\ \mathbf t\T & 1\end{bmatrix}\) is something else entirely. With zero translation the two happen to agree, so the bug stays hidden when you test near the origin.

    误区 2：把方向向量当成点来变换Pitfall 2: transforming a direction as if it were a point

    法向量、速度、力的方向、坐标轴方向都是方向，只应被旋转，不应被平移：用 \(R\mathbf d\)，或者用 \(w = 0\) 的齐次坐标。把它们当点 \((\mathbf d, 1)\) 变换，就会凭空加上 \(\mathbf t\)。反过来，把点当方向变换，位置就不会被平移到正确的地方。（注意：速度作为 twist 的一部分时，变换规则更复杂，见 5.8 节的伴随。）

    Normals, velocities, force directions and axis directions are directions: they should be rotated, not translated. Use \(R\mathbf d\), or homogeneous coordinates with \(w = 0\). Transforming them as points \((\mathbf d, 1)\) adds a spurious \(\mathbf t\); transforming points as directions leaves the position untranslated. (Caveat: when a velocity is part of a twist the rule is more involved, see the adjoint in Lesson 5.8.)

    误区 3：把 SE(3) 里的位姿当向量相加、平均Pitfall 3: adding or averaging poses in SE(3) as vectors

    两个 \(T\) 相加得到的 4×4 矩阵不是位姿，\((T_1 + T_2)/2\) 的左上角不是旋转矩阵（5.4 节会看到它怎样"缩水"）。求平均、插值、做优化都不能直接对矩阵元素做加法，需要李群的工具（5.6–5.9 节）。

    The sum of two \(T\)'s is not a pose, and the upper-left block of \((T_1 + T_2)/2\) is not a rotation matrix (Lesson 5.4 shows how it "shrinks"). Averaging, interpolating and optimising cannot add matrix entries directly; they need Lie-group tools (Lessons 5.6–5.9).

    误区 4：忘了左上角的 R 要保持正交Pitfall 4: letting the rotation block R drift out of orthogonality

    矩阵乘法在浮点数里会累积误差。把一个位姿连乘上万次（比如积分里程计）之后，左上角不再是严格的旋转矩阵，长度开始缩放、直角开始倾斜。对策是定期把 \(R\) 投影回 \(\SO\)（SVD 或 Gram–Schmidt），或者把旋转存成单位四元数，随时归一化。

    Floating-point matrix products accumulate error. After a pose has been multiplied tens of thousands of times (say integrating odometry), the upper-left block is no longer a strict rotation: lengths start to scale and right angles to tilt. The remedy is to project \(R\) back onto \(\SO\) periodically (SVD or Gram–Schmidt), or to store the rotation as a unit quaternion and normalise it as you go.

    1. 平面里把点平移 \((2, 3)\) 的 3×3 齐次矩阵是？1. Which 3×3 homogeneous matrix translates planar points by \((2, 3)\)?

Option A is a scaling, option C is the row-vector convention (not this course's), and option D is a shear.
    2. 机器人位姿 \(T\) 的平移是 \(\mathbf t = (4, 0, 0)\)。机器人的前进方向 \(\mathbf d = (1, 0, 0)\)（\(w = 0\)）在世界里经 \(T\) 变换后，当 \(R = I\) 时是？2. A robot pose \(T\) has translation \(\mathbf t = (4, 0, 0)\). With \(R = I\), what is the heading direction \(\mathbf d = (1, 0, 0)\) (with \(w = 0\)) after the transform \(T\)?
    \(w = 0\) 的方向不受平移影响：\(R\mathbf d + \mathbf t\cdot 0 = (1,0,0)\)。选项 B 是把它当成点 \((\mathbf d, 1)\) 来变换的结果；选项 A 加上了平移却保留 \(w = 0\)，自相矛盾。A direction with \(w = 0\) is unaffected by translation: \(R\mathbf d + \mathbf t\cdot 0 = (1,0,0)\). Option B is what you get by transforming it as a point \((\mathbf d, 1)\); option A adds the translation yet keeps \(w = 0\), which is inconsistent.
    3. 平面位姿 \(T\)：\(R = R(90°)\)，\(\mathbf t = (3, 1)\)。\(T^{-1}\) 的平移是？3. A planar pose \(T\) has \(R = R(90°)\) and \(\mathbf t = (3, 1)\). What is the translation of \(T^{-1}\)?
    \(-R\T\mathbf t\)，\(R\T = \begin{bmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{bmatrix}\)，\(R\T(3,1)^\top = (1,-3)\)，取负得 \((-1, 3)\)。\((1,-3)\) 忘了负号，\((-3,-1)\) 忘了旋转。\(-R\T\mathbf t\) with \(R\T = \begin{bmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{bmatrix}\): \(R\T(3,1)^\top = (1,-3)\), negated \((-1, 3)\). \((1,-3)\) forgets the minus sign and \((-3,-1)\) forgets the rotation.
    4. 平面两连杆：\(L_1 = 1\)，\(q_1 = 90°\)，\(q_2 = 0\)，末端在第二根杆的末端，\(L_2 = 1\)。\(T_{0E} = \mathrm{Rot}(q_1)\mathrm{Trans}(L_1)\mathrm{Rot}(q_2)\mathrm{Trans}(L_2)\) 的末端位置是？4. A planar two-link arm has \(L_1 = 1\), \(q_1 = 90°\), \(q_2 = 0\) and the end point at the end of the second link, \(L_2 = 1\). Where does \(T_{0E} = \mathrm{Rot}(q_1)\mathrm{Trans}(L_1)\mathrm{Rot}(q_2)\mathrm{Trans}(L_2)\) put the end point?
    \(\mathrm{Trans}(1)\mathrm{Rot}(0)\mathrm{Trans}(1)\) 把原点送到 \((2, 0)\)，再经 \(\mathrm{Rot}(90°)\) 变成 \((0, 2)\)。两根杆都沿着第一个关节转后的 \(+y\) 方向伸出去。\(\mathrm{Trans}(1)\mathrm{Rot}(0)\mathrm{Trans}(1)\) sends the origin to \((2, 0)\), and \(\mathrm{Rot}(90°)\) turns it into \((0, 2)\). Both links point along \(+y\) after the first joint turns.
    5. 下列关于 SE(3) 的说法，哪一个是对的？5. Which statement about SE(3) is true?
    封闭、结合、有单位元、有逆，加起来就是群；位姿的乘法不可交换。\(T\) 只有 \(R\) 块是正交的，\(T\T T \ne I\)。Closed, associative, with an identity and inverses: that is a group, and pose multiplication is not commutative. Only the \(R\) block of \(T\) is orthogonal, and \(T\T T \ne I\).
