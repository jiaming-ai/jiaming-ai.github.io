奇异值分解 SVDSingular Value Decomposition

任何矩阵，不管看起来多乱，都只做三件事：先转一下，再沿坐标轴拉伸，最后再转一下。所以单位圆永远会变成一个椭圆，椭圆的半轴长就是奇异值。从奇异值能读出机械臂往哪个方向动得快、往哪个方向动不了，也能读出一个问题离“无解”还有多远。 Every matrix, however messy it looks, does just three things: rotate, stretch along the axes, rotate again. So the unit circle always becomes an ellipse, and the ellipse's semi-axes are the singular values. They tell you which way a robot arm moves easily and which way it is stuck, and how close a problem is to having no answer at all.

    1为什么机器人需要它Why robots need this

    一台机械臂的每个关节都能转，但末端执行器在不同方向上的“灵活程度”并不一样。关节速度 \(\dot{\mathbf q}\) 经过雅可比矩阵 (Jacobian) \(J\) 变成末端速度 \(\dot{\mathbf x} = J\dot{\mathbf q}\)。给所有关节同样的速度预算（一个单位圆），末端能达到的速度就铺成一个椭圆，叫可操作度椭圆 (manipulability ellipse)。椭圆在某个方向很扁，机械臂在那个方向就使不上劲；椭圆压成一条线段，机械臂就进入了奇异位形 (singularity)，在某个方向上彻底动不了。

    这个椭圆的长短轴和朝向，由 \(J\) 的奇异值分解（SVD）完整描述。同一个工具在机器人软件里随处可见：

    • 最小二乘与伪逆：数值逆运动学、相机和手眼标定，都要解“方程比未知数多（或少）”的线性方程组（1.13 节）。
    • 点云 PCA：用一小片点估计桌面的法向量，或者估计一根杆子的主轴方向。
    • 秩与条件数：我的雅可比离奇异还有多远？这个标定问题的数据够不够？
    • 压缩与降维：用很少的数近似一张图像或一个大矩阵。

    Every joint of a robot arm can turn, yet the end effector is not equally nimble in every direction. Joint velocities \(\dot{\mathbf q}\) become end-effector velocity through the Jacobian: \(\dot{\mathbf x} = J\dot{\mathbf q}\). Give every joint the same speed budget (a unit circle) and the reachable end-effector velocities fill an ellipse, the manipulability ellipse. Where the ellipse is thin, the arm is weak; when it collapses to a segment, the arm is at a singularity and cannot move in some direction at all.

    The axes and orientation of that ellipse are exactly what the singular value decomposition (SVD) of \(J\) gives you. The same tool shows up all over robot software:

    • Least squares and the pseudo-inverse: numerical inverse kinematics, camera and hand–eye calibration, any linear system with more (or fewer) equations than unknowns (Lesson 1.13).
    • Point-cloud PCA: estimate the normal of a table top from a patch of points, or the main axis of a rod.
    • Rank and conditioning: how close to singular is my Jacobian? Does my calibration data pin down every parameter?
    • Compression and dimensionality reduction: approximate an image or a large matrix with very few numbers.

    2直觉：圆变成椭圆Intuition: circles become ellipses

    1.3 节里，我们看矩阵把方格网变成平行四边形网。这一节换一个更好用的“探针”：单位圆。圆上每个点都是一个长度为 1 的方向 \(\mathbf x\)，经过矩阵 \(A\) 变成 \(A\mathbf x\)。所有 \(A\mathbf x\) 合在一起，是什么形状？

    答案永远是一个椭圆（极端情况下退化成一条线段，甚至一个点）。椭圆有最长的半轴，也有最短的半轴。于是问题变成：哪一个输入方向被拉得最长？哪一个被压得最短？

    在下图左边拖动圆上的点 \(\mathbf x\)。旁边的曲线图画出 \(|A\mathbf x|\) 随方向角的变化，也可以直接在曲线上拖动。

    In Lesson 1.3 we watched a matrix turn a square grid into a grid of parallelograms. This time we use a better probe: the unit circle. Each point on it is a direction \(\mathbf x\) of length 1, and the matrix \(A\) sends it to \(A\mathbf x\). What shape do all the \(A\mathbf x\) make together?

    The answer is always an ellipse (in extreme cases squashed to a segment, or even a point). An ellipse has a longest semi-axis and a shortest one. So the question becomes: which input direction gets stretched the most, and which the least?

    Drag the point \(\mathbf x\) around the circle on the left. The plot next to it shows \(|A\mathbf x|\) against the input angle; you can drag along the curve too.

    • 找到被拉伸得最多的输入方向（让 \(|A\mathbf x|\) 达到最大）。Find the input direction that gets stretched the most (make \(|A\mathbf x|\) as large as possible).
    • 再找到被拉伸得最少的方向。它和上一个方向是什么关系？Now find the direction stretched the least. How is it related to the first one?

    换几个矩阵试试，你会发现两件总是成立的事：

    1. 拉伸最多的输入方向 \(\cv{\mathbf v_1}\) 和拉伸最少的输入方向 \(\cw{\mathbf v_2}\) 互相垂直。
    2. 它们的像 \(A\cv{\mathbf v_1}\) 和 \(A\cw{\mathbf v_2}\) 正好落在椭圆的长轴和短轴上，所以也互相垂直。

    Try a few matrices and you will notice two things that always hold:

    1. The most-stretched input direction \(\cv{\mathbf v_1}\) and the least-stretched one \(\cw{\mathbf v_2}\) are perpendicular.
    2. Their images \(A\cv{\mathbf v_1}\) and \(A\cw{\mathbf v_2}\) land exactly on the long and short axes of the ellipse, so they are perpendicular too.
    核心洞见Key insight

    每个矩阵都有一对“特别的”正交输入方向 \(\cv{\mathbf v_1}, \cw{\mathbf v_2}\)，它们被送到一对正交输出方向 \(\cv{\mathbf u_1}, \cw{\mathbf u_2}\) 上，长度只乘了 \(\sigma_1, \sigma_2\) 倍：\(A\mathbf v_i = \sigma_i \mathbf u_i\)。数 \(\sigma_1 \ge \sigma_2 \ge 0\) 叫奇异值 (singular values)，也就是椭圆的两个半轴长。这就是奇异值分解的全部内容，剩下的只是把它写成矩阵。

    Every matrix has a special pair of orthogonal input directions \(\cv{\mathbf v_1}, \cw{\mathbf v_2}\) that it sends onto a pair of orthogonal output directions \(\cv{\mathbf u_1}, \cw{\mathbf u_2}\), only scaling them by \(\sigma_1, \sigma_2\): \(A\mathbf v_i = \sigma_i \mathbf u_i\). The numbers \(\sigma_1 \ge \sigma_2 \ge 0\) are the singular values, the ellipse's semi-axes. That is the whole SVD; the rest is writing it as matrices.

    3动手玩：转、拉、转Play: rotate, stretch, rotate

    既然 \(A\) 把正交的 \(\cv{\mathbf v_1}, \cw{\mathbf v_2}\) 送到正交的 \(\cv{\mathbf u_1}, \cw{\mathbf u_2}\) 方向，它的动作就可以拆成三步：

    1. \(V^\top\)：旋转。把 \(\cv{\mathbf v_1}, \cw{\mathbf v_2}\) 转到 x 轴和 y 轴上。
    2. \(\Sigma\)：沿坐标轴拉伸。x 方向乘 \(\sigma_1\)，y 方向乘 \(\sigma_2\)。圆在这一步变成椭圆。
    3. \(U\)：再旋转。把两根坐标轴转到 \(\cv{\mathbf u_1}, \cw{\mathbf u_2}\) 方向（如果 \(A\) 会让平面翻面，这一步还带一次镜像）。

    拖动阶段滑块，或者点按钮，看单位圆一步一步变成 \(A\) 的椭圆。圆上的彩色小点和字母 F 帮你追踪每个点的去向；紫色虚线椭圆是终点。

    Since \(A\) sends the orthogonal \(\cv{\mathbf v_1}, \cw{\mathbf v_2}\) onto the orthogonal directions \(\cv{\mathbf u_1}, \cw{\mathbf u_2}\), its action splits into three steps:

    1. \(V^\top\): rotate. Turn \(\cv{\mathbf v_1}, \cw{\mathbf v_2}\) onto the x- and y-axes.
    2. \(\Sigma\): stretch along the axes. Multiply x by \(\sigma_1\) and y by \(\sigma_2\). This is where the circle becomes an ellipse.
    3. \(U\): rotate again. Turn the two axes onto \(\cv{\mathbf u_1}, \cw{\mathbf u_2}\) (if \(A\) flips the plane over, this step also includes a mirror).

    Scrub the stage slider or press the buttons and watch the unit circle turn into the ellipse of \(A\), one step at a time. The colored dots and the letter F let you track individual points; the dashed purple ellipse is the destination.

    把三步写成符号，就是本节的主角：

    Written in symbols, the three steps are the star of this lesson:

    $$ A \;=\; \underbrace{\hl{U}{U}}_{(3)}\;\underbrace{\hl{S}{\Sigma}}_{(2)}\;\underbrace{\hl{Vt}{V^\top}}_{(1)}, \qquad A\,\hl{v1}{\cv{\mathbf v_1}} = \hl{s1}{\sigma_1}\,\hl{u1}{\cv{\mathbf u_1}}, \qquad A\,\hl{v2}{\cw{\mathbf v_2}} = \hl{s2}{\sigma_2}\,\hl{u2}{\cw{\mathbf u_2}} $$

    4奇异值是矩阵的体检报告Singular values as a health check

    奇异值不关心 \(A\) 往哪个方向转，只关心它拉伸了多少。几个数就能说明很多事：

    • \(\sigma_1\)：\(A\) 能把单位向量拉到的最大长度，也叫 \(A\) 的 2-范数 \(\|A\|_2\)。
    • \(\sigma_2\)（一般地，最小的 \(\sigma_n\)）：最弱方向上还剩多少。\(\sigma_n = 0\) 说明有一个方向被压成了零，信息丢了，\(A\) 不可逆。
    • 秩 (rank) 等于非零奇异值的个数，也就是椭圆“真正有几维”。
    • 条件数 \(\kappa = \sigma_1/\sigma_n\)：椭圆有多扁。解 \(A\mathbf x = \mathbf b\) 时，\(\mathbf b\) 里的相对误差最多会被放大 \(\kappa\) 倍。

    拖动两根列向量 Aî、Aĵ，看右边的柱状图和读数怎么变。

    Singular values ignore which way \(A\) turns things; they only record how much it stretches. A handful of numbers says a lot:

    • \(\sigma_1\): the largest length \(A\) can give a unit vector, also called the 2-norm \(\|A\|_2\).
    • \(\sigma_2\) (in general the smallest, \(\sigma_n\)): how much survives in the weakest direction. \(\sigma_n = 0\) means some direction is squashed to nothing: information is lost and \(A\) is not invertible.
    • The rank is the number of nonzero singular values: how many dimensions the ellipse really has.
    • The condition number \(\kappa = \sigma_1/\sigma_n\) says how flat the ellipse is. When solving \(A\mathbf x = \mathbf b\), a relative error in \(\mathbf b\) can be amplified up to \(\kappa\) times.

    Drag the two columns Aî and Aĵ and watch the bar chart and readouts.

    • 让矩阵变成秩 1：\(\sigma_2 \to 0\)，椭圆塌成一条线段。这时条件数是多少？Make the matrix rank 1: \(\sigma_2 \to 0\) and the ellipse collapses to a segment. What happens to the condition number?
    真实系统里In real systems

    numpy.linalg.matrix_rank 和 Eigen 的秩判断，内部都是先做 SVD，再数有多少个奇异值大于某个容差。标定程序常把整组奇异值打印出来：如果最小的那个比最大的小十几个数量级，说明某个参数根本没被数据约束住，比如手眼标定时机械臂只绕一个轴转过。

    numpy.linalg.matrix_rank and Eigen's rank queries both run an SVD and count the singular values above a tolerance. Calibration tools often print the whole spectrum: if the smallest value is a dozen orders of magnitude below the largest, some parameter is simply not pinned down by the data, for example a hand–eye calibration where the arm only ever rotated about one axis.

    5第二种视角：一层一层叠起来A second view: a stack of rank-1 layers

    把 \(A = U\Sigma V^\top\) 按列和行展开，同一个分解可以读成一个求和：

    Expand \(A = U\Sigma V^\top\) column by column and row by row, and the same decomposition reads as a sum:

    $$ A \;=\; \sigma_1\,\cv{\mathbf u_1}\cv{\mathbf v_1}^{\!\top} + \sigma_2\,\cw{\mathbf u_2}\cw{\mathbf v_2}^{\!\top} + \cdots + \sigma_r\,\mathbf u_r\mathbf v_r^{\top}. $$

    每一项 \(\mathbf u_i\mathbf v_i^\top\) 都是“一列乘一行”，是秩为 1 的矩阵，画出来像一块格子布。奇异值从大到小排好，前面几层承载了大部分内容。只保留前 \(k\) 层，就得到 \(A\) 的秩 \(k\) 近似 \(A_k\)。Eckart–Young 定理说：在所有秩不超过 \(k\) 的矩阵里，\(A_k\) 离 \(A\) 最近。

    一张灰度图就是一个矩阵。下面这张 80×80 的图有 6400 个数，拖动 \(k\) 看看要叠多少层才认得出来。

    Each term \(\mathbf u_i\mathbf v_i^\top\) is "a column times a row", a rank-1 matrix that looks like a plaid when drawn. With the singular values sorted from large to small, the first few layers carry most of the content. Keep only the first \(k\) layers and you get the rank-\(k\) approximation \(A_k\). The Eckart–Young theorem says that among all matrices of rank at most \(k\), \(A_k\) is the closest to \(A\).

    A grayscale image is a matrix. The 80×80 picture below holds 6400 numbers. Slide \(k\) and see how many layers it takes before you can read it.

    • 找到最小的 \(k\)，使相对误差 \(\|A-A_k\|_F/\|A\|_F\) 低于 5%。它需要存多少个数？Find the smallest \(k\) for which the relative error \(\|A-A_k\|_F/\|A\|_F\) drops below 5%. How many numbers does it store?
    同一个分解，两种读法One decomposition, two readings

    几何读法：\(A = U\Sigma V^\top\) 是“转、拉、转”三个动作的复合，适合理解单个变换（雅可比、坐标变换）。
    代数读法：\(A = \sum_i \sigma_i\mathbf u_i\mathbf v_i^\top\) 是一摞按重要性排好序的秩 1 层，适合处理数据（图像、点云、传感器记录）。两种读法说的是同一组 \(\sigma, \mathbf u, \mathbf v\)。

    Geometric reading: \(A = U\Sigma V^\top\) is the composition of three actions, rotate–stretch–rotate. Use it to understand a single transformation (a Jacobian, a change of frame).
    Algebraic reading: \(A = \sum_i \sigma_i\mathbf u_i\mathbf v_i^\top\) is a stack of rank-1 layers sorted by importance. Use it for data (images, point clouds, sensor logs). Both readings use the same \(\sigma, \mathbf u, \mathbf v\).

    6再看数学The math

    定义Definition

    任意一个 \(m\times n\) 的实矩阵 \(A\)（不必是方阵，不必可逆）都可以写成

    Any real \(m\times n\) matrix \(A\) (not necessarily square, not necessarily invertible) can be written as

    $$ A = \hl{U}{U}\,\hl{S}{\Sigma}\,\hl{Vt}{V^\top}, \qquad U \in \R^{m\times m},\;\; V\in\R^{n\times n} \text{ orthogonal},\qquad \Sigma = \operatorname{diag}(\sigma_1,\dots,\sigma_p) \in \R^{m\times n}, $$ $$ \sigma_1 \ge \sigma_2 \ge \dots \ge \sigma_p \ge 0, \qquad p = \min(m, n). $$

    \(U\) 和 \(V\) 是 1.10 节的正交矩阵：列向量两两垂直、长度为 1，作用是旋转（可能带镜像）。\(\Sigma\) 只在对角线上有数。把 \(AV = U\Sigma\) 一列一列地写出来，就回到了 FIG 1 的发现：

    \(U\) and \(V\) are orthogonal matrices from Lesson 1.10: orthonormal columns, acting as rotations (possibly with a mirror). \(\Sigma\) has entries only on its diagonal. Write \(AV = U\Sigma\) one column at a time and you recover the discovery of FIG 1:

    $$ A\,\hl{v1}{\cv{\mathbf v_1}} = \hl{s1}{\sigma_1}\,\hl{u1}{\cv{\mathbf u_1}}, \quad A\,\hl{v2}{\cw{\mathbf v_2}} = \hl{s2}{\sigma_2}\,\hl{u2}{\cw{\mathbf u_2}}, \quad \dots,\quad A\mathbf v_i = \sigma_i\mathbf u_i. $$

    它们从哪里来：\(A^\top A\) 的特征分解Where they come from: the eigen-decomposition of \(A^\top A\)

    代入 \(A = U\Sigma V^\top\)，利用 \(U^\top U = I\)：

    Substitute \(A = U\Sigma V^\top\) and use \(U^\top U = I\):

    $$ A^\top A = V\,\Sigma^\top\Sigma\,V^\top = V \operatorname{diag}(\sigma_1^2,\dots,\sigma_n^2)\, V^\top, \qquad A A^\top = U\,\Sigma\Sigma^\top U^\top. $$

    这正是 1.11 节的特征分解：\(V\) 的列是 \(A^\top A\) 的特征向量，\(\sigma_i^2\) 是它的特征值；\(U\) 的列是 \(AA^\top\) 的特征向量。\(A^\top A\) 是对称矩阵，对称矩阵的特征向量天然正交，这就解释了为什么 \(\cv{\mathbf v_1} \perp \cw{\mathbf v_2}\)。它还是半正定的（\(\mathbf x^\top A^\top A\mathbf x = |A\mathbf x|^2 \ge 0\)），所以特征值非负，开方得到的 \(\sigma_i\) 是实数。求出 \(\mathbf v_i\) 以后，\(\mathbf u_i = A\mathbf v_i/\sigma_i\)。

    也可以这样理解：\(|A\mathbf x|^2 = \mathbf x^\top (A^\top A)\mathbf x\)。FIG 1 里那条曲线的平方，就是对称矩阵 \(A^\top A\) 的二次型，它的最大值、最小值落在特征向量上。

    This is exactly the eigen-decomposition of Lesson 1.11: the columns of \(V\) are eigenvectors of \(A^\top A\), and \(\sigma_i^2\) are its eigenvalues; the columns of \(U\) are eigenvectors of \(AA^\top\). \(A^\top A\) is symmetric, and eigenvectors of a symmetric matrix are automatically orthogonal, which is why \(\cv{\mathbf v_1} \perp \cw{\mathbf v_2}\). It is also positive semidefinite (\(\mathbf x^\top A^\top A\mathbf x = |A\mathbf x|^2 \ge 0\)), so its eigenvalues are nonnegative and their square roots \(\sigma_i\) are real. Once you have \(\mathbf v_i\), \(\mathbf u_i = A\mathbf v_i/\sigma_i\).

    Another way to see it: \(|A\mathbf x|^2 = \mathbf x^\top (A^\top A)\mathbf x\). The square of the curve in FIG 1 is the quadratic form of the symmetric matrix \(A^\top A\), and its maximum and minimum sit on eigenvectors.

    能直接读出来的量What you can read off

    $$ \|A\|_2 = \sigma_1, \qquad \operatorname{rank} A = \#\{\,i : \sigma_i > 0\,\}, \qquad \kappa(A) = \frac{\sigma_1}{\sigma_p}, \qquad |\det A| = \sigma_1\sigma_2\cdots\sigma_n \;\;(m = n). $$

    最后一条很直观：旋转不改变面积，只有第 ② 步的拉伸改变面积，倍数是 \(\sigma_1\sigma_2\)。\(\det A\) 的符号则藏在 \(U\)、\(V\) 是否带镜像里。

    The last one is intuitive: rotations preserve area, so only the stretch in step ② changes it, by a factor \(\sigma_1\sigma_2\). The sign of \(\det A\) hides in whether \(U\) or \(V\) contains a mirror.

    最佳低秩近似（Eckart–Young）Best low-rank approximation (Eckart–Young)

    $$ A_k = \sum_{i=1}^{k} \sigma_i\,\mathbf u_i\mathbf v_i^\top, \qquad \|A - A_k\|_2 = \sigma_{k+1}, \qquad \|A - A_k\|_F = \sqrt{\sigma_{k+1}^2 + \dots + \sigma_p^2}. $$

    而且没有任何秩 \(\le k\) 的矩阵能做得比这更好。直观地说：扔掉最薄的那几层，误差就是被扔掉的那几层的厚度。FIG 4 里的相对误差就是用第二个式子算的。

    No matrix of rank \(\le k\) can do better. Intuitively: throw away the thinnest layers and the error is exactly the thickness of what you threw away. The relative error in FIG 4 is computed with the second formula.

    伪逆预告A preview of the pseudo-inverse

    求逆就是把三步倒着走：先用 \(U^\top\) 转回来，再把每个方向除以 \(\sigma_i\)，最后用 \(V\) 转回去。遇到 \(\sigma_i = 0\) 的方向没法除，那就干脆放弃这个方向：

    Inverting means walking the three steps backwards: rotate back with \(U^\top\), divide each direction by \(\sigma_i\), rotate back with \(V\). A direction with \(\sigma_i = 0\) cannot be divided, so we simply give up on it:

    $$ A^{+} = V\,\Sigma^{+}\,U^\top, \qquad \Sigma^{+} = \operatorname{diag}\!\Big(\tfrac{1}{\sigma_1}, \dots, \tfrac{1}{\sigma_r}, 0, \dots, 0\Big). $$

    这就是 Moore–Penrose 伪逆，1.13 节会看到它给出最小二乘解。注意 \(1/\sigma_i\)：奇异值越小，逆在那个方向上放大得越厉害。

    This is the Moore–Penrose pseudo-inverse; Lesson 1.13 shows that it delivers the least-squares solution. Note the \(1/\sigma_i\): the smaller a singular value, the more the inverse amplifies that direction.

    三维：球变成椭球In 3D: spheres become ellipsoids

    一切照搬到三维：\(3\times3\) 矩阵把单位球变成椭球，三个半轴长是 \(\sigma_1\ge\sigma_2\ge\sigma_3\)。秩 2 的矩阵把球压成一张椭圆盘，秩 1 压成一根针。选一个预设，或者编辑矩阵，再拖动阶段滑块；拖动画面可以转动视角。

    Everything carries over to 3D: a \(3\times3\) matrix maps the unit sphere to an ellipsoid with semi-axes \(\sigma_1\ge\sigma_2\ge\sigma_3\). A rank-2 matrix flattens the sphere into an elliptical disc; rank 1 squashes it into a needle. Pick a preset or edit the matrix, then scrub the stage slider; drag the view to orbit.

    实际怎么算How it is computed in practice

    教科书上“先求 \(A^\top A\) 的特征值”的做法适合理解，不适合编程：构造 \(A^\top A\) 会把条件数平方，小奇异值的精度大量丢失。LAPACK（NumPy、Eigen 背后的库）先把 \(A\) 化为双对角形，再迭代求解（Golub–Kahan 方法）；小矩阵常用 Jacobi 法。直接调用 np.linalg.svd 就好。

    "First take the eigenvalues of \(A^\top A\)" is great for understanding and bad for code: forming \(A^\top A\) squares the condition number and destroys the accuracy of small singular values. LAPACK (behind NumPy and Eigen) first reduces \(A\) to bidiagonal form and then iterates (the Golub–Kahan approach); Jacobi methods are common for small matrices. Just call np.linalg.svd.

    7机器人中的它：可操作度椭圆与点云法向量In robotics: manipulability ellipses and point-cloud normals

    平面两连杆机械臂A planar two-link arm

    两连杆机械臂的末端位置是 \(x = \ell_1\cos\theta_1 + \ell_2\cos(\theta_1+\theta_2)\)，\(y = \ell_1\sin\theta_1 + \ell_2\sin(\theta_1+\theta_2)\)。对两个关节角求偏导，得到 \(2\times2\) 的雅可比：

    The end effector of a two-link arm sits at \(x = \ell_1\cos\theta_1 + \ell_2\cos(\theta_1+\theta_2)\), \(y = \ell_1\sin\theta_1 + \ell_2\sin(\theta_1+\theta_2)\). Differentiate with respect to the two joint angles to get a \(2\times2\) Jacobian:

    $$ \dot{\mathbf x} = J\dot{\mathbf q}, \qquad J = \begin{bmatrix} \ci{-\ell_1 s_1 - \ell_2 s_{12}} & \cj{-\ell_2 s_{12}} \\ \ci{\ell_1 c_1 + \ell_2 c_{12}} & \cj{\ell_2 c_{12}} \end{bmatrix}, \qquad |\det J| = \sigma_1\sigma_2 = \ell_1\ell_2\,|\sin\theta_2|. $$

    （\(s_1 = \sin\theta_1\)，\(c_{12} = \cos(\theta_1+\theta_2)\)，依此类推。）第 1 列是只转关节 1 时末端的速度，第 2 列是只转关节 2 时的速度。所有满足 \(|\dot{\mathbf q}| \le 1\) 的关节速度，被 \(J\) 映射成末端的速度椭圆：半轴为 \(\sigma_i\mathbf u_i\)。拖动肘部或末端，或者用滑块调关节角。

    (\(s_1 = \sin\theta_1\), \(c_{12} = \cos(\theta_1+\theta_2)\), and so on.) Column 1 is the end-effector velocity when only joint 1 turns; column 2 when only joint 2 turns. All joint velocities with \(|\dot{\mathbf q}| \le 1\) are mapped by \(J\) onto the end effector's velocity ellipse, with semi-axes \(\sigma_i\mathbf u_i\). Drag the elbow or the end effector, or use the joint sliders.

    • 把机械臂带到接近奇异的位形（\(\sigma_2/\sigma_1 < 0.05\)）。椭圆的短轴指向哪里？Bring the arm close to a singularity (\(\sigma_2/\sigma_1 < 0.05\)). Which way does the short axis point?
    • 找到一个各向同性的姿态：椭圆几乎是正圆（\(\kappa < 1.05\)）。这时 \(\theta_2\) 是多少？Find an isotropic pose where the ellipse is almost a circle (\(\kappa < 1.05\)). What is \(\theta_2\)?

    力椭圆是速度椭圆的“对偶”。末端施加的力 \(\mathbf F\) 需要的关节力矩是 \(\boldsymbol\tau = J^\top\mathbf F\)（3.4 节会推导）。在力矩预算 \(|\boldsymbol\tau|\le1\) 下，末端能施加的力构成另一个椭圆：主轴方向同样是 \(\mathbf u_i\)，但半轴长变成 \(1/\sigma_i\)。速度上最“懒”的方向，恰好是力上最“壮”的方向，就像自行车的低速挡：转得慢，但蹬得动。切换上图的“力椭圆”看看。手臂伸直时你能单手撑起很重的东西，就是这个道理。

    The force ellipse is the dual of the velocity ellipse. A force \(\mathbf F\) at the end effector needs joint torques \(\boldsymbol\tau = J^\top\mathbf F\) (derived in Lesson 3.4). With a torque budget \(|\boldsymbol\tau|\le1\), the forces the arm can exert form another ellipse with the same axis directions \(\mathbf u_i\) but semi-axes \(1/\sigma_i\). The direction that is slowest for velocity is strongest for force, like a low bicycle gear: slow, but easy to push. Switch the figure to "force" and see. It is also why you can hold up a heavy load with your arm locked straight.

    真实系统里In real systems

    Yoshikawa 可操作度 \(w = \sqrt{\det(JJ^\top)} = \sigma_1\sigma_2\cdots\) 常被用作运动规划的代价项，让机械臂远离奇异位形。实时逆运动学求解器会监控 \(\sigma_{\min}\)：它一变小，\(J^{-1}\) 就会要求巨大的关节速度，所以要改用阻尼最小二乘，把 \(1/\sigma\) 换成 \(\sigma/(\sigma^2+\lambda^2)\)（1.13 节和 3.6 节）。

    Yoshikawa's manipulability \(w = \sqrt{\det(JJ^\top)} = \sigma_1\sigma_2\cdots\) is a common cost term in motion planning to keep an arm away from singularities. Real-time inverse kinematics solvers watch \(\sigma_{\min}\): as it shrinks, \(J^{-1}\) demands huge joint speeds, so they switch to damped least squares, replacing \(1/\sigma\) with \(\sigma/(\sigma^2+\lambda^2)\) (Lessons 1.13 and 3.6).

    点云 PCA：法向量和主轴Point-cloud PCA: normals and main axes

    深度相机拍到桌面上的一小片点。想知道桌面的法向量，可以把这 \(N\) 个点减去均值，按行堆成一个 \(N\times3\) 的数据矩阵 \(X\)，然后做 SVD：\(X = U\Sigma V^\top\)。\(V\) 的三列是点云的三个主方向，\(\sigma_i/\sqrt N\) 是点云沿该方向的标准差。点云铺在一个平面上，所以沿法向几乎没有厚度：最小奇异值对应的 \(\mathbf v_3\) 就是法向量。反过来，一根杆子形状的点云，最大奇异值对应的 \(\mathbf v_1\) 就是它的主轴。

    A depth camera sees a small patch of a table top. To get the table's normal, subtract the mean from the \(N\) points, stack them as rows of an \(N\times3\) data matrix \(X\), and take the SVD \(X = U\Sigma V^\top\). The three columns of \(V\) are the cloud's principal directions, and \(\sigma_i/\sqrt N\) is the cloud's standard deviation along each. A patch lying on a plane has almost no thickness along the normal, so \(\mathbf v_3\), the direction of the smallest singular value, is the normal. Conversely, for a rod-shaped cloud, \(\mathbf v_1\), the direction of the largest singular value, is its main axis.

    • 在平面模式下加大噪声，直到估计的法向量偏离真值超过 20°。这时 \(\sigma_2\) 和 \(\sigma_3\) 是什么关系？In plane mode, raise the noise until the estimated normal is off by more than 20°. How do \(\sigma_2\) and \(\sigma_3\) compare at that point?

    PCL（Point Cloud Library）和 Open3D 的法向量估计做的正是这件事：对每个点取 k 个邻居，求协方差矩阵 \(X^\top X/N\)（其特征值就是 \(\sigma_i^2/N\)），取最小特征值的特征向量。

    The normal estimation in PCL (the Point Cloud Library) and Open3D does exactly this: for each point, take its k nearest neighbours, form the covariance \(X^\top X/N\) (whose eigenvalues are \(\sigma_i^2/N\)) and take the eigenvector of the smallest eigenvalue.

    8常见误区Common pitfalls

    误区 1：奇异值就是特征值Pitfall 1: singular values are eigenvalues

    不是。特征向量是被 \(A\) 送回自己方向的向量（\(A\mathbf v = \lambda\mathbf v\)）；奇异向量是被送到另一个正交方向的向量（\(A\mathbf v_i = \sigma_i\mathbf u_i\)）。旋转 90° 没有实特征向量，但两个奇异值都是 1；剪切 \(\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{bmatrix}\) 的特征值是 1 和 1，奇异值却是 1.618 和 0.618。两者只在对称半正定矩阵上一致（此时 \(U = V\)，正是 FIG 2 的第三个任务）。

    They are not. An eigenvector is sent back along its own direction (\(A\mathbf v = \lambda\mathbf v\)); a singular vector is sent onto another orthogonal direction (\(A\mathbf v_i = \sigma_i\mathbf u_i\)). A 90° rotation has no real eigenvectors, yet both singular values are 1; the shear \(\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{bmatrix}\) has eigenvalues 1 and 1 but singular values 1.618 and 0.618. The two coincide only for symmetric positive semidefinite matrices (then \(U = V\), the third task of FIG 2).

    误区 2：奇异向量是唯一的Pitfall 2: singular vectors are unique

    把 \(\mathbf v_i\) 和 \(\mathbf u_i\) 同时取反，\(A\mathbf v_i = \sigma_i\mathbf u_i\) 依然成立，所以不同的库、甚至同一个库在相邻两帧，可能给出符号相反的结果。如果两个奇异值相等（椭圆是圆），对应平面内任意一对正交方向都行。不要逐元素比较两次 SVD 的结果；点云法向量要用“朝向相机”之类的规则统一符号。

    Flip the sign of \(\mathbf v_i\) and \(\mathbf u_i\) together and \(A\mathbf v_i = \sigma_i\mathbf u_i\) still holds, so different libraries, or the same library on two consecutive frames, may return opposite signs. If two singular values are equal (the ellipse is a circle), any orthogonal pair in that plane works. Never compare two SVDs entry by entry; orient point-cloud normals with a rule such as "point toward the camera".

    误区 3：小奇异值无害Pitfall 3: tiny singular values are harmless

    求逆要除以 \(\sigma_i\)。\(\sigma_2 = 10^{-6}\) 意味着数据里 \(10^{-6}\) 量级的噪声会被放大成 1 量级的解。常用对策：把小于容差的 \(\sigma\) 当作 0（截断 SVD），或者用阻尼 \(\sigma/(\sigma^2+\lambda^2)\) 代替 \(1/\sigma\)。

    Inverting divides by \(\sigma_i\). \(\sigma_2 = 10^{-6}\) means noise of size \(10^{-6}\) in the data turns into an answer of size 1. Standard remedies: treat any \(\sigma\) below a tolerance as zero (truncated SVD), or use the damped \(\sigma/(\sigma^2+\lambda^2)\) instead of \(1/\sigma\).

    误区 4：U 和 V 是同一组基Pitfall 4: U and V are the same basis

    \(V\) 生活在输入空间，\(U\) 生活在输出空间。对 7 自由度机械臂的 \(6\times7\) 雅可比，\(V\) 是 \(7\times7\)（关节空间方向），\(U\) 是 \(6\times6\)（末端运动方向），连维数都不一样。“机械臂在哪个方向动不了”要看 \(U\) 的最后一列；“哪种关节运动不起作用”要看 \(V\) 的最后几列（零空间）。

    \(V\) lives in the input space and \(U\) in the output space. For the \(6\times7\) Jacobian of a 7-DoF arm, \(V\) is \(7\times7\) (joint-space directions) and \(U\) is \(6\times6\) (end-effector motions); they do not even have the same size. "Which direction can the arm not move?" is the last column of \(U\); "which joint motions do nothing?" are the last columns of \(V\) (the null space).

    9小练习Check yourself

    1. \(A = \begin{bmatrix}3&0\\0&-2\end{bmatrix}\) 的奇异值是？1. What are the singular values of \(A = \begin{bmatrix}3&0\\0&-2\end{bmatrix}\)?
    奇异值是长度，永远非负。负号是一次镜像，归到 \(U\) 里：\(A = \begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}3&0\\0&2\end{bmatrix}I\)。9 和 4 是 \(A^\top A\) 的特征值，即 \(\sigma_i^2\)。Singular values are lengths, so they are never negative. The minus sign is a mirror and goes into \(U\): \(A = \begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}3&0\\0&2\end{bmatrix}I\). 9 and 4 are the eigenvalues of \(A^\top A\), i.e. \(\sigma_i^2\).
    2. 某个姿态下，两连杆机械臂雅可比的奇异值是 \(\sigma_1 = 1.2,\ \sigma_2 = 0.004\)。下面哪句话对？2. At some pose, a two-link arm's Jacobian has singular values \(\sigma_1 = 1.2,\ \sigma_2 = 0.004\). Which statement is true?
    \(J\mathbf v_2 = \sigma_2\mathbf u_2\)，所以 \(\dot{\mathbf q} = \mathbf v_2/\sigma_2\) 才能产生单位速度 \(\mathbf u_2\)，其大小为 \(1/0.004 = 250\)。机械臂已经非常接近奇异位形，\(\kappa = 300\)。\(J\mathbf v_2 = \sigma_2\mathbf u_2\), so producing unit velocity \(\mathbf u_2\) takes \(\dot{\mathbf q} = \mathbf v_2/\sigma_2\), of size \(1/0.004 = 250\). The arm is very close to a singularity, and \(\kappa = 300\).
    3. 对哪一类矩阵，SVD 和特征分解完全是同一回事？3. For which matrices are the SVD and the eigen-decomposition the same thing?
    对称矩阵有正交的特征向量 \(A = Q\Lambda Q^\top\)；如果特征值还都 \(\ge 0\)，这就已经是一个 SVD（\(U = V = Q\)，\(\Sigma = \Lambda\)）。旋转矩阵的奇异值全是 1，特征值却是复数。A symmetric matrix has orthogonal eigenvectors, \(A = Q\Lambda Q^\top\); if its eigenvalues are also \(\ge 0\), that already is an SVD (\(U = V = Q\), \(\Sigma = \Lambda\)). A rotation has all singular values equal to 1 but complex eigenvalues.
    4. 桌面上一片去均值的点云，数据矩阵的奇异值为 \((9.1,\ 6.3,\ 0.05)\)。桌面的法向量是？4. A mean-centered patch of table-top points has data-matrix singular values \((9.1,\ 6.3,\ 0.05)\). What is the table's normal?
    点沿法向几乎没有散布，所以法向是“拉伸最少”的输入方向 \(\mathbf v_3\)。\(\mathbf u_i\) 是 \(N\) 维向量（每个点一个分量），不是三维空间里的方向。The points barely spread along the normal, so it is the least-stretched input direction \(\mathbf v_3\). The \(\mathbf u_i\) are \(N\)-dimensional (one entry per point), not directions in 3D space.
