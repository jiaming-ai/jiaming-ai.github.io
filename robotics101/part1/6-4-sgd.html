随机梯度下降Stochastic Gradient Descent

真实的代价函数常常是几百万条数据误差的平均。每走一步都把所有数据看一遍，太贵了。随机梯度下降 (SGD) 的想法很朴素：只抽一小撮样本（一个小批量）估计梯度，宁可走得歪一点，也要走得快。这个"歪"，也就是噪声，既带来代价（走到最后停不稳），也带来意外的好处（能逃出鞍点）。 Real cost functions are often averages over millions of data errors, and looking at all of the data at every step is far too expensive. The idea of stochastic gradient descent (SGD) is plain: estimate the gradient from a small random handful of samples (a minibatch) and accept a slightly crooked step in exchange for a much faster one. That crookedness, the noise, has a price (you never settle quite still at the end) and an unexpected benefit (it can shake you out of saddle points).

    1为什么机器人需要它Why robots need this

    机器人学习的数据又多又杂：一次遥操作采集就是几十万帧图像加动作，训练一个模仿学习策略的代价函数就是"每一帧上动作预测误差的平均"。如果每次更新参数都要遍历整个数据集，一轮训练可能要几个小时，而参数只更新了一次。

    所以训练几乎都用小批量：每次随机抽几十到几千帧，算这些帧上的平均梯度，更新一次，再抽下一批。同样的思路也出现在机器人在线运行时：标定参数一边采数据一边更新，不必等数据采完；每来一个新测量，就沿着它的梯度挪一小步。

    这一节回答三个问题：为什么抽一小撮样本算出来的梯度仍然"可用"？噪声对收敛有什么影响，学习率该怎么随时间变化？噪声什么时候反而是朋友？

    Robot-learning data is plentiful and messy: one teleoperation session is hundreds of thousands of frames of images and actions, and the cost for training an imitation policy is "the average action-prediction error over every frame". If every parameter update needed a pass over the whole dataset, one epoch could take hours for a single update.

    So training nearly always uses minibatches: draw a few dozen to a few thousand frames at random, compute the average gradient over them, update once, then draw the next batch. The same idea shows up when a robot runs online: calibration parameters are updated while data arrives, with no waiting for the full set; each new measurement nudges the estimate along its own gradient.

    This lesson answers three questions. Why is a gradient computed from a handful of samples still "usable"? What does the noise do to convergence, and how should the learning rate change over time? And when is noise actually a friend?

    2直觉：民意调查Intuition: an opinion poll

    想象要估计全国人口的平均身高。把所有人都量一遍最准，但太贵；随机抽 100 个人，算出来的平均值每次都不同，却围绕真值上下波动，抽得越多，波动越小。梯度也是平均值：\(\nabla f=\frac1N\sum_i\nabla f_i\)，是所有样本梯度的平均。随机抽 \(B\) 个样本再平均，就是一次"民意调查"，得到一个有噪声但没有偏向的估计。

    "没有偏向"是整件事的支点：噪声会让每一步走歪，但平均而言走的方向是对的。用 SGD 下山，就像一个带着不太准的指南针的徒步者：每一步都偏一点，走一百步，大方向没错。

    Imagine estimating the average height of a country. Measuring everyone is most accurate but far too costly; pick 100 people at random and the average differs every time, yet it fluctuates around the truth, less and less as you sample more. The gradient is also an average: \(\nabla f=\frac1N\sum_i\nabla f_i\), the mean of the per-sample gradients. Drawing \(B\) samples and averaging them is one "opinion poll": an estimate that is noisy but unbiased.

    "Unbiased" is the pivot of the whole subject: the noise makes every step crooked, but on average the direction is right. Descending with SGD is like hiking with a slightly unreliable compass: every step points a bit off, and after a hundred steps the overall direction is still correct.

    核心洞见Key insight

    SGD 每一步的成本只有 GD 的 \(B/N\)，却走得几乎一样"对"。早期（离最优点很远）时，梯度信号远大于噪声，小批量足够；晚期（离最优点很近）时，真梯度很小，噪声占了上风，这就是为什么要逐渐减小学习率或增大批量。

    Each SGD step costs only \(B/N\) of a GD step yet points almost as well. Early on (far from the optimum) the gradient signal dwarfs the noise and a small batch is enough; late on (near the optimum) the true gradient is tiny and noise wins, which is why you shrink the learning rate or grow the batch over time.

Plotted in "gradient space", the estimates form a cloud around the true gradient \(\nabla f(\x)\). The center of the cloud is the true gradient (unbiasedness), and the cloud shrinks as \(B\) grows, with standard deviation falling as \(1/\sqrt B\).     5学习率日程：先大后小Learning-rate schedules: big first, then small

    噪声让 SGD 在最小值附近不断被"踢"开。固定学习率 \(\eta\) 时，每一步被踢的幅度 \(\propto\eta\)，而回拉的力来自曲率，两者平衡下来，参数不会收敛到最优点，而是停在一个噪声球里，球的"代价高度"约为 \(\eta\,\mathrm{tr}(C)/(4B)\)（\(C\) 是单样本梯度在最优点的协方差，后面会推导）。想要更低，要么减小 \(\eta\)，要么增大 \(B\)。

    实践中的常规做法是让 \(\eta\) 随时间减小：开头用大学习率快速接近，结尾用小学习率压低噪声球。下面比较五种常见的日程；每条曲线是 24 次独立运行的平均，每次运行都是真实的 SGD。

    Noise keeps kicking SGD away from the minimum. With a constant learning rate \(\eta\) each kick has size \(\propto\eta\), while the pull-back comes from curvature; at balance the parameters do not converge to the optimum but sit in a noise ball whose "cost height" is about \(\eta\,\mathrm{tr}(C)/(4B)\) (\(C\) is the covariance of the per-sample gradient at the optimum, derived below). For lower you either decrease \(\eta\) or increase \(B\).

    The standard practice is to let \(\eta\) shrink over time: a large rate to approach quickly, a small rate at the end to squash the noise ball. Below are five common schedules; each curve is the average of 24 independent runs, every one of them a real SGD run.

    • 让余弦日程的最终误差比常数日程低 3 倍以上（默认设置就够，调一下滑块试试它为何稳健）。Get the cosine schedule's final error at least 3× below the constant schedule's (the defaults may already do it; wiggle the sliders to see how robust it is).
    • 不用任何衰减日程，只靠调 \(\eta_0\) 或 \(B\)，让常数日程的最终误差低于 \(10^{-3}\)。With no decay schedule at all, tune \(\eta_0\) or \(B\) so the constant schedule ends below \(10^{-3}\).
    • 把 \(\eta_0\) 调到最大并取 \(B=1\)：常数日程发散。衰减日程会在学习率降下来之前就被"炸飞"吗？Push \(\eta_0\) to the maximum with \(B=1\): the constant schedule diverges. Do the decaying schedules blow up before the rate comes down?

    6噪声什么时候是朋友：逃出鞍点When noise is a friend: escaping a saddle

    6.3 节的"鞍点"地形 \(f=\tfrac12x^2+\tfrac14y^4-\tfrac12y^2\)：沿 \(x\) 方向是碗，沿 \(y\) 方向，原点处是山脊（\(f_{yy}=-1\)），两侧才是 \((0,\pm1)\) 两个谷底。如果起点恰好在 \(y=0\) 这条线上，梯度的 \(y\) 分量永远是 0，GD 会老老实实滑进鞍点 \((0,0)\) 并停在那里。

    现在给每个样本加一个零均值的扰动 \(f_i(\x)=f(\x)+\boldsymbol\xi_i^\top\x\)（\(\sum_i\boldsymbol\xi_i=\mathbf 0\)，所以全批量梯度还是 \(\nabla f\)）。小批量梯度是 \(\nabla f(\x)+\bar{\boldsymbol\xi}_B\)：噪声自带 \(y\) 方向的分量，把 24 个小球从 \(y=0\) 线上踢开，山脊的负曲率再把它们推向两个谷之一。

    Take the "saddle" terrain of Lesson 6.3, \(f=\tfrac12x^2+\tfrac14y^4-\tfrac12y^2\): along \(x\) it is a bowl, along \(y\) the origin is a ridge (\(f_{yy}=-1\)), with the two valley floors at \((0,\pm1)\) on either side. If the start lies exactly on the line \(y=0\), the \(y\)-component of the gradient is always 0, and GD obediently slides into the saddle \((0,0)\) and stays there.

    Now give each sample a zero-mean perturbation \(f_i(\x)=f(\x)+\boldsymbol\xi_i^\top\x\) (with \(\sum_i\boldsymbol\xi_i=\mathbf 0\), so the full-batch gradient is still \(\nabla f\)). The minibatch gradient is \(\nabla f(\x)+\bar{\boldsymbol\xi}_B\): the noise carries a \(y\)-component, kicks the 24 balls off the line \(y=0\), and the ridge's negative curvature pushes each toward one of the two valleys.

    • 选 "GD"，运行：所有小球都停在鞍点。（梯度为零 ≠ 最小值，6.1 节。）Choose "GD" and run: every ball ends on the saddle. (Zero gradient ≠ minimum, Lesson 6.1.)
    • 选 "SGD"，运行：大部分小球逃出鞍点，分别落入上下两个谷底。Choose "SGD" and run: most balls escape and fall into the upper or lower valley.
    • 逃出之后，小球仍在谷底附近"抖动"。把 \(B\) 调大或 \(\sigma\) 调小，抖动的幅度怎么变？After escaping, the balls still jitter around the valley floors. How does the jitter change as you raise \(B\) or lower \(\sigma\)?

    有限和与小批量梯度Finite sums and the minibatch gradient

    机器学习与标定里的代价几乎总是样本平均：

    Costs in machine learning and calibration are almost always sample averages:

    $$ f(\x)=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^{N}f_i(\x),\qquad \hl{full}{\cx{\nabla f(\x)}}=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^{N}\nabla f_i(\x),\qquad \hl{mb}{\cv{\mathbf g_B(\x)}}=\frac1B\sum_{i\in\mathcal B}\nabla f_i(\x) $$

    其中 \(\mathcal B\) 是随机抽出的 \(B\) 个下标。若每个下标被抽到的概率相同，则 \(\mathbf g_B\) 是 \(\nabla f\) 的无偏估计；协方差为

    where \(\mathcal B\) is a random set of \(B\) indices. If every index is equally likely, \(\mathbf g_B\) is an unbiased estimator of \(\nabla f\), with covariance

    $$ \mathbb E[\mathbf g_B]=\nabla f(\x),\qquad \hl{cov}{\mathrm{Cov}[\mathbf g_B]}=\frac{\Sigma(\x)}{B}\cdot\frac{N-B}{N-1}\ \ \Big(\text{with replacement: }\frac{\Sigma(\x)}{B}\Big),\qquad \Sigma(\x)=\frac1N\sum_i\big(\nabla f_i-\nabla f\big)\big(\nabla f_i-\nabla f\big)^{\!\top} $$

    \(\Sigma\) 是单样本梯度的协方差。因子 \(1/B\) 就是 FIG 2 里点云随 \(B\) 缩小的原因；\((N-B)/(N-1)\) 是"不放回"抽样的修正，\(B=N\) 时为 0（没有随机性）。

    \(\Sigma\) is the covariance of the per-sample gradient. The factor \(1/B\) is why the cloud of FIG 2 shrinks with \(B\); \((N-B)/(N-1)\) is the correction for sampling without replacement and vanishes at \(B=N\) (no randomness left).

    SGD 的更新与收敛条件The SGD update and convergence conditions

    $$ \x_{k+1}=\x_k-\eta_k\,\cv{\mathbf g_{B}(\x_k)} $$

    要让 SGD 收敛到最优点（凸问题），Robbins–Monro 条件要求：\(\sum_k\eta_k=\infty\)（走得动：总路程够长）且 \(\sum_k\eta_k^2<\infty\)（停得住：噪声的累积方差有限）。\(\eta_k=\eta_0/(1+k/\tau)\) 这类 \(1/k\) 衰减满足条件，常数 \(\eta\) 不满足。

    For SGD to converge to the optimum (in the convex case), the Robbins–Monro conditions require \(\sum_k\eta_k=\infty\) (you can get there: the total path is long enough) and \(\sum_k\eta_k^2<\infty\) (you can stop: the accumulated noise variance is finite). A \(1/k\) decay such as \(\eta_k=\eta_0/(1+k/\tau)\) satisfies them; a constant \(\eta\) does not.

    噪声球的高度（二次函数上的推导）The height of the noise ball (derivation on a quadratic)

    在最优点附近 \(f\approx f^\star+\tfrac12\mathbf e^\top H\mathbf e\)，\(\mathbf e=\x-\x^\star\)。一步更新为 \(\mathbf e_{k+1}=(I-\eta H)\mathbf e_k-\eta\boldsymbol\zeta_k\)，其中噪声 \(\boldsymbol\zeta_k\) 均值为 0、协方差 \(C_B=C/B\)（\(C\) 是 \(\Sigma\) 在最优点处的值）。取稳态协方差 \(P=\mathbb E[\mathbf e\mathbf e^\top]\)，令 \(P=(I-\eta H)P(I-\eta H)+\eta^2C_B\)，展开并略去 \(\eta^2\) 项：

    Near the optimum \(f\approx f^\star+\tfrac12\mathbf e^\top H\mathbf e\) with \(\mathbf e=\x-\x^\star\). One update reads \(\mathbf e_{k+1}=(I-\eta H)\mathbf e_k-\eta\boldsymbol\zeta_k\), where the noise \(\boldsymbol\zeta_k\) has mean 0 and covariance \(C_B=C/B\) (\(C\) is \(\Sigma\) at the optimum). In the stationary state \(P=\mathbb E[\mathbf e\mathbf e^\top]\) satisfies \(P=(I-\eta H)P(I-\eta H)+\eta^2C_B\); expanding and dropping the \(\eta^2\) terms on the left:

    $$ HP+PH\approx\eta\,C_B\ \ \Longrightarrow\ \ \mathbb E\big[f-f^\star\big]=\tfrac12\mathrm{tr}(HP)\approx\frac{\eta}{4}\,\mathrm{tr}(C_B)=\hl{floor}{\frac{\eta\,\mathrm{tr}(C)}{4B}} $$

    取迹时用到 \(\mathrm{tr}(HP+PH)=2\,\mathrm{tr}(HP)\)。结论非常干净：噪声球的高度与 \(\eta\) 成正比，与 \(B\) 成反比。把 \(\eta\) 和 \(B\) 同时乘以 2 的话，球的高度不变，这就是深度学习里"批量加倍，学习率也加倍"的线性缩放经验（小批量范围内）的来源。FIG 3 的虚线正是这个公式。

    Taking the trace uses \(\mathrm{tr}(HP+PH)=2\,\mathrm{tr}(HP)\). The conclusion is clean: the noise ball's height is proportional to \(\eta\) and inversely proportional to \(B\). Doubling \(\eta\) and \(B\) together leaves it unchanged, the origin of the "double the batch, double the learning rate" rule of thumb in deep learning (in the small-batch regime). The dashed line in FIG 3 is exactly this formula.

    动量与 Adam 放在 SGD 里Momentum and Adam in the SGD setting

    6.3 节的动量、RMSProp、Adam，在实际训练里都是用小批量梯度代替 \(\nabla f\) 来运行的。动量对连续多步的 \(\mathbf g_B\) 取指数平均，等于把噪声平均掉了一部分，使有效的噪声球高度大致不变而有效学习率变为 \(\eta/(1-\beta)\)；Adam 的二阶矩 \(\mathbf s\) 里含有噪声的方差，因此在梯度很小、几乎全是噪声的坐标上它会自动把步子变小。这些在 Part V 的"损失、泛化与正则化"和"反向传播"章节里会反复用到；这里只需记住：噪声来自小批量，与用哪种优化器无关。

    The momentum, RMSProp and Adam of Lesson 6.3 are, in real training, all run on minibatch gradients instead of \(\nabla f\). Momentum takes an exponential average of consecutive \(\mathbf g_B\), averaging out part of the noise; Adam's second moment \(\mathbf s\) contains the noise variance, so on coordinates where the gradient is tiny and mostly noise it automatically shortens the step. These come back repeatedly in Part V ("Losses, generalization & regularization" and "Backpropagation"); for now remember: the noise comes from the minibatch, whichever optimizer you use.

    真实系统里In real systems

    对机器人学习，SGD 是日常：行为克隆、扩散策略、VLA 的训练都是在几十万到上亿条 (观测, 动作) 样本上用 AdamW + 小批量 + 余弦或线性衰减的学习率日程，前面还要加一小段预热（FIG 3 的最后一条曲线，Adam 的二阶矩刚启动时估计不准）。在线标定、在线系统辨识则是"流式"的：每个新测量只做一次小更新，不保存历史。标定的另一个现实问题就是 FIG 5 里的激励不足：姿态太单一时，\(H\) 的条件数很大，不论 GD 还是 SGD 都慢，要靠主动设计采集姿态来解决。

    For robot learning SGD is the daily bread: behaviour cloning, diffusion policies and VLAs are trained on hundreds of thousands to hundreds of millions of (observation, action) samples with AdamW, minibatches and a cosine or linear decaying learning-rate schedule, preceded by a short warm-up (the last curve of FIG 3; Adam's second-moment estimate is unreliable right after it starts). Online calibration and system identification are "streaming": every new measurement makes one small update and no history is stored. Another practical problem of calibration is the poor excitation of FIG 5: when postures are too uniform the condition number of \(H\) is large and both GD and SGD crawl; the remedy is to design the data-collection postures actively.

    9常见误区Common pitfalls

    误区 1：用训练过程中的损失当成"真实损失"Pitfall 1: treating the running training loss as the true loss

    训练日志里的损失是各个小批量上的损失，本身带噪声。曲线上下跳动不代表出了问题，也不代表过拟合；要比较两次运行，需要看平滑后的曲线或固定验证集上的损失，并多跑几个随机种子（FIG 3 就是 24 次平均）。

    The loss in a training log is measured on individual minibatches and is itself noisy. A jumpy curve does not mean something broke, nor that you are overfitting; to compare two runs look at smoothed curves or the loss on a fixed validation set, and run several seeds (FIG 3 averages 24).

    误区 2：把批量开到最大就一定最好Pitfall 2: the largest batch is always the best

    批量越大，每步噪声越小，但每步成本也线性增加。过了某个"临界批量"之后，再增大 \(B\) 只是浪费算力：梯度已经足够准，瓶颈在步数。另外，极大的批量常常泛化更差（噪声的正则化作用变弱），需要重新调学习率和日程。

    A larger batch lowers the per-step noise, but the per-step cost grows linearly. Beyond some "critical batch size" a bigger \(B\) just wastes compute: the gradient is accurate enough and the bottleneck is the number of steps. Very large batches also often generalize worse (the noise's regularizing effect weakens) and need the learning rate and schedule retuned.

    误区 3：忘了打乱数据Pitfall 3: forgetting to shuffle the data

    "无偏"要求小批量是总体的随机子集。机器人数据往往按时间顺序存放，相邻帧高度相关；若按顺序取，一个批量里全是同一个场景，梯度严重有偏，训练会在不同场景之间来回摆。务必在每个 epoch 前打乱，或在多个 episode 之间随机混合采样。

    "Unbiased" requires the minibatch to be a random subset of the population. Robot data is usually stored in time order with highly correlated neighbouring frames; take it in order and one batch contains a single scene, the gradient is badly biased and training swings between scenes. Shuffle before every epoch, or sample at random across episodes.

    误区 4：噪声总是坏事，或噪声总是好事Pitfall 4: noise is always bad, or noise is always good

    噪声既让你停不稳（FIG 1、3），又能把你从鞍点和尖锐的谷里踢出去（FIG 4）。这是一个有时间尺度的权衡：训练早期，噪声帮你探索；晚期，要把它压下去才能收敛。因此"先大后小"的日程是几乎所有系统的标配，而不是一种技巧。

    Noise stops you from settling (FIG 1, 3) and also kicks you out of saddles and sharp valleys (FIG 4). It is a trade-off with a time scale: early in training the noise helps you explore; late in training it must be suppressed for convergence. That is why "large first, small later" schedules are standard equipment in nearly every system rather than a trick.

    10小练习Check yourself

    1. 随机抽取（每个样本等概率）的小批量梯度 \(\mathbf g_B\) 的期望是什么？1. What is the expectation of a uniformly sampled minibatch gradient \(\mathbf g_B\)?
    每个样本梯度被抽到的概率相同，所以 \(\mathbb E[\mathbf g_B]=\frac1B\sum\mathbb E[\nabla f_i]=\nabla f\)，与 \(B\) 无关：这就是"无偏"。\(B\) 只影响方差（\(\propto1/B\)），不影响期望。Every per-sample gradient is equally likely, so \(\mathbb E[\mathbf g_B]=\frac1B\sum\mathbb E[\nabla f_i]=\nabla f\), independent of \(B\): that is unbiasedness. \(B\) affects only the variance (\(\propto1/B\)), not the mean.
    2. 把批量从 \(B=16\) 增大到 \(B=64\)（有放回抽样），小批量梯度的标准差大约变为原来的多少？2. Increase the batch size from \(B=16\) to \(B=64\) (sampling with replacement). By what factor does the standard deviation of the minibatch gradient change?
    方差 \(\propto1/B\)，标准差 \(\propto1/\sqrt B\)：\(B\) 变成 4 倍，标准差变成 \(1/\sqrt4=1/2\)。想把噪声再减半，要再把批量乘 4：回报是递减的，这也是误区 2 的来源。The variance is \(\propto1/B\), so the standard deviation is \(\propto1/\sqrt B\): quadruple \(B\) and it becomes \(1/\sqrt4=1/2\). Halving the noise again costs another factor of 4 in batch size: diminishing returns, hence Pitfall 2.
    3. 用常数学习率 \(\eta\)、固定批量 \(B\) 的 SGD 优化一个凸二次函数，足够多步之后会发生什么？3. You run SGD with constant learning rate \(\eta\) and fixed batch \(B\) on a convex quadratic. After many steps, what happens?
    步长足够小就不会发散（噪声有界）；但每步仍被随机踢开，稳态时的代价高度正比于 \(\eta\)、反比于 \(B\)（FIG 3 的虚线）。凸性只保证"没有坏的局部最小"，并不能消除小批量噪声。要收敛到精确最优点，需要 \(\eta_k\to0\)。A small enough step does not diverge (the noise is bounded), but every step is still randomly kicked, and the stationary cost height is proportional to \(\eta\) and inversely proportional to \(B\) (the dashed line of FIG 3). Convexity only rules out bad local minima; it does not remove minibatch noise. Converging to the exact optimum requires \(\eta_k\to0\).
    4. 以下学习率序列中，哪一个满足 Robbins–Monro 条件 \(\sum\eta_k=\infty,\ \sum\eta_k^2<\infty\)？4. Which learning-rate sequence satisfies the Robbins–Monro conditions \(\sum\eta_k=\infty,\ \sum\eta_k^2<\infty\)?
    \(\sum1/k\) 发散（调和级数），\(\sum1/k^2=\pi^2/6\) 收敛，满足两个条件。常数 \(\eta\) 的平方和发散；\(1/k^2\) 与指数衰减的和本身就收敛，意味着总路程有限，可能还没走到最优点就"冻住"了。\(\sum1/k\) diverges (harmonic series) and \(\sum1/k^2=\pi^2/6\) converges, so both conditions hold. A constant \(\eta\) has divergent \(\sum\eta^2\); for \(1/k^2\) and exponential decay even \(\sum\eta_k\) converges, so the total path length is finite and the iterate may "freeze" before reaching the optimum.
    5. FIG 5 里，为什么"窄姿态范围"的标定比"多样姿态"慢得多？5. In FIG 5, why is calibration with "narrow poses" much slower than with "diverse poses"?
    两种设置的数据条数、噪声大小相同，代价也都是凸二次。区别在 \(H=\frac1N\sum\begin{bmatrix}1&\cos q_2\\\cos q_2&1\end{bmatrix}\)：\(q_2\) 变化范围小时 \(\cos q_2\approx0.9\)，特征值约 1.9 和 0.09，\(\kappa\approx22\)；\(q_2\) 变化大时 \(\cos q_2\) 的平均值接近 0，特征值都接近 1。物理上：手臂总是几乎伸直的话，数据很难区分 \(l_1\) 和 \(l_2\) 各自是多少，只能确定它们的和。Both settings have the same number of points and noise, and both costs are convex quadratics. The difference is in \(H=\frac1N\sum\begin{bmatrix}1&\cos q_2\\\cos q_2&1\end{bmatrix}\): with a small range of \(q_2\), \(\cos q_2\approx0.9\), the eigenvalues are about 1.9 and 0.09 and \(\kappa\approx22\); with a wide range the mean of \(\cos q_2\) is near 0 and both eigenvalues are near 1. Physically: if the arm is always nearly straight, the data can hardly tell \(l_1\) and \(l_2\) apart and mostly pin down only their sum.
