地图表示Map Representations

SLAM 告诉机器人自己在哪里，可地图里到底该存什么？没有一种地图能回答所有问题：定位要的是认得出的特征，规划要的是能不能过、离障碍多远，任务要的是“杯子在厨房”。本节从激光雷达建占据栅格讲起（反向传感器模型、最大量程、位姿漂移造成的双墙），再到导航栈真正使用的代价地图与膨胀、距离场、拓扑地图、路标与语义地图，最后处理会变化的世界：地图选什么，取决于读它的规划器和估计器。 SLAM tells the robot where it is, but what should the map actually store? No single map answers every question: localization wants recognizable features, planning wants "can I fit, and how far from the obstacle", tasks want "the mug is in the kitchen". This lesson starts from occupancy-grid mapping with a lidar (the inverse sensor model, max-range readings, the double walls caused by pose drift), moves on to what navigation stacks actually use (costmaps and inflation, distance fields, topological maps, landmark and semantic maps), and ends with a world that changes: the right map is decided by the planner and the estimator that read it.

    1为什么机器人需要它Why robots need this

    15.2 节的 SLAM 解决了“我在哪里”，并顺手留下一份地图。但“地图”这个词掩盖了一个设计决定：要存什么。同一栋楼，扫地机器人存一张 5 cm 分辨率的占据栅格，自动驾驶车存车道线和交通灯的位置，视觉 SLAM 存几十万个带描述子的三维点，而一个听得懂“去厨房拿杯子”的机器人还要知道哪片区域叫厨房、杯子在哪个柜子上。

    原因是地图有不同的读者，每个读者问不同的问题。定位问“从这里看出去，传感器会看到什么”，它需要独特、可重复识别的东西（角点、柱子、特征点）。运动规划问“机器人的身体能不能放在这里、离障碍多远”，它需要空闲空间与净空（14.1 节的 A* 在栅格上搜索，14.3 节的 CHOMP 要距离场的梯度）。任务规划问“东西在哪、房间怎么连”，它需要物体和地点。没有一种表示能把三个问题都答得又快又省，所以真实系统总是同时维护几种地图，各自为一个读者服务。

    12.2 节已经介绍了体素、OctoMap、TSDF，以及对数几率形式的占据栅格（那里的 FIG 2），本节不再重复，而是往前走：激光雷达的反向传感器模型怎样定、最大量程读数怎么办、位姿不准时地图会变成什么样；导航栈在占据栅格之上又搭了什么（代价地图、距离场、拓扑图）；稀疏的路标地图与语义地图各自擅长什么；以及世界在变的时候，地图怎样“改口”和“遗忘”。

    The SLAM of Lesson 15.2 answers "where am I" and leaves a map behind as a by-product. But the word "map" hides a design decision: what to store. For the same building, a vacuum robot keeps a 5 cm occupancy grid, a self-driving car keeps the positions of lane lines and traffic lights, a visual SLAM system keeps hundreds of thousands of 3D points with descriptors, and a robot that understands "fetch the mug from the kitchen" must also know which area is called the kitchen and which cupboard holds the mug.

    The reason is that a map has different readers, and each asks a different question. Localization asks "what will my sensor see from here?" and needs distinctive, repeatably recognizable things (corners, poles, feature points). Motion planning asks "can the robot's body be here, and how far is the nearest obstacle?" and needs free space and clearance (the A* of Lesson 14.1 searches a grid, the CHOMP of Lesson 14.3 needs the gradient of a distance field). Task planning asks "where are things, and how are the rooms connected?" and needs objects and places. No single representation answers all three quickly and cheaply, so real systems always keep several maps, each serving one reader.

    Lesson 12.2 already introduced voxels, OctoMap, TSDFs and the occupancy grid in log-odds form (its FIG 2). This lesson does not repeat that; it goes further: how to set a lidar's inverse sensor model, what to do with max-range readings, and what the map turns into when the poses are wrong; what navigation stacks build on top of the occupancy grid (costmaps, distance fields, topological graphs); what sparse landmark maps and semantic maps are good at; and how a map "changes its mind" and "forgets" when the world moves.

    地图是给读者准备好的答案缓存。占据栅格答“是否被占”，代价地图答“对我这台机器人有多危险”，距离场答“离障碍多远、往哪躲”，拓扑图答“经过哪些房间和门”，路标答“从这里能认出什么”，语义地图答“这是什么”。上面那个任务里两张地图都没错：它们回答的是不同的问题。选地图，就是先问清楚谁来读它、问什么、要多快。

    3动手：用激光雷达建一张占据栅格Play: build an occupancy grid with a lidar

    12.2 节已经说明了占据栅格的核心：每格存“被占据”的对数几率 \(\ell\)，每次测量往上加一个数（4.2 节的“证据相加”）。真正动手时，你得决定两件那里没细讲的事。第一是反向传感器模型（inverse sensor model）：一束测得距离 \(z\) 的激光，对它经过的每个格子到底意味着什么？最常用的写法（Thrun 等《Probabilistic Robotics》第 9 章）只有一个参数：障碍的“厚度” \(\hl{alpha}{\alpha}\)。格心到传感器的距离 \(r\) 小于 \(z-\alpha/2\) 的格子加 \(\hl{lfree}{\ell_{\rm free}}\)（激光穿过了它，多半是空的）；\(|r-z|\le\alpha/2\) 的格子加 \(\hl{locc}{\ell_{\rm occ}}\)（激光停在这里）；更远的格子什么也不加（激光没到过那里）。第二是没有回波的射线：可能前方 5 m 内真的什么都没有，也可能打在了深色或透明的表面上没反射回来。你可以“忽略它”，也可以“把整条射线到最大量程都当作空闲”。

    还有一件事是地图之外的：画地图用的是估计的位姿。下面的机器人沿一条 57 m 的环线走过三个房间，每 0.2 s 扫描一次（360°、180 束、量程 5 m）。“里程计航向漂移”给估计的航向每走一米加一点偏差，地图就按这个有偏差的位姿去画，正如没有回环校正的里程计（15.1、15.2 节）。

    Lesson 12.2 explained the core of an occupancy grid: each cell stores the log-odds \(\ell\) of "occupied", and every measurement adds a number to it (the "evidence adds up" of Lesson 4.2). When you actually build one, you must decide two things not covered there. The first is the inverse sensor model: what does a beam that measured range \(z\) say about each cell it passes? The most common recipe (Thrun et al., Probabilistic Robotics, Ch. 9) has a single parameter, the obstacle "thickness" \(\hl{alpha}{\alpha}\). A cell whose centre is at distance \(r\) from the sensor gets \(\hl{lfree}{\ell_{\rm free}}\) if \(r<z-\alpha/2\) (the beam went through it, so it is probably empty), \(\hl{locc}{\ell_{\rm occ}}\) if \(|r-z|\le\alpha/2\) (the beam stopped here), and nothing if it is farther (the beam never got there). The second is the beam with no return: maybe nothing lies within 5 m, or maybe the beam hit a dark or transparent surface that sent nothing back. You can "ignore it", or "treat the whole beam up to the maximum range as free".

    One more thing lives outside the map: it is drawn with the estimated pose. The robot below drives a 57 m loop through all three rooms and scans every 0.2 s (360°, 180 beams, 5 m range). "Odometry heading drift" adds a small heading bias for every metre travelled, and the map is drawn with that biased pose, just like odometry without loop closure (Lessons 15.1 and 15.2).

    4给规划器的地图：代价地图、距离场与拓扑Maps for planners: costmaps, distance fields and topology

    代价地图与膨胀Costmaps and inflation

    占据栅格说的是“这一格里有没有东西”，可规划器关心的是“我的机器人放在这一格中心会不会撞”。对一个半径为 \(\rho\) 的圆形机器人，答案是：只要离最近障碍的距离小于 \(\rho\) 就会撞。这正是 7.2 节的构型空间障碍：把障碍向外“膨胀” \(\rho\)，机器人就能当成一个点来规划。ROS 的 costmap_2d（以及 ROS 2 的 Nav2）把这件事做成了一个分层结构（Lu 等 2014 的 layered costmap）：静态层来自 SLAM 建好的地图，障碍层来自实时传感器（这里是一辆可以拖动的推车），膨胀层按到最近障碍的距离 \(d\) 给每格一个 0 到 254 的代价，各层逐格取最大。代价的形状只有三个参数：内切半径 \(\hl{rins}{r_{\rm ins}}\)（以内一律 253，“必撞”）、膨胀半径 \(r_{\rm inf}\)（以外为 0）、以及中间指数衰减的速度 \(\hl{kscale}{k}\)。

    规划器（这里是 14.1 节的 A*，8 连通）把代价 ≥ 253 的格子当成墙，其他代价为 \(c\) 的格子步长乘以 \(1+3c/252\)：离墙近的格子能走，但贵。所以 \(r_{\rm ins}\) 决定“能不能过”，\(r_{\rm inf}\) 和 \(k\) 决定“愿意离墙多远”。

    An occupancy grid says "is there something in this cell", but the planner cares whether the robot, placed with its centre on this cell, would collide. For a circular robot of radius \(\rho\) the answer is: it collides whenever the nearest obstacle is closer than \(\rho\). This is exactly the configuration-space obstacle of Lesson 7.2: "inflate" the obstacles by \(\rho\) and the robot can be planned as a point. ROS's costmap_2d (and Nav2 in ROS 2) turned this into a layered structure (the layered costmap of Lu et al. 2014): a static layer from the map SLAM built, an obstacle layer from live sensors (here a cart you can drag), and an inflation layer that gives each cell a cost from 0 to 254 according to its distance \(d\) to the nearest obstacle; the layers are combined cell by cell with a maximum. The shape of the cost has three parameters: the inscribed radius \(\hl{rins}{r_{\rm ins}}\) (everything within it is 253, "certain collision"), the inflation radius \(r_{\rm inf}\) (cost 0 beyond it), and the speed \(\hl{kscale}{k}\) of the exponential decay in between.

    The planner (the A* of Lesson 14.1, 8-connected) treats cost ≥ 253 as a wall and multiplies the step length of any other cell of cost \(c\) by \(1+3c/252\): cells near walls are allowed but expensive. So \(r_{\rm ins}\) decides "can we get through at all", while \(r_{\rm inf}\) and \(k\) decide "how far from the walls do we want to stay".

    距离场：怎样从栅格算出来Distance fields: how to compute them from a grid

    膨胀层的核心是“每格到最近障碍格的距离”，也就是 距离变换。12.2 节讲了 SDF 是什么、它的梯度为什么能把轨迹推离障碍（CHOMP、TrajOpt 正是这样用它的，见 14.3 节）；这里关心的是从一张栅格怎样算出它。最直接的办法叫“野火”（brushfire）：从所有障碍格同时出发做一次 Dijkstra（14.1 节），像火从障碍向外烧。问题在于步长：只走上下左右（4 连通），量出来的是曼哈顿距离；8 个方向都算 1 步，量出来的是切比雪夫距离；斜步算 \(\sqrt2\) 的“倒角”（chamfer）距离好得多，但仍不精确。精确的欧氏距离变换（Felzenszwalb 与 Huttenlocher）则把二维问题拆成“先每列、再每行”的一维问题，每个一维问题用抛物线下包络在线性时间里解完。

    下图左边是一个小栅格（每格看作 10 cm），点击格子可以添加或删除障碍。选一种算法，看等距带（交替的青色条纹）的形状：一个孤立障碍点周围，曼哈顿是菱形，切比雪夫是正方形，倒角是八边形，只有精确 EDT 是圆。

    The heart of the inflation layer is "each cell's distance to the nearest obstacle cell": the distance transform. Lesson 12.2 covered what an SDF is and why its gradient pushes a trajectory away from obstacles (exactly how CHOMP and TrajOpt use it, Lesson 14.3); here the question is how to compute it from a grid. The most direct method is "brushfire": one Dijkstra run (Lesson 14.1) started from all obstacle cells at once, like a fire spreading outward. The catch is the step cost: with only up/down/left/right moves (4-connected) you measure the Manhattan distance; with all 8 moves counted as 1 step you get the Chebyshev distance; counting diagonal steps as \(\sqrt2\) gives the much better "chamfer" distance, but still not the true one. The exact Euclidean distance transform (Felzenszwalb and Huttenlocher) splits the 2D problem into 1D problems, "every column, then every row", and solves each in linear time with the lower envelope of parabolas.

Pick an algorithm and watch the shape of the equal-distance bands (alternating cyan stripes): around an isolated obstacle point, Manhattan gives a diamond, Chebyshev a square, chamfer an octagon, and only the exact EDT a circle.

    拓扑地图：从骨架到“房间与门”Topological maps: from skeleton to "rooms and doors"

    距离场还藏着一个结构：它的“山脊”，即到两个（或更多）障碍等距的点，连成一张网，叫广义 Voronoi 图（GVD，12.2 节称之为中轴）。沿着它走的机器人离两侧障碍一样远，是最安全的路线；它把二维的空闲空间压缩成一维的骨架。Thrun（1998）进一步观察到：骨架上净空出现局部极小的地方，就是门、走廊口这样的“瓶颈”。把每个这样的关键点（\(\hl{crit}{\mathbf c}\)）和它两侧最近的障碍点（“基点”）连起来，就是横穿瓶颈的关键线；沿关键线把空闲空间切开，剩下的每一块就是一个“地点”（房间）。地点做节点、关键线做连接它们的边，就得到一张拓扑地图：图很小（这里 3 个地点、2 扇门），规划在上面几乎不花时间，而且“经过办公室去厨房”这样的结果能直接说给人听。

    下图把这条流水线完整跑了一遍（按 Thrun 的思路做了简化，细节见第 5 节）：距离场 → 骨架 → 净空小于 0.7 m 的窄段里的局部极小 → 关键线 → 区域 → 图。拖动客厅里的桌子、改变客厅门的宽度，看拓扑怎样变。

    The distance field hides one more structure: its "ridges", the points equidistant from two (or more) obstacles, form a network called the generalized Voronoi diagram (GVD; Lesson 12.2 called it the medial axis). A robot that follows it stays equally far from the obstacles on both sides, the safest route, and it compresses 2D free space into a 1D skeleton. Thrun (1998) went one step further: where the clearance along the skeleton has a local minimum, there is a "bottleneck" such as a door or a corridor entrance. Connecting every such critical point (\(\hl{crit}{\mathbf c}\)) with its two nearest obstacle points (its "basis points") gives a critical line across the bottleneck; cut free space along these lines, each remaining piece is a "place" (a room). Places as nodes and critical lines as the edges between them make a topological map: the graph is tiny (here 3 places and 2 doors), planning on it costs almost nothing, and a result such as "through the office to the kitchen" can be told directly to a person.

    稀疏地图与语义地图Sparse maps and semantic maps

    到目前为止的地图都是稠密的：每一格都有值，不管那里有没有东西。另一类地图只存少数有用的东西。路标地图（landmark / feature map）只存可重复识别的点：EKF-SLAM 与 FastSLAM 估计的就是路标的坐标（15.2 节），视觉 SLAM（如 ORB-SLAM）存的是三维地图点，每个点带一个 32 字节的 ORB 描述子。它极其省内存、天生适合定位（用 11.4 节的 PnP 或 12.1 节的配准），但它不知道两个路标之间是不是空的，所以不能直接拿来规划。语义地图再往上一层，存的是“东西”和“地方”：物体的类别、位置和大小，房间的名字和连通关系。三维场景图（3D scene graph）把它们组织成层级：建筑 → 楼层 → 房间 → 地点 → 物体，可以回答“厨房里有没有杯子”这样的问题，也是语言驱动的机器人（Part VII）读取世界的接口。

    All the maps so far are dense: every cell has a value, whether or not anything is there. Another family stores only a few useful things. A landmark (feature) map stores only repeatably recognizable points: EKF-SLAM and FastSLAM estimate exactly the coordinates of landmarks (Lesson 15.2), and visual SLAM (ORB-SLAM, for instance) stores 3D map points, each with a 32-byte ORB descriptor. It is extremely compact and naturally suited to localization (with the PnP of Lesson 11.4 or the registration of Lesson 12.1), but it does not know whether the space between two landmarks is empty, so it cannot be used for planning directly. A semantic map goes one level higher and stores "things" and "places": object classes, positions and sizes, room names and how rooms connect. A 3D scene graph organizes them into layers, building → floor → room → place → object, which answers questions like "is there a mug in the kitchen" and is the interface through which language-driven robots (Part VII) read the world.

    反向传感器模型与对数几率The inverse sensor model in log-odds

    12.2 节推导过：在各格独立、测量条件独立的假设下，格子 \(i\) 的对数几率按 \(\ell_{t,i}=\ell_{t-1,i}+\operatorname{logit}p(m_i\mid z_t,x_t)-\ell_0\) 更新（\(\ell_0=0\)，即先验 \(p=0.5\)）。中间那一项 \(p(m_i\mid z_t,x_t)\) 叫反向传感器模型，因为它把因果倒过来写：传感器模型本来是“世界 → 测量”的 \(p(z\mid m,x)\)，这里要的却是“测量 → 这一格”。FIG 2 用的是最简单的分段形式（\(r_i\) 是格 \(i\) 的格心到传感器的距离，\(z\) 是这一束的读数；没有回波的射线在“忽略”策略下对所有格子都是 0）：

    Lesson 12.2 derived that, if cells are independent and measurements conditionally independent, the log-odds of cell \(i\) updates as \(\ell_{t,i}=\ell_{t-1,i}+\operatorname{logit}p(m_i\mid z_t,x_t)-\ell_0\) (with \(\ell_0=0\), the prior \(p=0.5\)). The middle term \(p(m_i\mid z_t,x_t)\) is called the inverse sensor model because it runs causality backwards: a sensor model is naturally "world → measurement", \(p(z\mid m,x)\), but here we need "measurement → this cell". FIG 2 uses the simplest piecewise form (\(r_i\) is the distance from the sensor to the centre of cell \(i\), \(z\) the beam's reading; under the "ignore" policy a beam with no return adds 0 to every cell):

    $$ \Delta\ell_i=\begin{cases}\hl{lfree}{\ell_{\rm free}}=\ln\dfrac{0.4}{0.6}=-0.405, & r_i<z-\hl{alpha}{\alpha}/2\\[6pt] \hl{locc}{\ell_{\rm occ}}=\ln\dfrac{0.7}{0.3}=+0.847, & |r_i-z|\le\hl{alpha}{\alpha}/2\\[6pt] 0, & r_i>z+\hl{alpha}{\alpha}/2\end{cases} $$

    \(\alpha\) 怎么选。占据带的宽度 \(\alpha\) 要盖住测距噪声（几个 \(\sigma\)）和栅格的离散化（终点所在格的格心离真实终点最远可达半个对角线 \(h/\sqrt2\)），所以 \(\alpha\) 通常取一到两个格宽。取得太大，表面前方（朝传感器一侧）\(\alpha/2\) 内本来空闲的格子每次都被加 \(\ell_{\rm occ}\)，墙就朝机器人“长胖”：这是 FIG 2 里 \(\alpha=0.4\) m 时约 400 个误占据格的来源。表面后方没有这个问题，因为射线停在表面上，不会再往后加。

    独立性假设的代价。每一束都被当成对每一格的独立证据，但同一次扫描里相邻的几束其实经过同一批格子、受同一个位姿误差影响；把它们当独立证据相加会让地图过度自信（4.2 节的“同一票数了两遍”）。Thrun（2003）用前向模型 \(p(z\mid m,x)\) 对整张地图做优化，能消除这种矛盾，但代价高得多；实际系统用钳位 \(\ell\in[\ell_{\min},\ell_{\max}]\) 来给自信设上限。

    位姿误差变成地图误差。一个航向误差 \(\delta\theta\) 会把距离 \(r\) 处的终点横向移动约 \(r\,\delta\theta\)。FIG 2 的漂移是每米 \(\kappa\)，走完长 \(L\) 的环线后航向误差为 \(\kappa L\)：\(\kappa=0.05\) °/m 时 \(57\times0.05\approx2.9^\circ\)，即 0.050 弧度，5 m 外的墙就偏了 \(5\times0.050\approx0.25\) m，两格半。同一面墙被两个不同的位姿各画一遍，就是“双墙”。这就是建图离不开 15.2 节的回环与位姿图：先把位姿估对，再画地图（或在回环后用新位姿把地图重画一遍）。

    Choosing \(\alpha\). The occupied band \(\alpha\) must cover the range noise (a few \(\sigma\)) and the grid's discretization (the centre of the end cell can be up to half a diagonal, \(h/\sqrt2\), from the true end point), so \(\alpha\) is usually one to two cell widths. Too large, and the truly free cells within \(\alpha/2\) in front of the surface (on the sensor side) get \(\ell_{\rm occ}\) every time, so walls "grow fat" toward the robot: this is where the roughly 400 false-occupied cells at \(\alpha=0.4\) m in FIG 2 come from. Behind the surface there is no such problem, because the beam stops at the surface and never adds anything beyond it.

    The price of independence. Every beam is treated as independent evidence about every cell, yet neighbouring beams of one scan cross the same cells and share the same pose error; adding them up as independent makes the map overconfident (the "same vote counted twice" of Lesson 4.2). Thrun (2003) optimizes the whole map with the forward model \(p(z\mid m,x)\), which removes these contradictions at a much higher cost; practical systems cap the confidence by clamping \(\ell\in[\ell_{\min},\ell_{\max}]\).

    Pose errors become map errors. A heading error \(\delta\theta\) moves the end point at range \(r\) sideways by about \(r\,\delta\theta\). The drift in FIG 2 is \(\kappa\) per metre, so after a loop of length \(L\) the heading error is \(\kappa L\): with \(\kappa=0.05\) °/m that is \(57\times0.05\approx2.9^\circ\), or 0.050 rad, and a wall 5 m away is shifted by \(5\times0.050\approx0.25\) m, two and a half cells. The same wall drawn from two different poses is a "double wall". This is why mapping cannot do without the loop closures and pose graphs of Lesson 15.2: first get the poses right, then draw the map (or redraw it with the new poses after a loop closure).

    距离变换：误差界与线性时间的精确算法Distance transforms: error bounds and an exact linear-time algorithm

    野火在无障碍的平面上走出的是格点路径，所以在方向 \((a,b)\)（\(a\ge b\ge0\)，单位为格）上它量到的是：4 连通 \(a+b\)，8 连通单位步长 \(a\)，倒角 \(a+(\sqrt2-1)b\)；真值是 \(\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\)。令 \(t=b/a\in[0,1]\)，倒角与真值之比为 \(f(t)=\big(1+(\sqrt2-1)t\big)/\sqrt{1+t^2}\)，在 \(t=\sqrt2-1\)（约 22.5°）处取最大值 \(\sqrt{4-2\sqrt2}\approx1.0824\)。于是 4 连通、8 连通（单位步长）和倒角三种野火的相对误差 \(e_4,e_8,e_{\rm ch}\) 的范围分别是：

    On an obstacle-free plane, brushfire walks lattice paths, so in the direction \((a,b)\) (\(a\ge b\ge0\), in cells) it measures: 4-connected \(a+b\), 8-connected with unit steps \(a\), chamfer \(a+(\sqrt2-1)b\); the truth is \(\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\). With \(t=b/a\in[0,1]\), the ratio of chamfer to truth is \(f(t)=\big(1+(\sqrt2-1)t\big)/\sqrt{1+t^2}\), which peaks at \(t=\sqrt2-1\) (about 22.5°) with the value \(\sqrt{4-2\sqrt2}\approx1.0824\). So the relative errors \(e_4,e_8,e_{\rm ch}\) of the 4-connected, 8-connected (unit-step) and chamfer brushfires lie in:

    $$ e_4\in[0,\ +41.4\%],\qquad e_8\in[-29.3\%,\ 0],\qquad e_{\rm ch}\in[0,\ +8.24\%] $$

    精确算法（Felzenszwalb & Huttenlocher 2012）利用平方距离可分离：\(\hl{dt}{D}(x,y)^2=\min_{(x',y')\in\mathcal O}\big[(x-x')^2+(y-y')^2\big]\)。先对每一列算 \(g(x,y)=\min_{y'}(y-y')^2\)（只看这一列的障碍），再对每一行算

    The exact algorithm (Felzenszwalb & Huttenlocher 2012) exploits the separability of the squared distance: \(\hl{dt}{D}(x,y)^2=\min_{(x',y')\in\mathcal O}\big[(x-x')^2+(y-y')^2\big]\). First compute, for every column, \(g(x,y)=\min_{y'}(y-y')^2\) (looking only at that column's obstacles), then for every row

    $$ \hl{dt}{D}(x,y)^2=\min_{x'}\Big[(x-x')^2+g(x',y)\Big] $$

    每个 \(x'\) 贡献一条开口向上、顶点在 \((x',g(x',y))\) 的抛物线，答案是这些抛物线的下包络。抛物线两两只交一次，所以从左到右扫一遍、用一个栈维护“当前还在包络上的抛物线”，就能在 \(O(n)\) 内求出整行的下包络，整张图 \(O(N)\)。FIG 4 与本页所有其他距离场（FIG 1、3、5）都用这个算法，结果和逐点暴力搜索一致到浮点精度；算法同时记下每格最近的那个障碍格（“特征变换”），FIG 5 的骨架就是用它找出来的：相邻两格的最近障碍突然相隔很远，说明它们跨过了一道山脊。

    Each \(x'\) contributes an upward parabola with apex \((x',g(x',y))\), and the answer is the lower envelope of these parabolas. Two parabolas cross only once, so a single left-to-right sweep with a stack of "parabolas still on the envelope" yields the lower envelope of a whole row in \(O(n)\), and the whole image in \(O(N)\). FIG 4 and every other distance field on this page (FIGs 1, 3 and 5) use this algorithm, and the results agree with a point-by-point brute-force search to floating-point precision; the algorithm also records the nearest obstacle cell of each cell (the "feature transform"), which is how the skeleton of FIG 5 is found: when the nearest obstacles of two neighbouring cells are suddenly far apart, the pair straddles a ridge.

    骨架、关键点与区域Skeleton, critical points and regions

    GVD 是自由空间中“最近障碍点不唯一”的点集。在 FIG 5 中，一个格子属于骨架的条件是：它的净空 \(D\ge0.3\) m，且某个 4 邻居的最近障碍格与它的相距超过两格；然后用 Zhang–Suen 细化把它削成一格宽。沿着骨架上一段净空小于 \(c_{\max}=0.7\) m 的窄段（它的两端都要接到宽阔处，否则只是伸进墙角的“毛刺”），净空序列的局部极小（要求两侧至少回升 4 cm）就是关键点 \(\hl{crit}{\mathbf c}\)；过 \(\mathbf c\)、垂直于骨架方向一直延伸到两侧障碍的线段是关键线。这是对 Thrun（1998）的简化：他的关键点是 Voronoi 图上净空的局部极小，关键线是把关键点和它的两个基点连起来；在门口这样的直瓶颈里，两种画法几乎重合。\(c_{\max}\)、4 cm 的回升要求、“毛刺”规则和 40 格的区域下限都是本页为了让分割稳定而自己加的阈值。所以在这里，“是不是门”取决于 \(c_{\max}\)：门宽超过 \(2c_{\max}=1.4\) m，它在算法眼里就只是房间里的一段宽敞地带，这就是 FIG 5 第一个任务的答案。

    The GVD is the set of free points whose nearest obstacle point is not unique. In FIG 5 a cell belongs to the skeleton if its clearance is \(D\ge0.3\) m and the nearest obstacle cell of some 4-neighbour is more than two cells away from its own; Zhang–Suen thinning then pares it to one cell wide. Along each narrow stretch of skeleton with clearance under \(c_{\max}=0.7\) m (both of whose ends must reach wide space, otherwise it is just a "whisker" reaching into a corner), the local minima of the clearance sequence (with a rise of at least 4 cm on each side) are the critical points \(\hl{crit}{\mathbf c}\); the segment through \(\mathbf c\), perpendicular to the skeleton and extended to the obstacles on both sides, is the critical line. This simplifies Thrun (1998): his critical points are the local minima of clearance on the Voronoi diagram, and a critical line joins a critical point to its two basis points; in a straight bottleneck such as a doorway the two constructions nearly coincide. \(c_{\max}\), the 4 cm rise, the "whisker" rule and the 40-cell minimum region size are thresholds this page adds to keep the segmentation stable. So here, "is it a door" depends on \(c_{\max}\): a door wider than \(2c_{\max}=1.4\) m is, to the algorithm, just a roomy stretch of one room, which answers the first task of FIG 5.

    改口与遗忘：钳位和衰减Changing its mind and forgetting: clamping and decay

    一个格子被观测为占据很多次之后，\(\ell\) 可能非常大；东西挪走以后，要清掉它需要 \(\ell/|\ell_{\rm free}|\) 次“穿过”。钳位把这个次数封顶：从 \(\ell_{\max}\) 降到判为占据的门槛 \(\ell_{\rm thr}\) 以下需要

    After a cell has been seen occupied many times its \(\ell\) can be very large; once the object is moved away, clearing it takes \(\ell/|\ell_{\rm free}|\) pass-throughs. Clamping caps that number: going from \(\ell_{\max}\) to below the occupied threshold \(\ell_{\rm thr}\) takes

    $$ n_{\rm clear}=\Big\lceil\frac{\ell_{\max}-\ell_{\rm thr}}{|\ell_{\rm free}|}\Big\rceil=\Big\lceil\frac{3.5-0.4}{0.405}\Big\rceil=8 $$

    钳位只对还能再看到的地方有效：要有射线重新穿过那一格。看不到的地方（传感器的盲区、被挡住的区域、量程以外）既不会被清掉，也不会被确认。衰减（每一步把 \(\ell\) 乘以 \(2^{-\Delta t/\tau}\)，向“未知”回落）不依赖新的观测，所以能清掉看不到的鬼影，代价是同样会忘掉看不到的真墙：半衰期为 \(\tau\) 时，一个被钳在 \(\ell_{\max}\) 的格子在约 \(3.13\tau\) 后就不再被当作占据。FIG 6 把三种策略并排放在一起。

    Clamping only works where the robot can look again: some beam has to pass through that cell once more. Places it cannot see (the sensor's blind spot, occluded areas, beyond the range) are neither cleared nor confirmed. Decay (multiplying \(\ell\) by \(2^{-\Delta t/\tau}\) at every step, sliding back toward "unknown") needs no new observation, so it can remove ghosts the robot cannot see, at the price of forgetting real walls it cannot see too: with half-life \(\tau\), a cell clamped at \(\ell_{\max}\) stops counting as occupied after about \(3.13\tau\). FIG 6 puts the three strategies side by side.

    $$ \ell(t)=\ell_0\,2^{-t/\hl{tau}{\tau}}\ \Rightarrow\ t_{\rm forget}=\hl{tau}{\tau}\log_2\frac{\ell_0}{\ell_{\rm thr}}=\hl{tau}{\tau}\log_2\frac{3.5}{0.4}\approx3.13\,\hl{tau}{\tau} $$

    6机器人中的它：会变的世界，与“谁来读地图”In robotics: a world that moves, and "who reads the map"

    占据栅格假设世界是静止的，可真实世界里人会走过、椅子会被挪走。下图里机器人停在一个房间的左侧，开机时原地转一圈，把四周（包括身后的墙）都看了一遍，之后用 270° 激光雷达（身后 90° 是盲区）每 0.1 s 扫描一次。第 2 到 15 s 有人走进来、站了 5 s、再从右边的门出去；第 20 s 椅子被搬走。三张地图用同一串扫描并行更新：不钳位、钳位 \([-2,3.5]\)、钳位 + 衰减（半衰期 \(\tau\)）。这里没有回波的射线按“空闲到最大量程”处理，否则从门口出去的射线永远清不掉门边的格子。

    An occupancy grid assumes a static world, but in reality people walk by and chairs get moved. In the figure below a robot is parked on the left side of a room; at power-up it turns once on the spot and sees all around it (including the wall behind it), and from then on it scans every 0.1 s with a 270° lidar (the 90° behind it is blind). Between 2 and 15 s a person walks in, stands for 5 s and leaves through the door on the right; at 20 s the chair is carried away. Three maps are updated in parallel from the same scans: no clamp, clamp \([-2,3.5]\), and clamp + decay (half-life \(\tau\)). Here beams with no return are treated as "free up to max range"; otherwise the beams leaving through the doorway could never clear the cells next to the door.

    • 选“不钳位”，播放到 30 s 以后：20 s 时搬走的椅子仍在地图里（至少 5 个鬼影格）。此刻切到“钳位”：一个鬼影也没有。Choose "no clamp" and play past 30 s: the chair carried away at 20 s is still in the map (at least 5 ghost cells). Now switch to "clamp": not a single ghost.
    • 选“钳位 + 衰减”、\(\tau\le10\) s，播放到 40 s 以后：盲区墙只剩不到一半还被当作墙，而“钳位”那张图里它一直是 100%。Choose "clamp + decay" with \(\tau\le10\) s and play past 40 s: less than half of the blind wall is still considered a wall, while in the "clamp" map it stays at 100% throughout.
    真实系统里In real systems

    2D 激光建图：GMapping（Grisetti 等 2007，Rao-Blackwellized 粒子滤波）、Cartographer（Hess 等 2016，把扫描插入局部“子图”概率栅格，后端做回环）和 slam_toolbox（ROS 2 Nav2 的启动文件建图时调用的 SLAM，15.2 节）输出的都是占据栅格，存成一张图片加一个 YAML 文件（分辨率和原点），交给定位（AMCL 的粒子滤波，4.10 节）和导航使用。导航：ROS 的 costmap_2d 定义了 0（空闲）、253（内切）、254（致命）、255（未知）这几个特殊代价；膨胀层出厂值是 inflation_radius 0.55 m、cost_scaling_factor 10（正是 FIG 3 的默认值），实际部署常把衰减调小、半径调大，让机器人走在通道中间。全局用静态层 + 膨胀，局部用跟着机器人走的“滚动窗口”代价地图，障碍层按射线“标记”和“清除”；时空体素层（STVL，Nav2 常用的一个代价地图插件）则让标记过的体素在设定的衰减时间后过期，和 FIG 6 第三种策略里“看不到也会遗忘”是同一个思路（它按时间删除体素，而不是让对数几率衰减）。三维：OctoMap（八叉树占据），Voxblox 与 nvblox（TSDF → ESDF，给无人机和机械臂的轨迹优化用）。足式机器人：ANYmal 一类的系统用 2.5D 高程图（Fankhauser 等的 elevation_mapping 与 grid_map 库），每格存地面高度和它的方差，因为“能不能踩”比“有没有东西”更重要。定位地图：ORB-SLAM 的地图点与关键帧，自动驾驶的高精地图（车道线、路标）。语义：Kimera 与 Hydra 实时构建三维场景图，ConceptGraphs 等把视觉语言模型的特征挂到物体节点上，让语言指令能查询地图。

    2D laser mapping: GMapping (Grisetti et al. 2007, a Rao-Blackwellized particle filter), Cartographer (Hess et al. 2016, which inserts scans into local probability-grid "submaps" and closes loops in a back end) and slam_toolbox (the SLAM package Nav2's launch files start for mapping, Lesson 15.2) all output occupancy grids, saved as an image plus a YAML file (resolution and origin), which then serve localization (AMCL's particle filter, Lesson 4.10) and navigation. Navigation: ROS's costmap_2d defines the special costs 0 (free), 253 (inscribed), 254 (lethal) and 255 (unknown); the inflation layer's factory values are inflation_radius 0.55 m and cost_scaling_factor 10 (exactly FIG 3's defaults), and deployments often lower the decay and raise the radius so the robot drives down the middle of corridors. The global plan uses the static layer plus inflation; locally a "rolling window" costmap moves with the robot, and its obstacle layer "marks" and "clears" along beams; the spatio-temporal voxel layer (STVL, a costmap plugin often used with Nav2) lets marked voxels expire after a set decay time, the same "forget what you cannot see" idea as FIG 6's third strategy (it deletes voxels by age rather than decaying log-odds). 3D: OctoMap (octree occupancy), Voxblox and nvblox (TSDF → ESDF, for trajectory optimization on drones and arms). Legged robots: systems like ANYmal use 2.5D elevation maps (the elevation_mapping and grid_map libraries of Fankhauser et al.), storing ground height and its variance per cell, because "can I step here" matters more than "is something here". Localization maps: ORB-SLAM's map points and keyframes, the HD maps of autonomous driving (lane lines, signs). Semantics: Kimera and Hydra build 3D scene graphs in real time, and ConceptGraphs and similar systems attach vision-language features to object nodes so that language commands can query the map.

    地图跟着读者选The map follows its reader

    把这一章串起来：估计器（15.1 的 VIO、15.2 的 SLAM 后端）要的是能反复认出来、能写进残差的东西，所以它维护路标或扫描子图；全局规划器（14.1 的 A*、14.2 的采样规划）要的是能不能过，所以读代价地图或拓扑图；轨迹优化与 MPC（14.3、13.4）要的是可微的距离，所以读 ESDF；任务规划（14.4、Part VII）要的是物体和地点，所以读语义地图。真实系统从同一份传感器数据和同一组位姿出发，同时维护几张地图，每张只为一个读者优化。

    Putting the chapter together: the estimator (the VIO of 15.1, the SLAM back end of 15.2) needs things it can recognize again and write into residuals, so it keeps landmarks or scan submaps; the global planner (the A* of 14.1, the sampling planners of 14.2) needs to know what is passable, so it reads a costmap or a topological graph; trajectory optimization and MPC (14.3, 13.4) need a differentiable distance, so they read an ESDF; task planning (14.4, Part VII) needs objects and places, so it reads a semantic map. Real systems start from the same sensor data and the same poses and keep several maps at once, each optimized for one reader.

    7常见误区Common pitfalls

    误区 1：把“未知”当成“空闲”Pitfall 1: treating "unknown" as "free"

    没观测过的格子和观测过是空的格子，在一张只有 0/1 的地图里看起来一样，但意义完全不同。FIG 2 的深色沙发是最直接的例子：激光打上去没有回波，如果把无回波当成“空闲到最大量程”，沙发连同它后面的墙都会被标成空的，规划器会径直开过去。全局规划器一般有 allow_unknown 一类的开关：探索时允许穿过未知区，正常导航时应当保守。深色、透明（玻璃）、镜面和非常低矮的障碍都是 2D 激光的典型盲点，需要别的传感器（深度相机、超声、碰撞开关）补上。

    A cell never observed and a cell observed empty look the same in a 0/1 map but mean entirely different things. The dark sofa in FIG 2 is the most direct example: beams hitting it return nothing, and if no-return is treated as "free up to max range", the sofa and the wall behind it are marked empty and the planner drives straight in. Global planners usually have an allow_unknown-style switch: allow crossing unknown space while exploring, be conservative in normal navigation. Dark, transparent (glass), mirror-like and very low obstacles are typical blind spots of a 2D lidar and need other sensors (depth cameras, ultrasound, bump switches) to cover them.

    误区 2：用漂移的位姿建图，或回环后不重画Pitfall 2: mapping with drifting poses, or not redrawing after a loop closure

    FIG 2 里每米 0.05° 的航向漂移（走完一圈不到 3°）就足以让墙面覆盖掉到约 91%、多出约 200 个误占据格。占据栅格本身没有“修正位姿”的能力：已经加进去的证据不会因为后来位姿被优化而自动搬家。所以 Cartographer 一类的系统把扫描插入局部子图，回环后只移动子图的位姿；更简单的做法是在位姿图优化之后，用新位姿把所有扫描重新插一遍。

    In FIG 2 a heading drift of 0.05° per metre (under 3° over the whole loop) is enough to drop wall coverage to about 91% and add about 200 false-occupied cells. The occupancy grid itself cannot "fix a pose": evidence already added does not move when the pose is later optimized. That is why systems like Cartographer insert scans into local submaps and, after a loop closure, move only the submap poses; the simpler alternative is to re-insert every scan with the new poses after the pose-graph optimization.

    误区 3：膨胀参数和机器人对不上Pitfall 3: inflation parameters that do not match the robot

    \(r_{\rm ins}\) 小于真实轮廓，规划出来的路会擦墙；\(r_{\rm ins}\) 太大，门就“关上了”（FIG 3：半径达到门宽的一半 0.5 m，就无路可走）。膨胀半径小于外接半径时，转弯时车角可能扫到墙。\(k\) 太大，代价只是贴着墙的一条窄带，路径一路贴墙（默认值的平均净空约 0.8 m）。还要记得栅格距离按格心算，会比真实距离乐观约半格；分辨率越粗，这个乐观越大。

    An \(r_{\rm ins}\) smaller than the real footprint gives paths that scrape walls; an \(r_{\rm ins}\) too large "closes" doors (FIG 3: once the radius reaches half the door width, 0.5 m, there is no path). An inflation radius smaller than the circumscribed radius lets the corners of the base sweep a wall while turning. A large \(k\) leaves the cost as a thin strip along the walls and the path hugs them (mean clearance about 0.8 m with the defaults). Also remember that grid distances are measured between cell centres and are optimistic by about half a cell; the coarser the resolution, the larger this optimism.

    误区 4：分辨率越细越好Pitfall 4: the finer the resolution the better

    格子边长减半，内存和每次扫描要更新的格子数都翻几倍（2D 是 4 倍，3D 是 8 倍）。更隐蔽的问题是证据变稀：2° 一束的雷达在 9 m 外两束相隔约 31 cm，10 cm 的格子里有一大半永远没有射线经过，地图上出现条纹状的“未知”（FIG 6 房间右侧就能看到）。分辨率应当和传感器的角分辨率、量程以及机器人需要分辨的最小间隙一起选。

    Halving the cell size multiplies memory and the cells updated per scan several times (4 times in 2D, 8 in 3D). The subtler problem is that evidence thins out: a lidar with 2° between beams has beams about 31 cm apart at 9 m, so most 10 cm cells out there are never crossed by any beam and the map shows striped "unknown" (visible on the right of the room in FIG 6). Choose the resolution together with the sensor's angular resolution, its range, and the smallest gap the robot needs to tell apart.

    误区 5：把会动的东西画进静态地图Pitfall 5: drawing moving things into the static map

    人、车、被推来推去的椅子不属于“地图”，属于“状态”。不钳位的对数几率会把站了 5 s 的人变成约 4 s 才清得掉的鬼影，把放了 20 s 的椅子变成到 60 s 时还清不掉的鬼影（FIG 6）；钳位能让地图快速改口，但只对看得见的地方有效；衰减能清掉看不见的鬼影，但同样会忘掉看不见的墙。成熟的系统把动态物体检测出来单独跟踪（卡尔曼滤波，4.8 节），只把静态部分写进长期地图，局部代价地图则用短时的滚动窗口。

    People, cars and chairs that get pushed around belong to the "state", not to the "map". Unclamped log-odds turn a person who stood for 5 s into a ghost that takes about 4 s to clear, and a chair that stood for 20 s into one still there at 60 s (FIG 6); clamping lets the map change its mind quickly, but only where it can see; decay clears ghosts it cannot see, but forgets walls it cannot see as well. Mature systems detect dynamic objects and track them separately (Kalman filters, Lesson 4.8), write only the static part into the long-term map, and keep the local costmap as a short-lived rolling window.

    8小练习Check yourself

    1. 机器人航向有 1° 的误差，它把 6 m 外的一面墙画到地图上。墙大约偏了多少？1. The robot's heading is off by 1°, and it draws a wall 6 m away into the map. Roughly how far is the wall displaced?
    横向偏移约为 \(r\,\delta\theta=6\times(\pi/180)\approx0.105\) m，也就是 10 cm 栅格的一整格。航向误差会被距离放大，所以远处的墙对航向最敏感，这正是 FIG 2 里漂移让墙“分层”的原因。The sideways shift is about \(r\,\delta\theta=6\times(\pi/180)\approx0.105\) m, a whole cell of a 10 cm grid. Heading errors are magnified by range, so far walls are the most sensitive to heading, which is why drift splits the walls into layers in FIG 2.
    2. 对数几率钳在 \([-2,3.5]\)，\(\ell_{\rm free}=-0.405\)，判为占据的门槛是 0.4。一个已被钳在 3.5 的格子，东西搬走后最少要几次“穿过”才不再被判为占据？2. Log-odds are clamped to \([-2,3.5]\), \(\ell_{\rm free}=-0.405\), and the occupied threshold is 0.4. A cell sitting at the clamp 3.5 loses its object: at least how many pass-throughs until it is no longer called occupied?
    需要 \(3.5-0.405n<0.4\)，即 \(n>7.65\)，所以 \(n=8\)：8 次后 \(\ell=0.26\)。选项 D 是不钳位时的情况：那时清除时间与历史成正比，这正是钳位要解决的问题。We need \(3.5-0.405n<0.4\), i.e. \(n>7.65\), so \(n=8\): after 8 of them \(\ell=0.26\). Option D describes the unclamped case, where the clearing time grows with the history, which is exactly what clamping is for.
    3. 用 4 连通的野火（每步代价 1）计算距离变换。和真实的欧氏距离相比，它的最大相对误差是多少？3. A distance transform is computed by 4-connected brushfire (unit step cost). Compared with the true Euclidean distance, what is its largest relative error?
    4 连通量到的是曼哈顿距离 \(a+b\)，在 45° 方向上是 \(2a\)，而真值是 \(\sqrt2a\)，比值 \(\sqrt2\)，即高估 41.4%。Dijkstra 在图上是精确的，但这张图的度量不是欧氏的。选项 B 是 8 连通单位步长的结果，选项 C 是倒角 \((1,\sqrt2)\) 的结果。4-connected brushfire measures the Manhattan distance \(a+b\); at 45° that is \(2a\) against a true \(\sqrt2a\), a ratio of \(\sqrt2\), i.e. 41.4% over. Dijkstra is exact on the graph, but the graph's metric is not Euclidean. Option B is the 8-connected unit-step result and option C the chamfer \((1,\sqrt2)\) result.
    4. 一台只有相机的机器人要在一栋大楼里随时重新定位（被搬到任意位置后找回自己）。最合适的主地图是？4. A camera-only robot must relocalize anywhere in a large building (find itself again after being carried to an arbitrary spot). Which main map fits best?
    重定位要回答“我看到的东西在地图里哪里”。特征点带描述子，可以把当前图像的特征和地图点匹配（11.5 节），再用 PnP 求位姿（11.4 节）；ORB-SLAM 的重定位就是这样做的。代价地图和 ESDF 是为规划准备的，相机看不到“代价”；拓扑图太粗，只能告诉你在哪个房间。Relocalization must answer "where in the map is what I see". Feature points carry descriptors, so the current image's features can be matched to map points (Lesson 11.5) and the pose solved by PnP (Lesson 11.4); ORB-SLAM relocalizes exactly this way. Costmaps and ESDFs are made for planning, and a camera cannot see "cost"; a topological graph is too coarse and can only tell you which room you are in.
    5. 一台圆形机器人半径 0.35 m，门宽 0.9 m。代价地图里 \(r_{\rm ins}=0.35\) m、\(r_{\rm inf}=1.0\) m。规划器会怎样？5. A circular robot has radius 0.35 m and a door is 0.9 m wide. The costmap has \(r_{\rm ins}=0.35\) m and \(r_{\rm inf}=1.0\) m. What does the planner do?
    门里离两侧门框都超过 0.35 m 的位置是可行的：门中线两侧各 \(0.45-0.35=0.10\) m，共约 0.2 m 宽。这些位置离障碍不到 \(r_{\rm inf}\)，所以有代价（膨胀区），但不是 253。膨胀半径只决定“贵不贵”，不决定“能不能过”，所以选项 A 错。Positions in the door farther than 0.35 m from both jambs are allowed: \(0.45-0.35=0.10\) m on each side of the centre line, about 0.2 m in all. They are closer than \(r_{\rm inf}\) to an obstacle, so they carry a cost (the inflation zone), but not 253. The inflation radius decides "how expensive", not "passable or not", so option A is wrong.
