操作空间控制Operational-Space Control

想让手爪走直线、在每个方向上都一样"听话"，就要在手爪所在的任务空间里写动力学。操作空间控制用 8.3 节的操作空间惯量 \(\Lambda\) 把期望加速度换成力，用 \(\boldsymbol\mu\) 抵消速度带来的漂移，再用 \(J^\top\) 换成关节力矩。这一节把它和雅可比转置控制放在一起比较，看环境在两种控制下感受到的刚度有何不同，看冗余臂怎样在动力学一致的零空间里做第二任务而不打扰手爪，以及为什么在奇异位形附近必须给等效质量封顶。 To make a gripper move in straight lines and respond the same way in every direction, write the dynamics in the task space where the gripper lives. Operational-space control turns a desired acceleration into a force with the operational-space inertia \(\Lambda\) of Lesson 8.3, cancels the velocity-induced drift with \(\boldsymbol\mu\), and maps the force to joint torques with \(J^\top\). This lesson compares it with Jacobian-transpose control, shows how differently the environment feels the two, shows how a redundant arm runs a second task in the dynamically consistent null space without disturbing the gripper, and explains why the apparent mass must be capped near singularities.

    1为什么机器人需要它Why robots need this

    很多任务用手爪的坐标来描述最自然：把杯子平移 30 cm，沿直线擦桌子，插孔时轴向推进、侧向保持柔顺。一种做法是先用 7.5 节的逆运动学把手爪路径换成关节轨迹，再交给关节控制器（13.1 节的 PID，或 8.3 节的计算力矩）。位置控制的工业臂通常就这么做。可是对力矩控制的手臂（Franka、KUKA iiwa，以及四足和人形机器人的腿），我们更希望控制器直接"站在手爪处"思考：离目标还差多少，该以多大的加速度靠近，碰到东西时该用多大的力。

    最简单的想法来自 3.4 节和 7.6 节的力对偶：在手爪处虚拟一根弹簧加一个阻尼器 \(\mathbf F=K_p\mathbf e-K_d\dot{\mathbf x}\)，再用 \(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top\mathbf F\) 送到关节。这叫雅可比转置控制。它稳定，而且不需要动力学模型。但 8.3 节已经告诉我们：手爪在不同方向上的等效质量 \(\Lambda\) 可以差好几倍甚至几十倍。同一根弹簧，往"轻"的方向拉得快，往"重"的方向拉得慢，于是路径会弯，响应还随姿态改变。

    操作空间控制（operational-space control，OSC，Khatib 1987）的答案是：用 8.3 节推导的任务空间动力学 \(\Lambda\ddot{\mathbf x}+\boldsymbol\mu+\mathbf p=\mathbf F\) 把手爪看成一个"质点"，像计算力矩控制那样把 \(\Lambda\)、\(\boldsymbol\mu\)、\(\mathbf p\) 抵消掉，让每个任务方向都变成一个独立的单位质量。这一节回答四个问题：(1) 为什么要乘 \(\Lambda\)、加 \(\boldsymbol\mu\)；(2) 和雅可比转置控制相比，得到了什么，又付出了什么；(3) 冗余臂多出来的自由度怎样才能不打扰手爪；(4) 奇异位形附近该怎么办。

    全篇沿用 8.3 节的平面两连杆臂（\(l_1=1\) m，\(l_2=0.8\) m，\(m_1=2\) kg，\(m_2=1.5\) kg，均匀杆），讲冗余时换成三连杆臂。为了把注意力集中在惯性上，所有手臂都在水平面里运动（俯视图，类似 SCARA），重力不作用在关节上，\(\mathbf p=0\)；竖直平面里的手臂只要再加上 8.3 节的重力补偿 \(\hat{\mathbf p}\) 即可。

    Many tasks are most naturally described in the gripper's coordinates: slide a cup 30 cm, wipe a table along a straight line, push along the axis of a peg while staying compliant sideways. One way is to turn the gripper path into a joint trajectory with the inverse kinematics of Lesson 7.5 and hand it to a joint controller (the PID of Lesson 13.1, or the computed torque of Lesson 8.3). Position-controlled industrial arms usually do exactly that. For torque-controlled arms, though (Franka, KUKA iiwa, the legs of quadrupeds and humanoids), we would rather have the controller think "at the gripper": how far it still is from the target, how hard it should accelerate toward it, and how much force to use when it touches something.

    The simplest idea is the force duality of Lesson 3.4 and Lesson 7.6: put a virtual spring and damper at the gripper, \(\mathbf F=K_p\mathbf e-K_d\dot{\mathbf x}\), and send it to the joints with \(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top\mathbf F\). This is Jacobian-transpose control. It is stable and needs no dynamic model. But Lesson 8.3 showed that the apparent mass \(\Lambda\) at the gripper can differ by a factor of several, even tens, between directions. The same spring pulls fast in a "light" direction and slowly in a "heavy" one, so the path bends and the response changes with posture.

    The answer of operational-space control (OSC, Khatib 1987) is to use the task-space dynamics \(\Lambda\ddot{\mathbf x}+\boldsymbol\mu+\mathbf p=\mathbf F\) derived in Lesson 8.3, treat the gripper as a "point mass", and cancel \(\Lambda\), \(\boldsymbol\mu\) and \(\mathbf p\) the way computed-torque control does, so that every task direction becomes an independent unit mass. This lesson answers four questions: (1) why multiply by \(\Lambda\) and add \(\boldsymbol\mu\); (2) what that buys, and what it costs, compared with Jacobian-transpose control; (3) how a redundant arm can use its extra freedom without disturbing the gripper; (4) what to do near singular postures.

    The page reuses Lesson 8.3's planar two-link arm (\(l_1=1\) m, \(l_2=0.8\) m, \(m_1=2\) kg, \(m_2=1.5\) kg, uniform rods) and switches to a three-link arm for redundancy. To keep the focus on inertia, every arm moves in a horizontal plane (a top view, like a SCARA), so gravity does not load the joints and \(\mathbf p=0\); an arm in a vertical plane only needs Lesson 8.3's gravity compensation \(\hat{\mathbf p}\) on top.

    2直觉：力不等于加速度Intuition: force is not acceleration

    8.3 节 FIG 3 里我们推过手爪：沿 \(\mathbf F\) 推，手爪的加速度是 \(\Lambda^{-1}\mathbf F\)，一般不沿 \(\mathbf F\)。做控制时把问题反过来：我们想要一个加速度 \(\mathbf a^*\)，应该施加什么力？

    • 雅可比转置的想法：把手爪当成质量为 \(\bar m\) 的质点，施加 \(\mathbf F=\bar m\,\mathbf a^*\)。真实的响应是 \(\bar m\Lambda^{-1}\mathbf a^*\)：大小和方向都被 \(\Lambda^{-1}\) 扭曲了。
    • 操作空间的想法：施加 \(\mathbf F=\Lambda\mathbf a^*\)，用真实的等效质量去"配"想要的加速度，\(\Lambda^{-1}\Lambda\mathbf a^*=\mathbf a^*\)。

    手臂在动的时候还有一层：即使关节力矩为零，手爪也会自己加速。一部分来自关节空间的离心力和科氏力 \(C\dot{\mathbf q}\)（8.3 节），一部分来自几何本身：关节匀速转动时手爪走的是圆弧，圆弧运动就有向心加速度，这就是 7.4 节末尾预告的 \(\dot J\dot{\mathbf q}\)。由 \(\ddot{\mathbf x}=J\ddot{\mathbf q}+\dot J\dot{\mathbf q}\) 和 \(\ddot{\mathbf q}=M^{-1}(\boldsymbol\tau-C\dot{\mathbf q})\)（水平面里没有重力）得到

    In FIG 3 of Lesson 8.3 we pushed on the gripper: push along \(\mathbf F\) and it accelerates by \(\Lambda^{-1}\mathbf F\), generally not along \(\mathbf F\). Control turns the question around: we want an acceleration \(\mathbf a^*\); which force should we apply?

    • The Jacobian-transpose idea: treat the gripper as a point of mass \(\bar m\) and apply \(\mathbf F=\bar m\,\mathbf a^*\). The real response is \(\bar m\Lambda^{-1}\mathbf a^*\): both its size and its direction are warped by \(\Lambda^{-1}\).
    • The operational-space idea: apply \(\mathbf F=\Lambda\mathbf a^*\), matching the wanted acceleration with the real apparent mass, so that \(\Lambda^{-1}\Lambda\mathbf a^*=\mathbf a^*\).

    When the arm is moving there is one more layer: even with zero joint torque the gripper accelerates by itself. Part of that comes from the joint-space centrifugal and Coriolis terms \(C\dot{\mathbf q}\) (Lesson 8.3), part from the geometry itself: with the joints turning at constant rates the gripper travels on arcs, and arcs need centripetal acceleration; that is the \(\dot J\dot{\mathbf q}\) previewed at the end of Lesson 7.4. From \(\ddot{\mathbf x}=J\ddot{\mathbf q}+\dot J\dot{\mathbf q}\) and \(\ddot{\mathbf q}=M^{-1}(\boldsymbol\tau-C\dot{\mathbf q})\) (no gravity in the horizontal plane) we get

    $$ \boldsymbol\tau=J^{\top}\mathbf F\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \ddot{\mathbf x}=\hl{Lam}{\Lambda^{-1}}\mathbf F+\hl{mu}{\mathbf d},\qquad \mathbf d=\dot J\dot{\mathbf q}-JM^{-1}C\dot{\mathbf q},\qquad \boldsymbol\mu=-\Lambda\,\mathbf d . $$

    \(\mathbf d\) 是"漂移"：不施力时手爪自己的加速度。8.3 节的 \(\boldsymbol\mu=\Lambda(JM^{-1}C-\dot J)\dot{\mathbf q}\) 恰好是抵消它所需的力（取负号）。所以完整的操作空间指令是 \(\mathbf F=\Lambda\hl{a}{\mathbf a^*}+\boldsymbol\mu\)，代入得 \(\ddot{\mathbf x}=\mathbf a^*\)，一点不差。下图左右拖动关节圆圈改变姿态，拖动琥珀色箭头的箭尖改变想要的加速度 \(\mathbf a^*\)，用滑块给关节加上速度。

    \(\mathbf d\) is the "drift": the gripper's own acceleration when no force is applied. Lesson 8.3's \(\boldsymbol\mu=\Lambda(JM^{-1}C-\dot J)\dot{\mathbf q}\) is exactly the force needed to cancel it (with a minus sign). So the full operational-space command is \(\mathbf F=\Lambda\hl{a}{\mathbf a^*}+\boldsymbol\mu\), and substituting gives \(\ddot{\mathbf x}=\mathbf a^*\) exactly.     核心洞见Key insight

    在手爪处，"力 = 质量 × 加速度"里的质量是一个随姿态变化的矩阵 \(\Lambda(\mathbf q)\)，而且手臂在动时还有一个与力无关的漂移 \(\mathbf d\)。操作空间控制就是在手爪处做一次计算力矩：先用 \(\boldsymbol\mu\) 抵消漂移，再用 \(\Lambda\) 把想要的加速度翻译成力。想要的加速度从哪里来，是下一步的事。

    At the gripper, the mass in "force = mass × acceleration" is a posture-dependent matrix \(\Lambda(\mathbf q)\), and a moving arm adds a force-independent drift \(\mathbf d\). Operational-space control is computed torque done at the gripper: cancel the drift with \(\boldsymbol\mu\), then translate the wanted acceleration into a force with \(\Lambda\). Where the wanted acceleration comes from is the next step.

    3动手玩：雅可比转置 vs 操作空间Play: Jacobian transpose vs. operational space

    想要的加速度来自任务空间的 PD：\(\mathbf a=\omega_n^2\mathbf e-2\omega_n\dot{\mathbf x}\)，\(\mathbf e=\mathbf x_d-\mathbf x\)。这是一个单位质量的临界阻尼弹簧，带宽 \(\omega_n\)。下面两个控制器用同一个 \(\omega_n\)，只差在把加速度换成力的方式：

    The wanted acceleration comes from a task-space PD: \(\mathbf a=\omega_n^2\mathbf e-2\omega_n\dot{\mathbf x}\), with \(\mathbf e=\mathbf x_d-\mathbf x\). That is a critically damped spring on a unit mass with bandwidth \(\omega_n\). The two controllers below share the same \(\omega_n\) and differ only in how they turn acceleration into force:

    $$ \hl{JT}{\boldsymbol\tau_{J^\top}=J^{\top}\,\bar m\big(\omega_n^2\mathbf e-2\omega_n\dot{\mathbf x}\big)},\qquad\qquad \hl{osc}{\boldsymbol\tau_{\mathrm{OSC}}=J^{\top}\Big(\hat\Lambda\big(\omega_n^2\mathbf e-2\omega_n\dot{\mathbf x}\big)+\hat{\boldsymbol\mu}\Big)} . $$

    雅可比转置控制的增益按一个"名义质量" \(\bar m\) 来定：如果手爪真是一个 \(\bar m\) kg 的质点，它就是临界阻尼。操作空间控制用模型 \(\hat\Lambda,\hat{\boldsymbol\mu}\)（帽子表示控制器心目中的模型）。模型准确时，手爪的误差满足 \(\ddot{\mathbf e}+2\omega_n\dot{\mathbf e}+\omega_n^2\mathbf e=0\)：两个方向各是一个独立的临界阻尼系统。从静止出发时 \(\mathbf e(t)=(1+\omega_nt)e^{-\omega_nt}\,\mathbf e(0)\)，误差向量只缩短、不转向，所以手爪走直线。

    拖动蓝色目标圈，松手后两条手臂同时从同一个起点出发（起点就是上一个目标）。也可以加一个控制器不知道的末端负载，或者把负载写进模型。

    The Jacobian-transpose gains are set for a "nominal mass" \(\bar m\): if the gripper really were a point of \(\bar m\) kg, it would be critically damped. Operational-space control uses a model \(\hat\Lambda,\hat{\boldsymbol\mu}\) (the hats mark the controller's belief). With an exact model the gripper error obeys \(\ddot{\mathbf e}+2\omega_n\dot{\mathbf e}+\omega_n^2\mathbf e=0\): two independent critically damped systems, one per direction. Starting from rest, \(\mathbf e(t)=(1+\omega_nt)e^{-\omega_nt}\,\mathbf e(0)\): the error vector only shrinks and never turns, so the gripper moves in a straight line.

    Drag the blue target ring and release it: both arms set off together from the same start, which is the previous target. You can also add a tip payload the controller does not know about, or write the payload into the model.

    核心洞见Key insight

    雅可比转置控制在手爪处装的是一根"不知道手臂多重"的弹簧，它的响应由 \(\Lambda(\mathbf q)^{-1}\) 塑形：方向不同、姿态不同，快慢就不同，路径因此弯曲。操作空间控制先把手爪变成单位质量，再装弹簧，于是每个方向的响应都一样，误差解耦、路径笔直。代价是它依赖模型：负载没写进 \(\hat\Lambda\)，等效增益就被悄悄缩小，阻尼不足而过冲。

    Jacobian-transpose control mounts a spring at the gripper that "does not know how heavy the arm is", so its response is shaped by \(\Lambda(\mathbf q)^{-1}\): fast or slow depending on direction and posture, which bends the path. Operational-space control first turns the gripper into a unit mass and then mounts the spring, so every direction responds the same way: the errors are decoupled and the path is straight. The price is its reliance on the model: leave a payload out of \(\hat\Lambda\) and the effective gains quietly shrink, the damping falls short, and the arm overshoots.

    4第二、第三视角：环境感受到的刚度，与冗余臂的零空间Second and third views: the stiffness the world feels, and the null space of a redundant arm

    环境感受到的刚度The stiffness the environment feels

    FIG 2 看的是自由运动。现在让手臂停在目标处，用一个外力 \(\mathbf F_{\text{ext}}\) 去推手爪。静止时 \(\dot{\mathbf x}=0\)、\(\boldsymbol\mu=0\)，两种控制器的手爪力分别是 \(\bar m\omega_n^2\mathbf e\) 和 \(\Lambda\omega_n^2\mathbf e\)，平衡时它们抵消外力，偏移 \(\boldsymbol\delta=-\mathbf e\) 满足

    FIG 2 was about free motion. Now let the arm hold still at the target and push on the gripper with an external force \(\mathbf F_{\text{ext}}\). At rest \(\dot{\mathbf x}=0\) and \(\boldsymbol\mu=0\), so the two controllers produce gripper forces \(\bar m\omega_n^2\mathbf e\) and \(\Lambda\omega_n^2\mathbf e\); at equilibrium these cancel the push, and the deflection \(\boldsymbol\delta=-\mathbf e\) satisfies

    $$ \underbrace{\bar m\,\omega_n^2\,\boldsymbol\delta=\mathbf F_{\text{ext}}}_{J^{\top}}\qquad\qquad \underbrace{\hl{K}{\omega_n^2\,\Lambda(\mathbf q)}\,\boldsymbol\delta=\mathbf F_{\text{ext}}}_{\mathrm{OSC}} . $$

    雅可比转置给出一根各向同性的弹簧，刚度 \(\bar m\omega_n^2\)。操作空间控制给环境的刚度却是 \(\omega_n^2\Lambda(\mathbf q)\)：轻方向软、重方向硬，并且随姿态变化。对这条手臂，最轻方向的等效质量在各种姿态下都在 0.45 到 0.5 kg 之间，而最重方向在手肘弯成直角附近约 2.2 kg，接近伸直或折叠时超过 20 kg。所以"把手爪变成单位质量"让运动整齐了，环境感受到的刚度却恰恰继承了 \(\Lambda\) 的不均匀。下图中拖动琥珀色的推力手柄，两条手臂在同一个推力下各自偏移。

    Jacobian transpose gives an isotropic spring of stiffness \(\bar m\omega_n^2\). Operational-space control, however, presents the environment with the stiffness \(\omega_n^2\Lambda(\mathbf q)\): soft in light directions, stiff in heavy ones, and changing with posture. For this arm the lightest apparent mass stays between 0.45 and 0.5 kg in every posture, while the heaviest is about 2.2 kg with the elbow near a right angle and exceeds 20 kg near the stretched or folded postures. So "turning the gripper into a unit mass" tidies up the motion, but the stiffness felt by the environment inherits exactly the unevenness of \(\Lambda\). In the figure, drag the amber push handle: both arms deflect under the same push.

    冗余臂：动力学一致的零空间Redundant arms: the dynamically consistent null space

    三连杆平面臂控制手爪的 2 维位置，还多出 1 个自由度：手爪不动时，手肘仍然可以摆（1.6 节的零空间，7.4 节叫它自运动）。我们想用这份自由做第二个任务，比如让第一根连杆朝向角度 \(\psi\)：关节力矩 \(\boldsymbol\tau_0=k_\psi(\psi-\theta_1)\,\mathbf e_1-k_d\dot{\mathbf q}\)（\(\mathbf e_1\) 是第一个单位向量，只拉关节 1；阻尼作用在所有关节上）。但 \(\boldsymbol\tau_0\) 不能直接加上去，它会推动手爪。要先用一个矩阵 \(N^\top\) 过滤：\(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top\mathbf F+N^\top\boldsymbol\tau_0\)。过滤后的力矩不得改变手爪加速度，即 \(JM^{-1}N^\top=0\)。

    3.4 节和 7.4 节在速度层面用过伪逆投影 \(I-J^+J\)。把它原样搬到力矩上是一个常见的错误：\(JM^{-1}(I-J^+J)\) 一般不为零。正确的选择是用 8.3 节误区 5 提到的动力学一致广义逆 \(\bar J=M^{-1}J^\top\Lambda\)：

    A three-link planar arm controlling the 2-D tip position has one degree of freedom to spare: with the tip still, the elbow can still swing (the null space of Lesson 1.6, called self-motion in Lesson 7.4). We want to use that freedom for a second task, say pointing the first link at an angle \(\psi\): joint torques \(\boldsymbol\tau_0=k_\psi(\psi-\theta_1)\,\mathbf e_1-k_d\dot{\mathbf q}\) (\(\mathbf e_1\) is the first unit vector, so only joint 1 is pulled; the damping acts on every joint). But \(\boldsymbol\tau_0\) cannot simply be added: it would push the tip. It must first be filtered by a matrix \(N^\top\): \(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top\mathbf F+N^\top\boldsymbol\tau_0\). The filtered torque must not change the tip acceleration, i.e. \(JM^{-1}N^\top=0\).

    Lessons 3.4 and 7.4 used the pseudo-inverse projector \(I-J^+J\) at the velocity level. Carrying it over unchanged to torques is a common mistake: \(JM^{-1}(I-J^+J)\) is not zero in general. The right choice uses the dynamically consistent generalized inverse \(\bar J=M^{-1}J^\top\Lambda\) mentioned in Pitfall 5 of Lesson 8.3:

    $$ \hl{N}{N^{\top}=I-J^{\top}\bar J^{\top}},\qquad \bar J=M^{-1}J^{\top}\Lambda, $$ $$ JM^{-1}N^{\top}=JM^{-1}-\underbrace{JM^{-1}J^{\top}}_{\Lambda^{-1}}\Lambda JM^{-1}=0 . $$

    一个概念，三个视角One idea, three views

    代数视角：\(\bar J\) 是用 \(M\) 加权的伪逆，它满足 \(J\bar J=I\)，在所有满足 \(J\dot{\mathbf q}=\dot{\mathbf x}\) 的关节速度里，\(\bar J\dot{\mathbf x}\) 的动能最小（8.3 节）。力学视角：速度和力矩是对偶的两类量（3.4 节的 \(\dot{\mathbf x}=J\dot{\mathbf q}\) 与 \(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top\mathbf F\)）。"不影响手爪"对速度说的是 \(J\dot{\mathbf q}=0\)，对力矩说的却是"不产生手爪加速度" \(JM^{-1}\boldsymbol\tau=0\)，中间隔着 \(M^{-1}\)，所以投影要按 \(M\) 加权。只有当 \(M\) 与单位阵成比例时，两种投影才一样。层级视角：\(N^\top\) 给出一个严格的优先级，主任务永远不受第二任务干扰，第二任务只能在剩下的自由里尽力而为。多层任务的层级就是 13.7 节全身控制的内容。

    Algebraic view: \(\bar J\) is the pseudo-inverse weighted by \(M\); it satisfies \(J\bar J=I\), and among all joint velocities with \(J\dot{\mathbf q}=\dot{\mathbf x}\) the one \(\bar J\dot{\mathbf x}\) has the least kinetic energy (Lesson 8.3). Mechanical view: velocities and torques are dual quantities (Lesson 3.4's \(\dot{\mathbf x}=J\dot{\mathbf q}\) and \(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top\mathbf F\)). For velocities "leave the tip alone" means \(J\dot{\mathbf q}=0\); for torques it means "create no tip acceleration", \(JM^{-1}\boldsymbol\tau=0\), with an \(M^{-1}\) in between, so the projector has to be weighted by \(M\). Only when \(M\) is proportional to the identity do the two projectors agree. Hierarchy view: \(N^\top\) sets a strict priority: the main task is never disturbed by the second, and the second does its best with the freedom left over. Hierarchies of many tasks are the subject of the whole-body control of Lesson 13.7.

    Take a fixed target \(\mathbf x_d\), symmetric positive definite \(K_p,K_d\), the law \(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top(K_p\mathbf e-K_d\dot{\mathbf x})+\hat{\mathbf g}\) (Takegaki and Arimoto, 1981), and exact gravity compensation.     The energy never increases, and no value of \(M\) was used, so the result is robust to mass errors and unknown payloads. If the arm comes to rest for good, then \(\boldsymbol\tau-\mathbf g=J^\top K_p\mathbf e=0\); when \(J\) has full row rank this forces \(\mathbf e=0\), and LaSalle's invariance principle gives convergence to the target (at a singular posture \(J^\top\) has a null space and the arm may stop with \(\mathbf e\ne0\)).     Let \(n>m\) and \(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top\mathbf F+N^\top\boldsymbol\tau_0\). The tip acceleration is \(\ddot{\mathbf x}=JM^{-1}(\boldsymbol\tau-\mathbf b)+\dot J\dot{\mathbf q}\), so \(\boldsymbol\tau_0\) contributes \(JM^{-1}N^\top\boldsymbol\tau_0\). We require this to vanish for every \(\boldsymbol\tau_0\):

    $$ JM^{-1}N^{\top}=0 . $$

    设 \(N^\top=I-J^\top G^\top\)，\(G\) 是某个满足 \(JG=I\) 的广义逆。条件变成 \(JM^{-1}=JM^{-1}J^\top G^\top=\Lambda^{-1}G^\top\)，即 \(G^\top=\Lambda JM^{-1}\)，\(G=M^{-1}J^\top\Lambda=\bar J\)。所以动力学一致的广义逆是唯一的（Khatib 1987）。伪逆对应 \(G=J^+=J^\top(JJ^\top)^{-1}\)，代入得 \(JM^{-1}(I-J^+J)\)，一般不为零：在 FIG 4 里它让手爪误差升到厘米量级。另外注意 \(N^\top\) 是投影：\((N^\top)^2=N^\top\)，它把 \(\boldsymbol\tau_0\) 中"会推手爪"的部分剔除，剩下的部分只改变自运动。

    Write \(N^\top=I-J^\top G^\top\) with some generalized inverse \(G\), \(JG=I\). The condition becomes \(JM^{-1}=JM^{-1}J^\top G^\top=\Lambda^{-1}G^\top\), i.e. \(G^\top=\Lambda JM^{-1}\) and \(G=M^{-1}J^\top\Lambda=\bar J\). So the dynamically consistent generalized inverse is unique (Khatib, 1987). The pseudo-inverse corresponds to \(G=J^+=J^\top(JJ^\top)^{-1}\), which gives \(JM^{-1}(I-J^+J)\), generally non-zero: in FIG 4 it drives the tip error to centimetres. Note also that \(N^\top\) is a projector, \((N^\top)^2=N^\top\): it strips from \(\boldsymbol\tau_0\) the part that would push the tip, and what remains only changes the self-motion.

    奇异位形与封顶的 \(\Lambda\)Singularities and a capped \(\Lambda\)

    设 \(J\) 的最小奇异值是 \(\sigma\)，相应的手爪方向是 \(\mathbf u\)。\(\Lambda^{-1}=JM^{-1}J^\top\) 在 \(\mathbf u\) 方向上大致正比于 \(\sigma^2\)，于是 \(m(\mathbf u)\propto1/\sigma^2\)，而 \(J^\top\) 在这个方向上只有 \(\sigma\) 的放大倍数，所以让手爪沿 \(\mathbf u\) 获得加速度 \(a\) 所需的力矩

    Let \(\sigma\) be the smallest singular value of \(J\) and \(\mathbf u\) the corresponding tip direction. Along \(\mathbf u\), \(\Lambda^{-1}=JM^{-1}J^\top\) is roughly proportional to \(\sigma^2\), so \(m(\mathbf u)\propto1/\sigma^2\), while \(J^\top\) only has a gain of \(\sigma\) in that direction. The torque needed to give the tip an acceleration \(a\) along \(\mathbf u\) is therefore

    $$ \|\boldsymbol\tau\|\;\sim\;\sigma\cdot\frac{1}{\sigma^{2}}\cdot a=\frac{a}{\sigma}\;\xrightarrow{\ \sigma\to0\ }\;\infty,\qquad \hl{cap}{\Lambda_\varepsilon=\big(\Lambda^{-1}+\varepsilon I\big)^{-1}},\qquad m_\varepsilon(\mathbf u)\le\frac1\varepsilon . $$

    FIG 5 里目标越过可达边界时，\(\mathbf e\) 不会归零，控制器一直要求沿伸直方向加速，于是力矩发散。补救办法有三类：(1) 封顶：用 \(\Lambda_\varepsilon\) 代替 \(\Lambda\)，把等效质量限制在 \(m_{\max}=1/\varepsilon\) 以下，这和 3.4 节的阻尼最小二乘是同一个思路，代价是在被封顶的方向上不再精确得到 \(\mathbf a\)；(2) 降维：对 \(\Lambda^{-1}\) 做特征分解，奇异值低于阈值的方向从任务里拿掉，改用雅可比转置或关节空间控制；(3) 躲开：用规划、关节限位和零空间任务让手臂远离奇异位形。实际系统通常三者并用。

    In FIG 5, when the target passes the edge of the workspace, \(\mathbf e\) cannot vanish, the controller keeps demanding acceleration along the stretched direction, and the torque diverges. There are three families of remedies: (1) cap: use \(\Lambda_\varepsilon\) instead of \(\Lambda\), limiting the apparent mass to \(m_{\max}=1/\varepsilon\), the same idea as the damped least squares of Lesson 3.4, at the cost of no longer getting \(\mathbf a\) exactly in the capped direction; (2) drop dimensions: eigen-decompose \(\Lambda^{-1}\), remove the directions whose singular values fall below a threshold from the task, and control them with Jacobian transpose or in joint space instead; (3) stay away: use planning, joint limits and null-space tasks to keep the arm clear of singular postures. Real systems usually combine all three.

    六维任务与姿态Six-dimensional tasks and orientation

    真实手臂的任务通常是手爪的完整位姿，\(J\) 是 7.4 节的 \(6\times n\) 雅可比。姿态误差不能用欧拉角相减，常用 5.6 节的对数映射，例如 \(\mathbf e_\omega=\Log(R_dR^\top)\)（以基座系表示的转角向量），并且要与 \(J\) 的角速度行用同一个坐标系。6 维的 \(\Lambda\) 把 kg 和 kg·m² 混在一起（8.3 节误区 5），所以转动和平移的增益要分开设计；把姿态误差放大一个特征长度再与位置误差比较，也是常见做法。

    On a real arm the task is usually the full gripper pose and \(J\) is the \(6\times n\) Jacobian of Lesson 7.4. Orientation errors cannot be formed by subtracting Euler angles; the usual choice is the logarithm map of Lesson 5.6, e.g. \(\mathbf e_\omega=\Log(R_dR^\top)\) (a rotation vector expressed in the base frame), and it must use the same frame as the angular rows of \(J\). A 6-D \(\Lambda\) mixes kg with kg·m² (Pitfall 5 of Lesson 8.3), so rotational and translational gains are designed separately; scaling the orientation error by a characteristic length before comparing it with the position error is another common practice.

    One of the most common "accidents" in real tasks is sending the gripper to a point just out of reach: the vision estimate is off, or a person pulls the arm outward. The controller is operational-space control with feedforward (\(\omega_n=8\) rad/s), with an optional cap on the apparent mass.

    真实系统里In real systems

    力矩控制的手臂：Franka 的 libfranka 以 1 kHz 接受关节力矩指令，franka::Model 给出质量矩阵、科氏力、重力和雅可比，KUKA LBR iiwa 每个关节都有力矩传感器并提供笛卡尔阻抗模式，DLR 的轻型臂（iiwa 的前身）是这类控制的经典平台（Albu-Schäffer、Ott 等）。值得注意的是，franka_ros 自带的笛卡尔阻抗示例控制器用的是 \(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top(-K\tilde{\mathbf x}-D\dot{\mathbf x})+\)科氏补偿，零空间用 \(J^\top\) 的伪逆投影，也就是本节的"雅可比转置加科氏补偿"，而不是完整的操作空间控制。这和 FIG 3 的结论一致：接触任务里，可预测的刚度往往比笔直的路径更重要。学习里的动作空间：robosuite 给机械臂的默认控制器（例如 Panda 的默认配置）是 OSC_POSE，它让策略输出手爪的位姿增量，由操作空间控制换成力矩；Martín-Martín 等（2019）比较了多种动作空间，发现在接触丰富的任务上，末端空间的可变阻抗控制让强化学习学得更好。人形与全身控制：Khatib 组把操作空间控制推广到带优先级的全身任务（Sentis、Khatib 2005），今天的全身控制器多半把同样的思想写成二次规划（13.7 节）。实践上的经验：Nakanishi 等（2008）在统一的记号下整理了速度、加速度和力三个层面的多种操作空间控制律，并在一条 7 自由度的拟人手臂上做了比较：在模型永远不完美的真机上，一种简化的、基于加速度的做法在任务精度、调参难度和鲁棒性上反而更有优势，而准确、物理上自洽的动力学参数估计是这些实验成功的关键；负载和末端工具的参数必须辨识（8.3 节 FIG 5），Franka 和 iiwa 都要求在软件里设置负载质量与质心。

    Torque-controlled arms: Franka's libfranka accepts joint torque commands at 1 kHz and its franka::Model provides the mass matrix, Coriolis terms, gravity and Jacobians; the KUKA LBR iiwa has a torque sensor in every joint and offers a Cartesian impedance mode; the DLR lightweight arm (the iiwa's ancestor) is the classic platform for this kind of control (Albu-Schäffer, Ott and colleagues). Notably, the Cartesian impedance example controller shipped with franka_ros uses \(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top(-K\tilde{\mathbf x}-D\dot{\mathbf x})\) plus Coriolis compensation and a null-space projector built from the pseudo-inverse of \(J^\top\): that is this lesson's "Jacobian transpose with Coriolis compensation", not full operational-space control. That fits FIG 3's lesson: in contact tasks a predictable stiffness often matters more than a straight path. Action spaces for learning: robosuite's default arm controller (in the default configuration of the Panda, for example) is OSC_POSE, which lets a policy output gripper pose increments that operational-space control turns into torques; Martín-Martín et al. (2019) compared action spaces and found that variable impedance control in end-effector space helps reinforcement learning on contact-rich tasks. Humanoids and whole-body control: Khatib's group extended operational-space control to prioritized whole-body tasks (Sentis and Khatib, 2005), and today's whole-body controllers mostly write the same ideas as quadratic programs (Lesson 13.7). Practical experience: Nakanishi et al. (2008) put many operational-space control laws, at the velocity, acceleration and force levels, into one notation and compared them on a seven-degree-of-freedom anthropomorphic arm: on hardware, where the model is never perfect, a simplified acceleration-based variant came out ahead in task performance, ease of tuning and robustness, and accurate, physically consistent estimates of the dynamic parameters were crucial to the experiments; payload and tool parameters have to be identified (FIG 5 of Lesson 8.3), and both Franka and iiwa require the payload mass and centre of mass to be set in software.

    7常见误区Common pitfalls

    误区 1：用速度层面的伪逆投影过滤力矩Pitfall 1: filtering torques with the velocity-level pseudo-inverse projector

    \(I-J^+J\) 在速度层面是对的（\(J(I-J^+J)\dot{\mathbf z}=0\)），但力矩要经过 \(M^{-1}\) 才变成加速度，\(JM^{-1}(I-J^+J)\ne0\)。FIG 4 里同样的姿态任务，用伪逆投影时手爪误差达到厘米量级，用 \(N^\top=I-J^\top\bar J^\top\) 时只有约 0.01 mm（仿真的离散误差）。如果主任务刚度很高，泄漏表现为看起来"莫名其妙"的小误差和抖动；如果刚度低，就是明显的偏移。直觉在哪里失灵？"零空间"这个词让人以为只有一个，其实"对谁为零"取决于度量：速度用欧氏度量，力矩要用 \(M\) 加权。

    \(I-J^+J\) is right at the velocity level (\(J(I-J^+J)\dot{\mathbf z}=0\)), but torques pass through \(M^{-1}\) before they become accelerations, and \(JM^{-1}(I-J^+J)\ne0\). In FIG 4, the same posture task drives the tip error to centimetres with the pseudo-inverse projector and to only about 0.01 mm (the simulation's discretization error) with \(N^\top=I-J^\top\bar J^\top\). With a stiff main task the leak shows up as small, "unexplained" errors and jitter; with a soft one it is an obvious offset. Where does intuition fail? The phrase "the null space" suggests there is only one, but "null for what" depends on the metric: velocities use the Euclidean one, torques must be weighted by \(M\).

    误区 2：直接对 \(JM^{-1}J^\top\) 求逆Pitfall 2: inverting \(JM^{-1}J^\top\) blindly

    在奇异位形附近 \(\Lambda\) 沿丢失的方向趋于无穷，力矩按 \(1/\sigma\) 增长。FIG 5 里目标只比可达边界远 2.5 cm（\(x_{\max}=1.8\) m），不封顶的控制器在 1.8 s 时就发散。目标并不需要"明显"够不着：视觉误差、人拉手臂、轨迹规划的一点超调都可能把手臂推到伸直。代码里要么封顶 \(\Lambda\)，要么检查 \(\sigma_{\min}(J)\) 并降维，并且始终保留力矩限幅。手臂完全折叠（\(\theta_2=\pi\)）是另一个奇异位形，FIG 2 因此禁止经过基座附近的直线。

    Near a singular posture \(\Lambda\) tends to infinity along the lost direction and the torque grows like \(1/\sigma\). In FIG 5 the target is only 2.5 cm beyond the reach limit (\(x_{\max}=1.8\) m) and the uncapped controller diverges at 1.8 s. The target does not need to be "obviously" out of reach: a vision error, a person pulling the arm, or a slight overshoot of a planned trajectory can all stretch the arm straight. In code, either cap \(\Lambda\) or check \(\sigma_{\min}(J)\) and drop dimensions, and always keep torque limits. The fully folded arm (\(\theta_2=\pi\)) is another singular posture, which is why FIG 2 forbids straight lines passing near the base.

    误区 3：以为操作空间控制让手爪"各向同性地柔顺"Pitfall 3: believing operational-space control makes the gripper "isotropically compliant"

    \(\ddot{\mathbf e}+K_d\dot{\mathbf e}+K_p\mathbf e=0\) 描述的是没有外力时的运动。有外力时，\(\ddot{\mathbf e}+K_d\dot{\mathbf e}+K_p\mathbf e=-\Lambda^{-1}\mathbf F_{\text{ext}}\)，环境感受到的刚度是 \(\Lambda K_p\)，随方向和姿态变化（FIG 3）。要在接触时得到指定的刚度，应该按 13.5 节的阻抗控制，直接以 N/m 设计 \(K\)；要同时改变表观惯量，则需要力传感器。

    \(\ddot{\mathbf e}+K_d\dot{\mathbf e}+K_p\mathbf e=0\) describes motion without external forces. With one, \(\ddot{\mathbf e}+K_d\dot{\mathbf e}+K_p\mathbf e=-\Lambda^{-1}\mathbf F_{\text{ext}}\), and the stiffness felt by the environment is \(\Lambda K_p\), which changes with direction and posture (FIG 3). To get a specified stiffness in contact, design \(K\) directly in N/m as in the impedance control of Lesson 13.5; changing the apparent inertia as well requires a force sensor.

    误区 4：漏掉 \(\boldsymbol\mu\)，或者漏掉 \(\dot J\dot{\mathbf q}\)Pitfall 4: leaving out \(\boldsymbol\mu\), or leaving out \(\dot J\dot{\mathbf q}\)

    慢速运动时 \(\boldsymbol\mu\) 很小，省掉它看起来没问题；但 \(\boldsymbol\mu\) 随速度平方增长，快速运动时漂移 \(\mathbf d\) 可以和想要的加速度一样大（FIG 1 第二个任务）。另一个常见的错误是只补偿了关节空间的科氏力 \(C\dot{\mathbf q}\)，却忘了 \(\dot J\dot{\mathbf q}\)：即使关节匀速转动（\(\ddot{\mathbf q}=0\)），手爪也在走圆弧，有向心加速度 \(\dot J\dot{\mathbf q}\)，而这一项并不包含在 \(C\dot{\mathbf q}\) 里。一个诱人的捷径是 \(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top\Lambda\mathbf a+\mathbf b\)：代入可得 \(\ddot{\mathbf x}=\mathbf a+\dot J\dot{\mathbf q}\)，它漏掉的正是 \(-J^\top\Lambda\dot J\dot{\mathbf q}\) 这一项。

    At low speed \(\boldsymbol\mu\) is small and dropping it seems harmless; but \(\boldsymbol\mu\) grows with the square of the speed, and in fast motion the drift \(\mathbf d\) can be as large as the wanted acceleration (FIG 1's second task). Another common mistake is compensating only the joint-space Coriolis terms \(C\dot{\mathbf q}\) and forgetting \(\dot J\dot{\mathbf q}\): even with the joints turning at constant rates (\(\ddot{\mathbf q}=0\)) the gripper travels on an arc and has the centripetal acceleration \(\dot J\dot{\mathbf q}\), which is not part of \(C\dot{\mathbf q}\). A tempting shortcut is \(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top\Lambda\mathbf a+\mathbf b\): substituting gives \(\ddot{\mathbf x}=\mathbf a+\dot J\dot{\mathbf q}\), so what it leaves out is precisely the term \(-J^\top\Lambda\dot J\dot{\mathbf q}\).

    误区 5：以为操作空间控制总比雅可比转置好Pitfall 5: assuming operational space always beats Jacobian transpose

    操作空间控制把模型当成真理：FIG 2 里一个 1 kg 的未建模负载就能让它冲过目标十几厘米，而雅可比转置几乎不受影响，因为它的稳定性证明根本不用 \(M\)。真实手臂上还有关节摩擦、电机转子惯量、关节弹性、通信延迟，这些都会让 \(\hat\Lambda\) 偏离 \(\Lambda\)。选择取决于任务：自由空间里要走得准、走得直，用操作空间控制并认真辨识模型；接触为主、模型不可靠时，雅可比转置加阻抗设计往往更稳妥。

    Operational-space control treats the model as the truth: in FIG 2 a 1 kg unmodelled payload makes it overshoot the target by more than ten centimetres, while Jacobian transpose is hardly affected, because its stability proof never uses \(M\). Real arms add joint friction, motor rotor inertia, joint elasticity and communication delay, all of which pull \(\hat\Lambda\) away from \(\Lambda\). The choice depends on the task: for accurate, straight motion in free space, use operational space and identify the model carefully; when contact dominates and the model is unreliable, Jacobian transpose with an impedance design is often the safer choice.

    8小练习Check yourself

    1. 模型准确时，操作空间控制 \(\mathbf F=\Lambda(K_p\mathbf e-K_d\dot{\mathbf x})+\boldsymbol\mu\)（\(K_p=\omega_n^2I\)，\(K_d=2\omega_nI\)，固定目标）让手爪从静止出发走直线。根本原因是什么？1. With an exact model, operational-space control \(\mathbf F=\Lambda(K_p\mathbf e-K_d\dot{\mathbf x})+\boldsymbol\mu\) (\(K_p=\omega_n^2I\), \(K_d=2\omega_nI\), fixed target) moves the gripper in a straight line from rest. What is the underlying reason?
    \(\Lambda\) 沿路径在变（A 错），控制器每一步都用当前的 \(\Lambda(\mathbf q)\) 和 \(\boldsymbol\mu\) 把它抵消掉，剩下 \(\ddot{\mathbf x}=\mathbf a\)。各向同性、解耦的线性系统从静止出发，解是 \(\mathbf e(t)=(1+\omega_nt)e^{-\omega_nt}\mathbf e(0)\)，方向不变。\(J^\top\) 本身并不保持方向（B 错，这正是雅可比转置控制路径弯曲的原因），重力只是另一个被 \(\hat{\mathbf p}\) 抵消的项（D 错）。\(\Lambda\) does change along the path (A is wrong); the controller cancels it at every step with the current \(\Lambda(\mathbf q)\) and \(\boldsymbol\mu\), leaving \(\ddot{\mathbf x}=\mathbf a\). An isotropic, decoupled linear system started from rest has the solution \(\mathbf e(t)=(1+\omega_nt)e^{-\omega_nt}\mathbf e(0)\), whose direction never changes. \(J^\top\) itself does not preserve direction (B is wrong; that is exactly why Jacobian-transpose paths bend), and gravity is just another term cancelled by \(\hat{\mathbf p}\) (D is wrong).
    2. 关于雅可比转置控制 \(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top(K_p\mathbf e-K_d\dot{\mathbf x})+\hat{\mathbf g}\)，下列哪个说法正确？2. Which statement about Jacobian-transpose control \(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top(K_p\mathbf e-K_d\dot{\mathbf x})+\hat{\mathbf g}\) is correct?
    \(\dot V=-\dot{\mathbf x}^\top K_d\dot{\mathbf x}\le0\) 的推导只用到斜对称性和 \(\dot{\mathbf e}=-J\dot{\mathbf q}\)，不用 \(M\) 的数值，所以 A 错。在 \(\Lambda\) 的特征方向上，自然频率是 \(\sqrt{k_p/m(\mathbf u)}\)，随方向变化，所以 C 错。在奇异位形处 \(J^\top\) 有零空间，\(J^\top K_p\mathbf e=0\) 不再推出 \(\mathbf e=0\)，所以 D 错。The derivation of \(\dot V=-\dot{\mathbf x}^\top K_d\dot{\mathbf x}\le0\) uses only skew-symmetry and \(\dot{\mathbf e}=-J\dot{\mathbf q}\), never the values of \(M\), so A is wrong. Along an eigen-direction of \(\Lambda\) the natural frequency is \(\sqrt{k_p/m(\mathbf u)}\), which varies with direction, so C is wrong. At a singular posture \(J^\top\) has a null space and \(J^\top K_p\mathbf e=0\) no longer implies \(\mathbf e=0\), so D is wrong.
    3. 一条 7 关节手臂用操作空间控制管理手爪位姿，同时想用多余的自由度让手肘远离障碍，手肘的力矩是 \(\boldsymbol\tau_0\)。怎样加入 \(\boldsymbol\tau_0\) 才不会干扰手爪？3. A 7-joint arm controls its gripper pose with operational-space control and wants to use the spare freedom to keep the elbow away from an obstacle, with elbow torque \(\boldsymbol\tau_0\). How should \(\boldsymbol\tau_0\) be added so that it does not disturb the gripper?
    "不干扰手爪"的意思是 \(\boldsymbol\tau_0\) 不产生手爪加速度：\(JM^{-1}N^\top=0\)。只有 \(N^\top=I-J^\top\bar J^\top\) 对任何 \(\boldsymbol\tau_0\) 都满足它（选项 D）。伪逆投影（选项 B）在速度层面正确，但 \(JM^{-1}(I-J^+J)\ne0\)，FIG 4 里它把手爪误差推到厘米量级。直接相加（选项 A）误差更大，缩小（选项 C）只是减小泄漏，并没有消除。"Not disturbing the gripper" means \(\boldsymbol\tau_0\) creates no tip acceleration: \(JM^{-1}N^\top=0\). Only \(N^\top=I-J^\top\bar J^\top\) satisfies it for every \(\boldsymbol\tau_0\) (option D). The pseudo-inverse projector (option B) is right at the velocity level, but \(JM^{-1}(I-J^+J)\ne0\), and in FIG 4 it pushes the tip error to centimetres. Adding directly (option A) is even worse, and scaling down (option C) only shrinks the leak without removing it.
    4. 手臂接近伸直时，\(J\) 的最小奇异值 \(\sigma\) 减小到原来的一半。要让手爪沿这个"难动"的方向得到同样的加速度，操作空间控制需要的关节力矩大约怎样变化？4. As the arm approaches the stretched posture, the smallest singular value \(\sigma\) of \(J\) halves. To give the gripper the same acceleration along that "hard" direction, roughly how does the joint torque required by operational-space control change?
    需要的手爪力是 \(m(\mathbf u)a\)，\(m(\mathbf u)\propto1/\sigma^2\) 变成 4 倍（B 只算了这一半）；这个力经 \(J^\top\) 换成力矩时又乘以 \(\sigma\)（缩小一半），所以 \(\|\boldsymbol\tau\|\propto a/\sigma\) 大约变成 2 倍。\(M\) 确实没变（C 错），但问题出在 \(J\)。\(\sigma\to0\) 时力矩发散，这就是 FIG 5 里不封顶时发生的事。The required tip force is \(m(\mathbf u)a\), and \(m(\mathbf u)\propto1/\sigma^2\) quadruples (B stops at this half); turning that force into torque through \(J^\top\) multiplies by \(\sigma\) (halving it), so \(\|\boldsymbol\tau\|\propto a/\sigma\) roughly doubles. \(M\) has indeed not changed (C is wrong), but the problem lies in \(J\). As \(\sigma\to0\) the torque diverges, which is what happens in FIG 5 without a cap.
    5. 对一条非冗余、非奇异的手臂（\(J\) 是可逆方阵），操作空间控制 \(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top(\Lambda\mathbf a+\boldsymbol\mu+\mathbf p)\) 与下面哪个控制律完全相同？5. For a non-redundant arm away from singularities (\(J\) square and invertible), operational-space control \(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top(\Lambda\mathbf a+\boldsymbol\mu+\mathbf p)\) is identical to which law?
    \(J\) 可逆时 \(J^\top\Lambda J=M\)，展开得 \(\boldsymbol\tau=MJ^{-1}(\mathbf a-\dot J\dot{\mathbf q})+\mathbf b\)，即分解加速度控制（Luh、Walker、Paul 1980）。两者的区别只在冗余、多任务和奇异位形处理上显现。When \(J\) is invertible, \(J^\top\Lambda J=M\), and expanding gives \(\boldsymbol\tau=MJ^{-1}(\mathbf a-\dot J\dot{\mathbf q})+\mathbf b\): resolved-acceleration control (Luh, Walker and Paul, 1980). The two differ only once redundancy, multiple tasks or singularity handling come in.
