模型预测控制 MPCModel Predictive Control

模型预测控制在每个控制周期里用模型预测未来几秒，在力矩、速度、障碍等约束下解一个优化问题，只执行第一步，下个周期再重新求解。这一节讲清楚它为什么是反馈、时域之外该放什么（LQR 的终端代价）、它和"把 LQR 输出截断"有什么不同、它在什么时候会把自己逼进死胡同（递归可行性），以及怎样在几毫秒内把它解完：稠密与稀疏 QP、热启动，再到非线性 MPC 与 MPPI 在自行车模型上的路径跟踪。 Model predictive control uses a model to predict the next few seconds at every control cycle, solves an optimization problem under torque, speed and obstacle constraints, applies only the first step, and solves again at the next cycle. This lesson explains why that is feedback, what belongs beyond the horizon (the LQR terminal cost), how it differs from clipping an LQR output, when it drives itself into a dead end (recursive feasibility), and how to solve it in a few milliseconds: condensed versus sparse QPs, warm starts, and finally nonlinear MPC and MPPI tracking a path with a bicycle model.

    1为什么机器人需要它Why robots need this

    13.3 节的 LQR 给出一个漂亮的答案：\(\mathbf u=-K\mathbf x\)，一次矩阵乘法，在它的假设下是最优的。可是它的假设里没有"不能"：没有力矩上限，没有关节限位，没有速度上限，没有障碍物，也没有"足底力必须在摩擦锥里"。真实机器人处处是"不能"：

    • 执行器饱和：电机力矩、电流、舵机转速都有上限。LQR 算出 50 N·m，电机只能给 20 N·m，截断之后 LQR 的最优性和稳定性证明都不再成立（第 3 节）。
    • 状态约束：关节限位、速度上限、车道边界、与人保持的距离。这些是"永远不许违反"的，而不是代价里的一项。
    • 预瞄：前方 20 米有个急弯，或者参考轨迹在 2 秒后会突然变化。一个只看当前误差的反馈律，等误差出现才反应，已经晚了。
    • 接触：四足机器人的足底只能推、不能拉，摩擦力不能超过 \(\mu f_z\)。这些不等式决定了身体能怎样加速。

    模型预测控制（Model Predictive Control，MPC）的想法很直接：既然有模型，就在线解一个有限时域的最优控制问题，把所有约束都写进去；只执行第一步，下个周期用新测到的状态再解一次。6.8 节已经看到，线性模型加二次代价加线性约束，这个问题就是一个 QP，并用一辆一维小车演示了"碰到约束就饱和"。这一节回答 6.8 节没回答的问题：为什么"只执行第一步再重解"是反馈；时域之外的未来该怎么算（终端代价，与 LQR 的精确关系）；它与"把 LQR 截断"差在哪里；它什么时候会把自己逼进无解的状态（递归可行性）；以及怎样在几毫秒内解完（稠密与稀疏的 QP、热启动、实时迭代）。最后把它用到非线性的自行车模型上，并和 6.11 节的采样式 MPC（MPPI）对比。

    The LQR of Lesson 13.3 gives a beautiful answer: \(\mathbf u=-K\mathbf x\), one matrix product, optimal under its assumptions. But its assumptions contain no "cannot": no torque limit, no joint limit, no speed limit, no obstacle, no "foot forces must stay in the friction cone". Real robots are full of "cannot":

    • Actuator saturation: motor torque, current and servo speed all have limits. LQR asks for 50 N·m, the motor can give 20 N·m, and once you clip, LQR's optimality and its stability proof no longer apply (Section 3).
    • State constraints: joint limits, speed limits, lane boundaries, a distance to keep from people. These must never be violated; they are not one more term in a cost.
    • Preview: there is a sharp bend 20 m ahead, or the reference will change abruptly in 2 s. A feedback law that only looks at the current error reacts once the error has appeared, which is already too late.
    • Contact: a quadruped's feet can push but not pull, and friction cannot exceed \(\mu f_z\). These inequalities decide how the body can accelerate.

    The idea of model predictive control (MPC) is direct: since you have a model, solve a finite-horizon optimal control problem online with every constraint written in; apply only the first step, and solve again at the next cycle from the newly measured state. Lesson 6.8 already showed that with a linear model, a quadratic cost and linear constraints this problem is a QP, and used a 1D cart to show inputs "saturating the moment a constraint binds". This lesson answers what 6.8 left open: why "apply the first step and re-solve" is feedback; how to account for the future beyond the horizon (the terminal cost, and its exact relation to LQR); how it differs from clipping LQR; when it drives itself into a state with no solution (recursive feasibility); and how to solve it in a few milliseconds (condensed and sparse QPs, warm starts, real-time iterations). Finally we apply it to a nonlinear bicycle model and compare it with the sampling-based MPC (MPPI) of Lesson 6.11.

    2直觉：看得见几秒，就只为这几秒做打算Intuition: plan for the seconds you can see

    夜里开车，车灯照亮前方 50 米。你按这 50 米里看到的东西决定怎么踩油门、打方向，可你不会把这个计划"执行完"：一秒钟后车灯照到了新的路面，你就按新看到的东西重新打算。计划很长，承诺很短：每次只兑现计划的第一小步。

    MPC 每个周期做三件事：

    Driving at night, your headlights light up 50 m of road. You decide throttle and steering from what you see in those 50 m, but you never "finish executing" that plan: a second later the headlights reveal new road and you re-plan from what you now see. Long plans, short commitments: each time you honour only the first small step of the plan.

    At every cycle MPC does three things:

    $$ \begin{aligned}&\text{1. measure } \mathbf x(t)\\ &\text{2. solve } \min_{\mathbf u_0,\dots,\mathbf u_{N-1}} \sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\ell(\mathbf x_k,\mathbf u_k)+V_f(\mathbf x_N)\ \ \text{s.t. model, constraints},\ \mathbf x_0=\mathbf x(t)\\ &\text{3. apply } \hl{u0}{\mathbf u_0^\star}\text{, discard the rest}\end{aligned} $$

    核心洞见Key insight

    MPC 是一个反馈律 \(\mathbf u=\kappa_N(\mathbf x)\)，只不过这个函数不是写成公式，而是"以 \(\mathbf x\) 为初值解一个优化问题，取第一个输入"。反馈来自重新测量、重新求解：模型不准、被踢一脚，下一个周期的计划就从真实的状态出发。开环执行同一个最优计划，模型误差会原封不动地留在结果里。

    MPC is a feedback law \(\mathbf u=\kappa_N(\mathbf x)\), except that the function is not written as a formula but as "solve an optimization problem starting from \(\mathbf x\) and take the first input". The feedback comes from measuring again and solving again: whether the model is off or the cart gets kicked, the next cycle's plan starts from the true state. Executing the same optimal plan open loop leaves every model error in the result.

    3动手玩：时域之外，和约束之内Play: beyond the horizon, and inside the constraints

    时域之外的代价：终端代价 \(P_f\)The cost beyond the horizon: the terminal cost \(P_f\)

    有限时域有一个天生的盲点：第 \(N\) 步以后发生什么，优化器完全不管。对一个不稳定的系统，这可能是致命的。考虑线性化的倒立摆 \(\ddot\theta=10\,\theta+u\)（\(u\) 是角加速度输入，零阶保持离散化，\(\Delta t=0.1\) s），代价 \(\theta^2+0.1\dot\theta^2+0.1u^2\)。如果时域很短又没有终端代价，优化器会想：反正 \(N\) 步之内摆还倒不下去，何必花力气？于是它几乎不出力，摆就真倒了。

    解决办法来自 6.12 节的贝尔曼原理：如果时域结束后剩下的代价能精确算出来，有限时域就等价于无限时域。约束不起作用时，从 \(\mathbf x_N\) 出发的无限时域最优代价正是 LQR 的 \(\mathbf x_N^\top P\mathbf x_N\)（\(P\) 是离散代数 Riccati 方程的解，13.3 节）。把它当终端代价 \(V_f(\mathbf x_N)=\mathbf x_N^\top\hl{Pf}{P_f}\mathbf x_N\)，\(P_f=P\)：

    A finite horizon has a built-in blind spot: whatever happens after step \(N\), the optimizer does not care. For an unstable system this can be fatal. Take the linearized inverted pendulum \(\ddot\theta=10\,\theta+u\) (\(u\) an angular-acceleration input, zero-order-hold discretization, \(\Delta t=0.1\) s) with cost \(\theta^2+0.1\dot\theta^2+0.1u^2\). With a short horizon and no terminal cost the optimizer reasons: the pendulum will not have fallen within \(N\) steps anyway, so why spend effort? It barely pushes, and the pendulum really falls.

    The fix comes from Bellman's principle in Lesson 6.12: if the cost left over after the horizon can be computed exactly, a finite horizon is equivalent to an infinite one. When no constraint is active, the infinite-horizon optimal cost from \(\mathbf x_N\) is exactly LQR's \(\mathbf x_N^\top P\mathbf x_N\) (\(P\) solves the discrete algebraic Riccati equation, Lesson 13.3). Use it as the terminal cost \(V_f(\mathbf x_N)=\mathbf x_N^\top\hl{Pf}{P_f}\mathbf x_N\) with \(P_f=P\):

    核心洞见Key insight

    终端代价是"时域之外的未来"的替身。\(P_f=P\) 时，只要约束不起作用，MPC 就等于 LQR，与 \(N\) 无关：时域只在约束起作用时才需要长。没有终端代价时，时域必须长到足以"看见"不作为的后果；本例需要 \(N\ge8\)（0.8 s），而且 \(N=8\) 时闭环代价仍是 LQR 的约 6 倍。工程上的结论：终端代价几乎总该放 LQR 的 \(P\)，它不花任何在线计算。

    The terminal cost stands in for the future beyond the horizon. With \(P_f=P\), as long as no constraint is active, MPC equals LQR whatever \(N\) is: the horizon only needs to be long where constraints act. Without a terminal cost the horizon must be long enough to "see" the consequence of doing nothing; here that takes \(N\ge8\) (0.8 s), and even at \(N=8\) the closed-loop cost is about 6 times LQR's. The engineering conclusion: the terminal cost should almost always be LQR's \(P\), which costs no online computation at all.

    约束之内：MPC 与"截断的 LQR"Inside the constraints: MPC versus "clipped LQR"

    最省事的处理约束的办法是：照算 LQR，再把输出截到 \([-u_{\max},u_{\max}]\)。它常常够用，但它有两个盲点。第一，LQR 不知道将来会饱和：它以为刹车时能出任意大的减速度，于是放心地加速，等真到了刹车的时候才发现力气不够，只好冲过头（和 13.1 节的积分饱和是同一类病）。第二，它根本看不见状态约束：前面有堵墙，它也照样冲。MPC 把 \(\hl{umax}{|u_k|\le u_{\max}}\) 和 \(p_k\le p_{\rm wall}\) 都写进预测里，所以会提前刹车。

    下图两辆小车从同一点出发去 \(p=0\)，前方有一堵可以拖动的墙。两者用同一个代价（\(Q=\mathrm{diag}(q,0.2)\)，\(R=1\)），MPC 的时域 \(N=30\)（3 s），终端代价 \(P\)。

    The laziest way to handle constraints: compute LQR as usual and clip the output to \([-u_{\max},u_{\max}]\). It often works, but it has two blind spots. First, LQR does not know it will saturate later: it believes it can brake as hard as it likes, so it accelerates confidently and only discovers when braking time comes that it lacks the strength, and overshoots (the same family of illness as integrator windup in Lesson 13.1). Second, it cannot see state constraints at all: a wall ahead changes nothing for it. MPC writes both \(\hl{umax}{|u_k|\le u_{\max}}\) and \(p_k\le p_{\rm wall}\) into the prediction, so it brakes early.

    In the figure two carts leave the same point for \(p=0\), with a draggable wall ahead. Both use the same cost (\(Q=\mathrm{diag}(q,0.2)\), \(R=1\)); MPC has horizon \(N=30\) (3 s) and terminal cost \(P\).

    核心洞见Key insight

    截断的 LQR 只在"此刻"尊重约束，MPC 在"整个预测"里尊重约束。差别出现在现在的选择会让将来的约束起作用的时候：加速太猛，刹车时就会饱和。如果饱和只发生在一开始，之后线性律能接得住，截断恰好就是最优的（上面第二个任务）；可一旦"现在"决定了"将来够不够力"，只有能预测的控制器才算得对。

    Clipped LQR respects the constraints "now"; MPC respects them over the whole prediction. The difference appears when today's choice makes tomorrow's constraint bind: accelerate too hard and braking will saturate. If saturation only happens at the very beginning and the linear law can take over afterwards, clipping happens to be optimal (the second task above); but as soon as "now" decides whether there will be enough strength "later", only a controller that predicts gets it right.

    4第二个视角：MPC 会把自己逼进死胡同A second view: MPC can corner itself

    约束带来一个 LQR 没有的新问题：优化问题可能无解。6.8 节讲过，初始状态本身违反硬约束时 QP 不可行。更隐蔽的是：此刻问题可行，MPC 自己选的动作却把系统带到了一个下一步不可行的状态。这叫失去递归可行性（recursive feasibility）。

    还是那辆小车（\(|u|\le1\)），墙在 \(p=0\)，目标在 \(p=-0.3\)。在相平面 \((p,v)\) 里有两个重要的集合：

    • 可挽救集 \(\hl{Cinf}{\mathcal C_\infty}\)：从这些状态出发，满力刹车能停在墙前。速度越大，需要的刹车距离越长（\(v^2/2u_{\max}\)），边界是一条抛物线。在这个集合外面，任何控制器都救不了。
    • \(N\) 步可行集 \(\hl{XN}{\mathcal X_N}\)：MPC 的 \(N\) 步问题此刻有解的状态。如果 \(N\) 步之内的刹车距离不够把车停下，但 \(N\) 步之内还没撞到墙，问题依然"可行"：撞墙发生在时域之外，优化器看不到。

    \(\mathcal X_N\) 比 \(\mathcal C_\infty\) 大出来的那一条（下图红色）就是陷阱：此刻可行，却已注定撞墙。更糟的是，MPC 可以从安全的绿色区域自己开进红色区域：它为了尽快到达目标而加速，而墙在时域之外。

    Constraints bring a problem LQR never had: the optimization can have no solution. Lesson 6.8 noted that the QP is infeasible when the initial state already violates a hard constraint. More insidious: the problem is feasible now, but the action MPC itself chooses carries the system into a state where the next problem is infeasible. This is losing recursive feasibility.

    Same cart (\(|u|\le1\)), wall at \(p=0\), target at \(p=-0.3\). Two sets in the phase plane \((p,v)\) matter:

    • The safe set \(\hl{Cinf}{\mathcal C_\infty}\): from these states, braking at full strength stops before the wall. The higher the speed, the longer the braking distance (\(v^2/2u_{\max}\)), so its boundary is a parabola. Outside it no controller can save the cart.
    • The \(N\)-step feasible set \(\hl{XN}{\mathcal X_N}\): the states from which MPC's \(N\)-step problem has a solution now. If braking for \(N\) steps is not enough to stop but the cart has not hit the wall within \(N\) steps, the problem is still "feasible": the crash happens beyond the horizon, where the optimizer cannot see.

    The strip by which \(\mathcal X_N\) exceeds \(\mathcal C_\infty\) (red in the figure) is the trap: feasible now, yet doomed. Worse, MPC can drive itself from the safe green region into the red one: it accelerates to reach the target quickly while the wall is beyond its horizon.

    核心洞见Key insight

    递归可行性靠的是终端集：要求计划结束时落在一个"控制不变集" \(\mathcal X_f\) 里，即一个进去以后有办法永远待在里面（且满足约束）的集合。这里 \(\mathcal X_f=\{v\le0\}\)：停下或后退的小车，令 \(u=0\) 就永远不会撞墙。于是上一步的计划去掉第一步、末尾补一步 \(u=0\)，就是下一步的一个可行计划，可行性一步步传下去（第 6 节的证明）。代价是保守：\(N\) 很短时，小车只敢以"\(N\) 步内能停下"的速度前进，慢得像爬。

    Recursive feasibility rests on a terminal set: require the plan to end inside a "control-invariant set" \(\mathcal X_f\), a set that, once entered, can be stayed in forever (while satisfying the constraints). Here \(\mathcal X_f=\{v\le0\}\): a cart that has stopped or is backing off never hits the wall with \(u=0\). Then the previous plan with its first step dropped and one step \(u=0\) appended is a feasible plan for the next problem, and feasibility is passed on step by step (the proof in Section 6). The price is conservatism: with a very short \(N\), the cart only dares move as fast as it can stop within \(N\) steps, and it crawls.

    5第三个视角：几毫秒内解完A third view: solving it within milliseconds

    同一个 QP，两种写法：稠密（缩并）与稀疏One QP, two ways to write it: condensed and sparse

    MPC 的 QP 有两种标准写法，对应 6.13 节的打靶与配点：

    • 稠密（缩并，condensed）：用动力学把所有状态消掉，\(\mathbf X=S_x\mathbf x_0+S_u\mathbf U\)，只剩 \(Nm\) 个输入变量。Hessian \(\hl{H}{H}\) 是 \(Nm\times Nm\) 的稠密矩阵，因为每个状态都依赖之前所有的输入。
    • 稀疏（sparse）：保留所有状态为变量，动力学作为等式约束。变量多了（\(N(n+m)\) 个），但 KKT 矩阵 \(\hl{KKT}{K}\) 是带状的：第 \(k\) 步只和第 \(k\pm1\) 步耦合。用 6.12 节的 Riccati 递推解它，代价随 \(N\) 线性增长。

    哪种更快，取决于状态维数 \(n\)、输入维数 \(m\) 和时域 \(N\)。下图上方两幅是两种矩阵的非零结构，下方是一次分解所需的浮点运算数随 \(N\) 的变化。

    MPC's QP comes in two standard forms, the counterparts of shooting and collocation in Lesson 6.13:

    • Condensed (dense): use the dynamics to eliminate every state, \(\mathbf X=S_x\mathbf x_0+S_u\mathbf U\), leaving only the \(Nm\) inputs as variables. The Hessian \(\hl{H}{H}\) is a dense \(Nm\times Nm\) matrix, because every state depends on all earlier inputs.
    • Sparse: keep every state as a variable and the dynamics as equality constraints. There are more variables (\(N(n+m)\)), but the KKT matrix \(\hl{KKT}{K}\) is banded: step \(k\) couples only to steps \(k\pm1\). Solved with the Riccati recursion of Lesson 6.12, its cost grows linearly in \(N\).

    实时预算与热启动Real-time budgets and warm starts

    控制器每 10 ms 要给出一个输入，可迭代求解器（ADMM、内点法、有效集法）需要的迭代数是不确定的。两条工程经验：

    • 热启动（warm start）：这一周期的最优计划，和上一周期的计划"向前平移一步、末尾补一步"非常接近。从这里出发，而不是从零出发，求解器只需修一修。
    • 迭代预算：给每个周期设一个迭代上限，到点就交出当前的近似解。热启动之后，几步迭代往往已经足够好；冷启动的几步迭代可能离最优解很远。

    下图用 OSQP 式的 ADMM（6.8 节）解小车撞墙问题（\(N=30\)，墙在 0.05 m，\(q=10\)），两辆车同时跑：紫色每个周期从零开始，蓝色从平移后的上一个解开始。柱子是每个周期用掉的迭代数（对数坐标），半透明的柱子表示用完预算仍未收敛。

    The controller must deliver an input every 10 ms, yet iterative solvers (ADMM, interior point, active set) need an uncertain number of iterations. Two engineering habits:

    • Warm starting: this cycle's optimal plan is very close to last cycle's plan "shifted one step forward with one step appended". Starting from there instead of from zero, the solver only has to touch it up.
    • An iteration budget: cap the iterations per cycle and hand over the current approximate solution when time is up. After a warm start a few iterations are often good enough; a few iterations from a cold start may be far from optimal.

    次优也稳定Suboptimal is still stable

    热启动在理论上也站得住：第 6 节的稳定性证明里，下一周期的候选计划正是"平移后的上一计划"，它已经让代价下降。所以只要求解器从这个候选出发、并且不让代价变大、不违反约束，即使远没有收敛，闭环依然稳定（Scokaert、Mayne 与 Rawlings，1999："可行即稳定"）。FIG 6 的 ADMM 并不严格满足这些条件（它的中间迭代可能略微违反约束，代价也不保证单调下降），但道理相同：热启动的起点就是平移后的上一计划，离最优解已经很近，所以几步迭代就够用；冷启动的几步迭代离最优解很远，也没有这种保证。

    Warm starting also stands on theory: in the stability proof of Section 6, the candidate plan for the next cycle is exactly "the previous plan, shifted", and it already decreases the cost. So as long as the solver starts from that candidate, never increases the cost and never violates a constraint, the closed loop stays stable even far from convergence (Scokaert, Mayne & Rawlings, 1999: "feasibility implies stability"). The ADMM of FIG 6 does not strictly meet these conditions (its intermediate iterates can slightly violate constraints, and its cost need not decrease monotonically), but the same reason is at work: the warm start begins at the shifted previous plan, already close to optimal, so a few iterations suffice; a few iterations from a cold start are far from optimal and come with no such guarantee.

    问题The problem

    线性 MPC 在每个采样时刻，以测得的 \(\mathbf x(t)\) 为初值解

    At every sampling instant, linear MPC solves, from the measured \(\mathbf x(t)\),

    $$ \begin{aligned} V_N(\mathbf x)=\min_{\mathbf u_0,\dots,\mathbf u_{N-1}}\ &\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\big(\mathbf x_k^\top Q\mathbf x_k+\mathbf u_k^\top R\mathbf u_k\big)+\mathbf x_N^\top\hl{Pf}{P_f}\mathbf x_N\\ \text{s.t.}\quad &\mathbf x_{k+1}=A\mathbf x_k+B\mathbf u_k,\ \ \mathbf x_0=\mathbf x,\ \ \hl{umax}{\mathbf u_k\in\mathcal U},\ \ \mathbf x_k\in\mathcal X,\ \ \mathbf x_N\in\mathcal X_f, \end{aligned} $$

    并执行 \(\kappa_N(\mathbf x)=\hl{u0}{\mathbf u_0^\star(\mathbf x)}\)。\(\mathcal U,\mathcal X\) 是多面体（线性不等式），\(\mathcal X_f\) 是终端集。跟踪一个参考 \(\mathbf x_{\rm ref}\) 时，把 \(\mathbf x\) 换成 \(\mathbf x-\mathbf x_{\rm ref}\)（参考须是平衡点，或者另加前馈）。

    and applies \(\kappa_N(\mathbf x)=\hl{u0}{\mathbf u_0^\star(\mathbf x)}\). \(\mathcal U,\mathcal X\) are polyhedra (linear inequalities) and \(\mathcal X_f\) is the terminal set. To track a reference \(\mathbf x_{\rm ref}\), replace \(\mathbf x\) by \(\mathbf x-\mathbf x_{\rm ref}\) (the reference must be an equilibrium, or a feedforward is added).

    缩并成稠密 QPCondensing into a dense QP

    把动力学展开：\(\mathbf x_k=A^k\mathbf x_0+\sum_{j\lt k}A^{k-1-j}B\mathbf u_j\)。叠成向量 \(\mathbf X=(\mathbf x_1,\dots,\mathbf x_N)\)、\(\mathbf U=(\mathbf u_0,\dots,\mathbf u_{N-1})\)：

    Unroll the dynamics: \(\mathbf x_k=A^k\mathbf x_0+\sum_{j\lt k}A^{k-1-j}B\mathbf u_j\). Stacking \(\mathbf X=(\mathbf x_1,\dots,\mathbf x_N)\) and \(\mathbf U=(\mathbf u_0,\dots,\mathbf u_{N-1})\):

    $$ \mathbf X=S_x\mathbf x_0+S_u\mathbf U,\quad S_x=\begin{bmatrix}A\\A^2\\ \vdots\\ A^N\end{bmatrix},\quad S_u=\begin{bmatrix}B&0&\cdots&0\\ AB&B&\cdots&0\\ \vdots&&\ddots&\\ A^{N-1}B&A^{N-2}B&\cdots&B\end{bmatrix}, $$ $$ \begin{aligned}&\min_{\mathbf U}\ \tfrac12\mathbf U^\top\hl{H}{H}\mathbf U+(F\mathbf x_0)^\top\mathbf U\quad\text{s.t.}\quad G\mathbf U\le\mathbf w+E\mathbf x_0,\\ &H=2\big(S_u^\top\bar Q S_u+\bar R\big),\qquad F=2S_u^\top\bar QS_x, \end{aligned} $$

    其中 \(\bar Q=\mathrm{diag}(Q,\dots,Q,P_f)\)，\(\bar R=\mathrm{diag}(R,\dots,R)\)。\(H\) 正定（\(R\succ0\) 即可），所以这是一个严格凸 QP，有唯一解。注意 \(\mathbf x_0\) 只出现在线性项和约束右端：\(H\) 与当前状态无关，线性定常系统可以离线算好、甚至离线分解。状态约束 \(\mathbf x_k\in\mathcal X\) 经 \(S_u\) 变成关于 \(\mathbf U\) 的稠密不等式，这就是 6.8 节 FIG 4 里"墙"的那些约束行。

    with \(\bar Q=\mathrm{diag}(Q,\dots,Q,P_f)\) and \(\bar R=\mathrm{diag}(R,\dots,R)\). \(H\) is positive definite (\(R\succ0\) suffices), so this is a strictly convex QP with a unique solution. Note that \(\mathbf x_0\) appears only in the linear term and the right-hand side: \(H\) does not depend on the current state, so for an LTI system it can be computed, and even factored, offline. State constraints \(\mathbf x_k\in\mathcal X\) become dense inequalities in \(\mathbf U\) through \(S_u\): these are the "wall" rows of FIG 4 in Lesson 6.8.

    无约束时就是 LQR。没有约束时 \(\mathbf U^\star=-H^{-1}F\mathbf x_0\)，第一块就是一个线性增益 \(K_N\)。若 \(P_f=P\) 满足 Riccati 方程 \(P=Q+A^\top PA-A^\top PB(R+B^\top PB)^{-1}B^\top PA\)，则 6.12 节的反向递推从 \(P_N=P\) 开始，每一步都得到同一个 \(P\)，于是 \(K_N=K_\infty\) 对所有 \(N\) 成立：这正是 FIG 2 里 \(N=1\) 也与 LQR 相同的原因。

    Without constraints it is LQR. Unconstrained, \(\mathbf U^\star=-H^{-1}F\mathbf x_0\), whose first block is a linear gain \(K_N\). If \(P_f=P\) solves the Riccati equation \(P=Q+A^\top PA-A^\top PB(R+B^\top PB)^{-1}B^\top PA\), the backward recursion of Lesson 6.12 started at \(P_N=P\) returns the same \(P\) at every step, so \(K_N=K_\infty\) for every \(N\): that is why FIG 2 matches LQR even at \(N=1\).

    稀疏形式与 RiccatiThe sparse form and Riccati

    不消元，取 \(\mathbf z=(\mathbf u_0,\mathbf x_1,\mathbf u_1,\dots,\mathbf x_N)\)，动力学写成等式约束 \(\mathbf x_{k+1}-A\mathbf x_k-B\mathbf u_k=0\)，乘子为 \(\boldsymbol\lambda_{k+1}\)。拉格朗日函数的 KKT 条件（6.7 节）是一个线性方程组：

    Without eliminating anything, take \(\mathbf z=(\mathbf u_0,\mathbf x_1,\mathbf u_1,\dots,\mathbf x_N)\) and write the dynamics as equality constraints \(\mathbf x_{k+1}-A\mathbf x_k-B\mathbf u_k=0\) with multipliers \(\boldsymbol\lambda_{k+1}\). The KKT conditions of the Lagrangian (Lesson 6.7) are a linear system:

    $$ \hl{KKT}{\begin{bmatrix}\mathcal H & \mathcal G^\top\\ \mathcal G & 0\end{bmatrix}}\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf z\\ \boldsymbol\lambda\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}0\\ \mathbf b(\mathbf x_0)\end{bmatrix}, $$ $$ \mathcal H=2\,\mathrm{diag}(R,Q,R,Q,\dots,P_f),\quad \mathcal G=\begin{bmatrix}-B & I & & & \\ & -A & -B & I & \\ && & \ddots & \end{bmatrix}. $$

    \(\mathcal H\) 块对角，\(\mathcal G\) 块双对角：重排变量后整个矩阵是带宽约 \(2n+m\) 的带状矩阵。消元的顺序如果从 \(k=N\) 往回做，得到的正是 Riccati 递推：每一步只处理一个 \((n+m)\) 维的小块，总代价 \(O(N(n+m)^3)\)。内点法每次迭代只是给 \(\mathcal H\) 的对角加上约束的权重（第 \(k\) 步的约束只影响第 \(k\) 块），所以结构不变，每次迭代仍是一次 Riccati 递推；HPIPM、FORCES Pro 等 MPC 专用求解器就是这么做的。

    \(\mathcal H\) is block diagonal and \(\mathcal G\) block bidiagonal: after reordering, the whole matrix is banded with bandwidth about \(2n+m\). Eliminating from \(k=N\) backwards yields exactly the Riccati recursion: each step handles one \((n+m)\)-dimensional block, for a total cost \(O(N(n+m)^3)\). An interior-point iteration only adds constraint weights to the diagonal of \(\mathcal H\) (stage-\(k\) constraints touch only block \(k\)), so the structure survives and each iteration is again one Riccati recursion; MPC-specific solvers such as HPIPM and FORCES Pro do exactly this.

    对比：稠密形式一次 Cholesky 是 \((Nm)^3/3\)，对 \(N\) 三次方；稀疏是 \(N\) 的一次方，但每步付出 \(n^3\)。所以 \(Nm\) 小、\(n\) 大（短时域、多状态少输入）时稠密划算，长时域时稀疏划算；介于其间的"部分缩并"（把每几步缩成一块）是很多实时求解器的默认选择。

    Compare: one dense Cholesky costs \((Nm)^3/3\), cubic in \(N\); the sparse form is linear in \(N\) but pays \(n^3\) per step. So the condensed form wins when \(Nm\) is small and \(n\) large (short horizons, many states, few inputs) and the sparse form wins for long horizons; "partial condensing" in between (condense every few steps into one block) is the default of many real-time solvers.

    稳定性：最优代价是李雅普诺夫函数Stability: the optimal cost is a Lyapunov function

    设终端集 \(\mathcal X_f\) 与终端代价满足两个条件（Mayne 等，2000）：在 \(\mathcal X_f\) 里有一个局部控制律 \(\mathbf u=-K_f\mathbf x\)，它满足约束、使 \(\mathcal X_f\) 不变；并且

    Suppose the terminal set \(\mathcal X_f\) and terminal cost satisfy two conditions (Mayne et al., 2000): inside \(\mathcal X_f\) there is a local control law \(\mathbf u=-K_f\mathbf x\) that respects the constraints and keeps \(\mathcal X_f\) invariant; and

    $$ V_f\big((A-BK_f)\mathbf x\big)-V_f(\mathbf x)\le-\big(\mathbf x^\top Q\mathbf x+\mathbf x^\top K_f^\top RK_f\mathbf x\big)\qquad\forall\,\mathbf x\in\mathcal X_f . $$

    取 \(K_f=K_\infty\)、\(V_f=\mathbf x^\top P\mathbf x\)，这个不等式取等号（这就是 Riccati 方程），\(\mathcal X_f\) 取 LQR 不碰约束的一个不变椭圆或多面体。证明思路（"平移论证"）：设 \(\mathbf x\) 处的最优计划是 \((\mathbf u_0^\star,\dots,\mathbf u_{N-1}^\star)\)，终点 \(\mathbf x_N^\star\in\mathcal X_f\)。到了下一个状态 \(\mathbf x^+=A\mathbf x+B\mathbf u_0^\star\)，构造候选计划

    With \(K_f=K_\infty\) and \(V_f=\mathbf x^\top P\mathbf x\) the inequality holds with equality (it is the Riccati equation), and \(\mathcal X_f\) is an invariant ellipse or polytope on which LQR does not touch the constraints. Proof idea (the "shift argument"): let the optimal plan at \(\mathbf x\) be \((\mathbf u_0^\star,\dots,\mathbf u_{N-1}^\star)\), ending at \(\mathbf x_N^\star\in\mathcal X_f\). At the next state \(\mathbf x^+=A\mathbf x+B\mathbf u_0^\star\), build the candidate plan

    $$ \hl{warm}{\tilde{\mathbf U}=\big(\mathbf u_1^\star,\ \mathbf u_2^\star,\ \dots,\ \mathbf u_{N-1}^\star,\ -K_f\mathbf x_N^\star\big)} . $$

    它满足所有约束（前 \(N-1\) 步本来就满足，最后一步由 \(\mathcal X_f\) 的不变性保证），所以 \(\mathbf x^+\) 处的问题可行：这就是递归可行性。它的代价是原最优代价减去第一步的代价，再把终端代价换成"多走一步加新的终端代价"，由上面的不等式，后者不会变大：

    It satisfies every constraint (the first \(N-1\) steps already did, and the last is covered by the invariance of \(\mathcal X_f\)), so the problem at \(\mathbf x^+\) is feasible: that is recursive feasibility. Its cost is the old optimal cost minus the first stage, with the terminal cost replaced by "one more stage plus the new terminal cost", which by the inequality above does not increase:

    $$ V_N(\mathbf x^+)\ \le\ J(\mathbf x^+,\tilde{\mathbf U})\ \le\ V_N(\mathbf x)-\big(\mathbf x^\top Q\mathbf x+\mathbf u_0^{\star\top}R\,\mathbf u_0^\star\big). $$

    \(V_N\) 每步至少下降一个正的阶段代价，又有下界 0，所以它是闭环的李雅普诺夫函数（13.2 节），原点渐近稳定。FIG 4 的终端约束 \(v_N\le0\) 是这个论证的一个简化版：\(\mathcal X_f=\{v\le0\}\) 在 \(u=0\) 下不变，只证了可行性，没要求代价下降。没有终端成分时，这两个结论都可能失败：FIG 2 的发散与 FIG 4 的不可行就是反例。时域足够长时它们通常又会成立（终端成分可以去掉），但"多长才够"依赖于问题，事先很难算。

    \(V_N\) drops by at least one positive stage cost per step and is bounded below by 0, so it is a Lyapunov function for the closed loop (Lesson 13.2) and the origin is asymptotically stable. FIG 4's terminal constraint \(v_N\le0\) is a simplified version of the argument: \(\mathcal X_f=\{v\le0\}\) is invariant under \(u=0\), which proves feasibility but asks for no cost decrease. Without terminal ingredients both conclusions can fail: the divergence in FIG 2 and the infeasibility in FIG 4 are counterexamples. With a long enough horizon they usually hold again (the terminal ingredients can be dropped), but how long is "long enough" depends on the problem and is hard to compute in advance.

    软约束Soft constraints

    状态约束硬性要求时，模型误差或扰动随时可能让问题不可行（6.8 节的误区）。实践中把它们写成软约束：\(\mathbf c(\mathbf x_k)\le\boldsymbol\sigma_k\)，\(\boldsymbol\sigma_k\ge0\)，代价加 \(\rho_1\mathbf 1^\top\boldsymbol\sigma+\rho_2\|\boldsymbol\sigma\|^2\)。线性项是"精确罚"：只要 \(\rho_1\) 大于对应硬约束的拉格朗日乘子，原问题可行时的解与硬约束完全相同，只在不可行时才允许最小的违反。FIG 7 里道路边界和障碍物就是这样处理的。

    Hard state constraints can make the problem infeasible at any moment because of model error or disturbances (the pitfall in Lesson 6.8). In practice they are made soft: \(\mathbf c(\mathbf x_k)\le\boldsymbol\sigma_k\), \(\boldsymbol\sigma_k\ge0\), with \(\rho_1\mathbf 1^\top\boldsymbol\sigma+\rho_2\|\boldsymbol\sigma\|^2\) added to the cost. The linear term is an "exact penalty": as long as \(\rho_1\) exceeds the Lagrange multiplier of the corresponding hard constraint, the solution equals the hard-constrained one whenever that is feasible, and only when it is not is the smallest violation allowed. FIG 7 handles road edges and the obstacle this way.

    非线性 MPC 与实时迭代；MPPINonlinear MPC and real-time iterations; MPPI

    模型非线性 \(\mathbf x_{k+1}=\mathbf f(\mathbf x_k,\mathbf u_k)\) 时，问题是一个非线性规划，通常用 SQP（6.7 节）：在当前猜测处线性化动力学，\(\delta\mathbf x_{k+1}=A_k\delta\mathbf x_k+B_k\delta\mathbf u_k\)，\(A_k=\partial\mathbf f/\partial\mathbf x\)，\(B_k=\partial\mathbf f/\partial\mathbf u\)，用 Gauss-Newton 近似代价的 Hessian（6.6 节），解一个 QP 得到修正量。实时迭代（RTI，Diehl 等，2005）只做一次 SQP 迭代：上一周期的解平移之后已经很接近最优，一次迭代就够；并且线性化可以在测到新状态之前准备好，测量到达后只剩解一个 QP。时变的 \(A_k,B_k\) 意味着每个周期都要重新缩并或重新做 Riccati，FIG 5 的"每步缩并"曲线就是这种情况。

    With a nonlinear model \(\mathbf x_{k+1}=\mathbf f(\mathbf x_k,\mathbf u_k)\) the problem is a nonlinear program, usually solved by SQP (Lesson 6.7): linearize the dynamics at the current guess, \(\delta\mathbf x_{k+1}=A_k\delta\mathbf x_k+B_k\delta\mathbf u_k\) with \(A_k=\partial\mathbf f/\partial\mathbf x\), \(B_k=\partial\mathbf f/\partial\mathbf u\), approximate the cost Hessian by Gauss-Newton (Lesson 6.6), and solve one QP for the correction. The real-time iteration (RTI, Diehl et al., 2005) performs only one SQP iteration per cycle: the shifted previous solution is already close to optimal, so one iteration suffices; and the linearization can be prepared before the new state is measured, leaving only one QP once the measurement arrives. Time-varying \(A_k,B_k\) mean re-condensing or redoing Riccati every cycle: the "condense every step" curve of FIG 5.

    MPPI（6.11 节）解的是同一个"滚动时域"问题，只是不求导：在平移后的上一计划附近采 \(K\) 条噪声序列，各自用模型往前仿真得到代价 \(S_i\)，按 \(w_i\propto\exp(-(S_i-S_{\min})/\lambda)\) 加权平均。它能直接用不连续的代价（撞了罚一个大数）和任意不可导的模型，代价是需要很多次仿真（通常在 GPU 上并行），而且只能把约束写成罚函数。

    MPPI (Lesson 6.11) solves the same receding-horizon problem without derivatives: sample \(K\) noisy sequences around the shifted previous plan, simulate each forward with the model to get a cost \(S_i\), and average with weights \(w_i\propto\exp(-(S_i-S_{\min})/\lambda)\). It can use discontinuous costs (a big penalty for a hit) and any non-differentiable model directly; the price is many simulations (usually parallel on a GPU), and constraints can only enter as penalties.

    自动驾驶的横向控制是 MPC 的经典应用。车用 9.1 节的运动学自行车模型：状态 \((x,y,\theta,\delta)\)，\(\dot x=v\cos\theta\)，\(\dot y=v\sin\theta\)，\(\dot\theta=\tfrac vL\tan\delta\)，控制量是转向角速度 \(\dot\delta=\omega\)，\(|\omega|\le0.4\) rad/s，\(|\delta|\le0.55\) rad，轴距 \(L=2.7\) m，车速 \(v\) 恒定。路面宽 7 m，前方有一个可以拖动的红色障碍物。三个控制器同时跑：

    • 非线性 MPC（蓝）：代价是横向误差、航向误差、转角和转向角速度的平方和；道路边界和障碍物写成横向误差的软约束 \(e_{\rm lo}(s_k)\le e_k\le e_{\rm hi}(s_k)\)。每 0.1 s 做一次实时迭代（一次 Gauss-Newton 线性化 + 一个 QP，用本页的内点法）。
    • MPPI（琥珀）：同一个模型、同一个代价（约束以同样的罚函数出现），\(K\) 条采样，噪声 \(\sigma=0.2\) rad/s，温度 \(\lambda=1\)。
    • 纯追踪（紫，9.1 节）：预瞄距离 \(\max(4,\,0.5v)\) m，同样的转向速率上限。它只看路径，看不见障碍。

    Lateral control of a car is a classic MPC application. The car is the kinematic bicycle of Lesson 9.1: state \((x,y,\theta,\delta)\), \(\dot x=v\cos\theta\), \(\dot y=v\sin\theta\), \(\dot\theta=\tfrac vL\tan\delta\), with the steering rate \(\dot\delta=\omega\) as input, \(|\omega|\le0.4\) rad/s, \(|\delta|\le0.55\) rad, wheelbase \(L=2.7\) m and constant speed \(v\). The road is 7 m wide, and a draggable red obstacle sits ahead. Three controllers run at once:

    • Nonlinear MPC (blue): the cost is the sum of squares of lateral error, heading error, steering angle and steering rate; road edges and the obstacle are soft constraints on the lateral error, \(e_{\rm lo}(s_k)\le e_k\le e_{\rm hi}(s_k)\). Every 0.1 s it does one real-time iteration (one Gauss-Newton linearization plus one QP, solved by this page's interior-point method).
    • MPPI (amber): the same model and the same cost (constraints appear through the same penalties), \(K\) samples, noise \(\sigma=0.2\) rad/s, temperature \(\lambda=1\).
    • Pure pursuit (purple, Lesson 9.1): look-ahead \(\max(4,\,0.5v)\) m with the same steering-rate limit. It sees the path but not the obstacle.

    真实系统里In real systems

    起源：MPC 最早来自炼油和化工的过程控制（动态矩阵控制 DMC 等，Qin 与 Badgwell 的综述统计了数千套工业应用），那里采样周期是分钟级的。机器人把它推到了毫秒级。四足：MIT Cheetah 3 的凸 MPC（Di Carlo 等，2018）把机身看作单刚体，绕当前偏航角线性化姿态动力学，以未来若干步的足底力为决策变量、摩擦锥线性化成金字塔，每个周期解一个 QP；计算出的足底力再交给更高频的腿部控制（13.7 节的全身控制会接着做）。ETH 的 OCS2 库在 ANYmal 等机器人上做非线性 MPC。自动驾驶：Autoware 的横向控制器提供一个线性 MPC 实现，默认用带一阶转向延迟的运动学自行车模型；赛车 MPC 用 MPCC（轮廓控制，Liniger 等，2015）同时优化路径进度。四旋翼：非线性 MPC 用全刚体模型直接输出推力与角速度指令。求解器：OSQP（ADMM，Stellato 等，2020）、qpOASES（有效集，热启动极好，Ferreau 等，2014）、HPIPM（稀疏内点法，Riccati 结构）、acados（Verschueren 等，2022：在 HPIPM 之上实现 RTI，自动生成 C 代码）、CasADi（建模与自动微分）；MPPI 则常在 GPU 上运行。显式 MPC：对很小的线性问题，可以离线算出分段仿射的 \(\kappa_N(\mathbf x)\)（Bemporad 等，2002），在线只查表，适合没有余力解 QP 的嵌入式芯片。

    Origins: MPC grew out of process control in refineries and chemical plants (dynamic matrix control, DMC, and others; Qin and Badgwell's survey counts thousands of industrial installations), with sampling periods of minutes. Robotics pushed it to milliseconds. Quadrupeds: the convex MPC of MIT Cheetah 3 (Di Carlo et al., 2018) treats the body as a single rigid body, linearizes the attitude dynamics about the current yaw, uses the foot forces over the coming steps as decision variables with friction cones linearized into pyramids, and solves one QP per cycle; the resulting foot forces go to a faster leg-level controller (Lesson 13.7's whole-body control continues from there). ETH's OCS2 library runs nonlinear MPC on ANYmal and other robots. Autonomous driving: Autoware's lateral controller offers a linear MPC implementation whose default model is the kinematic bicycle with a first-order steering delay; racing MPC uses MPCC (model predictive contouring control, Liniger et al., 2015) to optimize progress along the path at the same time. Quadrotors: nonlinear MPC on the full rigid-body model outputs thrust and body-rate commands directly. Solvers: OSQP (ADMM, Stellato et al., 2020), qpOASES (active set, excellent warm starts, Ferreau et al., 2014), HPIPM (sparse interior point with Riccati structure), acados (Verschueren et al., 2022: RTI on top of HPIPM with generated C code), CasADi (modelling and automatic differentiation); MPPI usually runs on a GPU. Explicit MPC: for very small linear problems the piecewise-affine \(\kappa_N(\mathbf x)\) can be computed offline (Bemporad et al., 2002) so that the online work is a table lookup, which suits embedded chips with no room for a QP solver.

    8常见误区Common pitfalls

    误区 1：时域越长越好，终端代价可有可无Pitfall 1: the longer the horizon the better, and the terminal cost is optional

    长时域的代价是计算（稠密形式对 \(N\) 三次方增长）和对模型误差更敏感的远期预测。没有终端代价时，短时域可以直接让闭环不稳定（FIG 2：\(N\le7\) 时摆会倒）。正确的顺序是：先放 \(P_f=P\)，让时域只需覆盖"约束会起作用的那段未来"，例如 FIG 4 里刹车所需的距离；再按计算预算选 \(N\)。

    A long horizon costs computation (cubic in \(N\) for the condensed form) and relies on far-future predictions that are more sensitive to model error. Without a terminal cost a short horizon can make the closed loop outright unstable (FIG 2: the pendulum falls for \(N\le7\)). The right order: first set \(P_f=P\), so that the horizon only has to cover "the part of the future where constraints act", such as the braking distance in FIG 4; then choose \(N\) to fit the computation budget.

    误区 2：所有约束都写成硬约束Pitfall 2: making every constraint hard

    输入约束（电机力矩、转向速率）是物理事实，硬约束天然可行。状态约束（位置、速度、车道）一旦遇上模型误差、测量噪声或扰动，硬约束就可能让 QP 不可行，控制器在最需要输出的时刻没有输出。状态约束应当写成带精确罚的软约束，并给"求解失败"准备一个后备动作（例如刹车或沿用平移后的上一计划）。

    Input constraints (motor torque, steering rate) are physical facts, and hard constraints on them are always feasible. State constraints (position, speed, lanes) meet model error, measurement noise or disturbances, and as hard constraints they can make the QP infeasible, leaving the controller with no output exactly when it matters. Make state constraints soft with an exact penalty, and prepare a fallback for "the solver failed" (brake, or reuse the shifted previous plan).

    误区 3：MPC 自带鲁棒性Pitfall 3: MPC is robust by construction

    重新测量、重新求解确实给了 MPC 反馈（FIG 1），但第 6 节的稳定性和可行性证明都假设模型精确。模型误差下，"平移的上一计划"不再可行，名义 MPC 可能违反约束甚至失稳。需要保证时，要用鲁棒 MPC（例如管道 MPC：把约束收紧一个由扰动界算出的余量）或随机 MPC（机会约束）。另外，跟踪常值参考而模型有偏差时，名义 MPC 会留下稳态误差，要像 13.1 节的积分项那样加一个扰动估计（偏移消除）。

    Measuring and re-solving does give MPC feedback (FIG 1), but the stability and feasibility proofs of Section 6 assume an exact model. With model error, "the shifted previous plan" is no longer feasible, and nominal MPC can violate constraints or even lose stability. When guarantees are needed, use robust MPC (e.g. tube MPC, which tightens the constraints by a margin computed from the disturbance bound) or stochastic MPC (chance constraints). Also, when tracking a constant reference with a biased model, nominal MPC leaves a steady-state error; add a disturbance estimate (offset-free MPC), the counterpart of the integral term in Lesson 13.1.

    误区 4：忽略计算延迟Pitfall 4: ignoring the computation delay

    求解要花时间：在 \(t\) 时刻测到的状态，算出的输入要到 \(t+\Delta\) 才生效。如果 \(\Delta\) 与采样周期相当，优化里应该从"预测的 \(t+\Delta\) 时刻的状态"出发，或者把延迟写进模型。RTI 把线性化挪到测量之前（准备阶段），正是为了把测量到输出的延迟压到只剩一个 QP。另一方面，求解时间不稳定也很危险：要设迭代上限（FIG 6），并确保上限内交出的解至少可行。

    Solving takes time: the state measured at \(t\) yields an input that takes effect at \(t+\Delta\). If \(\Delta\) is comparable to the sampling period, the optimization should start from the predicted state at \(t+\Delta\), or the delay should be part of the model. RTI moves the linearization before the measurement (the preparation phase) precisely so that only one QP separates measurement from output. Unpredictable solve times are dangerous too: cap the iterations (FIG 6) and make sure the solution delivered at the cap is at least feasible.

    误区 5：模型越精细越好Pitfall 5: the more detailed the model, the better

    MPC 只能和它的模型一样好，但模型也决定了能不能及时解完。FIG 7 的运动学自行车模型假设轮胎不侧滑，侧向加速度接近 \(\mu g\) 时就不对了（9.1 节的误区）。FIG 7 本身就越过了这条线：赛道最急的弯半径约 12.5 m，光是沿路径开过去就需要 \(v^2/R\)：10 m/s 时约 8 m/s²，12 m/s 时约 11.5 m/s²，14 m/s 时约 16 m/s²，而干燥路面上轮胎最多给出约 \(\mu g\approx9\) m/s²。所以 12 到 14 m/s 的结果只说明控制器在这个模型里的行为，真车会侧滑，这时应换成动力学自行车模型；可每多一个状态，稠密 QP 就更大、非线性更强。四足的凸 MPC 故意用单刚体模型，正是为了每个周期都能解一个凸 QP。选模型时要问：控制器需要预测的那几秒里，哪些效应真的重要？

    MPC is only as good as its model, but the model also decides whether it can be solved in time. FIG 7's kinematic bicycle assumes the tires do not slip sideways and becomes wrong as lateral acceleration approaches \(\mu g\) (the pitfall of Lesson 9.1). FIG 7 itself crosses that line: the tightest bend of the track has a radius of about 12.5 m, so merely following the path there takes \(v^2/R\), about 8 m/s² at 10 m/s, 11.5 m/s² at 12 m/s and 16 m/s² at 14 m/s, while tires on dry asphalt deliver at most about \(\mu g\approx9\) m/s². So the results at 12 to 14 m/s only describe how the controllers behave in this model; a real car would slide, and you would switch to a dynamic bicycle model. Yet every extra state makes the QP larger and the problem more nonlinear. The quadruped convex MPC deliberately uses a single-rigid-body model precisely so that every cycle is a convex QP. When choosing the model, ask which effects really matter over the few seconds the controller predicts.

A linear MPC with state dimension \(n=12\) and input dimension \(m=4\) has its horizon raised from \(N=20\) to \(N=80\).
