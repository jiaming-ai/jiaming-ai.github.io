学习动力学模型Learning Dynamics Models

动力学模型回答“在状态 s 下施加动作 a，下一刻会怎样”。写不出准确方程的时候（摩擦、负载、软体、接触），可以从机器人自己记录的数据里把它学出来，再拿它做预测、规划和控制。本节用一个电机驱动的单关节摆，在浏览器里现场训练这样的模型：为什么要预测变化量而不是下一个状态，为什么要归一化，为什么一步误差很小的模型滚动几十步就会离谱，多步损失怎样缓解这个问题，在物理模型上学残差为什么省数据、外推也更稳，以及怎样只看像素就学出一个隐空间里的动力学。 A dynamics model answers "if I apply action a in state s, what happens next?". When the equations cannot be written down accurately (friction, payloads, soft bodies, contact), the model can be learned from the robot's own logged data and then used to predict, plan and control. Using a motor-driven single-joint pendulum, this lesson trains such models live in the browser: why to predict the change rather than the next state, why to normalize, why a model with tiny one-step error goes badly wrong after a few dozen steps of rollout, how a multi-step loss helps, why learning a residual on top of a physics model saves data and extrapolates better, and how to learn dynamics in a latent space from pixels alone.

    1为什么机器人需要它Why robots need this

    Part IV 的控制器几乎都要一个模型：LQR（13.3 节）要 \(A,B\)，MPC（13.4 节）要在每个控制周期里把模型往前滚几十步。13.2 节从牛顿定律写出了摆的状态方程；8.3 节把多关节机械臂写成 \(M(\mathbf q)\ddot{\mathbf q}+\mathbf b=\boldsymbol\tau\)。这些方程都假设我们知道所有参数，而且所有力都在方程里。

    真实机器人不是这样。减速器里的摩擦随温度变，夹爪上多了一个没称过的工件，腿踩在泥地上，手里揉着一块面团，空气在桨叶下面打旋。这些东西要么很难写成方程，要么方程里的参数测不准。另一条路是从数据里学：让机器人动一动，记录下大量的 \((s_t,a_t,s_{t+1})\)，训练一个函数 \(s_{t+1}\approx f_\theta(s_t,a_t)\)。这就是学习动力学模型，也是所有 model-based 强化学习（本章的 24.2、24.3、24.4 节）的第一步。

    它看上去只是一个回归问题（16.1 节），可动力学模型的用法和普通回归不同：它的输出会被再喂回给它自己，一步接一步地滚动。一步上的一点点误差会被下一步当成输入，误差会累积，模型还会被带到训练数据没覆盖的状态。本节的大部分内容都是在处理这个“自己喂自己”带来的问题。

    Nearly every controller of Part IV needs a model: LQR (Lesson 13.3) needs \(A,B\), and MPC (Lesson 13.4) rolls a model forward dozens of steps in every control period. Lesson 13.2 wrote the pendulum's state equation from Newton's law; Lesson 8.3 wrote a multi-joint arm as \(M(\mathbf q)\ddot{\mathbf q}+\mathbf b=\boldsymbol\tau\). Those equations assume we know every parameter and that every force is in the equation.

    Real robots are not like that. Friction in a gearbox changes with temperature, the gripper carries a part nobody weighed, a leg steps into mud, a hand kneads dough, air swirls under a propeller. These are either hard to write as equations or their parameters cannot be measured well. The alternative is to learn from data: let the robot move, log many triples \((s_t,a_t,s_{t+1})\), and train a function \(s_{t+1}\approx f_\theta(s_t,a_t)\). This is learning a dynamics model, and it is the first step of all model-based reinforcement learning (Lessons 24.2, 24.3 and 24.4 of this chapter).

    It looks like a plain regression problem (Lesson 16.1), but a dynamics model is used differently from an ordinary regressor: its output is fed back into itself, step after step. A small error at one step becomes the next step's input, errors pile up, and the model gets carried into states the training data never covered. Most of this lesson is about the problems this "feeding itself" creates.

    核心洞见Key insight

    一个学出来的动力学模型只是“在训练数据附近”的一张箭头图。数据内它可以比手写的线性模型准得多；数据外它不知道自己不知道（FIG 1 里离数据较远的地方，红色几乎连成一片）。后面所有的问题，例如滚动时误差累积、规划器钻模型的空子，根源都在这里：使用模型的时候，状态未必还在训练数据附近。

    A learned dynamics model is a field of arrows that is only valid "near the training data". Inside the data it can be far more accurate than a hand-written linear model; outside it does not know that it does not know (in FIG 1 the red is nearly solid away from the data). Every later problem, from errors piling up during rollout to planners exploiting the model's mistakes, has this root: when the model is used, the state is not necessarily near the training data any more.

    3动手：预测什么、用什么单位Play: what to predict, and in what units

    FIG 1 的网络预测的是变化量 \(\Delta s=s_{t+1}-s_t\)，而且输入、输出都做了归一化。这两个选择看似细节，却往往决定了模型能不能用。原因很简单：控制周期很短，下一刻的状态和这一刻几乎一样。直接预测 \(s_{t+1}\) 时，网络大部分容量都花在“把输入原样抄过去”上，而我们真正关心的那一小点变化，在损失里只占很小的比例。预测 \(\hl{delta}{\Delta s}\) 则把“抄过去”写死在结构里，网络只学变化本身：

    FIG 1's network predicts the change \(\Delta s=s_{t+1}-s_t\), and both its inputs and outputs are normalized. These choices look like details but often decide whether the model is usable at all. The reason is simple: the control period is short, so the next state is almost the same as this one. When the network predicts \(s_{t+1}\) directly, most of its capacity goes into "copying the input through", and the small change we actually care about is a tiny part of the loss. Predicting \(\hl{delta}{\Delta s}\) builds the copying into the structure and leaves the network to learn only the change itself:

    $$ \hat s_{t+1}=s_t+\sigma_\Delta\odot\hl{delta}{\mathrm{MLP}_\theta\!\Big(\frac{(s_t,a_t)-\mu_x}{\sigma_x}\Big)}+\mu_\Delta,\qquad L(\theta)=\frac1n\sum_i\Big\|\frac{\Delta s_i-\mu_\Delta}{\sigma_\Delta}-\mathrm{MLP}_\theta(\cdot)\Big\|^2 $$

    \(\mu_x,\sigma_x\) 是输入在数据上的均值和标准差，\(\mu_\Delta,\sigma_\Delta\) 是变化量的均值和标准差，\(\odot\) 表示逐分量相乘。归一化有两个作用：输入的各个分量尺度相近，优化更顺；输出的各个分量在损失里权重相当。不归一化时，\(\Delta\omega\) 的典型大小（0.58 rad/s）是 \(\Delta\theta\)（0.13 rad）的 4 倍多，平方误差里它占了约 20 倍的权重，角度那一维几乎没人管。下图同时训练四个只在这两点上不同的网络（结构、初值、数据、小批量顺序都相同），比较它们在测试数据上的相对一步误差。

    \(\mu_x,\sigma_x\) are the inputs' mean and standard deviation over the data, \(\mu_\Delta,\sigma_\Delta\) those of the change, and \(\odot\) is elementwise multiplication. Normalization does two jobs: the input components get comparable scales, which makes optimization smoother, and the output components get comparable weight in the loss. Without it, the typical \(\Delta\omega\) (0.58 rad/s) is more than 4 times the typical \(\Delta\theta\) (0.13 rad), so it carries about 20 times the weight in the squared error and the angle is left almost unattended.     误差在滚动中累积，模型被带出数据Errors compound in rollouts, and the model is carried off its data

    规划器（13.4 节的 MPC、6.11 节的 CEM/MPPI）用模型的方式是开环滚动：给定起点和一串动作，\(\hat s_{t+1}=f_\theta(\hat s_t,a_t)\)，每一步都把自己上一步的预测当输入。一步误差 \(\varepsilon\) 很小，并不说明 H 步后误差也小：误差会被动力学放大，而一旦预测的状态离开数据，每一步的 \(\varepsilon\) 本身也变大。

    怎么知道什么时候不能再信模型？21.3 节的办法：训练几个只有初值和小批量顺序不同的模型（集成），看它们的分歧 \(\hl{spread}{\sigma_{\mathrm{ens}}}\)。下图用 5 个一步模型从同一个起点、按同一串力矩滚动，与真实轨迹比较。

    A planner (MPC of Lesson 13.4, CEM/MPPI of Lesson 6.11) uses a model by open-loop rollout: given a start and a sequence of actions, \(\hat s_{t+1}=f_\theta(\hat s_t,a_t)\), each step taking its own previous prediction as input. A small one-step error \(\varepsilon\) does not mean the error after H steps is small: errors get amplified by the dynamics, and once the predicted state leaves the data the per-step \(\varepsilon\) itself grows.

    How do we know when to stop trusting the model? The approach of Lesson 21.3: train several models that differ only in initialization and minibatch order (an ensemble) and look at their disagreement \(\hl{spread}{\sigma_{\mathrm{ens}}}\). The disagreement does grow, but it badly underestimates the real error.

    “泵能量”的那条轨迹说明了 model-based 方法最大的麻烦：规划器正是会去找这种轨迹的。如果目标是把杆甩上去，优化器会在模型里寻找能量涨得最快的动作，而模型在高能量区没有数据，它的回答是乱猜，甚至可能比真实系统“更容易”甩上去。这叫模型被利用（model exploitation）。常见的对策都在本章后面：让轨迹样本在集成的各个成员中传播、对它们的代价取平均，使计划必须在每个成员的预测下都行得通（PETS，24.2 节），只做很短的模型滚动（MBPO，同一节），以及用新控制器采到的数据不断重训模型、每步重新规划（24.2 节的数据循环，24.4 节的 MPC）。

    The "pump energy" trajectory shows the biggest headache of model-based methods: a planner goes looking for exactly such trajectories. If the goal is to swing the link up, the optimizer searches the model for the actions that raise the energy fastest, and in the high-energy region the model has no data and guesses; it may even find swinging up "easier" than the real system does. This is called model exploitation. The usual remedies come later in this chapter: propagate trajectory samples through the ensemble's members and average their costs, so that a plan has to work under every member's prediction (PETS, Lesson 24.2), keep model rollouts short (MBPO, same lesson), and keep retraining the model on data from the new controller while replanning at every step (the data loops of Lesson 24.2, the MPC of Lesson 24.4).

    多步损失：按使用方式训练Multi-step losses: train the model the way it is used

    一步损失只问“从真实状态出发，下一步预测得准不准”，可使用时模型的输入是它自己的预测。一个自然的修正是把训练也做成滚动：从记录的状态 \(s_t\) 出发，用记录的动作滚 k 步，每一步都和记录的状态比较，

    A one-step loss only asks "starting from the true state, is the next step right?", while in use the model's input is its own prediction. A natural fix is to make training a rollout too: start from a recorded state \(s_t\), roll k steps through the recorded actions, and compare every step with the recorded states,

    $$ L_k(\theta)=\hl{kstep}{\frac1k\sum_{j=1}^{k}\big\|\hat s_{t+j}-s_{t+j}\big\|^2_{\sigma_\Delta}},\qquad \hat s_t=s_t,\quad \hat s_{t+j}=f_\theta(\hat s_{t+j-1},a_{t+j-1}) $$

    其中 \(\|\cdot\|_{\sigma_\Delta}\) 表示先逐分量除以 \(\sigma_\Delta\) 再取范数。梯度要穿过整串滚动，这和 17.2 节的随时间反向传播（BPTT）完全一样：模型就是一个以 \(a_t\) 为输入、以状态为隐藏状态的 RNN。k = 1 时它就是一步损失。代价是每个样本要做 k 次前向和反向。下图在带传感器噪声的数据上比较两者：编码器读数有 0.02 rad 的噪声，角速度由差分估计，噪声 0.2 rad/s，这在真实机器人上很常见。

    Here \(\|\cdot\|_{\sigma_\Delta}\) means divide each component by \(\sigma_\Delta\) before taking the norm. The gradient has to pass through the whole rollout, exactly the backpropagation through time (BPTT) of Lesson 17.2: the model is an RNN with \(a_t\) as input and the state as its hidden state. With k = 1 it is the one-step loss. The price is k forward and backward passes per sample. The figure below compares the two on data with sensor noise: the encoder reading has 0.02 rad of noise and the angular velocity, estimated by differencing, 0.2 rad/s, which is common on real robots.

    • 换成“无噪声”数据再训练满 3000 步：多步损失不再占优，k 步模型 60 步后的误差不低于一步模型的 0.9 倍（k = 8 时 0.068 对 0.067 rad，k = 4 时 0.119 rad，反而更差）。Switch to "clean" data and train the full 3000 steps again: the multi-step loss no longer wins, and the k-step model's 60-step error is not below 0.9 times the one-step model's (at k = 8, 0.068 versus 0.067 rad; at k = 4, 0.119 rad, actually worse).

    为什么噪声让多步损失占优？一步损失把带噪声的 \(s_t\) 当作输入、带噪声的 \(s_{t+1}\) 当作目标；输入里的噪声会让回归系数系统性地偏小（统计里的“变量含误差”偏差），网络学到的动力学偏“迟钝”，这个偏差每一步都同向，滚动时就累积起来。k 步损失只在起点用一次带噪声的输入，后面的输入都是模型自己的平滑预测，偏差小得多。数据干净时，一步损失已经找到了好模型，多步损失只是多了一份更难的优化。所以多步损失不是免费的午餐：它是在“模型必然有误差”时，把误差往对滚动更无害的方向推。

    Why does noise favour the multi-step loss? The one-step loss takes the noisy \(s_t\) as input and the noisy \(s_{t+1}\) as target; noise in the inputs systematically shrinks regression coefficients (the "errors-in-variables" bias of statistics), so the learned dynamics are too "sluggish", and since this bias points the same way at every step, it accumulates in a rollout. The k-step loss uses a noisy input only once, at the start; afterwards the inputs are the model's own smooth predictions, and the bias is much smaller. With clean data the one-step loss already finds a good model, and the multi-step loss only adds a harder optimization. So the multi-step loss is no free lunch: when the model is bound to be wrong, it pushes the errors in a direction that hurts rollouts less.

    学模型就是最大似然Learning a model is maximum likelihood

    把转移写成概率模型 \(p_\theta(s'\mid s,a)\)，这正是 20.1 节 MDP 里的 \(P(s'\mid s,a)\)。假设 \(s'=f_\theta(s,a)+\varepsilon\)，\(\varepsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,\Sigma)\)，则数据的负对数似然是

    Write the transition as a probabilistic model \(p_\theta(s'\mid s,a)\), which is exactly the \(P(s'\mid s,a)\) of the MDP in Lesson 20.1. Assuming \(s'=f_\theta(s,a)+\varepsilon\) with \(\varepsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,\Sigma)\), the negative log-likelihood of the data is

    $$ -\log p_\theta(\mathcal D)=\frac12\sum_i\big(s_i'-f_\theta(s_i,a_i)\big)^{\!\top}\Sigma^{-1}\big(s_i'-f_\theta(s_i,a_i)\big)+\frac n2\log\det\Sigma+\text{const} $$

    \(\Sigma\) 固定为对角阵 \(\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_\Delta^2)\) 时，这正好是归一化的均方误差（16.2 节“损失 = 负对数似然”）。所以“对输出做归一化”其实是在声明“每一维的误差按它典型变化的大小来衡量”。如果让网络同时输出 \(\Sigma_\theta(s,a)\)（异方差，21.3 节 FIG 2），模型就能说“在这里我的预测本身就更不确定”，PETS 的概率集成就是这样做的（24.2 节）。

    With \(\Sigma\) fixed to the diagonal \(\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_\Delta^2)\) this is exactly the normalized mean squared error ("losses are negative log-likelihoods", Lesson 16.2). So "normalizing the outputs" really declares "measure each dimension's error against the size of its typical change". If the network also outputs \(\Sigma_\theta(s,a)\) (heteroscedastic, FIG 2 of Lesson 21.3), the model can say "my prediction is inherently less certain here", which is what the probabilistic ensembles of PETS do (Lesson 24.2).

    为什么预测 Δs：与积分器和 ResNet 的关系Why predict Δs: the link to integrators and ResNets

    连续系统 \(\dot s=F(s,a)\) 在一个周期上的精确离散化是 \(s_{t+1}=s_t+\int_0^{\Delta t}F\,dt=s_t+\Delta t\,F(s_t,a_t)+O(\Delta t^2)\)（13.2 节的离散化）。所以 \(\Delta s\) 的大小正比于 \(\Delta t\)，而 \(s_{t+1}\) 本身的大小与 \(\Delta t\) 无关。直接预测 \(s_{t+1}\) 时，绝对误差 \(\epsilon\) 相对于变化量的比例是 \(\epsilon/(\Delta t\,|F|)\)：控制频率翻倍，同样的网络误差在“变化量”上就翻倍。预测 \(\Delta s\) 就是让网络学 \(\Delta t\,F\)，和欧拉积分、和残差网络（ResNet 的 \(x+g(x)\)）、和神经 ODE 是同一个结构。

    FIG 1 的线性模型 \(\Delta s=W[s;a;1]\) 则是 13.2 节线性化的数据版：\(A_d=I+W_{:,1:2}\)，\(B_d=W_{:,3}\)。用最小二乘从数据拟合 \(A_d,B_d\) 是经典的系统辨识，在工作点附近往往已经够 LQR 用；它的局限正是 FIG 1 里大角度处 sin θ 的弯曲。

    The exact discretization of a continuous system \(\dot s=F(s,a)\) over one period is \(s_{t+1}=s_t+\int_0^{\Delta t}F\,dt=s_t+\Delta t\,F(s_t,a_t)+O(\Delta t^2)\) (discretization in Lesson 13.2). So \(\Delta s\) scales with \(\Delta t\) while \(s_{t+1}\) itself does not. When predicting \(s_{t+1}\) directly, an absolute error \(\epsilon\) is a fraction \(\epsilon/(\Delta t\,|F|)\) of the change: double the control rate and the same network error doubles relative to the change. Predicting \(\Delta s\) makes the network learn \(\Delta t\,F\), the same structure as Euler integration, as a residual network (ResNet's \(x+g(x)\)), and as a neural ODE.

    FIG 1's linear model \(\Delta s=W[s;a;1]\) is the data-driven version of Lesson 13.2's linearization: \(A_d=I+W_{:,1:2}\), \(B_d=W_{:,3}\). Fitting \(A_d,B_d\) from data by least squares is classical system identification, often good enough for LQR near an operating point; its limit is exactly the bending of sin θ at large angles in FIG 1.

    误差如何累积How errors compound

    记 \(e_t=\|\hat s_t-s_t\|\)，真实的下一步是 \(s_{t+1}=f(s_t,a_t)\)。若模型在滚动所到的每个（预测）状态上的一步误差不超过 \(\varepsilon\)，且真实动力学对状态是 \(\Lambda\)-Lipschitz 的，那么

    Let \(e_t=\|\hat s_t-s_t\|\) with the true next step \(s_{t+1}=f(s_t,a_t)\). If the model's one-step error is at most \(\varepsilon\) at every (predicted) state the rollout visits, and the true dynamics are \(\Lambda\)-Lipschitz in the state, then

    $$ e_{t+1}\le\|f_\theta(\hat s_t,a_t)-f(\hat s_t,a_t)\|+\|f(\hat s_t,a_t)-f(s_t,a_t)\|\le\varepsilon+\Lambda\,e_t\ \ \Rightarrow\ \ e_H\le\varepsilon\,\frac{\Lambda^H-1}{\Lambda-1} $$

    \(\Lambda<1\)（收缩、强阻尼）时误差有界，约为 \(\varepsilon/(1-\Lambda)\)；\(\Lambda=1\) 时线性增长 \(H\varepsilon\)；\(\Lambda>1\)（不稳定或混沌）时指数增长。几乎无阻尼的摆接近 \(\Lambda\approx1\)：小的频率误差变成随时间线性增长的相位误差。更糟的是前提“所到之处 \(\varepsilon\) 都小”：预测状态一旦离开数据，\(\varepsilon\) 本身就变大（FIG 3）。这和行为克隆的复合误差（22.1 节）是同一种机制：训练时的输入来自数据分布，使用时的输入来自模型自己。

    With \(\Lambda<1\) (contracting, heavily damped) the error stays bounded, about \(\varepsilon/(1-\Lambda)\); with \(\Lambda=1\) it grows linearly as \(H\varepsilon\); with \(\Lambda>1\) (unstable or chaotic) it grows exponentially. A nearly undamped pendulum is close to \(\Lambda\approx1\): a small frequency error becomes a phase error growing linearly with time. Worse is the premise "\(\varepsilon\) is small wherever it goes": once the predicted state leaves the data, \(\varepsilon\) itself grows (FIG 3). This is the same mechanism as the compounding error of behaviour cloning (Lesson 22.1): at training time the inputs come from the data distribution, at use time from the model itself.

    多步损失的梯度The gradient of the multi-step loss

    对 \(L_k\) 求导时，第 \(j\) 步的误差通过所有更早的步影响参数。记状态雅可比 \(J_i=\partial f_\theta/\partial s\big|_{(\hat s_i,a_i)}\)（连乘里时间越晚的雅可比越靠左）：

    Differentiating \(L_k\), the error at step \(j\) reaches the parameters through every earlier step. With the state Jacobians \(J_i=\partial f_\theta/\partial s\big|_{(\hat s_i,a_i)}\) (in the product, later Jacobians stand to the left):

    $$ \frac{\partial\hat s_{t+j}}{\partial\theta}=\sum_{i=0}^{j-1}\Big(\prod_{l=i+1}^{j-1}J_{t+l}\Big)\frac{\partial f_\theta}{\partial\theta}\Big|_{(\hat s_{t+i},a_{t+i})} $$

    雅可比的连乘和 17.2 节 RNN 里的一样：\(\|J\|>1\) 时梯度爆炸，\(<1\) 时消失，所以 k 不能太大，常用梯度裁剪；FIG 4 和 FIG 6 的代码里，\(\partial L/\partial\hat s\) 正是用 nn.js 的 net.backward 返回的输入梯度一步步往回传的。另一种做法是 DaD（Data as Demonstrator，Venkatraman 等 2015）：先滚动，再把“从预测状态回到真实轨迹”的修正作为新的一步训练样本加入数据，就像 DAgger 之于行为克隆。

    The product of Jacobians is the same as in Lesson 17.2's RNNs: with \(\|J\|>1\) gradients explode, with \(<1\) they vanish, so k cannot be too large and gradient clipping is common; in the code of FIG 4 and FIG 6, \(\partial L/\partial\hat s\) is passed back step by step using the input gradients returned by nn.js's net.backward. An alternative is DaD (Data as Demonstrator, Venkatraman et al. 2015): roll out first, then add "the correction from the predicted state back to the true trajectory" to the data as new one-step training samples, much as DAgger does for behaviour cloning.

    模型的不确定性Uncertainty in models

    21.3 节的分解直接适用：\(K\) 个成员，各自输出 \(\mu_k(s,a)\)（以及可选的 \(\Sigma_k\)），

    The decomposition of Lesson 21.3 applies directly: \(K\) members, each outputting \(\mu_k(s,a)\) (and optionally \(\Sigma_k\)),

    $$ \operatorname{Var}[s'\mid s,a]\approx\frac1K\sum_k\Sigma_k(s,a)+\hl{spread}{\frac1K\sum_k\big(\mu_k(s,a)-\bar\mu(s,a)\big)^2} $$

    第一项是偶然部分（过程噪声、没观测到的因素），第二项是认知部分（数据不够）。滚动时有两种传播方式：每个成员各滚各的（FIG 3 的做法），或者每一步随机重挑一个成员；PETS 的“轨迹采样”两种都考察过（24.2 节）。无论哪种，FIG 3 已经提醒过：成员共享结构和数据，在远离数据处往往一起错，分歧只是认知不确定性的一个偏乐观的估计。

    The first term is the aleatoric part (process noise, unobserved factors), the second the epistemic part (not enough data). In a rollout it can be propagated in two ways: each member rolls on its own (as in FIG 3), or a member is re-drawn at random at every step; the "trajectory sampling" of PETS studies both (Lesson 24.2). Either way, FIG 3 has already warned: the members share architecture and data, often go wrong together far from the data, and their disagreement is an optimistic estimate of the epistemic uncertainty.

    残差与隐空间模型Residual and latent models

    残差模型可以写在不同层次上：加在下一状态上（FIG 5 的 \(f_{\mathrm{phys}}+r_\theta\)），或加在力矩上，\(M(\mathbf q)\ddot{\mathbf q}+\mathbf b(\mathbf q,\dot{\mathbf q})=\boldsymbol\tau+\boldsymbol\tau_\theta(\mathbf q,\dot{\mathbf q},\boldsymbol\tau)\)，后者让网络只补“未建模的力”，再交给积分器。物理参数 \(\varphi\) 也可以和 \(\theta\) 一起拟合，但要小心两者“抢”同一部分误差，常见做法是先辨识 \(\varphi\) 再学 \(\theta\)，或者对 \(r_\theta\) 加正则让它尽量小。隐空间模型的损失（FIG 6）里，解码器的重建项防止所有 \(z\) 塌缩到同一点（18.4 节讲过的“塌缩”）；不用解码器的做法（TD-MPC）改用奖励和价值预测加上隐状态的一致性来防止塌缩，见 24.3 节。

    A residual can sit at different levels: on the next state (FIG 5's \(f_{\mathrm{phys}}+r_\theta\)), or on the torque, \(M(\mathbf q)\ddot{\mathbf q}+\mathbf b(\mathbf q,\dot{\mathbf q})=\boldsymbol\tau+\boldsymbol\tau_\theta(\mathbf q,\dot{\mathbf q},\boldsymbol\tau)\), where the network only supplies the "unmodelled force" and the integrator does the rest. The physical parameters \(\varphi\) can also be fitted together with \(\theta\), but beware of the two "competing" for the same part of the error; the usual practice is to identify \(\varphi\) first and then learn \(\theta\), or to regularize \(r_\theta\) to stay small. In the latent model's loss (FIG 6) the decoder's reconstruction term keeps all \(z\) from collapsing to one point (the "collapse" of Lesson 18.4); decoder-free approaches (TD-MPC) prevent collapse with reward and value prediction plus latent consistency instead; see Lesson 24.3.

    6机器人中的它In robotics

    学到的模型在机器人里有三种典型用法。当仿真器用：在里面训练策略或做规划，省下真实机器人的时间（24.2、24.3 节）。放进控制器：MPC 每个周期在模型上滚动、优化动作（24.4 节），或者用模型做前馈补偿。补全仿真器：把学到的部件插进物理仿真器，缩小仿真和现实的差距（23.1 节）。数据通常就是机器人日常运行时的日志：关节编码器、电流（力矩）、IMU、相机，频率从几十 Hz 到上千 Hz。

    Learned models are used in robots in three typical ways. As a simulator: train policies or plan inside it and save time on the real robot (Lessons 24.2, 24.3). Inside a controller: MPC rolls the model out and optimizes actions every period (Lesson 24.4), or the model supplies feedforward compensation. To complete a simulator: plug learned parts into a physics simulator to narrow the gap between simulation and reality (Lesson 23.1). The data are usually the robot's everyday logs: joint encoders, currents (torques), IMU, cameras, at rates from tens of Hz to over a thousand.

    真实系统里In real systems

    预测变化量与归一化：Nagabandi 等（ICRA 2018）用神经网络学动力学再接随机打靶 MPC，网络预测的正是状态差 \(s_{t+1}-s_t\)，输入和输出都按均值、标准差归一化，这两点在后来的 model-based RL 代码里几乎成了默认设置（例如 MBRL-Lib，Pineda 等 2021）。 学出来的执行器模型：Hwangbo 等（Science Robotics 2019）为四足机器人 ANYmal 训练了一个“执行器网络”，根据最近的位置误差和速度历史预测串联弹性驱动器实际输出的力矩，把它插进刚体仿真器后，在仿真里训练的策略才能直接用在真机上（23.1 节）。 残差物理：Neural Lander（Shi 等，ICRA 2019）在四旋翼的名义动力学上用网络学贴近地面时的气动残差力（并用谱归一化约束网络的 Lipschitz 常数，以保证控制器稳定）；TossingBot（Zeng 等，RSS 2019 / T-RO 2020）先用抛体物理算出投掷速度，再由网络预测一个残差修正。 学出来的仿真器：Sanchez-Gonzalez 等（ICML 2020）用图网络学流体、沙子和黏性材料的粒子动力学，滚动数千步（17.4 节），训练时给输入加噪声以减小滚动误差的累积。 从像素学：Embed to Control（Watter 等，NeurIPS 2015）学局部线性的隐空间动力学；PlaNet（Hafner 等，ICML 2019）用 RSSM 和多步的“隐空间超前预测”（latent overshooting）从图像学模型并直接在隐空间规划，后来的 Dreamer 系列在此基础上训练策略（24.3 节）。

    Predicting changes and normalizing: Nagabandi et al. (ICRA 2018) learned dynamics with a neural network and planned with random-shooting MPC; their network predicts exactly the state difference \(s_{t+1}-s_t\), with inputs and outputs normalized by mean and standard deviation, and both choices have since become near-defaults in model-based RL code (for example MBRL-Lib, Pineda et al. 2021). Learned actuator models: Hwangbo et al. (Science Robotics 2019) trained an "actuator network" for the quadruped ANYmal that predicts the torque its series-elastic actuators actually deliver from a short history of position errors and velocities; plugging it into a rigid-body simulator is what let policies trained in simulation run directly on the real robot (Lesson 23.1). Residual physics: Neural Lander (Shi et al., ICRA 2019) learns the aerodynamic residual force near the ground on top of a quadrotor's nominal dynamics (with spectral normalization bounding the network's Lipschitz constant so that the controller stays stable); TossingBot (Zeng et al., RSS 2019 / T-RO 2020) computes a throwing velocity from projectile physics and has a network predict a residual correction to it. Learned simulators: Sanchez-Gonzalez et al. (ICML 2020) learn particle dynamics of fluids, sand and viscous goop with graph networks and roll them out for thousands of steps (Lesson 17.4), adding noise to the inputs during training to reduce the accumulation of rollout error. From pixels: Embed to Control (Watter et al., NeurIPS 2015) learns locally linear latent dynamics; PlaNet (Hafner et al., ICML 2019) learns a model from images with an RSSM and a multi-step "latent overshooting" objective and plans directly in the latent space, and the later Dreamer family trains policies on top of it (Lesson 24.3).

    7常见误区Common pitfalls

    误区 1：只看一步验证损失Pitfall 1: looking only at the one-step validation loss

    一步误差 1–3% 的模型（FIG 1）照样会在 3 s 后错出 2 rad（FIG 3 泵能量），而 FIG 4 里（带噪声数据，k = 4）两个一步误差差不多的模型（0.009 对 0.007 rad），3 s 后的误差差了将近一倍（0.188 对 0.099 rad）。模型是拿来滚动的，就要在留出的轨迹上按实际使用的地平线、实际使用的动作分布评估滚动误差。

    A model with 1–3% one-step error (FIG 1) can still be 2 rad off after 3 s (FIG 3, pumping), and in FIG 4 (noisy data, k = 4) two models with similar one-step errors (0.009 versus 0.007 rad) differ by nearly a factor of two after 3 s (0.188 versus 0.099 rad). If the model is used for rollouts, evaluate rollout error on held-out trajectories at the horizon and with the action distribution it will actually be used with.

    误区 2：预测 s′、忘了归一化，或者部署时用错了统计量Pitfall 2: predicting s′, forgetting to normalize, or deploying with the wrong statistics

    FIG 2：用原始单位预测 s′ 时，θ 的相对误差是预测 Δs 加归一化时的 4 倍以上（归一化后预测 s′ 也还有约 3 倍），控制频率越高越糟。归一化用的均值、标准差是模型的一部分，必须和权重一起保存；在新数据上重算统计量而不重新训练，等于悄悄换了一个模型。还要防止某一维标准差接近 0（例如一个几乎不动的关节）时除以零。

    FIG 2: predicting s′ in raw units gives more than 4 times the θ relative error of predicting Δs with normalization (normalized s′ still about 3 times), and it gets worse at higher control rates. The normalization means and standard deviations are part of the model and must be saved with the weights; recomputing them on new data without retraining silently swaps in a different model. Also guard against dividing by zero when some dimension's standard deviation is near 0 (a joint that hardly moves, say).

    误区 3：把角度当成普通数字Pitfall 3: treating angles as ordinary numbers

    本节为了简单直接把 θ 喂给网络，所以网络不知道 θ 和 θ + 2π 是同一个姿态：FIG 3 里摆甩过头顶后，θ 超出了训练时见过的任何数值，误差立刻暴涨。实际中应输入 (sin θ, cos θ)，三维姿态用旋转矩阵或四元数（注意 q 和 −q 是同一个旋转），误差用折回后的角度差或 5.6 节的对数映射计算。

    For simplicity this lesson feeds θ directly to the network, so the network does not know that θ and θ + 2π are the same pose: once the pendulum swings over the top in FIG 3, θ goes beyond any value seen in training and the error explodes at once. In practice, input (sin θ, cos θ); for 3D orientation use a rotation matrix or a quaternion (remembering that q and −q are the same rotation), and compute errors as wrapped angle differences or with the logarithm map of Lesson 5.6.

    误区 4：以为用一个策略采的数据能评估任何策略Pitfall 4: assuming data from one policy can evaluate any policy

    模型只在采数据的那个策略到过的地方可信。一旦用它来优化新策略，优化器就会去模型最不可靠、却看起来最有利的地方（FIG 3 的泵能量）。对策：用集成的不确定性给规划加惩罚或约束，用新策略的数据不断重训模型（“采数据—学模型—规划”循环），并且只信任短的滚动。

    A model can be trusted only where the policy that collected the data has been. As soon as it is used to optimize a new policy, the optimizer heads for where the model is least reliable yet looks most favourable (FIG 3's pumping). Remedies: penalize or constrain planning with the ensemble's uncertainty, keep retraining the model on data from the new policy (the "collect data, learn model, plan" loop), and trust only short rollouts.

    误区 5：忽略延迟、噪声和“状态”是否完整Pitfall 5: ignoring delay, noise and whether the "state" is complete

    真实机器人的力矩命令往往要一两个周期后才起作用，差分出来的速度很吵（FIG 4），减速器的间隙、电机温度这些量没被测到。这时 \((s_t,a_t)\) 并不能决定 \(s_{t+1}\)，模型只能学到平均效果。办法是把最近几步的状态和动作历史一起作为输入（17.2 节的历史编码器），或者学一个隐状态（FIG 6）；而噪声大时，多步损失能减轻输入噪声造成的偏差（FIG 4）。

    On a real robot a torque command often takes effect only one or two periods later, differenced velocities are noisy (FIG 4), and gearbox backlash or motor temperature are not measured. Then \((s_t,a_t)\) does not determine \(s_{t+1}\) and the model can only learn the average effect. The remedies are to feed a short history of states and actions as input (the history encoders of Lesson 17.2) or to learn a latent state (FIG 6); and with heavy noise a multi-step loss reduces the bias that input noise causes (FIG 4).
