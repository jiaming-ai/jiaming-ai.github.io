PILCO、PETS 与 MBPOPILCO, PETS & MBPO

无模型强化学习要成千上万次试验，真机器人付不起。Model-based 方法先用每一条数据学一个动力学模型，再在模型里想办法：PILCO 用高斯过程，把状态分布解析地往前推，直接对策略求梯度；PETS 用概率网络集成，让一群粒子在不同模型里各走各的，再用 CEM 做模型预测控制；MBPO 从真实状态出发做很短的模型展开，把这些“假数据”喂给 SAC。三者回答的是同一个问题：怎样用一个处处有错的模型，又不被优化器钻它的空子。本节在浏览器里现场跑这三种方法，并看清样本效率和最终性能之间的取舍。 Model-free reinforcement learning needs thousands of trials, which a real robot cannot afford. Model-based methods first learn a dynamics model from every piece of data and then work inside the model: PILCO uses a Gaussian process, pushes the state distribution forward analytically and differentiates the expected cost with respect to the policy; PETS uses an ensemble of probabilistic networks, lets particles travel through different members, and plans with CEM in model-predictive control; MBPO branches short model rollouts off real states and feeds this synthetic data to SAC. All three answer the same question: how to use a model that is wrong in places without letting the optimizer exploit its errors. In this lesson all three run live in your browser, and you will see the trade-off between sample efficiency and final performance.

    1为什么机器人需要它Why robots need this

    20.4 节里，SAC 让一个单关节手臂学会转到目标角度，用了大约 1000 个环境步，按 0.05 s 一步算是 50 s 的“机器人时间”；PPO 用了几千到上万步。这还只是一个关节。在 MuJoCo 的四足、人形任务上，无模型方法通常要几十万到几百万步。仿真里这只是电费，真机器人上却意味着几天的磨损、复位和看护。

    无模型方法浪费的是什么？每一条转移 \((s,a,s')\) 都告诉了我们“世界怎么动”，而无模型方法只用它来改一下价值或策略，用完就基本丢了。Model-based 强化学习把这些信息存进一个动力学模型 \(\hat p(s'\mid s,a)\)（24.1 节讲了怎样学它），然后在模型里试错：想象动作序列的后果、在模型里算梯度、或者在模型里生成训练数据，只把最有希望的动作拿到真机上执行。2011 年的 PILCO 在一台真实的小车倒立摆上，只用了总共 17.5 s 的交互数据就学会了把摆甩起来并保持倒立。

    代价是：模型在数据少的地方一定会错，而优化器专门会去找“模型说好、其实不好”的地方。这就是本节三个经典方法共同面对的难题，它们的不同之处在于怎样表示模型的不确定性、怎样把它往前传，以及怎样使用模型：

    In Lesson 20.4, SAC taught a one-joint arm to turn to a target angle in about 1000 environment steps, which at 0.05 s per step is 50 s of "robot time"; PPO needed several thousand to tens of thousands. That is a single joint. On MuJoCo quadruped and humanoid tasks, model-free methods typically need hundreds of thousands to millions of steps. In simulation that only costs electricity; on a real robot it means days of wear, resets and supervision.

    What does a model-free method waste? Every transition \((s,a,s')\) says something about how the world moves, yet a model-free method uses it to nudge a value or a policy and then mostly forgets it. Model-based reinforcement learning stores this information in a dynamics model \(\hat p(s'\mid s,a)\) (Lesson 24.1 covered how to learn it) and then experiments inside the model: it imagines the consequences of action sequences, computes gradients through the model, or generates training data in it, and only takes the most promising actions to the real robot. In 2011, PILCO learned to swing up and balance a real cart-pole with a total of 17.5 s of interaction data.

    The price: the model is bound to be wrong where data are scarce, and an optimizer goes looking for exactly the places where the model says "good" and reality says otherwise. That is the problem shared by the three classic methods of this lesson. They differ in how they represent model uncertainty, how they carry it forward in time, and how they use the model:

    2直觉：优化器会钻模型的空子Intuition: the optimizer exploits the model

    先看一个只有一个动作参数的小问题。一台腿式机器人要选步幅 \(a\)（0 到 2 之间），目标是走得最快。真实速度先随步幅增大，在 \(a=0.95\) 附近最快（0.99），步幅超过 1.45 就会摔倒（速度记为 −0.6）。机器人一开始只谨慎地试过 6 个小步幅（0 到 0.55 之间），我们用这些数据训练一个网络，预测“步幅 → 速度”。

    Model-based 的循环是：拟合模型 → 在模型里选最好的动作 → 在真机上执行 → 把结果加进数据 → 重新拟合。问题出在第二步。网络在数据之外随意外推；ReLU 网络常常顺着最后几个点的斜率一直往上画。“在模型里选最好的”就会选中外推最乐观的地方，往往是步幅 2.0，而那里机器人会摔倒。规划器越强，越能找到模型的错误：它在和模型的误差“对抗”。

    补救的办法是让模型说出自己哪里没把握。用 5 个网络组成集成（21.3 节），成员在数据附近一致，在数据外各说各的。只取集成的平均并不够：成员们往往一起乐观。可以在打分时减去分歧，悲观地选择：\(\text{score}(a)=\bar\mu(a)-2\,\sigma_{\mathrm{ens}}(a)\)。

    Start with a small problem that has a single action parameter. A legged robot must choose its stride \(a\) (between 0 and 2) to walk as fast as possible. The true speed first grows with the stride, peaks near \(a=0.95\) (at 0.99), and past a stride of 1.45 the robot falls (speed recorded as −0.6). At first the robot has cautiously tried only 6 short strides (between 0 and 0.55), and we train a network on them to predict "stride → speed".

    The model-based loop is: fit the model → pick the best action in the model → execute it on the robot → add the outcome to the data → refit. The trouble is in step two. Outside the data the network extrapolates freely; a ReLU network often keeps drawing upward along the slope of the last few points. "Pick the best in the model" then picks wherever the extrapolation is most optimistic, often a stride of 2.0, where the robot falls. The stronger the planner, the better it finds the model's errors: it is playing against the model's mistakes.

    The remedy is to let the model say where it is unsure. An ensemble of 5 networks (Lesson 21.3) agrees near the data and disagrees away from it. Taking the ensemble mean alone is not enough, since the members are often optimistic together. Instead, subtract the disagreement when scoring and choose pessimistically: \(\text{score}(a)=\bar\mu(a)-2\,\sigma_{\mathrm{ens}}(a)\).

    $$ a^\star=\arg\max_{a}\ \hl{pess}{\bar\mu(a)-\kappa\,\sigma_{\mathrm{ens}}(a)},\qquad \bar\mu(a)=\frac1K\sum_{k=1}^K\hat J_k(a),\quad \sigma^2_{\mathrm{ens}}(a)=\frac1K\sum_{k=1}^K\big(\hat J_k(a)-\bar\mu(a)\big)^2 $$

    这里 \(\hat J_k\) 是第 \(k\) 个网络预测的速度，\(\kappa=2\)。在“悲观集成”模式下，把鼠标放在公式的打分项上，下图的蓝色悲观打分曲线会加粗。三种规划器各按一次“试一次”就是一次真机试验，看看谁摔倒、谁慢慢逼近最优。

    Note the price of pessimism: it never falls, but it tends to settle on a stride a little short of the optimum (the best of seed 1's 8 trials is 0.935, while the true optimum is 0.99), because it never tries anything outside what it is sure of. After the optimistic planner falls once, that data point pins the model down far out, and it learns more accurately afterwards: an exploited error is also a form of exploration, paid for with one fall. On a real robot a fall is often too expensive, which is why all three methods take uncertainty seriously.

    核心洞见Key insight

    模型的误差本身不可怕，可怕的是误差和优化器配合：在 \(\arg\max\) 之下，预测里“偶然偏高”的地方会被系统地挑出来，所以规划结果的预测值总是偏乐观。Model-based 方法能不能成，取决于它能不能 (1) 知道模型哪里不可信，(2) 让这些不可信的地方在规划时得不到好处，(3) 把模型用在它可信的范围内（短展开、反复重新规划）。

    Model error alone is not the danger; the danger is error combined with an optimizer. Under an \(\arg\max\), the places where the prediction happens to be too high are picked out systematically, so the predicted value of a plan is biased upward. Whether a model-based method works depends on whether it can (1) know where the model is unreliable, (2) keep those places from looking attractive to the planner, and (3) use the model only within its range of validity (short rollouts, frequent replanning).

    3动手：PETS 现场学会甩起摆Play: PETS learns a swing-up live

    本节的主要例子是经典的摆的甩起（swing-up）：角度 \(\theta\) 从竖直向下量起（\(\theta=\pi\) 为倒立），\(\ddot\theta=4a-10\sin\theta-0.1\dot\theta\)，力矩指令 \(|a|\le1\)。电机最多只能提供重力力矩的 40%，所以不能直接把摆抬上去，必须来回荡几次积累能量，再在顶端稳住。每一步 0.05 s，一次试验 100 步（5 s），每一步的代价是 \(c=\Delta\theta^2+0.1\dot\theta^2+0.01a^2\)，\(\Delta\theta\) 是离倒立的角度。最后 1 s 都在倒立 ±0.3 rad（约 17°）以内就算成功。

    PETS（Chua、Calandra、McAllister 与 Levine，NeurIPS 2018）的名字就是它的三件东西：Probabilistic Ensembles with Trajectory Sampling。

    • 概率集成（PE）：5 个网络，每个都像 21.3 节 FIG 2 那样输出下一步变化量的均值和方差（偶然不确定性），用高斯负对数似然训练；每个网络在自己的自助采样（bootstrap）数据上训练，成员之间的分歧就是认知不确定性。
    • 轨迹采样（TS）：评估一个动作序列时，从当前状态放出 \(P\) 个粒子，每个粒子每一步从某个成员的预测高斯里采样下一状态。TS∞ 让每个粒子从头到尾跟着同一个成员；TS1 每一步重新随机挑成员。序列的分数是所有粒子代价的平均。
    • CEM 规划 + MPC：用 6.11 节的交叉熵法搜索未来 \(H\) 步的动作序列：采一批序列，按上面的平均代价排名，用前几名重新拟合高斯，重复几轮。只执行第一个动作，下一步重新规划（13.4 节的滚动时域）。

    每次试验结束后，把新的 100 条转移加进数据，重新训练模型，再做下一次试验。第 0 次试验用随机动作。

    The main example of this lesson is the classic pendulum swing-up: the angle \(\theta\) is measured from hanging straight down (\(\theta=\pi\) is upright), \(\ddot\theta=4a-10\sin\theta-0.1\dot\theta\), with torque command \(|a|\le1\). The motor provides at most 40% of the gravity torque, so it cannot lift the pendulum directly; it has to pump energy by swinging back and forth a few times and then catch it at the top. A step is 0.05 s, a trial is 100 steps (5 s), and the cost per step is \(c=\Delta\theta^2+0.1\dot\theta^2+0.01a^2\), where \(\Delta\theta\) is the angle from upright. A trial succeeds if the last 1 s stays within ±0.3 rad (about 17°) of upright.

    PETS (Chua, Calandra, McAllister and Levine, NeurIPS 2018) is named after its three parts: Probabilistic Ensembles with Trajectory Sampling.

    • Probabilistic ensemble (PE): 5 networks, each outputting the mean and variance of the next-step change (aleatoric uncertainty) as in FIG 2 of Lesson 21.3, trained with the Gaussian negative log-likelihood; each network trains on its own bootstrap resample of the data, and the disagreement among members is the epistemic uncertainty.
    • Trajectory sampling (TS): to score an action sequence, release \(P\) particles from the current state; at every step each particle samples its next state from the Gaussian predicted by some member. TS∞ keeps each particle with the same member from start to end; TS1 picks a random member at every step. The score of the sequence is the average cost over all particles.
    • CEM planning + MPC: the cross-entropy method of Lesson 6.11 searches over action sequences \(H\) steps into the future: sample a batch of sequences, rank them by the average cost above, refit the Gaussian to the best few, and repeat a few times. Only the first action is executed, and the next step plans again (the receding horizon of Lesson 13.4).

    After each trial the 100 new transitions are added to the data, the model is retrained, and the next trial begins. Trial 0 uses random actions.

    4另一个视角：不确定性怎样往前传Another view: carrying uncertainty forward

    一步预测的不确定性好办：集成给出几组均值和方差。难的是多步：第 2 步的输入已经是一个分布，而不是一个点，网络对分布输入没有现成的公式。办法大致有三类（PETS 论文把它们放在同一个框架里比较）：

    • 只传均值（E）：每步只用预测均值，完全丢掉不确定性。
    • 粒子（TS1、TS∞）：用一群样本表示分布，每个粒子单独过模型。能表示任意形状，包括“要么往左倒、要么往右倒”这种双峰分布；代价是要很多粒子。
    • 矩匹配（MM）：每一步都用一个高斯（均值和协方差）概括分布，再往前推。PILCO 对高斯过程能精确算出下一步的均值和方差；对网络，PETS 的 MM 基线用粒子估计均值和协方差，再从这个高斯重新抽粒子。它简洁、平滑，但只能是单峰的。

    下图把三种传法放在一起：模型是一个固定的概率集成（训练数据：2 次随机试验 + 2 次带噪声的甩起，共 400 条），摆从倒立附近 \(\theta_0=\pi+\delta\) 静止释放，力矩为 0，向前预测 2 s。

    One-step uncertainty is easy: the ensemble gives several means and variances. The hard part is many steps: the input of step 2 is already a distribution, not a point, and a network has no ready formula for distributional inputs. There are roughly three approaches (the PETS paper compares them in one framework):

    • Mean only (E): use only the predicted mean at each step, discarding uncertainty altogether.
    • Particles (TS1, TS∞): represent the distribution by samples, each particle passing through the model on its own. Any shape can be represented, including bimodal ones like "falls either left or right"; the price is many particles.
    • Moment matching (MM): summarize the distribution by one Gaussian (mean and covariance) at each step and push that forward. For a Gaussian process, PILCO computes the next mean and variance exactly; for networks, the MM baseline of PETS estimates mean and covariance from particles and redraws the particles from that Gaussian. It is compact and smooth, but always unimodal.

    为什么矩匹配会说“还立着”？倒立是不稳定平衡点：往左倒和往右倒的粒子平均起来，均值恰好留在顶上；重新从这个高斯抽粒子，又把粒子拉回中间。单峰高斯没法表示“两边都可能，中间不可能”。TS1 每步换成员，也把成员间的分歧平均掉了不少：这里 \(\delta\) 在 0.04 到 0.09 之间时，它和矩匹配一样说 20 个粒子都还立着。PETS 论文在四个任务上比较了这些传法：模型的选择（概率集成）比传法的选择重要得多，TS1 与 TS∞ 相差不大；矩匹配在低维的倒立摆上有竞争力，在半猎豹这样的高维任务上则不可靠。

    但这并不说明矩匹配没用：在 PILCO 的设定里，它给出一个确定的、可微的期望代价，于是可以直接用梯度优化策略，这正是下一节的主题。

    Why does moment matching say "still up"? Upright is an unstable equilibrium: averaging particles that fall left with particles that fall right leaves the mean right at the top, and redrawing particles from that Gaussian pulls them back to the middle. A unimodal Gaussian cannot express "either side is possible, the middle is not". TS1, which switches members at every step, also averages away much of the members' disagreement: here, for \(\delta\) between 0.04 and 0.09, it says just like moment matching that all 20 particles stay up. The PETS paper compares these propagation schemes on four tasks: the choice of model (a probabilistic ensemble) matters much more than the choice of propagation, TS1 and TS∞ differ little, and moment matching is competitive on the low-dimensional cart-pole but unreliable on a higher-dimensional task such as the half-cheetah.

    That does not make moment matching useless: in PILCO's setting it yields a deterministic, differentiable expected cost, so the policy can be optimized directly by gradients, the topic of the next section.

    5PILCO：把策略搜索变成确定性的优化PILCO: policy search as deterministic optimization

    PILCO（Probabilistic Inference for Learning COntrol，Deisenroth 与 Rasmussen，ICML 2011）比 PETS 早七年，思路很不一样。它有一个参数化策略 \(\pi_\theta\)，目标是让 \(T\) 步内的期望代价 \(J(\theta)=\sum_t\mathbb E[c(s_t)]\) 最小。三个关键选择：

    1. 高斯过程模型：用 GP 学“状态变化量 \(\Delta=s_{t+1}-s_t\)”，每个输出维度一个 GP，平方指数核。GP 天然给出预测方差：数据附近小，远处回到先验（21.3 节的贝叶斯回归是它的有限维版本）。
    2. 解析的矩匹配：如果 \(s_t\) 服从高斯，那么对平方指数核的 GP，下一步变化量的均值和协方差可以精确算出（积分有闭式解），PILCO 再用这两个矩的高斯近似 \(p(s_{t+1})\)。这样一路推下去，得到每一步状态分布的近似 \(\mathcal N(\mu_t,\Sigma_t)\)，没有任何采样。
    3. 解析梯度：\(J(\theta)\) 是 \(\mu_t,\Sigma_t\) 的光滑确定函数，而 \(\mu_t,\Sigma_t\) 又是 \(\theta\) 的光滑函数。于是 \(dJ/d\theta\) 能用链式法则精确算出，交给 L-BFGS 或共轭梯度这样的确定性优化器。和 20.3 节靠采样估计、噪声很大的策略梯度相比，这是完全不同的局面。

    代价函数用的是饱和代价 \(c(s)=1-\exp\!\big(-\|s-s^\star\|^2/(2\sigma_c^2)\big)\)：离目标远时代价接近 1，不再增长。它对高斯状态的期望有闭式解（第 7 节）。

    下面是一个可以完整跑起来的小版本：一辆有黏性和库仑摩擦的小车（\(\ddot x=3a-0.8v-1.2\tanh 5v\)，模型看不到这个式子），要从 \(x=0\) 开到 \(x^\star=1\) 并停住，一次试验 25 步（2.5 s）。策略只有两个参数：\(a=\sin\big(w_1(x-x^\star)+w_2 v\big)\)（正弦把动作压在 \([-1,1]\) 内，PILCO 用过同样的压缩方式）。两个参数意味着我们可以把整个 \(J(w_1,w_2)\) 画成一张地图。GP 以 \((v,a)\) 为输入预测 \((\Delta x,\Delta v)\)，最多用 50 个数据点。

    PILCO (Probabilistic Inference for Learning COntrol, Deisenroth and Rasmussen, ICML 2011) predates PETS by seven years and thinks quite differently. It has a parametric policy \(\pi_\theta\) and minimizes the expected cost over \(T\) steps, \(J(\theta)=\sum_t\mathbb E[c(s_t)]\). Three key choices:

    1. A Gaussian-process model: a GP learns the state change \(\Delta=s_{t+1}-s_t\), one GP per output dimension, with a squared-exponential kernel. A GP gives a predictive variance for free: small near the data, back to the prior far away (the Bayesian regression of Lesson 21.3 is its finite-dimensional cousin).
    2. Analytic moment matching: if \(s_t\) is Gaussian, then for a GP with a squared-exponential kernel the mean and covariance of the next change can be computed exactly (the integrals have closed forms), and PILCO approximates \(p(s_{t+1})\) by the Gaussian with these two moments. Repeating this gives an approximation \(\mathcal N(\mu_t,\Sigma_t)\) of the state distribution at every step, with no sampling at all.
    3. Analytic gradients: \(J(\theta)\) is a smooth deterministic function of the \(\mu_t,\Sigma_t\), which are smooth functions of \(\theta\). So \(dJ/d\theta\) follows exactly from the chain rule and can be handed to a deterministic optimizer such as L-BFGS or conjugate gradients. Compared with the sampled, noisy policy gradients of Lesson 20.3, this is a completely different situation.

    The cost is a saturating cost, \(c(s)=1-\exp\!\big(-\|s-s^\star\|^2/(2\sigma_c^2)\big)\): close to 1 far from the target, where it stops growing. Its expectation under a Gaussian state has a closed form (Section 7).

    Below is a small version that runs completely: a cart with viscous and Coulomb friction (\(\ddot x=3a-0.8v-1.2\tanh 5v\), which the model never sees) must drive from \(x=0\) to \(x^\star=1\) and stop there, in trials of 25 steps (2.5 s). The policy has just two parameters, \(a=\sin\big(w_1(x-x^\star)+w_2 v\big)\) (the sine keeps the action within \([-1,1]\); PILCO used the same kind of squashing). Two parameters mean we can draw the whole \(J(w_1,w_2)\) as a map. The GP takes \((v,a)\) as input and predicts \((\Delta x,\Delta v)\), using at most 50 data points.

    饱和代价：不确定性何时是好事The saturating cost: when uncertainty is welcome

    PILCO 的期望代价有一个有趣的性质。设预测位置 \(x\sim\mathcal N(\mu,\sigma^2)\)，饱和代价的期望是

    PILCO's expected cost has an interesting property. With the predicted position \(x\sim\mathcal N(\mu,\sigma^2)\), the expected saturating cost is

    $$ \mathbb E[c]=1-\frac{\sigma_c}{\sqrt{\sigma_c^2+\sigma^2}}\exp\!\Big(-\frac{(\mu-x^\star)^2}{2(\sigma_c^2+\sigma^2)}\Big),\qquad \frac{\partial\,\mathbb E[c]}{\partial\sigma^2}<0\iff |\mu-x^\star|>\sqrt{\sigma_c^2+\sigma^2} $$

    离目标近时，不确定性越大期望代价越高，优化器会“求稳”；离目标很远时，均值本身已经没希望，一点运气（较大的方差）反而能让部分概率落到目标附近，期望代价下降，优化器会“愿意冒险”。Deisenroth 与 Rasmussen 把这称为饱和代价自带的探索。二次代价 \((\mu-x^\star)^2+\sigma^2\) 则永远惩罚方差。

    Close to the target, more uncertainty means more expected cost and the optimizer plays safe; far from the target, the mean alone is hopeless, and a bit of luck (a larger variance) puts some probability near the target, so the expected cost falls and the optimizer is willing to gamble. Deisenroth and Rasmussen describe this as exploration built into the saturating cost. A quadratic cost \((\mu-x^\star)^2+\sigma^2\) always penalizes variance.

    6MBPO：用短的模型展开喂 SACMBPO: feeding SAC with short model rollouts

    PETS 每一步都要做大量网络前向（本页 FIG 2 每步 3 万次），规划器就是策略，部署时也得一直规划；PILCO 的 GP 计算量随数据量的立方增长，难以扩展到高维。MBPO（Model-Based Policy Optimization，Janner、Fu、Zhang 与 Levine，NeurIPS 2019）换了一种用法：模型只负责造数据，策略学习交给 SAC（20.4 节）。

    关键问题是：在模型里展开多长？从初始状态在模型里跑完整个回合，误差会一路累积（24.1 节的复合误差），策略学到的是模型的错误。MBPO 的做法是分叉展开（branched rollout）：从真实缓冲区里随机取一个真实状态，用当前策略在模型里只走 \(k\) 步（论文里很多任务 \(k=1\)，有的随训练从 1 增加到 15 或 25），把这些短轨迹放进模型缓冲区。短展开的误差小，又因为起点来自真实数据，覆盖的是策略真正会去的状态。模型数据很多，于是每个真实环境步可以做 20–40 次 SAC 更新，而普通 SAC 一般只做 1 次。

    论文的理论部分给出一个下界：真实回报 \(\ge\) 分叉展开下的回报 − 一个惩罚项，惩罚里与模型误差相关的部分随 \(k\) 线性增长，与策略变化相关的部分随 \(\gamma^k\) 衰减（第 7 节）。模型越准，最优的 \(k\) 越大。

    下图在 20.4 节同一个单关节任务上比较三个学习者。SAC 的代码、网络、评估都和 20.4 节完全相同；“MBPO”用 5 个概率网络的集成（结构与 FIG 2 相同，隐藏层宽 32：[4, 32, 32, 4]），前 200 步随机动作，之后每 100 步重新训练模型，每个真实步从真实状态分叉出 10 条长 \(k\) 的展开，SAC 只从模型缓冲区采样，每个真实步更新 \(G=10\) 次。

    PETS runs a great many network evaluations per step (30,000 in FIG 2 on this page); the planner is the policy, so it must keep planning when deployed. PILCO's GP costs grow with the cube of the amount of data and are hard to scale to high dimensions. MBPO (Model-Based Policy Optimization, Janner, Fu, Zhang and Levine, NeurIPS 2019) uses the model differently: the model only generates data, and policy learning is left to SAC (Lesson 20.4).

    The key question is how long to roll out in the model. Running whole episodes in the model from the initial state lets errors pile up (the compounding error of Lesson 24.1), and the policy learns the model's mistakes. MBPO uses branched rollouts: take a random real state from the real buffer, run the current policy in the model for only \(k\) steps (\(k=1\) on many tasks in the paper, on others growing from 1 to 15 or 25 during training), and put these short trajectories in a model buffer. Short rollouts have small errors, and since they start from real data they cover the states the policy actually visits. Model data are plentiful, so each real environment step can afford 20–40 SAC updates where ordinary SAC usually does 1.

    The paper's theory gives a lower bound: true return \(\ge\) return under branched rollouts minus a penalty; the part of the penalty due to model error grows linearly in \(k\), and the part due to policy change decays like \(\gamma^k\) (Section 7). The more accurate the model, the larger the best \(k\).

    样本效率 vs 最终性能Sample efficiency vs final performance

    Model-based 方法的传统名声是“学得快、学不到顶”：模型的偏差会限制最终性能（PETS 和 MBPO 的论文开头都是这么说的）。PETS 的贡献是用不确定性感知的模型在若干基准任务上追平了无模型方法的最终表现（半猎豹上比 SAC 少用 8 倍、比 PPO 少用 125 倍样本）；MBPO 的贡献是用短展开把模型偏差限制在可控范围，同时保留 SAC 的最终性能（Ant 上 30 万步达到 SAC 300 万步的水平）。本页的摆和手臂上也是一样：学到的模型最终和完美模型、和无模型方法表现相当。差距会在模型难学的地方重新出现：接触、高维观测、长时程。

    Model-based methods have the traditional reputation of "learning fast but plateauing low": model bias limits final performance (both the PETS and MBPO papers open with this). PETS's contribution was to match the final performance of model-free methods on several benchmark tasks with uncertainty-aware models (8 times fewer samples than SAC and 125 times fewer than PPO on the half-cheetah); MBPO's was to keep model bias in check with short rollouts while retaining SAC's final performance (on Ant, 300 thousand steps reach the level of SAC at 3 million). The pendulum and arm on this page agree: the learned models end up as good as a perfect model and as model-free learning. The gap reappears where models are hard to learn: contact, high-dimensional observations, long horizons.

At time \(t\), release \(P\) particles for a candidate action sequence \(\mathbf a=a_{t:t+H-1}\):

    $$ \hl{ts}{s^p_{\tau+1}}\sim\hat p_{b(p,\tau)}\big(\cdot\mid s^p_\tau,a_\tau\big),\quad s^p_t=s_t,\qquad \hat C(\mathbf a)=\frac1P\sum_{p=1}^P\sum_{\tau=t}^{t+H-1}c\big(s^p_{\tau+1},a_\tau\big),\qquad b(p,\tau)=\begin{cases}b(p) & \text{TS}\infty\\ \text{uniform on }\{1..B\} & \text{TS1}\end{cases} $$

    CEM keeps a diagonal Gaussian over \(\mathbf a\); each iteration samples \(N\) sequences, takes the \(E\) elites with the smallest \(\hat C\) and refits the mean and variance (Lesson 6.11); finally the first action of the mean sequence is executed. The paper uses \(B=5\) and \(P=20\). Averaging \(\hat C\) is a risk-neutral treatment of model uncertainty: a sequence on which the members disagree is not penalized as such; its average cost is just usually higher because some particles do badly. FIG 1's pessimistic score is risk-averse; offline RL (Lesson 20.5 covers its model-free methods) also has model-based methods, such as MOPO (Yu et al., NeurIPS 2020), which explicitly subtract the model's uncertainty from the reward.

    Let \(\eta[\pi]\) be the true return and \(\eta^{\text{branch}}[\pi]\) the return of "follow the data-collecting policy in the real environment, branch at some point, and run the current policy in the model for \(k\) steps"; \(\epsilon_\pi\) is the largest total-variation distance between the old and new policies, \(\epsilon_{m'}\) the model's total-variation error on the new policy's state distribution, and \(r_{\max}\) the largest reward. Theorem 4.3 of MBPO states:

    $$ \eta[\pi]\ \ge\ \eta^{\text{branch}}[\pi]-2r_{\max}\Big[\frac{\gamma^{\hl{kroll}{k}+1}\epsilon_\pi}{(1-\gamma)^2}+\frac{\gamma^{\hl{kroll}{k}}\epsilon_\pi}{1-\gamma}+\frac{\hl{kroll}{k}}{1-\gamma}\,\epsilon_{m'}\Big] $$

    前两项随 \(k\) 指数减小（在模型里走得越久，越少依赖旧策略采来的数据），最后一项随 \(k\) 线性增大（模型误差一步步累积）。只有当 \(\epsilon_{m'}\) 足够小时，最小化这个惩罚的 \(k\) 才大于 0，也就是说“用模型”才划算。论文还用实验测量了模型误差随策略偏移增长得有多快：数据越多，增长越慢，这正是支撑“短而非零的展开”的证据。

    The first two terms shrink exponentially in \(k\) (the longer you run in the model, the less you rely on data from the old policy); the last grows linearly in \(k\) (model error accumulates step by step). Only when \(\epsilon_{m'}\) is small enough is the \(k\) that minimizes the penalty greater than 0, that is, only then does using the model pay. The paper also measured empirically how fast model error grows with policy shift: the more data, the slower the growth, which is the evidence behind "short but nonzero rollouts".

    8在机器人中In robotics

    真实系统与真实数字Real systems, real numbers

    PILCO：ICML 2011 论文在一台真实的小车倒立摆（小车 0.7 kg，摆 0.325 kg，水平力不超过 10 N）上从零学会甩起并保持倒立，策略是 50 个径向基函数的网络（305 个参数），总共只用了 17.5 s 的交互；同一篇论文还学会了小车双摆的甩起（约 60–90 s 交互）和仿真独轮车的平衡（约 30 s）。同年 RSS 的工作（Deisenroth、Rasmussen 与 Fox）用 PILCO 让一台低成本机械臂在 Kinect 视觉反馈下学会堆积木。Deisenroth、Fox 与 Rasmussen 在 TPAMI 2015 上总结了这条路线。PILCO 的主要局限是 GP 的计算量（随数据点数立方增长）和对状态维数的扩展性，以及策略搜索的局部性。

    PILCO: the ICML 2011 paper learned the swing-up and balancing of a real cart-pole (cart 0.7 kg, pendulum 0.325 kg, horizontal force at most 10 N) from scratch with a policy of 50 radial basis functions (305 parameters), using a total of 17.5 s of interaction; the same paper also learned a cart-double-pendulum swing-up (about 60–90 s of interaction) and balancing a simulated unicycle (about 30 s). Work at RSS the same year (Deisenroth, Rasmussen and Fox) used PILCO to teach a low-cost manipulator to stack blocks with Kinect visual feedback. Deisenroth, Fox and Rasmussen summarized the line of work in TPAMI 2015. PILCO's main limits are the GP's computational cost (cubic in the number of data points), its scaling to many state dimensions, and the locality of its policy search.

    PETS：在 MuJoCo 的倒立摆、7 自由度手臂够取（reacher）、7 自由度推物体（pusher）和半猎豹上，100 次试验以内达到或接近无模型方法的最终表现；只有在低维的倒立摆上，基于 GP 的方法（矩匹配）略胜一筹。在它之前，Nagabandi 等（ICRA 2018）已经用确定性网络 + 随机采样的 MPC 做运动控制，再用无模型方法微调；之后，PDDM（Nagabandi 等，CoRL 2019）把集成模型和改进的采样规划器用到灵巧手操作上，包括在真实的 ShadowHand 上做实验。PETS 风格的“学模型 + 采样 MPC”也是 24.4 节的出发点。

    PETS: on MuJoCo's cart-pole, a 7-DoF reacher, a 7-DoF pusher and the half-cheetah it reached or approached the final performance of model-free methods within 100 trials; only on the low-dimensional cart-pole was a GP-based method (with moment matching) slightly better. Before it, Nagabandi et al. (ICRA 2018) had used deterministic networks with random-shooting MPC for locomotion, followed by model-free fine-tuning; afterwards PDDM (Nagabandi et al., CoRL 2019) brought ensemble models and an improved sampling planner to dexterous manipulation, including experiments on a real ShadowHand. PETS-style "learned model + sampling MPC" is also the starting point of Lesson 24.4.

    MBPO：在 MuJoCo 上，每个真实步做 400 次模型展开、20–40 次策略更新，集成 7 个网络（4 层隐藏层，每层 200 或 400 个单元）。论文指出，Hopper 和 Walker2d 所需的数据如果按真实时间运行，分别相当于 14 和 40 分钟；在 Ant 这样纯 model-based 方法（如 PETS）难以应付的高维任务上也能学好。同一思路被后来的许多方法沿用。把模型换成在潜在空间里学的“世界模型”、把短展开换成在想象中学习，就是 24.3 节的 Dreamer 和 TD-MPC；离线情形下，MOPO 在 MBPO 式的展开上给奖励减去不确定性惩罚，MOReL 则构造一个在集成意见不一的地方“终止并重罚”的悲观模型。

    MBPO: on MuJoCo it does 400 model rollouts and 20–40 policy updates per real step, with an ensemble of 7 networks (4 hidden layers of 200 or 400 units). The paper notes that the data it needs on Hopper and Walker2d would take 14 and 40 minutes respectively if the simulator ran in real time, and that it also learns high-dimensional tasks such as Ant that trouble purely model-based methods like PETS. Many later methods reuse the idea. Learning the model in a latent space as a "world model" and replacing short rollouts by learning in imagination leads to Dreamer and TD-MPC in Lesson 24.3; in the offline setting, MOPO subtracts an uncertainty penalty from the reward of MBPO-style rollouts, and MOReL builds a pessimistic model that halts with a large penalty wherever the ensemble disagrees.

    怎么选：几十个参数、状态维数低、每次试验都很贵（真机、易损）：PILCO 式的 GP 方法仍然是最省数据的。状态几十维、能接受在线计算：PETS 式的“集成 + 采样 MPC”简单可靠，模型可以随时更新。需要一个快速执行的策略网络、或任务较长：MBPO 式地用模型给无模型算法加速，同时记得放一个同等更新比的无模型对照。

    Choosing: a few dozen parameters, a low-dimensional state, and expensive trials (real hardware, fragile): PILCO-style GP methods are still the most data-frugal. Tens of state dimensions and online computation acceptable: PETS-style "ensemble + sampling MPC" is simple and robust, and the model can be updated at any time. A fast policy network is needed, or tasks are longer: use the model to speed up a model-free algorithm as MBPO does, and remember a model-free control with the same update ratio.

    9常见误区Common pitfalls

    误区 1：相信规划器给出的预测回报Pitfall 1: trusting the planner's predicted return

    规划的结果是在模型上取了 \(\arg\max\) 的，它的预测值系统性偏乐观（FIG 1：单个网络在摔倒那次预测速度 1.11，真实是 −0.6）。评估一个 model-based 方法，要看真机或真仿真里的回报，而不是模型里的回报；调参时也要把“模型里的回报”和“真实回报”画在一起，差距变大是模型被钻空子的信号。

    A plan is the \(\arg\max\) over a model, so its predicted value is biased upward (FIG 1: the single network predicted a speed of 1.11 for the trial in which the robot fell, at −0.6). Judge a model-based method by its return on the real robot or the real simulator, not inside the model; when tuning, plot the model's return next to the real one, since a widening gap is the signature of an exploited model.

    误区 2：以为模型准了就够了Pitfall 2: thinking an accurate model is enough

    FIG 2 里，即使用完美的动力学，规划视野 \(H=10\) 也甩不起摆：规划器看不到先反向荡的好处。采样规划器的样本数、迭代数、视野和代价设计同样决定结果；“真模型”这个上限值得先测一下，它告诉你问题出在模型还是出在规划。

    In FIG 2, even the perfect dynamics cannot swing the pendulum up with horizon \(H=10\): the planner cannot see the benefit of first swinging the other way. A sampling planner's population, iterations, horizon and cost design matter just as much; measuring the "true model" ceiling first tells you whether a failure comes from the model or from the planning.

    误区 3：用单峰高斯描述会分叉的未来Pitfall 3: describing a branching future with one Gaussian

    矩匹配把“往左倒或往右倒”平均成“立在中间”，而且给出一个很窄的带（FIG 3）。在不稳定平衡点、接触的有无、绕过障碍物的左右这类地方，未来天然是多峰的。用粒子，或者至少检查一下粒子分布再决定是否可以用高斯近似。

    Moment matching averages "falls left or falls right" into "stands in the middle", with a narrow band (FIG 3). Near unstable equilibria, with or without contact, passing an obstacle on the left or the right, the future is naturally multimodal. Use particles, or at least look at the particle distribution before deciding that a Gaussian will do.

    误区 4：在模型里展开太长Pitfall 4: rolling out too long in the model

    从初始状态在模型里跑完整个回合，误差会复合（24.1 节），策略会学会利用这些误差。MBPO 从真实状态分叉、只走几步；论文报告在 Hopper 上 \(k=1\) 就保住了大部分收益，200 步的展开表现更差，500 步已经不足以学习。FIG 6 右图显示，模型刚训练完时 20 步误差是 1 步误差的 6 倍。要看的是你的闭环：不稳定的闭环会放大误差，稳定的闭环会把它收回来。

    Running whole episodes in the model from the initial state lets errors compound (Lesson 24.1) and the policy learns to exploit them. MBPO branches from real states and takes only a few steps; the paper reports that on Hopper \(k=1\) keeps most of the benefit, 200-step rollouts do worse, and 500-step rollouts are too inaccurate to learn from. FIG 6 (right) shows the 20-step error at 6 times the 1-step error right after the model is first trained. What matters is your closed loop: an unstable one amplifies errors, a stable one pulls them back.

    误区 5：没有公平的无模型对照Pitfall 5: no fair model-free control

    MBPO 每个真实步做 20–40 次更新（论文；FIG 6 里是 10 次），普通 SAC 只做 1 次。把“用模型”的收益和“多更新”的收益分开，需要一个同样更新比的无模型对照组：FIG 6 里 SAC \(G=10\) 不用模型就和 MBPO 一样快，REDQ 在 MuJoCo 上也追平了 MBPO。同样，比较“样本数”时要说清楚数的是什么：真实步、模型步还是仿真步。

    MBPO does 20–40 updates per real step (in the paper; 10 in FIG 6), plain SAC 1. Separating the benefit of "using a model" from that of "updating more" requires a model-free control with the same update ratio: in FIG 6, SAC with \(G=10\) and no model is as fast as MBPO, and REDQ matched MBPO on MuJoCo. Likewise, when comparing "samples", say what is counted: real steps, model steps or simulator steps.
