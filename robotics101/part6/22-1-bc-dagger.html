模仿学习：BC 与 DAggerImitation Learning: BC & DAgger

教机器人做一件事，最直接的办法是做给它看：录下专家的观测和动作，训练一个网络从观测预测动作。这就是行为克隆（BC），本质上是监督学习。可机器人一旦自己动起来，它的小错误会把它带到专家从没去过的地方，在那里它没有数据，错误越积越大：误差随任务长度 T 按 T² 增长。DAgger 让学习者自己开、让专家给它到过的状态打标签，把增长压到 T 量级。本节在浏览器里现场训练策略，看 BC 撞墙、看噪声注入和 DAgger 把它救回来，自己当一回“人类门控”的专家，再看为什么对多模态示教做均方误差回归会直直撞上障碍物。 The most direct way to teach a robot a task is to show it: record an expert's observations and actions and train a network to predict the action from the observation. That is behaviour cloning (BC), and at heart it is supervised learning. But as soon as the robot acts on its own, its small mistakes carry it to places the expert never went, where it has no data, and the mistakes snowball: the error grows like T² in the task length T. DAgger lets the learner drive and the expert label the states it reaches, which brings the growth down to order T. This lesson trains policies live in your browser: watch BC hit the wall, watch noise injection and DAgger rescue it, act as the expert in human-gated DAgger yourself, and see why squared-error regression on multimodal demonstrations drives straight into the obstacle.

    1为什么机器人需要它Why robots need this

    叠衣服、插充电线、把杯子放进洗碗机：这些任务很难写成代价函数，更难写成控制律，但一个人用遥操作手柄做一遍却很容易（遥操作设备与数据采集见 22.3 节）。模仿学习就是从这样的示教里学出一个策略 \(\pi_\theta(o)\)：输入机器人此刻的观测 \(o\)（相机图像、关节角），输出动作 \(a\)。

    最简单的做法叫行为克隆（behaviour cloning, BC）：把示教拆成一对对 \((o_t,a_t)\)，当作普通的监督学习数据集，用 16.1 节的回归（连续动作）或分类（离散动作）去拟合。今天大多数操作策略，从 ALOHA 上的 ACT、Diffusion Policy（22.2 节）到用大量示教训练的 RT-1 和各种 VLA，训练目标的核心都是行为克隆，只是网络和动作表示更讲究。

    可监督学习有一个默认前提：测试数据和训练数据来自同一个分布（16.2 节）。策略一上机就把它打破了：机器人自己的动作决定了它下一步看到什么。它犯一个小错，就到了专家没去过的状态；在那里它没有数据，更容易犯错；再错就离得更远。这一节要回答三个问题：

    • 这种误差累积有多严重？（答案：最坏情况下额外代价随任务长度按 \(T^2\) 增长，FIG 1、FIG 2。）
    • 怎么修？（让专家在学习者自己到过的状态上打标签：DAgger 及其人类门控版本，FIG 3、FIG 4；或者示教时故意加噪声，FIG 1。）
    • 示教里同一个状态有两种做法时怎么办？（均方误差会取平均，FIG 5；生成式策略见 22.2 节。）

    Folding a shirt, plugging in a charging cable, loading a cup into a dishwasher: such tasks are hard to write as a cost function and harder still as a control law, yet easy for a person to do once with a teleoperation device (teleoperation hardware and data collection are in Lesson 22.3). Imitation learning learns a policy \(\pi_\theta(o)\) from such demonstrations: it takes the robot's current observation \(o\) (camera images, joint angles) and outputs an action \(a\).

    The simplest version is behaviour cloning (BC): cut the demonstrations into pairs \((o_t,a_t)\), treat them as an ordinary supervised dataset, and fit them with the regression (continuous actions) or classification (discrete actions) of Lesson 16.1. Most of today's manipulation policies, from ACT on ALOHA and Diffusion Policy (Lesson 22.2) to RT-1 and the various VLAs trained on large demonstration sets, have behaviour cloning at the core of their training objective; they only use fancier networks and action representations.

    But supervised learning assumes that test data come from the same distribution as the training data (Lesson 16.2). A policy running on the robot breaks that at once: the robot's own actions decide what it sees next. One small mistake takes it to a state the expert never visited; there it has no data and is more likely to err again; the next mistake takes it further still. This lesson answers three questions:

    • How bad is this compounding? (Answer: in the worst case the extra cost grows like \(T^2\) in the task length, FIG 1 and FIG 2.)
    • How do we fix it? (Have the expert label the states the learner itself reaches: DAgger and its human-gated version, FIG 3 and FIG 4; or inject noise while demonstrating, FIG 1.)
    • What if the demonstrations show two ways to act in the same state? (Squared error averages them, FIG 5; generative policies are in Lesson 22.2.)

    2直觉：小错误把你带到老师没去过的地方Intuition: small mistakes take you where the teacher never went

    想象一个学开车的人，只看过教练开车的录像。教练开得很稳，车一直在车道正中，所以录像里从来没有“车已经偏向路边，该怎么回来”这种画面。学员自己上路，方向稍微打多一点，车偏了 20 cm。这个画面他没见过，只能猜；猜错了就偏得更多，更陌生，直到压线。

    关键的区别在于数据是谁产生的。普通监督学习里，测试样本由世界给出，和模型无关。模仿学习里，测试时的状态由学习者自己的策略产生：设专家策略为 \(\pi^*\)，它跑出来的状态分布是 \(\hl{dstar}{d_{\pi^*}}\)，训练数据就来自这里；学到的策略 \(\hat\pi\) 跑出来的状态分布是 \(\hl{dpi}{d_{\hat\pi}}\)，它在部署时面对的是这个分布。\(\hat\pi\) 和 \(\pi^*\) 差一点点，\(d_{\hat\pi}\) 就可能和 \(d_{\pi^*}\) 差很多，因为差异会沿着时间累积。这就是 16.2 节的协变量偏移，只不过偏移是策略自己造成的；19.4 节的自回归模型在生成时喂入自己的输出，遇到的“暴露偏差”是同一件事。

    下面所有图都在同一个场景里：一台仓库里的移动机器人（琥珀色）以 1 m/s 沿一条 0.9 m 宽、22.7 m 长的弯曲通道从左下走到右上，两边是货架，碰到就算失败。专家 \(\pi^*\) 是一个简单的控制器：沿通道方向走，同时以增益 3 /m 把自己拉回中线。学习者看到的不是坐标本身，而是 26 个数：\(x,y\) 和它们 12 种随机组合的正弦与余弦（随机傅里叶特征），代替相机那样的“丰富”传感器。像图像一样，机器人挪动一点，这些数就变很多，所以离示教路线 20 cm 的状态，在网络看来是完全陌生的输入。网络是 26 → 32 → 32 → 2 的 tanh MLP，输出前进方向，用均方误差训练。

    Picture someone learning to drive only from videos of an instructor. The instructor drives smoothly and stays in the middle of the lane, so the videos never show "the car has drifted toward the kerb, here is how to get back". The learner takes the wheel, steers a little too much, and the car is 20 cm off. That view was never in the videos, so the learner guesses; a wrong guess puts the car further off and the view gets stranger, until it crosses the line.

    The key difference is who generates the data. In ordinary supervised learning the test samples come from the world, regardless of the model. In imitation learning the test-time states are produced by the learner's own policy. Call the expert policy \(\pi^*\): the states it visits follow the distribution \(\hl{dstar}{d_{\pi^*}}\), and the training data come from there. The learned policy \(\hat\pi\) visits states distributed as \(\hl{dpi}{d_{\hat\pi}}\), and that is what it faces at deployment. A small difference between \(\hat\pi\) and \(\pi^*\) can make \(d_{\hat\pi}\) very different from \(d_{\pi^*}\), because the differences accumulate over time. This is the covariate shift of Lesson 16.2, except that the policy causes the shift itself; the "exposure bias" of the autoregressive models in Lesson 19.4, which feed their own outputs back in while generating, is the same thing.

    Every figure below uses one scene: a mobile robot (amber) in a warehouse drives at 1 m/s along a winding corridor 0.9 m wide and 22.7 m long, from bottom left to top right, with shelves on both sides; touching a shelf is a failure. The expert \(\pi^*\) is a simple controller: it heads along the corridor and pulls itself back to the centre line with a gain of 3 /m. The learner does not see its coordinates directly but 26 numbers: \(x,y\) and the sines and cosines of 12 random combinations of them (random Fourier features), a stand-in for a "rich" sensor such as a camera. As with an image, a small move changes these numbers a lot, so a state 20 cm from the demonstrated route is an unfamiliar input to the network. The network is a 26 → 32 → 32 → 2 tanh MLP that outputs a heading direction and is trained with squared error.

    核心洞见Key insight

    BC 的训练损失衡量的是 \(\hat\pi\) 在专家的状态分布 \(d_{\pi^*}\) 上有多准，而机器人的表现取决于它在自己的状态分布 \(d_{\hat\pi}\) 上有多准。两者之间没有监督学习的保证：训练损失小、验证损失也小的策略，闭环执行时照样可以一次都不成功。要让它可靠，训练数据必须覆盖学习者自己会去的地方。

    BC's training loss measures how accurate \(\hat\pi\) is on the expert's state distribution \(d_{\pi^*}\), while the robot's performance depends on how accurate it is on its own state distribution \(d_{\hat\pi}\). No supervised-learning guarantee connects the two: a policy with a small training loss, and a small validation loss too, can fail every single closed-loop run. To make it reliable, the training data must cover the places the learner itself will go.

    4误差为什么会累积：εT² 对 εTWhy errors compound: εT² versus εT

    把 FIG 1 抽象成一个最简单的模型。任务有 \(T\) 步，每步在专家的状态上，学习者以概率 \(\varepsilon\) 犯错并离开专家的轨迹；离开之后的每一步记代价 1（“不在该在的地方”）。

    • BC 学习者：只在专家的状态上训练过，离开之后不知道怎么回来，就一直在外面。到第 \(t\) 步时已经离开的概率是 \(1-(1-\varepsilon)^t\approx\varepsilon t\)，把它对 \(t\) 求和，总代价约为 \(\hl{bcT2}{\varepsilon T^2/2}\)：按 \(T^2\) 增长。直到 \(\varepsilon T\) 接近 1（几乎每次都会掉下去）才变平，那时代价已接近 \(T\)。
    • DAgger 学习者：在自己离开后的状态上也有专家标签，所以每步有概率 \(1/u\) 回到轨迹上（平均 \(u\) 步回来）。它在外面的时间比例约为 \(\varepsilon/(\varepsilon+1/u)\)，总代价约 \(\hl{dagT}{u\varepsilon T}\)：按 \(T\) 增长。

    下图上半部分是 BC 学习者的 24 次模拟（每行一次，横轴是时间，紫色格子表示那一步在轨迹外），下半部分是 DAgger 学习者（蓝色格子）。右边的对数坐标图是期望代价随 \(T\) 的变化（精确值，实线）和两个上界（虚线，第 8 节推导）：对数坐标里斜率 2 和斜率 1 的直线。

    Boil FIG 1 down to the simplest possible model. The task has \(T\) steps; at each step on the expert's states the learner errs with probability \(\varepsilon\) and leaves the expert's trajectory; every step spent off it costs 1 ("not where it should be").

    • The BC learner was trained only on the expert's states and, once off, does not know how to get back, so it stays off. The probability that it has left by step \(t\) is \(1-(1-\varepsilon)^t\approx\varepsilon t\); summing over \(t\) gives a total cost of about \(\hl{bcT2}{\varepsilon T^2/2}\): it grows like \(T^2\). It only flattens when \(\varepsilon T\) approaches 1 (it falls off almost every time), and by then the cost is close to \(T\).
    • The DAgger learner also has expert labels on the states it reaches after leaving, so each step it returns with probability \(1/u\) (it takes \(u\) steps on average). The fraction of time it spends off is about \(\varepsilon/(\varepsilon+1/u)\), and the total cost about \(\hl{dagT}{u\varepsilon T}\): it grows like \(T\).

    The top half of the figure is 24 simulated runs of the BC learner (one per row, time along the horizontal axis, purple cells = off the trajectory at that step), the bottom half the DAgger learner (blue cells). The log–log plot on the right shows the expected cost against \(T\) (exact, solid) and the two upper bounds (dashed, derived in Section 8): straight lines of slope 2 and slope 1 on log axes.

    With the defaults \(\varepsilon=0.02\), \(u=5\), \(T=100\), BC spends 57.5 steps off on average and DAgger only 8.8. The toy model is pessimistic about BC (once off, never back), but it captures the point: BC's problem is not a large per-step error \(\varepsilon\), but that the consequences of one error last until the end of the task. So the longer the task (a robot makes 10 to 50 decisions a second, so a 30-second task is hundreds to over a thousand steps), the worse BC fares.

    5DAgger：学习者来开，专家来标DAgger: the learner drives, the expert labels

    既然问题是“训练分布 \(d_{\pi^*}\) 不是测试分布 \(d_{\hat\pi}\)”，最直接的修法就是到 \(d_{\hat\pi}\) 上去采数据。Ross、Gordon 和 Bagnell（2011）提出的 DAgger（Dataset Aggregation，数据聚合）就是这么做的：

    If the problem is that the training distribution \(d_{\pi^*}\) is not the test distribution \(d_{\hat\pi}\), the most direct fix is to collect data on \(d_{\hat\pi}\). That is exactly what DAgger (Dataset Aggregation) by Ross, Gordon and Bagnell (2011) does:

    1. 用专家示教得到数据集 \(\mathcal D\)，训练出第一个策略 \(\hat\pi_1\)（这一步就是 BC）。Collect expert demonstrations into a dataset \(\mathcal D\) and train a first policy \(\hat\pi_1\) (this step is BC).
    2. 第 \(i\) 轮：用混合策略 \(\pi_i=\beta_i\pi^*+(1-\beta_i)\hat\pi_i\) 跑一遍（每一步以概率 \(\beta_i\) 执行专家的动作），记下到过的状态 \(s\)。Round \(i\): run the mixture \(\pi_i=\beta_i\pi^*+(1-\beta_i)\hat\pi_i\) (at each step the expert's action is executed with probability \(\beta_i\)) and record the states \(s\) it visits.
    3. 请专家给每个到过的状态标上它会怎么做：\(a=\pi^*(s)\)。注意执行的是学习者的动作，专家只负责“打标签”。Ask the expert to label every visited state with what it would do, \(a=\pi^*(s)\). Note that the learner's actions are the ones executed; the expert only provides labels.
    4. 把新数据并入所有旧数据：\(\hl{agg}{\mathcal D\leftarrow\mathcal D\cup\{(o(s),\pi^*(s))\}}\)，在整个 \(\mathcal D\) 上重新训练得到 \(\hat\pi_{i+1}\)，回到第 2 步。Aggregate the new data with all the old: \(\hl{agg}{\mathcal D\leftarrow\mathcal D\cup\{(o(s),\pi^*(s))\}}\), retrain on the whole of \(\mathcal D\) to get \(\hat\pi_{i+1}\), and go back to step 2.

    常用的选择是 \(\beta_1=1\)、之后 \(\beta_i=0\)：第一轮就是专家示教，之后完全由学习者开。下图从 FIG 1 默认的 BC 策略（0/6）出发。每按一次“一轮 DAgger”：学习者带着打滑噪声跑一遍（琥珀色线），撞墙或到终点就停；它到过的每个状态由专家打标签（蓝色点），并入数据集，在全部数据上再训练 800 步，然后用和 FIG 1 完全相同的 6 次评测打分。

    A common choice is \(\beta_1=1\) and \(\beta_i=0\) afterwards: the first round is the expert demonstration, and from then on the learner drives alone. The figure starts from FIG 1's default BC policy (0/6). Each press of "One DAgger round" lets the learner drive once with wheel slip (amber line) until it hits a shelf or reaches the goal; the expert labels every state it visited (blue dots), these are aggregated into the dataset, the network trains 800 more steps on all of it, and it is scored with exactly the same 6 evaluation runs as in FIG 1.

    With the defaults (aggregate, \(\beta=0\)) the successes are 0, 2, 3, 5, 6: after 4 rounds all runs succeed, and the training set has grown from 451 to 1519 samples. The early collection runs all hit a shelf (each covers only part of the route), but every round adds labels exactly "where the previous policy went wrong". With "newest round only" every round scores 0/6: the new policy learns the stretch where the last one failed and forgets the rest of the route. That is the weight of the word "aggregation": DAgger's guarantee (Section 8) relies on training on all past data. With \(\beta=0.5\) during collection the expert steps in every few steps, a single collection run covers the whole route, and it is 6/6 after the first round, at the price of more labels per round (about 460).

    另一条路：示教时加噪声（DART）Another route: noise while demonstrating (DART)

    DAgger 要求专家在学习者开车时随叫随到。FIG 1 的“示教噪声”滑块是一种更省事的办法：示教时就故意把专家推离轨迹，执行加了噪声的动作，但记录的标签是专家想要的动作。这样 \(d_{\pi^*}\) 本身就变宽，覆盖了学习者可能偏到的地方。Laskey 等（2017）的 DART 把这个思路系统化，并按学习者的误差大小来选噪声。噪声太小覆盖不够，太大则示教本身变差甚至失败。它的局限是：噪声只能模拟“附近”的偏差，学习者真正会犯的错误由它自己决定，DAgger 直接到那里采样。19.4 节的 FIG 4 已经在自回归轨迹模型上看过同样的效果（扰动示教状态、标上专家的回正动作）；这里换成闭环策略和轮子打滑。早在 1990 年前后，ALVINN 就用合成“车偏了”的图像来教网络回正，NVIDIA 2016 年的端到端驾驶网络用左右两侧相机的图像配上修正后的方向盘角，是同一个思想。

    DAgger needs the expert on call while the learner drives. FIG 1's "demo noise" slider is a cheaper alternative: push the expert off its trajectory while demonstrating, executing a noisy action but recording the action the expert intended. Then \(d_{\pi^*}\) itself widens and covers places the learner may drift to. DART (Laskey et al., 2017) made this systematic and chose the noise to match the learner's errors. Too little noise covers too little; too much makes the demonstrations themselves worse or even fail. Its limit: noise only imitates nearby deviations, whereas the mistakes a learner really makes are decided by the learner itself, and DAgger samples exactly there. FIG 4 of Lesson 19.4 showed the same effect for an autoregressive trajectory model (perturb the demonstrated states, label them with the expert's way back); here it is a closed-loop policy with wheel slip. Around 1990 ALVINN already taught its network to recover with synthesized "the car has drifted" images, and NVIDIA's 2016 end-to-end driving network paired images from left and right side cameras with corrected steering angles: the same idea.

    7同一个状态，两种做法：为什么 MSE 会取平均One state, two ways to act: why MSE averages

    DAgger 解决的是“去哪里采数据”。还有一个问题出在“怎么拟合数据”上。人示教时，面对同一个场景经常有好几种都对的做法：从障碍物左边绕或右边绕，先抓左边的杯子或右边的杯子。用均方误差（MSE）训练时，网络在每个观测上输出的是数据里动作的条件均值 \(\hl{mean}{\mathbb E[a\mid o]}\)（推导见第 8 节）。一半示教向左、一半向右，平均就是直走。

    DAgger fixes "where the data come from". A second problem lies in "how the data are fitted". Demonstrators often have several equally correct ways to act in the same scene: pass the obstacle on the left or on the right, pick up the left cup or the right one first. Trained with mean squared error (MSE), the network outputs the conditional mean of the actions in the data, \(\hl{mean}{\mathbb E[a\mid o]}\), at every observation (derived in Section 8). Half the demonstrations go left, half go right: the average goes straight.

    Lessons 19.1, 19.2 and 17.5 showed the single-action version. Here is the closed-loop version: the robot drives forward (up) at 1 m/s, a pillar of radius 0.4 m stands 3 m ahead, and at every step it chooses a sideways speed \(a\).     • MSE regression \(a=\pi_\theta(y)\): the BC used so far.
    • Classification + sampling every step: the sideways speed is split into 5 levels (−1.2, −0.6, 0, 0.6, 1.2 m/s), trained with cross-entropy, and at run time each step samples a level independently from the predicted distribution. It can represent "either is possible" (the action tokens of Lesson 17.5 follow this idea).
    • Told which side, \(a=\pi_\theta(y,z)\): the network is additionally told which side each demonstration took, \(z=\pm1\); at run time \(z\) is drawn once at the start of each trial and then kept. We hand it \(z\) directly to see how much "committing to a mode" buys; the conditional VAE of Lesson 19.1 needs no such label and learns a latent variable like it by itself.

    设定Setting

    和 6.12 节一样用“代价”的语言：有限时域 \(T\)，状态 \(s_t\)，动作 \(a_t\)，每步代价 \(c(s_t,a_t)\in[0,1]\)，策略 \(\pi\) 的总代价 \(J(\pi)=\mathbb E\big[\sum_{t=1}^T c(s_t,a_t)\big]\)。\(d^t_\pi\) 是执行 \(\pi\) 时第 \(t\) 步的状态分布，\(d_\pi=\tfrac1T\sum_t d^t_\pi\) 是整条轨迹上的平均状态分布。专家 \(\pi^*\) 是确定性的。学习者 \(\hat\pi\) 在状态 \(s\) 上的误差用 0–1 损失 \(\ell(s,\hat\pi)=\mathbb 1[\hat\pi(s)\ne\pi^*(s)]\) 衡量（连续动作时换成任何能上界它的代理损失）。

    行为克隆是在专家分布上做经验风险最小化：

    Use the language of cost, as in Lesson 6.12: a finite horizon \(T\), states \(s_t\), actions \(a_t\), a per-step cost \(c(s_t,a_t)\in[0,1]\), and the total cost of a policy \(\pi\), \(J(\pi)=\mathbb E\big[\sum_{t=1}^T c(s_t,a_t)\big]\). \(d^t_\pi\) is the distribution of the state at step \(t\) when running \(\pi\), and \(d_\pi=\tfrac1T\sum_t d^t_\pi\) the average state distribution along the trajectory. The expert \(\pi^*\) is deterministic. The learner's error at state \(s\) is the 0–1 loss \(\ell(s,\hat\pi)=\mathbb 1[\hat\pi(s)\ne\pi^*(s)]\) (for continuous actions, any surrogate loss that upper-bounds it).

    Behaviour cloning is empirical risk minimization on the expert's distribution:

    $$ \hat\pi_{\mathrm{BC}}=\arg\min_{\pi\in\Pi}\ \mathbb E_{s\sim \hl{dstar}{d_{\pi^*}}}\big[\ell(s,\pi)\big],\qquad \varepsilon=\mathbb E_{s\sim d_{\pi^*}}\big[\ell(s,\hat\pi_{\mathrm{BC}})\big]=\frac1T\sum_{t=1}^{T}\varepsilon_t,\quad \varepsilon_t=\mathbb E_{s\sim d^t_{\pi^*}}\big[\ell(s,\hat\pi)\big]. $$

    对随机策略 \(\pi_\theta(a\mid o)\)，这就是最大似然 \(\max_\theta\sum\log\pi_\theta(a_t\mid o_t)\)：固定方差的高斯策略给出 MSE，离散动作给出交叉熵（16.2 节的“损失 = 负对数似然”）。\(\varepsilon\) 是监督学习能控制的量：数据越多、模型越好，它越小，并且有泛化界保证它在专家分布上的值。

    For a stochastic policy \(\pi_\theta(a\mid o)\) this is maximum likelihood, \(\max_\theta\sum\log\pi_\theta(a_t\mid o_t)\): a Gaussian policy with fixed variance gives MSE, discrete actions give cross-entropy ("losses are negative log-likelihoods", Lesson 16.2). \(\varepsilon\) is what supervised learning controls: more data and a better model make it smaller, and generalization bounds guarantee its value on the expert's distribution.

    BC 的 \(T^2\) 界（Ross 与 Bagnell，2010）BC's \(T^2\) bound (Ross & Bagnell, 2010)

    $$ J(\hat\pi_{\mathrm{BC}})\;\le\;J(\pi^*)+\hl{bcT2}{T^2\varepsilon} . $$

    证明梗概（耦合论证）。让 \(\hat\pi\) 和 \(\pi^*\) 共用同一串随机数（同样的初始状态和转移噪声）。只要 \(\hat\pi\) 在前 \(k\) 步都和专家选了同一个动作，两条轨迹就完全一样，记 \(\tau\) 为 \(\hat\pi\) 第一次选了和专家不同动作的那一步，那么“\(\tau\le t\)”意味着“在专家轨迹的某个第 \(k\le t\) 步上 \(\hat\pi(s_k)\ne\pi^*(s_k)\)”。由并集界，

    Proof sketch (a coupling argument). Let \(\hat\pi\) and \(\pi^*\) share one stream of randomness (the same initial state and transition noise). As long as \(\hat\pi\) picks the expert's action for the first \(k\) steps, the two trajectories are identical, so, writing \(\tau\) for the first step at which \(\hat\pi\) picks a different action from the expert, "\(\tau\le t\)" implies "\(\hat\pi(s_k)\ne\pi^*(s_k)\) at some step \(k\le t\) of the expert's trajectory". By the union bound,

    $$ P(\tau\le t)\;\le\;\sum_{k=1}^{t}\varepsilon_k\;\le\;\sum_{k=1}^{T}\varepsilon_k=T\varepsilon . $$

    分岔之前两条轨迹的代价相同，分岔之后每步最多多付 1，所以额外代价 \(\le\sum_{t=1}^T P(\tau\le t)\le T\cdot T\varepsilon\)。这个界是紧的：Ross 与 Bagnell 构造了让 BC 真的付出 \(\Theta(T^2\varepsilon)\) 的例子，FIG 2 的“掉下去就回不来”模型就是这一类（在 \(\varepsilon T\ll1\) 时代价 \(\approx\varepsilon T^2/2\)）。直觉上，\(T^2\) 里一个 \(T\) 来自“有 \(T\) 次机会犯第一个错”，另一个 \(T\) 来自“犯错之后还要付最多 \(T\) 步的代价”。

    Before divergence the two trajectories pay the same cost; after it the learner pays at most 1 extra per step, so the extra cost is \(\le\sum_{t=1}^T P(\tau\le t)\le T\cdot T\varepsilon\). The bound is tight: Ross and Bagnell construct examples where BC really pays \(\Theta(T^2\varepsilon)\), and FIG 2's "once off, never back" model is of this kind (cost \(\approx\varepsilon T^2/2\) for \(\varepsilon T\ll1\)). Intuitively, one \(T\) in \(T^2\) comes from "\(T\) chances to make the first mistake", the other from "after the mistake, up to \(T\) more steps of cost".

    在自己的分布上训练：\(uT\varepsilon\)Training on your own distribution: \(uT\varepsilon\)

    记 \(Q^*_t(s,a)\) 为“第 \(t\) 步在 \(s\) 做 \(a\)，之后按专家走”的期望剩余代价（6.12 节的 cost-to-go，加上一步动作），\(V^*_t(s)=Q^*_t(s,\pi^*(s))\)。性能差分引理（Kakade 与 Langford，2002 的有限时域形式）把两个策略的代价差写成学习者自己的状态分布上的逐步“劣势”之和：

    Let \(Q^*_t(s,a)\) be the expected remaining cost of doing \(a\) in \(s\) at step \(t\) and following the expert afterwards (the cost-to-go of Lesson 6.12 with one action prepended), and \(V^*_t(s)=Q^*_t(s,\pi^*(s))\). The performance difference lemma (in the finite-horizon form of Kakade and Langford, 2002) writes the cost difference of two policies as a sum of per-step "disadvantages" over the learner's own state distribution:

    $$ J(\hat\pi)-J(\pi^*)=\sum_{t=1}^{T}\mathbb E_{s\sim \hl{dpi}{d^t_{\hat\pi}}}\Big[Q^*_t\big(s,\hat\pi(s)\big)-V^*_t(s)\Big]\;\le\;u\sum_{t=1}^{T}\mathbb E_{s\sim d^t_{\hat\pi}}\big[\ell(s,\hat\pi)\big]=\hl{dagT}{uT\hat\varepsilon} . $$

    这里 \(u\) 是“走错一步之后，按专家继续走最多多付多少”：\(Q^*_t(s,a)-V^*_t(s)\le u\)。专家能很快纠正错误的任务（比如 FIG 1 的通道，专家 1 秒内就能拉回中线），\(u\) 是个小常数；FIG 2 的 \(u\) 就是平均回来需要的步数。关键在于 \(\hat\varepsilon\) 是在 \(d_{\hat\pi}\) 上量的：如果我们在学习者自己的分布上把监督误差压小，代价就只按 \(T\) 线性增长。

    难点是 \(d_{\hat\pi}\) 取决于 \(\hat\pi\)，而 \(\hat\pi\) 又取决于训练数据，像追自己的尾巴。DAgger 把它看成在线学习：第 \(i\) 轮的损失函数是 \(\ell_i(\pi)=\mathbb E_{s\sim d_{\pi_i}}[\ell(s,\pi)]\)，在聚合数据 \(\hl{agg}{\mathcal D}\) 上训练就是在所有历史损失之和上取最优，即在线学习里的“跟随领先者”（Follow-The-Leader）。只要这个学习过程“无悔”（平均损失追得上事后最好的那个固定策略），Ross 等（2011）就证明：当轮数足够多（随 \(uT\) 增长，差一个对数因子），并且 \(\beta_i\) 衰减得足够快时，序列里至少有一个策略满足

    Here \(u\) bounds how much extra cost one wrong step causes if the expert takes over afterwards: \(Q^*_t(s,a)-V^*_t(s)\le u\). In tasks where the expert corrects mistakes quickly (FIG 1's corridor, where the expert is back on the centre line within a second), \(u\) is a small constant; FIG 2's \(u\) is exactly the average number of steps to get back. The crux is that \(\hat\varepsilon\) is measured on \(d_{\hat\pi}\): if we make the supervised error small on the learner's own distribution, the cost grows only linearly in \(T\).

    The difficulty is that \(d_{\hat\pi}\) depends on \(\hat\pi\), which depends on the training data: chasing one's own tail. DAgger treats it as online learning: round \(i\)'s loss is \(\ell_i(\pi)=\mathbb E_{s\sim d_{\pi_i}}[\ell(s,\pi)]\), and training on the aggregated data \(\hl{agg}{\mathcal D}\) minimizes the sum of all past losses, which is "Follow-The-Leader" in online learning. As long as this learner is "no-regret" (its average loss keeps up with the best fixed policy in hindsight), Ross et al. (2011) prove that with enough rounds (growing with \(uT\), up to a log factor) and with \(\beta_i\) decaying fast enough, at least one policy in the sequence satisfies

    $$ J(\hat\pi)\;\le\;J(\pi^*)+uT\varepsilon_N+O(1), $$

    其中 \(\varepsilon_N\) 是事后看来最好的策略在所有轮的混合分布上的误差，也就是“如果我们事先知道学习者会去哪里，监督学习能做到多好”。实践中通常直接取最后一个策略，或者用验证集选。FIG 3 里“只用最新一轮”丢掉了聚合，相当于每轮只追最新的损失 \(\ell_i\)，这不是无悔的，结果来回摇摆。

    噪声注入换一个角度看：执行 \(\pi^*\) 时加噪声，等于把训练分布从 \(d_{\pi^*}\) 换成 \(d_{\tilde\pi^*}\)（\(\tilde\pi^*\) 表示加了噪声的专家）。如果噪声造成的偏差和学习者误差造成的偏差差不多大，后者大部分落在前者覆盖的范围里，\(\hat\varepsilon\) 和 \(\varepsilon\) 就差不多；DART 正是按这个目标去选噪声的。

    where \(\varepsilon_N\) is the error of the best policy in hindsight on the mixture of all rounds' distributions, i.e. "how well supervised learning could do if we knew in advance where the learner would go". In practice one usually takes the last policy or picks one on a validation set. FIG 3's "newest round only" drops the aggregation and chases only the latest loss \(\ell_i\) each round, which is not no-regret, and it oscillates.

    Noise injection from this angle: executing \(\pi^*\) with noise replaces the training distribution \(d_{\pi^*}\) by \(d_{\tilde\pi^*}\) (\(\tilde\pi^*\) denotes the noisy expert). If the deviations caused by the noise are about as large as those caused by the learner's errors, most of the latter fall inside the region the former covers, and \(\hat\varepsilon\) is close to \(\varepsilon\); DART chooses its noise with exactly that goal.

    MSE 的最优解是条件均值The MSE optimum is the conditional mean

    对固定的观测 \(o\)，最小化 \(\mathbb E[(f-a)^2\mid o]=f^2-2f\,\mathbb E[a\mid o]+\mathbb E[a^2\mid o]\)，对 \(f\) 求导置零：

    For a fixed observation \(o\), minimize \(\mathbb E[(f-a)^2\mid o]=f^2-2f\,\mathbb E[a\mid o]+\mathbb E[a^2\mid o]\); setting the derivative in \(f\) to zero gives

    $$ f^*(o)=\hl{mean}{\mathbb E[a\mid o]},\qquad p(a\mid o)=p\,\delta(a-a_L)+(1-p)\,\delta(a-a_R)\ \Rightarrow\ f^*=p\,a_L+(1-p)\,a_R . $$

    \(a_L=-a_R\) 时 \(f^*=(1-2p)a_R\)，FIG 5 的 \((1-2p)\times0.9\) m 就是把它沿路径积分的结果。L1 损失给出条件中位数，\(p=0.5\) 时区间 \([a_L,a_R]\) 里任何一点都是最优解，同样可能落在中间。根子在于：MSE 是固定方差高斯的负对数似然，而单峰高斯只能用“放在中间、方差变大”来解释两个峰。解决办法是换一个能表示多峰的 \(p_\theta(a\mid o)\)：混合高斯（混合密度网络）、离散化后的分类（17.5 节）、隐变量模型（19.1 节）、能量模型（19.5 节的隐式 BC）或扩散与 flow matching（22.2 节）。FIG 5 还说明了第二点：即便 \(p_\theta\) 是对的，逐步独立采样也会在模式间来回跳，所以这些方法还要在时间上保持一致（动作分块、固定隐变量）。

    With \(a_L=-a_R\), \(f^*=(1-2p)a_R\); FIG 5's \((1-2p)\times0.9\) m is this integrated along the path. The L1 loss gives the conditional median, and at \(p=0.5\) every point of \([a_L,a_R]\) is optimal, so it too may land in the middle. The root cause: MSE is the negative log-likelihood of a fixed-variance Gaussian, and a unimodal Gaussian can only explain two peaks by "sitting in the middle with a wide spread". The cure is a \(p_\theta(a\mid o)\) that can represent several peaks: a Gaussian mixture (mixture density network), classification over discretized actions (Lesson 17.5), latent-variable models (Lesson 19.1), energy-based models (the implicit BC of Lesson 19.5) or diffusion and flow matching (Lesson 22.2). FIG 5 makes a second point too: even with the right \(p_\theta\), sampling independently at every step jumps between modes, so these methods must also stay consistent over time (action chunks, a fixed latent).

    最早的 BC：ALVINN。Pomerleau（1989）用一个小神经网络驾驶卡内基梅隆大学的 Navlab 无人车，从低分辨率的道路图像直接输出方向盘方向，并且已经遇到了本节的问题：人开车时车总在车道中间，网络学不到怎么回正，于是他用几何变换合成“车偏了”的图像并配上回正的方向（1991 年的论文）。端到端驾驶：NVIDIA 的 Bojarski 等（2016）用 CNN 从图像预测方向盘角，左右两侧相机的图像配上加了修正量的方向盘角，用来教网络从偏离中恢复。DAgger 的实验：Ross 等（2011）在赛车游戏 Super Tux Kart 和 Super Mario Bros 上验证了 DAgger 远好于 BC；Ross 等（ICRA 2013）用 DAgger 训练一架四旋翼无人机仅凭单目相机在树林中避障飞行，由人类在回放的视频上给无人机到过的状态打标签。

    The first BC: ALVINN. Pomerleau (1989) used a small neural network to steer Carnegie Mellon's Navlab vehicle, mapping low-resolution road images directly to a steering direction, and already met this lesson's problem: a human driver keeps the car centred, so the network never learns to recover; he synthesized "the car has drifted" images by geometric transformation and paired them with corrective steering (in his 1991 paper). End-to-end driving: Bojarski et al. at NVIDIA (2016) predicted steering from images with a CNN, pairing images from left and right side cameras with corrected steering angles to teach the network to recover from offsets. DAgger's experiments: Ross et al. (2011) showed DAgger far ahead of BC on the racing game Super Tux Kart and on Super Mario Bros; Ross et al. (ICRA 2013) used DAgger to train a quadrotor to fly through a forest avoiding trees from a single camera, with a human labelling the states the drone had visited on replayed video.

    人在回路：HG-DAgger（Kelly 等，ICRA 2019）在驾驶任务上验证了“安全员接管”式的数据采集；SafeDAgger、EnsembleDAgger、ThriftyDAgger 让策略自己判断何时请人帮忙。今天很多机器人团队部署策略时都会记录人类接管，把这些片段加入下一轮训练，就是 HG-DAgger 的思路。现代 BC：RT-1（Brohan 等，2022）用约 13 万条移动操作机器人的示教做 BC，把动作离散成 token 训练 Transformer；ALOHA 上的 ACT（Zhao 等，RSS 2023）和 Diffusion Policy（Chi 等，RSS 2023）用动作分块和生成式动作头解决了本节的两个问题中的一部分（22.2 节）。经验教训：robomimic（Mandlekar 等，CoRL 2021）系统比较了多种离线模仿学习方法，发现带历史的 BC（BC-RNN）在人类示教上很强，而按验证损失挑选检查点并不可靠，必须实际执行评测；Codevilla 等（ICCV 2019）在驾驶上报告了 BC 的数据偏差、因果混淆，以及结果随训练随机种子大幅波动。

    Humans in the loop: HG-DAgger (Kelly et al., ICRA 2019) demonstrated "safety driver takes over" data collection on driving tasks; SafeDAgger, EnsembleDAgger and ThriftyDAgger let the policy itself decide when to ask for help. Many robotics teams today log human takeovers when deploying policies and add those segments to the next round of training, which is the HG-DAgger idea. Modern BC: RT-1 (Brohan et al., 2022) applied BC to about 130,000 demonstrations collected with mobile manipulators, discretizing actions into tokens for a transformer; ACT on ALOHA (Zhao et al., RSS 2023) and Diffusion Policy (Chi et al., RSS 2023) address part of both of this lesson's problems with action chunking and generative action heads (Lesson 22.2). Lessons learned: robomimic (Mandlekar et al., CoRL 2021) systematically compared offline imitation methods and found BC with history (BC-RNN) strong on human demonstrations, while picking checkpoints by validation loss was unreliable and real rollouts were needed; Codevilla et al. (ICCV 2019) reported dataset bias, causal confusion and large variation across training seeds for BC in driving.

    和其他方法的关系：BC 只用示教、不需要奖励。有奖励时，BC 常用来初始化强化学习（20.4 节），离线强化学习的很多方法（TD3+BC、IQL）可以看成“BC 加上一点价值函数的指导”（20.5 节）。逆强化学习和 GAIL 从示教里学奖励或判别器，再用强化学习在自己的分布上优化，这是绕开误差累积的另一条路（20.6 节）。

    Relations to other methods: BC uses only demonstrations and needs no reward. When a reward exists, BC often initializes reinforcement learning (Lesson 20.4), and many offline RL methods (TD3+BC, IQL) can be read as "BC plus a little guidance from a value function" (Lesson 20.5). Inverse RL and GAIL learn a reward or a discriminator from demonstrations and then optimize with RL on the policy's own distribution, another way around compounding errors (Lesson 20.6).

    11常见误区Common pitfalls

    误区 1：用训练或验证损失判断策略好坏Pitfall 1: judging a policy by its training or validation loss

    FIG 1 的策略在示教路线附近只差约 4°，验证集同样取自示教，自然也很好看，可 6 次闭环评测全部失败。开环的逐样本损失量的是 \(d_{\pi^*}\) 上的误差，成败取决于 \(d_{\hat\pi}\) 上的误差。策略一定要闭环评测（真机或仿真），多次试验、多个随机种子，报告成功率而不只是损失。

    FIG 1's policy is only about 4° off near the demonstrated route, and a validation set drawn from the demonstrations looks just as good, yet all 6 closed-loop runs fail. An open-loop per-sample loss measures the error on \(d_{\pi^*}\); success depends on the error on \(d_{\hat\pi}\). Always evaluate policies in closed loop (on the robot or in simulation), with many trials and several random seeds, and report success rates, not just losses.

    误区 2：“多采点示教就好了”Pitfall 2: "just collect more demonstrations"

    更多同样的示教只会把同一条细线描得更粗一点点：FIG 1 里 4 条无噪声示教仍然 0/6。数据要的是覆盖：包含偏离与恢复的示教（噪声注入、有意识地示范“回正”）、学习者自己到过的状态（DAgger、接管数据）、多样的起点和场景。数据量相同时，多样性往往比条数更重要。

    More of the same demonstrations only thicken the same thin line a little: in FIG 1, 4 noise-free demonstrations still score 0/6. What the data need is coverage: demonstrations that contain deviations and recoveries (noise injection, deliberately demonstrating corrections), states the learner itself visits (DAgger, takeover data), and varied starts and scenes. For the same amount of data, diversity often matters more than the count.

    误区 3：对多模态示教用 MSE，或者以为“换成分类就行了”Pitfall 3: MSE on multimodal demonstrations, or thinking "just switch to classification"

    MSE 学条件均值，两种做法的平均可能哪种都不是（FIG 5 直直撞上柱子）。换成能表示多峰的输出是必要的，但还不够：FIG 5 的分类头每步独立采样，在 \(p=0.5\) 时一样 20 次全撞。多峰的策略还需要在时间上坚持一个模式。反过来，示教者如果每次做法都一样（比如统一规定从左边绕），简单的 MSE BC 就够用，这也是为什么采集示教时常常约定统一的策略。

    MSE learns the conditional mean, and the average of two ways to act may be neither (FIG 5 drives straight into the pillar). An output that can represent several peaks is necessary but not sufficient: FIG 5's classification head, sampling independently at every step, also hits the pillar in all 20 trials at \(p=0.5\). A multimodal policy must also stick to one mode over time. Conversely, if demonstrators always act the same way (say, an agreed rule to pass on the left), plain MSE BC is enough, which is why data collection often fixes such conventions.

    误区 4：以为加历史一定有帮助Pitfall 4: assuming history always helps

    示教里相邻两步的动作几乎一样，所以“上一个动作”是预测“这一个动作”的极好特征。网络若能看到历史，就可能学会抄上一步而不看场景，部署时一旦自己的上一步错了，就一直错下去（“copycat” 问题，Wen 等，2020）。更一般地，看到的信息越多，越可能抓住和专家动作相关、但不是原因的特征（因果混淆，de Haan 等，2019）。历史要加，但要在闭环评测里检验，必要时对历史做 dropout 或只提供观测历史、不提供动作历史。

    In demonstrations consecutive actions are nearly identical, so "the previous action" is an excellent predictor of "this action". A network that sees history may learn to copy the previous step instead of looking at the scene, and at deployment, once its own previous step is wrong, it stays wrong (the "copycat" problem, Wen et al., 2020). More generally, the more information the policy sees, the more likely it latches onto features that correlate with the expert's actions without causing them (causal confusion, de Haan et al., 2019). Add history, but check it in closed-loop evaluation, and if needed apply dropout to the history or provide observation history without action history.

    误区 5：DAgger 的实际坑：不聚合、不安全、标签难打Pitfall 5: DAgger in practice: no aggregation, no safety, hard labels

    只在新数据上训练会遗忘（FIG 3 的“只用最新一轮”每轮 0/6），聚合是理论保证的一部分，不是可选项。让半成品策略在真机上自己开可能撞坏东西：用 \(\beta\) 混合专家动作、设置安全边界、或采用人类门控（FIG 4）。最后，人类很难在不掌握控制权时给出好标签（不知道这一下打多少方向合适），这正是 HG-DAgger 这类“让人接管”的方法流行的原因。

    Training only on the new data forgets (FIG 3's "newest round only" scores 0/6 every round); aggregation is part of the guarantee, not an option. Letting a half-trained policy drive a real robot can break things: mix in expert actions with \(\beta\), set safety limits, or use human gating (FIG 4). Finally, people find it hard to give good labels when they are not in control (how much should I steer here?), which is why "let a person take over" methods such as HG-DAgger are popular.
