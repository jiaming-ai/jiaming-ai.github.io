静力学与 wrenchStatics & Wrenches

机器人的活儿都干在接触里：拧螺丝、打磨、插孔、搬东西。接触靠力，而刚体上的"一个力"远不止三个数：同样 10 N，推在门把手上和推在门轴上，转动效果天差地别。把力矩和力打包成一个六维向量，就是 wrench，它与 twist 互为对偶，功率正是两者的内积。有了它，换坐标系只要用伴随的转置，关节力矩就是 τ = g(q) + JᵀF，手臂在哪里有力、哪里无力，一个力椭球就说清楚了。 Robots do their jobs in contact: driving screws, polishing, inserting pegs, carrying loads. Contact means force, and "a force" on a rigid body is more than three numbers: the same 10 N pushed on a door handle or on the hinge turns the door very differently. Pack the moment and the force into one six-dimensional vector and you get a wrench, the dual of the twist: their inner product is power. With it, changing frames needs only the transpose of the adjoint, the joint torques are τ = g(q) + JᵀF, and one force ellipsoid tells you where an arm is strong and where it is helpless.

    1为什么机器人需要它Why robots need this

    拧螺丝要沿轴向压、同时绕轴转；打磨要贴着曲面维持一个恒定的法向力；插孔对不准时，要让接触力把零件"引"进去；搬箱子时，手腕要承受箱子的重量，以及重量对手腕的力矩。这些任务里，机器人和世界之间交换的不是位置，而是力。可是作用在刚体上的力不只是三个数。作用点不同，同一个力对物体的转动效果就不同，所以描述接触，必须把力和它对参考点的力矩放在一起，成为一个六维量：wrench（力旋量）。

    Driving a screw means pressing along its axis while turning about it; polishing means holding a constant normal force on a curved surface; inserting a misaligned peg means letting the contact force "guide" it in; carrying a box means the wrist must bear the box's weight and the moment that weight exerts on the wrist. In these tasks what the robot exchanges with the world is not position but force. Yet a force on a rigid body is more than three numbers: the same force applied at different points turns the body differently. So describing contact requires putting the force together with its moment about a reference point into one six-dimensional quantity: a wrench.

    It is the partner of the twist from Lesson 7.4. A twist says how a rigid body moves (angular and linear velocity), a wrench says how the world pushes on it (moment and force), and pairing the two gives power. This lesson answers four questions:

    • How do we describe a contact force with one 6D vector, and convert it between the sensor frame, the tool-tip frame and the world frame? (FIG 1, FIG 2)
    • What joint torques hold up a wrench at the gripper, and how much more do the weights of the links and the payload cost? (FIG 3)
    • Given the motors' torque limits, where in the workspace is the arm strongest and where weakest? (FIG 4, FIG 5)
    • A wrist force sensor's reading includes the weight of the tool. How do we subtract it and keep the true contact force? (FIG 6)

    Lesson 3.4 already derived \(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top\mathbf F\) in the plane by virtual work and drew the force ellipse. This lesson is its 3D sequel and does not repeat those derivations. It adds three new things: the moment becomes a vector (it has a direction and a reference point), changing frames uses the transpose of the adjoint, and once the arm is not lying flat on a table, gravity becomes the largest "external force" in statics.

    2直觉：wrench = 力 + 相对一点的力矩Intuition: a wrench is a force plus its moment about a point

    推一扇门。力的大小方向都一样，推在门把手上，门轻松打开；推在门轴上，门纹丝不动。差别在于杠杆臂：力沿作用线可以滑动而效果不变，但作用线离转轴越远，转动效果越大。要记录"外界对这个刚体做了什么"，就先选一个参考点 \(O\)，然后记下两样东西：总力 \(\mathbf f\)，以及所有力对 \(O\) 的总力矩 \(\mathbf m\)（\(\mathbf m=\sum\mathbf x_i\times\mathbf f_i\)，叉积见 1.8）。这一对就是 \(O\) 处的 wrench。

    参考点换到 \(O'\)，总力不变，力矩却变了，因为每个力的杠杆臂变了：若 \(\mathbf r\) 是从 \(O\) 指向 \(O'\) 的向量，则 \(\mathbf m'=\sum(\mathbf x_i-\mathbf r)\times\mathbf f_i=\mathbf m-\mathbf r\times\mathbf f\)。注意是减号：参考点向力的作用线靠近，力矩变小；落在作用线上，力矩恰好为零。再看两个特殊情形。一对大小相等方向相反、不共线的力叫力偶：总力为零，力矩 \(\mathbf m\) 与参考点无关（拧瓶盖时双手的作用），所以力偶是"自由向量"，可以放在刚体上任何地方。一般的 wrench 是力加力偶，总存在一条中心轴：参考点落在这条线上时，力矩与力平行，大小为 \(|h|\,\|\mathbf f\|\)，\(h\) 叫螺距。它正是 twist 的螺旋轴（5.6）在力学里的镜像。

    Push a door. The force has the same size and direction, yet pushed at the handle the door swings open, pushed at the hinge it does not budge. The difference is the lever arm: a force may slide along its line of action without changing its effect, but the farther that line is from the axis, the stronger the turning effect. To record "what the world does to this rigid body", first choose a reference point \(O\), then write down two things: the total force \(\mathbf f\) and the total moment \(\mathbf m\) of all forces about \(O\) (\(\mathbf m=\sum\mathbf x_i\times\mathbf f_i\); cross product in 1.8). That pair is the wrench at \(O\).

    Move the reference point to \(O'\) and the total force is unchanged, yet the moment changes, because every lever arm changes: if \(\mathbf r\) points from \(O\) to \(O'\), then \(\mathbf m'=\sum(\mathbf x_i-\mathbf r)\times\mathbf f_i=\mathbf m-\mathbf r\times\mathbf f\). Note the minus sign: moving the reference point toward the force's line shrinks the moment, and a point on the line of action gives exactly zero. Two special cases. Two equal and opposite non-collinear forces form a couple: the total force is zero and the moment \(\mathbf m\) does not depend on the reference point (think of both hands turning a bottle cap), so a couple is a "free vector" that can sit anywhere on the body. A general wrench is a force plus a couple, and always has a central axis: for a reference point on that line the moment is parallel to the force, of size \(|h|\,\|\mathbf f\|\), where \(h\) is called the pitch. It is the mechanical mirror of the twist's screw axis (5.6).

    约定：moment 在前，force 在后Convention: moment first, force second

    本课（和整个课程）的 wrench 写作 \(\mathbf F=(\mathbf m,\mathbf f)\in\mathbb R^6\)，力矩在前、力在后，与 twist \(\boldsymbol\xi=(\boldsymbol\omega,\mathbf v)\)（角速度在前）一一对应，这样功率就是 \(P=\boldsymbol\xi^\top\mathbf F=\boldsymbol\omega\cdot\mathbf m+\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf f\)。单位：力矩 N·m，力 N，功率 W。

    Throughout this lesson (and the course) a wrench is written \(\mathbf F=(\mathbf m,\mathbf f)\in\mathbb R^6\), moment first, force second, matching the twist \(\boldsymbol\xi=(\boldsymbol\omega,\mathbf v)\) (angular first) entry by entry, so that power is \(P=\boldsymbol\xi^\top\mathbf F=\boldsymbol\omega\cdot\mathbf m+\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf f\). Units: moment N·m, force N, power W.

    核心洞见Key insight

    wrench 不是"一个力"，而是"力 + 参考点"。换参考点，力不变、力矩按 \(\mathbf m'=\mathbf m-\mathbf r\times\mathbf f\) 变。有一个量不随参考点变：\(\mathbf f\cdot\mathbf m\)（力在力矩方向上的投影），它就是螺距 \(h=\mathbf f\cdot\mathbf m/\|\mathbf f\|^2\) 的分子。读数里可以验证。

    A wrench is not "a force" but "a force plus a reference point". Move the point and the force stays, while the moment changes by \(\mathbf m'=\mathbf m-\mathbf r\times\mathbf f\). One quantity does not depend on the reference point: \(\mathbf f\cdot\mathbf m\) (the component of the moment along the force), the numerator of the pitch \(h=\mathbf f\cdot\mathbf m/\|\mathbf f\|^2\). You can check it in the readout.

    In a real robot a wrench always has to change frames: a wrist force sensor reports in the sensor frame {s}, what you care about is the force at the tool-tip contact {c}, and the controller wants quantities in the world frame {w}. Physically it is one and the same interaction; only the reference point and the axes differ. There is one rule, which follows from power invariance (derived in 5.8, and again in Section 5). Let \(T_{ab}\) be the pose of {b} in {a}:

    $$ \underbrace{\boldsymbol\xi_a=\operatorname{Ad}_{T_{ab}}\,\boldsymbol\xi_b}_{\text{twist: }b\to a},\qquad \underbrace{\mathbf F_b=\operatorname{Ad}_{T_{ab}}^{\!\top}\,\mathbf F_a}_{\text{wrench: }a\to b},\qquad\text{so}\qquad \mathbf F_s=\operatorname{Ad}_{T_{cs}}^{\!\top}\,\mathbf F_c. $$

    手臂在哪里有力？负载地图Where is the arm strong? A payload map

    重力项对每个关节都是姿态的函数，所以"这条臂能举多重"没有一个数字的答案，而是一张地图。对垂直平面（\(q_1=0\)）里的每个手爪位置，用解析逆运动学（肘部朝上、工具俯仰角 \(\psi\) 固定）求出姿态，再解出使所有六个关节力矩都不超限的最大负载。力矩对负载质量是线性的，\(\tau_i=a_i+m\,b_i\)，所以答案是闭式的。哪个关节先到上限，也一并标出。

    三维的力椭球与对偶Force ellipsoids in 3D and the duality

    3.4 节在平面里画过：关节速度的单位球 \(\|\dot{\boldsymbol\theta}\|\le1\) 经 \(J\) 映成手爪速度椭圆（半轴 \(\sigma_i\)），单位关节力矩球 \(\|\boldsymbol\tau\|\le1\) 经 \(J^\top\) 的"逆"映成力椭圆（半轴 \(1/\sigma_i\)，方向相同）。三维里把 \(J\) 换成线速度部分 \(J_v\in\mathbb R^{3\times6}\)（点 \(\mathbf p\) 的速度：\(J_v=J_{s,\text{lin}}-[\mathbf p]_\times J_{s,\text{ang}}\)），得到两个椭球：速度椭球（琥珀）和力椭球（紫）。两者主轴相同，但一个的长轴正是另一个的短轴：手爪容易动的方向出不了大力，难动的方向顶得住大力。手臂拉直时 \(\sigma_{\min}\to0\)：速度椭球被压成一个盘，力椭球拉成一根针，沿手臂方向，结构本身承担载荷，电机几乎不出力（3.4 节"速度与力"）。

    切换到"转动"：用 \(J_\omega\)（角速度部分）得到角速度椭球和力矩椭球。注意单位：\(J_v\) 是 m/rad，\(J_\omega\) 是无量纲，不能把两块拼成 6×6 再做 SVD（3.4 节误区 4），所以平动与转动要分开看，或者先用一个特征长度做归一化。

    Lesson 3.4 drew this in the plane: the unit ball of joint rates \(\|\dot{\boldsymbol\theta}\|\le1\) maps through \(J\) to a tip-velocity ellipse (semi-axes \(\sigma_i\)), and the unit ball of joint torques \(\|\boldsymbol\tau\|\le1\) maps through the "inverse" of \(J^\top\) to a force ellipse (semi-axes \(1/\sigma_i\), same directions). In 3D replace \(J\) by its linear-velocity part \(J_v\in\mathbb R^{3\times6}\) (velocity of the point \(\mathbf p\): \(J_v=J_{s,\text{lin}}-[\mathbf p]_\times J_{s,\text{ang}}\)) and you get two ellipsoids: the velocity ellipsoid (amber) and the force ellipsoid (purple). They share principal axes, but the long axis of one is the short axis of the other: along the direction where the tip moves easily it cannot push hard, and along the direction where it barely moves it holds a large force. When the arm is stretched out, \(\sigma_{\min}\to0\): the velocity ellipsoid is squashed into a disc and the force ellipsoid stretched into a needle along the arm; the structure itself carries the load and the motors hardly work (the "velocity and force" callout of Lesson 3.4).

    Switch to "rotation": use \(J_\omega\) (the angular part) to get the angular-velocity and moment ellipsoids. Mind the units: \(J_v\) is in m/rad and \(J_\omega\) is dimensionless, so you cannot stack them into a 6×6 and take one SVD (Pitfall 4 of Lesson 3.4). Look at translation and rotation separately, or normalize with a characteristic length first.

    几何：一条作用线上的力，加上一个力偶，画成箭头（FIG 1）。代数：一个 6 维向量 \((\mathbf m,\mathbf f)\)，随坐标系用 \(\operatorname{Ad}^\top\) 变换（FIG 2）。能量：一个把 twist 变成功率的线性泛函 \(\boldsymbol\xi\mapsto\boldsymbol\xi^\top\mathbf F\)。第三种看法最深：twist 是"向量"，wrench 是"余向量"（对偶空间里的元素）。向量和余向量变换时用互逆转置的矩阵（\(\operatorname{Ad}\) 与 \(\operatorname{Ad}^{-\top}\)），就像速度 \(J\dot{\boldsymbol\theta}\) 用 \(J\)、力矩 \(J^\top\mathbf F\) 用 \(J^\top\)。凡是看到"转置"，想想"功率要保持不变"。

    Geometry: a force on a line of action plus a couple, drawn as arrows (FIG 1). Algebra: a 6-vector \((\mathbf m,\mathbf f)\) that changes frame by \(\operatorname{Ad}^\top\) (FIG 2). Energy: a linear functional \(\boldsymbol\xi\mapsto\boldsymbol\xi^\top\mathbf F\) that turns twists into power. The third view is the deepest: a twist is a "vector", a wrench is a "covector" (an element of the dual space). Vectors and covectors transform with inverse-transpose matrices (\(\operatorname{Ad}\) versus \(\operatorname{Ad}^{-\top}\)), just as velocity uses \(J\) in \(J\dot{\boldsymbol\theta}\) and torque uses \(J^\top\) in \(J^\top\mathbf F\).     wrench 的定义，以及为什么是（力矩，力）The definition, and why (moment, force)

    设刚体上有一组力 \(\mathbf f_i\)，作用在点 \(\mathbf x_i\)（都在坐标系 {a} 里写）。{a} 原点处的 wrench 是

    Let a rigid body carry forces \(\mathbf f_i\) at points \(\mathbf x_i\) (all written in frame {a}). The wrench at the origin of {a} is

    $$ \mathbf F_a=\begin{bmatrix}\hl{m}{\cw{\mathbf m}}\\ \hl{f}{\cv{\mathbf f}}\end{bmatrix},\qquad \cv{\mathbf f}=\sum_i\mathbf f_i,\qquad \cw{\mathbf m}=\sum_i\mathbf x_i\times\mathbf f_i . $$

    刚体的 twist \(\boldsymbol\xi=(\boldsymbol\omega,\mathbf v)\)，其中 \(\mathbf v\) 是刚体上此刻位于 {a} 原点的那个点的速度（5.8 节），点 \(\mathbf x_i\) 的速度是 \(\mathbf v+\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf x_i\)。所有力做功的功率：

    The body's twist is \(\boldsymbol\xi=(\boldsymbol\omega,\mathbf v)\), where \(\mathbf v\) is the velocity of the point of the body currently at the origin of {a} (Lesson 5.8); the point \(\mathbf x_i\) moves with velocity \(\mathbf v+\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf x_i\).     其中用到标量三重积 \(\mathbf f\cdot(\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf x)=\boldsymbol\omega\cdot(\mathbf x\times\mathbf f)\)（1.8 节）。这就说明了顺序：为了让功率是两个 6 维向量的普通内积，wrench 必须按 twist 的 \((\boldsymbol\omega,\mathbf v)\) 排成 \((\mathbf m,\mathbf f)\)。

    using the scalar triple product \(\mathbf f\cdot(\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf x)=\boldsymbol\omega\cdot(\mathbf x\times\mathbf f)\) (Lesson 1.8). This explains the ordering: for the power to be an ordinary inner product of two 6-vectors, the wrench must be arranged as \((\mathbf m,\mathbf f)\) to match the twist's \((\boldsymbol\omega,\mathbf v)\).

    We want a reference point \(\mathbf c\) with \(\mathbf m_c=\mathbf m-\mathbf c\times\mathbf f\) parallel to \(\mathbf f\). Take \(\mathbf c_0=\mathbf f\times\mathbf m/\|\mathbf f\|^2\); by the double cross product identity \(\mathbf c_0\times\mathbf f=\big[\mathbf m\|\mathbf f\|^2-\mathbf f(\mathbf f\cdot\mathbf m)\big]/\|\mathbf f\|^2\), so

    $$ \mathbf m_{c_0}=\mathbf m-\mathbf c_0\times\mathbf f=\hl{axis}{h}\,\mathbf f,\qquad h=\frac{\mathbf f\cdot\mathbf m}{\|\mathbf f\|^2},\qquad \text{axis}=\{\mathbf c_0+s\,\mathbf f\}. $$

    沿 \(\mathbf f\) 方向平移参考点不改变 \(\mathbf c\times\mathbf f\)，所以整条直线都是中心轴；在这条线上力矩最小（\(\|\mathbf m\|\) 取最小值 \(|h|\,\|\mathbf f\|\)）。与 twist 的螺旋轴 \((\boldsymbol\omega,\mathbf v)\)（5.6 节）逐项对应，只是把 \((\boldsymbol\omega,\mathbf v)\) 换成 \((\mathbf f,\mathbf m)\)。顺带一个重要的概念：若 \(\boldsymbol\xi^\top\mathbf F=0\)，称两者互易，该 wrench 沿这个运动不做功。铰链提供的约束 wrench 与它允许的 twist 互易：这是 8.4 节描述接触约束的语言。

    Sliding the reference point along \(\mathbf f\) does not change \(\mathbf c\times\mathbf f\), so the whole line is the central axis; on it the moment is smallest (\(\|\mathbf m\|\) takes its minimum \(|h|\,\|\mathbf f\|\)). It mirrors the twist's screw axis \((\boldsymbol\omega,\mathbf v)\) (Lesson 5.6) term by term, with \((\boldsymbol\omega,\mathbf v)\) replaced by \((\mathbf f,\mathbf m)\). The constraint wrenches of a hinge are reciprocal to the twists it allows, which is the language of contact constraints in Lesson 8.4.

    虚功：为什么是 JᵀF（三维版）Virtual work: why JᵀF (the 3D version)

    3.4 节在平面里推过。三维里完全一样，只需把"力×位移"换成"wrench×twist"。关节虚位移 \(\delta\mathbf q\) 使手爪产生虚位移 twist \(\delta\boldsymbol\xi=J\delta\mathbf q\)（\(J\) 是 7.4 节的空间或机体雅可比，\(\mathbf F\) 取同一个系里的 wrench）。手爪对环境做的虚功必须由电机提供（扣除位能的增加 \(\delta U\)）：

    Lesson 3.4 did this in the plane. In 3D it is the same with "force × displacement" replaced by "wrench × twist". A virtual joint displacement \(\delta\mathbf q\) gives the gripper a virtual twist \(\delta\boldsymbol\xi=J\delta\mathbf q\) (with \(J\) the space or body Jacobian of Lesson 7.4 and \(\mathbf F\) the wrench in the same frame). The virtual work the gripper does on the environment must be supplied by the motors (minus the gain in potential energy \(\delta U\)):

    $$ \boldsymbol\tau^\top\delta\mathbf q=\delta U+\mathbf F^\top\delta\boldsymbol\xi=\Big(\tfrac{\partial U}{\partial\mathbf q}\Big)^{\!\top}\delta\mathbf q+\mathbf F^\top J\,\delta\mathbf q\quad\forall\,\delta\mathbf q\ \Longrightarrow\ \boldsymbol\tau=\mathbf g(\mathbf q)+J^\top\mathbf F . $$

    \(J_s^\top\mathbf F_s=J_b^\top\mathbf F_b\) 的验证：\(J_b=\operatorname{Ad}_{T_{bs}}J_s\)，\(\mathbf F_b=\operatorname{Ad}_{T_{sb}}^{\!\top}\mathbf F_s\)，故 \(J_b^\top\mathbf F_b=J_s^\top\operatorname{Ad}_{T_{bs}}^{\!\top}\operatorname{Ad}_{T_{sb}}^{\!\top}\mathbf F_s=J_s^\top(\operatorname{Ad}_{T_{sb}}\operatorname{Ad}_{T_{bs}})^{\top}\mathbf F_s=J_s^\top\mathbf F_s\)。位能 \(U=\sum_k m_kg\,z_k(\mathbf q)\)，所以 \(\mathbf g=\sum_k m_kg\,J_{v,k}^\top\hat{\mathbf z}\)，其中 \(J_{v,k}\) 是第 \(k\) 个质点的线速度雅可比，只有靠近基座一侧、会移动这个质点的关节才有非零列。（FIG 3 的重力项、FIG 4 的负载地图都是这样算的。这里的 \(\mathbf g(\mathbf q)\) 是静止时的保持力矩；速度不为零时还有惯性与科氏项，那是第 8 章动力学的内容，8.3。）

    To check \(J_s^\top\mathbf F_s=J_b^\top\mathbf F_b\): \(J_b=\operatorname{Ad}_{T_{bs}}J_s\) and \(\mathbf F_b=\operatorname{Ad}_{T_{sb}}^{\!\top}\mathbf F_s\), so \(J_b^\top\mathbf F_b=J_s^\top\operatorname{Ad}_{T_{bs}}^{\!\top}\operatorname{Ad}_{T_{sb}}^{\!\top}\mathbf F_s=J_s^\top(\operatorname{Ad}_{T_{sb}}\operatorname{Ad}_{T_{bs}})^{\top}\mathbf F_s=J_s^\top\mathbf F_s\). The potential energy is \(U=\sum_k m_kg\,z_k(\mathbf q)\), hence \(\mathbf g=\sum_k m_kg\,J_{v,k}^\top\hat{\mathbf z}\), where \(J_{v,k}\) is the linear-velocity Jacobian of the \(k\)-th point mass; only joints on the base side of that mass, which can move it, have nonzero columns. (This is how the gravity term of FIG 3 and the payload map of FIG 4 are computed. \(\mathbf g(\mathbf q)\) is the holding torque at rest; when moving there are also inertial and Coriolis terms, the subject of Chapter 8, 8.3.)

    The joint torques can also be computed without \(J\): balance forces and moments link by link from the tip to the base. Let \(\mathbf F_i\) be the wrench that link \(i-1\) applies to link \(i\) through joint \(i\) (in frame {i}), and \(\mathbf F_{n+1}\) the wrench the gripper applies to the environment. At rest link \(i\) feels \(\mathbf F_i\), the reaction \(-\mathbf F_{i+1}\) (moved into frame {i}) and gravity, and the three balance:

    $$ \mathbf F_i=\operatorname{Ad}_{T_{i+1,i}}^{\!\top}\mathbf F_{i+1}+\mathbf F^{\text{g}}_i,\qquad \tau_i=\mathbf F_i^\top\mathbf A_i,\qquad i=n,\dots,1 . $$

    其中 \(\mathbf F^{\text{g}}_i\) 是支撑连杆 \(i\) 自重所需的 wrench（重力 wrench 的反号）；\(\mathbf A_i\) 是关节 \(i\) 在 {i} 系里的螺旋轴，\(\tau_i=\mathbf F_i^\top\mathbf A_i\) 取的是关节能传递的那个分量：电机只承担 wrench 在自己轴上的投影，其余五个分量由轴承的约束反力承担（与上面的"互易"同一回事）。这就是牛顿–欧拉逆动力学的静态特例，8.2 加上加速度项就是完整算法。

    where \(\mathbf F^{\text{g}}_i\) is the wrench needed to support link \(i\)'s weight (minus gravity's wrench on it) and \(\mathbf A_i\) is joint \(i\)'s screw axis in frame {i}, and \(\tau_i=\mathbf F_i^\top\mathbf A_i\) picks the one component the joint can transmit: the motor carries only the projection of the wrench onto its own axis, while the other five components are carried by the bearing's constraint forces (the same thing as "reciprocal" above).     雅可比降秩时：Jᵀ 的零空间When the Jacobian loses rank: the null space of Jᵀ

    \(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top\mathbf F\)：若 \(\mathbf F\in\ker J^\top=(\operatorname{im}J)^\perp\)，则 \(\boldsymbol\tau=0\)。六轴臂在非奇异位形下 \(J\) 是可逆的 6×6，\(\ker J^\top=\{0\}\)，任何非零 wrench 都需要关节出力。奇异位形下 \(\ker J^\top\neq\{0\}\)：存在非零 wrench（FIG 5 里的"针"）完全由结构承担，电机出力为零。反过来估计接触力：\(\hat{\mathbf F}=J^{-\top}(\boldsymbol\tau_{\text{meas}}-\mathbf g)\)，测量误差被放大 \(1/\sigma_{\min}\) 倍，在奇异方向上估计会崩溃。

    With \(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top\mathbf F\): if \(\mathbf F\in\ker J^\top=(\operatorname{im}J)^\perp\), then \(\boldsymbol\tau=0\). A six-axis arm away from singularities has an invertible 6×6 \(J\), \(\ker J^\top=\{0\}\), and any nonzero wrench needs joint effort. At a singularity \(\ker J^\top\neq\{0\}\): some nonzero wrenches (the "needle" in FIG 5) are carried entirely by the structure, at zero motor effort. Estimating a contact force the other way round, \(\hat{\mathbf F}=J^{-\top}(\boldsymbol\tau_{\text{meas}}-\mathbf g)\), amplifies the measurement error by \(1/\sigma_{\min}\), and the estimate breaks down along singular directions.

    预告：摩擦锥与抓取方程Previews: the friction cone and the grasp equation

    摩擦（8.4）：点接触能传递的 wrench 不是任意的。在接触系里（\(z\) 沿法向）是 \((\mathbf m,\mathbf f)=(0,\ f_x,f_y,f_z)\)，且 \(f_z\ge0\)、\(\sqrt{f_x^2+f_y^2}\le\mu f_z\)：六维 wrench 空间里的一个圆锥，称摩擦锥。抓取（9.3）：\(k\) 个接触把各自的 wrench 用 \(\operatorname{Ad}^\top\) 搬到物体系再相加，\(\mathbf w=\sum_iG_i\mathbf f_i=G\mathbf f_c\)，静止要求 \(G\mathbf f_c=-\mathbf w_{\text{ext}}\)（例如重力）且每个 \(\mathbf f_i\) 都在摩擦锥内。接触力能不能"顶住"任意方向的外力，就是力封闭的问题。

    Friction (8.4): a point contact cannot transmit an arbitrary wrench. In the contact frame (\(z\) along the normal) it is \((\mathbf m,\mathbf f)=(0,\ f_x,f_y,f_z)\) with \(f_z\ge0\) and \(\sqrt{f_x^2+f_y^2}\le\mu f_z\): a circular cone in the 6D wrench space, the friction cone. Grasping (9.3): \(k\) contacts move their wrenches to the object frame with \(\operatorname{Ad}^\top\) and add them, \(\mathbf w=\sum_iG_i\mathbf f_i=G\mathbf f_c\); equilibrium requires \(G\mathbf f_c=-\mathbf w_{\text{ext}}\) (for instance gravity) with every \(\mathbf f_i\) inside its friction cone. Whether the contacts can "resist" an external wrench in any direction is the question of force closure.

    真实系统里In real systems

    六维力/力矩传感器：ATI（Mini/Gamma/Nano 系列）、Robotiq FT 300、OnRobot HEX 等装在腕部，报告传感器系里的 wrench，使用前要先"去皮"（tare）并做重力补偿。关节力矩传感：Franka Emika Panda 的每个关节都有力矩传感器，KUKA LBR iiwa 同样；控制器用 \(\hat{\mathbf F}_{\text{ext}}=J^{-\top}(\boldsymbol\tau_{\text{meas}}-\mathbf g-\dots)\) 估计末端受到的外力（libfranka 把估计值放在机器人状态里，顺序为先力后力矩）。电机电流估计：没有力矩传感器的臂（例如 UR 的 CB3 一代）靠电流估计关节力矩，再用 \(J^\top\) 反推，精度受齿轮摩擦限制（9.4 节）。重力补偿 + 手把手示教：控制器输出 \(\boldsymbol\tau=\mathbf g(\mathbf q)\)，人推一下手臂就能轻松移动，这是示教与数据采集的基础。阻抗/导纳控制（Hogan 1985；Part IV）：把 \(\mathbf F=K\,\delta\mathbf x+\dots\) 做成控制律，插孔、打磨、人机协作都靠它。遥操作与力反馈：力觉手柄（如 Phantom、Omega 系列）把远端测到的 wrench 经 \(J^\top\) 变成手柄电机力矩。足式机器人：脚底接触 wrench 经 \(J^\top\) 变成腿部关节力矩，全身控制把它们当约束来优化（9.2 节）。

    Six-axis F/T sensors: ATI (Mini, Gamma, Nano series), Robotiq FT 300, OnRobot HEX and others sit at the wrist and report a wrench in the sensor frame; they must be tared and gravity-compensated before use. Joint torque sensing: every joint of the Franka Emika Panda has a torque sensor, as does the KUKA LBR iiwa; the controller estimates the external wrench \(\hat{\mathbf F}_{\text{ext}}=J^{-\top}(\boldsymbol\tau_{\text{meas}}-\mathbf g-\dots)\) (libfranka exposes the estimate in the robot state, ordered force first, then torque). Motor-current estimation: arms without torque sensors (for example the UR CB3 generation) estimate joint torques from motor current and invert with \(J^\top\), limited by gear friction (Lesson 9.4). Gravity compensation + hand guiding: the controller outputs \(\boldsymbol\tau=\mathbf g(\mathbf q)\), so a light push moves the arm freely, the basis of kinesthetic teaching and demonstration collection. Impedance/admittance control (Hogan 1985; Part IV): make \(\mathbf F=K\,\delta\mathbf x+\dots\) the control law; peg-in-hole insertion, polishing and human-robot collaboration depend on it. Teleoperation with force feedback: haptic devices (the Phantom and Omega series, for instance) turn the wrench measured at the remote side into handle motor torques through \(J^\top\). Legged robots: foot contact wrenches become leg joint torques through \(J^\top\), and whole-body controllers optimize over them as constraints (Lesson 9.2).

    7常见误区Common pitfalls

    误区 1：把 wrench 当 twist 来换系（用 Ad 而不是 Adᵀ）Pitfall 1: transforming a wrench like a twist (Ad instead of Adᵀ)

    Ad 和 Adᵀ 的块结构看起来很像，只差一个转置，代码里最容易写错，而且不会报错：数字照样算出来，只是错的。FIG 2 的功率检查就是用来抓这个错误的。一个反例：传感器在世界系里的位置是 \(\mathbf p=(1,0,0)\)，朝向与世界相同，施加纯力 \(\mathbf f=(0,0,1)\) N（力矩为零）。正确答案 \(\mathbf F_s=\operatorname{Ad}^\top\mathbf F_w\) 给出力矩 \(-\mathbf p\times\mathbf f=(0,1,0)\) N·m，而用 Ad 得到的是 \((0,0,0,\ 0,0,1)\)：杠杆臂造成的力矩整个丢了。写完换系的函数，永远用 \(\boldsymbol\xi_a^\top\mathbf F_a=\boldsymbol\xi_b^\top\mathbf F_b\) 做单元测试。

    Ad and Adᵀ have nearly the same block structure, differing by one transpose, so this is the easiest thing to get wrong in code, and it raises no error: you still get numbers, just wrong ones. The power check in FIG 2 exists to catch it. A counterexample: the sensor sits at \(\mathbf p=(1,0,0)\) in the world, with the world's orientation, and the world wrench is a pure force \(\mathbf f=(0,0,1)\) N (zero moment). The correct \(\mathbf F_s=\operatorname{Ad}^\top\mathbf F_w\) gives the moment \(-\mathbf p\times\mathbf f=(0,1,0)\) N·m, while using Ad gives \((0,0,0,\ 0,0,1)\): the moment due to the lever arm is lost entirely. After writing any frame-change function, unit-test it with \(\boldsymbol\xi_a^\top\mathbf F_a=\boldsymbol\xi_b^\top\mathbf F_b\).

    误区 2：(m, f) 与 (f, m) 的顺序，以及 Ad 的块顺序Pitfall 2: (m, f) versus (f, m), and the block order of Ad

    不同库的约定不同：本课、Modern Robotics、Featherstone 的空间向量、Drake 都是（角/力矩在前，线/力在后）；ROS 的 geometry_msgs/Twist 是先 linear 后 angular，Wrench 是分开的 force、torque 两个字段；Pinocchio 的 6 维向量先线性后角；libfranka 的外力估计也是先力后力矩。顺序一变，Ad 的块也要对调（\(\begin{bmatrix}R&0\\ [\mathbf p]_\times R&R\end{bmatrix}\) 变成 \(\begin{bmatrix}R&[\mathbf p]_\times R\\ 0&R\end{bmatrix}\)）。接别人的数据或库时，先用 \(\boldsymbol\xi^\top\mathbf F\) 的一个已知例子验证顺序。

    Libraries disagree: this lesson, Modern Robotics, Featherstone's spatial vectors and Drake put angular/moment first and linear/force second; ROS's geometry_msgs/Twist has linear first then angular, and Wrench has separate force and torque fields; Pinocchio's 6-vectors are linear first, then angular; libfranka's external-force estimate is force first, then torque. When the order changes, the blocks of Ad must swap (\(\begin{bmatrix}R&0\\ [\mathbf p]_\times R&R\end{bmatrix}\) becomes \(\begin{bmatrix}R&[\mathbf p]_\times R\\ 0&R\end{bmatrix}\)). When you plug in someone else's data or library, check the order with one known example of \(\boldsymbol\xi^\top\mathbf F\).

    误区 3：换了参考点却忘了杠杆臂Pitfall 3: moving the reference point and forgetting the lever arm

    "我只在工具尖端推了 10 N 的力，传感器怎么读到了力矩？"因为传感器的参考点在手腕，尖端在 0.4 m 外：\(\mathbf m_s=\mathbf p\times\mathbf f\)，4 N·m 的力矩纯粹是杠杆臂造成的，没有人"扭"过工具。反过来，若控制器要的是尖端的接触力，直接用传感器原点的 \(\mathbf f\) 虽然数值对（力不随点变），但算接触力矩时必须换点。把"力沿作用线滑动不变"与"力垂直于作用线挪开就变"分清：前者只改变力矩为零（\(\mathbf r\parallel\mathbf f\)）的参考点。

    "I only pushed 10 N at the tool tip; why does the sensor read a moment?" Because the sensor's reference point is at the wrist, 0.4 m from the tip: \(\mathbf m_s=\mathbf p\times\mathbf f\), and the 4 N·m is purely the lever arm; nobody twisted the tool. Conversely, if the controller wants the contact force at the tip, using the sensor-origin \(\mathbf f\) gives the right numbers (force does not depend on the point), but computing the contact moment requires moving the point. Keep apart "a force may slide along its line of action unchanged" and "moving the point off the line changes the moment": only reference points with \(\mathbf r\parallel\mathbf f\) leave the moment unchanged.

    误区 4：忘了传感器的重力偏置（以及它随姿态变化）Pitfall 4: forgetting the sensor's gravity bias (and that it varies with posture)

    在一个姿态下"去皮"（读数清零）只补偿了那个姿态的重力。手腕一转，重力在传感器系里的分量就变了（FIG 6）。必须用姿态实时计算 \(\mathbf f_g^s=R_{ws}^\top(0,0,-m_tg)\) 和 \(\mathbf c_t\times\mathbf f_g^s\)，并标定 \(m_t\)、\(\mathbf c_t\)。参数有误会留下与姿态有关的残差，比如质量差 10% 会留下 10% 的重力残差，在力控制里表现为"手臂自己在慢慢爬"。传感器还有温漂，需要定期去皮。负载也包括抓着的零件：抓起一个零件后，\(m_t\)、\(\mathbf c_t\) 都变了。

    Taring (zeroing the reading) in one posture only compensates gravity in that posture. Turn the wrist and gravity's components in the sensor frame change (FIG 6). You must compute \(\mathbf f_g^s=R_{ws}^\top(0,0,-m_tg)\) and \(\mathbf c_t\times\mathbf f_g^s\) from the live orientation, and calibrate \(m_t\) and \(\mathbf c_t\). Wrong parameters leave a posture-dependent residual: a 10% mass error leaves 10% of the weight uncompensated, which shows up in force control as the arm "slowly creeping". Sensors also drift with temperature and need periodic re-taring. And the payload includes whatever is grasped: after picking up a part, both \(m_t\) and \(\mathbf c_t\) change.

    误区 5：把 τ = JᵀF 当成任何位形、任何电机都成立的真理Pitfall 5: treating τ = JᵀF as true at every posture and for every motor

    两个破绽。其一，奇异位形：FIG 5 的"针"说明某些方向的力矩/力不需要关节出力，反过来也意味着你无法通过关节主动控制这个方向的力，从关节力矩估计力时误差被放大 \(1/\sigma_{\min}\) 倍。其二，电机力矩 ≠ 关节力矩：\(\boldsymbol\tau\) 是作用在连杆上的力矩；电机出的力矩要先过减速器，经过齿轮摩擦、反向间隙、弹性才到关节，用电流乘力矩常数估计 \(\boldsymbol\tau\) 在低速、换向时误差很大（9.4 节）。这也是 Franka、iiwa 要在关节输出端装力矩传感器的原因。另外，\(\mathbf g(\mathbf q)\) 只是静态的保持力矩，运动时还要加惯性与科氏项（第 8 章）。

    Two cracks. First, singular postures: the "needle" of FIG 5 says that along some directions force and moment need no joint effort, which also means you cannot actively control force in that direction through the joints, and an estimate of force from joint torques is amplified by \(1/\sigma_{\min}\). Second, motor torque ≠ joint torque: \(\boldsymbol\tau\) is the torque on the link; the motor torque passes through a gearbox, with gear friction, backlash and elasticity on its way to the joint, so estimating \(\boldsymbol\tau\) as motor current times the torque constant is badly off at low speed and during reversals (Lesson 9.4). That is why the Franka and iiwa put torque sensors on the joint output. Finally, \(\mathbf g(\mathbf q)\) is only the static holding torque; moving adds inertial and Coriolis terms (Chapter 8).

    1. 原点 \(O\) 处的 wrench 是 \(\mathbf m=(0,-3,0)\) N·m，\(\mathbf f=(0,0,10)\) N。参考点移到 \(O'=(0.5,0,0)\) m（坐标轴不变），新的力矩 \(\mathbf m'\) 是？1. The wrench at the origin \(O\) is \(\mathbf m=(0,-3,0)\) N·m, \(\mathbf f=(0,0,10)\) N. The reference point moves to \(O'=(0.5,0,0)\) m (axes unchanged). What is the new moment \(\mathbf m'\)?
    \(\mathbf r=(0.5,0,0)\)，\(\mathbf r\times\mathbf f=(0\cdot10-0\cdot0,\ 0\cdot0-0.5\cdot10,\ 0)=(0,-5,0)\)，\(\mathbf m'=\mathbf m-\mathbf r\times\mathbf f=(0,-3,0)-(0,-5,0)=(0,2,0)\)。检验：原来力的作用点在 \(x=0.3\)（因为 \(\mathbf m=\mathbf x\times\mathbf f=(0,-3,0)\)），相对 \(O'\) 的杠杆臂是 \((-0.2,0,0)\)，\((-0.2,0,0)\times(0,0,10)=(0,2,0)\)，吻合。选项 A 是把减号写成了加号，C 是忘了换点。\(\mathbf r=(0.5,0,0)\), \(\mathbf r\times\mathbf f=(0\cdot10-0\cdot0,\ 0\cdot0-0.5\cdot10,\ 0)=(0,-5,0)\), so \(\mathbf m'=\mathbf m-\mathbf r\times\mathbf f=(0,-3,0)-(0,-5,0)=(0,2,0)\). Check: the force must act at \(x=0.3\) (since \(\mathbf m=\mathbf x\times\mathbf f=(0,-3,0)\)); relative to \(O'\) its lever arm is \((-0.2,0,0)\), and \((-0.2,0,0)\times(0,0,10)=(0,2,0)\), as found.     2. 传感器系 {s} 在世界系 {w} 中的位姿是 \(T_{ws}\)，世界系里的 wrench 是 \(\mathbf F_w\)。传感器读到的是？2. The sensor frame {s} has pose \(T_{ws}\) in the world frame {w}, and the world-frame wrench is \(\mathbf F_w\). What does the sensor read?

Here the old frame is {w} and the new one {s}, so use \(T_{ws}\). B is the rule for twists; C has the pose reversed (that is {w} in {s}); D ignores that both reference point and axes change.
    3. 刚体的 twist 是 \(\boldsymbol\xi=(\boldsymbol\omega,\mathbf v)=(0,0,2,\ 0.5,0,0)\)（rad/s，m/s），受到的 wrench 是 \(\mathbf F=(\mathbf m,\mathbf f)=(0,0,1.5,\ 4,0,0)\)（N·m，N）。wrench 对刚体做功的功率是？3. A rigid body has twist \(\boldsymbol\xi=(\boldsymbol\omega,\mathbf v)=(0,0,2,\ 0.5,0,0)\) (rad/s, m/s) and carries the wrench \(\mathbf F=(\mathbf m,\mathbf f)=(0,0,1.5,\ 4,0,0)\) (N·m, N). What power does the wrench deliver?
    \(P=\boldsymbol\omega\cdot\mathbf m+\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf f=2\times1.5+0.5\times4=3+2=5\) W。选 0 W 的人把两个向量的顺序配错了（拿 \(\boldsymbol\omega\) 去乘 \(\mathbf f\)，\(\mathbf v\) 去乘 \(\mathbf m\)），这正是误区 2。3 W 和 2 W 各漏掉了一项。\(P=\boldsymbol\omega\cdot\mathbf m+\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf f=2\times1.5+0.5\times4=3+2=5\) W. Anyone choosing 0 W paired the two orderings wrongly (multiplying \(\boldsymbol\omega\) with \(\mathbf f\) and \(\mathbf v\) with \(\mathbf m\)), which is Pitfall 2. 3 W and 2 W each drop one term.
    4. 一根水平放置的均匀杆，长 1 m、质量 3 kg，一端接在肩关节上（轴水平且垂直于杆），另一端固定着一个 2 kg 的重物（可看成质点）。肩关节要提供多大的保持力矩？（\(g=9.81\) m/s²）4. A horizontal uniform rod, 1 m long and 3 kg, is attached at one end to a shoulder joint (axis horizontal and perpendicular to the rod), with a 2 kg point mass fixed at the other end. What holding torque must the shoulder supply? (\(g=9.81\) m/s²)
    \(\tau=\sum m_kg\,x_k=3\times9.81\times0.5+2\times9.81\times1=14.7+19.6=34.3\) N·m。这就是 \(\mathbf g=\sum m_kg\,J_{v,k}^\top\hat{\mathbf z}\)：杆的质量集中在中点（杠杆臂 0.5 m），重物在端点（杠杆臂 1 m）。A 漏了杆，D 漏了重物，C 把杆当成全部质量都在端点。\(\tau=\sum m_kg\,x_k=3\times9.81\times0.5+2\times9.81\times1=14.7+19.6=34.3\) N·m. This is \(\mathbf g=\sum m_kg\,J_{v,k}^\top\hat{\mathbf z}\): the rod's mass acts at the midpoint (lever arm 0.5 m), the weight at the end (1 m). A forgets the rod, D forgets the weight, C puts the whole rod mass at the end.
    5. 手臂完全伸直，手爪沿手臂方向向外推 100 N（对着一堵墙）。关于关节力矩与力椭球，哪个说法正确？5. The arm is fully stretched out and the gripper pushes 100 N outward along the arm (against a wall). Which statement about joint torques and the force ellipsoid is correct?
    力沿手臂的作用线通过肩、肘关节，对它们的杠杆臂为零，\(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top\mathbf F\approx0\)（\(\mathbf F\) 落在 \(\ker J^\top\) 里）。力椭球的半轴 \(1/\sigma_{\min}\to\infty\)，这个方向"一个单位力矩就能顶出很大的力"。代价是这个方向上手爪动不了，也无法靠关节主动调节力（误区 5）。The force's line of action runs through the shoulder and elbow, so its lever arm about them is zero and \(\boldsymbol\tau=J^\top\mathbf F\approx0\) (\(\mathbf F\) lies in \(\ker J^\top\)). The force ellipsoid's semi-axis \(1/\sigma_{\min}\to\infty\): "one unit of torque can hold a huge force" along this direction. The price: the tip cannot move in this direction, and the joints cannot regulate the force there either (Pitfall 5).
