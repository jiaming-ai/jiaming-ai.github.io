逆运动学：解析与数值Inverse Kinematics: Analytic & Numerical

任务说的是"手爪放到这里、姿态这样"，电机只听得懂关节角。逆运动学（IK）就是这中间的翻译：它可能无解、可能有几个解、也可能有无穷多个。能写出闭式解的机械臂几微秒就能给出全部解；一般的机构则要靠 SE(3) 上的牛顿迭代，一次只找到一个解，找到哪一个还取决于初值。本节从平面 2R 的"两圆相交"出发，推出一台带球腕的真实 6R 臂的 8 个解，再看数值 IK 如何收敛、为什么会失败，以及沿路径跟踪时该选哪一个解。 The task says "put the hand HERE, oriented like THIS"; the motors only understand joint angles. Inverse kinematics (IK) is the translator in between, and it may return no solution, a few, or a whole continuum. Arms with a closed-form solution produce all solutions in microseconds; general mechanisms need Newton iteration on SE(3), which finds one solution at a time, and which one depends on the initial guess. We start from the "two circles meet" picture of the planar 2R, derive the 8 solutions of a real 6R arm with a spherical wrist, then watch numerical IK converge and fail, and finally decide which solution to pick while tracking a path.

    1为什么机器人需要它Why robots need this

    你对机械臂下的命令是"手爪去那个杯子的把手，朝向这样"，电机能执行的却是"每个关节转到多少度"。7.3 节的正运动学 \(T(\mathbf q)\) 把关节角映成手爪位姿，是一个函数；逆运动学要反过来：给定想要的位姿 \(T_d\in\SE\)，求 \(\mathbf q\) 使 \(T(\mathbf q)=T_d\)。反方向的问题却不再是函数了：

    • 可能无解：目标太远，或者落在手臂折不进去的"洞"里。
    • 可能有有限多个解：同一个手爪位姿，手臂可以"肘上"也可以"肘下"，腕可以翻也可以不翻。
    • 可能有无穷多个解：冗余臂能在手爪不动时改变形状（3.4 节的零空间），一些奇异位形下非冗余臂也会如此。

    每个笛卡尔速度指令、每个抓取候选（一次要试成百上千个）、遥操作里的每一帧、规划器采样出的每个目标，都要先过 IK 这一关，所以它既要快，又要可靠：知道有没有解，知道有几个，知道现在该选哪一个。

    两大流派各有所长。解析（闭式）IK 利用机构的几何，把方程解成公式，几微秒内给出全部解，但每种构型都要单独推导，只对满足条件的机构存在（本节的 Pieper 准则）。数值 IK 把 3.4 节的"线性化、解一步、重复"搬到 SE(3) 上，对任何机构都适用，但一次只给出一个解，依赖初值，并且可能失败。3.4 节已经在平面上用伪逆和阻尼最小二乘做过数值 IK，这里不再重复，而是：

    • 先看为什么平面 2R 有两个解、解在哪里消失（FIG 1），以及位置与位姿约束如何决定解的"个数"与"维数"（FIG 2）；
    • 再推导一台真实 6R 臂的闭式解，得到 8 个分支（FIG 3）；
    • 然后在 SE(3) 上做数值 IK，看它怎样收敛、怎样失败，以及"初值决定去哪"（FIG 4、FIG 5）；
    • 最后沿一条路径跟踪，看选错分支会让关节突然翻转（FIG 6）。

    The command you give an arm is "put the gripper on that mug's handle, oriented like this", while the motors can only execute "turn each joint to this angle". Forward kinematics \(T(\mathbf q)\) (Lesson 7.3) maps joint angles to a hand pose, and it is a function. Inverse kinematics runs it backwards: given a desired pose \(T_d\in\SE\), find \(\mathbf q\) with \(T(\mathbf q)=T_d\). The backwards problem is no longer a function:

    • It may have no solution: the target is too far, or sits in a "hole" the arm cannot fold into.
    • It may have finitely many: for one hand pose the arm can bend "elbow up" or "elbow down", and the wrist can flip or not.
    • It may have infinitely many: a redundant arm can reshape itself while the hand stays put (the null space of Lesson 3.4), and even non-redundant arms do so at some singular postures.

    Every Cartesian velocity command, every grasp candidate (hundreds are tried at once), every frame of teleoperation and every goal a planner samples has to pass through IK first. It therefore has to be fast and reliable: know whether a solution exists, how many there are, and which to choose now.

    Two schools exist. Analytic (closed-form) IK exploits the mechanism's geometry, solves the equations as formulas, returns all solutions in microseconds, but must be derived per arm geometry and exists only for mechanisms meeting a condition (Pieper's criterion below). Numerical IK carries Lesson 3.4's "linearize, solve one step, repeat" over to SE(3); it works for any mechanism, but returns one solution at a time, depends on the initial guess, and can fail. Lesson 3.4 already did numerical IK for planar arms with the pseudo-inverse and damped least squares, so we do not repeat that. Instead:

    • first see why the planar 2R has two solutions and where they disappear (FIG 1), and how position vs. pose constraints set the number and dimension of the solutions (FIG 2);
    • then derive the closed-form solution of a real 6R arm and its 8 branches (FIG 3);
    • then run numerical IK on SE(3) and watch it converge, fail, and "let the initial guess decide" (FIG 4, FIG 5);
    • finally track a path and see what choosing the wrong branch does to the joints (FIG 6).

    2直觉：两个圆相交Intuition: two circles meet

    最小的例子：平面两连杆臂，连杆长 \(l_1,l_2\)，手爪要到点 \(\mathbf p=(x,y)\)。关节 1 在原点，所以肘部一定在以原点为圆心、半径 \(l_1\) 的圆上；手爪必须落在目标，所以肘部离目标的距离恰是 \(l_2\)，即肘部又在以目标为圆心、半径 \(l_2\) 的圆上。肘部同时在两个圆上，就是两个圆的交点。

    两个圆交于几点，立刻读出解的个数：两点（"肘上"和"肘下"，关于原点到目标的连线互为镜像）、一点（两圆相切，手臂完全伸直或完全折回）、零点（太远，或落在内圈的"洞"里）。可达区域因此是一个圆环 \(|l_1-l_2|\le r\le l_1+l_2\)，\(r=\|\mathbf p\|\)。拖动目标试试；再用滑块改变 \(l_2\)，看圆环的"洞"怎样随两根连杆的长短变化。

    The smallest example: a planar two-link arm with link lengths \(l_1,l_2\) must bring the hand to \(\mathbf p=(x,y)\). Joint 1 sits at the origin, so the elbow lies on the circle of radius \(l_1\) around the origin. The hand must end at the target, so the elbow is exactly \(l_2\) from the target: it also lies on the circle of radius \(l_2\) around the target. The elbow is on both circles at once, so it is one of their intersection points.

    The number of intersections reads off the number of solutions: two (elbow up and elbow down, mirror images across the line from the origin to the target), one (the circles touch: the arm is fully stretched or fully folded), or none (too far, or inside the inner "hole"). The reachable region is therefore an annulus \(|l_1-l_2|\le r\le l_1+l_2\), \(r=\|\mathbf p\|\). Drag the target; then change \(l_2\) with the slider and watch the hole change with the relative link lengths.

    核心洞见Key insight

    逆运动学是方程组而不是公式：解的个数随目标位置变化，在可达区域的边界上，两个解合并成一个然后消失。这不是数值误差，而是几何本身。闭式 IK 把"几个解、在哪里"一次说清；数值 IK 只能告诉你它走到了哪一个。

    Inverse kinematics is a system of equations, not a formula: the number of solutions changes with the target, and on the boundary of the reachable region two solutions merge into one and then vanish. That is geometry, not numerical error. Closed-form IK states "how many, and where" in one go; numerical IK only tells you which one it happened to reach.

    3动手玩：位置、位姿与解的维数Play: position, pose and the dimension of the solution set

    "到那个点"与"到那个点，并且手爪朝这个方向"不是一回事。数一数未知数（关节数 \(n\)）和约束数（\(m\)：平面里位置 2 个，位姿 3 个）：\(n-m>0\) 时解是一个 \((n-m)\) 维的连续族（可以在手爪不动时"自运动"），\(n=m\) 时通常是有限个解，\(n<m\) 时一般无解。这正是 7.1 节的自由度计数在逆运动学里的样子，也是 3.4 节零空间的非线性版本：零空间是解集在一点处的切空间。

    下图用 3R 或 4R 平面臂演示。右图把关节角画成自由参数的函数：3R 只要位置时，自由参数是手爪朝向 \(\varphi\)，每条曲线是一族连续解（实线肘上、虚线肘下）；要求位姿就是在 \(\varphi\) 处竖直切一刀，只剩下有限个点。切刀落在曲线的空隙里，就是"位置够得着、这个朝向却做不到"。

    "Reach that point" and "reach that point with the hand pointing this way" are different problems. Count the unknowns (the number of joints \(n\)) and the constraints (\(m\): 2 for a planar position, 3 for a planar pose). If \(n-m>0\) the solutions form an \((n-m)\)-dimensional continuous family (the arm can "self-move" while the hand stays), if \(n=m\) there are generically finitely many, and if \(n<m\) there are generically none. This is Lesson 7.1's degrees-of-freedom count in its inverse-kinematics form, and the nonlinear version of Lesson 3.4's null space: the null space is the tangent space of the solution set at a point.

    同一个想法的三个视角One idea, three views

    几何：解是几个圆（球）的交点，交点个数随目标变化。计数：未知数减约束数给出解集的维数 \(n-m\)。线性化：在某个解处，\(J\Delta\mathbf q=\mathbf 0\) 的解（3.4 节的零空间）是解集的切空间。三者说的是同一件事：IK 的解集是构型空间里的一个"流形"，维数 \(n-m\)，有限个解只是 0 维的特例。

    Geometry: solutions are intersections of circles (spheres), and their number changes with the target. Counting: unknowns minus constraints gives the solution-set dimension \(n-m\). Linearization: at a solution, the solutions of \(J\Delta\mathbf q=\mathbf 0\) (Lesson 3.4's null space) form the tangent space of the solution set. All three say the same thing: the IK solution set is a "manifold" in configuration space of dimension \(n-m\), and finitely many solutions is just the 0-dimensional special case.

    腕心解耦：8 个解Wrist decoupling: eight solutions

    这里用的是 7.3、7.4 节同一台 Robot.arm6：J1 绕竖直轴偏航，J2、J3 是肩与肘的俯仰（正俯仰让 \(+x\) 方向向下倾，右手定则），J4–J6 是"滚转–俯仰–滚转"的腕，三根腕轴交于腕心 \(\mathbf p_w\)，工具原点在腕心前方 \(d_6\) 处。关键观察：腕心的位置只由前三个关节决定，不管手腕怎么转。于是六个未知数拆成两个小问题：

    1. 位置：由目标位姿算出腕心 \(\hl{pw}{\mathbf p_w}=\mathbf p-d_6R\,\mathbf e_x\)，用它解 \(q_1,q_2,q_3\)。\(q_1\) 是腕心的方位角（有两个分支：\(q_1\) 与 \(q_1+\pi\)，手臂"从前面"够，或者 J1 转 180° 后从"身后"翻过来够）；然后在过肩部的竖直平面里，就是 FIG 1 的 2R 问题（肘上、肘下）。
    2. 姿态：前三个关节已知后，剩下的旋转 \(R_3^6=(R_0^3)^\top R\) 必须由腕补上。腕是"滚转–俯仰–滚转"，把一个旋转矩阵分解成这三个角，有两个分支（\(q_5\) 的符号，即腕翻转）。

    三个二选一，共 \(2\times2\times2=8\) 个解。下图把它们全部画出来：灰度淡的是越出关节限位的解，实心的是当前选中的解，表格给出每个解的关节角、FK 往返误差（把解代回正运动学，再与目标位姿求 SE(3) 误差）与限位检查。拖滑块改变目标位姿，或用"随机可达位姿"生成一个按构造一定可达的目标。

The key observation: the position of the wrist centre depends only on the first three joints, however the wrist turns. The six unknowns therefore split into two small problems:

    1. Position: from the target pose compute the wrist centre \(\hl{pw}{\mathbf p_w}=\mathbf p-d_6R\,\mathbf e_x\) and use it to solve \(q_1,q_2,q_3\). \(q_1\) is the azimuth of the wrist centre (two branches: \(q_1\) and \(q_1+\pi\): the arm reaches the target "from the front", or turns J1 by 180° and reaches "over its back"); then in the vertical plane through the shoulder it is FIG 1's 2R problem (elbow up, elbow down).
    2. Orientation: with the first three joints known, the remaining rotation \(R_3^6=(R_0^3)^\top R\) must be supplied by the wrist. The wrist is "roll–pitch–roll", so decomposing a rotation matrix into these three angles has two branches (the sign of \(q_5\), the wrist flip).

    为什么 8 个解只看得到 2 种手臂形状Why 8 solutions show only 2 arm shapes

    这台臂的 J1 与 J2 轴相交（肩没有偏置）。把 J1 多转 180°，再让 J2 翻过竖直方向（\(q_2\to-\pi-q_2\)）、\(q_3\to-q_3\)，连杆 1、2 回到完全相同的位置：手臂"从身后翻过来"，只是关节轴的指向反了。腕翻转同理：\(q_4\to q_4+\pi,\ q_5\to-q_5,\ q_6\to q_6+\pi\) 给出同一个手爪位姿。这些"翻转"在 FK 看来是不同的 \(\mathbf q\)，几何上却是同一个姿势，区别在于关节限位（例如连杆 1 放得较低时，背身解需要 \(q_2\) 超出它的范围）、线缆缠绕与连续性（FIG 6）。有横向肩偏置的臂（如 UR 系列），前 / 后两个肩分支才是形状不同的"左 / 右肩"。

    This arm's J1 and J2 axes intersect (no shoulder offset). Turn J1 a further 180°, flip J2 over the vertical (\(q_2\to-\pi-q_2\)) and negate \(q_3\), and links 1 and 2 return to exactly the same place: the arm "reaches over its back", merely with the joint axes pointing the other way. The wrist flip is the same trick: \(q_4\to q_4+\pi,\ q_5\to-q_5,\ q_6\to q_6+\pi\) gives the same hand pose. FK sees these as different \(\mathbf q\), yet geometrically it is one posture; the differences are joint limits (with link 1 held low, the over-the-back solution needs \(q_2\) beyond its range), cable wrap and continuity (FIG 6). For arms with a lateral shoulder offset (such as the UR series) the front / back shoulder branches are genuinely different "left / right shoulder" shapes.

    数值 IK：在 SE(3) 上线性化、重复Numerical IK: linearize on SE(3), repeat

    没有闭式解（或懒得推导）时，就用 3.4 节的老办法，只是把"位置误差"换成位姿误差。当前位姿 \(T(\mathbf q)\) 与目标 \(T_d\) 之间的误差，用 5.6 节的对数映射表示成机体系下的一个 twist：\(\mathbf e=\Log\big(T(\mathbf q)^{-1}T_d\big)\in\R^6\)（前三个分量是转角向量，后三个是平移）。它正好是 7.4 节机体雅可比 \(J_b\) 的"自然输入"：\(J_b\Delta\mathbf q\approx\mathbf e\)，解出 \(\Delta\mathbf q\)（伪逆或阻尼最小二乘），更新 \(\mathbf q\leftarrow\mathbf q+\Delta\mathbf q\)，重复。这就是 6.6 节的牛顿 / 高斯–牛顿法，只不过残差活在 SE(3) 上。

    下图从 4 个不同的初值同时出发（编号 1–4，四种颜色），对同一个目标位姿迭代。拖动"迭代 k"或按播放，看手臂怎样走向目标，右侧是每次迭代的误差 \(\|\mathbf e\|\)（对数）。每一步的关节增量被限幅到 0.8 rad（相当于速度限制）。四种目标位姿分别演示：正常收敛、手臂伸直的奇异位形、刚好够不着、远超范围。

    With no closed form (or no patience to derive one), use Lesson 3.4's recipe, only with a pose error in place of a position error. The error between the current pose \(T(\mathbf q)\) and the goal \(T_d\) is expressed with Lesson 5.6's logarithm as a body-frame twist: \(\mathbf e=\Log\big(T(\mathbf q)^{-1}T_d\big)\in\R^6\) (first three components: rotation vector, last three: translation). That is exactly the natural input of Lesson 7.4's body Jacobian \(J_b\): \(J_b\Delta\mathbf q\approx\mathbf e\). Solve for \(\Delta\mathbf q\) (pseudo-inverse or damped least squares), update \(\mathbf q\leftarrow\mathbf q+\Delta\mathbf q\), repeat. This is Lesson 6.6's Newton / Gauss–Newton method with the residual living on SE(3).

    平面 2R 的闭式解The planar 2R in closed form

    设目标 \(\mathbf p=(x,y)\)，\(r=\|\mathbf p\|\)。由原点、肘、目标构成的三角形，边长分别是 \(l_1,l_2,r\)，余弦定理给出 \(\theta_2\)；\(\theta_1\) 等于目标的方位角 \(\gamma=\operatorname{atan2}(y,x)\) 减去连杆 1 与"原点–目标"连线的夹角：

    Let the target be \(\mathbf p=(x,y)\) and \(r=\|\mathbf p\|\). The triangle formed by the origin, the elbow and the target has sides \(l_1,l_2,r\); the law of cosines gives \(\theta_2\), and \(\theta_1\) is the bearing \(\gamma=\operatorname{atan2}(y,x)\) of the target minus the angle between link 1 and the origin–target line:

    $$ \cos\theta_2=\frac{r^2-l_1^2-l_2^2}{2\,l_1l_2},\qquad \theta_2=\pm\arccos(\cdot) $$ $$ \theta_1=\gamma-\operatorname{atan2}\!\big(l_2\sin\theta_2,\;l_1+l_2\cos\theta_2\big). $$

    \(|\cos\theta_2|>1\) 即无解，与两圆不相交完全等价。两个符号对应的两个解，是关于原点–目标连线的镜像：\((\theta_1,\theta_2)\mapsto(2\gamma-\theta_1,\,-\theta_2)\)。\(\cos\theta_2=\pm1\) 时两个解合并，\(\theta_2\) 对 \(r\) 的导数发散（\(\mathrm d\theta_2/\mathrm dr\propto1/\sin\theta_2\)），这正是 3.4 节里雅可比奇异的位置：解的数目变化的地方，就是雅可比降秩的地方。

    \(|\cos\theta_2|>1\) means no solution, which is the same as the two circles missing each other. The two signs give two solutions that are mirror images across the origin–target line: \((\theta_1,\theta_2)\mapsto(2\gamma-\theta_1,\,-\theta_2)\). At \(\cos\theta_2=\pm1\) the two merge and \(\theta_2\) as a function of \(r\) has a diverging slope (\(\mathrm d\theta_2/\mathrm dr\propto1/\sin\theta_2\)): this is exactly where Lesson 3.4's Jacobian is singular. Where the number of solutions changes, the Jacobian drops rank.

    Pieper 准则：什么时候有闭式解Pieper's criterion: when a closed form exists

    一般的 6R 臂，逆运动学是 6 个耦合的三角方程。Pieper（1968）发现：若三个相邻关节的轴交于一点（典型情形是球腕），位置与姿态解耦，方程组分裂成两个小问题，闭式解存在，且至多 8 个解。原因很直接：这个交点（腕心）的位置只由它之前的关节决定，不受腕转动的影响，所以

    For a general 6R arm, inverse kinematics is six coupled trigonometric equations. Pieper (1968) found that if three adjacent joint axes meet at a point (the typical case is a spherical wrist), position and orientation decouple: the system splits into two small problems with a closed-form solution and at most 8 solutions. The reason is direct: the intersection point (the wrist centre) is moved only by the joints before it, not by the wrist rotation, so

    $$ \hl{pw}{\mathbf p_w}=\mathbf p-d_6\,R\,\mathbf e_x \;=\; \operatorname{FK}_{\text{position}}(q_1,q_2,q_3)\quad\text{(no }q_4,q_5,q_6\text{)} $$ $$ R=R_0^3(q_1,q_2,q_3)\,\underbrace{R_3^6(q_4,q_5,q_6)}_{\text{wrist}}. $$

    三根轴平行（如 UR 系列的肩、肘、腕 1）也能得到闭式解，只是推导不同。反过来，一旦腕有偏置（三轴不再交于一点），上面的分解就不精确了，必须换成别的几何构造，或改用数值 IK。

    Three parallel axes (the shoulder, elbow and first wrist joint of UR-type arms) also lead to a closed form, derived differently. Conversely, as soon as the wrist is offset (three axes no longer meet), the decomposition above is no longer exact: use another geometric construction or fall back on numerical IK.

    有几个解How many solutions

    球腕 6R 臂：肩 2 × 肘 2 × 腕 2 = 8（FIG 3）。一般的 6R 臂（没有交点）最多 16 个解：Raghavan–Roth（1993）把问题消元成关于某个关节半角正切的一个 16 次多项式，其中的实根就是实解。冗余臂（\(n>6\)）有连续的解族，维数 \(n-6\)；7R 臂对完整位姿的解是 1 维的曲线（肘部绕"肩–腕"连线转动的"摆动角"），常见做法是把这个自由参数固定或优化，再套闭式解。

    A spherical-wrist 6R arm: shoulder 2 × elbow 2 × wrist 2 = 8 (FIG 3). A general 6R arm (no intersection) has at most 16: Raghavan and Roth (1993) eliminated the unknowns down to a degree-16 polynomial in the tangent of a half-angle of one joint, whose real roots are the real solutions. Redundant arms (\(n>6\)) have a continuous family of dimension \(n-6\); for a 7R arm and a full pose it is a 1-dimensional curve (the elbow's "swivel angle" about the shoulder–wrist line), and a common strategy is to fix or optimize that parameter and then apply a closed form.

    数值 IK = SE(3) 上的牛顿法Numerical IK = Newton's method on SE(3)

    $$ \mathbf e(\mathbf q)=\Log\!\big(T(\mathbf q)^{-1}T_d\big)\in\R^6,\qquad \mathbf q\leftarrow\mathbf q+\Delta\mathbf q $$ $$ \Delta\mathbf q=J_b^{+}\,\mathbf e\quad\text{or}\quad J_b^\top\big(J_bJ_b^\top+\lambda^2I\big)^{-1}\mathbf e $$

    这是对 \(\tfrac12\|\mathbf e(\mathbf q)\|^2\) 的高斯–牛顿法（6.6、6.9 节）。机体系下的误差 \(\mathbf e\) 与机体雅可比 \(J_b\)（7.4 节）配对；若误差写在空间系里，就改用 \(J_s\)。\(\mathbf e\) 对 \(\mathbf q\) 的精确导数是 \(-J_l^{-1}(\mathbf e)\,J_b\)，其中 \(J_l\) 是 SE(3) 的左雅可比，它与单位阵相差 \(O(\|\mathbf e\|)\)；所以用 \(-J_b\) 代替它，迭代在解附近仍是二次收敛（FIG 4 里的 \(10^{-2}\to10^{-4}\to10^{-8}\)），前提是 \(J_b\) 满秩。远离解时不保证任何事：这就是 FIG 5 的吸引域，需要限幅、回溯线搜索（6.5 节）或阻尼（Levenberg–Marquardt）来保护。阻尼只改变路径，不改变不动点（\(\mathbf e=\mathbf 0\)），但在 \(J_b\) 降秩时会停在误差平台上（FIG 4 的"伸直"目标）。

    量纲与权重。 \(\mathbf e\) 的前三个分量是弧度，后三个是米。"1 弧度"与"1 米"并不等价，所以把行加权：\(\min_{\Delta\mathbf q}\|W(J_b\Delta\mathbf q-\mathbf e)\|^2+\lambda^2\|\Delta\mathbf q\|^2\)，\(W=\operatorname{diag}(w_\omega I_3,\,w_vI_3)\)。典型做法是取 \(w\) 为各自的容差的倒数（如 1 mm 对应 \(w_v=10^3\)，0.1° 对应 \(w_\omega\approx570\)）。位置优先的任务则把 \(w_\omega\) 取得更小。

    This is Gauss–Newton on \(\tfrac12\|\mathbf e(\mathbf q)\|^2\) (Lessons 6.6, 6.9). The body-frame error \(\mathbf e\) pairs with the body Jacobian \(J_b\) (Lesson 7.4); an error written in the space frame pairs with \(J_s\) instead. The exact derivative of \(\mathbf e\) with respect to \(\mathbf q\) is \(-J_l^{-1}(\mathbf e)\,J_b\), where \(J_l\) is the left Jacobian of SE(3), which differs from the identity by \(O(\|\mathbf e\|)\). Using \(-J_b\) instead therefore still converges quadratically near a solution (FIG 4's \(10^{-2}\to10^{-4}\to10^{-8}\)), provided \(J_b\) has full rank. Far from a solution nothing is guaranteed: that is the basin picture of FIG 5, and it takes clamping, backtracking line search (Lesson 6.5) or damping (Levenberg–Marquardt) to protect the iteration. Damping changes the path but not the fixed points (\(\mathbf e=\mathbf 0\)); when \(J_b\) drops rank, however, it can stall on an error plateau (FIG 4's "stretched" target).

    Units and weights. The first three components of \(\mathbf e\) are radians and the last three are metres, and "1 rad" is not equivalent to "1 m". Weight the rows: \(\min_{\Delta\mathbf q}\|W(J_b\Delta\mathbf q-\mathbf e)\|^2+\lambda^2\|\Delta\mathbf q\|^2\) with \(W=\operatorname{diag}(w_\omega I_3,\,w_vI_3)\). A typical choice makes each weight the inverse of its tolerance (1 mm gives \(w_v=10^3\), 0.1° gives \(w_\omega\approx570\)); a position-first task uses a smaller \(w_\omega\).

    限位、次要任务与优化式 IKJoint limits, secondary tasks and optimization-based IK

    闭式解先求全部解，再按关节限位、碰撞、离当前构型的距离筛选；数值 IK 则要把限位放进迭代：最粗糙的是把 \(\mathbf q\) 截断到限位内（容易卡住），更好的是把每一步写成带不等式约束的 QP：\(\min\|J_b\Delta\mathbf q-\mathbf e\|^2+\lambda^2\|\Delta\mathbf q\|^2\)，\(\mathbf q_{\min}\le\mathbf q+\Delta\mathbf q\le\mathbf q_{\max}\)（6.8 节）。冗余臂可以像 3.4 节那样把次要目标（远离限位、保持肘部朝向）投影到零空间 \((I-J^+J)\mathbf z\)。再往前一步是把整个 IK 当作一个非线性优化问题（6.9 节），代价和约束写成什么都行（可达、碰撞距离、姿态、平滑），用 SQP/内点法求解；Part VI 还会看到用神经网络直接给出 IK 的初值或解。

    A closed-form solver first returns all solutions and then filters them by joint limits, collisions and distance from the current configuration. A numerical solver has to put the limits into the iteration: the crudest way clamps \(\mathbf q\) into its limits (and can get stuck); better is to write each step as a QP with inequality constraints, \(\min\|J_b\Delta\mathbf q-\mathbf e\|^2+\lambda^2\|\Delta\mathbf q\|^2\) subject to \(\mathbf q_{\min}\le\mathbf q+\Delta\mathbf q\le\mathbf q_{\max}\) (Lesson 6.8). A redundant arm can, as in Lesson 3.4, project secondary goals (stay away from limits, keep the elbow oriented) into the null space with \((I-J^+J)\mathbf z\). One step further treats the whole IK as a nonlinear optimization problem (Lesson 6.9) with any costs and constraints (reachability, collision distance, orientation, smoothness) solved by SQP or interior-point methods; Part VI will also use neural networks to give IK initial guesses or solutions directly.

    真实系统里In real systems

    IKFast（Diankov 的 OpenRAVE）：对给定的串联臂（通常 6 自由度）自动生成闭式 C++ 求解器，给出全部解，几微秒，常作为 MoveIt 的 IK 插件。UR 系列有公开的闭式解（Hawkins 2013）；7 自由度的 Franka Panda 没有唯一闭式解，已发表的解析求解器通常先固定一个冗余参数（如 \(q_7\) 或肘部摆动角）再解析求解。Trac-IK（Beeson & Ames 2015）同时跑两个求解器——带随机重启的 KDL 牛顿迭代和一个 SQP 求解器——谁先成功取谁，显著提高了带关节限位的数值 IK 成功率。KDL（Orocos）提供牛顿–拉弗森与 LM 求解器；MoveIt 的运动学插件接口可换用 KDL、Trac-IK、IKFast 或 BioIK 等；Pinocchio 提供 \(\mathrm{SE}(3)\) 上的误差与雅可比，常用来写微分 IK 与基于 QP 的 IK（Pink、mink 等库）；Drake 的 InverseKinematics 把 IK 写成带约束的非线性规划。遥操作与 VR 重定向：把操作者的手腕位姿每帧映射到机器人关节，就是上面的"热启动数值 IK"，为了平滑和避奇异通常加阻尼与次要任务；人形机器人动作重定向则是同一思想的全身版本，常用 QP 形式同时满足多个手脚目标、平衡与关节限位。在计算机动画里，FABRIK（Aristidou & Lasenby 2011）等启发式方法以极低成本给出看起来自然的解。

    IKFast (Diankov's OpenRAVE) generates closed-form C++ solvers automatically for a given serial arm (usually 6 DoF), returning all solutions in microseconds, and is a common MoveIt IK plugin. UR-series arms have a published closed form (Hawkins 2013); the 7-DoF Franka Panda has no unique closed form, and the published analytic solvers typically fix one redundant parameter (such as \(q_7\) or the elbow swivel angle) and then solve analytically. Trac-IK (Beeson & Ames 2015) runs two solvers concurrently, a KDL Newton iteration with random restarts and an SQP solver, and returns whichever succeeds first, which markedly raises the success rate of numerical IK with joint limits. KDL (Orocos) offers Newton–Raphson and LM solvers; MoveIt's kinematics-plugin interface swaps between KDL, Trac-IK, IKFast, BioIK and others; Pinocchio provides the \(\mathrm{SE}(3)\) errors and Jacobians and is used to write differential and QP-based IK (libraries such as Pink and mink); Drake's InverseKinematics poses IK as a constrained nonlinear program. Teleoperation and VR retargeting map the operator's wrist pose to robot joints every frame, which is exactly the warm-started numerical IK above, usually with damping and secondary tasks for smoothness and singularity avoidance; humanoid motion retargeting is the whole-body version of the same idea, typically a QP that meets several hand and foot targets, balance and joint limits at once. In computer animation, heuristics such as FABRIK (Aristidou & Lasenby 2011) give natural-looking solutions at very low cost.

    7常见误区Common pitfalls

    误区 1：每一帧"挑一个解"，不管上一帧Pitfall 1: picking "a solution" every frame without looking at the last one

    闭式 IK 返回的是一个无序的集合。取"第一个"或"误差最小的"，随着目标移动，下标对应的分支会变，关节在一帧里翻转 180°（FIG 6）。要按与上一帧的关节距离（含 \(\pm\pi\) 回绕）选，同时检查限位与碰撞；起点处才用别的准则（离"舒适姿态"最近）。选"最近"也不是万能：路径必须经过奇异位形或限位时，连续的分支可能不可行，只能重新规划。

    Closed-form IK returns an unordered set. Taking "the first one" or "the one with the smallest error" lets the branch behind that index change as the target moves, and the joints flip by 180° in one frame (FIG 6). Choose by joint-space distance to the previous frame (with \(\pm\pi\) wrap-around) and check limits and collisions; only at the start use another criterion (closest to a "comfortable posture"). "Nearest" is not magic either: when the path must cross a singularity or a limit, the continuous branch may be infeasible and the path has to be replanned.

    误区 2：数值 IK 总会"给出一个答案"，就以为成功了Pitfall 2: numerical IK always "returns something", so it must have worked

    目标不可达、停在局部极小、迭代次数用完，求解器都会返回一个 \(\mathbf q\)。FIG 4 里"远超范围"的目标让误差停在一个非零的平台上；FIG 5 里红色区域根本不收敛。务必检查残差 \(\|\mathbf e\|\)（分别按位置和姿态容差），失败时换初值重启，或者用上一帧的解、闭式解当种子。Trac-IK 的随机重启就是这个思路。

    If the target is unreachable, the iteration sits in a local minimum or the iteration budget runs out, the solver still returns a \(\mathbf q\). FIG 4's "far out of reach" target leaves the error on a non-zero plateau, and FIG 5's red regions never converge. Always check the residual \(\|\mathbf e\|\) (separately against position and orientation tolerances), restart from another initial guess on failure, or seed with the previous frame's solution or a closed-form one. Trac-IK's random restarts follow this idea.

    误区 3：忘了角度回绕与限位的先后Pitfall 3: ignoring angle wrap-around when checking limits

    \(\operatorname{atan2}\) 给出 \((-\pi,\pi]\) 的角度，但 \(q\) 与 \(q\pm2\pi\) 是同一个姿态。若关节限位是 \([0^\circ,340^\circ]\)，\(\operatorname{atan2}\) 返回的 \(-170^\circ\) 看起来越限，而等价的 \(190^\circ\) 完全可行；许多真实关节的行程超过一圈，必须把每个解换成限位内的等价角再判断。计算距离时也要先回绕（\(q_i-q_i'\) 取 \(\operatorname{wrap}(\cdot)\)），否则 \(3.1\) 与 \(-3.1\) 会被认为相差 6.2 rad。

    \(\operatorname{atan2}\) returns angles in \((-\pi,\pi]\), yet \(q\) and \(q\pm2\pi\) are the same posture. With a joint limit of \([0^\circ,340^\circ]\), the \(-170^\circ\) returned by \(\operatorname{atan2}\) looks out of range while the equivalent \(190^\circ\) is perfectly feasible; many real joints travel more than one turn, so map each solution to its equivalent angle inside the limits before judging it. Distances must be wrapped too (take \(\operatorname{wrap}(q_i-q_i')\)); otherwise \(3.1\) and \(-3.1\) look 6.2 rad apart.

    误区 4：把弧度和米直接加在一起Pitfall 4: adding radians and metres

    \(\|\mathbf e\|\) 里转角与平移直接相加，等于规定了"1 rad ≡ 1 m"；换成毫米，位置就压倒了姿态。手腕离工具越远，同一个转角造成的手爪位置偏差越大。务必按任务的容差加权（见上一节），收敛判据也要分开写：位置小于几个 mm 且姿态小于零点几度。

    Adding rotation and translation inside \(\|\mathbf e\|\) declares "1 rad ≡ 1 m"; switch to millimetres and the position swamps the orientation. The farther the wrist is from the tool, the more the same angle displaces the hand. Always weight by the task tolerances (previous section), and write the stopping criterion separately: position within a few mm and orientation within a fraction of a degree.

    误区 5：把某一台臂的闭式解当成通用公式Pitfall 5: treating one arm's closed form as a universal formula

    本节的推导依赖精确的几何：腕轴交于一点、零偏置、特定的零位与轴方向。真实手臂常有小偏置（如 Franka 的腕偏置、UR 的肩偏置）、标定误差和不同的关节符号约定；一个 5 mm 的偏置就让上面的腕心解耦不再精确。要么用对应构型的专门推导（或 IKFast 自动生成），要么用闭式解当种子、再用数值 IK 精修。

    This lesson's derivation depends on exact geometry: wrist axes meeting at a point, no offsets, a specific zero pose and axis directions. Real arms have small offsets (the Franka's wrist offset, the UR's shoulder offset), calibration errors and different joint sign conventions; a 5 mm offset makes the wrist-centre decoupling above inexact. Either use a derivation for that exact geometry (or let IKFast generate it), or use the closed form as a seed and polish with numerical IK.

    8小练习Check yourself

    1. 平面 2R 臂，\(l_1=1.2\)，\(l_2=0.8\)。目标离基座 \(r=0.3\)。有几个 IK 解？1. A planar 2R arm has \(l_1=1.2\), \(l_2=0.8\). The target is \(r=0.3\) from the base. How many IK solutions?
    可达环是 \(|l_1-l_2|=0.4\le r\le l_1+l_2=2.0\)。\(r=0.3<0.4\)：目标在内圈的"洞"里，即使手臂完全折回，手爪也停在离基座 0.4 处。无穷多个解只出现在 \(l_1=l_2\) 且目标恰好在基座上的特殊情形。The reachable annulus is \(|l_1-l_2|=0.4\le r\le l_1+l_2=2.0\). Here \(r=0.3<0.4\): the target sits in the inner hole, and even with the arm folded back the hand stops 0.4 from the base. Infinitely many solutions arise only in the special case \(l_1=l_2\) with the target exactly at the base.
    2. 球腕 6R 臂，目标位姿在工作空间内部且不是奇异位形。闭式 IK 一般返回几个解？2. For a spherical-wrist 6R arm and a target pose inside the workspace, away from singularities, how many solutions does closed-form IK generally return?
    肩（\(q_1\) 与 \(q_1+\pi\)）× 肘（上 / 下）× 腕（\(q_5\) 的两个符号）= 8（其中有的在几何上是同一个姿势的"翻转"，见 FIG 3 的说明）。16 是一般 6R 臂（没有交点）的上界（Raghavan–Roth）。Shoulder (\(q_1\) and \(q_1+\pi\)) × elbow (up / down) × wrist (two signs of \(q_5\)) = 8 (some of them being geometrically "flipped" copies of one posture, see the note under FIG 3). 16 is the upper bound for a general 6R arm without an intersection point (Raghavan–Roth).
    3. 三根腕轴交于一点，为什么让 IK 变简单？3. Why does having the three wrist axes meet at one point make IK easy?
    腕心 \(\mathbf p_w=\mathbf p-d_6R\mathbf e_x\) 可以直接由目标位姿算出，又只依赖 \(q_1,q_2,q_3\)。先用它解前三个关节，剩下的旋转交给腕。这与质量、雅可比是否为常数、解是否唯一都无关（解有 8 个）。The wrist centre \(\mathbf p_w=\mathbf p-d_6R\mathbf e_x\) can be computed directly from the target pose and depends only on \(q_1,q_2,q_3\). Solve those first, and leave the remaining rotation to the wrist. It has nothing to do with mass, a constant Jacobian or uniqueness (there are 8 solutions).
    4. 牛顿 IK 的误差依次是 \(3\times10^{-2},\ 9\times10^{-4},\ 8\times10^{-7},\ 6\times10^{-13}\)。这是什么收敛速度？4. The error of a Newton IK run goes \(3\times10^{-2},\ 9\times10^{-4},\ 8\times10^{-7},\ 6\times10^{-13}\). What kind of convergence is that?
    \((3\times10^{-2})^2=9\times10^{-4}\)，\((9\times10^{-4})^2\approx8\times10^{-7}\)，\((8\times10^{-7})^2\approx6\times10^{-13}\)：有效数字的位数每步翻倍，这是牛顿法在非奇异解附近的二次收敛。阻尼最小二乘或奇异位形附近则表现为线性甚至平台。\((3\times10^{-2})^2=9\times10^{-4}\), \((9\times10^{-4})^2\approx8\times10^{-7}\), \((8\times10^{-7})^2\approx6\times10^{-13}\): the number of correct digits doubles each step, which is Newton's quadratic convergence near a non-singular solution. Damped least squares or a singular solution instead shows linear convergence or a plateau.
    5. 一台 7R 臂要到达一个完整的 6D 位姿，解集（一般情况下）是什么？5. A 7R arm must reach a full 6D pose. What does its solution set generally look like?
    未知数 7、约束 6，\(n-m=1\)：解是构型空间里的一条曲线（肘部绕肩–腕连线转动）。这条曲线在每一点的切方向，就是 3.4 节的一维零空间。未知数多于约束恰好意味着有（无穷）多个解，而不是没有。With 7 unknowns and 6 constraints, \(n-m=1\): the solution set is a curve in configuration space (the elbow swivelling about the shoulder–wrist line). Its tangent at each point is Lesson 3.4's one-dimensional null space. More unknowns than constraints means (infinitely) many solutions, not none.
