体素、网格与 SDFVoxels, Meshes & SDFs

点云只是一堆点：它回答不了"这个位置会不会撞上？""离最近的障碍还有多远？""往哪边推才能脱离？"。这一节把世界存成规划器真正能查询的样子：占据栅格（体素）、三角网格，以及最有用的一种——符号距离场 SDF：每个点存"到表面的距离，里面为负"，它的梯度就是法向，且处处满足 \(\|\nabla d\|=1\)。我们还会看到怎样用对数几率把传感器射线融合成地图，怎样用 marching squares 把 SDF 变回表面，怎样用球追踪渲染它，以及 TSDF 融合（KinectFusion、nvblox 的核心）如何把带噪声的深度平均成光滑的曲面，并变成避障代价。 A point cloud is only a bag of points: it cannot answer "will this pose collide?", "how far is the nearest obstacle?" or "which way is out?". This lesson stores the world in forms a planner can actually query: occupancy grids (voxels), triangle meshes and, most useful of all, the signed distance field (SDF): every point stores its distance to the surface, negative inside, so the gradient is the surface normal and \(\|\nabla d\|=1\) everywhere. You will fuse sensor rays into a map with log-odds, turn an SDF back into a surface with marching squares, render it with sphere tracing, and watch TSDF fusion (the heart of KinectFusion and nvblox) average noisy depth into a clean surface that becomes an obstacle cost.

    1为什么机器人需要它Why robots need this

    深度相机和激光雷达（10.3、12.1）给你的是点。可是机器人要用它们回答的几乎都是别的问题：

    • 碰撞检测：机械臂在这个构型（7.2 节）下会不会碰到东西？需要一个"这个点在不在物体里面"的判断。
    • 间距查询：离最近的障碍物还有多远？轨迹优化（6.13）的障碍代价靠它。
    • 脱离方向：已经（快要）撞上了，朝哪个方向推最快脱离？这是一个方向，也就是梯度（3.2）。
    • 地图融合：同一堵墙被看了一百次，每次都有噪声、遮挡，还有行人走过，怎样合成一张可信的图？这是概率（4.7）和平均。
    • 表面与渲染：物体的外表长什么样？物理仿真、抓取规划、图形学（12.3）都要一张表面。

    同一个形状可以用点、体素、网格、符号距离场、隐式函数存下来。每一种表示能便宜地回答一部分问题，另一部分则完全答不了。本节的目标是：看懂每种表示"擅长什么、代价是什么"，并学会它们之间怎样互相转换（体素 ↔ SDF ↔ 网格）。重点是 SDF——它是现代机器人避障、建图和神经隐式表示（12.4）共同的语言。

    Depth cameras and LiDAR (10.3, 12.1) hand you points. But almost every question a robot wants to ask is a different one:

    • Collision checking: does the arm collide in this configuration (Lesson 7.2)? That needs an "is this point inside an object?" test.
    • Clearance: how far is the nearest obstacle? The obstacle cost of trajectory optimization (6.13) runs on it.
    • Way out: already (nearly) in contact, in which direction does pushing escape fastest? A direction is a gradient (3.2).
    • Map fusion: the same wall is seen a hundred times, each time with noise, occlusion and a pedestrian walking by. How do you merge it into one trustworthy map? That is probability (4.7) and averaging.
    • Surface and rendering: what does the outside of the object look like? Physics simulation, grasp planning and graphics (12.3) all need a surface.

    The same shape can be stored as points, voxels, a mesh, a signed distance field or an implicit function. Each representation answers some of these questions cheaply and others not at all. The goal here is to see what each is good at and what it costs, and to learn the conversions between them (voxels ↔ SDF ↔ mesh). The star is the SDF, the common language of modern robot collision avoidance, mapping, and neural implicit representations (12.4).

    占据栅格：把传感器射线变成地图Occupancy grids: turning sensor rays into a map

    机器人看到的世界不是一次给全的。占据栅格（Elfes, 1989）把平面切成格子，每格是一个二值随机变量"被占据 / 空闲"，并假设各格相互独立。一条测距射线给出两类证据：射线穿过的格子多半是空的；射线终止的那个格子多半被占据了；射线之外的格子，什么也没看见——仍然未知。把每一条证据用贝叶斯公式（4.7）并进去，在对数几率下就是简单的加法。

    下图：一个 200° 视场的激光测距仪在房间里移动（房间里有一个盒子、一根柱子）。拖动机器人（琥珀色圆点）和朝向（蓝色小圆），沿途扫描并建图；或者按"自动巡航"。灰色 = 未知，青色 = 空闲，红色 = 占据，越深越确定。

    A robot does not see the world all at once. An occupancy grid (Elfes, 1989) cuts the plane into cells, each a binary random variable "occupied / free", assumed independent. A range beam gives two kinds of evidence: the cells it passed through are probably empty; the cell where it ended is probably occupied; cells outside the beam were not observed and stay unknown. Fold each piece of evidence in with Bayes' rule (4.7) and, in log-odds, it becomes simple addition.

    3动手玩：符号距离场Play: the signed distance field

    占据栅格只回答"是 / 否"。符号距离场多存一点：到表面的距离，并用符号区分内外（本课约定里面为负）：

    An occupancy grid answers only yes / no. A signed distance field stores a bit more: the distance to the surface, with the sign telling inside from outside (this course's convention: inside is negative):

    $$ d(\mathbf x)=\begin{cases}+\min_{\mathbf y\in\partial\Omega}\|\mathbf x-\mathbf y\| & \mathbf x\notin\Omega\\[2pt] -\min_{\mathbf y\in\partial\Omega}\|\mathbf x-\mathbf y\| & \mathbf x\in\Omega\end{cases}\qquad\Rightarrow\qquad \|\hl{grad}{\nabla d}\|=1,\quad \mathbf c=\mathbf x-d(\mathbf x)\,\hl{cp}{\nabla d(\mathbf x)}\ \text{(closest surface point)}. $$

    下面是你的第一个 SDF 实验室。选一种形状（圆 A、盒子 B、胶囊 AB，或它们的布尔组合），拖动 A、B 的中心和琥珀色探针。颜色是 \(d\) 的值（青 = 外，紫 = 内），细线是 \(d=\pm0.25,\pm0.5,\dots\) 的等值线，粗线是 \(d=0\)，也就是表面。探针处：琥珀色箭头是 \(\nabla d\)（外向法向，长度恒为 1），紫色虚线连向最近表面点 \(\mathbf x-d\nabla d\)，绿色虚线圆是以 \(\hl{ball}{|d|}\) 为半径的"安全球"：球内一定没有表面。探针外的圆盘是半径 \(\hl{rho}{\rho}\) 的机器人：当且仅当 \(d<\rho\) 时碰撞，红色虚线就是 \(d=\rho\) 的等值线，也就是机器人圆心不能越过的边界。

    核心洞见Key insight

    SDF 一张场回答了前面的三个问题：符号回答"在不在里面"，数值回答"离得多远"，梯度回答"往哪走能出去"。机器人（圆盘）的碰撞检测被压缩成一次查询 \(d(\mathbf x)<\rho\)；脱离碰撞的最短位移就是沿 \(\nabla d\) 走 \(\rho-d\)。这就是为什么轨迹优化、MPC、全身避障都爱用它。

    One SDF answers three of the opening questions at once: the sign says whether you are inside, the value says how far, the gradient says which way out. A disc robot's collision test collapses to a single query \(d(\mathbf x)<\rho\); the shortest displacement to escape is \(\rho-d\) along \(\nabla d\). That is why trajectory optimization, MPC and whole-body avoidance love it.

    4第二、第三视角：从 SDF 到表面，再到融合Second and third views: from SDF to surface, and fusing many views

    SDF → 表面：marching squares 与球追踪SDF → surface: marching squares and sphere tracing

    有了 SDF，怎样得到"看得见的表面"？有两条路。(a) 抽取：在网格上采样 \(d\)，找出符号变化的格子，用线性插值求出零点，连成折线（二维的 marching squares）或三角形（三维的 marching cubes，Lorensen & Cline 1987）。一个格子的 4 个角各有"里 / 外"两种状态，共 \(2^4=16\) 种情形（下方图例，编号是 4 位二进制）；三维是 \(2^8=256\) 种，按对称性归为 15 类，有些类有歧义（同一组符号对应不同连法），要靠额外规则或网格中心值来消歧；marching cubes 在尖锐的棱角处会被网格"磨圆"，dual contouring（Ju 等 2002）则每格放一个顶点，利用梯度信息保住棱角。(b) 直接追踪：不生成网格，沿相机射线向前走，每一步走 \(d(\mathbf x)\)：以 \(\mathbf x\) 为圆心、半径 \(d\) 的球里肯定没有表面，所以这一步绝对不会"跨过"表面（球追踪，Hart 1996）。

    左图拖动网格大小，看折线怎样逼近真实轮廓（虚线）；把鼠标放在一个格子上，图例会高亮它属于哪一种情形。右图拖动射线的起点（绿）和方向点（琥珀），并改变场的缩放 \(s\)，让 \(f=s\,d\)：它不再是距离，而是"陡了 \(s\) 倍"的函数。

    Given an SDF, how do you get a visible surface? Two routes. (a) Extraction: sample \(d\) on a grid, find cells where the sign changes, locate the zero by linear interpolation, and join the crossings into a polyline (2D marching squares) or triangles (3D marching cubes, Lorensen & Cline 1987). Each of a cell's 4 corners is inside or outside, giving \(2^4=16\) cases (legend below; the number is the 4-bit pattern); 3D has \(2^8=256\), which reduce to 15 classes by symmetry, and some are ambiguous (the same signs admit different connections) and need extra rules or the cell-centre value to resolve. Marching cubes rounds off sharp edges; dual contouring (Ju et al. 2002) places one vertex per cell using gradient information and keeps them. (b) Direct tracing: skip the mesh and march along the camera ray, each step of length \(d(\mathbf x)\): the ball of radius \(d\) around \(\mathbf x\) certainly contains no surface, so the step can never jump over it (sphere tracing, Hart 1996).

    TSDF 融合：把很多次带噪声的观测平均成一张表面TSDF fusion: averaging many noisy views into one surface

    真实的传感器每次都有噪声，每次只看到一部分。截断符号距离函数（TSDF）融合（Curless & Levoy 1996；KinectFusion，Newcombe 等 2011 用 GPU 做到了实时）的做法极其朴素：让每个体素存一个 \(D\) 和一个权重 \(W\)。每一条射线测得距离 \(z\)，射线上深度为 \(t\) 的体素得到一个观测 \(\psi=\min(z-t,\ \mu)\)（表面前方为正，后方为负）；只更新离表面不太远的体素（\(z-t\ge-\mu\)，\(\mu\) 是截断距离）。然后把新观测并入加权平均：\(D\leftarrow\dfrac{WD+w\psi}{W+w}\)，\(W\leftarrow W+w\)。零等值面就是融合后的表面。

    为什么平均能消除噪声：每一次观测的误差互相独立，平均 \(N\) 个以后，标准差按 \(1/\sqrt N\) 下降；而且这个平均是在场上做的，不需要先把不同视角的点一一对应起来（12.1 节的 ICP 里那个难点）。下图：一个深度传感器沿圆周从不同方向扫描同一个物体（一圈 60 条射线）。点"+1 次扫描"，对比原始点（噪声不变）与融合曲面（越来越准）；右图是沿竖线（可拖动）的一维切片：融合的 \(D\)（琥珀）对比真实的截断 SDF（虚线）。

    A real sensor is noisy and sees only part of the scene each time. Truncated signed distance function (TSDF) fusion (Curless & Levoy 1996; KinectFusion, Newcombe et al. 2011, made it real-time on a GPU) is disarmingly simple: every voxel stores a value \(D\) and a weight \(W\). Each beam measures a range \(z\); the voxel at depth \(t\) along it receives an observation \(\psi=\min(z-t,\ \mu)\) (positive in front of the surface, negative behind), and only voxels not too far behind the surface (\(z-t\ge-\mu\), with \(\mu\) the truncation distance) are updated. The new observation is folded in by a weighted average: \(D\leftarrow\dfrac{WD+w\psi}{W+w}\), \(W\leftarrow W+w\). The zero level set is the fused surface.

    Why averaging kills noise: the observations' errors are independent, so after \(N\) of them the standard deviation falls as \(1/\sqrt N\); and the average is taken on the field, with no need to first match points between views (the hard part of ICP in Lesson 12.1).     一个想法，三张面孔One idea, three faces

    占据栅格、TSDF 做的是同一件事：每个体素存一个"累计证据"，每次观测用一个可加的量更新（对数几率相加 / 加权平均）。区别在证据的含义：占据栅格存"这里是实体的概率"，TSDF 存"到表面的（截断）距离"，后者多带了位置信息，所以能抽出亚体素精度的表面，也能直接给出法向。两者都是 4.7 节贝叶斯滤波在静态状态上的特例（没有预测步，只有测量更新）。

    Occupancy grids and TSDFs do the same thing: every voxel stores accumulated evidence and each observation updates it with an additive quantity (log-odds sum / weighted mean). They differ in what the evidence means: an occupancy grid stores "the probability this is solid", a TSDF stores "the (truncated) distance to the surface"; the latter carries position information, so it yields sub-voxel surfaces and normals directly. Both are special cases of the Bayes filter of Lesson 4.7 on a static state (no prediction step, only measurement updates).

    5再看数学The math

    占据栅格：对数几率Occupancy grids: log-odds

    设某格被占据的概率为 \(p\)，几率 \(\mathrm{odds}=p/(1-p)\)，对数几率 \(\ell=\ln\dfrac{p}{1-p}\)，逆变换 \(p=\dfrac1{1+e^{-\ell}}\)。给定测量 \(z\)，贝叶斯公式在几率形式下是"后验几率 = 先验几率 × 似然比"。各格独立、测量条件独立时：

    Let a cell be occupied with probability \(p\), its odds \(p/(1-p)\), its log-odds \(\ell=\ln\dfrac{p}{1-p}\), with inverse \(p=\dfrac1{1+e^{-\ell}}\). Bayes' rule in odds form reads "posterior odds = prior odds × likelihood ratio". For independent cells and conditionally independent measurements:

    $$ \ell_t=\ell_{t-1}+\underbrace{\ln\frac{p(\text{occ}\mid z_t)}{1-p(\text{occ}\mid z_t)}}_{\text{inverse sensor model: }\ell_{\rm hit}\text{ or }\ell_{\rm miss}}-\ell_0,\qquad \ell_0=\ln\frac{p_0}{1-p_0}=0\ \text{ for } p_0=\tfrac12 . $$

    每次测量只是加一个常数，所以更新是 \(O(1)\)，顺序无关，也不会数值下溢。钳位 \(\ell\in[\ell_{\min},\ell_{\max}]\) 让地图在环境变化后很快"改口"（FIG 2 的鬼影）。例如 \(p_{\rm hit}=0.7\)、\(p_{\rm miss}=0.4\)：\(\ell_{\rm hit}=+0.847\)，\(\ell_{\rm miss}=-0.405\)。OctoMap（Hornung 等 2013）的默认值正是 \(p_{\rm hit}=0.7\)、\(p_{\rm miss}=0.4\)，并把 \(\ell\) 钳在概率约 0.12 到 0.97 之间。

    Each measurement adds a constant, so an update is \(O(1)\), order-independent and free of underflow. Clamping \(\ell\in[\ell_{\min},\ell_{\max}]\) lets the map change its mind quickly after the world changes (the ghosts of FIG 2). For example \(p_{\rm hit}=0.7\), \(p_{\rm miss}=0.4\) give \(\ell_{\rm hit}=+0.847\), \(\ell_{\rm miss}=-0.405\). OctoMap (Hornung et al. 2013) uses exactly these defaults, \(p_{\rm hit}=0.7\) and \(p_{\rm miss}=0.4\), and clamps \(\ell\) to probabilities of about 0.12 to 0.97.

    SDF：定义、Eikonal 方程与梯度SDFs: definition, the Eikonal equation and the gradient

    为什么 \(\|\nabla d\|=1\)。距离是 1-Lipschitz 的：\(|d(\mathbf x)-d(\mathbf y)|\le\|\mathbf x-\mathbf y\|\)（从 \(\mathbf x\) 走到 \(\mathbf y\)，到表面的距离最多变化走过的长度），所以 \(\|\nabla d\|\le1\)。另一方面，设 \(\mathbf c\) 是 \(\mathbf x\) 的最近表面点，沿线段从 \(\mathbf x\) 向 \(\mathbf c\) 走，距离恰好以单位速率减小，于是这个方向的方向导数是 \(-1\)。两者合起来：\(\nabla d\) 是单位向量，且指向远离 \(\mathbf c\) 的方向，即 \(\nabla d=(\mathbf x-\mathbf c)/d\)，推出最近点公式 \(\mathbf c=\mathbf x-d\nabla d\)。这就是 Eikonal 方程 \(\|\nabla d\|=1\)（几何光学里波前传播的方程）。它在中轴上不成立：那里有两个最近点，\(d\) 像山脊一样有折痕，梯度不存在（FIG 3 的紫色点）。

    对变换友好。刚体变换 \((R,\mathbf t)\) 后的物体，其 SDF 为 \(d'(\mathbf x)=d(R^\top(\mathbf x-\mathbf t))\)，仍然是精确的 SDF（刚体运动保持距离）；整体放大 \(s\) 倍则是 \(d'(\mathbf x)=s\,d(\mathbf x/s)\)（只写 \(d(\mathbf x/s)\) 会让梯度模变成 \(1/s\)）。把物体向外"膨胀" \(r\)（与半径 \(r\) 的圆盘做 Minkowski 和）得到 \(d-r\)：因此圆盘（球）机器人与物体碰撞 \(\Leftrightarrow d(\mathbf c)<\rho\)，这就是 7.2 节"构型空间障碍物"的最简单一例。非圆形的机器人常用若干个球近似：\(\min_i\big(d(\mathbf c_i)-\rho_i\big)<0\) 即碰撞，而 \(\nabla d\) 还告诉你每个球该往哪里推。

    布尔运算只是 min / max。并集 \(\min(d_A,d_B)\)，交集 \(\max(d_A,d_B)\)，差集 \(A\setminus B\) 是 \(\max(d_A,-d_B)\)。要诚实：它们只在一侧精确——并集的 \(\min\) 在外部等于真实距离，但在内部低估深度（一部分 A 的表面其实埋在 B 里）；交集的 \(\max\) 在内部精确、外部低估；差集在 B 的内部附近误差最大（FIG 3 的"差"）。好在它们仍然是 1-Lipschitz，球追踪依旧安全（只是保守）。要得到圆润的融合，常用多项式平滑最小值：\(\mathrm{smin}(a,b,k)=\min(a,b)-\dfrac{h^2k}{4}\)，\(h=\dfrac{\max(k-|a-b|,0)}{k}\)（这个形式由 Inigo Quilez 推广）。混合区里 \(\|\nabla\|<1\)，不再是严格的距离。

    Why \(\|\nabla d\|=1\). Distance is 1-Lipschitz: \(|d(\mathbf x)-d(\mathbf y)|\le\|\mathbf x-\mathbf y\|\) (walking from \(\mathbf x\) to \(\mathbf y\) changes the distance to the surface by at most the length walked), so \(\|\nabla d\|\le1\). Conversely, let \(\mathbf c\) be a closest surface point of \(\mathbf x\); walking along the segment from \(\mathbf x\) toward \(\mathbf c\), the distance falls at exactly unit rate, so the directional derivative that way is \(-1\). Together: \(\nabla d\) is a unit vector pointing away from \(\mathbf c\), i.e. \(\nabla d=(\mathbf x-\mathbf c)/d\), which gives the closest-point formula \(\mathbf c=\mathbf x-d\nabla d\). This is the Eikonal equation \(\|\nabla d\|=1\) (the wavefront equation of geometric optics). It fails on the medial axis: there are two closest points, \(d\) has a ridge-like crease and the gradient does not exist (the purple dots of FIG 3).

    Friendly to transforms. After a rigid motion \((R,\mathbf t)\) the body's SDF is \(d'(\mathbf x)=d(R^\top(\mathbf x-\mathbf t))\), still exact (rigid motions preserve distance); scaling the body by \(s\) gives \(d'(\mathbf x)=s\,d(\mathbf x/s)\) (writing just \(d(\mathbf x/s)\) would make the gradient norm \(1/s\)). Growing the body outward by \(r\) (a Minkowski sum with a disc of radius \(r\)) gives \(d-r\): hence a disc (sphere) robot collides with the body \(\Leftrightarrow d(\mathbf c)<\rho\), the simplest configuration-space obstacle of Lesson 7.2. Non-round robots are often approximated by several spheres: collision iff \(\min_i\big(d(\mathbf c_i)-\rho_i\big)<0\), and \(\nabla d\) tells each sphere where to be pushed.

    Booleans are just min / max. Union \(\min(d_A,d_B)\), intersection \(\max(d_A,d_B)\), difference \(A\setminus B\) is \(\max(d_A,-d_B)\). To be honest: they are exact on one side only. The union's \(\min\) equals the true distance outside but underestimates depth inside (part of A's surface is actually buried in B); the intersection's \(\max\) is exact inside and underestimates outside; the difference is worst near the inside of B (FIG 3's "Subtract"). They remain 1-Lipschitz, so sphere tracing stays safe (only conservative). For rounded blends the polynomial smooth minimum is common: \(\mathrm{smin}(a,b,k)=\min(a,b)-\dfrac{h^2k}{4}\), \(h=\dfrac{\max(k-|a-b|,0)}{k}\) (popularized by Inigo Quilez). In the blend zone \(\|\nabla\|<1\): no longer a strict distance.

    网格：顶点、面与法向Meshes: vertices, faces and normals

    三角网格 = 顶点数组 \(\{\mathbf v_i\}\) + 面（三个顶点编号）。三角形 \((\mathbf v_0,\mathbf v_1,\mathbf v_2)\) 的法向和面积来自叉积（1.8）：\(\mathbf n=\dfrac{(\mathbf v_1-\mathbf v_0)\times(\mathbf v_2-\mathbf v_0)}{\|\cdots\|}\)，面积 \(=\tfrac12\|\cdots\|\)，顶点顺序（逆时针）决定法向朝哪边。水密（watertight）指每条边恰好被两个面共享（流形，manifold），没有洞和悬边；水密网格才有明确的内外（射线奇偶或绕数）。对亏格 0 的闭合网格，欧拉公式 \(V-E+F=2\)。网格到 SDF：到最近三角形的距离，符号取自最近特征的"伪法向"（Bærentzen & Aanæs 2005）。常用半边（half-edge）结构做邻接遍历。

    A triangle mesh = an array of vertices \(\{\mathbf v_i\}\) + faces (three vertex indices each). A triangle \((\mathbf v_0,\mathbf v_1,\mathbf v_2)\) has normal and area from the cross product (1.8): \(\mathbf n=\dfrac{(\mathbf v_1-\mathbf v_0)\times(\mathbf v_2-\mathbf v_0)}{\|\cdots\|}\), area \(=\tfrac12\|\cdots\|\), and the (counter-clockwise) vertex order decides which way the normal points. Watertight means every edge is shared by exactly two faces (manifold) with no holes or dangling edges; only a watertight mesh has a well-defined inside (ray parity or winding number). For a closed genus-0 mesh, Euler's formula gives \(V-E+F=2\). Mesh → SDF: distance to the nearest triangle, with the sign taken from the nearest feature's "pseudo-normal" (Bærentzen & Aanæs 2005). Half-edge structures are the usual way to walk adjacency.

    球追踪的安全性与速度Why sphere tracing is safe, and how fast it is

    $$ t_{k+1}=t_k+\frac{f(\mathbf o+t_k\hat{\mathbf r})}{L},\qquad L\ge\|\nabla f\|\ \Rightarrow\ \frac{f(\mathbf x)}{L}\le d(\mathbf x)\ \ (\text{no overshoot}),\qquad n_{\rm steps}\approx\frac{\ln(\epsilon/h_0)}{\ln(1-\sin\alpha)} . $$

    \(f\) 在表面上为 0 且 Lipschitz 常数为 \(L\)，则 \(f(\mathbf x)\le L\,d(\mathbf x)\)，所以步长取 \(f/L\) 永远不会越过表面；真正的 SDF 有 \(L=1\)。FIG 4 里 \(f=s\,d\)，\(L=s\)：\(s>1\) 时不加 \(1/L\) 就会穿透，\(s<1\) 则安全但慢。步数：射线以掠射角 \(\alpha\) 靠近一个平面，每一步高度乘以 \((1-\sin\alpha)\)，\(h_0\) 是起始高度、\(\epsilon\) 是命中阈值，所以 \(\alpha\) 越小步数越多（在 node 里对 \(\alpha=0.05,0.1,0.3\) rad 验证过：预测 135、66、20 步，实测 136、67、21 次求值，后者多出的 1 次是最后那次命中判定）。

    If \(f\) vanishes on the surface and has Lipschitz constant \(L\), then \(f(\mathbf x)\le L\,d(\mathbf x)\), so stepping by \(f/L\) can never cross the surface; a true SDF has \(L=1\). In FIG 4, \(f=s\,d\) so \(L=s\): for \(s>1\) it pierces unless you divide by \(L\), and \(s<1\) is safe but slow. The step count: a ray approaching a plane at grazing angle \(\alpha\) multiplies its height by \((1-\sin\alpha)\) each step, with \(h_0\) the start height and \(\epsilon\) the hit threshold, so small \(\alpha\) means many steps (checked in node for \(\alpha=0.05,0.1,0.3\) rad: predicted 135, 66, 20 steps, measured 136, 67, 21 evaluations, the extra one being the final hit test).

    marching squares 的误差与 dual contouringMarching squares error, and dual contouring

    在格长 \(h\) 的网格上，对光滑、曲率为 \(\kappa\) 的表面，线性插值给出的顶点落在真实表面上的偏差量级是 \(O(\kappa h^2)\)（在 node 里对圆验证：\(n=8,16,32,64\) 时顶点处最大 \(|d|\) 实测为 \(2.3\times10^{-2}\)、\(6.9\times10^{-3}\)、\(1.9\times10^{-3}\)、\(5.1\times10^{-4}\)，每次翻倍约降 3.7 倍）。尖角处则不同：线性插值会把棱角削平，误差只按 \(O(h)\) 下降，FIG 4 的形状有尖角，所以读数里的误差降得慢一些。Dual contouring 在每个格里存一个顶点，位置取"各交点切平面到该点距离平方和最小"（一个小的二次误差函数 QEF）的解，因此能保住尖角。

    On a grid of spacing \(h\), for a smooth surface of curvature \(\kappa\), linear interpolation puts vertices off the true surface by \(O(\kappa h^2)\) (checked in node on a circle: at \(n=8,16,32,64\) the largest \(|d|\) at the vertices is \(2.3\times10^{-2}\), \(6.9\times10^{-3}\), \(1.9\times10^{-3}\), \(5.1\times10^{-4}\), falling about 3.7× per doubling). Sharp corners are different: linear interpolation shaves them off and the error drops only as \(O(h)\); FIG 4's shape has corners, so its readout falls more slowly. Dual contouring stores one vertex per cell, placed at the minimizer of the summed squared distances to the tangent planes at the cell's edge crossings (a small quadratic error function, QEF), and so keeps sharp corners.

    TSDF：加权平均就是最小二乘TSDF: the weighted mean is a least-squares solution

    $$ D_N=\arg\min_D\sum_{i=1}^{N}w_i\,(D-\psi_i)^2=\frac{\sum_i w_i\psi_i}{\sum_i w_i}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad D\leftarrow\frac{WD+w\psi}{W+w},\ \ W\leftarrow W+w,\qquad \operatorname{std}(D_N)=\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt N}\ (w_i=1). $$

    所以更新规则是对一维最小二乘的递推解（1.13 节；4.4 节里两个高斯的融合是同一个加权平均）。权重 \(w\) 可以反映传感器的可信度（远处的深度更噪、斜入射更不可信）；KinectFusion 还把 \(W\) 封顶，使旧数据逐渐被遗忘，能适应变化。需要指出两点：(1) 沿射线的 \(z-t\) 是"射线方向的距离"，斜入射时比到表面的真实最短距离大，所以 TSDF 不是严格的欧氏距离，只在表面附近大致可用；(2) 截断以外的数值没有意义。

    So the update is the recursive solution of a 1D least-squares problem (Lesson 1.13; fusing two Gaussians in Lesson 4.4 is the same weighted mean). The weight \(w\) can encode sensor trust (depth is noisier far away, and at oblique incidence). KinectFusion also caps \(W\) so old data is gradually forgotten, which lets the map adapt. Two caveats: (1) \(z-t\) is the distance along the ray, which at oblique incidence exceeds the true shortest distance to the surface, so a TSDF is not a strict Euclidean distance, only roughly one near the surface; (2) values beyond the truncation carry no meaning.

    从 TSDF 到 ESDF。规划器要的是处处有效的欧氏 SDF（ESDF），而不是只有一层薄壳的 TSDF。做法：把 TSDF 的符号（或权重阈值）当作占据 / 空闲标记，再做距离变换（精确的欧氏距离变换可以在每个维度上 \(O(N)\) 完成，Felzenszwalb & Huttenlocher 2012）。机器人建图的库一般做增量版本：只传播被新数据改变的体素（Voxblox、FIESTA 的思路）。

    From TSDF to ESDF. A planner needs a Euclidean SDF (ESDF) valid everywhere, not a TSDF that is a thin shell. The recipe: take the TSDF's sign (or a weight threshold) as the occupied / free labels and run a distance transform (the exact Euclidean distance transform costs \(O(N)\) per dimension; Felzenszwalb & Huttenlocher 2012). Robot mapping libraries usually run an incremental version, propagating only through voxels changed by new data (the idea of Voxblox and FIESTA).

    神经隐式表示（指路）Neural implicit representations (pointer)

    用神经网络 \(f_\theta(\mathbf x)\) 代替网格上的数值：DeepSDF（Park 等 2019）学一个"形状码 + 坐标 → 符号距离"的网络；Occupancy Networks（Mescheder 等 2019）输出占据概率；IGR（Gropp 等 2020）把 Eikonal 条件写成损失 \(\mathbb E(\|\nabla f_\theta\|-1)^2\) 来逼网络成为 SDF；NeuS、VolSDF（2021）把 SDF 放进体渲染，从多视图图像恢复表面。它们的优势是连续、紧凑、可微，代价是查询要跑网络、不能直接编辑。12.4 节会再用到这些思想。

    Replace the grid values with a neural network \(f_\theta(\mathbf x)\): DeepSDF (Park et al. 2019) learns a network mapping "shape code + coordinate → signed distance"; Occupancy Networks (Mescheder et al. 2019) output an occupancy probability; IGR (Gropp et al. 2020) writes the Eikonal condition as a loss \(\mathbb E(\|\nabla f_\theta\|-1)^2\) to push the network toward an SDF; NeuS and VolSDF (2021) put the SDF inside volume rendering to recover surfaces from multi-view images. Their strength is a continuous, compact, differentiable function; the price is a network evaluation per query and no direct editing. Lesson 12.4 returns to these ideas.

    6机器人中的它：让轨迹被 SDF 推开In robotics: letting the SDF push a trajectory away

    SDF 在机器人里最直接的用途是避障代价。回忆 6.13 节：把轨迹看成一串路点 \(\mathbf q_1,\dots,\mathbf q_n\)，代价 = 平滑项 + 障碍项。障碍项在路点离障碍的距离 \(d\) 小于安全边距 \(\varepsilon\) 时罚 \(c(d)=\tfrac12(\varepsilon-d)^2\)，它的负梯度 \(-\nabla c=(\varepsilon-d)\nabla d\) 把路点沿 SDF 梯度推离障碍。6.13 里障碍是一个解析的圆盘，\(d\) 随手可写；而真实的障碍是点云、网格、或一张融合出来的 TSDF——于是你需要一张查询表：把障碍预先烘焙成栅格上的 ESDF，运行时对栅格做双线性插值（得到 \(d\)），再对插值函数求梯度（得到 \(\nabla d\)）。这正是 CHOMP（Ratliff 等 2009）以及后来许多基于栅格 ESDF 的规划器（如 Voxblox、FIESTA 之上的无人机规划）的做法。

    下图复现这个流程：拖动红色圆圈（障碍中心）、绿色圆圈（起点 / 终点）。按"优化"，路点沿 \(-\nabla F\)（平滑项 + 障碍项）下降；红色小箭头是障碍项的推力。把"网格 ESDF"换成"解析 SDF"、把栅格边长调大，观察栅格误差怎样影响结果。

    The most direct use of an SDF in robotics is the obstacle cost. Recall Lesson 6.13: view a trajectory as waypoints \(\mathbf q_1,\dots,\mathbf q_n\) with cost = smoothness + obstacle. The obstacle term penalizes \(c(d)=\tfrac12(\varepsilon-d)^2\) when a waypoint's distance \(d\) to the obstacle is below a safety margin \(\varepsilon\), and its negative gradient \(-\nabla c=(\varepsilon-d)\nabla d\) pushes the waypoint along the SDF gradient, away from the obstacle. In 6.13 the obstacle was an analytic disc, so \(d\) was easy to write; real obstacles are point clouds, meshes or a fused TSDF, so you need a lookup table: bake the obstacles into an ESDF on a grid, and at run time bilinearly interpolate it (giving \(d\)) and differentiate the interpolant (giving \(\nabla d\)). This is the recipe of CHOMP (Ratliff et al. 2009) and of many later grid-ESDF planners (for instance drone planners built on Voxblox or FIESTA).

    Navigation: ROS's costmap_2d uses an occupancy grid plus an "inflation layer" (cost rising as obstacles get closer and decaying with distance); inflation is essentially a distance transform of the obstacles. Optimization: CHOMP-style methods approximate the robot's body by a set of spheres (or sample points) and use the environment SDF for each one's cost and gradient. Learning: the Volumetric Grasping Network (Breyer et al., CoRL 2020) feeds a scene TSDF to a network to predict grasps.

    7常见误区Common pitfalls

    误区 1：占据栅格就是 SDFPitfall 1: an occupancy grid is an SDF

    占据栅格只有"是 / 否"：它没有距离，更没有梯度。直接对 0/1 的栅格求"梯度"，只在边界那一圈格子里非零，且方向被 8 个邻居量化。要拿它做避障代价，先做一次距离变换得到 ESDF（costmap 的膨胀层正是这样做的）。反过来，从 SDF 取符号总能得到占据，信息只会损失，不会凭空出现。

    An occupancy grid has only yes / no: no distance, let alone a gradient. Taking the "gradient" of a 0/1 grid is nonzero only on the one-cell boundary shell and is quantized to the 8 neighbours. To use it for an obstacle cost, first run a distance transform to get an ESDF (exactly what the costmap's inflation layer does). Conversely, the sign of an SDF always gives an occupancy; information is only lost that way, never created.

    误区 2：未知 = 空闲Pitfall 2: unknown = free

    FIG 2 里盒子背后的灰色格子 \(\ell=0\)，\(p=0.5\)：不是"可能空闲"，而是"没有任何信息"。把未知当空闲，规划器会把机器人送进没看过的死角（可能是墙）；把未知当占据，机器人在开阔环境里就走不出第一步。大多数系统给未知单独的状态，探索任务还会专门走向未知与空闲的交界（frontier）。

    The grey cells behind the box in FIG 2 have \(\ell=0\), \(p=0.5\): not "probably free" but "no information at all". Treat unknown as free and the planner sends the robot into an unobserved blind corner (maybe a wall); treat it as occupied and the robot cannot take its first step in open space. Most systems keep unknown as its own state, and exploration deliberately heads for the frontier between unknown and free.

    误区 3：截断的 SDF 在远处也是距离Pitfall 3: a truncated SDF is a distance far from the surface

    TSDF 在离表面超过 \(\mu\) 的地方恒等于 \(\pm\mu\)（甚至根本没有数据），梯度为零或无意义。若直接拿它做 CHOMP 式避障，离障碍 \(\mu\) 以外的路点感受不到任何推力。规划需要的是远处也有效的 ESDF；而且斜入射的 TSDF 本来就不是严格的欧氏距离（数学一节）。

    Beyond \(\mu\) from the surface a TSDF is constant \(\pm\mu\) (or has no data at all), and its gradient is zero or meaningless. Use it directly for CHOMP-style avoidance and any waypoint farther than \(\mu\) from an obstacle feels no force. Planning needs an ESDF valid at long range; and an obliquely-observed TSDF is not a strict Euclidean distance anyway (see the maths).

    误区 4：min / max 布尔运算给出的就是 SDFPitfall 4: min / max booleans give you an SDF

    FIG 3 的"差"在一侧偏离真实距离可超过 0.2（取决于摆放）；平滑混合更是 \(\|\nabla\|<1\)。后果：按 \(d-r\) 做的"膨胀"不再是真正的 \(r\) 偏移；用 \(d\) 当距离估计碰撞余量会过于乐观或悲观；球追踪仍安全（\(d\) 仍是真实距离的下界，只要符号处理正确），但会变慢。需要精确距离时，重新做距离变换。另外要留意符号约定：本课里面为负；有的库（例如只存"到障碍的距离"的 ESDF）把障碍内部存成 0 或正数，换库时先看文档。

    FIG 3's "Subtract" can deviate from the true distance by more than 0.2 on one side (depending on the layout), and the smooth blend even has \(\|\nabla\|<1\). Consequences: an "offset" made by \(d-r\) is no longer a true \(r\)-offset; using \(d\) as a clearance estimate is too optimistic or too pessimistic; sphere tracing stays safe (\(d\) remains a lower bound on the true distance as long as signs are handled correctly) but slows down. When you need exact distance, redo a distance transform. Mind the sign convention too: here inside is negative; some libraries (for example ESDFs that store only "distance to the nearest obstacle") store 0 or positive values inside obstacles, so read the docs when switching libraries.

    误区 5：任何网格都能判断里外Pitfall 5: any mesh can tell inside from outside

    扫描得到的网格常有洞、悬边、自相交、重复面。没有水密性，"里面"就没有定义：射线奇偶法对同一个点会随射线方向得出不同答案，SDF 的符号也会翻转成一片一片的。碰撞用的网格通常要先修复，或者换成凸分解，或者用绕数（winding number）这类对小缺陷更稳健的方法定符号。

    Scanned meshes often have holes, dangling edges, self-intersections and duplicate faces. Without watertightness "inside" is undefined: ray parity can give different answers for the same point depending on the ray direction, and the SDF sign flips in patches. Collision meshes usually need repair first, or a convex decomposition, or a sign rule such as the winding number that tolerates small defects.

    误区 6：分辨率够用了，梯度也一定好用Pitfall 6: enough resolution means a usable gradient

    两个独立的陷阱。一是格子 vs 机器人：格长应小于要保留的最小细节（薄墙、窄缝）和机器人尺寸的一部分，否则 2 cm 的薄壁在 5 cm 的体素里会消失，窄门会被"填满"。二是中轴：\(\nabla d\) 在中轴上不连续，沿 \(\nabla d\) 做梯度下降的优化器（如 FIG 6）在两个障碍正中间会左右抖动或选边；栅格太粗时，插值函数的梯度还会在格子边界上跳变。适当的安全边距、平滑（如把 ESDF 略微模糊）和优化器的阻尼都能缓解，但无法消除局部最优。

    Two separate traps. One is cell versus robot: the cell size must be well below the smallest detail to keep (thin walls, narrow gaps) and a fraction of the robot's size, or a 2 cm wall vanishes in 5 cm voxels and a narrow door gets filled in. The other is the medial axis: \(\nabla d\) is discontinuous there, so an optimizer descending along \(\nabla d\) (like FIG 6) jitters or picks a side midway between two obstacles; with coarse grids the interpolant's gradient also jumps at cell borders. Sensible margins, smoothing (e.g. slightly blurring the ESDF) and optimizer damping help, but local minima stay.

    8小练习Check yourself

    1. 仓储机器人用 5 cm 的体素稠密地存 20 m × 20 m × 4 m 的空间，每个体素存 float32 的 TSDF 和 float32 的权重（共 8 字节）。需要多少内存？1. A warehouse robot stores a 20 m × 20 m × 4 m volume densely with 5 cm voxels, each holding a float32 TSDF and a float32 weight (8 bytes in all). How much memory is needed?
    每个方向 \(400,400,80\) 格，共 \(12.8\times10^6\) 格，乘 8 字节是 102.4 MB（12.8 MB 对应每格 1 字节）。这还只是一个仓库的一层楼；分辨率提高到 2.5 cm 就是 8 倍。所以稀疏的哈希体素块只给表面附近分配内存，才是真实系统的做法。The grid is \(400\times400\times80\) cells, \(12.8\times10^6\) in all; times 8 bytes that is 102.4 MB (12.8 MB would be 1 byte per cell). That is just one floor of one warehouse, and halving the voxel size to 2.5 cm costs 8×. Hence real systems allocate sparse, hashed voxel blocks only near surfaces.
    2. 某格先后被 3 次"命中"（\(p_{\rm hit}=0.7\)）和 1 次"穿过"（\(p_{\rm miss}=0.4\)）更新，初始 \(p_0=0.5\)（不考虑钳位）。它现在被占据的概率约为？2. A cell is updated by 3 "hits" (\(p_{\rm hit}=0.7\)) and 1 "pass-through" (\(p_{\rm miss}=0.4\)), starting from \(p_0=0.5\) (ignore clamping). Its probability of being occupied is now about?
    \(\ell=3\ln\frac{0.7}{0.3}+\ln\frac{0.4}{0.6}=3(0.847)-0.405=2.136\)，\(p=1/(1+e^{-2.136})=0.894\)。0.63 是把四个概率直接取了平均（错误：证据应该相乘，也就是几率相乘）；0.93 是忘了那次穿过。\(\ell=3\ln\frac{0.7}{0.3}+\ln\frac{0.4}{0.6}=3(0.847)-0.405=2.136\), so \(p=1/(1+e^{-2.136})=0.894\). 0.63 averages the four probabilities (wrong: evidence multiplies, i.e. odds multiply); 0.93 forgets the pass-through.
    3. 圆盘机器人半径 \(\rho=0.3\)，圆心处 \(d=0.15\)，\(\nabla d=(0.6,\,0.8)\)（已是单位向量）。使它刚好脱离碰撞的最小位移是？3. A disc robot has radius \(\rho=0.3\); at its centre \(d=0.15\) and \(\nabla d=(0.6,\,0.8)\) (already a unit vector). What is the smallest displacement that just frees it from collision?
    沿 \(\nabla d\) 每走单位长度 \(d\) 增加 1，需要把 \(d\) 从 0.15 增加到 0.3，所以走 \(\rho-d=0.15\)：\(0.15\,(0.6,0.8)=(0.09,0.12)\)。取反方向会更深地进入物体；\((0.06,0.08)\) 只走了 0.1，\(d\) 才到 0.25。对精确的 SDF，这个位移（到该局部的最近表面）是最短的。Moving along \(\nabla d\) raises \(d\) by 1 per unit length, and \(d\) must rise from 0.15 to 0.3, so move \(\rho-d=0.15\): \(0.15\,(0.6,0.8)=(0.09,0.12)\). The opposite sign goes deeper into the object; \((0.06,0.08)\) has length 0.1 and only reaches \(d=0.25\). For an exact SDF this is the shortest escape (to the locally closest surface).
    4. 一个体素当前 \(D=0.10\)，\(W=3\)。又来一次观测 \(\psi=-0.02\)，权重 \(w=1\)。更新后的 \(D\) 与 \(W\)？4. A voxel currently has \(D=0.10\), \(W=3\). A new observation \(\psi=-0.02\) with weight \(w=1\) arrives. What are \(D\) and \(W\) afterwards?
    \(D'=(3\times0.10+1\times(-0.02))/(3+1)=0.28/4=0.07\)，\(W'=4\)。旧值有三份权重，所以新观测只拉动它 1/4 的距离（0.08 是没有算新观测的符号）。\(D'=(3\times0.10+1\times(-0.02))/(3+1)=0.28/4=0.07\), \(W'=4\). The old value carries three units of weight, so the new observation moves it only a quarter of the way (0.08 mistakes the sign of the observation).
    5. 有人把真正的 SDF 乘以 2 得到 \(f=2d\)（零集不变），再用球追踪渲染，射线穿过了物体。为什么，怎么办？5. Someone doubles a true SDF to \(f=2d\) (same zero set) and sphere-traces it, and rays pierce the object. Why, and what fixes it?
    球追踪的安全性只依赖"步长不超过到表面的真实距离"。\(f=2d\) 的梯度模是 2，直接用它当步长就可能走过头；除以 \(L\) 后恢复安全（代价是步数变多）。这也是为什么用神经网络学到的"近似 SDF"渲染时要么保证 Eikonal 项学得好，要么把步长乘一个小于 1 的系数。Sphere tracing's safety depends only on "the step never exceeds the true distance to the surface". \(f=2d\) has gradient norm 2, so using it as the step can overshoot; dividing by \(L\) restores safety (at the price of more steps). This is also why a learned, approximate SDF from a neural network is rendered either with a well-trained Eikonal term or with a step multiplier below 1.
