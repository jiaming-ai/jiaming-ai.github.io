内参、外参与畸变Intrinsics, Extrinsics & Distortion

针孔模型里的 \(P=K[R|t]\) 把一个三维点变成一个像素，可是真正的相机还有三组要弄清的数：相机在世界里的位姿（外参），光线怎样落到像素格子上（内参 K），镜头偏离理想针孔的程度（畸变）。这一课拆开这三组参数，讲清每个数的单位、方向和约定，并用一面"墙"检验你有没有搞对。 The \(P=K[R|t]\) of the pinhole model turns a 3D point into a pixel, but a real camera still has three sets of numbers to get right: where it sits in the world (extrinsics), how rays land on the pixel grid (the intrinsic matrix K), and how far the lens departs from an ideal pinhole (distortion). This lesson takes the three apart, spells out the units, directions and conventions of every number, and checks your understanding on a wall.

    1为什么机器人需要它Why robots need this

    一台手眼系统里的深度相机看见一只杯子，杯子在像素 \((u,v)\)，深度是 \(z\)。要抓它，机器人得把这个像素变成基座坐标系里的一个三维点，路上要走三步：像素 \(\to\) 相机坐标系里的射线（靠内参 K），修正镜头弯曲了的光线（靠畸变系数），相机坐标系 \(\to\) 基座坐标系（靠外参，也就是手眼标定的结果，见 7.3 的工具系与基座系）。每一步都有几个数，每个数都有单位和方向的约定。

    这些数错一点，后果比想象中大。取 \(f=600\) 像素、深度 \(z=2\) m：横向坐标是 \(X=(u-c_x)\,z/f\)，如果主点 \(c_x\) 偏了 10 像素，杯子的横向位置就偏了 \(10\cdot2/600\approx3.3\) cm，已经超出大多数夹爪的容差；焦距偏 5%，离光轴 0.5 m 的点偏 2.5 cm。更糟的是误差和位置有关：一面平整的墙在点云里会弯成"香蕉"，两面垂直的墙夹角变成 85°。

    10.1 节给出针孔模型，10.2 节把投影写成了 \(\lambda\tilde{\mathbf x}=K[R|t]\tilde{\mathbf X}\)。这一课打开这个式子：K 里每个元素是什么，\([R|t]\) 为什么是相机位姿的逆，镜头畸变怎么建模、为什么不能直接求逆，以及 OpenCV、OpenGL、ROS 三套坐标约定怎么互相换算。下一课 10.4 会反过来从图像里估计这些参数。

    A depth camera in a hand-eye system sees a mug at pixel \((u,v)\) with depth \(z\). To grasp it, the robot must turn that pixel into a 3D point in the base frame, in three steps: pixel \(\to\) a ray in the camera frame (the intrinsics K), undo the lens's bending of the rays (the distortion coefficients), and camera frame \(\to\) base frame (the extrinsics, which is what hand-eye calibration delivers; see tool and base frames in 7.3). Every step has a few numbers, and every number comes with a unit and a direction convention.

    Small errors in these numbers cost more than you would expect. Take \(f=600\) px and depth \(z=2\) m: the lateral coordinate is \(X=(u-c_x)\,z/f\), so a principal point \(c_x\) that is 10 px off moves the mug sideways by \(10\cdot2/600\approx3.3\) cm, already beyond the tolerance of most grippers; a 5% error in focal length shifts a point 0.5 m off the axis by 2.5 cm. Worse, the error depends on position: a flat wall bends into a "banana" in the point cloud, and two perpendicular walls meet at 85°.

    Lesson 10.1 gave the pinhole model and 10.2 wrote the projection as \(\lambda\tilde{\mathbf x}=K[R|t]\tilde{\mathbf X}\). This lesson opens that formula: what each entry of K means, why \([R|t]\) is the inverse of the camera pose, how lens distortion is modelled and why it cannot simply be inverted, and how the OpenCV, OpenGL and ROS frame conventions convert into one another. The next lesson, 10.4, goes the other way and estimates these parameters from images.

    2直觉：内参 K 是一次二维仿射变换Intuition: K is a 2D affine map

    先把投影拆成两半。第一半与相机的"硬件"无关：相机坐标系（OpenCV 约定：x 向右，y 向下，z 向前）里的点 \((X,Y,Z)\) 除以深度，得到归一化坐标 \((x,y)=(X/Z,\,Y/Z)\)。它没有单位，就是光线方向的斜率，等价于一张放在 \(z=1\) 处的"理想底片"。第二半才是传感器：K 把这张理想底片上的坐标（以"焦距"为单位）换成像素。它只做三件事：

    • 缩放：\(f_x,f_y\)，单位是像素，等于"一个单位斜率对应多少像素"。镜头变长（变焦），\(f\) 变大，视场变窄。
    • 平移：主点 \((c_x,c_y)\)，光轴与传感器的交点。它不是平移相机，也不是转动相机：所有深度上的点都平移同样的像素数，没有视差。理想情况在图像中心，实际偏几个到几十个像素。
    • 变形：\(f_x\neq f_y\)（像素不是正方形）和斜切 \(s\)（传感器行列不垂直）。现代相机几乎总是 \(f_x\approx f_y\)，\(s\approx0\)，但模型里留着它们。

    下面的实验把这几件事摆在一起。左边从外面看相机和它的视锥，右边是相机拍到的图像（像素坐标 \(v\) 向下；图中坐标轴已按这个方向标出）。注意：改 K 时相机的位置和朝向一点没变，只有"底片上的刻度"变了。

    Split the projection into two halves. The first has nothing to do with the camera's hardware: a point \((X,Y,Z)\) in the camera frame (OpenCV convention: x right, y down, z forward) is divided by its depth to give normalized coordinates \((x,y)=(X/Z,\,Y/Z)\). They are unitless, the slopes of the ray, equivalent to an "ideal film" placed at \(z=1\). Only the second half involves the sensor: K converts coordinates on that ideal film (measured in units of "focal length") into pixels. It does just three things:

    • Scale: \(f_x,f_y\), measured in pixels, the number of pixels per unit slope. Zoom in and \(f\) grows while the field of view narrows.
    • Shift: the principal point \((c_x,c_y)\), where the optical axis meets the sensor. It is not a translation or rotation of the camera: points at every depth shift by the same number of pixels, so there is no parallax. Ideally it is the image center; in practice it is a few to a few tens of pixels off.
    • Shape: \(f_x\neq f_y\) (non-square pixels) and the skew \(s\) (sensor rows and columns not perpendicular). Modern cameras have \(f_x\approx f_y\) and \(s\approx0\), but the model keeps them.

On the left you see the camera and its frustum from outside; on the right, the image it takes (pixel coordinate \(v\) points down; the axes are labelled that way). Note that changing K leaves the camera's position and orientation untouched: only the ruler printed on the film changes.

    焦距的单位，和视场角The unit of focal length, and the field of view

    \(f\) 以像素计，和镜头标称的毫米焦距 \(f_{\text{mm}}\) 差一个像素间距：\(f_x=f_{\text{mm}}/p_x\)（\(p_x\) 是像素在传感器上的宽度）。4 mm 的镜头配 3 µm 的像素，\(f_x\approx1333\) px。同一个镜头换成更高分辨率的传感器（像素更小，或者同样的传感器读出更多像素），\(f_x\) 的数字就变了，镜头和视场角却没变，这就是为什么 K 必须绑定到具体的分辨率上。视场角只由 \(f\) 和图像宽度的比值决定：

    \(f\) is in pixels; it differs from the lens's nominal focal length \(f_{\text{mm}}\) by the pixel pitch: \(f_x=f_{\text{mm}}/p_x\) (\(p_x\) is the pixel width on the sensor). A 4 mm lens with 3 µm pixels gives \(f_x\approx1333\) px. Swap in a sensor with more pixels (smaller pixels, or the same sensor read out at a higher resolution) and the number \(f_x\) changes while the lens and its field of view do not, which is why K is always tied to a specific resolution. The field of view depends only on the ratio of \(f\) to the image size:

    $$ \tan\frac{\theta_h}{2}=\frac{w}{2f_x},\qquad \tan\frac{\theta_v}{2}=\frac{h}{2f_y},\qquad \theta_h=2\arctan\frac{w}{2f_x}. $$

    这就是视场角公式（见"数学"一节的推导）。例：\(w=640\)，\(\theta_h=90^\circ\) 时 \(f_x=320\)；\(60^\circ\) 时 \(f_x\approx554\)；\(120^\circ\) 时 \(f_x\approx185\)。主点偏离中心时左右两侧的半角不再相等：左边是 \(\arctan(c_x/f_x)\)，右边是 \(\arctan((w-c_x)/f_x)\)，FIG 1 的读数里能看到。

    改变图像大小时 K 怎么跟着变？如果把图像缩放 \(a\) 倍，每个像素坐标都乘 \(a\)，所以 \(f_x,f_y,c_x,c_y\) 都乘 \(a\)（\(s\) 也是）；如果裁出一个左上角在 \((x_0,y_0)\) 的窗口，像素坐标整体平移，只有 \(c_x,c_y\) 减去偏移，\(f\) 不变。归一化坐标和畸变系数与像素无关，所以畸变系数 D 在缩放和裁剪下保持不变。写成矩阵是 \(K'=S\,T\,K\)，在"数学"一节推导。

    This is the field-of-view formula (derived in "The math"). Examples: for \(w=640\), \(\theta_h=90^\circ\) needs \(f_x=320\); \(60^\circ\) needs \(f_x\approx554\); \(120^\circ\) needs \(f_x\approx185\). When the principal point is off-centre the two half-angles differ: \(\arctan(c_x/f_x)\) on the left and \(\arctan((w-c_x)/f_x)\) on the right, as the readout of FIG 1 shows.

    How does K follow when the image size changes? Scaling the image by \(a\) multiplies every pixel coordinate by \(a\), so \(f_x,f_y,c_x,c_y\) (and \(s\)) all scale by \(a\). Cropping out a window with top-left corner \((x_0,y_0)\) translates all pixel coordinates: only \(c_x,c_y\) lose the offset and \(f\) stays. Normalized coordinates and the distortion coefficients do not involve pixels at all, so the distortion coefficients D are unchanged by resizing and cropping. In matrix form \(K'=S\,T\,K\), derived in "The math".

    3动手玩：外参与整条投影链Play: extrinsics and the whole projection chain

    内参管"相机内部"，外参管"相机在世界里的哪里"。世界系里一个点 \(\mathbf X_w\) 要经过三次变换才变成像素：

    1. 世界 \(\to\) 相机：\(\mathbf X_c={}^{c}T_{w}\,\mathbf X_w=R\mathbf X_w+\mathbf t\)。这里的 \([R|t]\) 就是外参，它把世界坐标变成相机坐标。
    2. 相机 \(\to\) 归一化平面：\((x,y)=(X_c/Z_c,\;Y_c/Z_c)\)，除以深度（这一步丢掉了深度信息）。
    3. 归一化 \(\to\) 像素：\((u,v)\) 由 K 给出（再加上上一节说的畸变，我们放在后面）。

    最容易混淆的是方向：外参是相机位姿 \({}^{w}T_{c}\) 的逆。相机位姿说"相机坐标系的原点和三根轴在世界里的样子"，它的平移部分就是相机中心 \(C\)；外参是把世界点搬到相机里去，它的平移 \(\mathbf t=-R\,C\) 是"世界原点在相机坐标系里的位置"，不是相机的位置。FIG 2 让你自己摆相机，看读数里的 \([R|t]\) 和 \(C=-R^{\top}\mathbf t\)。

    Intrinsics concern the inside of the camera; extrinsics concern where the camera is in the world. A point \(\mathbf X_w\) in the world frame becomes a pixel through three transformations:

    1. World \(\to\) camera: \(\mathbf X_c={}^{c}T_{w}\,\mathbf X_w=R\mathbf X_w+\mathbf t\). This \([R|t]\) is the extrinsics: it converts world coordinates into camera coordinates.
    2. Camera \(\to\) normalized plane: \((x,y)=(X_c/Z_c,\;Y_c/Z_c)\), the division by depth (the step that throws depth away).
    3. Normalized \(\to\) pixels: \((u,v)\) from K (plus the distortion of the next section, which we postpone).

    The direction is the usual source of confusion: the extrinsics are the inverse of the camera pose \({}^{w}T_{c}\). The pose says "what the camera frame's origin and axes look like in the world", and its translation is the camera center \(C\); the extrinsics carry world points into the camera, and their translation \(\mathbf t=-R\,C\) is "the position of the world origin in the camera frame", not the position of the camera. FIG 2 lets you place the camera yourself and watch \([R|t]\) and \(C=-R^{\top}\mathbf t\) in the readout.

    4再换几个视角：畸变、去畸变与鱼眼More views: distortion, undistortion and fisheye

    镜头不是针孔：Brown–Conrady 模型A lens is not a pinhole: the Brown–Conrady model

    真实镜头会把光线"弯"一点，所以直线在图像里不再是直线。标准做法是在归一化坐标里先弯一下，再交给 K 换成像素：\((x,y)\to(x_d,y_d)\to(u,v)\)。弯的方式用两组项描述：径向项 \(k_1,k_2,k_3\)，让点沿半径方向向内或向外移动，移动量随半径 \(r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\) 迅速增大；切向项 \(p_1,p_2\)，来自镜片与传感器没有完全对中（"偏心畸变"）。

    \(k_1<0\) 时放大率随半径减小，图像边缘被"收"回来，直线向外鼓，叫桶形畸变，广角镜头常见；\(k_1>0\) 反过来，叫枕形，长焦常见；\(k_1\) 与 \(k_2\) 符号相反时可以得到先桶后枕的"胡须形"。FIG 3 里的棋盘格是真实算出来的：对输出图像的每个像素，用迭代去畸变求出它对应的归一化射线，再查表格上的颜色。

    A real lens bends the rays a little, so straight lines are no longer straight in the image. The standard recipe applies the bending in normalized coordinates and only then hands the result to K: \((x,y)\to(x_d,y_d)\to(u,v)\). The bending has two groups of terms: radial terms \(k_1,k_2,k_3\), which move points along the radius, inward or outward, by an amount that grows quickly with \(r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\); and tangential terms \(p_1,p_2\), which come from the lens elements not being perfectly centred on the sensor ("decentering distortion").

    With \(k_1<0\) the magnification falls with radius, the edges are pulled in and straight lines bulge outwards: barrel distortion, common in wide-angle lenses. \(k_1>0\) does the opposite, pincushion, common in telephotos; \(k_1\) and \(k_2\) of opposite sign give a "mustache" that is barrel near the center and pincushion near the edge. The checkerboard in FIG 3 is computed for real: for every output pixel it finds the corresponding normalized ray by iterative undistortion and looks up the colour on the board.

    反过来：去畸变没有闭式解The other way: undistortion has no closed form

    前向的 \(D\) 是一个多项式，随便算。可是图像里测到的是畸变后的点 \((x_d,y_d)\)，要做三角化、PnP、极线几何都需要无畸变的 \((x,y)\)，也就是解 \(D(x,y)=(x_d,y_d)\)。这是两个变量的高次方程组（\(k_3\ne0\) 时 \(r\) 的七次），没有闭式解，只能迭代。常见的三种：

    • A. 残差修正：\(\mathbf x\leftarrow\mathbf x-\bigl(D(\mathbf x)-\mathbf x_d\bigr)\)，即把 \(D\) 近似成恒等映射。本课的 Vision.undistort 就是它。
    • B. 不动点（OpenCV 的做法）：\(\mathbf x\leftarrow(\mathbf x_d-\Delta_{\text{tan}}(\mathbf x))/\rho(r^2)\)，其中 \(\rho=1+k_1r^2+k_2r^4+k_3r^6\)；OpenCV 的 undistortPoints 就是这类迭代，经典接口默认只迭代几次（5 次）。
    • N. Newton：\(\mathbf x\leftarrow\mathbf x-J_D(\mathbf x)^{-1}(D(\mathbf x)-\mathbf x_d)\)，二次收敛，每步多算一个 \(2\times2\) 雅可比。

    A 和 B 是线性收敛，收敛因子等于迭代映射的雅可比范数；N 则几步就到机器精度。FIG 4 把它们同时跑出来。这里还藏着一个重要的事实：当 \(k_1\) 很大时，\(r_d(r)\) 在 \(r^\ast\) 处到达最大值后往回走（FIG 3 右图的红点），超过它的 \(r_d\) 根本没有原像，迭代无论怎么做都收敛不到零残差。畸变系数是在标定图像覆盖的范围内拟合的，范围之外的"逆"没有意义。

    The forward map \(D\) is a polynomial and cheap. But the image gives you the distorted point \((x_d,y_d)\), and triangulation, PnP and epipolar geometry all need the undistorted \((x,y)\): you must solve \(D(x,y)=(x_d,y_d)\). That is a coupled system of high-degree polynomials in two unknowns (degree 7 in \(r\) when \(k_3\ne0\)) and it has no closed-form solution; you iterate. Three common schemes:

    • A. Residual correction: \(\mathbf x\leftarrow\mathbf x-\bigl(D(\mathbf x)-\mathbf x_d\bigr)\), i.e. pretend \(D\) is the identity. This is what this course's Vision.undistort does.
    • B. Fixed point (what OpenCV does): \(\mathbf x\leftarrow(\mathbf x_d-\Delta_{\text{tan}}(\mathbf x))/\rho(r^2)\) with \(\rho=1+k_1r^2+k_2r^4+k_3r^6\); OpenCV's undistortPoints is an iteration of this type, and the classic interface runs only a few (5) iterations by default.
    • N. Newton: \(\mathbf x\leftarrow\mathbf x-J_D(\mathbf x)^{-1}(D(\mathbf x)-\mathbf x_d)\), quadratically convergent at the price of a \(2\times2\) Jacobian per step.

    A and B converge linearly, with a rate equal to the norm of the iteration map's Jacobian; N reaches machine precision in a few steps. FIG 4 runs all three side by side. It also exposes an important fact: for a large \(k_1\), \(r_d(r)\) reaches a maximum at \(r^\ast\) and then turns back (the red dot in FIG 3, right), so a \(r_d\) beyond that maximum has no preimage and no iteration can drive the residual to zero. Distortion coefficients are fitted over the region the calibration images covered; the "inverse" outside it is meaningless.

    去畸变一张图，不需要求逆Undistorting a whole image needs no inversion

    求点的无畸变坐标要迭代，可是求一整张图的无畸变图像不用：对输出图（理想针孔图）的每个像素 \((u,v)\)，先算归一化坐标，用前向的 \(D\) 算出它在原始图里的位置，再在那里插值取色。OpenCV 的 initUndistortRectifyMap + remap 就是这样做的：映射表只算一次，之后每帧只是查表。反过来，想"把无畸变图渲染成带畸变的图"（比如仿真里模拟真实镜头）就要对每个像素求逆，FIG 3 的棋盘格就是这样画出来的。

    Undistorting a point takes an iteration, but undistorting a whole image does not: for each pixel \((u,v)\) of the output (ideal pinhole) image, compute its normalized coordinates, apply the forward \(D\) to find where that ray landed in the raw image, and interpolate the colour there. This is how OpenCV's initUndistortRectifyMap + remap work: the map is computed once and every frame is a table lookup. The reverse task, rendering an ideal image with distortion (for example to mimic a real lens in simulation), needs the inverse at every pixel, and that is how the checkerboard in FIG 3 is drawn.

    视场角超过 180°：鱼眼Fields of view beyond 180°: fisheye

    针孔的像距满足 \(r=f\tan\theta\)（\(\theta\) 是光线与光轴的夹角）。\(\theta\to90^\circ\) 时 \(r\to\infty\)：针孔相机的视场角永远到不了 180°，而且越靠近极限边缘被拉伸得越厉害。鱼眼镜头换一种映射，让大角度的光线也能落进有限的传感器：最常用的等距模型 \(r=f\theta\)，还有立体投影 \(r=2f\tan(\theta/2)\)、等立体角 \(r=2f\sin(\theta/2)\)、正交 \(r=f\sin\theta\)。Kannala–Brandt 通用模型在等距模型上再加一个角度的多项式：\(r=f\,\theta_d\)，\(\theta_d=\theta(1+k_1\theta^2+k_2\theta^4+k_3\theta^6+k_4\theta^8)\)，OpenCV 的 fisheye 模块用的就是这个。

    A pinhole obeys \(r=f\tan\theta\) (\(\theta\) is the angle between the ray and the optical axis). As \(\theta\to90^\circ\), \(r\to\infty\): a pinhole camera can never reach a 180° field of view, and the closer it gets to the limit the more the image is stretched. Fisheye lenses use a different mapping so that large-angle rays still land on a finite sensor. The most common is the equidistant model \(r=f\theta\); others are stereographic \(r=2f\tan(\theta/2)\), equisolid \(r=2f\sin(\theta/2)\) and orthographic \(r=f\sin\theta\). The Kannala–Brandt generic model adds an angular polynomial to the equidistant model: \(r=f\,\theta_d\) with \(\theta_d=\theta(1+k_1\theta^2+k_2\theta^4+k_3\theta^6+k_4\theta^8)\); OpenCV's fisheye module uses it.

    鱼眼图像不能先"去畸变"成针孔图再用：180° 的视场投到一个有限的平面上是不可能的，只能裁掉边缘，或者投到球面/立方体贴图上。实用的做法是直接在单位球上的射线里工作（11.1 节的对极几何可以写成射线的形式），或把鱼眼切成几块虚拟针孔相机。

    A fisheye image cannot simply be "undistorted" into a pinhole image: a 180° field cannot land on a finite plane, so you either crop the edges or project onto a sphere or cube map. The practical alternative is to work with rays on the unit sphere directly (the epipolar geometry of Lesson 11.1 can be written with rays) or to cut the fisheye into several virtual pinhole cameras.

    5再看数学The math

    提示：把鼠标悬停在公式中带底色的符号上，FIG 1 中对应的元素会被高亮。Tip: hover the highlighted symbols in the formulas; the matching element in FIG 1 lights up.

    内参矩阵与像素 ↔ 归一化坐标The intrinsic matrix and the pixel ↔ normalized maps

    $$ K=\begin{bmatrix}\hl{f}{\cv{f_x}} & \hl{s}{\cx{s}} & \hl{c}{\cw{c_x}}\\ 0 & \hl{f}{\cv{f_y}} & \hl{c}{\cw{c_y}}\\ 0&0&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad \begin{aligned}u&=f_x\,x+s\,y+c_x\\ v&=f_y\,y+c_y\end{aligned}\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad \begin{aligned}y&=(v-c_y)/f_y\\ x&=(u-c_x-s\,y)/f_x\end{aligned} $$

    \(\tilde{\mathbf u}=K\tilde{\mathbf x}_n\)，其中 \(\tilde{\mathbf x}_n=(x,y,1)^\top\) 是归一化坐标，\(\tilde{\mathbf u}=(u,v,1)^\top\)。K 是上三角矩阵，逆矩阵也是：\(K^{-1}=\begin{bmatrix}1/f_x & -s/(f_xf_y) & (sc_y-c_xf_y)/(f_xf_y)\\ 0&1/f_y&-c_y/f_y\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}\)。所以像素 \(\to\) 射线也是线性的：\(\mathbf d\propto K^{-1}\tilde{\mathbf u}\)，射线上深度为 \(Z\) 的点是 \(\mathbf X_c=Z\,K^{-1}\tilde{\mathbf u}\)（\(Z\) 是沿光轴的深度，不是到相机中心的距离，见 FIG 6）。

    5 个自由度。K 只有 5 个自由度：\(f_x,f_y,s,c_x,c_y\)，右下角固定为 1，因为 \(\tilde{\mathbf u}\) 是齐次坐标，整个矩阵乘一个常数不改变像素。为什么最后一行是 \((0,0,1)\)？因为相机坐标系的 z 轴就是光轴，齐次像素的第三个分量只是深度，不能混入 \(x,y\)。为什么左下的 \(K_{21}\) 也是 0？因为相机的 x 轴取得与传感器的像素行平行；剩下的任何转动都算进外参 \(R\)（这也是 RQ 分解唯一的原因）。

    \(\tilde{\mathbf u}=K\tilde{\mathbf x}_n\) with \(\tilde{\mathbf x}_n=(x,y,1)^\top\) the normalized coordinates and \(\tilde{\mathbf u}=(u,v,1)^\top\). K is upper triangular, and so is its inverse: \(K^{-1}=\begin{bmatrix}1/f_x & -s/(f_xf_y) & (sc_y-c_xf_y)/(f_xf_y)\\ 0&1/f_y&-c_y/f_y\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}\). So pixel \(\to\) ray is linear as well: the ray direction is \(\mathbf d\propto K^{-1}\tilde{\mathbf u}\), and the point at depth \(Z\) on it is \(\mathbf X_c=Z\,K^{-1}\tilde{\mathbf u}\) (\(Z\) is the depth along the optical axis, not the distance to the camera center; see FIG 6).

    Five degrees of freedom. K has just five: \(f_x,f_y,s,c_x,c_y\). The bottom-right 1 is fixed because \(\tilde{\mathbf u}\) is homogeneous and scaling the whole matrix changes no pixel. Why is the last row \((0,0,1)\)? The camera z-axis is the optical axis, so the third homogeneous pixel component is just the depth and must not mix in \(x,y\). Why is \(K_{21}\) zero too? The camera x-axis is chosen parallel to the sensor rows; any remaining rotation goes into the extrinsics \(R\) (which is also why the RQ decomposition is unique).

    外参、相机位姿与相机中心Extrinsics, camera pose and camera center

    用 5.1、5.3 节的记号，\({}^{a}T_{b}\) 把 \(b\) 系里的坐标变到 \(a\) 系（\(\mathbf X_a={}^{a}T_{b}\mathbf X_b\)），相继变换按 1.4 节"从右往左"复合。相机位姿 \({}^{w}T_{c}\) 和外参 \({}^{c}T_{w}\) 互为逆：

    In the notation of Lessons 5.1 and 5.3, \({}^{a}T_{b}\) converts coordinates in frame \(b\) to frame \(a\) (\(\mathbf X_a={}^{a}T_{b}\mathbf X_b\)), and successive transforms compose right to left as in Lesson 1.4. The camera pose \({}^{w}T_{c}\) and the extrinsics \({}^{c}T_{w}\) are each other's inverse:

    $$ {}^{c}T_{w}=\begin{bmatrix}R&\mathbf t\\ \mathbf 0^\top&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad {}^{w}T_{c}=\bigl({}^{c}T_{w}\bigr)^{-1}=\begin{bmatrix}R^\top&-R^\top\mathbf t\\ \mathbf 0^\top&1\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}{}^{w}R_{c}&C\\ \mathbf 0^\top&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad \boxed{C=-R^\top\mathbf t,\quad \mathbf t=-R\,C.} $$

    相机中心为什么是 \(-R^\top\mathbf t\)。相机中心是投影不到任何地方的那个点：\(P\tilde C=\mathbf 0\)，即 \([R|\mathbf t]\tilde C=R\,C+\mathbf t=\mathbf 0\)，所以 \(C=-R^{-1}\mathbf t=-R^\top\mathbf t\)（\(R\) 正交）。\(\mathbf t\) 本身是世界原点在相机系里的坐标：\(R\cdot\mathbf 0+\mathbf t\)。只有 \(R=I\) 时 \(\mathbf t=-C\)。另外，\(R\) 的行是相机三根轴在世界系里的方向（\({}^{w}R_c=R^\top\) 的列）；第三行就是光轴方向。

    装在机器人上的相机。外参往往是一串变换的复合：\({}^{c}T_{w}={}^{c}T_{b}\,{}^{b}T_{w}\)，其中 \({}^{b}T_{w}\) 是机器人的定位结果（或者臂的正运动学，7.3 节），\({}^{c}T_{b}\) 是相机相对机器人本体的安装位姿的逆，由手眼标定得到（10.4 节）。

    Why the camera center is \(-R^\top\mathbf t\). The camera center is the one point that projects nowhere: \(P\tilde C=\mathbf 0\), i.e. \([R|\mathbf t]\tilde C=R\,C+\mathbf t=\mathbf 0\), so \(C=-R^{-1}\mathbf t=-R^\top\mathbf t\) (\(R\) is orthogonal). The vector \(\mathbf t\) itself is the coordinates of the world origin in the camera frame: \(R\cdot\mathbf 0+\mathbf t\). Only for \(R=I\) is \(\mathbf t=-C\). Also, the rows of \(R\) are the directions of the camera's three axes in the world (the columns of \({}^{w}R_c=R^\top\)); the third row is the optical axis.

    A camera on a robot. The extrinsics are often a composition: \({}^{c}T_{w}={}^{c}T_{b}\,{}^{b}T_{w}\), where \({}^{b}T_{w}\) is the robot's localization result (or an arm's forward kinematics, Lesson 7.3) and \({}^{c}T_{b}\) is the inverse of the camera's mounting pose on the body, obtained by hand-eye calibration (Lesson 10.4).

    整条链与 11 个自由度The whole chain and its 11 degrees of freedom

    $$ \lambda\,\tilde{\mathbf u}=K\,[I\,|\,\mathbf 0]\;{}^{c}T_{w}\,\tilde{\mathbf X}_w=K\,[R\,|\,\mathbf t]\,\tilde{\mathbf X}_w=P\,\tilde{\mathbf X}_w,\qquad \lambda=Z_c. $$

    \(P\) 是 \(3\times4\)，12 个数，整体乘常数不改变投影，所以最多 11 个自由度。数一下参数：K 有 5 个，\(R\) 有 3 个（SO(3) 的维数），\(\mathbf t\) 有 3 个，恰好 \(5+3+3=11\)：P 里没有多余的东西，也没有缺少的东西。这也是一个测得的 P 能用"RQ 分解"唯一地拆回 K、R、t 的原因（Hartley–Zisserman 第 6 章）。带畸变的完整模型是 \(\tilde{\mathbf u}=K\,D\bigl(\pi(\,{}^{c}T_{w}\tilde{\mathbf X}_w)\bigr)\)，\(\pi\) 是除以深度，D 在 \(\pi\) 之后、K 之前。

    \(P\) is \(3\times4\), twelve numbers, and scaling it changes no projection, so there are at most 11 degrees of freedom. Count the parameters: 5 in K, 3 in \(R\) (the dimension of SO(3)), 3 in \(\mathbf t\), exactly \(5+3+3=11\): P has nothing redundant and nothing missing. That is also why a measured P can be split back uniquely into K, R and t by an "RQ decomposition" (Hartley–Zisserman, Ch. 6). The full model with distortion is \(\tilde{\mathbf u}=K\,D\bigl(\pi(\,{}^{c}T_{w}\tilde{\mathbf X}_w)\bigr)\), with \(\pi\) the division by depth; D sits after \(\pi\) and before K.

    改变分辨率：K 怎么变Changing the resolution: how K changes

    设新旧像素坐标之间是仿射关系 \(u'=a_xu-x_0\)，\(v'=a_yv-y_0\)（先缩放 \(a\) 倍，再把窗口左上角平移到原点）。写成齐次形式 \(\tilde{\mathbf u}'=A\tilde{\mathbf u}\)，那么 \(\tilde{\mathbf u}'=A\,K\,\tilde{\mathbf x}_n\)，也就是说新的内参是

    Let the new and old pixel coordinates be related by an affine map \(u'=a_xu-x_0\), \(v'=a_yv-y_0\) (scale by \(a\), then move the window's top-left corner to the origin). In homogeneous form \(\tilde{\mathbf u}'=A\tilde{\mathbf u}\), so \(\tilde{\mathbf u}'=A\,K\,\tilde{\mathbf x}_n\): the new intrinsics are

    $$ K'=\underbrace{\begin{bmatrix}a_x&0&-x_0\\0&a_y&-y_0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}}_{A}K=\begin{bmatrix}a_xf_x & a_xs & a_xc_x-x_0\\ 0&a_yf_y&a_yc_y-y_0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad D'=D. $$

    归一化坐标和 D 都在 K 之前，不受影响。一个细节：本课（和 FIG 1）把像素当作面积，坐标原点在左上角像素的角上，理想相机的主点是 \((w/2,h/2)\)，缩放时 \(c'=a\,c\)。OpenCV 把整数坐标放在像素中心，理想主点是 \(((w-1)/2,(h-1)/2)\)，缩放变成 \(c'=a(c+\tfrac12)-\tfrac12\)；差半个像素，对精细的三维重建不是小事。

    Normalized coordinates and D both come before K and are unaffected. One subtlety: this lesson (and FIG 1) treats pixels as areas with the origin at the corner of the top-left pixel, so an ideal camera has its principal point at \((w/2,h/2)\) and scaling gives \(c'=a\,c\). OpenCV puts integer coordinates at pixel centers, the ideal principal point is \(((w-1)/2,(h-1)/2)\), and scaling becomes \(c'=a(c+\tfrac12)-\tfrac12\): half a pixel apart, which matters for precise reconstruction.

    畸变模型、折返半径与逆The distortion model, the turning radius and its inverse

    $$ \begin{aligned} x_d&=x\,\rho(r^2)+2p_1xy+p_2(r^2+2x^2),\\ y_d&=y\,\rho(r^2)+p_1(r^2+2y^2)+2p_2xy,\end{aligned}\qquad \rho(r^2)=1+k_1r^2+k_2r^4+k_3r^6,\quad r^2=x^2+y^2. $$

    这是 Brown–Conrady 模型（OpenCV 的 plumb_bob；Conrady 1919 提出偏心畸变，Brown 1966 给出摄影测量中的公式）。OpenCV 把系数存成 \((k_1,k_2,p_1,p_2,k_3)\)，本课代码里的数组是 \([k_1,k_2,k_3,p_1,p_2]\)，换库时注意顺序。更复杂的变体包括有理模型（\(\rho\) 的分母再加 \(k_4r^2+k_5r^4+k_6r^6\)）、薄棱镜和倾斜传感器项。

    径向部分对 \(r\) 求导：\(\dfrac{\mathrm d r_d}{\mathrm d r}=1+3k_1r^2+5k_2r^4+7k_3r^6\)。例如只有 \(k_1<0\) 时，导数在 \(r^\ast=1/\sqrt{3|k_1|}\) 处为零，\(r_d\) 的最大值是 \(r_{d,\max}=\tfrac23r^\ast\)；FIG 3 的红点和 FIG 4 的虚线圆就是它（\(k_1=-0.3\) 时 \(r^\ast\approx1.05\)，已经超出常见相机的图像范围，\(k_1=-0.9\) 时 \(r^\ast\approx0.61\)，图像里就能碰到）。折返点之外 D 不是单射，任何"逆"都不是唯一的，常见的办法是把去畸变限制在标定覆盖的半径之内。

    Newton 迭代需要 \(J_D\)，它是 \(2\times2\) 矩阵，对 \(x_d\) 的两个分量求导即可得到。若需要对整个图像去畸变，则像上面的"视角"提示那样，预先算好查表。

    This is the Brown–Conrady model (OpenCV's plumb_bob; Conrady introduced decentering distortion in 1919 and Brown gave the photogrammetric formulas in 1966). OpenCV stores the coefficients as \((k_1,k_2,p_1,p_2,k_3)\) while the code in this page uses the array \([k_1,k_2,k_3,p_1,p_2]\): mind the order when switching libraries. More elaborate variants are the rational model (a denominator \(1+k_4r^2+k_5r^4+k_6r^6\) on \(\rho\)), thin-prism terms and tilted-sensor terms.

    Differentiate the radial part: \(\dfrac{\mathrm d r_d}{\mathrm d r}=1+3k_1r^2+5k_2r^4+7k_3r^6\). With only \(k_1<0\), for example, the derivative vanishes at \(r^\ast=1/\sqrt{3|k_1|}\) and the maximum of \(r_d\) is \(r_{d,\max}=\tfrac23r^\ast\); that is the red dot of FIG 3 and the dashed circle of FIG 4 (for \(k_1=-0.3\), \(r^\ast\approx1.05\), outside the image of a typical camera; for \(k_1=-0.9\), \(r^\ast\approx0.61\), which the image does reach). Beyond the turning point D is not injective and no "inverse" is unique; the usual remedy is to restrict undistortion to the radius the calibration covered.

    Newton's iteration needs \(J_D\), a \(2\times2\) matrix obtained by differentiating the two components with respect to \(x\) and \(y\). To undistort a whole image, precompute a lookup table as in the "view" box above.

    深度图 → 三维点A depth image to 3D points

    $$ \mathbf X_c=Z\,K^{-1}\tilde{\mathbf u}\ \ (\text{after undistorting }\mathbf u),\qquad \mathbf X_w={}^{w}T_{c}\,\mathbf X_c,\qquad \text{range }d\ \Rightarrow\ \mathbf X_c=\dfrac{d}{\lVert K^{-1}\tilde{\mathbf u}\rVert}\,K^{-1}\tilde{\mathbf u}. $$

    这就是 FIG 6 的全部算法：像素中心 \((i+\tfrac12,j+\tfrac12)\) 先去畸变，再乘 \(Z\,K^{-1}\)，最后用相机位姿搬到世界系。大多数深度相机的深度图存的是 \(Z\)（沿光轴），有些传感器（激光雷达、部分 ToF）给的是距离 \(d\)，两者差一个因子 \(\lVert K^{-1}\tilde{\mathbf u}\rVert\)，把它们混用会让视野边缘的墙往外鼓。

    That is the entire algorithm of FIG 6: undistort the pixel center \((i+\tfrac12,j+\tfrac12)\), multiply by \(Z\,K^{-1}\), then carry the point into the world with the camera pose. Most depth cameras store \(Z\) (along the optical axis); some sensors (lidar, certain ToF cameras) give the range \(d\), which differs by the factor \(\lVert K^{-1}\tilde{\mathbf u}\rVert\); mixing the two makes walls bulge outward near the edges of the field of view.

    视场角公式的推导Deriving the field-of-view formulas

    与光轴夹角 \(\alpha\)（在 \(xz\) 平面内）的光线有 \(x=\tan\alpha\)，落在 \(u=f_x\tan\alpha+c_x\)。图像右边缘 \(u=w\) 对应 \(\tan\alpha_R=(w-c_x)/f_x\)，左边缘 \(u=0\) 对应 \(\tan\alpha_L=c_x/f_x\)。总视场角 \(\theta_h=\alpha_L+\alpha_R=\arctan\frac{c_x}{f_x}+\arctan\frac{w-c_x}{f_x}\)，主点居中时 \(c_x=w/2\)，化为 \(2\arctan\frac{w}{2f_x}\)。垂直方向同理；对角线 \(2\arctan\frac{\sqrt{w^2+h^2}}{2f}\)（\(f_x=f_y=f\)，主点居中）。鱼眼的等距模型 \(r=f\theta\) 给出 \(\theta_h=w/f\)（弧度），所以 \(f\le w/\pi\) 时视场角 \(\ge180^\circ\)。

    A ray at angle \(\alpha\) from the optical axis (in the \(xz\) plane) has \(x=\tan\alpha\) and lands at \(u=f_x\tan\alpha+c_x\). The right image edge \(u=w\) corresponds to \(\tan\alpha_R=(w-c_x)/f_x\), the left edge \(u=0\) to \(\tan\alpha_L=c_x/f_x\). The total is \(\theta_h=\alpha_L+\alpha_R=\arctan\frac{c_x}{f_x}+\arctan\frac{w-c_x}{f_x}\), which for a centred principal point \(c_x=w/2\) reduces to \(2\arctan\frac{w}{2f_x}\). The vertical field is analogous, and the diagonal is \(2\arctan\frac{\sqrt{w^2+h^2}}{2f}\) (\(f_x=f_y=f\), centred principal point). The equidistant fisheye \(r=f\theta\) gives \(\theta_h=w/f\) radians, so \(f\le w/\pi\) yields a field of view \(\ge180^\circ\).

    6机器人中的它：坐标约定与深度图 → 点云In robotics: frame conventions and depth image → point cloud

    一台机器人上同时有好几套相机坐标系的说法。OpenCV 与 ROS 的光学系（optical frame）：x 向右，y 向下，z 向前，本课一直用它。OpenGL（以及 Blender、glTF 里的相机）：x 向右，y 向上，相机看向 \(-z\)，所以和 OpenCV 差一个绕 x 轴的 180° 旋转 \(\mathrm{diag}(1,-1,-1)\)（Unity 还是左手系）。ROS 本体系（REP-103）：x 向前，y 向左，z 向上，相机的本体系 camera_link 沿用它，而 camera_..._optical_frame 才是光学系。从本体系到光学系是一个固定的旋转，URDF 里常写成 rpy="-π/2 0 -π/2"。它们三个都是同一个相机，只是轴的取法不同，换算与相机在哪里无关。

    FIG 6 有两种模式。"坐标约定"把三套坐标系画在同一台相机上，并列出它们之间的固定旋转。"深度图 → 点云"是整课的综合检验：一个房间角（两面垂直的墙和地面）和一个球，从一台镜头带桶形畸变的深度相机看过去，得到 \(64\times48\) 的深度图。你来选择重建时用的 K，看点云是不是还贴着真墙（虚线是墙上的真实直线）。

    A robot carries several different camera-frame conventions at once. OpenCV and the ROS optical frame: x right, y down, z forward, the one this lesson has used throughout. OpenGL (and cameras in Blender and glTF): x right, y up, the camera looks along \(-z\), so it differs from OpenCV by a 180° rotation about x, \(\mathrm{diag}(1,-1,-1)\) (Unity, in addition, uses a left-handed frame). The ROS body convention (REP-103): x forward, y left, z up; a camera's body frame camera_link follows it and camera_..._optical_frame is the optical frame. Body to optical is a fixed rotation, often written in a URDF as rpy="-π/2 0 -π/2". All three describe the same camera with different axis choices, and converting between them does not depend on where the camera is.

    FIG 6 has two modes. "Frame conventions" draws the three frames on one camera and lists the fixed rotations between them. "Depth image → point cloud" is the integrated test of the whole lesson: a room corner (two perpendicular walls and a floor) and a sphere, seen by a depth camera whose lens has barrel distortion, give a \(64\times48\) depth image. You choose the K used for the reconstruction and watch whether the cloud still sits on the true walls (the dashed lines are true straight lines on them).

    真实系统里In real systems

    ROS 的 sensor_msgs/CameraInfo：K（3×3，行主序）是原始（带畸变）图像的内参；D 是畸变系数，长度取决于 distortion_model（plumb_bob 是 \((k_1,k_2,t_1,t_2,k_3)\)，rational_polynomial 是 8 个，另有 equidistant 鱼眼模型）；R 是双目校正用的旋转（单目为单位阵）；P 是 3×4 的校正后图像的投影矩阵，双目的右相机 \(P_{03}=-f_x'B\)。还有 binning 与 roi，用来描述缩小和裁剪过的图像，image_geometry 之类的工具据此调整 K，就是上面的 \(K'=AK\)。image_proc 用这些做校正；标定可用 camera_calibration 或 Kalibr。

    深度相机：Intel RealSense 在出厂时按每路数据流标定好了内参（rs2_intrinsics：宽高、ppx/ppy 即主点、fx/fy、畸变模型与 5 个系数）和各路流之间的外参，SDK 里的 rs2_deproject_pixel_to_point 就是 \(Z\,K^{-1}\tilde{\mathbf u}\)（再加上模型自己的去畸变），对齐彩色与深度用的是流间外参；D400 系列深度流常报告为无畸变（已校正）。特征匹配前先去畸变：ORB-SLAM 一类的系统对检测到的关键点去畸变（而不是整张图），再在归一化平面上做几何。AMR 与全景：鱼眼相机常用于环视和近场避障，需要 OpenCV fisheye 或 Kalibr 里的等距/全向模型，而不是 plumb_bob。坐标约定：REP-103 规定本体系与光学系，REP-105 规定 map → odom → base_link 的 TF 树；相机的光学系挂在 base_link 下面，这条外参链正是上面 \({}^{c}T_{w}={}^{c}T_{b}\,{}^{b}T_{w}\)。典型事故：驱动把流从 1280×720 切到 640×480，下游节点却仍然用 1280×720 的 K，整个点云的横向尺度错了 2 倍。

    ROS sensor_msgs/CameraInfo: K (3×3, row-major) holds the intrinsics of the raw (distorted) image; D holds the distortion coefficients, whose length depends on distortion_model (plumb_bob is \((k_1,k_2,t_1,t_2,k_3)\), rational_polynomial has 8, and there is also an equidistant fisheye model); R is the rectification rotation for stereo (identity for a single camera); P is the 3×4 projection matrix of the rectified image, with \(P_{03}=-f_x'B\) for the right camera of a stereo pair. binning and roi describe images that were reduced or cropped, and tools such as image_geometry use them to adapt K, which is the \(K'=AK\) above. image_proc rectifies with these; calibration is done with camera_calibration or Kalibr.

    Depth cameras: Intel RealSense factory-calibrates the intrinsics of every stream (rs2_intrinsics: width and height, ppx/ppy as the principal point, fx/fy, a distortion model and five coefficients) and the extrinsics between streams; the SDK's rs2_deproject_pixel_to_point is \(Z\,K^{-1}\tilde{\mathbf u}\) (plus the model's own undistortion), and colour-depth alignment uses the inter-stream extrinsics; the D400 depth stream is often reported as distortion-free (already rectified). Undistort before matching features: systems in the ORB-SLAM family undistort the detected keypoints (not the whole image) and then do geometry on the normalized plane. AMRs and surround view: fisheye cameras are common for surround view and near-field obstacle avoidance and need OpenCV's fisheye or the equidistant/omnidirectional models of Kalibr, not plumb_bob. Frame conventions: REP-103 fixes the body and optical frames and REP-105 the map → odom → base_link TF tree; the camera's optical frame hangs below base_link, and that chain of extrinsics is exactly the \({}^{c}T_{w}={}^{c}T_{b}\,{}^{b}T_{w}\) above. A classic accident: the driver switches the stream from 1280×720 to 640×480 while a downstream node keeps the 1280×720 K, and the whole cloud is wrong by a factor of 2 laterally.

    7常见误区Common pitfalls

    误区 1：把相机位姿和外参搞反Pitfall 1: confusing the camera pose with the extrinsics

    OpenCV 的 solvePnP 返回的 rvec, tvec、标定得到的每张图的 \([R|\mathbf t]\)，都是世界 \(\to\) 相机：\({}^{c}T_{w}\)。TF、URDF 里的相机"位置"和 SLAM 输出的轨迹是 \({}^{w}T_{c}\)。直接把 tvec 当作相机位置，在 \(R=I\) 时差一个符号，一般情况下既差符号又差一个旋转。直觉在这里失效的原因：\(\mathbf t\) 的单位是长度，看起来像"相机在哪儿"，其实是"世界原点在相机里的哪儿"。验证办法：用 FIG 2 的做法，把相机移到已知位置，检查 \(-R^\top\mathbf t\) 是不是它。

    The rvec, tvec returned by OpenCV's solvePnP and the per-image \([R|\mathbf t]\) from calibration are all world \(\to\) camera: \({}^{c}T_{w}\). The camera "position" in TF and URDF and the trajectory output by SLAM are \({}^{w}T_{c}\). Using tvec as the camera position is wrong by a sign when \(R=I\) and by a sign and a rotation in general. Intuition fails here because \(\mathbf t\) has units of length and looks like "where the camera is", while it is really "where the world origin is, seen from the camera". To check, do what FIG 2 does: put the camera at a known position and verify that \(-R^\top\mathbf t\) is it.

    误区 2：在像素里做畸变，或者弄错系数顺序Pitfall 2: applying distortion in pixels, or mixing up the coefficient order

    畸变系数是在归一化坐标里定义的：\(r\) 的单位是"焦距"，\(r=0.5\) 表示 26.6°，与分辨率无关。如果把 \(r\) 当成像素半径，同一组 \(k_1\) 在 640×480 和 1280×960 下就得到完全不同的弯曲。这也是为什么缩放图像时 D 不变而 K 要变。顺序同样致命：OpenCV 是 \((k_1,k_2,p_1,p_2,k_3)\)，把 \(p_1\) 当成 \(k_3\) 不会报错，只会让边缘几十个像素出错。

    The coefficients are defined on normalized coordinates: \(r\) is measured in units of "focal length", \(r=0.5\) means 26.6°, independent of resolution. Treat \(r\) as a pixel radius and the same \(k_1\) bends a 640×480 image completely differently from a 1280×960 one. That is also why D stays fixed while K changes when you rescale an image. The order is just as dangerous: OpenCV uses \((k_1,k_2,p_1,p_2,k_3)\), and reading \(p_1\) as \(k_3\) raises no error, it merely puts tens of pixels of error at the edges.

    误区 3：K 用错了分辨率，或者以为主点就是图像中心Pitfall 3: using K at the wrong resolution, or assuming the principal point is the image center

    K 属于一个具体的分辨率、裁剪窗口和像素 binning 设置。换了任何一个而没有按 \(K'=AK\) 更新，点云的尺度（缩放）或位置（裁剪）就是错的，而且错得"很整齐"，不容易被肉眼发现，FIG 1 的红色虚线展示了这一点。反方向的错误是假设 \(c=(w/2,h/2)\)、\(f_x=f_y\)：真实相机的主点常常偏离中心几到几十个像素，偏差对近处物体的影响 \(\Delta X=\Delta c\cdot Z/f\) 随深度线性增长。而且 OpenCV 与"面积型"约定差半个像素。

    K belongs to one specific resolution, crop window and pixel binning. Change any of them without updating K by \(K'=AK\) and the cloud's scale (resize) or position (crop) is wrong, and wrong in a very tidy way that the eye does not catch; the red dashed outline in FIG 1 shows this. The opposite mistake is to assume \(c=(w/2,h/2)\) and \(f_x=f_y\): the principal point of a real camera is often a few to a few tens of pixels off, and its effect \(\Delta X=\Delta c\cdot Z/f\) grows linearly with depth. And OpenCV's pixel-center convention differs from the "pixels as areas" convention by half a pixel.

    误区 4：以为畸变有闭式逆，或者在标定范围之外使用它Pitfall 4: assuming distortion has a closed-form inverse, or using it outside the calibrated region

    去畸变需要迭代（FIG 4），迭代次数太少（比如只迭代 1 次）会在图像边缘留下几个像素的残差。更隐蔽的是外推：标定板一般只覆盖图像的中间部分，边角没有数据时，高阶项 \(k_2,k_3\) 会拟合出一个在中间很准、边缘乱飞的多项式。一旦越过折返半径，去畸变在数学上就没有答案。对策：让标定板覆盖到图像的边角，限制 \(k_3\)（很多人固定 \(k_3=0\)），并检查 \(\mathrm d r_d/\mathrm d r>0\) 在整幅图像内成立。

    Undistortion needs iteration (FIG 4), and too few iterations (say one) leave a residual of several pixels at the image edge. A more insidious failure is extrapolation: the calibration board usually covers only the middle of the image, and with no data at the corners the higher-order terms \(k_2,k_3\) fit a polynomial that is accurate in the middle and wild at the edge. Past the turning radius undistortion has no mathematical answer at all. Remedies: let the calibration board reach the corners, constrain \(k_3\) (many people fix \(k_3=0\)), and check that \(\mathrm d r_d/\mathrm d r>0\) holds over the whole image.

    误区 5：OpenGL 约定喂给 OpenCV 函数，深度和距离不分Pitfall 5: feeding OpenGL conventions to OpenCV functions, and mixing depth with range

    渲染器、NeRF 数据集（如 Blender 导出的位姿）常用 OpenGL 相机系，用它去调 OpenCV 的投影函数会得到上下颠倒、前后不分的结果：先右乘 \(\mathrm{diag}(1,-1,-1)\) 再用。图像里 \(v\) 是向下的，矩阵画成 \(y\) 向上的图时也容易再翻一次。另外，深度图存的是沿光轴的 \(Z\) 还是到相机中心的距离，要看传感器的文档；混用的后果是边缘的点比中心的点多"鼓"出 \(1/\cos\) 倍。

    Renderers and NeRF datasets (for example poses exported from Blender) often use the OpenGL camera frame; feeding them to OpenCV's projection functions gives results that are upside-down and front-back confused. Right-multiply by \(\mathrm{diag}(1,-1,-1)\) first. In images \(v\) points down, and a figure that draws a matrix with \(y\) up makes it easy to flip once more. Also, whether a depth image stores \(Z\) along the optical axis or the distance to the camera center depends on the sensor's documentation; mixing them bulges the points near the edges by a factor \(1/\cos\) relative to those at the center.

    1. \(K=\begin{bmatrix}800&0&320\\0&800&240\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}\)，相机系中的点 \(\mathbf X_c=(0.3,\,-0.15,\,2)\)（单位 m）落在哪个像素？1. \(K=\begin{bmatrix}800&0&320\\0&800&240\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}\) and the camera-frame point is \(\mathbf X_c=(0.3,\,-0.15,\,2)\) (metres). Which pixel does it land on?
    先除以深度：\(x=0.3/2=0.15\)，\(y=-0.15/2=-0.075\)。再乘 K：\(u=800\cdot0.15+320=440\)，\(v=800\cdot(-0.075)+240=180\)。\(y<0\) 在光轴上方（y 向下），所以 \(v\) 比 \(c_y\) 小。B 忘了除以深度（\(800\cdot0.3+320=560\)）；C 的符号全反了；D 的 \(v\) 符号反了。Divide by depth first: \(x=0.3/2=0.15\), \(y=-0.15/2=-0.075\). Then apply K: \(u=800\cdot0.15+320=440\), \(v=800\cdot(-0.075)+240=180\). \(y<0\) is above the optical axis (y points down), so \(v\) is smaller than \(c_y\). B forgot the division by depth (\(800\cdot0.3+320=560\)); C flips both signs; D has the sign of \(v\) wrong.
    2. 外参 \(R=\begin{bmatrix}0&-1&0\\1&0&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}\)，\(\mathbf t=(1,2,0)\)（世界 \(\to\) 相机）。相机中心在世界系的哪里？2. The extrinsics (world \(\to\) camera) are \(R=\begin{bmatrix}0&-1&0\\1&0&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}\), \(\mathbf t=(1,2,0)\). Where is the camera center in the world?
    \(C=-R^\top\mathbf t\)。\(R^\top=\begin{bmatrix}0&1&0\\-1&0&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}\)，\(R^\top\mathbf t=(2,-1,0)\)，取负得 \((-2,1,0)\)。验算：\(RC+\mathbf t=(0\cdot(-2)-1\cdot1,\ 1\cdot(-2),\,0)+(1,2,0)=(0,0,0)\)。A 是 \(-\mathbf t\)（只在 \(R=I\) 时对），B 是 \(\mathbf t\) 本身，D 是 \(-R\mathbf t\)（用了 \(R\) 而不是 \(R^\top\)）。\(C=-R^\top\mathbf t\). \(R^\top=\begin{bmatrix}0&1&0\\-1&0&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}\), \(R^\top\mathbf t=(2,-1,0)\), negated: \((-2,1,0)\). Check: \(RC+\mathbf t=(0\cdot(-2)-1\cdot1,\ 1\cdot(-2),\,0)+(1,2,0)=(0,0,0)\). A is \(-\mathbf t\) (right only when \(R=I\)), B is \(\mathbf t\) itself, D is \(-R\mathbf t\) (using \(R\) rather than \(R^\top\)).
    3. 640×480 图像的内参 \(f_x=f_y=500\)，\(c=(320,240)\)。取出正中间 320×240 的窗口（左上角在 \((160,120)\)，不缩放）。新的内参是？3. A 640×480 image has \(f_x=f_y=500\), \(c=(320,240)\). You cut out the central 320×240 window (top-left corner at \((160,120)\), no scaling). What are the new intrinsics?
    裁剪只平移像素坐标：\(c'=c-(x_0,y_0)=(160,120)\)，焦距（像素/单位斜率）不变，视场角缩小了，因为同一个 \(f\) 现在只覆盖更少的像素。A 是把图像缩小一半（那样视场角不变）；C 忘了更新主点；D 没有道理。Cropping only translates pixel coordinates: \(c'=c-(x_0,y_0)=(160,120)\), while the focal length (pixels per unit slope) is unchanged and the field of view shrinks because the same \(f\) now spans fewer pixels. A is what you would get by halving the image size (then the field of view stays); C forgot to update the principal point; D makes no sense.
    4. 只有 \(k_1=-0.2\)，\(f=500\)，归一化坐标 \((0.6,\,0)\) 的点，它的水平像素偏移 \(u-c_x\) 是多少？4. With only \(k_1=-0.2\) and \(f=500\), what is the horizontal pixel offset \(u-c_x\) of the point at normalized coordinates \((0.6,\,0)\)?
    \(r^2=0.36\)，\(\rho=1-0.2\cdot0.36=0.928\)，\(x_d=0.6\cdot0.928=0.5568\)，\(u-c_x=500\cdot0.5568\approx278\)。\(k_1<0\) 是桶形：点被拉向中心。C 是 \(k_1>0\)（枕形）的结果。畸变在归一化坐标里作用，再乘 \(f\)。\(r^2=0.36\), \(\rho=1-0.2\cdot0.36=0.928\), \(x_d=0.6\cdot0.928=0.5568\), so \(u-c_x=500\cdot0.5568\approx278\). \(k_1<0\) is barrel: the point is pulled toward the center. C is what \(k_1>0\) (pincushion) would give. Distortion acts on normalized coordinates, and \(f\) multiplies afterwards.
    5. 一个渲染器（OpenGL 相机系：x 右，y 上，看向 \(-z\)）给出相机系坐标 \((1,\,2,\,-5)\)。同一点在 OpenCV 相机系里是？5. A renderer (OpenGL camera frame: x right, y up, looking along \(-z\)) gives the camera-frame coordinates \((1,\,2,\,-5)\). What are the coordinates of the same point in the OpenCV camera frame?
    OpenCV 的 y 向下、z 向前，OpenGL 的 y 向上、z 向后，两者差 \(\mathrm{diag}(1,-1,-1)\)：\((1,2,-5)\mapsto(1,-2,5)\)。结果 \(z=5>0\)，点在相机前方，符合预期（在 OpenGL 里在前方的点 \(z<0\)）。A 只翻了 z，C 把 x 也翻了，D 什么也没做。OpenCV has y down and z forward, OpenGL has y up and z backward, so they differ by \(\mathrm{diag}(1,-1,-1)\): \((1,2,-5)\mapsto(1,-2,5)\). The result has \(z=5>0\), in front of the camera as it should be (in OpenGL a point in front has \(z<0\)). A flipped only z, C also flipped x, and D did nothing.
