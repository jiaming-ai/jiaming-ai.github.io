点云与 ICP 配准Point Clouds & ICP

激光雷达和深度相机交给机器人的不是图像，而是一堆三维点。要把两次扫描拼起来、算出机器人走了多远、判断物体摆在哪里，都要回答同一个问题：哪一个刚体运动能让这堆点和那堆点重合？如果知道点与点的对应关系，答案是一次 SVD 的闭式解（Kabsch）；不知道时，就先用最近邻去猜对应，再求解，再猜，如此反复，这就是 ICP。本节从深度图反投影、体素滤波和法向量出发，交互地演示闭式解与反射陷阱，ICP 的收敛盆与局部极小，点到面 ICP 为什么更快，走廊里为什么会"退化"，最后用一个 2D 激光里程计看漂移是怎样出现的。 LiDARs and depth cameras hand the robot not an image but a pile of 3-D points. Stitching two scans, measuring how far the robot moved, and deciding where an object sits all ask the same question: which rigid motion makes this pile of points land on that one? With known point-to-point correspondences the answer is a closed form from one SVD (Kabsch); without them, guess the correspondences by nearest neighbors, solve, guess again, and repeat. That is ICP. We start from depth-image back-projection, voxel filtering and normals, then play with the closed form and its reflection trap, ICP's basin of attraction and local minima, why point-to-plane ICP converges faster, why corridors are degenerate, and finally watch drift appear in a 2-D LiDAR odometry.

    1为什么机器人需要它Why robots need this

    一台激光雷达每秒吐出几十万个三维点，深度相机每帧给出几万个。它们没有"这是桌子、那是墙"的标签，只是一堆带噪声的坐标。机器人用这堆点做三件最基本的事，而它们都归结为配准（registration）：找一个刚体运动 \(T=(R,\mathbf t)\in\SE\)，使一堆点 \(P\)（源）经过 \(T\) 之后与另一堆点 \(Q\)（目标）重合。

    • 里程计与建图：机器人向前走了一步，前后两帧扫描大部分重叠。把后一帧挪到前一帧上所需的刚体运动，就是这一步的位移；把每一帧按各自的位姿拼起来就是地图（LOAM、KISS-ICP、FAST-LIO 都是这个思路）。
    • 物体定位与位姿精修：物体的 CAD 点云（模型）和深度相机看到的局部点云对齐，就得到物体位姿。网络或 PnP 给出一个粗估计，ICP 把它精修到毫米级，然后才交给抓取规划（9.3 节）。
    • 标定与多机位拼接：两个传感器同时看同一个场景，让两堆点重合，就得到它们之间的外参。

    难点在于对应关系是未知的：两次扫描打到的不是同一批点。如果对应已知，问题有闭式解，一次 SVD 搞定（1.12 节）；对应未知时，问题变成非凸的，得靠迭代，而迭代只能保证走到局部最优。本节的主线：点云长什么样 → 对应已知时的闭式解 → ICP → 点到面 ICP → 什么时候会错（局部极小、退化）→ 机器人里怎么用。

    A LiDAR emits hundreds of thousands of 3-D points per second and a depth camera tens of thousands per frame. They come with no "this is a table, that is a wall" labels, just noisy coordinates. A robot does three basic things with them, all of which are registration: find a rigid motion \(T=(R,\mathbf t)\in\SE\) that, applied to one point set \(P\) (the source), makes it coincide with another, \(Q\) (the target).

    • Odometry and mapping: the robot moved one step, and consecutive scans largely overlap. The rigid motion that carries the new scan onto the old one is the step's displacement; placing every scan at its own pose gives the map (LOAM, KISS-ICP and FAST-LIO all follow this idea).
    • Object localization and pose refinement: align the object's CAD point cloud (the model) with the partial cloud a depth camera sees and you have the object pose. A network or PnP gives a rough estimate, ICP refines it to millimetres, and only then is it handed to grasp planning (Lesson 9.3).
    • Calibration and multi-view stitching: two sensors look at the same scene; make their point sets coincide and you have the extrinsics between them.

    The difficulty is that correspondences are unknown: two scans never hit the same points. With correspondences, the problem has a closed form, one SVD (Lesson 1.12); without them it is non-convex, needs iteration, and iteration can only promise a local optimum. The thread of this lesson: what a point cloud is → the closed form with known matches → ICP → point-to-plane ICP → how it goes wrong (local minima, degeneracy) → how robots use it.

    2直觉：点云是一个集合，配准是找一个刚体运动Intuition: a cloud is a set, registration finds a rigid motion

    点云从哪来Where a point cloud comes from

    深度相机的每个像素 \((u,v)\) 存一个深度 \(z\)。用 10.3 节的内参 \(K\) 反投影，再用相机位姿 \({}^{w}T_{c}\) 搬到世界系：

    Every pixel \((u,v)\) of a depth camera stores a depth \(z\). Back-project it with the intrinsics \(K\) of Lesson 10.3, then move it to the world frame with the camera pose \({}^{w}T_{c}\):

    $$ \mathbf p^{c}=z\,K^{-1}\begin{bmatrix}u\\ v\\ 1\end{bmatrix},\qquad \mathbf p^{w}={}^{w}T_{c}\,\mathbf p^{c}. $$

    这样得到的是有序点云（organized）：保留 \((u,v)\) 网格，邻居就是相邻像素，免费。LiDAR 的多圈扫描、多帧拼接、滤波之后，就只剩一个无序点云（unorganized）：一个没有顺序、没有连接关系的集合，找邻居要靠空间索引。它还有几个脾气：深度噪声随距离增大（结构光和 ToF 相机的噪声大致与 \(z^2\) 成正比，下图取 \(\sigma(z)=\sigma\,(z/2)^2\)）；密度随距离变稀；只有朝向传感器的一面被看见。预处理有三件标配：体素滤波（每个体素只留一个质心，让密度均匀、点数变少）、最近邻搜索（k-d 树，平均每次查询 \(O(\log N)\)，暴力是 \(O(N)\)）、法向量（取点的 \(k\) 个近邻，做协方差的特征分解，最小特征值对应的特征向量就是法向，\(\lambda_{\min}/\sum\lambda\) 称为曲率）。

    This gives an organized cloud: it keeps the \((u,v)\) grid, so neighbours are neighbouring pixels, for free. After a LiDAR's multiple sweeps, merging and filtering, only an unorganized cloud remains: a set with no order and no connectivity, where finding neighbours takes a spatial index. Clouds have a few more habits: depth noise grows with range (structured-light and ToF noise scales roughly with \(z^2\); the figure uses \(\sigma(z)=\sigma\,(z/2)^2\)); density thins out with distance; and only the side facing the sensor is seen. Three standard preprocessing steps: the voxel filter (keep one centroid per voxel, which evens out density and cuts the count), nearest-neighbor search (a k-d tree answers a query in \(O(\log N)\) on average, brute force takes \(O(N)\)), and normals (take a point's \(k\) neighbours, eigen-decompose their covariance; the eigenvector of the smallest eigenvalue is the normal, and \(\lambda_{\min}/\sum\lambda\) is called the curvature).

    已知对应关系：闭式解Known correspondences: the closed form

    先解简单的版本：已知 \(\mathbf a_i\) 对应 \(\mathbf b_i\)，求使 \(\sum_i\|R\mathbf a_i+\mathbf t-\mathbf b_i\|^2\) 最小的 \(R\in\SO,\mathbf t\)。四步：

    1. 去中心：\(\bar{\mathbf a},\bar{\mathbf b}\) 是两组点的质心，\(\mathbf a_i'=\mathbf a_i-\bar{\mathbf a}\)，\(\mathbf b_i'=\mathbf b_i-\bar{\mathbf b}\)。平移从问题里消失了。
    2. 互协方差矩阵 \(H=\sum_i\mathbf a_i'\mathbf b_i'^{\top}\)（\(3\times3\)）：它记录了"\(\mathbf a\) 的每个方向在多大程度上跟 \(\mathbf b\) 的每个方向同向变化"。
    3. SVD：\(H=U\Sigma V^{\top}\)，旋转 \(\hat R=V\,\mathrm{diag}(1,1,\det(VU^{\top}))\,U^{\top}\)。
    4. 平移 \(\hat{\mathbf t}=\bar{\mathbf b}-\hat R\bar{\mathbf a}\)。

    第 3 步里的 \(\det\) 修正是整节最容易被忽略的一行：SVD 给出的 \(VU^{\top}\) 可能是反射（行列式 \(-1\)），而反射并不是刚体运动。下图让你把 \(B\) 做成镜像，亲眼看到"没有这一行会发生什么"。如果两组点之间还差一个尺度（比如单目 SLAM 的地图和激光地图，或者两个尺度未知的重建），Umeyama（1991）把闭式解推广到相似变换 \(s,R,\mathbf t\)。

    Start with the easy version: given that \(\mathbf a_i\) matches \(\mathbf b_i\), find the \(R\in\SO,\mathbf t\) minimizing \(\sum_i\|R\mathbf a_i+\mathbf t-\mathbf b_i\|^2\). Four steps:

    1. Center: \(\bar{\mathbf a},\bar{\mathbf b}\) are the centroids, \(\mathbf a_i'=\mathbf a_i-\bar{\mathbf a}\), \(\mathbf b_i'=\mathbf b_i-\bar{\mathbf b}\). Translation drops out of the problem.
    2. The cross-covariance \(H=\sum_i\mathbf a_i'\mathbf b_i'^{\top}\) (\(3\times3\)): it records how much each direction of \(\mathbf a\) varies together with each direction of \(\mathbf b\).
    3. SVD: \(H=U\Sigma V^{\top}\), and the rotation is \(\hat R=V\,\mathrm{diag}(1,1,\det(VU^{\top}))\,U^{\top}\).
    4. Translation \(\hat{\mathbf t}=\bar{\mathbf b}-\hat R\bar{\mathbf a}\).

    The \(\det\) correction in step 3 is the easiest line of the whole lesson to forget: the SVD's \(VU^{\top}\) can be a reflection (determinant \(-1\)), and a reflection is not a rigid motion. The figure below lets you mirror \(B\) and see exactly what happens without that line. If the two sets also differ by a scale (a monocular SLAM map against a LiDAR map, say, or two reconstructions of unknown scale), Umeyama (1991) extends the closed form to a similarity \(s,R,\mathbf t\).

    核心洞见Key insight

    对应关系已知时，刚体配准是一个有闭式解的最小二乘：质心对齐，再对互协方差做一次 SVD。对应关系未知时，一个自然的想法是"先猜对应，再用闭式解"：猜对应用最近邻，每猜一次就用闭式解把源点云挪近一点，再猜。这就是 ICP，下一节让它动起来。

    With known correspondences, rigid registration is a least-squares problem with a closed form: align the centroids, then one SVD of the cross-covariance. Without correspondences the natural idea is "guess them, then use the closed form": guess with nearest neighbors, use the closed form to move the source a bit closer, and guess again. That is ICP, and the next section sets it in motion.

    3动手玩：ICP 实验室Play: the ICP lab

    ICP（Iterative Closest Point，Besl 与 McKay、Chen 与 Medioni，1992）每一轮做四件事：① 变换——用当前估计 \(T\) 把源点云挪到目标附近；② 匹配——每个源点在目标里找最近邻，得到对应关系；③ 剔除（可选）——丢掉最差的一部分配对；④ 求解——用 Kabsch 闭式解求出使这些配对距离平方和最小的新 \(T\)。重复，直到 \(T\) 不再变化。

    下面的图里，琥珀色是源点云（带一个已知的初始错位），紫色是目标。物体是不对称的"玩具"（椭球身体、头、侧瘤、竖尾鳍，没有任何对称性，所以正确的对齐是唯一的）。青色线是当前的对应关系。每点一次单步，就执行一轮；运行会一直迭代到收敛。右上的曲线是 RMSE（匹配距离的均方根）和相对真值的旋转误差，右下是收敛盆：对每个初始角度（绕所选轴）各跑一次 ICP，画出最终的旋转误差。绿色带内（<5°）算对齐成功。

    ICP (Iterative Closest Point; Besl and McKay, and Chen and Medioni, 1992) does four things per round: ① transform the source by the current estimate \(T\); ② match every source point to its nearest neighbor in the target; ③ reject (optional) the worst fraction of pairs; ④ solve the Kabsch closed form for the \(T\) that minimizes the squared distances of the remaining pairs. Repeat until \(T\) stops changing.

The object is an asymmetric "toy" (ellipsoid body, head, side lump, vertical tail fin; no symmetry, so the right alignment is unique). Cyan lines are the current correspondences. Step performs one round, Run iterates to convergence. The upper plot on the right shows the RMSE (root-mean-square matching distance) and the rotation error against the ground truth; the lower plot is the basin of attraction: one ICP run for each initial angle (about the chosen axis), plotting the final rotation error. Inside the green band (<5°) the alignment counts as a success.

    一个想法，两种看法One idea, two views

    交替最小化：ICP 在 \(E(T,c)=\sum_i\|T\mathbf p_i-\mathbf q_{c(i)}\|^2\) 上交替优化：固定 \(T\) 时，最近邻 \(c\) 使 \(E\) 最小；固定 \(c\) 时，Kabsch 使 \(E\) 最小。每一步都不会让 \(E\) 变大，所以 \(E\) 单调下降（FIG 3 的 RMSE 曲线不会上升），但只能收敛到局部极小。这和 \(k\)-means 的"分配—更新"是同一种结构。距离场上的下降：把 \(f(T)=\sum_i d(T\mathbf p_i,Q)^2\)（\(d\) 是到点集的距离）当作要最小化的函数，最近邻对应相当于只用 \(d^2\) 的一阶信息，对每个点把目标曲面近似成"一个点"，点到面 ICP（下一节）则把曲面近似成"一个平面"，信息更多，收敛更快。

    Alternating minimization: ICP alternates on \(E(T,c)=\sum_i\|T\mathbf p_i-\mathbf q_{c(i)}\|^2\): for fixed \(T\) the nearest neighbor minimizes \(E\) over \(c\); for fixed \(c\) Kabsch minimizes \(E\) over \(T\). Neither step can increase \(E\), so \(E\) decreases monotonically (FIG 3's RMSE curve never rises) but only reaches a local minimum. It has the same structure as \(k\)-means' assign-then-update. Descent on a distance field: treat \(f(T)=\sum_i d(T\mathbf p_i,Q)^2\) (\(d\) the distance to the set) as the function to minimize; nearest-neighbor matching approximates the target surface at each point by "a point", while point-to-plane ICP (next section) approximates it by "a plane". More information, faster convergence.

    4第二、第三视角：点到面与退化Second and third views: point-to-plane and degeneracy

    点到面：沿着曲面滑动是不受罚的Point-to-plane: sliding along the surface is free

    点到点 ICP 要求源点落在它匹配到的那个目标点上。可是目标点只是曲面的一次随机采样：源点沿着曲面滑到旁边一点，同样是对的，却要受罚。在大片平坦的地面或墙面上，这使点到点 ICP 的收敛变得很慢，每轮只挪一点点（线性收敛，比例很接近 1）。点到面（Chen 与 Medioni 的原始形式）只罚源点到目标点切平面的垂直距离：

    Point-to-point ICP demands that a source point land on its matched target point. But that target point is just a random sample of the surface: the source point sliding to a neighbouring spot on the surface is equally right, yet it is penalized. On large flat floors and walls this makes point-to-point ICP crawl, moving only a little per round (linear convergence with a ratio close to 1). Point-to-plane (the original Chen and Medioni form) only penalizes the distance from the source point to the target point's tangent plane:

    $$ E_{\text{pl}}(T)=\sum_i\Big(\mathbf n_i^{\top}\big(T\mathbf p_i-\mathbf q_i\big)\Big)^2 . $$

    它对 \(T\) 不再有 Kabsch 那样的闭式解，但可以用 Gauss-Newton（6.6 节）：在当前估计处线性化，解一个 \(6\times6\) 的法方程，再用指数映射（5.6 节）更新。推导放在"再看数学"一节。下面的图在三个场景里同时跑点到点和点到面，用真值位姿误差 \(\|\mathrm{Log}(T\,T_\ast^{-1})\|\)（旋转以弧度、平移以米，合成一个数）比较。同时算出 \(6\times6\) 矩阵 \(\sum_iJ_i^{\top}J_i\) 的特征值：它们衡量每一个运动方向被数据约束得有多紧。

    场景：墙角（地面 + 后墙 + 侧墙，三个互相垂直的面，什么运动都被约束）；隧道（地面 + 两侧墙，三个面都平行于 \(x\) 轴）；隧道 + 立柱（隧道中间放一个方盒，盒子的前后面把 \(x\) 方向约束住了）。

    It no longer has a Kabsch-style closed form in \(T\), but Gauss-Newton (Lesson 6.6) works: linearize at the current estimate, solve a \(6\times6\) normal equation, and update with the exponential map (Lesson 5.6). The derivation is in "The math". The figure runs point-to-point and point-to-plane side by side on three scenes and compares them with the ground-truth pose error \(\|\mathrm{Log}(T\,T_\ast^{-1})\|\) (rotation in radians and translation in metres, combined in one number). It also computes the eigenvalues of the \(6\times6\) matrix \(\sum_iJ_i^{\top}J_i\): they measure how tightly the data constrains each direction of motion.

    The scenes: a corner (floor + back wall + side wall, three mutually perpendicular planes, everything is constrained); a tunnel (floor + two side walls, all three planes parallel to the \(x\) axis); a tunnel + pillar (a box in the middle, whose front and back faces constrain the \(x\) direction).

    三种说法，一件事Three phrasings, one fact

    几何：平面只约束沿它法向的运动，隧道的三个面的法向都垂直于 \(x\) 轴，所以沿 \(x\) 的平移没有任何面能"感觉到"。代数：\(\sum J_i^{\top}J_i\) 在该方向上特征值为 0，法方程奇异。统计：估计的协方差大致是 \(\sigma^2(\sum J_i^{\top}J_i)^{-1}\)，特征值趋于 0 意味着该方向上的方差趋于无穷。三种说法都解释了同一件事：走廊里的激光里程计在沿走廊方向上会漂。

    Geometry: a plane only constrains motion along its normal; the three tunnel planes all have normals perpendicular to the \(x\) axis, so no plane can "feel" a translation along \(x\). Algebra: \(\sum J_i^{\top}J_i\) has eigenvalue 0 in that direction and the normal equation is singular. Statistics: the estimate's covariance is roughly \(\sigma^2(\sum J_i^{\top}J_i)^{-1}\), and an eigenvalue tending to 0 means the variance in that direction tends to infinity. All three explain the same fact: a LiDAR odometry in a corridor drifts along the corridor.

    问题与 Kabsch 推导The problem and the Kabsch derivation

    已知配对 \((\mathbf a_i,\mathbf b_i)\)，求

    Given pairs \((\mathbf a_i,\mathbf b_i)\), find

    $$ \min_{R\in\SO,\ \mathbf t\in\R^3}\ \sum_{i=1}^{n}\big\|R\mathbf a_i+\mathbf t-\mathbf b_i\big\|^2 . $$

    第一步，平移解耦。对 \(\mathbf t\) 求导令其为 0，得 \(\mathbf t=\bar{\mathbf b}-R\bar{\mathbf a}\)。代回去，代价只剩旋转：\(\sum_i\|R\mathbf a_i'-\mathbf b_i'\|^2\)，其中 \(\mathbf a_i'=\mathbf a_i-\hl{cent}{\bar{\mathbf a}}\)。第二步，展开。\(R\) 是正交矩阵，\(\|R\mathbf a'\|=\|\mathbf a'\|\)，所以

    Step 1, translation decouples. Setting the derivative in \(\mathbf t\) to zero gives \(\mathbf t=\bar{\mathbf b}-R\bar{\mathbf a}\). Substituting back leaves a cost in the rotation alone, \(\sum_i\|R\mathbf a_i'-\mathbf b_i'\|^2\), with \(\mathbf a_i'=\mathbf a_i-\hl{cent}{\bar{\mathbf a}}\). Step 2, expand. \(R\) is orthogonal, \(\|R\mathbf a'\|=\|\mathbf a'\|\), so

    $$ \sum_i\|R\mathbf a_i'-\mathbf b_i'\|^2=\sum_i\|\mathbf a_i'\|^2+\sum_i\|\mathbf b_i'\|^2-2\,\operatorname{tr}\!\big(R\,\hl{H}{H}\big),\qquad H=\sum_i\mathbf a_i'\mathbf b_i'^{\top}=U\Sigma V^{\top}. $$

    只需最大化 \(\operatorname{tr}(RH)=\operatorname{tr}(R\,U\Sigma V^{\top})=\operatorname{tr}(\Sigma\,V^{\top}RU)=\sum_k\sigma_k Z_{kk}\)，其中 \(Z=V^{\top}RU\) 是正交矩阵，对角元不超过 1。所以最大值在 \(Z=I\)，即 \(R=VU^{\top}\) 取到（这正是经典的正交 Procrustes 问题的解，用的是 1.12 节的 SVD）。但我们要求 \(\det R=+1\)，而 \(\det Z=\det(V^{\top}RU)=\det R\cdot\det V\det U\)：如果 \(\det(VU^{\top})=-1\)，\(Z=I\) 不被允许，此时最好的行列式为 \(-1\) 的正交矩阵是 \(Z=\mathrm{diag}(1,1,-1)\)（牺牲最小的奇异值 \(\sigma_3\)）。合起来：

    We need only maximize \(\operatorname{tr}(RH)=\operatorname{tr}(R\,U\Sigma V^{\top})=\operatorname{tr}(\Sigma\,V^{\top}RU)=\sum_k\sigma_k Z_{kk}\), where \(Z=V^{\top}RU\) is orthogonal and its diagonal entries cannot exceed 1. The maximum is at \(Z=I\), i.e. \(R=VU^{\top}\) (this is exactly the classic orthogonal Procrustes solution, built on the SVD of Lesson 1.12). But we need \(\det R=+1\), and \(\det Z=\det R\cdot\det V\det U\): if \(\det(VU^{\top})=-1\), then \(Z=I\) is not allowed, and the best orthogonal matrix with determinant \(-1\) is \(Z=\mathrm{diag}(1,1,-1)\) (sacrificing the smallest singular value \(\sigma_3\)). Together:

    $$ \hl{R}{\hat R}=V\,\mathrm{diag}\big(1,1,\det(VU^{\top})\big)\,U^{\top},\qquad \hat{\mathbf t}=\bar{\mathbf b}-\hat R\bar{\mathbf a},\qquad \text{(Umeyama)}\ \ \hat s=\frac{\operatorname{tr}(\Sigma D)}{\sum_i\|\mathbf a_i'\|^2},\ \ \hat{\mathbf t}=\bar{\mathbf b}-\hat s\hat R\bar{\mathbf a}, $$

    其中 \(D=\mathrm{diag}(1,1,\det(VU^{\top}))\)。唯一性要求 \(\operatorname{rank}H\ge2\)：如果所有点共线（\(\operatorname{rank}H=1\)），绕这条线的旋转是自由的；共面时 \(\sigma_3=0\)，行列式修正选的符号不改变代价，两个候选都是最优，只有 \(\det=+1\) 的是刚体。这是 Kabsch（1976）、Horn（1987，四元数）、Arun 等（1987）、Umeyama（1991）彼此等价的结果。Barfoot 的《State Estimation for Robotics》把它放在李群的框架里，并推广到带权重和不确定性的情形。

    with \(D=\mathrm{diag}(1,1,\det(VU^{\top}))\). Uniqueness needs \(\operatorname{rank}H\ge2\): if all points are collinear (\(\operatorname{rank}H=1\)), rotation about the line is free; for coplanar points \(\sigma_3=0\), the determinant fix does not change the cost, both candidates are optimal, and only the one with \(\det=+1\) is a rigid motion. These are the equivalent results of Kabsch (1976), Horn (1987, with quaternions), Arun et al. (1987) and Umeyama (1991). Barfoot's State Estimation for Robotics places it in the Lie-group framework and extends it to weights and uncertainty.

    ICP 是交替最小化，所以单调但只是局部ICP is alternating minimization: monotone but only local

    $$ \underbrace{\hl{pairs}{c_{k+1}(i)=\arg\min_j\|T_k\mathbf p_i-\mathbf q_j\|}}_{\text{match}},\qquad \underbrace{T_{k+1}=\arg\min_{T\in\SE}\sum_i\|T\mathbf p_i-\mathbf q_{c_{k+1}(i)}\|^2}_{\text{Kabsch}},\qquad E(T_{k+1},c_{k+1})\le E(T_k,c_{k+1})\le E(T_k,c_k). $$

    每一步都不增加 \(E\)，\(E\ge0\)，所以单调收敛（Besl 与 McKay 的定理）。但极限只是 \(E\) 的局部极小，且收敛是线性的。剔除最差的一部分配对（修剪 ICP，Chetverikov 等）时，等于对"哪些配对被保留"也做交替最小化，单调性依然成立，而且是处理部分重叠的标准做法。更一般地，用鲁棒核 \(\rho\)（Huber、Cauchy、Geman–McClure）代替平方，经 IRLS 变成每个配对的权重 \(w_i=\rho'(r_i)/r_i\)，Kabsch 变成带权版本；KISS-ICP 里用的是自适应的距离阈值加核。

    No step increases \(E\), and \(E\ge0\), so it converges monotonically (the Besl and McKay theorem). But the limit is only a local minimum of \(E\), and convergence is linear. Rejecting the worst fraction of pairs (trimmed ICP, Chetverikov et al.) is alternating minimization over "which pairs are kept" as well, so monotonicity still holds, and it is the standard answer to partial overlap. More generally, replacing the square with a robust kernel \(\rho\) (Huber, Cauchy, Geman-McClure) turns, via IRLS, into per-pair weights \(w_i=\rho'(r_i)/r_i\) and a weighted Kabsch; KISS-ICP uses an adaptive distance threshold with a kernel.

    点到面的线性化The point-to-plane linearization

    用左扰动 \(T\leftarrow\mathrm{Exp}(\boldsymbol\xi)\,T\)，\(\boldsymbol\xi=(\boldsymbol\omega,\mathbf v)\)（角速度分量在前，与全课程一致）。记 \(\mathbf p_i'=T\mathbf p_i\)。一阶近似下 \(\mathrm{Exp}(\boldsymbol\xi)\mathbf p'\approx\mathbf p'+\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf p'+\mathbf v\)，于是残差 \(r_i=\mathbf n_i^{\top}(\mathbf p_i'-\mathbf q_i)\) 变为

    Use a left perturbation \(T\leftarrow\mathrm{Exp}(\boldsymbol\xi)\,T\) with \(\boldsymbol\xi=(\boldsymbol\omega,\mathbf v)\) (angular part first, as everywhere in the course). Write \(\mathbf p_i'=T\mathbf p_i\). To first order \(\mathrm{Exp}(\boldsymbol\xi)\mathbf p'\approx\mathbf p'+\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf p'+\mathbf v\), so the residual \(r_i=\mathbf n_i^{\top}(\mathbf p_i'-\mathbf q_i)\) becomes

    $$ r_i(\boldsymbol\xi)\approx r_i+\mathbf n_i^{\top}(\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf p_i')+\mathbf n_i^{\top}\mathbf v=r_i+\underbrace{\big[(\mathbf p_i'\times\mathbf n_i)^{\top}\ \ \mathbf n_i^{\top}\big]}_{J_i\ (1\times6)}\boldsymbol\xi ,\qquad \Big(\sum_i J_i^{\top}J_i\Big)\hl{null}{\boldsymbol\xi}=-\sum_i J_i^{\top}r_i . $$

    这里用到了标量三重积 \(\mathbf n\cdot(\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf p)=\boldsymbol\omega\cdot(\mathbf p\times\mathbf n)\)（1.8 节）。解出 \(\boldsymbol\xi\) 后用 \(T\leftarrow\mathrm{Exp}(\boldsymbol\xi)T\) 更新（用 M.expSE3），这就是流形上的 Gauss-Newton（6.10 节）：每步都在李代数里解线性问题，更新后仍然是合法的 SE(3) 元素，不需要再把 \(R\) 正交化。数值检验：把 \(J_i\) 与 \(r_i(\boldsymbol\xi)=\mathbf n^{\top}(\mathrm{Exp}(\boldsymbol\xi)\mathbf p'-\mathbf q)\) 的中心差分比较，最大偏差在 \(10^{-10}\) 量级。小角度近似 \(R\approx I+[\boldsymbol\omega]_\times\) 是同一阶近似，只是更新不严格落在 SO(3) 上，需要事后投影。为什么更快？点到点把每个目标点当作一个孤立的点，等价于用一个"曲率无穷大"的曲面去逼近；点到面利用了曲面的一阶信息（法向），相当于对距离函数做 Gauss-Newton，实际收敛快得多（Pottmann 等 2006 对收敛性做了几何分析）。

    This uses the scalar triple product \(\mathbf n\cdot(\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf p)=\boldsymbol\omega\cdot(\mathbf p\times\mathbf n)\) (Lesson 1.8). After solving for \(\boldsymbol\xi\), update with \(T\leftarrow\mathrm{Exp}(\boldsymbol\xi)T\) (M.expSE3); this is Gauss-Newton on a manifold (Lesson 6.10): each step solves a linear problem in the Lie algebra, and the updated pose is again a valid SE(3) element, with no re-orthogonalization of \(R\). A numerical check, comparing \(J_i\) with central differences of \(r_i(\boldsymbol\xi)=\mathbf n^{\top}(\mathrm{Exp}(\boldsymbol\xi)\mathbf p'-\mathbf q)\), agrees to about \(10^{-10}\). The small-angle form \(R\approx I+[\boldsymbol\omega]_\times\) is the same first-order approximation, but the update does not lie exactly on SO(3) and must be projected afterwards. Why is it faster? Point-to-point treats each target point as an isolated point, which is like approximating the surface by one of infinite curvature; point-to-plane uses the surface's first-order information (the normal), which amounts to Gauss-Newton on the distance function and converges much faster in practice (Pottmann et al., 2006, analyze the convergence geometrically).

    退化与不确定性Degeneracy and uncertainty

    记 \(A=\sum_iJ_i^{\top}J_i\)。一个平面（法向 \(\mathbf n\)）只对满足 \(\mathbf n\cdot(\mathbf v+\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf p)\neq0\) 的运动有"感觉"。\(A\) 的零空间就是所有这些约束都满足的运动，即让所有残差保持不变的螺旋运动：隧道里的 \(\boldsymbol\xi=(\mathbf 0,\hat{\mathbf x})\)，大平地上的"在平面内平移 + 绕法向转动"，圆柱面上的"沿轴平移 + 绕轴转动"，球面上的"绕球心转动"。Gelfand 等（2003）用 \(A\) 的特征分解来选"几何稳定"的采样点；Zhang、Kaess 与 Singh（2016）把它用到激光里程计，检测到退化方向后不再在这个方向上更新状态。注意 \(A\) 里旋转和平移的量纲不同，特征值要在统一尺度后才有意义（FIG 4 把旋转部分乘以场景尺度 \(s\)，并除以点数）。

    在高斯噪声假设下，配准的协方差近似为 \(\sigma^2A^{-1}\)，可当作位姿图（Part IV）里这条边的信息矩阵 \(A/\sigma^2\)。但这只是一个下界：它没有考虑对应关系本身的错误和采样间距，实际协方差更大（Censi 2007 给出了更精细的闭式估计）。把 ICP 的 RMSE 当作"置信度"是不安全的（见误区）。

    Write \(A=\sum_iJ_i^{\top}J_i\). A plane with normal \(\mathbf n\) only "feels" motions with \(\mathbf n\cdot(\mathbf v+\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf p)\neq0\). The null space of \(A\) consists of motions satisfying all these constraints, i.e. the screw motions that leave every residual unchanged: \(\boldsymbol\xi=(\mathbf 0,\hat{\mathbf x})\) in a tunnel, "in-plane translation + rotation about the normal" on a big flat floor, "translation along the axis + rotation about the axis" on a cylinder, "rotation about the center" on a sphere. Gelfand et al. (2003) used the eigendecomposition of \(A\) to pick "geometrically stable" sample points; Zhang, Kaess and Singh (2016) brought it to LiDAR odometry, detecting degenerate directions and not updating the state along them. Note that rotation and translation entries of \(A\) have different units, so eigenvalues only mean something after a common scaling (FIG 4 multiplies the rotation part by the scene scale \(s\) and divides by the point count).

    Under Gaussian noise the covariance of the registration is approximately \(\sigma^2A^{-1}\), which can serve as the information matrix \(A/\sigma^2\) of this edge in a pose graph (Part IV). But this is only a lower bound: it ignores errors in the correspondences and the sampling spacing, and the real covariance is larger (Censi 2007 gives a finer closed-form estimate). Treating ICP's RMSE as a "confidence" is unsafe (see the pitfalls).

    All of these answer the same question: how do you make local, noisy, possibly degenerate alignment reliable? The answer is a combination: a good initial guess (IMU, odometry, constant-velocity model), a local map instead of only the previous frame, detection of degenerate directions, robust kernels, and finally feeding every relative pose as an edge into a pose graph (Part IV) with loop closures to pull accumulated drift back.

    7常见误区Common pitfalls

    误区 1：以为 ICP 总能把两堆点对齐Pitfall 1: believing ICP always aligns two clouds

    ICP 是局部方法：它只能收敛到离初值最近的局部极小。FIG 3 的收敛盆（绕 z 轴）只有大约 ±60° 宽，初始角度再大就会收敛到"头尾对调"的对齐，而且迭代过程光滑、RMSE 也在单调下降，看不出任何异常。机器人里的补救办法是好的初值（里程计、IMU、恒速模型、上一帧的位姿）、多起点或全局配准（特征匹配 + RANSAC）、以及用独立于 ICP 的指标检查结果（重叠率、内点比例、与里程计是否一致）。

    ICP is a local method: it can only converge to the nearest local minimum from its start. FIG 3's basin (about the z axis) is only about ±60° wide; beyond that it converges to the "head-and-tail swapped" alignment, smoothly, with the RMSE decreasing monotonically, and nothing looks wrong. The remedies on a robot are a good initial guess (odometry, IMU, a constant-velocity model, the previous pose), multiple starts or global registration (feature matching + RANSAC), and checking the result with a metric independent of ICP (overlap ratio, inlier fraction, consistency with odometry).

    误区 2：忽略部分重叠、动态物体和杂点Pitfall 2: ignoring partial overlap, dynamic objects and clutter

    最近邻匹配总会给每个源点一个目标点，哪怕那个源点在目标里根本没有对应物（另一半房间、路过的行人和车）。这些错误配对用平方距离放大，把解拉偏（FIG 3："部分重叠 + 杂点"下保留 100% 时旋转误差十几度）。用修剪 ICP、距离阈值、鲁棒核，或先去掉动态物体（语义分割、多帧一致性）。注意修剪比例是对重叠率的假设：设得比真实重叠率高，坏点仍然混进来；设得太低，约束又不够。

    Nearest-neighbor matching always hands every source point a target point, even when that source point has no counterpart in the target (the other half of the room, a pedestrian or a car passing by). These wrong pairs are amplified by the squared distance and pull the solution off (FIG 3: with "partial + clutter" and 100% kept, the rotation error is over ten degrees). Use trimmed ICP, distance gates, robust kernels, or remove dynamic objects first (semantic segmentation, multi-frame consistency). Note that the trim fraction is an assumption about the overlap: set it above the true overlap and bad points still get in; set it too low and the constraints run short.

    误区 3：残差很小就等于位姿对Pitfall 3: a small residual means a correct pose

    FIG 5 里"恒等初值"的 ICP 在走廊里每一帧都收敛得很好：两条直线的重合度极高，RMSE 很小，位姿却完全错了。残差只度量"点离曲面有多远"，不度量"沿着曲面滑了多远"。对称的物体（圆柱、球、有对称性的零件）、平坦的地面、长走廊、隧道、空旷的大厅都有这个问题。要检查 \(A=\sum J^{\top}J\) 的最小特征值（FIG 4），或者让另一个传感器（IMU、轮速、相机）提供这个方向上的信息。

    In FIG 5 the "identity start" ICP converges beautifully on every corridor frame: the two lines overlap almost perfectly, the RMSE is tiny, and the pose is completely wrong. The residual measures how far a point is from the surface, not how far along the surface it has slid. Symmetric objects (cylinders, spheres, symmetric parts), flat floors, long corridors, tunnels and empty halls all have this problem. Check the smallest eigenvalue of \(A=\sum J^{\top}J\) (FIG 4), or let another sensor (IMU, wheel encoders, a camera) supply the information in that direction.

    误区 4：忘记 det 修正，也忘记 ICP 的"小细节"Pitfall 4: forgetting the det fix, and ICP's other small details

    没有 \(\det\) 修正的 Kabsch 在数据含噪声、近似平面或近似对称时会偶尔输出反射（FIG 2：镜像时 \(\det=-1\)，残差看起来更小）。其它常犯的细节：单位（毫米和米混用，距离阈值差三个数量级；点到面的旋转与平移量纲不同，不做尺度的法方程病态）；降采样过狠（体素大于薄结构，如电线、杆子，点云里直接消失，且体素边界会产生混叠）；多对一（最近邻会让很多源点挤到同一个目标点上，这是边缘和稀疏处的典型现象，用双向检验或修剪缓解）；法向量（PCA 法向在棱边会被抹平，噪声大时要增大 \(k\)；FIG 1）。

    Kabsch without the \(\det\) fix will occasionally output a reflection when the data are noisy, nearly planar or nearly symmetric (FIG 2: mirrored data give \(\det=-1\) and an even smaller-looking residual). Other details that bite: units (mixing millimetres and metres puts distance gates three orders of magnitude off; point-to-plane rotation and translation have different units, so an unscaled normal equation is ill-conditioned); over-aggressive downsampling (a voxel larger than a thin structure such as a wire or a pole makes it vanish, and voxel boundaries cause aliasing); many-to-one matches (nearest neighbors crowd many source points onto one target point, typical at edges and sparse areas, eased by mutual-consistency checks or trimming); normals (PCA normals are smeared at edges, and noise calls for a larger \(k\); FIG 1).

    误区 5：把 ICP 的 RMSE 当成不确定性Pitfall 5: using ICP's RMSE as the uncertainty

    RMSE 小，是因为同一批点被反复挪近；它里面混着采样间距、对应错误和模型误差，不是位姿误差的方差。把它直接当成位姿图边的权重，会让这些边过于自信，一两条带有偏差的激光边就能拖歪整条轨迹。至少要用 \(A/\sigma^2\)（并按经验放大），或者用 Censi 一类的方法估协方差，并且在检测到退化方向时把这个方向的信息置零，而不是让 \(A^{-1}\) 爆炸。

    The RMSE is small because the same points keep being moved closer; it mixes sampling spacing, wrong matches and model error, and it is not the variance of the pose error. Using it directly as a pose-graph edge weight makes those edges overconfident, and one or two biased LiDAR edges can bend the whole trajectory. At the least use \(A/\sigma^2\) (inflated empirically), or estimate the covariance with something like Censi's method, and when a degenerate direction is detected zero out the information in that direction rather than letting \(A^{-1}\) blow up.

    8小练习Check yourself

    1. Kabsch 算法里 \(H=U\Sigma V^{\top}\)，算出 \(VU^{\top}\) 的行列式为 \(-1\)。正确的做法是？1. In Kabsch, \(H=U\Sigma V^{\top}\) and \(\det(VU^{\top})=-1\). What should you do?
    \(VU^{\top}\) 是反射，残差也许更小，但不是刚体运动。在 \(\det=+1\) 的约束下，最好的解是牺牲代价最小的方向：把最小奇异值对应的奇异向量翻号。\(-VU^{\top}\)（三维里）行列式 \(+1\) 但不是最优：它翻了所有三个方向。\(VU^{\top}\) is a reflection; its residual may be smaller but it is not a rigid motion. Under \(\det=+1\) the best solution sacrifices the cheapest direction: flip the singular vector of the smallest singular value. \(-VU^{\top}\) (in 3-D) has determinant \(+1\) but is not optimal: it flips all three directions.
    2. 关于 ICP 的收敛，哪一句是对的？2. Which statement about ICP's convergence is correct?
    交替最小化的两步都不增加代价，下有界所以收敛（Besl 与 McKay），但没有全局保证（FIG 3 的收敛盆）。带噪声的两次采样 RMSE 到不了 0；而退化场景里错误的位姿也能有很小的 RMSE（FIG 5）。Both steps of the alternating minimization are non-increasing and the cost is bounded below, so it converges (Besl and McKay), without any global guarantee (FIG 3's basin). Two noisy samples never reach RMSE 0, and in degenerate scenes a wrong pose can have a tiny RMSE (FIG 5).
    3. 已知两组点的质心 \(\bar{\mathbf a}=(1,2,3)\)，\(\bar{\mathbf b}=(4,6,8)\)，估计的旋转是绕 \(z\) 轴转 \(90^\circ\)（\((x,y,z)\mapsto(-y,x,z)\)）。\(\hat{\mathbf t}\) 是多少？3. The two sets' centroids are \(\bar{\mathbf a}=(1,2,3)\) and \(\bar{\mathbf b}=(4,6,8)\), and the estimated rotation is \(90^\circ\) about \(z\) (\((x,y,z)\mapsto(-y,x,z)\)). What is \(\hat{\mathbf t}\)?
    \(\hat{\mathbf t}=\bar{\mathbf b}-\hat R\bar{\mathbf a}\)，\(\hat R\bar{\mathbf a}=(-2,1,3)\)，所以 \(\hat{\mathbf t}=(4+2,\ 6-1,\ 8-3)=(6,5,5)\)。直接用 \(\bar{\mathbf b}-\bar{\mathbf a}=(3,4,5)\) 是忘了旋转。\(\hat{\mathbf t}=\bar{\mathbf b}-\hat R\bar{\mathbf a}\) and \(\hat R\bar{\mathbf a}=(-2,1,3)\), so \(\hat{\mathbf t}=(4+2,\ 6-1,\ 8-3)=(6,5,5)\). Using \(\bar{\mathbf b}-\bar{\mathbf a}=(3,4,5)\) forgets the rotation.
    4. 一台激光雷达在一条笔直、墙面光滑的长隧道里做点到面 ICP，隧道里没有别的东西。哪个量最可能出问题？4. A LiDAR does point-to-plane ICP in a long straight tunnel with smooth walls and nothing else in it. Which quantity is most likely to go wrong?
    三个面的法向都垂直于隧道轴，沿轴的平移不会改变任何点到面的距离，\(\sum J^{\top}J\) 在该方向上奇异。高度被地面约束，左右偏移被两侧墙约束，偏航由两侧墙的位置随纵向的变化约束。All three surfaces have normals perpendicular to the tunnel axis, so a translation along the axis changes no point-to-plane distance and \(\sum J^{\top}J\) is singular in that direction. Height is constrained by the floor, the lateral offset by the side walls, and yaw by how the wall positions change along the tunnel.
    5. ICP 每轮要为 \(N\) 个源点各找一个最近邻，目标有 \(M\) 个点。用 k-d 树，相对暴力搜索，平均每轮的匹配代价大约从什么变成什么？5. Each ICP round finds a nearest neighbor for each of \(N\) source points against \(M\) target points. Using a k-d tree instead of brute force, the average matching cost per round goes roughly from what to what?
    k-d 树建一次 \(O(M\log M)\)，每次最近邻查询平均 \(O(\log M)\)（最坏情况和高维下会退化）。FIG 1 的读数里能看到每次查询访问的节点数远小于 \(M\)。真实系统还会用增量 k-d 树或体素哈希，避免每帧重建。The k-d tree is built once in \(O(M\log M)\), and each nearest-neighbor query costs \(O(\log M)\) on average (it degrades in the worst case and in high dimensions). FIG 1's readout shows the number of nodes a query visits, far below \(M\). Real systems use incremental k-d trees or voxel hashing to avoid rebuilding per frame.
