逆强化学习与偏好学习Inverse RL & Preference Learning

强化学习假设奖励是现成的，可真实机器人的奖励几乎总要人来写，而且很难写对。本节换一个方向：让机器人从人的示教或人的偏好里学出奖励。先看清这个逆问题为什么天生有歧义（缩放、势函数整形、一整片都能解释示教的奖励），再在地形格子世界里实时训练最大熵 IRL，看它为什么只能学到示教“暴露”出来的那部分；然后把 GAIL 理解为“判别器当奖励”的占用度量匹配，并用一张新地图检验：学到的奖励能迁移，克隆来的策略不能。最后转向偏好：Bradley–Terry 模型从“A 比 B 好”里拟合奖励模型，RLHF 和 DPO 用它微调策略，KL 约束与奖励模型被“过度优化”的代价都可以在图中亲手调出来。 Reinforcement learning assumes the reward is given, but on a real robot a person has to write it, and writing it right is hard. This lesson turns the problem around: the robot learns the reward from human demonstrations or human preferences. We first see why this inverse problem is ambiguous by nature (scaling, potential shaping, a whole region of rewards that explain the demonstrations), then train maximum-entropy IRL live on a terrain grid and see why it can only learn what the demonstrations reveal. GAIL then appears as occupancy matching with a discriminator as the reward, and a new map puts both to the test: a learned reward transfers, a cloned policy does not. Finally we turn to preferences: the Bradley–Terry model fits a reward model to "A is better than B", RLHF and DPO use it to fine-tune a policy, and the price of the KL constraint and of over-optimizing a reward model can be dialled in by hand.

    1为什么机器人需要它Why robots need this

    20.1 节到 20.5 节的所有算法都从一个奖励函数 \(r(s,a)\) 出发：值迭代、Q-learning、PPO、SAC 都只是在最大化它的期望回报。可是在真实任务里，奖励不是环境给的，而是工程师写的。一台越野小车该怎样在“走草地”“绕远路”“冒险穿过泥坑”之间权衡？一只机械臂把杯子递给人时，“动作自然”怎么写成公式？手写的奖励常常漏掉某个条件，强化学习又极其擅长钻这种空子：它会找到一种让数字变大、却完全不是你想要的行为（reward hacking）。

    另一方面，人往往做得出来或者评判得出来，只是说不清楚。司机会开，却说不出自己给泥坑的权重是多少；你分不清两段抓取动作各自得几分，却能马上说出哪一段更好。于是有两条路：

    • 逆强化学习（IRL）：给定专家的示教轨迹，反推一个奖励，使专家的行为在这个奖励下是（近似）最优的；然后用这个奖励在新环境里重新规划或训练策略。GAIL 是它的一个近亲：不显式输出奖励，而是用一个判别器（19.2 节）当奖励直接训练策略。
    • 偏好学习：让人比较两段行为“哪个更好”，用 Bradley–Terry 模型拟合一个奖励模型，再用强化学习（RLHF）或直接用偏好对（DPO）微调策略。今天的大语言模型就是这样对齐的，同样的工具也正在用于机器人策略。

    和行为克隆（22.1 节）相比，学奖励多绕了一步，换来的是“为什么”：奖励描述的是目标，而不是在某个地方该往哪走，所以地图变了、障碍物挪了，机器人可以用同一个奖励重新规划（FIG 4）。

    Every algorithm from Lesson 20.1 to Lesson 20.5 starts from a reward function \(r(s,a)\): value iteration, Q-learning, PPO and SAC all just maximize its expected return. In a real task, however, the reward is not handed over by the environment; an engineer writes it. How should an off-road robot trade "drive on grass" against "take the long way" against "risk crossing the mud"? When an arm hands a cup to a person, how do you write "moves naturally" as a formula? Hand-written rewards often miss a condition, and reinforcement learning is extremely good at exploiting exactly that: it finds a behaviour that makes the number large and is not at all what you wanted (reward hacking).

    On the other hand, people can often do the task or judge it while being unable to state it. A driver can drive but cannot tell you what weight they give to mud; you cannot score two grasping motions on an absolute scale, but you can say at once which one is better. That gives two routes:

    • Inverse reinforcement learning (IRL): given expert demonstrations, infer a reward under which the expert's behaviour is (nearly) optimal, then use that reward to re-plan or train a policy in new situations. GAIL is a close relative: it never outputs a reward explicitly, but trains the policy directly with a discriminator (Lesson 19.2) as the reward.
    • Preference learning: ask a person which of two behaviours is better, fit a reward model with the Bradley–Terry model, then fine-tune the policy with reinforcement learning (RLHF) or directly from the preference pairs (DPO). This is how today's large language models are aligned, and the same tools are being applied to robot policies.

    Compared with behaviour cloning (Lesson 22.1), learning a reward takes a detour and buys the "why": a reward describes the goal rather than which way to move in a given place, so when the map changes or an obstacle moves, the robot can re-plan with the same reward (FIG 4).

    2直觉：一个逆问题，很多个答案Intuition: one inverse problem, many answers

    正向的强化学习是“给定奖励，求最优行为”；逆强化学习是“给定行为，求奖励”。逆问题的通病是解不唯一。本节贯穿全篇的例子是一台越野小车：在 10×7 的格子地图上从左边的 S 开到右边的 G，每一步走到上下左右相邻的格子。地面有三种：铺装路面、草地、泥地。我们假设奖励是地形特征的线性组合，每走进一个格子 \(s'\) 得到

    Forward reinforcement learning is "given a reward, find the optimal behaviour"; inverse reinforcement learning is "given behaviour, find the reward". Inverse problems share a common disease: the answer is not unique. The running example of this lesson is an off-road robot on a 10×7 grid map, driving from S on the left to G on the right, one step at a time to a neighbouring cell. There are three kinds of ground: pavement, grass and mud. We assume the reward is linear in terrain features: every move into a cell \(s'\) earns

    $$ r_{\mathbf w}(s\to s')=\hl{wvec}{\mathbf w}^{\top}\boldsymbol\phi(s')=w_{\mathrm s}+w_{\mathrm g}\,\phi_{\mathrm g}(s')+w_{\mathrm m}\,\phi_{\mathrm m}(s'),\qquad R_{\mathbf w}(\tau)=\sum_t r_{\mathbf w}(s_t\to s_{t+1})=\mathbf w^{\top}\mathbf f(\tau) $$

    其中 \(\phi_{\mathrm g}(s')\) 在 \(s'\) 是草地时为 1、否则为 0，\(\phi_{\mathrm m}\) 对泥地同理，\(w_{\mathrm s}\) 是每走一步的奖励（负数就是时间代价）。\(\mathbf f(\tau)\) 是轨迹的特征计数：走了几步、踩了几格草、几格泥。到达 G 就结束（回报不打折，\(\gamma=1\)）。下图左边紫色是专家的路线：先往上一格，沿着草带绕过泥地，共 11 步、踩了 8 格草。右边是奖励权重的平面（横轴 \(w_{\mathrm g}\)，纵轴 \(w_{\mathrm m}\)，\(w_{\mathrm s}\) 固定为 −1），拖动蓝点改变奖励，左图的蓝色路线就是这个奖励下的最优路线（6.12 节的值迭代）。青色区域是所有“让专家路线成为最优”的奖励，也就是 IRL 的全部候选答案。

    Here \(\phi_{\mathrm g}(s')\) is 1 if \(s'\) is grass and 0 otherwise, \(\phi_{\mathrm m}\) likewise for mud, and \(w_{\mathrm s}\) is the reward for each step (negative: a cost of time). \(\mathbf f(\tau)\) are the trajectory's feature counts: how many steps, how many grass cells, how many mud cells. Reaching G ends the episode (no discounting, \(\gamma=1\)). In the figure, purple on the left is the expert's route: one cell up, then along the grass band around the mud, 11 steps with 8 grass cells. On the right is the plane of reward weights (\(w_{\mathrm g}\) across, \(w_{\mathrm m}\) up, \(w_{\mathrm s}\) fixed at −1). Drag the blue point to change the reward; the blue route on the left is the optimal route under it (value iteration from Lesson 6.12). The cyan region is every reward that makes the expert's route optimal: all of IRL's candidate answers.

    核心洞见Key insight

    IRL 的歧义有三层。(1) 缩放：把 \(\mathbf w\) 乘以任何正数，最优路线不变，所以图里干脆固定了 \(w_{\mathrm s}=-1\)。(2) 整形：给奖励加上 \(\Phi(s')-\Phi(s)\) 这样的势函数差（Ng、Harada 与 Russell，1999），任何从 S 到 G 的路线回报都只差同一个常数 \(\Phi(G)-\Phi(S)\)，排序完全不变。(3) 一整片：即便去掉前两种，示教也只能把奖励限制在一个区域里（青色楔形），而 \(\mathbf w=\mathbf 0\) 这种“什么都无所谓”的奖励让任何行为都是最优的，永远在候选集里。所以每一种实用的 IRL 算法都要额外加一条原则来挑出一个答案：最大间隔、最大熵、对抗训练……下一节的最大熵 IRL 就是其中最常用的一种。

    IRL's ambiguity has three layers. (1) Scale: multiply \(\mathbf w\) by any positive number and the optimal route does not change, which is why the figure fixes \(w_{\mathrm s}=-1\). (2) Shaping: add a potential difference \(\Phi(s')-\Phi(s)\) to the reward (Ng, Harada and Russell, 1999) and every route from S to G changes its return by the same constant \(\Phi(G)-\Phi(S)\), so the ranking is untouched. (3) A whole region: even after removing the first two, a demonstration only confines the reward to a region (the cyan wedge), and the "nothing matters" reward \(\mathbf w=\mathbf 0\), under which every behaviour is optimal, is always a candidate. Every practical IRL algorithm therefore adds a principle that picks one answer: maximum margin, maximum entropy, adversarial training… The maximum-entropy IRL of the next section is the most widely used of them.

    3动手：最大熵 IRLPlay: maximum-entropy IRL

    真实的示教不是一条完美路线，而是很多条有点随意的路线：有人多拐了一下，有人偶尔抄近路。Ziebart 等（2008）的最大熵 IRL 把这种随意性写进模型：专家按回报的指数挑路线，回报越高越常走，但每条路线都有可能，

    Real demonstrations are not one perfect route but many somewhat casual ones: one person takes an extra turn, another cuts a corner. The maximum-entropy IRL of Ziebart et al. (2008) builds that casualness into the model: the expert picks routes with probability exponential in their return, so better routes are more common but every route is possible,

    $$ p_{\mathbf w}(\tau)=\frac{\exp\big(\mathbf w^{\top}\mathbf f(\tau)\big)}{Z(\mathbf w)},\qquad \nabla_{\mathbf w}\,\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^{N}\log p_{\mathbf w}(\tau_i)=\hl{fD}{\bar{\mathbf f}_{\mathcal D}}-\hl{fw}{\mathbb E_{p_{\mathbf w}}[\mathbf f]} $$

    这是一个指数族分布（19.5 节的能量模型，能量就是 \(-R_{\mathbf w}\)），它的对数似然对 \(\mathbf w\) 是凹的，梯度只有一句话：示教的平均特征计数减去模型的期望特征计数。梯度为零时两者相等，这叫特征匹配：学到的模型平均走的步数、踩的草、踩的泥和示教一模一样。期望特征计数不用枚举所有路线，用两遍动态规划就能精确算出：一遍向后的“软”值迭代（max 换成 log-sum-exp），得到每一步的随机策略；一遍向前传播状态分布，数出每种特征的期望次数（第 7 节）。

    下图用 \(\mathbf w^{\star}=(-2,-0.3,-4)\)（步数、草、泥）按上式随机生成 \(N\) 条示教，然后从 \(\mathbf w=\mathbf 0\)（所有在 24 步内到得了 G 的路线等可能）开始梯度上升。注意这里 \(w_{\mathrm s}\) 也要学：最大熵模型里奖励的大小有意义，它决定专家有多“随意”。

    This is an exponential-family distribution (an energy-based model as in Lesson 19.5, with energy \(-R_{\mathbf w}\)); its log-likelihood is concave in \(\mathbf w\), and the gradient is one sentence: the demonstrations' mean feature counts minus the model's expected feature counts. At zero gradient they are equal, which is called feature matching: on average the learned model takes as many steps, crosses as much grass and as much mud as the demonstrations. The expected counts need no enumeration of routes; two dynamic-programming sweeps give them exactly: a backward "soft" value iteration (max replaced by log-sum-exp) yields a stochastic policy for every time step, and a forward pass of the state distribution counts the expected number of each feature (Section 7).

    The figure draws \(N\) demonstrations at random from the formula above with \(\mathbf w^{\star}=(-2,-0.3,-4)\) (step, grass, mud), then runs gradient ascent from \(\mathbf w=\mathbf 0\) (every route that reaches G within 24 steps equally likely). Note that \(w_{\mathrm s}\) is learned as well: in the maximum-entropy model the size of the reward matters, because it sets how "casual" the expert is.

    (1) The maximum-entropy principle squeezes FIG 1's wedge into a point: among all distributions that match the feature counts it picks the one with the highest entropy (Section 7 shows the two statements are equivalent), so the step and grass weights are pinned down, scale included; with 500 demonstrations and 300 or more iterations, \(w_{\mathrm s}\) and \(w_{\mathrm g}\) both land within ±0.05 of the truth. (2) But it can only learn what the demonstrations reveal. The default 20 demonstrations have a mud count of 0, and "the model expects zero mud" can only be achieved by \(w_{\mathrm m}\to-\infty\), so that weight never converges; pick a set that happens to contain one mud cell ("New demos") and it stops at a finite value. With the true \(w_{\mathrm m}=-4\) the expert steps into mud only 0.011 times per demonstration on average, so even with 500 demonstrations the mud weight takes a long time to creep towards −4. (3) After convergence the model's mean negative log-likelihood of these 20 demonstrations (about 8.5 nats) is even slightly lower than that of the true reward: it fits this particular sample, chance features included.

    4另一个视角：GAIL，判别器当奖励Another view: GAIL, the discriminator as the reward

    最大熵 IRL 的内循环要反复“在当前奖励下求最优策略”，这在格子世界里是两遍动态规划，在一台连续控制的机器人上就是一整轮强化学习。Ho 与 Ermon（2016）注意到，如果最终要的只是一个会模仿的策略，奖励只是中间产物，那么“IRL 之后再 RL”可以合并成一个问题：让策略的状态-动作占用度量 \(\rho_\pi(s,a)\) 和专家的 \(\rho_E(s,a)\) 一样。度量两个分布的差别，正是 19.2 节判别器的本事：

    The inner loop of maximum-entropy IRL repeatedly "solves for the optimal policy under the current reward": two dynamic-programming sweeps on a grid, a full round of reinforcement learning on a robot with continuous controls. Ho and Ermon (2016) observed that if all you want in the end is a policy that imitates, the reward is only an intermediate product, and "IRL, then RL" can be merged into one problem: make the policy's state-action occupancy measure \(\rho_\pi(s,a)\) equal to the expert's \(\rho_E(s,a)\). Measuring the difference between two distributions is exactly what the discriminator of Lesson 19.2 does:

    $$ \min_{\pi}\ \max_{D}\ \ \mathbb E_{\hl{rhoE}{\rho_E}}\big[\log D(s,a)\big]+\mathbb E_{\hl{rhopi}{\rho_\pi}}\big[\log\big(1-D(s,a)\big)\big]-\lambda H(\pi),\qquad r(s,a)=\log \hl{disc}{D(s,a)} $$

    判别器 \(D(s,a)\) 学“这个状态-动作对来自专家的概率”；策略用强化学习最大化一个由 \(D\) 给出的奖励，做得越像专家，得分越高。这里写的是 FIG 3 用的 \(\log D\)；原论文用的是 \(-\log(1-D)\)，两者的平衡点相同，区别见第 7 节。它就是 19.2 节的 GAN，只不过“生成器”是一个在环境里行动的策略，样本要靠执行策略得到，梯度不能穿过环境传回来，所以要用强化学习（原文用 TRPO）而不是反向传播来更新策略。

    下图在同一张地图上运行 GAIL。为了让图里没有采样噪声，占用度量是精确算出来的（真实的 GAIL 从执行策略得到的样本里估计），每次迭代：判别器（2→24→24→1 的 MLP，输入是格子坐标和移动方向，看不到地形，就像真实的 GAIL 只看原始状态）做 2 步 Adam；策略向当前奖励 \(\log D\) 下的熵正则最优策略（温度 \(\lambda=0.3\) 的软值迭代）迈半步。

    The discriminator \(D(s,a)\) learns "the probability that this state-action pair came from the expert"; the policy maximizes a reward given by \(D\) with reinforcement learning: the more expert-like, the higher the score. Written here is the \(\log D\) used in FIG 3; the original paper uses \(-\log(1-D)\), with the same equilibrium; Section 7 explains the difference. It is the GAN of Lesson 19.2, except that the "generator" is a policy acting in an environment. Its samples come from running the policy and gradients cannot flow back through the environment, so the policy is updated by reinforcement learning (TRPO in the original paper), not by backpropagation.

    The figure runs GAIL on the same map. To keep sampling noise out of the picture, the occupancy measures are computed exactly (real GAIL estimates them from rollouts). Each iteration: the discriminator (a 2→24→24→1 MLP whose input is the cell coordinates and the move direction; it does not see the terrain, just as real GAIL sees only raw states) takes 2 Adam steps; the policy takes half a step towards the entropy-regularized optimal policy under the current reward \(\log D\) (soft value iteration at temperature \(\lambda=0.3\)).

    一个目标，三种写法One objective, three ways to write it

    IRL 视角：GAIL 等于“先用某种正则化的 IRL 求奖励，再用 RL 求策略”，Ho 与 Ermon 证明了这个复合正好是占用度量匹配；选不同的奖励正则化，就得到不同的“距离”：线性奖励类对应特征匹配（Abbeel 与 Ng，2004，以及 FIG 2），GAN 判别器对应 JS 散度。GAN 视角：判别器的最优解 \(D^*=\rho_E/(\rho_E+\rho_\pi)\)（19.2 节），所以 \(\log D^*-\log(1-D^*)=\log\rho_E-\log\rho_\pi\)：奖励在“专家常去、策略少去”的地方高。收敛时 \(\rho_\pi=\rho_E\)，\(D^*\equiv\tfrac12\)，奖励退化成常数。这正是 GAIL 不适合当“奖励学习”用的原因：它输出的是一个会模仿的策略，不是一个能拿到别处重用的奖励（FIG 4）。AIRL（Fu、Luo 与 Levine，2018）改造了判别器的结构，使它在收敛时仍保留一个可迁移的奖励。

    IRL view: GAIL equals "find a reward with some regularized IRL, then find a policy with RL"; Ho and Ermon proved that this composition is exactly occupancy-measure matching, and different reward regularizers give different "distances": a linear reward class gives feature matching (Abbeel and Ng, 2004, and FIG 2), a GAN discriminator gives the JS divergence. GAN view: the optimal discriminator is \(D^*=\rho_E/(\rho_E+\rho_\pi)\) (Lesson 19.2), so \(\log D^*-\log(1-D^*)=\log\rho_E-\log\rho_\pi\): the reward is high where the expert goes often and the policy rarely. At convergence \(\rho_\pi=\rho_E\), \(D^*\equiv\tfrac12\), and the reward degenerates to a constant. That is exactly why GAIL is not suited to "reward learning": it outputs a policy that imitates, not a reward you can reuse elsewhere (FIG 4). AIRL (Fu, Luo and Levine, 2018) restructures the discriminator so that a transferable reward survives at convergence.

    6偏好：从“A 比 B 好”学奖励Preferences: learning a reward from "A is better than B"

    示教要求人会做，偏好只要求人会比。Bradley–Terry 模型（1952）假设每段行为有一个潜在的分数 \(R\)，人选 A 而不选 B 的概率是分数差的 sigmoid：

    Demonstrations require a person who can do the task; preferences only require a person who can compare. The Bradley–Terry model (1952) assumes each behaviour has a latent score \(R\), and the probability that a person picks A over B is the sigmoid of the score difference:

    $$ P(A\succ B)=\hl{bt}{\sigma\big(R_\psi(A)-R_\psi(B)\big)}=\frac{e^{R_\psi(A)}}{e^{R_\psi(A)}+e^{R_\psi(B)}},\qquad \mathcal L(\psi)=-\sum_{(A\succ B)\in\mathcal D}\log\sigma\big(R_\psi(A)-R_\psi(B)\big) $$

    拟合 \(\psi\) 就是在“分数差”上做逻辑回归（16.1 节）。它也是 FIG 2 的最大熵模型在“只有两个候选”时的特例：在 A、B 两条轨迹之间按 \(e^{R}\) 挑一条。下图里一台移动机器人要从左到右绕过一个障碍物，候选路线有 80 条，各自的偏移和抖动不同。每条路线有三个特征：比直线多走的长度 \(\phi_1\)、靠近障碍物的风险 \(\phi_2\)（最小间隙越小越接近 1，撞上时为 1）、抖动程度 \(\phi_3\)。一个“模拟的人”心里的分数是 \(R^*=-5\phi_1-8\phi_2-5\phi_3\)，按 \(P(A\succ B)=\sigma(\beta(R^*_A-R^*_B))\) 回答，\(\beta\) 越大这个人越一致。奖励模型 \(R_\psi=\psi^\top\boldsymbol\phi\) 对三个特征线性，每来一个标注就用牛顿法重新拟合（带一个很小的 L2 正则 \(0.01\|\psi\|^2\)，避免数据完全可分时 \(\psi\) 无界）。你也可以自己当标注员。

    Fitting \(\psi\) is logistic regression on score differences (Lesson 16.1). It is also FIG 2's maximum-entropy model in the special case of only two candidates: choose between trajectories A and B with probability proportional to \(e^{R}\). In the figure a mobile robot must get from left to right around an obstacle, and there are 80 candidate paths with different offsets and wiggles. Each path has three features: the extra length beyond a straight line \(\phi_1\), the risk of passing close to the obstacle \(\phi_2\) (closer to 1 as the minimum clearance shrinks, 1 on contact), and how much it wiggles \(\phi_3\). A "simulated person" scores paths with \(R^*=-5\phi_1-8\phi_2-5\phi_3\) and answers with \(P(A\succ B)=\sigma(\beta(R^*_A-R^*_B))\); the larger \(\beta\), the more consistent the person. The reward model \(R_\psi=\psi^\top\boldsymbol\phi\) is linear in the three features and is re-fitted by Newton's method after every label (with a tiny L2 penalty \(0.01\|\psi\|^2\) so that \(\psi\) stays bounded when the data are perfectly separable). You can also be the labeller yourself.

    RLHF 与 DPO：用偏好微调策略RLHF and DPO: fine-tuning a policy with preferences

    有了奖励模型 \(\hat r\)，就可以优化策略。但如果直接最大化 \(\hat r\)，策略会跑到奖励模型从没见过、因而估得不准的地方去。Christiano 等（2017）最早把“从比较里学奖励模型、再用深度 RL 优化它”做成了可行的流程；用于语言模型时，标准做法（Ziegler 等，2019；Ouyang 等，2022）是再加一个 KL 约束，让策略不要离一个参考策略 \(\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\)（通常是监督微调或行为克隆得到的策略）太远：

    With a reward model \(\hat r\) in hand we can optimize a policy. Maximize \(\hat r\) directly, however, and the policy runs off to places the reward model has never seen and therefore estimates badly. Christiano et al. (2017) made "learn a reward model from comparisons, then optimize it with deep RL" practical; for language models the standard recipe (Ziegler et al., 2019; Ouyang et al., 2022) adds a KL constraint that keeps the policy close to a reference policy \(\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\) (usually the result of supervised fine-tuning or behaviour cloning):

    $$ \max_\pi\ \mathbb E_{a\sim\pi}\big[\hat r(a)\big]-\beta\,\hl{kl}{D_{\mathrm{KL}}\big(\pi\,\|\,\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\big)}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \hl{pistar}{\pi^*(a)}=\frac{1}{Z}\,\hl{piref}{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(a)}\,\exp\big(\hat r(a)/\beta\big) $$

    右边的闭式解（第 7 节推导）说明 KL 正则的最优策略就是把参考策略按 \(e^{\hat r/\beta}\) 重新加权。在语言模型或机器人策略上 \(a\) 的空间太大，没法归一化，所以 RLHF 用 PPO（20.4 节）去逼近它。DPO（Rafailov 等，2023）反过来用：既然 \(\hat r(a)=\beta\log\frac{\pi^*(a)}{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(a)}+\beta\log Z\)，就把这个式子代进 BT 损失，\(Z\) 在分数差里消掉，直接在偏好对上训练策略，不需要单独的奖励模型，也不需要强化学习：

    The closed form on the right (derived in Section 7) says that the KL-regularized optimal policy is just the reference policy re-weighted by \(e^{\hat r/\beta}\). For a language model or a robot policy the space of \(a\) is far too large to normalize, so RLHF approximates it with PPO (Lesson 20.4). DPO (Rafailov et al., 2023) runs the formula backwards: since \(\hat r(a)=\beta\log\frac{\pi^*(a)}{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(a)}+\beta\log Z\), substitute it into the BT loss, where \(Z\) cancels in the score difference, and train the policy directly on the preference pairs, with no separate reward model and no reinforcement learning:

    $$ \mathcal L_{\mathrm{DPO}}(\theta)=-\sum_{(a_w\succ a_l)\in\mathcal D}\log\sigma\Big(\beta\log\frac{\hl{dpo}{\pi_\theta(a_w)}}{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(a_w)}-\beta\log\frac{\pi_\theta(a_l)}{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(a_l)}\Big) $$

    下图是一个最小的例子：同一个杯子有 6 个候选抓取，参考策略（从示教克隆来的）偏爱前两个，而真正成功率最高的是抓取 4。偏好数据是从参考策略里成对抽取抓取，再按 BT 模型和真实奖励 \(r^*\) 随机标注“哪个更好”。RLHF 一侧先拟合表格形式的奖励模型 \(\hat r\)，再用上面的闭式解得到 \(\pi^*\)（相当于 PPO 完美收敛）；DPO 一侧从 \(\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\) 出发，用 Adam 直接最小化 \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{DPO}}\)。两边用同一份数据、同一个极小的 L2 正则（\(0.001\sum_a(r(a)-\bar r)^2\)，DPO 加在它隐含的奖励 \(\beta\log\frac{\pi_\theta}{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}}\) 上）。

    The figure is a minimal example: one mug, 6 candidate grasps; the reference policy (cloned from demonstrations) favours the first two, while the grasp that actually succeeds most often is grasp 4. Preference data are made by drawing pairs of grasps from the reference policy and labelling "which is better" at random with the BT model and the true reward \(r^*\). The RLHF side first fits a tabular reward model \(\hat r\), then takes \(\pi^*\) from the closed form above (as if PPO had converged perfectly); the DPO side starts from \(\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\) and minimizes \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{DPO}}\) directly with Adam. Both use the same data and the same tiny L2 penalty (\(0.001\sum_a(r(a)-\bar r)^2\), applied in DPO to its implied reward \(\beta\log\frac{\pi_\theta}{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}}\)).

    问题的表述与 Ng–Russell 条件Problem statement and the Ng–Russell condition

    MDP \((\mathcal S,\mathcal A,P,\gamma)\) 已知，奖励 \(r\) 未知；给定专家策略 \(\pi_E\)（或它的示教）。IRL 要找 \(r\)，使 \(\pi_E\) 在 \(r\) 下最优，即对每个状态，专家的动作的 \(Q\) 值不低于任何别的动作（20.1 节的 Bellman 最优方程）：

    The MDP \((\mathcal S,\mathcal A,P,\gamma)\) is known, the reward \(r\) is not; we are given an expert policy \(\pi_E\) (or demonstrations of it). IRL seeks \(r\) such that \(\pi_E\) is optimal under \(r\): in every state the expert's action has a \(Q\)-value at least as high as any other action (the Bellman optimality equation of Lesson 20.1):

    $$ \hl{amb}{\mathcal R(\pi_E)}=\Big\{\,r\ :\ Q^{\pi_E}_r\big(s,\pi_E(s)\big)\ \ge\ Q^{\pi_E}_r(s,a)\quad\forall s,\ \forall a\,\Big\} $$

    对有限 MDP，\(Q^{\pi_E}_r\) 对 \(r\) 是线性的（\(V^{\pi}=(I-\gamma P_\pi)^{-1}r_\pi\)），所以 \(\mathcal R(\pi_E)\) 是由线性不等式围成的凸锥：它包含 \(r=0\)，对正数缩放封闭，这就是 FIG 1 的青色楔形。Ng 与 Russell（2000）对状态奖励写出了这组不等式，并用线性规划在其中挑一个“让专家动作领先得最多”的奖励（最大间隔），再加 L1 正则偏好简单的奖励。最大间隔规划（Ratliff、Bagnell 与 Zinkevich，2006）把同样的思想推广到结构化输出：专家路径的回报要比其他每条路径高出一个与“差多少”成比例的间隔。

    For a finite MDP, \(Q^{\pi_E}_r\) is linear in \(r\) (\(V^{\pi}=(I-\gamma P_\pi)^{-1}r_\pi\)), so \(\mathcal R(\pi_E)\) is a convex cone cut out by linear inequalities: it contains \(r=0\) and is closed under positive scaling, and it is FIG 1's cyan wedge. Ng and Russell (2000) wrote these inequalities for state rewards and used a linear program to pick, among them, the reward under which the expert's actions lead by the largest margin, with an L1 penalty favouring simple rewards. Maximum margin planning (Ratliff, Bagnell and Zinkevich, 2006) extends the idea to structured outputs: the expert's path must beat every other path by a margin proportional to how different that path is.

    势函数整形不改变最优策略Potential shaping does not change optimal policies

    对任何函数 \(\Phi:\mathcal S\to\mathbb R\)，令 \(r'(s,a,s')=r(s,a,s')+\hl{shape}{\gamma\Phi(s')-\Phi(s)}\)。沿一条轨迹求和，中间项两两抵消：

    For any function \(\Phi:\mathcal S\to\mathbb R\), let \(r'(s,a,s')=r(s,a,s')+\hl{shape}{\gamma\Phi(s')-\Phi(s)}\). Summed along a trajectory, the middle terms cancel in pairs:

    $$ \sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\gamma^t\big(\gamma\Phi(s_{t+1})-\Phi(s_t)\big)=\gamma^T\Phi(s_T)-\Phi(s_0)\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad Q'^{*}(s,a)=Q^{*}(s,a)-\Phi(s) $$

    每个状态的所有动作的 \(Q\) 值减去同一个数，\(\arg\max\) 不变（Ng、Harada 与 Russell，1999；他们还证明了：在不知道转移模型的前提下，势函数形式是唯一一种保证不改变最优策略的整形）。FIG 1 用 \(\gamma=1\)、\(\Phi(s)=-k\,d(s)\)，\(d\) 是到 G 的曼哈顿距离，于是每条从 S 到 G 的路线的回报都恰好加了 \(\Phi(G)-\Phi(S)=9k\)。所以 IRL 从示教里最多只能把奖励确定到“差一个整形项”。Skalse 等（2023）系统地刻画了不同数据源（最优示教、玻尔兹曼示教、偏好）能确定奖励到什么程度。

    Every action's \(Q\)-value in a state drops by the same number, so the \(\arg\max\) does not change (Ng, Harada and Russell, 1999; they also showed that, without knowledge of the transition model, the potential form is the only shaping guaranteed to leave optimal policies unchanged). FIG 1 uses \(\gamma=1\) and \(\Phi(s)=-k\,d(s)\), with \(d\) the Manhattan distance to G, so every route from S to G gains exactly \(\Phi(G)-\Phi(S)=9k\). IRL can therefore pin a reward down at best "up to a shaping term". Skalse et al. (2023) characterize systematically how far different data sources (optimal demonstrations, Boltzmann demonstrations, preferences) determine a reward.

    最大熵 IRL：从原则到算法Maximum-entropy IRL: from principle to algorithm

    原则。在所有满足特征匹配 \(\mathbb E_p[\mathbf f]=\bar{\mathbf f}_{\mathcal D}\) 的路线分布 \(p\) 里，选熵最大的那个（4.11 节的熵：除了数据要求的，不多假设任何东西）。写拉格朗日函数 \(\mathcal L=-\sum_\tau p\log p+\mathbf w^\top(\sum_\tau p\,\mathbf f-\bar{\mathbf f}_{\mathcal D})+\mu(\sum_\tau p-1)\)，对 \(p(\tau)\) 求导置零，得 \(\log p(\tau)=\mathbf w^\top\mathbf f(\tau)+\mu-1\)，也就是第 3 节的 \(p_{\mathbf w}\propto e^{\mathbf w^\top\mathbf f}\)：拉格朗日乘子 \(\mathbf w\) 就是奖励权重。它的对偶问题恰好是最大化示教的对数似然，

    Principle. Among all route distributions \(p\) that match the features, \(\mathbb E_p[\mathbf f]=\bar{\mathbf f}_{\mathcal D}\), pick the one with maximum entropy (the entropy of Lesson 4.11: assume nothing beyond what the data require). The Lagrangian is \(\mathcal L=-\sum_\tau p\log p+\mathbf w^\top(\sum_\tau p\,\mathbf f-\bar{\mathbf f}_{\mathcal D})+\mu(\sum_\tau p-1)\); setting its derivative with respect to \(p(\tau)\) to zero gives \(\log p(\tau)=\mathbf w^\top\mathbf f(\tau)+\mu-1\), which is the \(p_{\mathbf w}\propto e^{\mathbf w^\top\mathbf f}\) of Section 3: the Lagrange multipliers \(\mathbf w\) are the reward weights. Its dual problem is exactly maximizing the log-likelihood of the demonstrations,

    $$ \ell(\mathbf w)=\frac1N\sum_i\mathbf w^\top\mathbf f(\tau_i)-\log Z(\mathbf w),\qquad \nabla\ell=\bar{\mathbf f}_{\mathcal D}-\mathbb E_{p_{\mathbf w}}[\mathbf f],\qquad \nabla^2\ell=-\operatorname{Cov}_{p_{\mathbf w}}[\mathbf f]\preceq0 $$

    Hessian 是负的特征协方差，所以 \(\ell\) 是凹的，梯度上升不会卡在局部最优。协方差在某个方向上为零（例如示教和模型都几乎不踩泥）时，\(\ell\) 在那个方向上几乎是平的，这就是 FIG 2 里泥的权重一直漂的原因。

    计算 \(\mathbb E_{p_{\mathbf w}}[\mathbf f]\)。对确定性转移，“按 \(e^{R}\) 挑动作序列”可以分解成一个随时间变化的策略。FIG 2 和 FIG 3 用长度 \(T=24\) 的视界，并要求在最后一步时位于 G（G 是吸收状态，留在 G 不再得奖励）：

    The Hessian is minus the feature covariance, so \(\ell\) is concave and gradient ascent cannot get stuck in a local optimum. Where the covariance vanishes in some direction (say, neither the demonstrations nor the model ever touch mud), \(\ell\) is almost flat along it, which is why the mud weight in FIG 2 keeps drifting.

    Computing \(\mathbb E_{p_{\mathbf w}}[\mathbf f]\). With deterministic transitions, "choose the action sequence with probability \(\propto e^{R}\)" factorizes into a time-varying policy. FIGs 2 and 3 use a horizon of \(T=24\) and require being at G at the last step (G is absorbing, and staying there earns nothing):

    $$ V_T(s)=\begin{cases}0&s=G\\-\infty&s\neq G\end{cases},\qquad Q_t(s,a)=r_{\mathbf w}(s,a)+V_{t+1}(s'),\qquad V_t(s)=\log\sum_a e^{Q_t(s,a)},\qquad \pi_t(a|s)=e^{Q_t(s,a)-V_t(s)} $$ $$ d_0=\delta_S,\qquad d_{t+1}(s')=\sum_{s,a}d_t(s)\,\pi_t(a|s)\,[s\xrightarrow{a}s'],\qquad \mathbb E_{p_{\mathbf w}}[\mathbf f]=\sum_{t,s,a}d_t(s)\,\pi_t(a|s)\,\boldsymbol\phi(s,a) $$

    向后这一遍就是 6.12 节的值迭代把 \(\max\) 换成了 \(\log\sum\exp\)（“软”最大值），\(V_0(S)=\log Z(\mathbf w)\)；向前这一遍是把起点的概率质量沿策略推开。两遍的代价都是 \(O(T\,|\mathcal S|\,|\mathcal A|)\)，FIG 2 每次迭代不到 1 ms。对随机转移，直接按 \(e^{R}\) 给轨迹加权会让智能体“相信自己能挑运气”；Ziebart 等（2010）的最大因果熵改为只对策略的因果熵最大化，结果是同样的软 Bellman 递推，只是 \(V_{t+1}(s')\) 换成 \(\mathbb E_{s'}[V_{t+1}(s')]\)。这也正是 SAC（20.4 节）里的软 Bellman 方程：最大熵 IRL 和最大熵 RL 是同一个模型的逆问题与正问题。

    深度版本。把 \(\mathbf w^\top\boldsymbol\phi\) 换成神经网络 \(r_\theta(s)\)（Wulfmeier 等，2015），梯度变成 \(\sum_s(\bar d_{\mathcal D}(s)-d_{\theta}(s))\nabla_\theta r_\theta(s)\)：示教访问多于模型的状态奖励上调，反之下调。在连续空间里没法精确算 \(d_\theta\)，引导代价学习（Finn、Levine 与 Abbeel，2016）用当前策略的样本做重要性采样来估计 \(\log Z\)，策略同时用 RL 更新，这已经和下面的 GAIL 很像了。

    The backward sweep is the value iteration of Lesson 6.12 with \(\max\) replaced by \(\log\sum\exp\) (a "soft" maximum), and \(V_0(S)=\log Z(\mathbf w)\); the forward sweep pushes the probability mass at the start out along the policy. Both cost \(O(T\,|\mathcal S|\,|\mathcal A|)\); one iteration of FIG 2 takes under 1 ms. With stochastic transitions, weighting trajectories directly by \(e^{R}\) makes the agent "believe it can choose its luck"; the maximum causal entropy formulation of Ziebart et al. (2010) maximizes only the causal entropy of the policy instead, and the result is the same soft Bellman recursion with \(V_{t+1}(s')\) replaced by \(\mathbb E_{s'}[V_{t+1}(s')]\). That is exactly the soft Bellman equation of SAC (Lesson 20.4): maximum-entropy IRL and maximum-entropy RL are the inverse and forward problems of the same model.

    Deep versions. Replace \(\mathbf w^\top\boldsymbol\phi\) by a neural network \(r_\theta(s)\) (Wulfmeier et al., 2015) and the gradient becomes \(\sum_s(\bar d_{\mathcal D}(s)-d_{\theta}(s))\nabla_\theta r_\theta(s)\): raise the reward of states the demonstrations visit more than the model, lower it where the model visits more. In continuous spaces \(d_\theta\) cannot be computed exactly; guided cost learning (Finn, Levine and Abbeel, 2016) estimates \(\log Z\) by importance sampling from the current policy, which is itself updated with RL, and that is already very close to GAIL below.

    GAIL：占用度量匹配GAIL: occupancy-measure matching

    策略的占用度量 \(\rho_\pi(s,a)=\sum_t\gamma^t P(s_t=s,a_t=a\mid\pi)\) 和策略一一对应（\(\pi(a|s)=\rho_\pi(s,a)/\sum_{a'}\rho_\pi(s,a')\)），任何奖励的期望回报都是它的线性函数 \(\sum_{s,a}\rho_\pi(s,a)r(s,a)\)。Ho 与 Ermon（2016）证明：用凸正则 \(\psi(r)\) 的最大因果熵 IRL 再接 RL，所得策略等于

    A policy's occupancy measure \(\rho_\pi(s,a)=\sum_t\gamma^t P(s_t=s,a_t=a\mid\pi)\) corresponds one-to-one with the policy (\(\pi(a|s)=\rho_\pi(s,a)/\sum_{a'}\rho_\pi(s,a')\)), and every reward's expected return is linear in it, \(\sum_{s,a}\rho_\pi(s,a)r(s,a)\). Ho and Ermon (2016) proved that maximum-causal-entropy IRL with a convex regularizer \(\psi(r)\), followed by RL, yields the policy

    $$ \mathrm{RL}\circ\mathrm{IRL}_\psi(\pi_E)=\arg\min_\pi\ -H(\pi)+\psi^{*}\big(\hl{rhopi}{\rho_\pi}-\hl{rhoE}{\rho_{E}}\big) $$

    其中 \(\psi^*\) 是 \(\psi\) 的凸共轭，它就是度量“两个占用度量差多少”的距离。选一个特定的 \(\psi\)，\(\psi^*\) 就变成（差一个常数的）\(\max_D\mathbb E_{\rho_\pi}[\log(1-D)]+\mathbb E_{\rho_E}[\log D]\)，也就是 19.2 节的 GAN 目标与 JS 散度；选“奖励在某个线性特征类里”，就退化成特征匹配。实际算法交替：判别器在专家样本和策略样本上做二分类，策略用 TRPO/PPO 最大化判别器给的奖励。

    奖励的符号与偏置。原文的判别器输出“来自策略”的概率，策略的代价是 \(\log D_\pi\)，换成本页的写法就是奖励 \(-\log(1-D)\ge0\)。另一种常见选择是本页 FIG 3 用的 \(\log D\le0\)，还有 AIRL 风格的 \(\log D-\log(1-D)\)。三者的不动点相同，但在会提前结束的任务里偏置不同：恒正的奖励鼓励“活得越久越好”，恒负的奖励鼓励尽快结束（Kostrikov 等，2019 详细分析了这一点，并给出处理吸收状态的办法）。FIG 3 要求在 24 步内到达 G，选 \(\log D\) 时策略不会故意绕圈子。

    where \(\psi^*\) is the convex conjugate of \(\psi\), and it is the distance that measures "how different two occupancy measures are". For one particular \(\psi\), \(\psi^*\) becomes (up to a constant) \(\max_D\mathbb E_{\rho_\pi}[\log(1-D)]+\mathbb E_{\rho_E}[\log D]\), the GAN objective and JS divergence of Lesson 19.2; for "rewards in a linear feature class" it reduces to feature matching. The practical algorithm alternates: the discriminator classifies expert samples against policy samples, and the policy maximizes the discriminator's reward with TRPO/PPO.

    The sign of the reward and its bias. In the original paper the discriminator outputs the probability of "came from the policy" and the policy's cost is \(\log D_\pi\), which in this page's notation is the reward \(-\log(1-D)\ge0\). Another common choice is the \(\log D\le0\) used in FIG 3, and there is the AIRL-style \(\log D-\log(1-D)\). All three have the same fixed point but different biases in tasks that can end early: an always-positive reward encourages "survive as long as possible", an always-negative one encourages ending quickly (Kostrikov et al., 2019 analyse this in detail and show how to handle absorbing states). FIG 3 requires reaching G within 24 steps, and with \(\log D\) the policy has no incentive to circle around on purpose.

    Bradley–Terry 的最大似然Maximum likelihood for Bradley–Terry

    记 \(\Delta\boldsymbol\phi=\boldsymbol\phi(A)-\boldsymbol\phi(B)\)，\(y=1\) 表示选了 A。线性奖励模型下 BT 的负对数似然就是以 \(\Delta\boldsymbol\phi\) 为输入的逻辑回归：

    Write \(\Delta\boldsymbol\phi=\boldsymbol\phi(A)-\boldsymbol\phi(B)\) and \(y=1\) when A was chosen. With a linear reward model, BT's negative log-likelihood is logistic regression with input \(\Delta\boldsymbol\phi\):

    $$ \mathcal L(\psi)=\sum_i\Big[\log\big(1+e^{\psi^\top\Delta\boldsymbol\phi_i}\big)-y_i\,\psi^\top\Delta\boldsymbol\phi_i\Big]+\lambda\|\psi\|^2,\qquad \nabla\mathcal L=\sum_i\big(\sigma(\psi^\top\Delta\boldsymbol\phi_i)-y_i\big)\Delta\boldsymbol\phi_i+2\lambda\psi $$

    Hessian \(\sum_i\sigma(1-\sigma)\Delta\boldsymbol\phi_i\Delta\boldsymbol\phi_i^\top+2\lambda I\) 正定，FIG 5 每次用牛顿法几步就收敛。BT 等价于假设人看到的分数带有独立的 Gumbel 噪声后取较大者（随机效用模型），它推出“偏好可传递”：若 \(P(A\succ B)>\tfrac12\) 且 \(P(B\succ C)>\tfrac12\) 则 \(P(A\succ C)>\tfrac12\)。真实的人、尤其是多个标注员的集合，常常违反这一点，这是第 9 节一个误区的来源。对神经网络奖励模型（RLHF 里通常是一个去掉输出头、加一个标量头的语言模型），损失的形式完全相同，只是 \(\psi^\top\boldsymbol\phi\) 换成了网络输出。

    The Hessian \(\sum_i\sigma(1-\sigma)\Delta\boldsymbol\phi_i\Delta\boldsymbol\phi_i^\top+2\lambda I\) is positive definite, and FIG 5's Newton iteration converges in a few steps each time. BT is equivalent to assuming that the person sees each score plus independent Gumbel noise and picks the larger (a random-utility model), and it implies that preferences are transitive: if \(P(A\succ B)>\tfrac12\) and \(P(B\succ C)>\tfrac12\) then \(P(A\succ C)>\tfrac12\). Real people, and especially pools of several labellers, often violate this, which is the source of one of the pitfalls in Section 9. For a neural reward model (in RLHF, usually a language model with its output head replaced by a scalar head) the loss has exactly the same form, with \(\psi^\top\boldsymbol\phi\) replaced by the network's output.

    KL 正则最优策略与 DPOThe KL-regularized optimal policy and DPO

    对固定的 \(\hat r\)，把目标改写成一个 KL：

    For a fixed \(\hat r\), rewrite the objective as a KL divergence:

    $$ \mathbb E_\pi[\hat r]-\beta D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\pi\|\pi_{\mathrm{ref}})=-\beta\sum_a\pi(a)\log\frac{\pi(a)}{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(a)e^{\hat r(a)/\beta}}=-\beta\,D_{\mathrm{KL}}\big(\pi\,\|\,\pi^*\big)+\beta\log Z,\qquad \pi^*=\frac{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\,e^{\hat r/\beta}}{Z} $$

    KL 非负、只在两分布相同时为 0，所以最优解唯一，就是 \(\pi^*\)（这是 Gibbs 变分原理，第 3 节的最大熵模型是 \(\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\) 均匀时的特例）。取对数：\(\hat r(a)=\beta\log\frac{\pi^*(a)}{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(a)}+\beta\log Z\)。把它代入 BT 的似然，\(\beta\log Z\) 在 \(\hat r(a_w)-\hat r(a_l)\) 里抵消，就得到第 6 节的 \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{DPO}}\)：对奖励模型做 BT 最大似然，和对策略做 DPO，是同一个优化问题的两种参数化。DPO 对 \(\theta\) 的梯度是

    KL is non-negative and zero only when the two distributions agree, so the optimum is unique and equals \(\pi^*\) (the Gibbs variational principle; the maximum-entropy model of Section 3 is the special case of a uniform \(\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\)). Taking logs: \(\hat r(a)=\beta\log\frac{\pi^*(a)}{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(a)}+\beta\log Z\). Substitute this into the BT likelihood; \(\beta\log Z\) cancels in \(\hat r(a_w)-\hat r(a_l)\), and out comes the \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{DPO}}\) of Section 6: BT maximum likelihood for a reward model and DPO for a policy are two parameterizations of the same optimization problem. DPO's gradient with respect to \(\theta\) is

    $$ \nabla_\theta\mathcal L_{\mathrm{DPO}}=-\beta\sum_{(a_w,a_l)}\sigma\big(\hat r_\theta(a_l)-\hat r_\theta(a_w)\big)\Big[\nabla_\theta\log\pi_\theta(a_w)-\nabla_\theta\log\pi_\theta(a_l)\Big],\qquad \hat r_\theta=\beta\log\frac{\pi_\theta}{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}} $$

    每一对的权重是“隐含奖励把这一对排错的程度”：排错得越厉害，越用力地抬高赢家、压低输家。如果某个动作在数据里从没输过，BT 的最大似然要求它的奖励趋于无穷，DPO 就会把别的动作的概率一路压向 0。这是 Azar 等（2024）提出 IPO 的动机；FIG 6 里一个很小的 L2 正则把它挡住了，但 40 个比较时抓取 6 的“四战全胜”仍然把它的奖励估计推到了最高。

    Each pair is weighted by how badly the implied reward ranks it: the worse the error, the harder it lifts the winner and pushes down the loser. If an action never loses in the data, BT maximum likelihood wants its reward to go to infinity, and DPO pushes the other actions' probabilities all the way to 0. This motivated IPO (Azar et al., 2024); in FIG 6 a tiny L2 penalty stops the blow-up, but with 40 comparisons grasp 6's "four wins out of four" still pushes its reward estimate to the top.

    9常见误区Common pitfalls

    误区 1：以为 IRL 找回了“真正的”奖励Pitfall 1: believing IRL recovered "the true" reward

    示教最多把奖励确定到一个缩放、一个整形项和一片等价区域（FIG 1），最大熵之类的原则再从中挑一个。学到的奖励在示教覆盖到的权衡上可信，在没覆盖到的方向上只是外推：FIG 2 里泥的权重就是例子，它取决于你训练了多久，而不是专家怎么想。检验学到的奖励，要在新环境里用它规划、看行为是否合理（FIG 4），而不是和某个“真值”比数字。

    Demonstrations pin a reward down at best up to a scale, a shaping term and a region of equivalent rewards (FIG 1), and a principle such as maximum entropy picks one of them. The learned reward is trustworthy for the trade-offs the demonstrations cover and only an extrapolation in other directions: the mud weight in FIG 2 is an example, and its value depends on how long you trained, not on what the expert thinks. Test a learned reward by planning with it in new environments and checking the behaviour (FIG 4), not by comparing numbers with some "true" value.

    误区 2：忽略了对专家的假设Pitfall 2: ignoring the assumptions about the expert

    每种方法都假设了专家“怎么犯错”：最优（Ng–Russell）、按 \(e^{R}\) 随机（最大熵）、按 \(\sigma(\beta\Delta R)\) 随机（BT）。示教者如果有系统性偏差，比如遥操作接口让人总是走得很慢、或者不敢靠近障碍物，这些偏差会被当成偏好学进奖励里，再被规划器忠实地执行。必要时要把示教者的限制（控制延迟、视野）建进模型，或者混合多种反馈（示教 + 纠正 + 偏好）。

    Every method assumes how the expert errs: optimally (Ng–Russell), at random in proportion to \(e^{R}\) (maximum entropy), or with probability \(\sigma(\beta\Delta R)\) (BT). If the demonstrator has systematic biases, for example a teleoperation interface that makes people move slowly or keeps them away from obstacles, those biases are learned into the reward as preferences and then faithfully executed by the planner. When necessary, model the demonstrator's limitations (control latency, field of view) or mix kinds of feedback (demonstrations + corrections + preferences).

    误区 3：把奖励模型优化到底Pitfall 3: optimizing the reward model all the way

    奖励模型只是对人的偏好的一个有限样本拟合。策略优化得越狠（\(\beta\) 越小、PPO 跑得越久），越会找到奖励模型高估的地方：FIG 6 里 40 个比较时，真实奖励在 \(\beta\approx1\) 处最高，再往下调反而变差。实践中要保留 KL 约束、监控真实指标（人工评估、任务成功率）、在新采集的策略样本上继续收集偏好、或者用奖励模型集成估计不确定性（21.3 节）。

    A reward model is a fit to a finite sample of human preferences. The harder the policy optimizes (smaller \(\beta\), PPO run longer), the more it finds the places the reward model overestimates: in FIG 6 with 40 comparisons, the true reward is highest at \(\beta\approx1\) and gets worse below. In practice keep the KL constraint, monitor real metrics (human evaluation, task success), keep collecting preferences on fresh samples from the current policy, or estimate uncertainty with an ensemble of reward models (Lesson 21.3).

    误区 4：把 GAIL 的判别器当成奖励函数重用Pitfall 4: reusing GAIL's discriminator as a reward function

    GAIL 的判别器是一个对当前策略的分类器，收敛时理论上处处为 ½；拿它在新环境里从头训练一个策略，得到的最多是噪声；而 GAIL 训练出的策略本身也不会迁移（FIG 4）。另外，奖励取 \(-\log(1-D)\) 还是 \(\log D\) 会带来“想活得久”或“想早结束”的偏置，在有终止条件的任务里要特别注意吸收状态的处理。需要可迁移的奖励时，用 AIRL 这类有结构约束的方法，或者直接用 IRL。

    GAIL's discriminator is a classifier against the current policy and is, in theory, ½ everywhere at convergence; training a fresh policy with it in a new environment yields noise at best, and the policy GAIL trained does not transfer either (FIG 4). Moreover, using \(-\log(1-D)\) or \(\log D\) as the reward introduces a bias towards living long or ending early, so in tasks with termination conditions treat absorbing states with care. When you need a transferable reward, use a structured method such as AIRL, or IRL itself.

    误区 5：以为偏好数据是干净的Pitfall 5: assuming preference data are clean

    真实的偏好有噪声（FIG 5 的小 \(\beta\)）、因人而异、会不可传递，还会受到与任务无关的因素影响（语言模型里著名的例子是标注员偏爱更长的回答，奖励模型于是学会了“越长越好”）。BT 把多个标注员的分歧压成一个平均分数，少数人的偏好会被抹掉。要记录标注员一致率、对比较对做去偏（如长度控制）、对困难或有分歧的对单独处理；对机器人，还要注意视频片段的长度和视角本身就会影响人的判断。

    Real preferences are noisy (FIG 5's small \(\beta\)), differ between people, can be intransitive, and are swayed by factors unrelated to the task (the famous language-model example is labellers preferring longer answers, so the reward model learns "longer is better"). BT squashes disagreement between labellers into one average score and erases minority preferences. Track inter-labeller agreement, de-bias the comparisons (for example by controlling for length), and treat hard or disputed pairs separately; for robots, remember that the length and viewpoint of the video clips themselves influence people's judgement.

    1. 专家的路线在奖励 \(r\) 下最优。下面哪一个奖励不一定让同一条路线仍然最优？（\(\gamma=1\)，所有路线都从 S 到 G）1. The expert's route is optimal under reward \(r\). Which of these rewards does not necessarily keep the same route optimal? (\(\gamma=1\), every route goes from S to G)
    选项 B。每步加常数 \(c\) 让一条路线的回报加上 \(c\times\)步数，长路线占便宜，排序可能改变（在 FIG 1 里相当于改了 \(w_{\mathrm s}\)，例如 \(c\) 足够大时绕远的铺装路线会胜出）。正数缩放（A）不改变排序；势函数整形（C）让每条路线都加同一个 \(\Phi(G)-\Phi(S)\)。Option B. Adding \(c\) to every step adds \(c\times\)(number of steps) to a route's return, favouring longer routes, so the ranking can change (in FIG 1 this changes \(w_{\mathrm s}\); with \(c\) large enough the long pavement detour wins). Positive scaling (A) keeps the ranking; potential shaping (C) adds the same \(\Phi(G)-\Phi(S)\) to every route.
    2. 最大熵 IRL 训练收敛（梯度为零）时，下面哪一项一定成立？2. When maximum-entropy IRL has converged (zero gradient), which statement must hold?
    选项 C。梯度就是 \(\bar{\mathbf f}_{\mathcal D}-\mathbb E_{p_{\mathbf w}}[\mathbf f]\)，为零即特征匹配。它不保证找回真值（A）：样本有限时会有估计误差，示教没覆盖的特征（FIG 2 的泥）甚至收敛不了。示教本身各不相同，一个分布不可能让它们都成为最可能的路线（B），也不会只把概率放在这 N 条上（D）。Option C. The gradient is \(\bar{\mathbf f}_{\mathcal D}-\mathbb E_{p_{\mathbf w}}[\mathbf f]\), and zero means feature matching. It does not guarantee recovering the truth (A): finite samples give estimation error, and a feature the demonstrations never cover (the mud of FIG 2) does not even converge. The demonstrations differ from each other, so no distribution makes all of them the most likely route (B), nor does it put probability only on these N routes (D).
    3. GAIL 训练到判别器在所有状态-动作对上都输出 ½。这说明什么？3. GAIL has trained to the point where the discriminator outputs ½ on every state-action pair. What does that tell you?
    选项 D。\(D^*=\rho_E/(\rho_E+\rho_\pi)\)，处处为 ½ 当且仅当 \(\rho_\pi=\rho_E\)：这是 GAIL 的目标，不是故障（排除 A）。常数奖励在新环境里没有用（排除 B，FIG 4）。GAIL 并不最大化某个固定的任务奖励，“回报”这个说法在这里没有意义（排除 C）。Option D. \(D^*=\rho_E/(\rho_E+\rho_\pi)\) is ½ everywhere exactly when \(\rho_\pi=\rho_E\): that is GAIL's goal, not a fault (not A). A constant reward is useless in a new environment (not B; FIG 4). GAIL does not maximize any fixed task reward, so "return" has no meaning here (not C).
    4. 奖励模型给出 \(R(A)=2.0\)，\(R(B)=0.9\)。按 Bradley–Terry 模型，人选 A 的概率大约是多少？4. A reward model gives \(R(A)=2.0\) and \(R(B)=0.9\). Under the Bradley–Terry model, roughly what is the probability that a person picks A?
    选项 A。\(P=\sigma(2.0-0.9)=\sigma(1.1)=1/(1+e^{-1.1})\approx0.750\)。选项 B 是 \(2.0/(2.0+0.9)\)，把分数当成了正的“强度”而不是对数强度；BT 只依赖分数差，绝对分数无关（排除 D）。Option A. \(P=\sigma(2.0-0.9)=\sigma(1.1)=1/(1+e^{-1.1})\approx0.750\). Option B is \(2.0/(2.0+0.9)\), which treats the scores as positive "strengths" instead of log-strengths; BT depends only on the score difference, not the absolute scores (not D).
    5. 在 KL 正则的 RLHF 目标 \(\mathbb E_\pi[\hat r]-\beta D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\pi\|\pi_{\mathrm{ref}})\) 里，把 \(\beta\) 一路调到接近 0，会怎样？5. In the KL-regularized RLHF objective \(\mathbb E_\pi[\hat r]-\beta D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\pi\|\pi_{\mathrm{ref}})\), what happens as \(\beta\) is turned down towards 0?
    选项 C。\(\pi^*\propto\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}e^{\hat r/\beta}\)，\(\beta\to0\) 时趋于 \(\arg\max\hat r\) 上的点质量（FIG 6 的 40 个比较：抓取 6，真实奖励跌到 0.50）。\(\beta\to\infty\) 才回到 \(\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\)（排除 A）。真实奖励只有在奖励模型的最大值也是真实最大值时才单调上升（排除 B）。对任何 \(\beta\)，两者优化同一个目标，最优解相同（排除 D）。Option C. \(\pi^*\propto\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}e^{\hat r/\beta}\) tends to a point mass on \(\arg\max\hat r\) as \(\beta\to0\) (FIG 6 with 40 comparisons: grasp 6, with the true reward falling to 0.50). Only \(\beta\to\infty\) returns to \(\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\) (not A). The true reward rises monotonically only if the reward model's maximum is also the true maximum (not B). For every \(\beta\) the two optimize the same objective and share the optimum (not D).
