自编码器 (Autoencoder)Autoencoders

机器人相机一帧有几十万个数，可场景真正在变的往往只有几个量：物块在哪、夹爪张开多少。自编码器让一个网络把输入压缩成很短的编码，再从编码把输入还原出来，训练目标就是输入本身，不需要任何标签。线性的自编码器恰好学到 PCA 的子空间；非线性的能沿着弯曲的数据流形压缩，用 2 个数做到 PCA 要十几个主成分才能做到的事。本节在浏览器里现场训练六个小网络，看它们怎样压缩、去噪、在哪里失败，以及为什么“编码好”不等于“能生成”，也不等于“能拿来控制”。 A frame from a robot camera holds hundreds of thousands of numbers, yet what actually changes in the scene is often a handful of quantities: where the block is, how far the gripper is open. An autoencoder trains a network to squeeze its input into a short code and to rebuild the input from that code; the training target is the input itself, so no labels are needed. A linear autoencoder learns exactly the PCA subspace; a nonlinear one can compress along a curved data manifold and do with 2 numbers what PCA needs a dozen or more components for. This lesson trains six small networks live in the browser to see how they compress, how they denoise, where they fail, and why a good code is neither a generator nor automatically a good state for control.

    1为什么机器人需要它Why robots need this

    一台 640×480 的彩色相机，每帧给出 921 600 个数。把这样的向量直接交给控制器或强化学习算法，几乎学不动：输入维度巨大，相邻像素高度相关，而决定任务的量（物体位置、姿态、夹爪开合）只有几个。我们希望先把观测压成一个短向量 \(\mathbf z\)，让它保留“场景处在什么状态”，丢掉其余的东西，再在 \(\mathbf z\) 上做规划、学策略、检测异常。

    18.1 节的 PCA 已经是一种压缩：找一个 \(k\) 维线性子空间，使数据投影上去之后丢掉的方差最少。但 PCA 只会用平直的子空间。一个圆盘在桌面上平移，这些图像构成一张只有 2 个自由度的“曲面”，可它在像素空间里弯得厉害：圆盘挪开一个直径，新旧两张图几乎不重叠，彼此近似正交。用平面去贴这种曲面，需要很多维。

    自编码器（autoencoder，AE）是 PCA 的非线性推广：一个编码器网络 \(f_\phi\) 把 \(\mathbf x\) 映成短编码 \(\mathbf z\)，一个解码器网络 \(g_\theta\) 再把 \(\mathbf z\) 还原成 \(\hat{\mathbf x}\)，用 16.3 节的反向传播一起训练，目标是让 \(\hat{\mathbf x}\) 尽量接近 \(\mathbf x\)。标签就是输入本身，所以它是自监督的：机器人随手录下的视频就是训练数据。它也是本章后面几种方法的起点：VAE（19.1 节）给编码加上概率解释，使它能生成；对比学习（18.3 节）和 Masked Modeling / JEPA（18.4 节）换掉“逐像素还原”这个目标；去噪自编码器则直接通向扩散模型（19.6 节）。

    A 640×480 colour camera delivers 921 600 numbers per frame. Hand such a vector straight to a controller or a reinforcement-learning algorithm and it barely learns: the input is huge, neighbouring pixels are strongly correlated, and the quantities that matter for the task (object position, pose, gripper opening) are only a few. We would like to first squeeze the observation into a short vector \(\mathbf z\) that keeps "what state the scene is in" and drops the rest, and then plan, learn policies and detect anomalies on \(\mathbf z\).

    The PCA of Lesson 18.1 is already a compression: find the \(k\)-dimensional linear subspace onto which the data can be projected with the least lost variance. But PCA only knows flat subspaces. Slide a disc across a table and the images form a "surface" with only 2 degrees of freedom, yet that surface is badly curved in pixel space: move the disc by one diameter and the old and new images hardly overlap, so they are nearly orthogonal. Fitting such a surface with flat pieces takes many dimensions.

    An autoencoder (AE) is the nonlinear generalization of PCA: an encoder network \(f_\phi\) maps \(\mathbf x\) to a short code \(\mathbf z\), a decoder network \(g_\theta\) maps \(\mathbf z\) back to \(\hat{\mathbf x}\), and both are trained together by the backpropagation of Lesson 16.3 so that \(\hat{\mathbf x}\) comes as close to \(\mathbf x\) as possible. The label is the input itself, so it is self-supervised: video the robot records anyway is training data. It is also the starting point of several later methods: the VAE (Lesson 19.1) gives the code a probabilistic meaning so that it can generate; contrastive learning (Lesson 18.3) and masked modeling / JEPA (Lesson 18.4) replace the "rebuild every pixel" objective; and the denoising autoencoder leads straight to diffusion models (Lesson 19.6).

    2直觉：从一个窄口挤过去Intuition: squeeze through a narrow neck

    自编码器的结构像一个沙漏：宽的输入，窄的中间，宽的输出。

    An autoencoder is shaped like an hourglass: a wide input, a narrow middle, a wide output.

    $$ \hl{z}{\mathbf z}=\hl{enc}{f_\phi}(\mathbf x)\in\R^{k},\qquad \hl{rec}{\hat{\mathbf x}}=\hl{dec}{g_\theta}(\mathbf z)\in\R^{D},\qquad k\ll D,\qquad \min_{\phi,\theta}\ \frac1N\sum_{i=1}^{N}\big\|\mathbf x_i-g_\theta(f_\phi(\mathbf x_i))\big\|^2 $$

    中间那层叫瓶颈（bottleneck），它的输出 \(\mathbf z\) 叫编码或隐变量（latent code）。如果 \(k\ge D\)，网络只要学会“原样照抄”就能把误差降到零，什么也没学到；正是因为 \(k\) 很小，网络被迫找出数据里最省字数的描述方式。如果数据点其实都落在一条曲线上，一个数（沿曲线走了多远）就够了。

    下面是最小的例子：机器人示教时夹爪在桌面上走过的一条弯曲路径，160 个二维位置（单位 m，带 3 cm 噪声）。编码器是 2→24→1 的小网络（tanh），解码器是 1→24→2。PCA 只能用一条直线来表示一维结构；自编码器的解码器 \(\hl{dec}{g_\theta}\) 把整条一维的“编码轴”弯成一条曲线，训练就是让这条曲线去贴合数据。

    The middle layer is the bottleneck, and its output \(\mathbf z\) is the code or latent. If \(k\ge D\) the network could drive the error to zero by just copying its input, having learned nothing; it is precisely because \(k\) is small that the network is forced to find the most economical description of the data. If all data points actually lie on one curve, a single number (how far along the curve) is enough.

    Here is the smallest example: a curved path traced by a robot's gripper on a table during a demonstration, 160 planar positions (in metres, with 3 cm of noise). The encoder is a small 2→24→1 network (tanh), the decoder 1→24→2. PCA can only represent one-dimensional structure by a straight line; the autoencoder's decoder \(\hl{dec}{g_\theta}\) bends the whole one-dimensional "code axis" into a curve, and training makes that curve hug the data.

    核心洞见Key insight

    瓶颈逼着网络学会数据的“坐标系”：编码器给每个点在数据流形上的一个坐标，解码器把坐标还原成点。PCA 的坐标轴是直的，自编码器的可以是弯的。但这个坐标只为“还原得好”而设计，没有任何东西要求它光滑、均匀或有意义：圆上的剪口就是第一个警告。

    The bottleneck forces the network to learn a "coordinate system" for the data: the encoder gives each point a coordinate on the data manifold and the decoder turns coordinates back into points. PCA's axes are straight; an autoencoder's can bend. But the coordinate is designed only for "rebuilds well", and nothing asks it to be smooth, even or meaningful: the cut on the circle is the first warning.

    核心洞见Key insight

    PCA 按“方差”压缩，自编码器按“自由度”压缩。当数据是少数几个物理量的非线性函数（相机图像几乎总是这样），两者的差距可以是一个数量级。但能达到的编码长度和容易训练出来的编码长度是两回事：瓶颈略宽于真实自由度，往往训练得更好。

    PCA compresses by variance; an autoencoder compresses by degrees of freedom. When the data are a nonlinear function of a few physical quantities (camera images almost always are), the gap can be an order of magnitude. But the shortest code that is achievable and the code that is easy to train are different things: a bottleneck slightly wider than the true number of degrees of freedom often trains better.

    4另外三个视角：线性情形、隐空间、去噪Three more views: the linear case, the latent space, denoising

    线性自编码器学到的就是 PCA 子空间A linear autoencoder learns the PCA subspace

    去掉所有非线性，自编码器还剩什么？编码器变成一个矩阵 \(E\)（加偏置），解码器变成矩阵 \(W\)（加偏置），重建 \(\hat{\mathbf x}=W E\mathbf x+\mathbf c\) 必然落在 \(W\) 的列空间（平移到某一点）里。第 5 节会证明：只要第 \(k\) 和第 \(k+1\) 个特征值不相等，平方误差的最优解里 \(W\) 的列空间就是前 \(k\) 个主方向张成的子空间，误差恰好等于 PCA-\(k\) 的误差。下面的例子是两个手指关节的角度（单位 rad）：它们倾向于一起弯曲，所以数据沿一条斜线分布，就像 18.1 节的“协同”（synergy）。线性自编码器 2→1→2 用 Adam 从随机初值开始训练。

    Strip out every nonlinearity and what is left of an autoencoder? The encoder becomes a matrix \(E\) (plus a bias), the decoder a matrix \(W\) (plus a bias), and the reconstruction \(\hat{\mathbf x}=W E\mathbf x+\mathbf c\) always lies in the column space of \(W\), shifted to some point. Section 5 proves that as long as the \(k\)-th and \((k+1)\)-th eigenvalues differ, at the optimum of the squared error the column space of \(W\) is the subspace spanned by the first \(k\) principal directions, and the error equals PCA-\(k\)'s exactly.     核心洞见Key insight

    普通自编码器只要求“编码能被还原”，对编码在隐空间里怎么分布没有任何约束：没有先验，不知道该从哪里采样，空隙处的解码结果没有保证。要能生成，就得让训练同时规定编码的分布，这正是 19.1 节 VAE 做的事：编码器输出一个分布，再用 KL 项把它拉向标准正态。

    A plain autoencoder only asks that codes can be decoded; it says nothing about how the codes are spread over the latent space: no prior, no idea where to sample, no guarantee about what the gaps decode to. To generate, training must also shape the distribution of codes, which is exactly what the VAE of Lesson 19.1 does: the encoder outputs a distribution, and a KL term pulls it toward a standard normal.

    去噪自编码器：学会回到数据上Denoising autoencoders: learning to step back onto the data

    真实相机有噪声。去噪自编码器（denoising AE，DAE）的改动只有一处：输入换成被破坏的版本 \(\hl{noise}{\tilde{\mathbf x}}=\mathbf x+\sigma\boldsymbol\varepsilon\)，目标仍是干净的 \(\mathbf x\)。网络没法再“照抄输入”，只能学会把偏离数据流形的点推回流形上。下面两个网络结构完全相同（144→32→144，没有窄瓶颈），同时训练：一个用干净输入（普通 AE），一个用加噪输入（DAE），都在带同样噪声的测试图上评估。

    Real cameras are noisy. The denoising autoencoder (DAE) changes one thing: the input is replaced by a corrupted version \(\hl{noise}{\tilde{\mathbf x}}=\mathbf x+\sigma\boldsymbol\varepsilon\) while the target stays the clean \(\mathbf x\). The network can no longer "copy the input"; it has to learn to push points that stray from the data manifold back onto it. The two networks below have identical architectures (144→32→144, no narrow bottleneck) and train side by side: one on clean inputs (a plain AE), one on noisy inputs (the DAE); both are evaluated on test images carrying the same noise.

    At the default σ = 0.3, doing nothing costs 0.091; after 1000 steps the plain AE is at 0.014 and the DAE at 0.0043, about a third of it. The plain AE removes plenty of noise too, because a 32-unit hidden layer is a constraint in itself; but it has never seen noise, and the parts of the noise that "look like a disc edge" pass straight through. The price is that the DAE is slightly worse on clean inputs (about 0.0012 against the plain AE's 0.0003): what it learned is "the input is probably noisy, pull it toward the data", and it pulls a clean image a little too. The larger σ, the larger the gap: at σ = 0.5, after 2000 steps, about 0.040 against 0.010.

    核心洞见Key insight

    去噪不需要窄瓶颈：“从破坏中恢复”这个任务本身就逼网络学到数据的结构。DAE 的输出减去输入，指向数据更密集的方向（第 5 节：它正比于加噪后数据密度的对数梯度，即 score）。把这个想法推到底，在很多个噪声水平上训练去噪器，再一步步去噪，就是扩散模型（19.6 节）。

    Denoising needs no narrow bottleneck: the task "recover from corruption" itself forces the network to learn the structure of the data. The DAE's output minus its input points toward denser data (Section 5: it is proportional to the gradient of the log density of the noised data, the score). Push the idea all the way, training a denoiser at many noise levels and then denoising step by step, and you have a diffusion model (Lesson 19.6).

    目标函数与梯度Objective and gradient

    给定数据 \(\mathbf x_1,\dots,\mathbf x_N\in\R^D\)，编码器 \(f_\phi:\R^D\to\R^k\)，解码器 \(g_\theta:\R^k\to\R^D\)，训练最小化重建损失

    Given data \(\mathbf x_1,\dots,\mathbf x_N\in\R^D\), an encoder \(f_\phi:\R^D\to\R^k\) and a decoder \(g_\theta:\R^k\to\R^D\), training minimizes the reconstruction loss

    $$ \mathcal L(\phi,\theta)=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^{N}\ell\big(\mathbf x_i,\ \hl{rec}{\hat{\mathbf x}_i}\big),\qquad \hat{\mathbf x}_i=\hl{dec}{g_\theta}\big(\hl{enc}{f_\phi}(\mathbf x_i)\big),\qquad \ell(\mathbf x,\hat{\mathbf x})=\frac1D\|\mathbf x-\hat{\mathbf x}\|^2 $$

    本页所有误差都是这个“每个分量的均方误差”。选平方误差等价于假设 \(\mathbf x=\hat{\mathbf x}+\)高斯噪声，再做最大似然（4.6 节）；像素在 0 到 1 之间、接近二值的图像，也常用逐像素的二元交叉熵（伯努利似然）。梯度就是两段网络串起来的反向传播（16.3 节）：先把 \(\nabla_{\hat{\mathbf x}}\ell\) 反传过解码器，得到参数梯度和对编码的梯度 \(\partial\ell/\partial\mathbf z\)，再把 \(\partial\ell/\partial\mathbf z\) 反传过编码器：

    Every error on this page is this "mean squared error per component". Choosing the squared error amounts to assuming \(\mathbf x=\hat{\mathbf x}+\) Gaussian noise and doing maximum likelihood (Lesson 4.6); for images with pixels in 0 to 1 that are nearly binary, a per-pixel binary cross-entropy (a Bernoulli likelihood) is also common. The gradient is backpropagation through the two networks chained together (Lesson 16.3): first push \(\nabla_{\hat{\mathbf x}}\ell\) back through the decoder, which gives its parameter gradients and the gradient with respect to the code, \(\partial\ell/\partial\mathbf z\); then push \(\partial\ell/\partial\mathbf z\) back through the encoder:

    $$ \frac{\partial\ell}{\partial\theta}=\Big(\frac{\partial\hat{\mathbf x}}{\partial\theta}\Big)^{\!\top}\nabla_{\hat{\mathbf x}}\ell,\qquad \frac{\partial\ell}{\partial\mathbf z}=\Big(\frac{\partial\hat{\mathbf x}}{\partial\mathbf z}\Big)^{\!\top}\nabla_{\hat{\mathbf x}}\ell,\qquad \frac{\partial\ell}{\partial\phi}=\Big(\frac{\partial\mathbf z}{\partial\phi}\Big)^{\!\top}\frac{\partial\ell}{\partial\mathbf z} $$

    每一项都是 16.3 节的向量–雅可比积，代价约为一次前向传播的几倍。页面里的代码正是这样写的：解码器的 backward 返回 \(\partial\ell/\partial\mathbf z\)，再交给编码器的 backward。

    Each term is a vector–Jacobian product of Lesson 16.3 and costs a small multiple of a forward pass. That is literally how the page's code is written: the decoder's backward returns \(\partial\ell/\partial\mathbf z\), which is handed to the encoder's backward.

    线性自编码器 = PCA 子空间Linear autoencoder = PCA subspace

    Let the data be centred (the optimal biases do exactly that) and stacked into a \(D\times N\) matrix \(X\) with SVD \(X=U\Sigma V^\top\) (Lesson 1.12); the first \(k\) columns of \(U\), \(\hl{pc}{U_k}\), are the first \(k\) principal directions. A linear autoencoder has \(\mathbf z=E\mathbf x\) and \(\hat{\mathbf x}=\hl{W}{W}\mathbf z\) with \(E\in\R^{k\times D}\) and \(W\in\R^{D\times k}\), and its total error is

    $$ \big\|X-\hl{W}{W}EX\big\|_F^2\ \ \ge\ \min_{\operatorname{rank}(Y)\le k}\|X-Y\|_F^2=\sum_{i>k}\sigma_i^2,\qquad WEX=\hl{pc}{U_k}U_k^\top X\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \big\|X-WEX\big\|_F^2=\sum_{i>k}\sigma_i^2 $$

    不等号成立是因为 \(WEX\) 的秩不超过 \(k\)，而右边是 Eckart–Young 定理（1.12 节）给出的最佳秩 \(k\) 近似误差；右边的等价关系在 \(\sigma_k>\sigma_{k+1}\) 时成立，因为这时最佳秩 \(k\) 近似唯一，就是 \(U_kU_k^\top X\)。所以全局最优处 \(W\) 的列空间就是 \(\operatorname{span}(U_k)\)，误差等于 PCA-\(k\) 的误差。但具体的 \(W\) 不唯一：对任意可逆的 \(k\times k\) 矩阵 \(A\)，

    The inequality holds because \(WEX\) has rank at most \(k\), and the right side is the best rank-\(k\) approximation error of the Eckart–Young theorem (Lesson 1.12); the equivalence on the right holds when \(\sigma_k>\sigma_{k+1}\), because then the best rank-\(k\) approximation is unique, namely \(U_kU_k^\top X\). So at the global optimum the column space of \(W\) is \(\operatorname{span}(U_k)\) and the error equals PCA-\(k\)'s. But the particular \(W\) is not unique: for any invertible \(k\times k\) matrix \(A\),

    $$ W=U_kA,\quad E=A^{-1}U_k^\top\ \ \Longrightarrow\ \ WE=U_kU_k^\top $$

    gives the same reconstruction. FIG 3's "code ×2" is \(A=\tfrac12\) (with \(k=1\)), so the \(k\) coordinates of the code are in general some mixture of principal components rather than the components themselves. To recover the principal directions, run PCA once more on the learned \(k\)-dimensional codes (only \(k\times k\), cheap). Baldi and Hornik (1989) proved even more: this loss has no bad local minima, and every critical point other than the global optimum is a saddle, which is why FIG 3 converges from any start. With nonlinearities this guarantee is gone (FIG 1's plateaus, FIG 2's creases).

    去噪自编码器学的是 scoreA denoising autoencoder learns the score

    DAE 最小化 \(\mathbb E_{\mathbf x,\boldsymbol\varepsilon}\|\mathbf x-r(\hl{noise}{\tilde{\mathbf x}})\|^2\)，\(r=g_\theta\circ f_\phi\)，\(\tilde{\mathbf x}=\mathbf x+\sigma\boldsymbol\varepsilon\)，\(\boldsymbol\varepsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,I)\)。对每个 \(\tilde{\mathbf x}\) 单独看，平方误差的最优解是条件均值（4.6 节的 MMSE 估计）；对高斯破坏，Tweedie 公式把它写成：

    The DAE minimizes \(\mathbb E_{\mathbf x,\boldsymbol\varepsilon}\|\mathbf x-r(\hl{noise}{\tilde{\mathbf x}})\|^2\) with \(r=g_\theta\circ f_\phi\), \(\tilde{\mathbf x}=\mathbf x+\sigma\boldsymbol\varepsilon\) and \(\boldsymbol\varepsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,I)\). Taken one \(\tilde{\mathbf x}\) at a time, the minimizer of a squared error is the conditional mean (the MMSE estimate); for Gaussian corruption Tweedie's formula writes it as

    $$ r^\star(\tilde{\mathbf x})=\mathbb E[\mathbf x\mid\tilde{\mathbf x}]=\tilde{\mathbf x}+\sigma^2\,\nabla_{\tilde{\mathbf x}}\log p_\sigma(\tilde{\mathbf x}),\qquad p_\sigma=p*\mathcal N(0,\sigma^2I) $$

    其中 \(p\) 是数据的密度，\(p_\sigma\) 是它与噪声卷积后（加噪数据）的密度。所以足够大的 DAE 输出减去输入，是 \(\sigma^2\) 乘以加噪数据密度的 score：一个指向数据更密处的向量场。Vincent（2011）把这一点和 score matching 联系起来，Alain 与 Bengio（2014）分析了 \(\sigma\to0\) 的极限。这正是 19.6 节扩散模型训练的东西，只是那里同时在许多个 \(\sigma\) 上训练。

    Here \(p\) is the data density and \(p_\sigma\) its convolution with the noise (the density of the noised data). So for a large enough DAE, output minus input is \(\sigma^2\) times the score of the noised data density: a vector field pointing toward denser data. Vincent (2011) connected this to score matching, and Alain and Bengio (2014) analysed the \(\sigma\to0\) limit. It is exactly what the diffusion models of Lesson 19.6 train, only there at many values of \(\sigma\) at once.

    稀疏与收缩自编码器Sparse and contractive autoencoders

    除了把编码变窄，还可以给编码加别的约束，这时隐层甚至可以比输入更宽（过完备）。稀疏自编码器惩罚隐层激活 \(\mathbf h=f_\phi(\mathbf x)\)，让每个输入只激活少数单元：

    Instead of narrowing the code one can constrain it in other ways, and then the hidden layer may even be wider than the input (overcomplete). A sparse autoencoder penalizes the hidden activations \(\mathbf h=f_\phi(\mathbf x)\) so that each input switches on only a few units:

    $$ \mathcal L'=\mathcal L+\beta\,\frac1N\sum_i\|\mathbf h_i\|_1,\qquad \mathcal L''=\mathcal L+\beta\sum_j\operatorname{KL}\big(\rho\,\|\,\hat\rho_j\big),\ \ \hat\rho_j=\frac1N\sum_i h_{ij}\in(0,1) $$

    第一种是 L1 惩罚；第二种形式 \(\mathcal L''\)（sigmoid 隐层，把每个单元的平均激活 \(\hat\rho_j\) 拉向一个小目标 \(\rho\)，如 0.05）来自 Ng 的讲义。在自然图像小块上训练，稀疏编码的单元往往变成局部的边缘检测器。收缩自编码器（Rifai 等，2011）惩罚编码器的雅可比 \(\lambda\|\partial f_\phi/\partial\mathbf x\|_F^2\)，让编码对偏离流形的微小扰动不敏感，和 DAE 的效果相近。

    The first is an L1 penalty; the second form, \(\mathcal L''\) (sigmoid hidden units, pulling each unit's mean activation \(\hat\rho_j\) toward a small target \(\rho\) such as 0.05), comes from Ng's lecture notes. Trained on natural image patches, the units of a sparse code tend to become localized edge detectors. A contractive autoencoder (Rifai et al., 2011) penalizes the encoder's Jacobian, \(\lambda\|\partial f_\phi/\partial\mathbf x\|_F^2\), making the code insensitive to small perturbations off the manifold, with an effect similar to the DAE's.

    为什么普通自编码器不能生成Why a plain autoencoder cannot generate

    要生成，需要一个能采样的分布 \(p(\mathbf z)\)，并且解码器在它的大部分质量上都被训练过。普通 AE 的训练只碰到 \(N\) 个点 \(f_\phi(\mathbf x_i)\)，“编码的分布”就是这些点的经验分布，形状由优化随意决定（FIG 4 的空隙、折痕、薄片）。损失里没有任何一项惩罚“空隙处解码成什么”。VAE 的两处改动正对着这两个问题：编码器输出一个分布 \(q_\phi(\mathbf z\mid\mathbf x)\) 而不是一个点，使解码器在每个编码附近的一整团区域上都被训练；再加一项 \(\operatorname{KL}(q_\phi(\mathbf z\mid\mathbf x)\,\|\,\mathcal N(0,I))\)，让所有编码合起来铺满标准正态，于是可以从 \(\mathcal N(0,I)\) 采样（19.1 节推导 ELBO）。

    Generating requires a distribution \(p(\mathbf z)\) we can sample from, with the decoder trained on most of its mass. A plain AE only ever touches the \(N\) points \(f_\phi(\mathbf x_i)\); "the code distribution" is their empirical distribution, with a shape the optimizer chose arbitrarily (FIG 4's gaps, creases and sheets). Nothing in the loss penalizes what the gaps decode to. The VAE's two changes address exactly these problems: the encoder outputs a distribution \(q_\phi(\mathbf z\mid\mathbf x)\) instead of a point, so the decoder is trained on a whole blob around each code; and a term \(\operatorname{KL}(q_\phi(\mathbf z\mid\mathbf x)\,\|\,\mathcal N(0,I))\) makes all codes together fill a standard normal, so that one can sample from \(\mathcal N(0,I)\) (Lesson 19.1 derives the ELBO).

    真实系统Real systems

    从像素做强化学习：Lange 与 Riedmiller（2010）先用深度自编码器把原始图像压成很短的编码，再在编码上做批量强化学习，是这条路线的早期工作。Finn 等（2016）的 deep spatial autoencoder 在编码层用 spatial softmax 输出一组二维“特征点”坐标，让 PR2 机器人从图像学习视觉运动技能，特征点比一般的编码更像物体位置。SAC+AE（Yarats 等，2021）在 DeepMind Control 的像素任务上，把自编码器的重建损失作为辅助任务和 SAC 一起训练，明显提高了样本效率。世界模型：Dreamer 一类方法（24.3 节）在学到的隐空间里预测未来，其中一部分用重建损失训练表示，TD-MPC 一类则不重建图像。异常检测：只在正常数据上训练的自编码器，对没见过的情况重建误差会变大，常用作简单的失败或分布外检测器。预训练视觉编码器：如今机器人更常用的是对比学习或掩码重建（MAE）预训练的编码器（18.3、18.4 节），MAE 本身就是一种“遮住大部分再重建”的自编码器。

The deep spatial autoencoder of Finn et al. (2016) uses a spatial softmax at the code layer to output a set of 2D "feature point" coordinates, with which a PR2 robot learned visuomotor skills from images; feature points behave more like object positions than an ordinary code does. SAC+AE (Yarats et al., 2021) trains an autoencoder's reconstruction loss as an auxiliary task together with SAC on DeepMind Control tasks from pixels and clearly improves sample efficiency. World models: methods of the Dreamer family (Lesson 24.3) predict the future in a learned latent space, partly trained with a reconstruction loss, while the TD-MPC family does not reconstruct images. Anomaly detection: an autoencoder trained only on normal data reconstructs unseen situations worse, and its reconstruction error is a common simple failure or out-of-distribution detector. Pretrained visual encoders: today robots more often use encoders pretrained by contrastive learning or masked reconstruction (MAE) (Lessons 18.3 and 18.4); MAE is itself an autoencoder that hides most of the input and rebuilds it.

    7常见误区Common pitfalls

    误区 1：重建误差低，编码就好用Pitfall 1: low reconstruction error means a useful code

    FIG 6 里两个自编码器的重建误差差不多，一个能伺服，一个卡在跳变处。重建误差只说明信息在编码里，不说明它以光滑、可比较的形式存在。还有一个机器人常见的坑：均方误差对每个像素一视同仁，图像里只占几个像素的小物体（夹爪尖、要抓的螺丝）对损失几乎没有贡献，自编码器可以把它们糊掉而误差几乎不变，可它们恰恰是任务关键。检查编码时，要用下游任务（伺服、回归位置、策略效果）来评价，而不只看重建。

    In FIG 6 the two autoencoders reconstruct about equally well; one servoes, the other gets stuck at a jump. Reconstruction error only says the information is in the code, not that it is there in a smooth, comparable form. Another common trap on robots: the mean squared error treats every pixel alike, so a small object covering a few pixels (the gripper tip, the screw to grasp) barely contributes to the loss, and the autoencoder can blur it away at almost no cost, although it is exactly what the task needs. Judge a code by the downstream task (servoing, regressing positions, policy performance), not by reconstruction alone.

    误区 2：瓶颈宽度随便选Pitfall 2: picking the bottleneck width carelessly

    太窄（FIG 2 的 \(K=1\)，或 FIG 1 用一个数编码一个圆）会迫使网络折叠或剪开数据；太宽且没有别的约束，网络可以学成近似恒等映射，连噪声一起照抄。FIG 2 还显示，恰好等于自由度的宽度（\(K=2\)）不一定最好训：\(K=4\) 的误差低一个数量级。实践中先扫几个宽度，并配合去噪或稀疏这类正则，而不是只靠窄瓶颈。

    Too narrow (\(K=1\) in FIG 2, or one number for a circle in FIG 1) forces the network to fold or cut the data; too wide with no other constraint and the network can learn something close to the identity, copying the noise along. FIG 2 also shows that a width exactly equal to the number of degrees of freedom (\(K=2\)) is not necessarily the easiest to train: \(K=4\) is an order of magnitude lower. In practice sweep a few widths and combine them with regularizers such as denoising or sparsity rather than relying on a narrow bottleneck alone.

    误区 3：把普通自编码器当生成模型Pitfall 3: using a plain autoencoder as a generative model

    “训练好之后随机取一个编码解码”看起来可行，FIG 4 说明它不可靠：\(K=8\) 时重建近乎完美，随机编码却只有三分之一多一点解码成圆盘。编码之间做线性插值也有同样的问题，插值路径可能穿过空隙或折痕。需要采样或插值时，用 VAE（19.1）、扩散模型（19.6）或流匹配（19.7）这类真正建模了分布的方法。

    "Train it, then decode a random code" looks plausible, and FIG 4 shows it is unreliable: with \(K=8\) reconstructions are near perfect, yet only a little over a third of random codes decode to a disc. Linear interpolation between codes has the same problem, since the path can cross gaps or creases. When you need sampling or interpolation, use a method that actually models the distribution, such as a VAE (19.1), a diffusion model (19.6) or flow matching (19.7).

    误区 4：以为编码空间里的距离有意义Pitfall 4: assuming distances in the code space mean something

    FIG 1 的闭合圆上，剪口两侧相隔几厘米的点编码相差最大；FIG 6 的普通 AE 在某处跳变。自编码器的损失不包含任何关于“相近的输入应有相近的编码”的项，距离、方向、尺度都没有保证（FIG 3 连线性情形的尺度都是任意的）。需要可比较的编码时，要显式地加约束：慢变或时间相关的先验（FIG 6）、对比学习（18.3）的正负样本、或 VAE 的 KL 项。

    On FIG 1's closed circle, points a few centimetres apart on either side of the cut get the most different codes; FIG 6's plain AE jumps somewhere. The autoencoder's loss has no term saying "nearby inputs should get nearby codes", so distances, directions and scales are not guaranteed (in FIG 3 even the linear case has an arbitrary scale). When codes must be comparable, add the constraint explicitly: a slowness or temporal prior (FIG 6), the positive and negative pairs of contrastive learning (18.3), or the VAE's KL term.

    误区 5：跳过 PCA 基线，或把线性 AE 的权重当主成分Pitfall 5: skipping the PCA baseline, or reading a linear AE's weights as principal components

    PCA 一行代码、没有超参数、结果唯一，应该总是先算它：如果 PCA 用同样维数已经够好，就不需要自编码器（比如手部关节的协同几乎是线性的）。反过来，线性自编码器只学到子空间，它的 \(W\) 的列既不正交也不按方差排序（第 5 节的 \(A\)），不能直接当主成分来解读。另外别忘了输入归一化：不同单位的分量（米和弧度、像素和力）不缩放，平方误差就会被数值最大的那个分量主导，PCA 和 AE 都是如此。

    PCA is one line of code, has no hyperparameters and a unique answer, so always compute it first: if PCA at the same dimension is already good enough you do not need an autoencoder (hand joint synergies, for instance, are nearly linear). Conversely, a linear autoencoder learns only the subspace; the columns of its \(W\) are neither orthogonal nor ordered by variance (the \(A\) of Section 5), so do not read them as principal components. And do not forget to normalize the inputs: if components in different units (metres and radians, pixels and forces) are not rescaled, the squared error is dominated by whichever is numerically largest, for PCA and AE alike.
