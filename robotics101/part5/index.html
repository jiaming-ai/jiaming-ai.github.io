PART V

机器学习基础Machine Learning Foundations

六章、29 节，备齐现代机器人学习要用的整套工具：从数据中学习与网络架构讲怎样拟合、怎样求梯度、该搭什么样的网络，从逻辑回归走到 Transformer 和动作 token；自监督学习讲没有标签时怎样学到好的表示；生成模型讲怎样学会整个分布，从 VAE 一路走到扩散与 flow matching，也就是 Diffusion Policy 和 π0 的引擎；强化学习讲怎样从奖励里学策略，从 Bellman 方程走到 PPO、SAC 与离线 RL；专题讲微调、scaling law、不确定性与多任务。每一节都有在浏览器里现场运行的实验，大多是现场训练的小网络，例子都来自机器人。 Six chapters and 29 lessons that assemble the toolbox of modern robot learning: learning from data and network architectures cover how to fit, how to get gradients and which network to build, from logistic regression to transformers and action tokens; self-supervised learning covers learning good representations without labels; generative models cover learning a whole distribution, from VAEs to diffusion and flow matching, the engines of Diffusion Policy and π0; reinforcement learning covers learning a policy from rewards, from the Bellman equations to PPO, SAC and offline RL; special topics cover fine-tuning, scaling laws, uncertainty and multi-task learning. Every lesson has labs that run live in your browser, most of them small networks trained on the spot, on robot examples.

Chapter 16 is the foundation of the others: losses, generalization and backpropagation come back again and again in the later chapters. Architectures (chapter 17) feed self-supervised learning (chapter 18) and generative models (chapter 19); reinforcement learning (chapter 20) rests mainly on Part I's probability and dynamic programming, and its inverse-RL lesson borrows the discriminator of a GAN. From earlier parts come mainly Part I's linear algebra, calculus, probability and optimization, plus configuration space from Part II and the state-space model from Part IV.
