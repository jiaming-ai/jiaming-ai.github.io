Flow Matching（流匹配）Flow Matching

让每一粒噪声沿一条直线匀速走向一个数据点，再训练一个神经网络去学“经过这里的所有直线的平均速度”：这就是 flow matching。训练只是一个普通的回归，不需要积分 ODE，也不需要噪声调度表；生成时从高斯噪声出发，沿学到的速度场积分几步就得到样本。本节从 19.3 节的连续 flow 和 19.6 节的扩散模型出发，先看概率路径和“取平均”这个核心技巧，在浏览器里现场训练一个速度场，再看为什么步数少了会糊、怎样用最优传输和 reflow 把路径拉直，最后搭一个 π0 风格的 flow matching 动作头，让它生成能绕开障碍物的多模态动作块。 Let every grain of noise travel at constant speed along a straight line to a data point, then train a neural network to learn "the average velocity of all the lines passing through here": that is flow matching. Training is plain regression, with no ODE to integrate and no noise schedule to tune; generation starts from Gaussian noise and integrates the learned velocity field for a few steps. Building on the continuous flows of Lesson 19.3 and the diffusion models of Lesson 19.6, this lesson looks at probability paths and the central "take the average" trick, trains a velocity field live in your browser, shows why too few steps blur the samples and how optimal transport and reflow straighten the paths, and ends with a π0-style flow-matching action head that generates multimodal action chunks around an obstacle.

    1为什么机器人需要它Why robots need this

    机器人策略要输出的往往不是“一个动作”，而是“动作的分布”。同一个场景下，示教者有时从左边绕开障碍，有时从右边；同一个杯子可以从好几个方向抓。19.1 节已经看到，用均方误差回归动作会学到两个模式的平均，正好撞上障碍物。所以我们需要一个能表达多峰分布、又能快速采样的生成模型。

    19.6 节的扩散模型能做到前一半：它把数据一步步加噪成高斯，再学习如何一步步去噪，样本质量很好，Diffusion Policy（22.2 节）就是这样生成动作的。但机器人有一个很硬的约束：时间。一个以 50 Hz 运行的控制器，每 20 ms 就要给出下一个动作；如果采样一次要几十到上百次网络前向，就很难赶上。19.3 节的连续 flow 是另一条路：沿一个向量场积分 ODE 就能采样，可是按最大似然训练它，每一步训练都要把 ODE 积分一遍。

    Flow matching（Lipman 等，2023；同期的 rectified flow 与 stochastic interpolants 是同一类想法）把两者的优点拼在一起：它学的也是一个把噪声“流”成数据的速度场 \(\mathbf v_\theta(\mathbf x,t)\)，但训练时不积分，只是对一个随手就能写出来的目标做回归；而且这个目标来自直线路径，所以通常几步积分就够了。π0 这类视觉-语言-动作模型的动作头用的就是它：每次推理只积分 10 步，就生成一整段 50 步的动作块。

    What a robot policy must output is often not "an action" but "a distribution over actions". In the same scene a demonstrator sometimes passes an obstacle on the left and sometimes on the right; the same cup can be grasped from several directions. Lesson 19.1 already showed that regressing actions with a mean squared error learns the average of the two modes, which drives straight into the obstacle. So we need a generative model that can represent multi-peaked distributions and can also sample fast.

    The diffusion models of Lesson 19.6 do the first half: they add noise to the data step by step until it is Gaussian and learn to remove it step by step; sample quality is excellent, and Diffusion Policy (Lesson 22.2) generates actions exactly this way. But robots face a hard constraint: time. A controller running at 50 Hz must produce its next action every 20 ms, and a sampler that needs tens to hundreds of network passes struggles to keep up. The continuous flows of Lesson 19.3 are another road: sampling means integrating an ODE along a vector field, but training them by maximum likelihood integrates the ODE at every training step.

    Flow matching (Lipman et al., 2023; the concurrent rectified flow and stochastic interpolants are the same family of ideas) combines the strengths of both. It also learns a velocity field \(\mathbf v_\theta(\mathbf x,t)\) that "flows" noise into data, but training never integrates anything: it is regression onto a target you can write down directly. And because that target comes from straight paths, a few integration steps are usually enough. The action heads of vision-language-action models such as π0 use exactly this: each inference integrates only 10 steps and produces a whole chunk of 50 future actions.

    2直觉：直线连起来，速度取平均Intuition: join with straight lines, average the velocities

    3.6 节把向量场想成一条河：撒一把沙子，每粒沙子顺着水流漂。现在我们想设计一条河，让 \(t=0\) 时撒下的一团高斯沙子 \(p_0=\mathcal N(\mathbf 0,I)\)，到 \(t=1\) 时恰好堆成数据的形状 \(q\)。最笨也最直接的办法：随机配对，每粒噪声 \(\mathbf x_0\sim p_0\) 配一个数据点 \(\mathbf x_1\sim q\)，让它沿直线匀速走过去：

    Lesson 3.6 pictured a vector field as a river: sprinkle sand and each grain drifts with the current. Now we want to design a river so that a Gaussian cloud of sand sprinkled at \(t=0\), \(p_0=\mathcal N(\mathbf 0,I)\), has piled up into the shape of the data \(q\) by \(t=1\). The bluntest, most direct recipe: pair at random, give each grain of noise \(\mathbf x_0\sim p_0\) a data point \(\mathbf x_1\sim q\), and let it walk there along a straight line at constant speed:

    $$ \mathbf x_t=(1-t)\,\mathbf x_0+t\,\mathbf x_1,\qquad \frac{d\mathbf x_t}{dt}=\hl{cond}{\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_0},\qquad t\in[0,1]. $$
    时间方向的约定（与 19.6 节相反）Time convention (the reverse of Lesson 19.6)

    本节沿用 Lipman 等和 π0 的写法：时间从噪声走向数据，\(t=0\) 是噪声，\(t=1\) 是数据；\(\mathbf x_0\) 是噪声，\(\mathbf x_1\) 是数据，生成时从 \(t=0\) 往前积分到 \(t=1\)。19.6 节的 DDPM 正好相反：\(t\) 数的是加噪步数，\(t=0\) 是数据 \(\mathbf x_0\)，\(t=T\) 是噪声，生成时 \(t\) 倒着走。对照表：本节的 \(\mathbf x_0\) 对应 19.6 节的噪声 \(\boldsymbol\varepsilon\)（约等于 \(\mathbf x_T\)），本节的 \(\mathbf x_1\) 对应 19.6 节的 \(\mathbf x_0\)，本节的 \(t\) 大致对应 19.6 节的 \(1-t/T\)。

    This lesson follows Lipman et al. and π0: time runs from noise to data, \(t=0\) is the noise and \(t=1\) the data; \(\mathbf x_0\) is the noise, \(\mathbf x_1\) the data, and generation integrates forwards from \(t=0\) to \(t=1\). The DDPM of Lesson 19.6 is the other way round: \(t\) counts noising steps, \(t=0\) is the data \(\mathbf x_0\), \(t=T\) the noise, and generation runs \(t\) backwards. To translate: this lesson's \(\mathbf x_0\) is 19.6's noise \(\boldsymbol\varepsilon\) (roughly \(\mathbf x_T\)), this lesson's \(\mathbf x_1\) is 19.6's \(\mathbf x_0\), and this lesson's \(t\) corresponds roughly to 19.6's \(1-t/T\).

    在每个时刻 \(t\)，所有沙粒的位置构成一个分布 \(\hl{pt}{p_t}\)，从 \(p_0\)（高斯）连续地变到 \(p_1=q\)（数据）。这一串分布叫概率路径。问题在于：这些直线会交叉。两条直线在同一个时刻穿过同一点，却要朝不同方向走，而一个向量场在一个点只能给出一个速度。解决办法就是本节的核心：在每个点 \((\mathbf x,t)\)，取所有经过这里的直线速度的平均，

    At each time \(t\) the positions of all grains form a distribution \(\hl{pt}{p_t}\) that moves continuously from \(p_0\) (the Gaussian) to \(p_1=q\) (the data). This family of distributions is called a probability path. The trouble is that the lines cross. Two lines pass through the same point at the same time heading in different directions, yet a vector field can give only one velocity per point. The fix is the heart of this lesson: at every point \((\mathbf x,t)\), take the average of the velocities of all lines that pass through it,

    $$ \hl{marg}{\mathbf u_t(\mathbf x)}=\mathbb E\big[\,\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_0\;\big|\;\mathbf x_t=\mathbf x\,\big]=\int \frac{\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x}{1-t}\;\hl{post}{p(\mathbf x_1\mid \mathbf x_t=\mathbf x)}\;d\mathbf x_1 . $$

    第二个等号用了 \(\mathbf x_0=(\mathbf x-t\mathbf x_1)/(1-t)\)：知道了现在的位置和终点，起点就确定了。\(p(\mathbf x_1\mid\mathbf x_t=\mathbf x)\) 是“一粒此刻在 \(\mathbf x\) 的沙子，终点是哪里”的后验。一个不显然、却是本节最重要的事实是：沿这个平均速度场漂流，整团沙子在每个时刻的分布仍然恰好是 \(p_t\)（第 5 节证明）。单粒沙子的路线变了：它们不再走直线，而是走一条弯曲、互不交叉的曲线，但整体的“人群密度”与直线配对时一模一样。下图在一维里把两者画在一起。横轴是时间，纵轴是位置。

    The second equality uses \(\mathbf x_0=(\mathbf x-t\mathbf x_1)/(1-t)\): once you know where a grain is now and where it ends, its start is fixed. \(p(\mathbf x_1\mid\mathbf x_t=\mathbf x)\) is the posterior "where does a grain that is at \(\mathbf x\) right now end up". The non-obvious fact, and the most important one of this lesson, is this: drifting along this averaged field moves the whole cloud so that its distribution at every time is still exactly \(p_t\) (proved in Section 5). Individual grains take different routes: no longer straight lines but curved paths that never cross, yet the overall "crowd density" is identical to that of the straight-line pairing. The figure puts both side by side in one dimension: time runs horizontally, position vertically.

    核心洞见Key insight

    平均速度 \(\mathbf u_t(\mathbf x)\) 没法直接算，因为后验 \(p(\mathbf x_1\mid\mathbf x_t)\) 要对整个数据集求和。但我们不需要算它：16.2 节说过，用平方误差回归一个带噪声的目标，学到的是目标的条件均值。所以只要随机抽 \((\mathbf x_0,\mathbf x_1,t)\)，让网络在 \(\mathbf x_t\) 处回归那条直线的速度 \(\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_0\)，它学到的自然就是 \(\mathbb E[\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_0\mid\mathbf x_t]=\mathbf u_t\)。每个训练目标都“错”（它只是一条直线的速度），平均起来却恰好对。这就是 flow matching 的全部秘密。

    The averaged velocity \(\mathbf u_t(\mathbf x)\) cannot be computed directly, because the posterior \(p(\mathbf x_1\mid\mathbf x_t)\) sums over the whole dataset. But we never need to compute it: as Lesson 16.2 showed, regressing a noisy target with a squared error learns the target's conditional mean. So draw \((\mathbf x_0,\mathbf x_1,t)\) at random and let the network, at \(\mathbf x_t\), regress the velocity of that one line, \(\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_0\); what it learns is automatically \(\mathbb E[\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_0\mid\mathbf x_t]=\mathbf u_t\). Every single training target is "wrong" (it is only one line's velocity), yet on average they are exactly right. That is the whole secret of flow matching.

    Write that recipe down as code and you have the training loop of conditional flow matching (CFM). At each step, draw a batch of noise \(\mathbf x_0\sim\mathcal N(\mathbf 0,I)\), a batch of data \(\mathbf x_1\sim q\) and times \(t\sim\mathcal U[0,1]\), form \(\mathbf x_t\), let the network predict a velocity there, and compare it with the line's velocity \(\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_0\) in squared error:

    $$ \hl{loss}{\mathcal L_{\mathrm{CFM}}(\theta)}=\mathbb E_{t,\,\mathbf x_0,\,\mathbf x_1}\Big\|\,\hl{vth}{\mathbf v_\theta\big((1-t)\mathbf x_0+t\mathbf x_1,\;t\big)}-(\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_0)\Big\|^2 $$

    没有 ODE、没有雅可比行列式、没有噪声调度表：就是一个回归问题，梯度用 16.3 节的反向传播。下图的网络是一个 \([5,48,48,2]\) 的 GELU MLP，输入是 \((x,y,t,\sin\pi t,\cos\pi t)\)，共 2738 个参数；优化器是 Adam（学习率 0.003，2000 步后降到 0.001），每步 64 个样本。数据是二维的高斯混合，这样页面能算出精确的平均速度场 \(\mathbf u_t\)（第 5 节给出公式），于是我们可以直接量出网络离理想答案还有多远：

    No ODE, no Jacobian determinant, no noise schedule: just a regression problem, with gradients from the backpropagation of Lesson 16.3. The network in the figure is a \([5,48,48,2]\) GELU MLP whose input is \((x,y,t,\sin\pi t,\cos\pi t)\), 2738 parameters in all; the optimizer is Adam (learning rate 0.003, lowered to 0.001 after 2000 steps) on batches of 64. The data are 2-D Gaussian mixtures, so the page can compute the exact averaged field \(\mathbf u_t\) (the formula is in Section 5) and measure directly how far the network still is from the ideal answer:

    $$ \mathcal L_{\mathrm{CFM}}(\theta)=\underbrace{\mathbb E\,\big\|\mathbf v_\theta(\mathbf x_t,t)-\mathbf u_t(\mathbf x_t)\big\|^2}_{\hl{ferr}{\mathcal E(\theta)}}+\underbrace{\mathbb E\,\big\|\mathbf u_t(\mathbf x_t)-(\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_0)\big\|^2}_{\hl{floor}{\mathcal L^{*}}} $$

    第一项是场误差 \(\mathcal E(\theta)\)，训练能把它压向零；第二项 \(\mathcal L^*\) 与 \(\theta\) 无关，是直线速度围绕平均值的散布：同一点上直线们彼此越不一致，它越大。所以 CFM 的损失永远不会降到零，就像 16.2 节验证损失有一个噪声地板一样。采样时从 500 个固定的高斯样本出发，用 Euler 法积分 \(\mathbf x\leftarrow\mathbf x+h\,\mathbf v_\theta(\mathbf x,t)\)，\(h=1/N\)（2.5、3.6 节）。

    The first term is the field error \(\mathcal E(\theta)\), which training can push towards zero; the second, \(\mathcal L^*\), does not depend on \(\theta\): it is the scatter of the line velocities around their average, larger wherever the lines through a point disagree more. So the CFM loss never reaches zero, just as the validation loss in Lesson 16.2 has a noise floor. Sampling starts from 500 fixed Gaussian samples and integrates \(\mathbf x\leftarrow\mathbf x+h\,\mathbf v_\theta(\mathbf x,t)\) with Euler's method, \(h=1/N\) (Lessons 2.5 and 3.6).

    少步采样：步数、积分方法和路径的形状Few-step sampling: step count, solver and the shape of the path

    训练好之后，生成一个样本就是解一个 ODE：\(\dot{\mathbf x}=\mathbf v_\theta(\mathbf x,t)\)，从 \(t=0\) 积到 \(t=1\)。每次网络前向（NFE，number of function evaluations）都要花时间，所以问题是：给定一个 NFE 预算，样本有多准？下图用精确的平均场（不是网络）把“学得好不好”和“积分得准不准”分开，只看后者。除了直线路径，也可以选一条余弦路径 \(\mathbf x_t=\sin(\tfrac{\pi t}{2})\mathbf x_1+\cos(\tfrac{\pi t}{2})\mathbf x_0\)：它的信号和噪声系数平方和为 1，与 19.6 节扩散模型常用的“方差保持”型路径属于同一类（第 5 节）；把时间反过来（\(t=1-\tau/T\)），它就是 19.6 节的余弦日程（略去那里的小偏移 \(s\)），\(\sin\tfrac{\pi t}{2}\approx\sqrt{\bar\alpha_\tau}\)。积分方法可选 Euler（每步 1 次 NFE，一阶）或中点法（每步 2 次 NFE，二阶，3.6 节 FIG 4 里的 Heun 也是二阶）。

    Once trained, generating a sample means solving an ODE, \(\dot{\mathbf x}=\mathbf v_\theta(\mathbf x,t)\), from \(t=0\) to \(t=1\). Every network pass (NFE, number of function evaluations) costs time, so the question is: for a given NFE budget, how accurate is the sample? The figure uses the exact averaged field (not a network) to separate "how well was it learned" from "how accurately is it integrated", and looks only at the latter. Besides the straight path you can pick a cosine path, \(\mathbf x_t=\sin(\tfrac{\pi t}{2})\mathbf x_1+\cos(\tfrac{\pi t}{2})\mathbf x_0\): its signal and noise coefficients have squares summing to 1, the same family as the "variance-preserving" paths common in the diffusion models of Lesson 19.6 (Section 5); with time reversed (\(t=1-\tau/T\)) it is 19.6's cosine schedule (dropping its small offset \(s\)), \(\sin\tfrac{\pi t}{2}\approx\sqrt{\bar\alpha_\tau}\). The solver is Euler (1 NFE per step, first order) or the midpoint method (2 NFE per step, second order; the Heun method of Lesson 3.6's FIG 4 is second order too).

    概率路径与连续性方程Probability paths and the continuity equation

    记 \(\mathbf z=(\mathbf x_0,\mathbf x_1)\) 为一对配对，按某个联合分布 \(\pi(\mathbf z)\) 抽取；独立配对时 \(\pi=p_0\otimes q\)。每一对给出一条条件路径：\(\mathbf x_t=\psi_t(\mathbf z)=(1-t)\mathbf x_0+t\mathbf x_1\)，速度 \(\hl{cond}{\mathbf u_t(\mathbf x\mid\mathbf z)=\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_0}\)。对所有配对平均，就得到边缘的概率路径 \(\hl{pt}{p_t}\) 和边缘速度场：

    Let \(\mathbf z=(\mathbf x_0,\mathbf x_1)\) be a pair drawn from some joint distribution \(\pi(\mathbf z)\); with independent pairing \(\pi=p_0\otimes q\). Each pair defines a conditional path \(\mathbf x_t=\psi_t(\mathbf z)=(1-t)\mathbf x_0+t\mathbf x_1\) with velocity \(\hl{cond}{\mathbf u_t(\mathbf x\mid\mathbf z)=\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_0}\). Averaging over all pairs gives the marginal probability path \(\hl{pt}{p_t}\) and the marginal velocity field:

    $$ p_t(\mathbf x)=\int p_t(\mathbf x\mid\mathbf z)\,\pi(\mathbf z)\,d\mathbf z,\qquad \hl{marg}{\mathbf u_t(\mathbf x)}=\int \mathbf u_t(\mathbf x\mid\mathbf z)\,\frac{p_t(\mathbf x\mid\mathbf z)\,\pi(\mathbf z)}{p_t(\mathbf x)}\,d\mathbf z=\mathbb E\big[\mathbf u_t(\mathbf x_t\mid\mathbf z)\,\big|\,\mathbf x_t=\mathbf x\big]. $$

    这里 \(p_t(\mathbf x\mid\mathbf z)\) 是集中在 \(\psi_t(\mathbf z)\) 一点上的分布；分式就是后验 \(p(\mathbf z\mid\mathbf x_t=\mathbf x)\)。一个速度场 \(\mathbf v\) “生成”一条概率路径，是指沿它漂流的样本在每个时刻的分布都是 \(p_t\)，等价地，二者满足连续性方程（质量守恒，3.6 节的散度在这里出现）：

    Here \(p_t(\mathbf x\mid\mathbf z)\) is the distribution concentrated on the single point \(\psi_t(\mathbf z)\), and the fraction is the posterior \(p(\mathbf z\mid\mathbf x_t=\mathbf x)\). A velocity field \(\mathbf v\) "generates" a probability path if samples drifting along it are distributed as \(p_t\) at every time; equivalently, the two satisfy the continuity equation (conservation of mass, where the divergence of Lesson 3.6 appears):

    $$ \frac{\partial p_t}{\partial t}+\nabla\!\cdot\big(p_t\,\mathbf v_t\big)=0\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad \frac{d}{dt}\log p_t\big(\mathbf x(t)\big)=-\nabla\!\cdot\mathbf v_t\big(\mathbf x(t)\big)\qquad\big(\dot{\mathbf x}=\mathbf v_t(\mathbf x)\big). $$

    右边那个形式沿着轨迹 \(\dot{\mathbf x}=\mathbf v_t(\mathbf x)\) 成立，它正是 19.3 节 FIG 5 上方的瞬时换元公式；把左式展开成 \(\partial_tp+\mathbf v\cdot\nabla p+p\,\nabla\cdot\mathbf v=0\)，前两项合起来就是沿轨迹的全导数。

    定理 1（边缘场生成边缘路径）。每条条件路径都满足自己的连续性方程 \(\partial_tp_t(\mathbf x\mid\mathbf z)+\nabla\cdot\big(p_t(\mathbf x\mid\mathbf z)\,\mathbf u_t(\mathbf x\mid\mathbf z)\big)=0\)（一个点沿直线匀速移动，当然质量守恒）。对 \(\pi(\mathbf z)\) 积分，对时间和空间的导数都可以拿到积分外面：

    The form on the right holds along trajectories \(\dot{\mathbf x}=\mathbf v_t(\mathbf x)\) and is exactly the instantaneous change of variables above FIG 5 of Lesson 19.3; expand the left form into \(\partial_tp+\mathbf v\cdot\nabla p+p\,\nabla\cdot\mathbf v=0\), and the first two terms together are the total derivative along a trajectory.

    Theorem 1 (the marginal field generates the marginal path). Each conditional path satisfies its own continuity equation, \(\partial_tp_t(\mathbf x\mid\mathbf z)+\nabla\cdot\big(p_t(\mathbf x\mid\mathbf z)\,\mathbf u_t(\mathbf x\mid\mathbf z)\big)=0\) (a point moving along a straight line at constant speed obviously conserves mass). Integrate over \(\pi(\mathbf z)\); the time and space derivatives come out of the integral:

    $$ \frac{\partial p_t(\mathbf x)}{\partial t}=-\nabla\!\cdot\!\int p_t(\mathbf x\mid\mathbf z)\,\mathbf u_t(\mathbf x\mid\mathbf z)\,\pi(\mathbf z)\,d\mathbf z=-\nabla\!\cdot\big(p_t(\mathbf x)\,\mathbf u_t(\mathbf x)\big), $$

    最后一步就是 \(\mathbf u_t\) 的定义（分子乘上 \(p_t\)）。所以 \(\mathbf u_t\) 满足 \(p_t\) 的连续性方程：从 \(p_0\) 出发沿 \(\mathbf u_t\) 积分，在 \(t=1\) 恰好得到 \(q\)。这就是 FIG 1 里紫色直线和蓝色曲线给出同一片底色的原因。

    and the last step is just the definition of \(\mathbf u_t\) (multiply its numerator by \(p_t\)). So \(\mathbf u_t\) satisfies the continuity equation of \(p_t\): start from \(p_0\), integrate along \(\mathbf u_t\), and at \(t=1\) you get exactly \(q\). That is why the purple lines and the blue curves of FIG 1 produce the same shading.

    The DDPM of Lesson 19.6 also uses a Gaussian path: its signal coefficient \(\alpha\) and noise coefficient \(\sigma\) satisfy \(\alpha^2+\sigma^2=1\) (variance preserving), only time runs the other way (DDPM's step 0 is the data; see the convention note in Section 2), and the network predicts the noise \(\boldsymbol\varepsilon\) (the \(\mathbf x_0\) of this lesson).     where every hat denotes a conditional mean given \(\mathbf x_t\), and \(\hat{\mathbf v}=\mathbf u_t(\mathbf x_t)\). The constant depends on how curved the trajectories are: if every trajectory is a straight line traversed at constant speed, \(\mathbf v\) is constant along it and a single Euler step is exact, \(\hl{euler}{\mathbf x_1=\mathbf x_0+\mathbf v_\theta(\mathbf x_0,0)}\). Which pairing achieves this? Benamou and Brenier (2000) showed that optimal transport with quadratic cost can be written as "the flow of least kinetic energy":

    $$ \min_{\pi}\ \mathbb E_{(\mathbf x_0,\mathbf x_1)\sim\pi}\|\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_0\|^2\;=\;\min_{p_t,\mathbf v_t\,:\ \partial_tp_t+\nabla\cdot(p_t\mathbf v_t)=0}\ \int_0^1\!\!\int p_t(\mathbf x)\,\|\mathbf v_t(\mathbf x)\|^2\,d\mathbf x\,dt . $$

    最优的流沿直线匀速搬运，粒子互不相撞。所以用最优传输配对再走直线，边缘轨迹就是直线（FIG 4 右边那些青色的配对线段 \(\hl{pair}{(\mathbf x_0,\mathbf x_1)}\) 短而几乎不交叉）。小批量 OT 只在每个批次内部求最优，是近似，批次越大越接近；reflow 用一个已有的 ODE 映射作配对，Liu 等证明这样做不会增加任何凸代价下的传输代价，并且反复 reflow 会让轨迹越来越直。代价是要多训练一次，并且配对来自模型本身，模型的误差会被带进新的数据里。

    The optimal flow carries mass along straight lines at constant speed, and particles never collide. So pair by optimal transport, walk straight lines, and the marginal trajectories are straight (the cyan pair segments \(\hl{pair}{(\mathbf x_0,\mathbf x_1)}\) on the right of FIG 4 are short and hardly cross). Minibatch OT optimizes only within each batch, an approximation that improves with batch size; reflow uses an existing ODE map as the pairing, which Liu et al. proved never increases the transport cost under any convex cost, and repeated reflow makes the trajectories straighter and straighter. The price is another round of training, and the pairs come from the model itself, so its errors are baked into the new data.

    条件生成、引导与似然Conditioning, guidance and likelihood

    条件生成：把条件 \(\mathbf c\)（文本、图像特征、机器人观测）作为网络的额外输入，\(\mathbf v_\theta(\mathbf x,t,\mathbf c)\)，训练时配对取自同一个 \(\mathbf c\) 下的数据，其余不变；第 6 节的动作头就是这样。无分类器引导（19.6 节）原样适用于速度：\(\tilde{\mathbf v}=\mathbf v_\theta(\mathbf x,t,\varnothing)+w\,\big(\mathbf v_\theta(\mathbf x,t,\mathbf c)-\mathbf v_\theta(\mathbf x,t,\varnothing)\big)\)。似然：训练好的流是一个连续 normalizing flow，19.3 节的公式 \(\log p_1(\mathbf x_1)=\log p_0(\mathbf x_0)-\int_0^1\nabla\cdot\mathbf v_\theta\,dt\) 照样成立，需要时可以倒着积分求密度。时间的抽样：本页用 \(t\sim\mathcal U[0,1]\)；Stable Diffusion 3（Esser 等，2024）用 logit-正态分布把训练集中在中间的 \(t\)，π0 用一个偏向噪声一端的 Beta 型分布。这只改变损失在不同时刻上的权重，不改变最优解 \(\mathbf u_t\)。

    Conditional generation: feed the condition \(\mathbf c\) (text, image features, robot observations) to the network as an extra input, \(\mathbf v_\theta(\mathbf x,t,\mathbf c)\), and draw each training pair from data under the same \(\mathbf c\); nothing else changes. The action head of Section 6 does exactly this. Classifier-free guidance (Lesson 19.6) carries over to velocities as is: \(\tilde{\mathbf v}=\mathbf v_\theta(\mathbf x,t,\varnothing)+w\,\big(\mathbf v_\theta(\mathbf x,t,\mathbf c)-\mathbf v_\theta(\mathbf x,t,\varnothing)\big)\). Likelihood: the trained flow is a continuous normalizing flow, so the formula of Lesson 19.3, \(\log p_1(\mathbf x_1)=\log p_0(\mathbf x_0)-\int_0^1\nabla\cdot\mathbf v_\theta\,dt\), still holds, and densities can be computed by integrating backwards when needed. Sampling the time: this page uses \(t\sim\mathcal U[0,1]\); Stable Diffusion 3 (Esser et al., 2024) uses a logit-normal distribution that concentrates training on intermediate \(t\), and π0 uses a Beta-shaped distribution that leans towards the noisy end. This only changes how the loss is weighted over time, not the optimum \(\mathbf u_t\).

    6机器人中的它：π0 风格的 flow matching 动作头In robotics: a π0-style flow-matching action head

    π0（Black 等，Physical Intelligence，2024）是一个视觉-语言-动作模型：一个预训练的视觉-语言模型（PaliGemma）读入多路相机图像、语言指令和机器人本体状态，旁边接一个约 3 亿参数的动作专家，用 flow matching 生成一整个动作块（未来 50 步的连续动作）。推理时从高斯噪声出发积分 10 步（步长 0.1）；观测部分的注意力键值只算一次并缓存，10 步里只重复运行较小的动作专家。这样它能以高达 50 Hz 的频率控制双臂等机器人完成叠衣服这类灵巧任务。与 17.5 节的离散动作 token 相比，这条路线直接输出连续动作，不需要动作 tokenizer。

    π0 (Black et al., Physical Intelligence, 2024) is a vision-language-action model: a pretrained vision-language model (PaliGemma) reads several camera images, a language instruction and the robot's proprioceptive state, and next to it sits an action expert of about 300 million parameters that generates a whole action chunk (the next 50 continuous actions) by flow matching. At inference it integrates 10 steps (step size 0.1) from Gaussian noise; the attention keys and values of the observation part are computed once and cached, so only the smaller action expert reruns during the 10 steps. This lets it control robots such as bimanual arms at up to 50 Hz in dexterous tasks like folding laundry. Compared with the discrete action tokens of Lesson 17.5, this route outputs continuous actions directly and needs no action tokenizer.

    真实系统里In real systems

    π0 与后续模型：π0 的动作专家与视觉-语言骨干共享 Transformer 的注意力，但有自己的权重，观测 token 不看动作 token（分块的注意力掩码，17.3 节）；它的后继 π0.5 保留了 flow matching 动作专家。NVIDIA 的 GR00T N1（2025）用一个以 flow matching 训练的扩散 Transformer 作为人形机器人的动作模块。 为什么是 flow matching：与 Diffusion Policy（Chi 等，2023，22.2 节）的 DDPM/DDIM 相比，它的训练目标更简单，没有噪声调度表要调，确定性的 ODE 采样用 10 步左右就够；但两者在数学上很接近（第 5 节），具体选哪个常常取决于工程习惯。 动作块：一次生成多步动作并按“滚动时域”执行（22.2 节），既摊薄了推理延迟，也让多模态动作在一个块内保持一致，不会在两个模式之间来回跳。 加速：需要更少步数时，可以用 reflow 或蒸馏，例如 Consistency Policy（Prasad 等，2024）把扩散策略蒸馏成一到几步的策略。 数据侧的约定：动作要按数据集统计量归一化到单位尺度，使高斯噪声和动作的尺度相当；部署时必须用同一套统计量反归一化（17.5 节对动作 token 也有同样的要求）。

    π0 and its successors: π0's action expert shares the transformer's attention with the vision-language backbone but has its own weights, and the observation tokens do not attend to the action tokens (a block-wise attention mask, Lesson 17.3); its successor π0.5 keeps a flow-matching action expert. NVIDIA's GR00T N1 (2025) uses a diffusion transformer trained with flow matching as the action module of a humanoid. Why flow matching: compared with the DDPM/DDIM of Diffusion Policy (Chi et al., 2023; Lesson 22.2), the training target is simpler, there is no noise schedule to tune, and deterministic ODE sampling needs only about 10 steps; but mathematically the two are close (Section 5), and the choice often comes down to engineering habit. Action chunks: generating several steps at once and executing them in a receding-horizon fashion (Lesson 22.2) both amortizes the inference latency and keeps a multimodal action consistent within a chunk, instead of jumping back and forth between modes. Speed-ups: when even fewer steps are needed, reflow or distillation help; Consistency Policy (Prasad et al., 2024), for example, distils a diffusion policy into one with one to a few steps. Data conventions: actions must be normalized to unit scale with dataset statistics so that the Gaussian noise and the actions have comparable scales, and deployment must de-normalize with exactly the same statistics (Lesson 17.5 asks the same of action tokens).

    7常见误区Common pitfalls

    误区 1：时间方向和符号的约定混用Pitfall 1: mixing up the conventions for time and sign

    本节（和 Lipman 等）约定 \(t=0\) 是噪声、\(t=1\) 是数据，目标是 \(\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_0\)；DDPM 的步数从数据（第 0 步）数到噪声，有的代码库把噪声放在 \(t=1\)，目标写成 \(\boldsymbol\varepsilon-\mathbf x\)。照着一篇论文的公式、用另一份代码的约定，就会朝错误的方向积分，样本一路跑出画面，或者训练目标和采样器的时间轴对不上。最便宜的检查：训练完后看 \(t\) 接近噪声一端时的场，它应当大致指向数据的均值（FIG 2 把 \(t\) 拉到 0 时的样子），并且一步 Euler 应当把所有样本送到 \(\mathbb E[\mathbf x_1]\) 附近（FIG 3）。

    This lesson (like Lipman et al.) puts noise at \(t=0\) and data at \(t=1\) with target \(\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_0\); DDPM counts steps from the data (step 0) to the noise, and some codebases put the noise at \(t=1\) and write the target as \(\boldsymbol\varepsilon-\mathbf x\). Take the formulas from one paper and the convention from another codebase, and you integrate in the wrong direction (the samples run off the screen) or the training target and the sampler disagree about the time axis. The cheapest check: after training, look at the field near the noise end; it should point roughly at the mean of the data (FIG 2 with \(t\) dragged to 0), and one Euler step should send every sample near \(\mathbb E[\mathbf x_1]\) (FIG 3).

    误区 2：拿损失值判断模型好坏Pitfall 2: judging the model by its loss value

    CFM 损失有一个地板 \(\mathcal L^*\)，它由数据和配对决定，与网络无关。FIG 2 里双月的损失从 4.38 降到 3.0 附近就停了，看上去“没怎么学”，其实场误差从 1.34 降到了 0.06；FIG 4 里 reflow 的损失比独立配对低两个数量级，却不代表它的网络更准，只是它的地板低。比较模型要看样本（SW、FID 这类分布距离，机器人上则是任务成功率），或者像本页这样在有精确答案的玩具问题上量场误差。

    The CFM loss has a floor \(\mathcal L^*\) set by the data and the pairing, not by the network. In FIG 2 the moons' loss falls from 4.38 to about 3.0 and stops, which looks like "hardly learning", yet the field error has dropped from 1.34 to 0.06; in FIG 4 reflow's loss is two orders of magnitude below that of independent pairing, which does not make its network more accurate, only its floor lower. Compare models by their samples (distribution distances such as SW or FID, and task success rates on robots), or, as on this page, by the field error on a toy problem with a known answer.

    误区 3：以为“直线路径”意味着一步就够Pitfall 3: assuming "straight paths" means one step is enough

    直的是条件路径；独立配对时，平均出来的边缘轨迹是弯的。一步 Euler 的结果是条件均值 \(\mathbb E[\mathbf x_1]\)（无条件时）或 \(\mathbb E[\mathbf a\mid\mathbf o]\)（有条件时），也就是均方误差回归的答案，多模态全部丢掉：FIG 3 的样本云缩成一个点，FIG 5 的碰撞率超过 90%。想用一两步，就要先把轨迹拉直（OT 配对、reflow）或做蒸馏，并在任务指标上验证。

    What is straight is the conditional path; with independent pairing the averaged marginal trajectories are curved. One Euler step returns the conditional mean, \(\mathbb E[\mathbf x_1]\) (unconditionally) or \(\mathbb E[\mathbf a\mid\mathbf o]\) (with a condition), which is exactly the answer of mean-squared-error regression with every mode thrown away: FIG 3's sample cloud shrinks to a point and FIG 5's collision rate exceeds 90%. To use one or two steps, straighten the trajectories first (OT pairing, reflow) or distil, and verify on the task metric.

    误区 4：在条件生成里跨条件做 OT 配对Pitfall 4: OT pairing across conditions in conditional generation

    小批量 OT 把每个噪声配给离它最近的数据点。如果批次里混着不同的观测 \(\mathbf o\)，OT 会把为观测 A 准备的噪声配给观测 B 的动作，网络学到的就不再是 \(p(\mathbf a\mid\mathbf o)\)。条件生成时，要么只在同一个条件内部做 OT，要么就用独立配对；reflow 则要用同一个条件下的 ODE 生成配对。另外，OT 配对是在整个批次上求的，批次很小时它只是一个粗糙的近似。

    Minibatch OT pairs each noise sample with the nearest data point. If a batch mixes different observations \(\mathbf o\), OT pairs noise meant for observation A with an action of observation B, and the network no longer learns \(p(\mathbf a\mid\mathbf o)\). For conditional generation, either do OT only within one condition or use independent pairing; reflow must likewise generate its pairs with the ODE under the same condition. Also, the OT pairing is solved over one batch, so with small batches it is only a rough approximation.

    误区 5：数据尺度和 \(t\to1\) 的数值问题Pitfall 5: data scale and the numerics near \(t\to1\)

    噪声是标准高斯，所以数据（动作）也要归一化到差不多的尺度：如果关节角以弧度计、夹爪以毫米计，同一份噪声对它们的意义完全不同，路径的形状也被扭曲。另一个陷阱是用 \(\mathbf u_t(\mathbf x\mid\mathbf x_1)=(\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x)/(1-t)\) 这个形式算目标：\(t\) 接近 1 时分母趋于零，数值噪声被放大。直接用 \(\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_0\)（本页的做法）就没有这个问题；如果网络改为预测 \(\hat{\mathbf x}_1\) 再换算成速度，同样会在 \(t\to1\) 除以 \(1-t\)，要截断 \(t\) 的范围或对损失加权。

    The noise is a standard Gaussian, so the data (actions) must be normalized to a similar scale: with joint angles in radians and the gripper in millimetres, the same noise means something completely different for each, and the shape of the path gets distorted. Another trap is computing the target in the form \(\mathbf u_t(\mathbf x\mid\mathbf x_1)=(\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x)/(1-t)\): as \(t\) approaches 1 the denominator goes to zero and numerical noise is amplified. Using \(\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_0\) directly (as this page does) avoids it; if the network instead predicts \(\hat{\mathbf x}_1\) and converts it to a velocity, it divides by \(1-t\) as \(t\to1\) too, so clip the range of \(t\) or weight the loss.

    1. 一对训练样本：\(\mathbf x_0=(1,-1)\)，\(\mathbf x_1=(3,1)\)，\(t=0.25\)。网络的输入位置 \(\mathbf x_t\) 和回归目标分别是什么？1. One training pair: \(\mathbf x_0=(1,-1)\), \(\mathbf x_1=(3,1)\), \(t=0.25\). What are the network's input position \(\mathbf x_t\) and its regression target?

    2. 训练了很久，CFM 损失停在约 2.9，不再下降。最可能的解释是什么？2. After long training the CFM loss sits at about 2.9 and will not go lower. What is the most likely explanation?
    \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{CFM}}=\mathcal L_{\mathrm{FM}}+\mathcal L^*\)，而 \(\mathcal L^*=\mathbb E\|\mathbf u_t(\mathbf x_t)-(\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_0)\|^2\) 与 \(\theta\) 无关。即使网络完美（\(\mathbf v_\theta=\mathbf u_t\)），损失也等于 \(\mathcal L^*\)，FIG 2 里双月的 \(\mathcal L^*=2.89\)。选项 A、B 追求损失为零，而在独立配对下这不可能；选项 D 会让网络只学会一个时刻的场，无法积分。\(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{CFM}}=\mathcal L_{\mathrm{FM}}+\mathcal L^*\), and \(\mathcal L^*=\mathbb E\|\mathbf u_t(\mathbf x_t)-(\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_0)\|^2\) does not depend on \(\theta\). Even a perfect network (\(\mathbf v_\theta=\mathbf u_t\)) has loss \(\mathcal L^*\); for FIG 2's moons \(\mathcal L^*=2.89\). Options A and B chase a zero loss, which is impossible with independent pairing; option D would teach the network the field at one time only, which cannot be integrated.
    3. 用独立配对和直线路径训练出精确的 \(\mathbf u_t\)，从 \(\mathbf x_0\sim\mathcal N(\mathbf 0,I)\) 出发只做一步 Euler（\(h=1\)）。得到的样本是什么？3. With independent pairing and the straight path, the exact \(\mathbf u_t\) has been learned. Starting from \(\mathbf x_0\sim\mathcal N(\mathbf 0,I)\), you take a single Euler step (\(h=1\)).
