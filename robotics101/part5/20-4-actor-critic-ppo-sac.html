Actor-Critic：PPO 与 SACActor-Critic: PPO & SAC

策略梯度会告诉策略往哪里改，但它用整段回报来打分，噪声大得惊人。Actor-Critic 方法再训练一个"评论家"（critic）网络来估计价值，用它给每一步打分，噪声就小多了。本节先看评论家怎样用 GAE 在偏差和方差之间折中，再看今天机器人学习里最常用的两个算法：PPO 用截断的概率比限制每次更新的步子，靠成百上千个并行仿真环境吃饱数据；SAC 最大化"奖励 + 熵"，用重放缓冲区反复利用旧数据，再用双评论家压住高估。两个算法都在你的浏览器里实时训练同一个关节到达任务，最后讨论什么时候用哪一个。 A policy gradient tells the policy which way to change, but it scores every action with a whole noisy return. Actor-critic methods train a second network, the critic, to estimate values and score each step with them, which removes most of that noise. This lesson first shows how the critic trades bias against variance through GAE, then the two algorithms that dominate robot learning today: PPO, which clips a probability ratio so that each update stays small and feeds on hundreds or thousands of parallel simulated environments, and SAC, which maximizes reward plus entropy, reuses old data from a replay buffer and holds down overestimation with twin critics. Both train live in your browser on the same joint-reaching task, and the lesson ends with when to use which.

    1为什么机器人需要它Why robots need this

    20.3 节的 REINFORCE 已经能训练一个连续动作的高斯策略：采一整段轨迹，用回报 \(G_t\) 乘以 \(\nabla_\theta\log\pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\) 求平均。它是无偏的，但在机器人任务上几乎用不起来，原因有三个：

    • 方差太大。一条腿式机器人的轨迹有上千步，\(G_t\) 混进了之后每一步的随机性：脚下一滑、一次碰撞，都会让同一个动作得到完全不同的分数。要把噪声平均掉，需要海量轨迹。
    • 步长难定。梯度只在当前策略附近有意义。步子小了学得慢，步子大了策略一下子变坏，而变坏的策略又采回更坏的数据，可能再也爬不回来。
    • 数据只用一次。策略梯度要求数据来自当前策略，策略一更新，旧数据就作废了。在仿真里还能忍；在真机上，每一个样本都是电机磨损和实验员的时间。

    Actor-Critic 的思路是再训练一个评论家：一个估计价值 \(V_\phi(s)\) 或 \(Q_\phi(s,a)\) 的网络（20.1–20.2 节的价值函数，换成神经网络并在线学习）。演员（actor，也就是策略 \(\pi_\theta\)）用评论家给出的"这一步比平均好多少"（优势）来更新，而不是用整段回报。在这个框架上，两个算法分别解决了后两个问题：

    • PPO（Proximal Policy Optimization，Schulman 等，2017）：同策略（on-policy），用"截断的概率比"把每次更新限制在旧策略附近，可以放心地在同一批数据上做多轮梯度；数据多就用并行环境去采。这是 Isaac Gym / Isaac Lab 里训练腿式机器人的主力。
    • SAC（Soft Actor-Critic，Haarnoja 等，2018）：异策略（off-policy），把所有经验存进重放缓冲区反复使用；目标里加上策略的熵，让探索自动发生；用两个评论家取较小值，压住 Q 值的高估。它的样本效率高，适合直接在真机上学习。

    本节用同一个小任务贯穿全篇：一个受重力作用的单关节手臂，必须在 2 秒内转到随机给定的目标角度并保持住（这需要学会重力补偿），动作是归一化的电机力矩 \(a\in[-1,1]\)。PPO 和 SAC 都在浏览器里从零开始训练它。

    Lesson 20.3's REINFORCE can already train a Gaussian policy for continuous actions: sample whole trajectories and average the return \(G_t\) times \(\nabla_\theta\log\pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\). It is unbiased, but on robot tasks it is nearly unusable, for three reasons:

    • The variance is huge. A legged robot's trajectory has thousands of steps, and \(G_t\) mixes in the randomness of every later step: a slipping foot or a collision gives the same action a completely different score. Averaging that noise away takes an enormous number of trajectories.
    • The step size is hard to choose. The gradient is only meaningful near the current policy. Small steps learn slowly; a large step can ruin the policy, the ruined policy collects even worse data, and it may never climb back.
    • Data is used once. The policy gradient needs data from the current policy, so every update throws the old data away. In simulation that is tolerable; on hardware every sample costs motor wear and an experimenter's time.

    The actor-critic idea is to train a second network, the critic, that estimates a value \(V_\phi(s)\) or \(Q_\phi(s,a)\) (the value functions of Lessons 20.1–20.2, now neural networks learned online). The actor (the policy \(\pi_\theta\)) is updated with the critic's estimate of "how much better than average this step was" (the advantage) instead of the whole return. On top of this framework, two algorithms address the last two problems:

    • PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization, Schulman et al., 2017) is on-policy. A "clipped probability ratio" keeps each update close to the old policy, so it can safely take several gradient epochs on one batch, and it gets large batches from parallel environments. It is the workhorse for training legged robots in Isaac Gym / Isaac Lab.
    • SAC (Soft Actor-Critic, Haarnoja et al., 2018) is off-policy. It keeps all experience in a replay buffer and reuses it, adds the policy's entropy to the objective so that exploration happens by itself, and takes the smaller of two critics to hold down Q-value overestimation. It is sample-efficient and suits learning directly on real robots.

    One small task runs through the whole page: a single arm joint under gravity must turn to a random target angle within 2 seconds and hold it there (which requires learning gravity compensation); the action is a normalized motor torque \(a\in[-1,1]\). PPO and SAC both train it from scratch in your browser.

    2直觉：评论家给每一步打分Intuition: the critic scores each step

    20.3 节已经看到，从回报里减去一个只依赖状态的基线 \(b(s_t)\) 不改变梯度的期望，却能大大降低方差，最好的基线接近 \(V^\pi(s_t)\)。Actor-Critic 把这件事推进一步：既然有了一个学出来的 \(V_\phi\)，就连回报本身也可以用它来估计。最短的版本只看一步，叫时序差分误差（TD error，20.2 节）：

    Lesson 20.3 showed that subtracting a state-only baseline \(b(s_t)\) from the return leaves the expected gradient unchanged and cuts the variance a lot, and that the best baseline is close to \(V^\pi(s_t)\). Actor-critic takes one more step: once we have a learned \(V_\phi\), we can use it to estimate the return itself. The shortest version looks one step ahead and is the temporal-difference (TD) error of Lesson 20.2:

    $$ \hl{delta}{\delta_t} \;=\; r_{t+1}+\gamma\,\hl{Vhat}{V_\phi}(s_{t+1})-\hl{Vhat}{V_\phi}(s_t). $$

    "这一步拿到的奖励，加上评论家对下一状态的估值，减去评论家对这一状态的估值"：如果 \(V_\phi=V^\pi\)，它的期望正好是优势 \(A^\pi(s_t,a_t)=Q^\pi(s_t,a_t)-V^\pi(s_t)\)。它只含一步的随机性，方差很小；代价是一旦评论家估错，误差会原样漏进来，产生偏差。另一头是蒙特卡洛：把整段回报 \(G_t\) 减去 \(V_\phi(s_t)\)，没有偏差（评论家只当基线用），但方差最大。广义优势估计 GAE（Schulman 等，2016）用一个旋钮 \(\lambda\in[0,1]\) 在两者之间连续插值：

    "The reward of this step, plus the critic's value of the next state, minus its value of this state": if \(V_\phi=V^\pi\), its expectation is exactly the advantage \(A^\pi(s_t,a_t)=Q^\pi(s_t,a_t)-V^\pi(s_t)\). It contains the randomness of one step only, so its variance is small; the price is that any error in the critic leaks straight in as bias. At the other end is Monte Carlo: the whole return \(G_t\) minus \(V_\phi(s_t)\), unbiased (the critic serves only as a baseline) but with the largest variance. Generalized advantage estimation, GAE (Schulman et al., 2016), interpolates continuously between the two with one knob \(\lambda\in[0,1]\):

    $$ \hat A_t^{\mathrm{GAE}(\gamma,\hl{lam}{\lambda})}=\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}(\gamma\hl{lam}{\lambda})^{l}\,\hl{delta}{\delta_{t+l}},\qquad \lambda=0:\ \hat A_t=\delta_t,\qquad \lambda=1:\ \hat A_t=G_t-V_\phi(s_t). $$

    下图用一个能精确算出真值的小例子来看这个折中。一个送货机器人在 10 格走廊里，从第 2 格出发去第 9 格的充电桩：每走一步奖励 \(-1\)，到达充电桩额外 \(+10\)；它的策略以 0.6 的概率向右、0.4 的概率向左，每次移动有 0.2 的概率打滑原地不动，\(\gamma=0.95\)。我们要估计"在起点向右走"的价值 \(Q^\pi(s_0,\rightarrow)\)，等价于估计它的优势（两者只差一个常数 \(V_\phi(s_0)\)）。评论家 \(V_\phi\) 等于真值加上每格一个固定的随机误差（标准差 \(\sigma_V\)）。估计量的均值和方差都是精确算出来的（两条 Bellman 式递推，见第 5 节），直方图是 1500 条采样轨迹的实际估计值。

    The figure looks at this trade-off on a small example whose true values can be computed exactly. A delivery robot in a 10-cell corridor starts in cell 2 and heads for the charging dock in cell 9: every step gives reward \(-1\) and arriving at the dock gives an extra \(+10\). Its policy goes right with probability 0.6 and left with 0.4, every move slips (the robot stays put) with probability 0.2, and \(\gamma=0.95\). We estimate the value of "going right at the start", \(Q^\pi(s_0,\rightarrow)\), which is the same as estimating its advantage (the two differ by the constant \(V_\phi(s_0)\)). The critic \(V_\phi\) is the true value plus a fixed random error in each cell (standard deviation \(\sigma_V\)). The estimator's mean and variance are computed exactly (two Bellman-style recursions, Section 5); the histogram shows the actual estimates from 1500 sampled trajectories.

    核心洞见Key insight

    评论家是用偏差换方差。完美的评论家下，\(\lambda=0\) 只看一步、方差约为蒙特卡洛的百分之一，又没有偏差；评论家越不准，越要靠更长的真实回报（更大的 \(\lambda\)）来稀释它的误差。实践里评论家是边学边用的，误差不大不小，所以 PPO 常用 \(\lambda\approx0.95\)、\(\gamma\approx0.99\)：大部分噪声已经消掉，评论家的误差也被几十步的真实奖励冲淡了。注意这里的最优 \(\lambda\) 依赖评论家误差的具体形状，并不是一个普适常数。

    The critic trades variance for bias. With a perfect critic, \(\lambda=0\) looks one step ahead, has about one hundredth of the Monte Carlo variance and no bias at all; the worse the critic, the more you must dilute its errors with longer real returns (larger \(\lambda\)). In practice the critic is learned while it is used and is moderately wrong, so PPO typically uses \(\lambda\approx0.95\) with \(\gamma\approx0.99\): most of the noise is gone, and the critic's errors are washed out by tens of steps of real rewards. Note that the best \(\lambda\) here depends on the particular shape of the critic's error; it is not a universal constant.

    3动手玩：PPO，步子迈小一点Play: PPO, keep each step small

    有了优势估计 \(\hat A_t\)，最直接的做法是对 \(\hat A_t\log\pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\) 做一步梯度上升，然后把数据扔掉。这太浪费：采数据很贵，更新却很便宜。能不能在同一批数据上多走几步？问题是数据来自旧策略 \(\pi_{\theta_k}\)，策略一变，"新策略下的期望"就要靠重要性采样（4.9 节）来纠正，权重就是概率比（这里记作 \(\rho\)，因为 \(r\) 已经表示奖励；PPO 原文记作 \(r_t\)）：

    With advantage estimates \(\hat A_t\) in hand, the most direct move is one gradient-ascent step on \(\hat A_t\log\pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\), after which the data is discarded. That is wasteful: collecting data is expensive and updates are cheap. Can we take several steps on the same batch? The catch is that the data came from the old policy \(\pi_{\theta_k}\); once the policy changes, expectations under the new policy have to be corrected with importance sampling (Lesson 4.9), whose weight is the probability ratio (written \(\rho\) here because \(r\) is the reward; the PPO paper calls it \(r_t\))

    $$ \begin{gathered} \hl{ratio}{\rho_t(\theta)}=\frac{\pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)}{\pi_{\theta_k}(a_t|s_t)},\qquad L^{\text{PG}}(\theta)=\hat{\mathbb E}_t\big[\hl{ratio}{\rho_t(\theta)}\,\hat A_t\big],\\ L^{\text{CLIP}}(\theta)=\hat{\mathbb E}_t\Big[\min\big(\hl{ratio}{\rho_t}\hat A_t,\ \hl{clip}{\operatorname{clip}(\rho_t,1-\epsilon,1+\epsilon)}\,\hat A_t\big)\Big]. \end{gathered} $$

    \(L^{\text{PG}}\) 在 \(\theta=\theta_k\) 处的梯度正好是策略梯度（\(\nabla\rho_t=\nabla\log\pi_\theta\)），但离旧策略越远，它越是建立在少数几个权重很大的样本上，估计值就越不可信，而优化器恰恰会利用这种不可信：哪里估计虚高就往哪里冲。PPO 的 \(L^{\text{CLIP}}\) 做了一件很简单的事：一个样本的概率比一旦超出 \([1-\epsilon,1+\epsilon]\)，并且继续变化只会让目标"更好看"，它对梯度的贡献就变成零。好动作的概率最多涨到 \(1+\epsilon\) 倍，坏动作的概率最多降到 \(1-\epsilon\) 倍；反方向（把坏动作变得更可能）则照常受罚，不被截断。

    下图是一个只有动作没有状态的例子，正好能把"真实的改进"精确算出来。动作 \(a\) 是推关节的力度：推得越猛越好，可是超过 0.9 会触发安全停机，优势骤降 3。旧策略是 \(\mathcal N(0,0.4^2)\)，它的 16 个样本里没有一个落在停机区，所以数据根本看不见这个悬崖。新策略 \(\mathcal N(m,0.4^2)\)，拖动琥珀色手柄（或在右图里点击）改变 \(m\)。

    At \(\theta=\theta_k\) the gradient of \(L^{\text{PG}}\) is exactly the policy gradient (\(\nabla\rho_t=\nabla\log\pi_\theta\)), but the further the policy moves, the more the estimate rests on a few samples with huge weights and the less it can be trusted, and the optimizer exploits exactly that: it rushes to wherever the estimate is inflated. PPO's \(L^{\text{CLIP}}\) does something very simple: once a sample's ratio leaves \([1-\epsilon,1+\epsilon]\) in the direction that would make the objective look even better, that sample stops contributing gradient. A good action's probability can grow by at most \(1+\epsilon\), a bad action's can shrink to at most \(1-\epsilon\) of its old value; the opposite direction (making a bad action more likely) is still penalized in full and never clipped.

    The figure is an example with actions but no states, so the true improvement can be computed exactly. The action \(a\) is how hard to push the joint: the harder the better, but beyond 0.9 a safety stop trips and the advantage drops by 3. The old policy is \(\mathcal N(0,0.4^2)\), and none of its 16 samples lands in the stop zone, so the data cannot see the cliff at all. The new policy is \(\mathcal N(m,0.4^2)\): drag the amber handle (or click in the right panel) to change \(m\).

    At \(\epsilon=0.2\) the top of \(L^{\text{CLIP}}\) is at \(m\approx0.15\) (KL about 0.07), where the true improvement is about +0.25; the true optimum is at \(m\approx0.30\) (+0.36). The unclipped \(L^{\text{PG}}\) instead peaks at \(m\approx0.53\) with +0.66, where the true improvement is only +0.09; at \(m=0.8\) it still promises +0.52 while the truth is \(-0.99\). Clipping gives a conservative but reliable small step; several such steps, each with fresh data and its own clipping, are far safer than one jump to the top of the estimate.

    Now put this into the full algorithm. One PPO iteration: (1) run the current policy for \(H=40\) steps in each of \(N\) parallel environments; (2) compute GAE advantages with the critic and normalize them over the batch; (3) shuffle the \(NH\) samples into minibatches of 128 and run \(K\) epochs of Adam on \(L^{\text{CLIP}}\) and on the critic's regression loss; (4) discard the data and go back to (1). Actor and critic are 3→32→32→1 tanh networks; the policy is a Gaussian whose mean comes from the network and whose standard deviation is a learned parameter; \(\gamma=0.97\), \(\lambda=0.95\). After each iteration the deterministic policy (its mean) is evaluated on 24 fixed (start, target) pairs: the curve is the mean angle error to the target at the end of the 2 seconds.

    核心洞见Key insight

    在种子 1 下，默认设置第 7 次迭代（4480 步）第一次低于 3°，之后在 1°–8° 之间起伏，大约 30 次迭代后稳定在 1° 左右；\(N=64\) 只要 6 次迭代，却用了 15 360 步。也就是说，并行环境不省样本，省的是迭代次数：在 GPU 上一次推进几千个仿真环境的时间和推进一个差不多，所以真实耗时由迭代次数决定。关掉截断、做 10 轮时，每次迭代的 KL 从 0.002–0.05 跳到约 0.2–15，误差可能跳回 100° 以上。截断后的 KL 小而稳定，这才是"近端"（proximal）的意思。

    With seed 1, the default run first drops below 3° at iteration 7 (4480 steps), then wanders between 1° and 8° and settles near 1° after about 30 iterations; \(N=64\) needs only 6 iterations but 15,360 steps. In other words, parallel environments do not save samples, they save iterations: stepping thousands of simulated environments on a GPU takes about as long as stepping one, so wall-clock time is set by the number of iterations. With clipping off and 10 epochs, the KL per iteration jumps from 0.002–0.05 to roughly 0.2–15 and the error can leap back above 100°. With clipping the KL stays small and steady, which is what "proximal" means.

    4第二个视角：SAC，异策略加最大熵A second view: SAC, off-policy and maximum entropy

    PPO 的每个样本只服务于采它的那个策略。另一条路是学 \(Q(s,a)\)：Bellman 方程 \(Q(s,a)=r+\gamma\,\mathbb E[V(s')]\) 对任何行为策略收集的转移 \((s,a,r,s')\) 都成立（20.2 节的 Q-learning 正是这样），所以旧数据可以存进重放缓冲区一直用下去。DQN 在离散动作上取 \(\max_a\)；连续动作取不了 max，就让演员网络去找让 \(Q\) 大的动作，这就是 DDPG（Lillicrap 等，2016）和它的改进 TD3（Fujimoto 等，2018）的做法。SAC 在此之上改了目标本身：

    Each PPO sample serves only the policy that collected it. The other road is to learn \(Q(s,a)\): the Bellman equation \(Q(s,a)=r+\gamma\,\mathbb E[V(s')]\) holds for transitions \((s,a,r,s')\) collected by any behaviour policy (that is how Q-learning works in Lesson 20.2), so old data can live in a replay buffer and be reused indefinitely. DQN takes \(\max_a\) over discrete actions; with continuous actions there is no max to take, so an actor network learns to find actions with large \(Q\). That is DDPG (Lillicrap et al., 2016) and its refinement TD3 (Fujimoto et al., 2018). SAC goes further and changes the objective itself:

    $$ J(\pi)=\sum_t\mathbb E\Big[r_{t+1}+\hl{alpha}{\alpha}\,\hl{H}{\mathcal H\big(\pi(\cdot|s_t)\big)}\Big],\qquad \pi^\star(a|s)\;\propto\;\exp\!\big(\hl{Qa}{Q^\star(s,a)}/\hl{alpha}{\alpha}\big). $$

    每一步除了奖励，还奖励策略的熵 \(\mathcal H\)（4.11 节）：在同样好的动作之间保持随机。温度 \(\alpha\) 决定熵值多少钱。这个"软"目标的最优策略是 Q 值的玻尔兹曼分布：\(\alpha\to0\) 时退化为 \(\arg\max\)，\(\alpha\) 很大时接近均匀。SAC 的演员是一个 tanh 压缩的高斯（输出 \(\mu,\log\sigma\)，\(a=\tanh(\mu+\sigma\varepsilon)\)），它在每个状态上最小化 \(\mathrm{KL}\big(\pi\,\|\,\pi^\star\big)\)，等价于最小化 \(\mathbb E[\alpha\log\pi(a|s)-Q(s,a)]\)，梯度靠重参数化（19.1 节）穿过采样。

    下图把状态拿掉，只看一个固定的 \(Q(a)\)：抓取一个物体有两个好的接近角度，左边的稍好（1.0），右边的稍差（0.8）。拖动 \(\alpha\)，看玻尔兹曼目标怎么变；按"训练"，看单峰的演员怎样去拟合它。

    Besides the reward, every step also rewards the policy's entropy \(\mathcal H\) (Lesson 4.11): stay random among equally good actions. The temperature \(\alpha\) sets the price of entropy. The optimal policy of this "soft" objective is the Boltzmann distribution of the Q-values: it tends to \(\arg\max\) as \(\alpha\to0\) and becomes nearly uniform for large \(\alpha\). SAC's actor is a tanh-squashed Gaussian (it outputs \(\mu,\log\sigma\), and \(a=\tanh(\mu+\sigma\varepsilon)\)); in each state it minimizes \(\mathrm{KL}\big(\pi\,\|\,\pi^\star\big)\), which is the same as minimizing \(\mathbb E[\alpha\log\pi(a|s)-Q(s,a)]\), with the gradient passed through the sampling by reparameterization (Lesson 19.1).

    Two things are worth remembering. That is a limit of the tanh-Gaussian's expressiveness, and the motivation for flow-based policies mentioned in Lesson 19.3. Second, a good \(\alpha\) depends on the scale of the reward: multiply the reward by 10 and the same \(\alpha\) is effectively 10 times smaller. That is what automatic temperature is for: instead of guessing \(\alpha\), specify how much entropy you want (by default \(\bar{\mathcal H}=-\dim\mathcal A\)) and let \(\alpha\) adjust to deliver it.

    20.3 节的策略梯度定理可以写成优势的形式：\(\nabla_\theta J=\mathbb E\big[\sum_t\nabla_\theta\log\pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\,A^\pi(s_t,a_t)\big]\)。任何满足 \(\mathbb E[\hat A_t|s_t,a_t]\approx A^\pi(s_t,a_t)\) 的估计都可以代入。TD 误差满足 \(\mathbb E[\delta_t|s_t,a_t]=\mathbb E[r_{t+1}|s_t,a_t]+\gamma\mathbb E[V(s_{t+1})]-V(s_t)=Q^\pi-V^\pi=A^\pi\)，前提是 \(V=V^\pi\)。把 \(k\) 步的估计 \(\hat A_t^{(k)}=\sum_{l<k}\gamma^l r_{t+l+1}+\gamma^kV(s_{t+k})-V(s_t)=\sum_{l<k}\gamma^l\delta_{t+l}\)（裂项相消）按权重 \((1-\lambda)\lambda^{k-1}\) 平均，就得到 GAE：

    Lesson 20.3's policy gradient theorem can be written with the advantage: \(\nabla_\theta J=\mathbb E\big[\sum_t\nabla_\theta\log\pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\,A^\pi(s_t,a_t)\big]\). Any estimate with \(\mathbb E[\hat A_t|s_t,a_t]\approx A^\pi(s_t,a_t)\) can be plugged in. The TD error satisfies \(\mathbb E[\delta_t|s_t,a_t]=\mathbb E[r_{t+1}|s_t,a_t]+\gamma\mathbb E[V(s_{t+1})]-V(s_t)=Q^\pi-V^\pi=A^\pi\), provided \(V=V^\pi\). Averaging the \(k\)-step estimates \(\hat A_t^{(k)}=\sum_{l<k}\gamma^l r_{t+l+1}+\gamma^kV(s_{t+k})-V(s_t)=\sum_{l<k}\gamma^l\delta_{t+l}\) (the sum telescopes) with weights \((1-\lambda)\lambda^{k-1}\) gives GAE:

    $$ \hat A_t=\sum_{l\ge0}(\gamma\hl{lam}{\lambda})^l\hl{delta}{\delta_{t+l}}=\hl{delta}{\delta_t}+\gamma\hl{lam}{\lambda}\,\hat A_{t+1},\qquad \hat G_t=\hat A_t+\hl{Vhat}{V_\phi}(s_t),\qquad L_V(\phi)=\hat{\mathbb E}_t\big[(V_\phi(s_t)-\hat G_t)^2\big]. $$

    右边的递推就是代码里从轨迹末尾往回扫的那一行。\(\hat G_t\) 叫 \(\lambda\)-回报，评论家对它做回归（PPO 的做法）。如果回合因为时间上限被截断而不是真的结束，最后一步要用 \(V_\phi(s_T)\) 自举，而不是当作 0（误区 1）；本页的关节任务就是这样处理的。FIG 1 的精确均值和方差来自两条递推：设 \(c=r+\gamma(1-\lambda)V_\phi(s')\)，则 \(G=c+\gamma\lambda G'\)，于是 \(U(s)=\mathbb E[G|s]=\mathbb E[c+\gamma\lambda U(s')]\)，\(W(s)=\mathbb E[G^2|s]=\mathbb E[c^2+2c\gamma\lambda U(s')+\gamma^2\lambda^2W(s')]\)，都是压缩映射，迭代即收敛。

    The recursion on the right is the one line in the code that sweeps backward from the end of a trajectory. \(\hat G_t\) is the \(\lambda\)-return, and the critic regresses onto it (as PPO does).     为什么"别离旧策略太远"是对的？性能差引理（Kakade & Langford，2002）给出精确的改进量：

    Why is "do not stray far from the old policy" the right rule? The performance difference lemma (Kakade & Langford, 2002) gives the exact improvement:

    $$ J(\pi')-J(\pi)=\frac{1}{1-\gamma}\,\mathbb E_{s\sim d^{\pi'}}\,\mathbb E_{a\sim\pi'}\big[A^\pi(s,a)\big]\;\approx\;\frac{1}{1-\gamma}\,\mathbb E_{s\sim d^{\pi}}\,\mathbb E_{a\sim\pi}\big[\hl{ratio}{\rho(\theta)}\,A^\pi(s,a)\big]. $$

    右边把新策略的状态分布 \(d^{\pi'}\) 换成旧的 \(d^\pi\)，于是可以用旧数据估计，这就是代理目标 \(L^{\text{PG}}\)；只有当 \(\pi'\) 离 \(\pi\) 不远时这个替换才可靠（状态分布的差距可以用两策略间的 KL 来界定）。TRPO（Schulman 等，2015）直接求解带约束的问题 \(\max L^{\text{PG}}\ \text{s.t.}\ \mathbb E_s[\mathrm{KL}(\pi_{\theta_k}\|\pi_\theta)]\le\delta\)，需要共轭梯度和线搜索。PPO 用 \(\min(\rho\hat A,\operatorname{clip}(\rho)\hat A)\) 的一阶近似代替约束，只要普通的小批量 SGD。完整的损失通常是

    The right-hand side replaces the new policy's state distribution \(d^{\pi'}\) by the old one \(d^\pi\), so it can be estimated from old data: that is the surrogate \(L^{\text{PG}}\). The replacement is reliable only while \(\pi'\) stays close to \(\pi\) (the gap in state distributions can be bounded by the KL between the policies). TRPO (Schulman et al., 2015) solves the constrained problem \(\max L^{\text{PG}}\ \text{s.t.}\ \mathbb E_s[\mathrm{KL}(\pi_{\theta_k}\|\pi_\theta)]\le\delta\) directly, with conjugate gradients and a line search. PPO replaces the constraint with the first-order device \(\min(\rho\hat A,\operatorname{clip}(\rho)\hat A)\) and needs only ordinary minibatch SGD. The full loss is usually

    $$ \mathcal L(\theta,\phi)=-L^{\text{CLIP}}(\theta)+c_1\,L_V(\phi)-c_2\,\hat{\mathbb E}_t\big[\mathcal H(\pi_\theta(\cdot|s_t))\big],\qquad \log\pi_\theta(a|s)=-\frac{(a-\mu_\theta(s))^2}{2\sigma^2}-\log\sigma-\tfrac12\log2\pi . $$

    逐个样本看 \(\min(\rho\hat A,\operatorname{clip}(\rho,1-\epsilon,1+\epsilon)\hat A)\)：\(\hat A>0\) 时它等于 \(\min(\rho,1+\epsilon)\hat A\)，\(\rho>1+\epsilon\) 后对 \(\theta\) 的梯度为 0；\(\hat A<0\) 时它等于 \(\max(\rho,1-\epsilon)\hat A\)，\(\rho<1-\epsilon\) 后梯度为 0。另一侧（\(\hat A>0\) 而 \(\rho<1-\epsilon\)，或 \(\hat A<0\) 而 \(\rho>1+\epsilon\)）仍是 \(\rho\hat A\)，梯度照常把策略拉回来，这正是 FIG 2 下图里两条曲线的形状。本页 PPO 的 \(c_2=0\)，演员和评论家分开两个网络；读数中的 KL 用估计式 \(\hat{\mathbb E}[(\rho-1)-\log\rho]\)，它对每个样本都非负。

    Look at \(\min(\rho\hat A,\operatorname{clip}(\rho,1-\epsilon,1+\epsilon)\hat A)\) per sample: for \(\hat A>0\) it equals \(\min(\rho,1+\epsilon)\hat A\), whose gradient in \(\theta\) is 0 once \(\rho>1+\epsilon\); for \(\hat A<0\) it equals \(\max(\rho,1-\epsilon)\hat A\), with zero gradient once \(\rho<1-\epsilon\). On the other side (\(\hat A>0\) with \(\rho<1-\epsilon\), or \(\hat A<0\) with \(\rho>1+\epsilon\)) it is still \(\rho\hat A\) and the gradient pulls the policy back as usual: exactly the shape of the two curves in FIG 2's bottom panel. This page's PPO uses \(c_2=0\) and separate actor and critic networks; the readout's KL uses the estimator \(\hat{\mathbb E}[(\rho-1)-\log\rho]\), which is non-negative for every sample.

    软 Bellman 方程与 SAC 的三个损失The soft Bellman equation and SAC's three losses

    在最大熵目标下，价值里也要算上未来的熵，于是有软 Bellman 方程 \(Q(s,a)=r+\gamma\,\mathbb E_{s'}[V(s')]\)，\(V(s)=\mathbb E_{a\sim\pi}[Q(s,a)-\alpha\log\pi(a|s)]\)。SAC 用两个评论家 \(Q_{\phi_1},Q_{\phi_2}\)、它们的目标网络 \(\bar\phi_j\leftarrow\tau\phi_j+(1-\tau)\bar\phi_j\) 和重放缓冲区 \(\mathcal D\)：

    Under the maximum-entropy objective the value also counts future entropy, which gives the soft Bellman equation \(Q(s,a)=r+\gamma\,\mathbb E_{s'}[V(s')]\), \(V(s)=\mathbb E_{a\sim\pi}[Q(s,a)-\alpha\log\pi(a|s)]\). SAC uses two critics \(Q_{\phi_1},Q_{\phi_2}\), their target networks \(\bar\phi_j\leftarrow\tau\phi_j+(1-\tau)\bar\phi_j\), and a replay buffer \(\mathcal D\):

    $$ \begin{aligned} y&=r+\gamma\Big(\hl{min}{\min_{j=1,2}}Q_{\bar\phi_j}(s',\tilde a')-\hl{alpha}{\alpha}\log\pi_\theta(\tilde a'|s')\Big),\quad \tilde a'\sim\pi_\theta(\cdot|s'), & L_Q(\phi_j)&=\mathbb E_{\mathcal D}\big[(Q_{\phi_j}(s,a)-y)^2\big],\\ L_\pi(\theta)&=\mathbb E_{s\sim\mathcal D,\ \varepsilon}\Big[\hl{alpha}{\alpha}\log\pi_\theta(\tilde a|s)-\hl{min}{\min_j}\hl{Qa}{Q_{\phi_j}(s,\tilde a)}\Big],\quad \tilde a=\tanh(\mu_\theta(s)+\sigma_\theta(s)\varepsilon), & L(\alpha)&=\mathbb E\big[-\alpha\big(\log\pi_\theta(\tilde a|s)+\bar{\mathcal H}\big)\big]. \end{aligned} $$

    目标 \(y\) 里的 \(\tilde a'\) 是从当前策略新采的，不是缓冲区里存的旧动作，这就是它能异策略的原因：Bellman 方程只需要 \((s,a,r,s')\)，而 \(s'\) 之后的行为由当前策略决定。tanh 压缩改变了密度，要加上雅可比修正 \(\log\pi(a|s)=\log\mathcal N(u;\mu,\sigma^2)-\log(1-\tanh^2u)\)。演员损失对 \(\theta\) 的梯度通过 \(\tilde a\) 传进评论家（\(\partial Q/\partial a\)），这就是"价值梯度"：动作沿着 Q 增大的方向被推。温度损失的梯度 \(-(\log\pi+\bar{\mathcal H})\) 在熵低于目标时让 \(\alpha\) 增大，反之减小（本页用 \(\log\alpha\) 作参数）。

    The \(\tilde a'\) in the target \(y\) is freshly sampled from the current policy, not the old action stored in the buffer; that is why the method is off-policy: the Bellman equation only needs \((s,a,r,s')\), and what happens after \(s'\) is decided by the current policy. The tanh squashing changes the density and needs the Jacobian correction \(\log\pi(a|s)=\log\mathcal N(u;\mu,\sigma^2)-\log(1-\tanh^2u)\). The actor loss's gradient in \(\theta\) flows through \(\tilde a\) into the critic (\(\partial Q/\partial a\)): this is the "value gradient", pushing the action in the direction in which Q grows. The temperature loss's gradient \(-(\log\pi+\bar{\mathcal H})\) raises \(\alpha\) when the entropy is below the target and lowers it otherwise (this page parameterizes \(\log\alpha\)).

    为什么演员损失是 KL？对固定状态，\(\mathrm{KL}(\pi\|\pi^\star)=\mathbb E_\pi[\log\pi-Q/\alpha]+\log Z=\tfrac1\alpha\mathbb E_\pi[\alpha\log\pi-Q]+\log Z\)，\(\log Z\) 与 \(\theta\) 无关，所以两者最小值相同。FIG 4 的读数正是这样算 KL 的。

    Why is the actor loss a KL? For a fixed state, \(\mathrm{KL}(\pi\|\pi^\star)=\mathbb E_\pi[\log\pi-Q/\alpha]+\log Z=\tfrac1\alpha\mathbb E_\pi[\alpha\log\pi-Q]+\log Z\), and \(\log Z\) does not depend on \(\theta\), so both have the same minimizer. FIG 4's readout computes the KL exactly this way.

    TD3：确定性演员的三个技巧TD3: three tricks for a deterministic actor

    DDPG 的演员是确定性的 \(a=\mu_\theta(s)\)，由确定性策略梯度 \(\nabla_\theta J=\mathbb E_s\big[\nabla_aQ(s,a)|_{a=\mu_\theta(s)}\nabla_\theta\mu_\theta(s)\big]\)（Silver 等，2014）更新，探索靠给动作加噪声。它在实践中很容易被评论家的高估带偏。TD3（Fujimoto 等，2018）加了三个修正：(1) 截断双 Q：目标取两个评论家的较小值（FIG 5）；(2) 目标策略平滑：目标里的动作加上截断的高斯噪声 \(\tilde a'=\mu_{\bar\theta}(s')+\operatorname{clip}(\mathcal N(0,\tilde\sigma^2),-c,c)\)，让评论家在动作邻域里平滑，不给尖峰留机会；(3) 延迟更新：评论家每更新 2 次，演员和目标网络才更新 1 次。SAC 也用第 (1) 条（两篇论文几乎同时发表）；它的随机策略和熵项起到了与 (2) 类似的平滑作用。

    DDPG's actor is deterministic, \(a=\mu_\theta(s)\), updated by the deterministic policy gradient \(\nabla_\theta J=\mathbb E_s\big[\nabla_aQ(s,a)|_{a=\mu_\theta(s)}\nabla_\theta\mu_\theta(s)\big]\) (Silver et al., 2014), with exploration from noise added to the action. In practice it is easily led astray by the critic's overestimation. TD3 (Fujimoto et al., 2018) adds three fixes: (1) clipped double Q: the target takes the smaller of two critics (FIG 5); (2) target policy smoothing: the target action gets clipped Gaussian noise, \(\tilde a'=\mu_{\bar\theta}(s')+\operatorname{clip}(\mathcal N(0,\tilde\sigma^2),-c,c)\), so the critic is smooth over a neighbourhood of actions and spikes have no room; (3) delayed updates: the actor and the target networks are updated once per 2 critic updates. SAC uses the same fix (1) (the two papers appeared at almost the same time); its stochastic policy and entropy term smooth the target much like (2).

    7常见误区Common pitfalls

    误区 1：把时间上限当成终止状态Pitfall 1: treating a time limit as a terminal state

    机器人仿真几乎总有回合时长上限（本页是 2 秒）。时间到了，机器人并没有"死"，它此后的价值不是 0。如果在 GAE 或 SAC 的目标里把这一步当成终止（乘以 \(1-\text{done}\)），评论家就会学到"快到 2 秒时价值突然跳变"（本页奖励为负，跳向 0），而它的输入里根本没有时间，只能把这种噪声抹平到所有状态上。正确做法是区分 terminated（真的结束：摔倒、到达）与 truncated（时间到），对后者照常用 \(V(s_T)\) 或 \(Q(s',\tilde a')\) 自举。Gymnasium 的 step 返回值就是这样把两者分开的。

    Robot simulations almost always have an episode time limit (2 seconds on this page). When time runs out the robot has not "died", and its future value is not 0. If GAE or the SAC target treats that step as terminal (multiplying by \(1-\text{done}\)), the critic learns that "value jumps near 2 seconds" (towards 0 here, where rewards are negative), but time is not among its inputs, so it can only smear this noise over every state. The fix is to separate terminated (a real end: a fall, reaching a goal) from truncated (time is up), and to keep bootstrapping with \(V(s_T)\) or \(Q(s',\tilde a')\) for the latter. Gymnasium's step returns the two flags separately for exactly this reason.

    误区 2：以为截断把概率比限制在 \(1\pm\epsilon\) 内Pitfall 2: thinking clipping keeps the ratio inside \(1\pm\epsilon\)

    截断只让超出范围的那些样本不再贡献梯度；神经网络是共享参数的，其余样本的梯度照样会把被截断样本的概率继续推远。FIG 3 读数里的"最大概率比"即使在 \(\epsilon=0.2\) 时也通常在 1.5 到 4 之间，偶尔超过 10，远在 \(1.2\) 之外。轮数 \(K\) 越多、学习率越大，越明显。所以实践里要监控每次迭代的 KL：rsl_rl 按目标 KL 调学习率，另一些实现在 KL 超过阈值时提前停止这一批的训练。

    Clipping only stops the out-of-range samples from contributing gradient; the network shares parameters, so the gradients of the remaining samples keep pushing the clipped samples' probabilities further. Even at \(\epsilon=0.2\) the "max ratio" in FIG 3's readout is usually between 1.5 and 4 and now and then above 10, far outside \(1.2\). The more epochs \(K\) and the larger the learning rate, the worse. So in practice you monitor the KL per iteration: rsl_rl adapts the learning rate to a target KL, and other implementations stop training on a batch early once the KL crosses a threshold.

    误区 3：忽略奖励和观测的尺度Pitfall 3: ignoring the scale of rewards and observations

    SAC 的固定 \(\alpha\) 是相对奖励尺度而言的：同一个 \(\alpha=0.2\)，在每步奖励约为 1 的任务里是适度探索，在每步奖励约为 100 的任务里几乎等于没有熵项。PPO 对优势做了归一化，对奖励尺度不那么敏感，但评论家仍要回归原始尺度的回报，太大的回报会让价值损失主导梯度。常见做法：用自动温度；归一化观测（运行均值与方差）；把奖励缩放到每步 \(O(1)\)。这些"细节"在 PPO 上影响很大（Engstrom 等，2020；Andrychowicz 等，2021）。

    SAC's fixed \(\alpha\) is relative to the reward scale: the same \(\alpha=0.2\) is moderate exploration when rewards are about 1 per step and practically no entropy bonus when they are about 100. PPO normalizes advantages and is less sensitive to reward scale, but its critic still regresses returns at the raw scale, and huge returns let the value loss dominate the gradient. Common practice: use automatic temperature, normalize observations (running mean and variance), and scale rewards to \(O(1)\) per step. Such "details" matter a lot for PPO (Engstrom et al., 2020; Andrychowicz et al., 2021).

    误区 4：认为双评论家"修好了"价值估计Pitfall 4: believing twin critics "fix" the value estimate

    取较小值把高估换成了低估（FIG 5 中约 \(-0.56\sigma\)），只是因为演员不会去追逐被低估的动作，这个偏差才比较无害。评论家仍然处在 20.2 节"致命三角"（函数逼近 + 自举 + 异策略）里：更新太快、目标网络跟得太紧、每步更新次数太多，都可能让 Q 值发散。监控评论家的 Q 估计与实际回报之差，是调 SAC 时最有用的诊断之一。

    Taking the minimum trades overestimation for underestimation (about \(-0.56\sigma\) in FIG 5); it is only fairly harmless because the actor does not chase underestimated actions. The critic still lives in the "deadly triad" of Lesson 20.2 (function approximation + bootstrapping + off-policy data): updating too fast, a target network that tracks too tightly, or too many updates per step can all make the Q-values diverge. Comparing the critic's Q estimates with the actual returns is one of the most useful diagnostics when tuning SAC.

    误区 5：用训练时的随机策略评估，或只看一种代价Pitfall 5: evaluating the noisy training policy, or counting only one cost

    PPO 和 SAC 训练时都在采样动作，部署时一般用均值动作（本页的评估曲线就是这样做的），两者的表现可能差得很多；报告结果时要说明用的是哪一个。比较算法时也要说明横轴：环境步数、并行仿真步数、梯度更新次数还是墙钟时间。FIG 6 里同一组曲线，换一个横轴，胜负就倒过来了。

    PPO and SAC both sample actions during training, while deployment usually takes the mean action (as this page's evaluation curves do), and the two can perform very differently; say which one you report. When comparing algorithms, also say what the horizontal axis is: environment steps, parallel sim steps, gradient updates, or wall-clock time.
