损失、泛化与正则化Losses, Generalization & Regularization

训练只能在手头的数据上把损失压低，机器人要的却是在没见过的情况里也做对。损失函数决定你惩罚什么（它其实是对噪声的一个假设），泛化问的是训练集上的好成绩能不能带到新数据上，正则化则是防止灵活的模型把训练数据连同噪声一起背下来的各种办法。本节在浏览器里现场训练小网络：看它过拟合、早停、被 L2、dropout 和数据增广拉回来，再看偏差与方差、L1 与 L2、双下降，以及机器人最大的泛化难题：部署时的数据分布与训练时不同。 Training can only push the loss down on the data at hand; a robot needs to be right in situations it has never seen. The loss decides what you penalize (it is really an assumption about the noise), generalization asks whether a good score on the training set carries over to new data, and regularization is the family of tricks that stop a flexible model from memorizing the training data, noise and all. This lesson trains small networks live in your browser: watch one overfit, stop early, and get pulled back by L2, dropout and data augmentation; then see bias and variance, L1 versus L2, double descent, and the robot's biggest generalization problem: at deployment the data come from a different distribution than in training.

    1为什么机器人需要它Why robots need this

    机器人越来越多的部件是从数据里学出来的：关节摩擦的补偿模型、“这次抓取会不会成功”的预测器、从人类示教学来的整套策略。16.1 节用最大似然拟合了回归和分类模型，并在留出的测试集上打分。训练时我们最小化的是训练集上的平均损失，可我们真正在乎的是机器人明天遇到的情况：新的物体、新的光照、新的速度。两者之差就是泛化问题。

    有三件事决定泛化好不好。第一，损失函数：平方误差、L1、Huber 和交叉熵各自隐含着对噪声的不同假设，遇到传感器野值或类别标签时表现完全不同。第二，容量与正则化：一个参数比数据点多得多的网络能把训练误差压到几乎为零，包括把噪声也背下来；验证集、早停、L2、dropout、数据增广是防止这件事的工具。第三，对机器人最要命的是分布偏移：机器人自己的动作会改变它接下来看到的东西，部署时的输入分布和训练时不同，而模型在训练分布之外几乎没有任何保证（22.1 节的模仿学习会把这个问题放大成误差累积）。

    More and more of a robot is learned from data: a compensation model for joint friction, a predictor of "will this grasp succeed", a whole policy learned from human demonstrations. Lesson 16.1 fitted regression and classification models by maximum likelihood and scored them on a held-out test set. During training we minimize the average loss on the training set, but what we really care about is what the robot meets tomorrow: new objects, new lighting, new speeds. The difference between the two is the generalization problem.

    Three things decide how well a model generalizes. First, the loss: squared error, L1, Huber and cross-entropy each carry a different assumption about the noise, and they behave completely differently on sensor outliers or class labels. Second, capacity and regularization: a network with far more parameters than data points can drive its training error to almost zero, memorizing the noise along the way; validation sets, early stopping, L2, dropout and data augmentation are the tools against that. Third, and worst for robots, distribution shift: a robot's own actions change what it sees next, so the inputs at deployment are distributed differently from those in training, and a model has almost no guarantee outside its training distribution (imitation learning in Lesson 22.1 turns this into compounding errors).

    2直觉：损失函数是对噪声的一个假设Intuition: a loss is an assumption about the noise

    4.6 节已经推出：如果测量噪声是高斯的，最大似然就是最小化平方误差；如果是拉普拉斯的（尾巴更厚），就是最小化绝对误差，估计值从均值变成中位数。换句话说，选损失就是选噪声模型。对拟合过程更直观的看法是每个数据点对拟合施加的“拉力”：损失 \(\hl{rho}{\rho(r)}\) 对残差 \(r\) 的导数 \(\hl{psi}{\psi(r)=\rho'(r)}\) 叫影响函数。平方损失的拉力与残差成正比，一个离谱的点拉力最大；L1 的拉力恒为 ±1，与残差大小无关；Huber 损失在 \(|r|\le\delta\) 时是平方、之外是线性，拉力被截在 ±δ。

    下图是一个力传感器的标定：横轴是加的载荷，纵轴是读数，10 个点基本在一条直线上，外加一个可以拖动的点。把它拖成一个“野值”（比如接触瞬间的冲击读数），看三条拟合直线各被拉走多少。

    Lesson 4.6 derived that if the measurement noise is Gaussian, maximum likelihood means minimizing the squared error; if it is Laplace (heavier tails), it means minimizing the absolute error, and the estimate moves from the mean to the median. In other words, choosing a loss is choosing a noise model. A more tangible view of what happens during fitting is the "pull" each data point exerts on the fit: the derivative \(\hl{psi}{\psi(r)=\rho'(r)}\) of the loss \(\hl{rho}{\rho(r)}\) with respect to the residual \(r\), called the influence function. Under the squared loss the pull grows with the residual, so the most absurd point pulls hardest; under L1 the pull is always ±1, whatever the residual; the Huber loss is quadratic for \(|r|\le\delta\) and linear beyond, so its pull is capped at ±δ.

Turn it into an "outlier" (say an impact spike at the moment of contact) and watch how far each of the three fitted lines gets pulled.

    分类：为什么用交叉熵而不是平方误差Classification: why cross-entropy and not squared error

    分类器输出一个 logit \(z\)，经过 sigmoid 得到概率 \(\sigma(z)\)（多类时是 softmax，16.1 节）。最大似然给出交叉熵 \(\hl{ce}{-\log\sigma(z)}\)（真实标签 \(y=1\) 时）。你也可以图省事，对概率用平方误差 \(\hl{sqp}{(\sigma(z)-y)^2}\)，两者的最小值都在 \(\sigma(z)=1\)。区别在于模型错得很自信时：比如 \(z=-6\)，模型以 99.75% 的把握说“不会成功”而实际成功了。交叉熵对 \(z\) 的梯度是 \(\sigma(z)-y\approx-1\)，推力最大；平方误差的梯度多了一个因子 \(\sigma(z)(1-\sigma(z))\approx0.0025\)，几乎推不动：sigmoid 在饱和区是平的，平方误差把这种平坦原样传给了梯度，交叉熵的对数恰好把它抵消掉。

    A classifier outputs a logit \(z\) and turns it into a probability \(\sigma(z)\) with the sigmoid (softmax for several classes, Lesson 16.1). Maximum likelihood gives the cross-entropy \(\hl{ce}{-\log\sigma(z)}\) (for the true label \(y=1\)). You could also take a shortcut and use the squared error on the probability, \(\hl{sqp}{(\sigma(z)-y)^2}\); both are minimized at \(\sigma(z)=1\). The difference shows when the model is confidently wrong: say \(z=-6\), so the model says "will fail" with 99.75% confidence and the grasp actually succeeded. The gradient of cross-entropy with respect to \(z\) is \(\sigma(z)-y\approx-1\), the strongest push there is; the gradient of the squared error carries an extra factor \(\sigma(z)(1-\sigma(z))\approx0.0025\) and barely pushes at all: the sigmoid is flat when saturated, the squared error passes that flatness on to the gradient, and the logarithm in the cross-entropy cancels it exactly.

    • 把初始 logit 设到 −5 或更低（模型自信地错了），ε = 0，按“训练”：交叉熵不到 10 步就越过 0.5，平方误差至少要多 10 倍的步数（−5 时 85 步，−6 时 214 步）。Set the initial logit to −5 or lower (confidently wrong), ε = 0, and press "Train": cross-entropy crosses 0.5 within 10 steps, squared error needs at least 10 times as many (85 steps from −5, 214 from −6).
    • 选标签平滑 ε = 0.1 或 0.2 并训练至少 150 步：交叉熵的 logit 停在有限的 \(z^\ast\)（ε = 0.1 时为 2.944），不再无限增大；对比 ε = 0 时 400 步后 \(z\) 仍在缓慢上涨。Choose label smoothing ε = 0.1 or 0.2 and train for at least 150 steps: the cross-entropy logit settles at a finite \(z^\ast\) (2.944 for ε = 0.1) instead of growing forever; compare ε = 0, where \(z\) is still creeping up after 400 steps.

    第二个任务揭示了交叉熵的另一面：对于可分的数据，它的最小值在 \(z\to\infty\)，模型会被一直推向“100% 确定”，权重越来越大。标签平滑（label smoothing）把目标从 1 改成 \(1-\varepsilon+\varepsilon/K\)（\(K\) 类，图里 \(K=2\)，即 \(1-\varepsilon/2\)），最优 logit 就成了有限值 \(z^\ast=\log\frac{1-\varepsilon/2}{\varepsilon/2}\)。这是一种正则化：它阻止模型过度自信，对标注噪声也更宽容；代价是预测概率被系统性地压低，需要校准好的概率时要小心。

    The second task reveals the other side of cross-entropy: on separable data its minimum is at \(z\to\infty\), so the model keeps being pushed towards "100% sure" and its weights keep growing. Label smoothing changes the target from 1 to \(1-\varepsilon+\varepsilon/K\) (\(K\) classes; \(K=2\) in the figure, i.e. \(1-\varepsilon/2\)), and the best logit becomes the finite \(z^\ast=\log\frac{1-\varepsilon/2}{\varepsilon/2}\). This is a regularizer: it stops the model from becoming overconfident and is more forgiving of label noise; the price is that predicted probabilities are pulled down systematically, so be careful where calibrated probabilities matter.

    核心洞见Key insight

    选损失要回答两个问题：噪声长什么样（高斯 → 平方，有野值 → Huber 或 L1，类别 → 交叉熵），以及梯度在哪里还有力气（平方误差加 sigmoid 在饱和区没有梯度，交叉熵有）。下面三节转向第二件事：损失再合适，模型也可能把训练数据背下来。

    Choosing a loss answers two questions: what does the noise look like (Gaussian → squared, outliers → Huber or L1, classes → cross-entropy), and where does the gradient still have strength (squared error behind a sigmoid has none in the saturated region, cross-entropy does). The next three sections turn to the second issue: even with the right loss, a model can memorize its training data.

    Notice what happens in the default run: by step 2000 the training loss is only about \(2\times10^{-5}\); the network passes almost exactly through every training point, noise included, and the amber curve bends wildly between them. Methods with randomness (dropout, jitter, minibatches once there are more than 16 points) keep the validation loss jittering a little, which is why the readout shows both "now" and "last-1000-step mean".     有意思的细节Things worth noticing

    (1) 验证损失的最低值（约 0.025）贴近噪声水平 0.0225：这个模型能做到的最好，就是只剩下不可约的测量噪声。(2) 过拟合不是因为“训练太久”本身，而是参数太多、数据太少、又没有约束；同样训练 5000 步，96 个点就几乎不过拟合。(3) 一个运行的数字依赖于随机种子：按“换一组数据”试几次，最低点可能在 100 到 450 步之间，但“训练一路降、验证先降后升”的形状每次都在。

    (1) The lowest validation loss (about 0.025) sits close to the noise floor 0.0225: the best this model can do is leave only the irreducible measurement noise. (2) Overfitting is not caused by "training too long" as such, but by too many parameters, too little data and no constraint; trained for the same 5000 steps, 96 points hardly overfit. (3) The numbers of one run depend on the random seed: press "New data" a few times and the minimum may land anywhere between about 100 and 450 steps, but the shape, training loss falling and validation loss falling then rising, is there every time.

    4另一个视角：偏差、方差与交叉验证Another view: bias, variance and cross-validation

    为什么模型越灵活越容易过拟合？设想你可以从同一个分布里抽出很多个不同的训练集，每个都拟合一次。偏差是这些拟合的平均离真实函数有多远：模型太僵硬（比如用直线拟合 S 形曲线），平均下来也是错的。方差是这些拟合彼此差多少：模型太灵活，就会跟着每个训练集特有的噪声走，换一组数据就换一个样子。平方误差恰好能分解成三部分：

    Why does a more flexible model overfit more easily? Imagine you could draw many different training sets from the same distribution and fit each once. The bias is how far the average of these fits is from the true function: a model that is too rigid (a straight line through an S-shaped curve) is wrong even on average. The variance is how much the fits differ from each other: a model that is too flexible follows the noise peculiar to each training set and changes shape with every new set. The squared error splits exactly into three parts:

    $$ \mathbb E\big[(y-\hat f(x))^2\big] = \hl{noise}{\sigma^2} + \hl{bias}{\big(\mathbb E[\hat f(x)]-f(x)\big)^2} + \hl{var}{\mathbb E\Big[\big(\hat f(x)-\mathbb E[\hat f(x)]\big)^2\Big]} $$

    左边是期望测试误差，期望是对“抽一个训练集”（以及新样本的噪声）取的；右边第一项是测量噪声（不可约），第二项是偏差的平方，第三项是方差（推导见第 7 节）。下图用多项式回归把它们算出来：多项式的次数就是“容量”旋钮。它是线性最小二乘，有闭式解，所以可以一次对 40 个训练集各拟合一遍。

    The left side is the expected test error, the expectation being over "draw a training set" (and the noise of the new example); on the right, the first term is the measurement noise (irreducible), the second the squared bias, the third the variance (derivation in Section 7).     At λ = 0 the curve is the textbook U: degrees 0 and 1 have a large bias (a line cannot follow the jump of friction near zero speed), degree 5 has the lowest expected test error (0.053), at degree 9 the variance is already 0.40, more than ten times the bias² (0.025), and at degree 12 the test error exceeds 20. The conclusion of the second task matters more: a flexible model with the right regularization beats the best small model; degrees 9 to 12 with λ = 0.01 reach a test error of about 0.042 to 0.045. This is the normal situation in deep learning: use a bigger network than needed, then keep the variance down with regularization and enough data.

    Cross-validation is how you estimate the test error in reality, where you have one dataset, not 40. \(k\)-fold cross-validation splits the data into \(k\) parts, trains on \(k-1\) of them and evaluates on the remaining one, in turn, and averages (Section 7).     5正则化：L2 让参数变小，L1 让参数变成零Regularization: L2 makes parameters small, L1 makes them zero

    正则化就是在训练损失后面加一项惩罚 \(\lambda\,\Omega(\boldsymbol\theta)\)。4.6 节说过它就是一个先验：\(\hl{pen}{\Omega=\|\boldsymbol\theta\|_2^2}\)（L2，岭回归，权重衰减）对应高斯先验，\(\Omega=\|\boldsymbol\theta\|_1\)（L1，lasso）对应拉普拉斯先验。两者都让参数变小，但方式完全不同。几何上看，带惩罚的最小化等价于“在一个范数球里找损失最低的点”（λ 越大，球越小）：L2 的球是圆的，损失的等高椭圆一般在某个没有坐标为零的地方碰到它；L1 的球是一个菱形，有尖角在坐标轴上，椭圆往往先碰到尖角，于是某些坐标恰好等于零。

    Regularization adds a penalty \(\lambda\,\Omega(\boldsymbol\theta)\) to the training loss. Lesson 4.6 showed that this is a prior: \(\hl{pen}{\Omega=\|\boldsymbol\theta\|_2^2}\) (L2, ridge, weight decay) corresponds to a Gaussian prior and \(\Omega=\|\boldsymbol\theta\|_1\) (L1, lasso) to a Laplace prior. Both make parameters smaller, but in completely different ways. Geometrically, penalized minimization is equivalent to "find the lowest-loss point inside a norm ball" (larger λ, smaller ball): the L2 ball is round, and the elliptical loss contours usually touch it where no coordinate is zero; the L1 ball is a diamond with corners on the axes, the ellipse tends to hit a corner first, and some coordinates come out exactly zero.

    • 选“L1”，调 λ 让右图里非零的系数恰好是第 1、3、6 个：lasso 自己挑出了真正有用的特征（λ 在 0.14 到 0.40 之间都可以）。再切到“L2”看：没有一个系数会变成零。Choose "L1" and tune λ so that exactly coefficients 1, 3 and 6 are nonzero on the right: the lasso has picked out the useful features by itself (any λ between 0.14 and 0.40 works). Then switch to "L2": no coefficient ever becomes zero.

    其他正则化手段Other regularizers

    深度学习里显式的 L1/L2 只是一部分，更常见的是下面这些，FIG 3 里试过其中三种：

    • 权重衰减：每步把权重乘以略小于 1 的数。对普通 SGD 这与 L2 惩罚等价，对 Adam 不等价（第 7 节），所以现在通常用 AdamW 的“解耦”权重衰减。
    • 早停：用验证集挑训练得最好的那一刻。对二次损失上的梯度下降，训练 \(t\) 步大致等价于 \(\lambda\approx1/(\eta t)\) 的 L2（第 7 节）：训练时间本身就是一个正则化旋钮。
    • Dropout：训练时每个隐藏单元以概率 \(p\) 被随机置零，留下的乘以 \(1/(1-p)\)；测试时全部保留。网络不能依赖任何单个单元，相当于训练了大量共享权重的子网络的集成。
    • 数据增广：对输入做不改变标签的变换，制造“免费”的新样本。对机器人图像是随机裁剪、平移、颜色抖动；对 1 维输入就是 FIG 3 的输入抖动，训练时加小噪声在一定条件下等价于一种平滑正则（Tikhonov 正则化，Bishop 1995）。增广编码了你对不变性的先验知识，常常是最有效的一种。
    • 标签平滑：FIG 2，限制分类器的自信程度。
    • SGD 的噪声：6.4 节的小批量噪声本身也有正则化作用，这也是极大批量训练常常泛化更差、需要重新调学习率的原因之一。

    In deep learning explicit L1/L2 are only part of the story; more common are the following, three of which you tried in FIG 3:

    • Weight decay: multiply the weights by a number slightly below 1 at every step. For plain SGD this is equivalent to an L2 penalty, for Adam it is not (Section 7), which is why AdamW's "decoupled" weight decay is the usual choice today.
    • Early stopping: use the validation set to pick the best moment of training. For gradient descent on a quadratic loss, training for \(t\) steps is roughly equivalent to L2 with \(\lambda\approx1/(\eta t)\) (Section 7): training time itself is a regularization knob.
    • Dropout: during training each hidden unit is zeroed at random with probability \(p\) and the survivors are multiplied by \(1/(1-p)\); at test time all units are kept. The network cannot rely on any single unit, which amounts to training a huge ensemble of weight-sharing subnetworks.
    • Data augmentation: apply label-preserving transformations to the inputs to make "free" new examples. For robot images: random crops, shifts, colour jitter; for a 1D input: the input jitter of FIG 3, and training with small input noise is, under some conditions, equivalent to a smoothness regularizer (Tikhonov regularization, Bishop 1995). Augmentation encodes your prior knowledge of invariances and is often the most effective of all.
    • Label smoothing: FIG 2; limits how confident a classifier can get.
    • SGD noise: the minibatch noise of Lesson 6.4 regularizes too, one reason why very large batches often generalize worse and need the learning rate retuned.

    6再多一点参数：双下降Even more parameters: double descent

    按照 U 形曲线，参数远多于数据的现代网络应该过拟合得一塌糊涂，可它们常常泛化得很好。一个可以完全看清楚的例子：用 \(p\) 个随机 ReLU 特征 \(\phi_j(x)=\max(0,s_j(x-t_j))\)（随机的拐点 \(t_j\) 和方向 \(s_j\)）做线性回归，只学最后一层权重。\(p<n\) 时用最小二乘；\(p>n\) 时有无穷多组权重能精确穿过所有训练点，我们取其中范数最小的那一组 \(\hl{minnorm}{\mathbf w=\Phi^{+}\mathbf y}\)（1.13 节的伪逆）。测试误差随 \(p\) 先降、在 \(\hl{thresh}{p\approx n}\) 处冲上一个尖峰、然后再次下降，这叫双下降。

    By the U-shaped curve, modern networks with far more parameters than data should overfit terribly, yet they often generalize well. An example you can see completely: linear regression on \(p\) random ReLU features \(\phi_j(x)=\max(0,s_j(x-t_j))\) (random kinks \(t_j\) and directions \(s_j\)), learning only the last-layer weights. For \(p<n\) use least squares; for \(p>n\) infinitely many weight vectors pass exactly through all training points, and we take the one with the smallest norm, \(\hl{minnorm}{\mathbf w=\Phi^{+}\mathbf y}\) (the pseudo-inverse of Lesson 1.13). The test error first falls as \(p\) grows, shoots up to a spike at \(\hl{thresh}{p\approx n}\), and then falls again: double descent.

    • λ = 0：先把 \(p\) 拉到 15 到 17 之间，看平均误差冲到上千（左图的拟合在点之间剧烈震荡）；再拉到 \(p\ge100\)：平均误差回落到绿色虚线以下，比经典区里最好的还低。最后试试 λ = 10⁻⁵：尖峰消失。λ = 0: first move \(p\) to between 15 and 17 and see the mean error shoot into the thousands (the fit on the left oscillates wildly between the points); then move to \(p\ge100\): the mean error falls back below the green dashed line, lower than the best of the classical regime. Finally try λ = 10⁻⁵: the spike is gone.

    数字：经典区（\(p<n\)）里平均误差最低约 0.054（\(p=6\)）；\(p=15\) 时平均误差约 8600，\(p\ge100\) 时回落到 0.017 到 0.019。看紫色小点就会发现，平均值的巨大尖峰几乎全来自 20 次抽样中的一次（它在 \(p=15\) 时误差约 \(10^5\)）；中位数（典型的一次抽样）也有一个温和得多的鼓包：从 \(p=8\) 时的 0.027 升到 \(p=12\) 到 13 时的 0.08，再降到 \(p\ge100\) 时的 0.013，不到它在经典区最好值的一半。为什么会有尖峰？\(p\approx n\) 时特征矩阵 \(\Phi\) 接近方阵，它的最小奇异值可能很小，为了精确穿过每一个带噪声的点，权重必须非常大（方差随 \(\sum 1/s_i^2\) 增长，第 7 节）；\(p\) 再大，能穿过所有点的解有很多，最小范数的那个最“平缓”，方差反而下降。一点点岭正则化（λ = 10⁻⁵）就把尖峰抹平，这是实践中很少看到尖峰的一个原因：几乎总有某种显式或隐式的正则化。深度网络里也观察到了随模型大小、甚至随训练轮数出现的双下降（Nakkiran 等，2020）。本节只点到为止；它说明“参数个数”并不是衡量容量的好指标，有效容量还取决于优化器隐式偏好的那类解。

    The numbers: the lowest mean error in the classical regime (\(p<n\)) is about 0.054 (\(p=6\)); at \(p=15\) the mean error is about 8600, and for \(p\ge100\) it falls back to 0.017 to 0.019. The purple dots show that the huge spike of the mean comes almost entirely from one of the 20 draws (whose error at \(p=15\) is about \(10^5\)); the median (a typical draw) has a much gentler bump: from 0.027 at \(p=8\) up to 0.08 at \(p=12\) to 13, and down to 0.013 for \(p\ge100\), less than half its best in the classical regime. Why the spike? At \(p\approx n\) the feature matrix \(\Phi\) is nearly square and its smallest singular value can be tiny, so passing exactly through every noisy point needs very large weights (the variance grows like \(\sum 1/s_i^2\), Section 7); with larger \(p\) many solutions pass through all points, the minimum-norm one is the "gentlest", and its variance goes down. A tiny ridge (λ = 10⁻⁵) flattens the spike, which is one reason it is rarely seen in practice: there is almost always some explicit or implicit regularization. Double descent has also been observed in deep networks, as a function of model size and even of training epochs (Nakkiran et al., 2020). This section only touches on it; its lesson is that "number of parameters" is a poor measure of capacity, because the effective capacity also depends on which solutions the optimizer implicitly prefers.

    风险、经验风险与泛化差距Risk, empirical risk and the generalization gap

    模型 \(f_{\boldsymbol\theta}\)，逐样本损失 \(\ell(\hat y,y)\)，数据来自分布 \(p(x,y)\)。我们想要小的风险（期望损失），手里却只有 \(n\) 个样本上的经验风险：

    A model \(f_{\boldsymbol\theta}\), a per-example loss \(\ell(\hat y,y)\), data from a distribution \(p(x,y)\). We want a small risk (expected loss), but all we have is the empirical risk over \(n\) examples:

    $$ R(\boldsymbol\theta)=\mathbb E_{(x,y)\sim p}\big[\ell(f_{\boldsymbol\theta}(x),y)\big],\qquad \hl{train}{\hat R(\boldsymbol\theta)}=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n \ell(f_{\boldsymbol\theta}(x_i),y_i),\qquad \Delta=R(\hat{\boldsymbol\theta})-\hat R(\hat{\boldsymbol\theta}) $$

    对固定的 \(\boldsymbol\theta\)，\(\hat R\) 是 \(R\) 的无偏估计；但 \(\hat{\boldsymbol\theta}\) 是用同一批数据挑出来的，挑选过程偏向那些恰好在这批数据上表现好的参数，所以 \(\hat R(\hat{\boldsymbol\theta})\) 系统性地偏低，泛化差距 \(\Delta\) 为正。验证集上的损失 \(\hl{val}{\hat R_{\mathrm{val}}}\) 用的是没参与挑选的数据，因此是 \(R\) 的公正估计；可一旦你用它来挑选（早停、调 λ），它也会开始偏低，只是偏得少得多，这就是还要留一个测试集的原因。

    For a fixed \(\boldsymbol\theta\), \(\hat R\) is an unbiased estimate of \(R\); but \(\hat{\boldsymbol\theta}\) was chosen using the same data, and the choice favours parameters that happen to do well on this particular sample, so \(\hat R(\hat{\boldsymbol\theta})\) is biased low and the generalization gap \(\Delta\) is positive. The validation loss \(\hl{val}{\hat R_{\mathrm{val}}}\) uses data that took no part in the choice, so it is an unbiased estimate of \(R\); once you use it to choose (early stopping, tuning λ) it starts to be biased low too, only much less, which is why a test set is kept as well.

    L2 与 L1 的解The L2 and L1 solutions

    对线性最小二乘 \(\frac1{2n}\|X\mathbf w-\mathbf y\|^2\)，记 \(A=X^\top X/n\)、\(\hat{\mathbf w}\) 为无正则化的解，则损失等于 \(\tfrac12(\mathbf w-\hat{\mathbf w})^\top A(\mathbf w-\hat{\mathbf w})\) 加常数（FIG 5 用的就是这个形式）。加上 \(\tfrac\lambda2\|\mathbf w\|_2^2\)，令梯度为零：

    For linear least squares \(\frac1{2n}\|X\mathbf w-\mathbf y\|^2\), with \(A=X^\top X/n\) and \(\hat{\mathbf w}\) the unregularized solution, the loss equals \(\tfrac12(\mathbf w-\hat{\mathbf w})^\top A(\mathbf w-\hat{\mathbf w})\) plus a constant (the form FIG 5 uses). Add \(\tfrac\lambda2\|\mathbf w\|_2^2\) and set the gradient to zero:

    $$ \mathbf w_{\mathrm{L2}}=(A+\lambda I)^{-1}A\,\hat{\mathbf w}=\sum_i \frac{d_i}{d_i+\lambda}\,(\mathbf u_i^\top\hat{\mathbf w})\,\mathbf u_i\qquad(A=\textstyle\sum_i d_i\mathbf u_i\mathbf u_i^\top) $$

    L2 把每个特征方向按 \(d_i/(d_i+\lambda)\) 收缩：数据约束强的方向（大 \(d_i\)）几乎不动，数据几乎不约束的方向（小 \(d_i\)，方差的主要来源）被压向零，但永远不会恰好为零。L1 不可导，用次梯度：在 \(A\) 为对角阵（特征正交）时问题按坐标分开，每个坐标的最优条件 \(d_i(w_i-\hat w_i)+\lambda\,\partial|w_i|\ni0\) 给出软阈值

    L2 shrinks each feature direction by \(d_i/(d_i+\lambda)\): directions the data constrain strongly (large \(d_i\)) barely move, directions the data hardly constrain (small \(d_i\), the main source of variance) are pushed towards zero, but never exactly to zero. L1 is not differentiable, so use subgradients: when \(A\) is diagonal (orthogonal features) the problem separates by coordinate, and the optimality condition \(d_i(w_i-\hat w_i)+\lambda\,\partial|w_i|\ni0\) of each coordinate gives the soft threshold

    $$ w_i=\operatorname{sign}(\hat w_i)\,\max\!\Big(0,\ |\hat w_i|-\frac{\lambda}{d_i}\Big) $$

    只要 \(|\hat w_i|\le\lambda/d_i\)，解就精确为零：这就是稀疏性的代数来源，对应几何上的菱形尖角。特征相关时没有闭式解，FIG 5 用坐标下降：反复对每个坐标做上面这个软阈值，其他坐标暂时固定。lasso 的已知弱点也在这里：一组高度相关的有用特征，它往往只留一个、随意丢掉其余的。

    Whenever \(|\hat w_i|\le\lambda/d_i\), the solution is exactly zero: this is the algebraic source of sparsity, matching the corners of the diamond. With correlated features there is no closed form, and FIG 5 uses coordinate descent: apply this soft threshold to each coordinate in turn with the others held fixed, and repeat. The lasso's known weakness shows here too: from a group of highly correlated useful features it tends to keep one and drop the rest more or less arbitrarily.

    早停、权重衰减与优化器Early stopping, weight decay and the optimizer

    对上面的二次损失从 \(\mathbf w=0\) 出发做梯度下降（学习率 \(\eta\)），沿第 \(i\) 个特征方向第 \(t\) 步的分量是 \(\big(1-(1-\eta d_i)^t\big)\,\hat w_i\)。与 L2 的 \(d_i/(d_i+\lambda)\) 比较：两者都让大 \(d_i\) 方向先学到、小 \(d_i\) 方向被抑制；当 \(\eta d_i\ll1\) 时，\(t\) 步的早停大致相当于 \(\lambda\approx1/(\eta t)\)（Goodfellow 等《深度学习》7.8 节）。所以 FIG 3 里验证损失先降后升，是因为网络先学到了数据支持强的平滑部分，后学到的是被噪声主导的方向。

    权重衰减 ≠ L2（对 Adam）。L2 惩罚把 \(2\lambda\boldsymbol\theta\) 加进梯度；SGD 的一步 \(\boldsymbol\theta\leftarrow\boldsymbol\theta-\eta(\mathbf g+2\lambda\boldsymbol\theta)=(1-2\eta\lambda)\boldsymbol\theta-\eta\mathbf g\) 正好就是“先衰减再走一步”。Adam 却会把整个梯度除以它的二阶矩估计（6.3、6.4 节），于是 \(2\lambda\boldsymbol\theta\) 这一项也被逐坐标缩放，梯度大的参数几乎不被衰减。AdamW（Loshchilov & Hutter, 2019）把衰减从梯度里拿出来单独做：\(\boldsymbol\theta\leftarrow\boldsymbol\theta-\eta\,(\mathbf u_t+\lambda\boldsymbol\theta)\)，其中 \(\mathbf u_t\) 是 Adam 本来要走的那一步（只由数据损失的梯度算出）。FIG 3 用的是写进损失的 L2 惩罚（加在梯度里，再交给 Adam），这在我们这里也足够有效。

    Dropout：训练时隐藏单元输出 \(h_j\) 换成 \(m_j h_j/(1-p)\)，\(m_j\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(1-p)\)，于是 \(\mathbb E[m_jh_j/(1-p)]=h_j\)，测试时直接用 \(h_j\)，期望不变（“inverted dropout”）。

    Run gradient descent with learning rate \(\eta\) on the quadratic above, starting from \(\mathbf w=0\); after \(t\) steps the component along feature direction \(i\) is \(\big(1-(1-\eta d_i)^t\big)\,\hat w_i\). Compare with L2's \(d_i/(d_i+\lambda)\): both let large-\(d_i\) directions be learned first and hold back small-\(d_i\) ones; for \(\eta d_i\ll1\), stopping after \(t\) steps is roughly \(\lambda\approx1/(\eta t)\) (Goodfellow et al., Deep Learning, Section 7.8). That is why FIG 3's validation loss falls and then rises: the network learns the smooth part strongly supported by the data first and the noise-dominated directions later.

    Weight decay ≠ L2 (for Adam). An L2 penalty adds \(2\lambda\boldsymbol\theta\) to the gradient; an SGD step \(\boldsymbol\theta\leftarrow\boldsymbol\theta-\eta(\mathbf g+2\lambda\boldsymbol\theta)=(1-2\eta\lambda)\boldsymbol\theta-\eta\mathbf g\) is exactly "decay, then step". Adam, however, divides the whole gradient by its second-moment estimate (Lessons 6.3 and 6.4), so the \(2\lambda\boldsymbol\theta\) term is rescaled per coordinate as well, and parameters with large gradients are barely decayed. AdamW (Loshchilov & Hutter, 2019) takes the decay out of the gradient and applies it separately: \(\boldsymbol\theta\leftarrow\boldsymbol\theta-\eta\,(\mathbf u_t+\lambda\boldsymbol\theta)\), where \(\mathbf u_t\) is the step Adam would take (computed from the gradient of the data loss only). FIG 3 uses an L2 penalty written into the loss (added to the gradient, then handed to Adam), which is effective enough here.

    Dropout: during training a hidden output \(h_j\) is replaced by \(m_j h_j/(1-p)\) with \(m_j\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(1-p)\), so \(\mathbb E[m_jh_j/(1-p)]=h_j\) and at test time \(h_j\) is used as is, with the same expectation ("inverted dropout").

    最小范数解与双下降The minimum-norm solution and double descent

    \(\Phi\in\R^{n\times p}\) 的奇异值分解 \(\Phi=\sum_i s_i\mathbf u_i\mathbf v_i^\top\)。\(p\ge n\) 且满秩时，所有插值解中范数最小的是 \(\mathbf w=\Phi^+\mathbf y=\sum_i \frac{\mathbf u_i^\top\mathbf y}{s_i}\mathbf v_i\)（1.13 节）。观测噪声 \(\boldsymbol\epsilon\) 通过 \(1/s_i\) 被放大，噪声在权重上造成的方差是 \(\sigma^2\sum_i 1/s_i^2\)；\(p\) 刚好等于 \(n\) 附近时最小的 \(s_i\) 最接近零，所以出现尖峰。加岭正则把 \(1/s_i\) 换成 \(s_i/(s_i^2+n\lambda)\)，上限是 \(1/(2\sqrt{n\lambda})\)，尖峰随之消失。

    Take the SVD \(\Phi=\sum_i s_i\mathbf u_i\mathbf v_i^\top\) of \(\Phi\in\R^{n\times p}\). For \(p\ge n\) and full rank, the minimum-norm interpolating solution is \(\mathbf w=\Phi^+\mathbf y=\sum_i \frac{\mathbf u_i^\top\mathbf y}{s_i}\mathbf v_i\) (Lesson 1.13). Observation noise \(\boldsymbol\epsilon\) is amplified by \(1/s_i\), and the variance it causes in the weights is \(\sigma^2\sum_i 1/s_i^2\); near \(p=n\) the smallest \(s_i\) is closest to zero, hence the spike. A ridge replaces \(1/s_i\) by \(s_i/(s_i^2+n\lambda)\), which is at most \(1/(2\sqrt{n\lambda})\), and the spike disappears.

    分布偏移Distribution shift

    上面所有的保证都假设训练数据和测试数据来自同一个分布。协变量偏移是最常见的破坏方式（下标 tr 表示训练，te 表示测试）：\(p(y\mid x)\) 不变（物理规律没变），但输入的分布从 \(p_{\mathrm{tr}}(x)\) 变成了 \(\hl{shift}{p_{\mathrm{te}}(x)}\)（机器人去了没去过的状态）。测试风险可以改写成训练分布上的加权期望：

    Every guarantee above assumes that training and test data come from the same distribution. Covariate shift is the most common way this breaks (subscripts tr for training, te for test): \(p(y\mid x)\) stays the same (the physics has not changed), but the input distribution moves from \(p_{\mathrm{tr}}(x)\) to \(\hl{shift}{p_{\mathrm{te}}(x)}\) (the robot goes to states it has not visited). The test risk can be rewritten as a weighted expectation over the training distribution:

    $$ R_{\mathrm{te}}=\mathbb E_{x\sim p_{\mathrm{te}}}\big[\ell\big]=\mathbb E_{x\sim p_{\mathrm{tr}}}\Big[\frac{\hl{shift}{p_{\mathrm{te}}(x)}}{p_{\mathrm{tr}}(x)}\,\ell\Big] $$

    这就是重要性加权（Shimodaira, 2000），前提是 \(p_{\mathrm{tr}}(x)>0\) 处处覆盖 \(p_{\mathrm{te}}\)。如果测试分布落在训练分布完全没有覆盖的地方，权重是无穷大，任何重新加权都救不了：模型在那里的预测只取决于它的结构偏好（外推），数据不提供任何约束。下一节就是这种情形。

    This is importance weighting (Shimodaira, 2000), and it requires \(p_{\mathrm{tr}}(x)>0\) wherever \(p_{\mathrm{te}}\) has mass. If the test distribution lands where the training distribution has no coverage at all, the weight is infinite and no reweighting can help: the model's predictions there depend only on its structural preferences (extrapolation), with no constraint from data. The next section is exactly that case.

    Three observations. (3) The only remedy that really works is to go there and collect data. A whole family of robotics methods follows that idea: DAgger runs the policy and asks an expert to label the states it reaches (Lesson 22.1); domain randomization widens the training distribution in simulation until the real world falls inside it (Lesson 23.2).

    真实系统里In real systems

    鲁棒损失：DQN（Mnih 等，Nature 2015）把 TD 误差截断在 [−1, 1]，等价于 Huber 损失；Fast R-CNN（Girshick, 2015）用 smooth L1 回归检测框；ACT（Zhao 等，RSS 2023，22.2 节）用 L1 而不是 L2 作为动作的重建损失。 模仿学习里的 MSE：示教在同一个状态下可能有两种做法（从左绕或从右绕），MSE 学到的是它们的平均，可能直接撞上障碍；这就是 ACT、Diffusion Policy 等生成式策略存在的原因（22.1、22.2 节）。 数据增广：从像素学习的强化学习中，随机平移/裁剪一类的增广带来了很大提升（RAD：Laskin 等，2020；DrQ：Kostrikov 等，2020），视觉运动策略普遍使用随机裁剪和颜色抖动。 验证损失与真实成功率：robomimic（Mandlekar 等，CoRL 2021）的研究发现，按最低验证损失挑选的策略检查点并不可靠，需要在环境里实际评估；原因之一正是闭环执行时的分布偏移。 集成与不确定性：PETS（Chua 等，2018）用概率网络的集成学习动力学模型（24.2 节）；深度集成（Lakshminarayanan 等，2017）是估计不确定性的常用基线（21.3 节）。 按轨迹划分数据：同一条示教轨迹里相邻的帧几乎一样，随机按帧划分训练/验证会让验证集“偷看”到训练数据，应按轨迹、场景或物体划分。

    Robust losses: DQN (Mnih et al., Nature 2015) clips the TD error to [−1, 1], which is equivalent to a Huber loss; Fast R-CNN (Girshick, 2015) regresses boxes with a smooth L1 loss; ACT (Zhao et al., RSS 2023, Lesson 22.2) uses L1 rather than L2 as its action reconstruction loss. MSE in imitation learning: demonstrations may contain two ways to act in the same state (pass left or pass right); MSE learns their average, which may drive straight into the obstacle; this is why generative policies such as ACT and Diffusion Policy exist (Lessons 22.1 and 22.2). Data augmentation: in reinforcement learning from pixels, random shift/crop augmentations brought large gains (RAD: Laskin et al., 2020; DrQ: Kostrikov et al., 2020), and visuomotor policies commonly use random crops and colour jitter. Validation loss versus real success: the robomimic study (Mandlekar et al., CoRL 2021) found that picking policy checkpoints by lowest validation loss is unreliable and that policies must be evaluated in the environment; closed-loop distribution shift is one reason. Ensembles and uncertainty: PETS (Chua et al., 2018) learns dynamics models with an ensemble of probabilistic networks (Lesson 24.2); deep ensembles (Lakshminarayanan et al., 2017) are a standard baseline for uncertainty (Lesson 21.3). Split by trajectory: neighbouring frames of one demonstration are nearly identical, so splitting train/validation randomly by frame lets the validation set "peek" at the training data; split by trajectory, scene or object instead.

    9常见误区Common pitfalls

    误区 1：在测试集上调参，或数据泄漏Pitfall 1: tuning on the test set, or leaking data

    每用测试集做一次选择（挑模型、挑 λ、挑训练轮数），它就多一分乐观。调参只用验证集或交叉验证，测试集最后只看一次。机器人数据里泄漏更隐蔽：同一条轨迹的相邻帧、同一个物体的不同角度、同一个场景的不同时刻高度相关，随机按样本划分会让验证误差和训练误差一样漂亮，部署时却完全不是那么回事。按轨迹、物体或场景划分。

    Every choice made on the test set (which model, which λ, how many epochs) makes it a little more optimistic. Tune on the validation set or by cross-validation, and look at the test set once, at the end. In robot data leakage is subtler: neighbouring frames of one trajectory, different views of one object and different moments in one scene are highly correlated, so a random per-sample split gives a validation error as pretty as the training error, and deployment looks nothing like it. Split by trajectory, object or scene.

    误区 2：把低验证损失当成好机器人Pitfall 2: taking a low validation loss for a good robot

    验证集仍然来自训练时的分布（比如人类示教的状态）。机器人闭环执行时，自己的小错误会把它带到示教里没有的状态（FIG 7、22.1 节），开环的逐样本损失看不到这一点。验证损失是必要的诊断，但策略要在真实或仿真环境里评估成功率，最好多次、多个随机种子。

    The validation set still comes from the training distribution (for instance the states visited by human demonstrations). Running closed loop, a robot's own small errors take it to states the demonstrations never visited (FIG 7, Lesson 22.1), and an open-loop per-sample loss cannot see that. The validation loss is a necessary diagnostic, but policies must be evaluated by their success rate in a real or simulated environment, ideally many times and over several random seeds.

    误区 3：不想就用 MSEPitfall 3: reaching for MSE without thinking

    MSE 假设高斯噪声、单峰的目标。传感器有野值时一个坏点就能拉偏整个模型（FIG 1）；分类时它在饱和区没有梯度（FIG 2）；目标有多个合理答案时它给出平均值，可能哪个都不是（避障时从左绕和从右绕的平均就是撞上去）。L1 给出中位数，同样只有一个答案；多峰的目标需要能表示分布的模型（第 19 章的生成模型、22.2 节）。

    MSE assumes Gaussian noise and a unimodal target. With sensor outliers one bad point can drag the whole model (FIG 1); for classification it has no gradient when saturated (FIG 2); when the target has several valid answers it returns their average, which may be none of them (the average of passing left and passing right is a collision). L1 returns the median, again a single answer; multimodal targets need models that represent a distribution (the generative models of Chapter 19, Lesson 22.2).

    误区 4：特征没标准化就加 L1/L2Pitfall 4: adding L1/L2 to unstandardized features

    惩罚 \(\lambda\|\mathbf w\|^2\) 对所有系数一视同仁，但系数的大小取决于特征的单位：同一个关节速度用 rad/s 还是 deg/s 表示，系数相差 57 倍，被惩罚的程度也天差地别。加正则前先把每个特征减均值、除标准差；偏置（截距）一般不惩罚（FIG 4 也是这样做的）。在网络里，偏置和归一化层的参数通常也不做权重衰减。

    The penalty \(\lambda\|\mathbf w\|^2\) treats all coefficients alike, but a coefficient's size depends on its feature's units: the same joint velocity in rad/s or in deg/s gives coefficients 57 times apart, penalized completely differently. Standardize every feature (subtract the mean, divide by the standard deviation) before regularizing, and usually leave the bias (intercept) unpenalized (as FIG 4 does). In networks, biases and normalization-layer parameters are usually exempt from weight decay as well.

    误区 5：在 Adam 里当 L2 用权重衰减，或测试时忘了关 dropoutPitfall 5: treating weight decay in Adam as L2, or leaving dropout on at test time

    两个实现层面的坑。(a) 在 Adam 里把 L2 惩罚加进梯度，效果和 SGD 里不同（第 7 节），想要权重衰减就用 AdamW，而且换优化器时 λ 要重新调。(b) dropout、数据增广和 BatchNorm 训练时与测试时行为不同；部署前忘了切到评估模式（PyTorch 的 model.eval()），预测会带随机噪声，机器人动作会抖。

    Two implementation traps. (a) Adding an L2 penalty to the gradient in Adam does not behave as it does in SGD (Section 7); if you want weight decay use AdamW, and retune λ whenever you change optimizer. (b) Dropout, data augmentation and BatchNorm behave differently in training and at test time; forget to switch to evaluation mode before deployment (PyTorch's model.eval()) and predictions carry random noise and the robot's actions jitter.

    5. 你用 50 条示教轨迹训练行为克隆策略，把所有帧打乱后随机分出 10% 做验证集。验证损失几乎和训练损失一样低，可真机上成功率只有 30%。最主要的两个原因是？5. You train a behaviour-cloning policy on 50 demonstration trajectories, shuffling all frames and holding out a random 10% for validation. The validation loss is nearly as low as the training loss, yet on the real robot the success rate is only 30%. What are the two main reasons?
    同一条轨迹里相邻帧几乎一样，验证帧的“邻居”就在训练集里，所以验证损失说明不了对新轨迹的泛化（误区 1，应按轨迹划分）。即使划分正确，逐帧损失也是开环的：机器人自己的小误差会把它带离示教分布，在那里策略没有数据约束（FIG 7、误区 2），22.1 节会讲这如何累积。Neighbouring frames of a trajectory are nearly identical, so each validation frame's "neighbours" sit in the training set and the validation loss says little about generalizing to new trajectories (Pitfall 1: split by trajectory). Even with a correct split the per-frame loss is open loop: the robot's own small errors carry it off the demonstration distribution, where nothing constrains the policy (FIG 7, Pitfall 2); Lesson 22.1 shows how this compounds.
