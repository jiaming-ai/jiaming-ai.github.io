CROSS: Our Most Robust Mapping Yet

October 30, 20262026年10月30日
Research研究

CROSS 是我们迄今最鲁棒的建图系统。暗光、动态物体、纹理稀少和突然转弯，都是机器人在真实环境里建图时绕不开的挑战。我们关心的是：环境变差之后，机器人还能不能继续建立可用的地图。

看最难的那一段

这段 59 秒视频展示四种情况：夜间暗光、走廊里快速运动与急转弯、夜间灯光眩光，以及走廊突然关灯。左侧看真实相机画面，右侧看完整会话的轨迹和建图误差，把环境变化和地图结果放在一起看。

在展示的夜间序列中，CROSS 双目纯视觉配置完成了序列，而多种依赖特征跟踪的基线丢失跟踪。快速走廊案例重点展示的 CROSS 配置则使用了轮式里程计。视频标明了传感器输入，比较时也需要保留这些条件。

跟踪不断，地图也要可用

持续输出位姿，并不等于地图不会漂移。因此视频把丢失跟踪和完整会话的轨迹误差分开呈现，也保留了在暗光中持续输出位姿的其他系统。

这段视频直接展示暗光、眩光、快速运动和急转弯。动态物体与低纹理是我们关注的更广泛建图难题，这个剪辑没有把它们作为独立受控测试。视频使用 2026 年 10 月 23 日渲染时的基准快照；结果会随序列和传感器配置变化。

了解 CROSS · 完整基准

CROSS is our most robust mapping system yet. Darkness, moving objects, low-texture scenes and abrupt turns are the kinds of challenges that make mapping in real environments difficult. Our priority is a map the robot can keep using as those conditions change.

Watch the difficult stretches

This 59-second video follows four cases: a night run, fast motion with sharp turns in a corridor, night-time glare, and a corridor with the lights switched off. The camera view sits beside the full-session trajectories and mapping-error comparisons, so the conditions and the resulting map can be inspected together.

In the dark night sequence shown, CROSS stereo with images only completes the sequence while several feature-tracking baselines lose tracking. In the fast corridor case, the highlighted CROSS configuration uses wheel odometry. These are different sensor configurations, labelled in the video; their results should be read with those inputs in mind.

Tracking continuity and map quality both matter

A system can keep producing poses while its map drifts. The video therefore distinguishes lost tracking from whole-session trajectory error. It also includes systems that continue predicting poses in the dark, rather than treating every baseline as a tracking failure.

The clip directly illustrates darkness, glare, fast motion and sharp turns. Moving objects and low-texture scenes are broader mapping challenges we care about, rather than separate controlled tests demonstrated in this cut. The video reflects the benchmark snapshot used for its October 23, 2026 render; results vary by sequence and sensor configuration.
