My First Life — The Entrepreneur第一人生——创业者

I studied computing at the National University of Singapore from 2007 to 2011. I was not the most brilliant student, but I was restless. While others were content to graduate and find respectable jobs, I felt an itch to build something of my own. I tried, and failed, more times than I care to remember.我于 2007 至 2011 年在新加坡国立大学学习计算机。我不是最聪明的学生，却是最坐不住的那一个。当别人满足于毕业后找一份体面的工作时，我心里总有一股想亲手造点什么的冲动。我尝试过，也失败过——次数多得我都不愿细数。

After graduation, I co-founded iCarsClub (PP Car Rental) and built a peer-to-peer car-sharing platform that allowed owners to rent out their vehicles when they were not using them.毕业后，我联合创立了 iCarsClub（PP租车），搭建了一个点对点的租车共享平台，让车主可以在不用车的时候把车租出去。

We grew the platform to more than one million users and raised over US$100 million in funding. The company was later acquired by a NASDAQ-listed company. In 2014, I was named to Forbes 30 Under 30 for our work in car-sharing technology and entrepreneurship.我们把平台做到了一百多万用户，累计融资超过 1 亿美元，公司后来被一家纳斯达克上市公司收购。2014 年，我因在汽车共享技术与创业方面的工作入选福布斯中国「30 Under 30」。

Success, as it turned out, was less solid than it seemed. The business made sense only at a scale we couldn't reach without exhausting ourselves. So we sold it. The decision was rational, perhaps even wise. Still, when it was over, I found myself wondering what exactly I had been chasing all along.事实证明，成功远没有看上去那么牢靠。这门生意只有做到一个规模才说得通，而那个规模，我们不把自己耗尽就无法抵达。于是我们把它卖掉了。这个决定很理性，或许也很明智。可当一切结束，我还是忍不住想：这些年，我究竟在追逐什么？

The Gap Year — A Search for Meaning间隔年——寻找意义

In 2018, I went to Oxford. I told my friends I was studying theology, but what I was really studying was myself. For the first time in years, I had time to think without the noise of business.2018 年，我去了牛津。我对朋友们说我在学神学，其实我真正研究的是自己。多年来第一次，我有时间在远离商业喧嚣的地方思考。

I read from Plato and Aristotle to Descartes, Hume, and Sartre — from the existence of God to the nature of morality and meaning. I was particularly drawn to the existentialists. Each offered something different: Christianity, its comfort; philosophy, its doubt. If there is no meaning given to us, then perhaps it is our task to create one.我从柏拉图、亚里士多德读到笛卡尔、休谟和萨特——从上帝的存在，到道德与意义的本质。我尤其被存在主义者吸引。他们各自给出了不同的东西：基督教给予慰藉，哲学给予怀疑。如果意义不是被赋予的，那么创造意义，也许就是我们自己的任务。

That year was quiet. I walked a great deal, spoke little, and left Oxford with no grand revelation — only a clearer sense that meaning, like a business, must be built with one's own hands. As it turned out, that detour also shaped my later research, teaching me to think from first principles and to question every assumption.那一年很安静。我走了很多路，说话很少，离开牛津时没有任何宏大的顿悟——只是更清楚地意识到：意义就像一门生意，必须亲手去建造。后来我发现，这段“绕路”也塑造了我之后的研究，让我学会从第一性原理出发思考，并质疑每一个假设。

My Second Life — The Researcher第二人生——研究者

After Oxford, I returned to the familiar disorder of startups. I helped build a property technology firm, then an online education venture called Vivaling. Both did reasonably well, but my mind was elsewhere.从牛津回来后，我又回到了熟悉而混乱的创业世界。我参与创办了一家房地产科技公司，随后又做了一个名为 Vivaling 的在线教育项目。两者都做得不错，但我的心思已经在别处。

In 2019, I came across GPT-2. It wasn't very good, but it was enough. I began writing small models to generate Chinese poetry, fascinated by the thought that a machine could play with language at all. What began as curiosity soon became obsession.2019 年，我接触到了 GPT-2。它算不上好，但已经足够。我开始写一些小模型来生成中文诗歌，着迷于机器竟然也能摆弄语言。好奇心很快变成了痴迷。

I decided to enter research. Coming from an unconventional background, I knew I would have to find my own way in. I wrote to Harold, told him my story, and asked for a chance. To his credit, he said yes.我决定投身研究。背景不同寻常，我知道只能自己想办法闯进去。我给 Harold 写了信，讲了我的故事，请求一个机会。他答应了——这一点我至今感激。

I joined his lab in 2022 while taking a certification course in AI and robotics. That first year was intense. I read more than six hundred papers — more than I had read in my entire first life — and came to genuinely enjoy it. I earned straight A's, moved into a master's program, completed it within a year, published my first paper, and was admitted to the PhD program in 2024.2022 年，我一边修读 AI 与机器人认证课程，一边加入了他的实验室。第一年非常紧张。我读了六百多篇论文——比我整个第一人生读过的还要多——并且真心享受其中。我拿到了全 A，转入硕士项目，一年内完成学业，发表了第一篇论文，并于 2024 年被博士项目录取。

Now I am a PhD candidate at the CLeAR Lab, studying how robots can move safely through the open world — places they've never seen, with no map to guide them. It is a simple goal, though not an easy one: to help machines find their way, as I once tried to find mine.现在，我是 CLeAR Lab 的博士生，研究机器人如何在开放世界中安全地移动——去到从未见过的地方，没有地图可循。目标很简单，却并不容易：帮助机器找到自己的路，就像我曾经努力寻找自己的路一样。

The Other Life — The Art of Living另一种人生——生活的艺术

Work has never been the whole of life. I have always believed that one must also learn to live — to pursue beauty, mastery, and delight with the same seriousness one gives to labor.工作从来不是生活的全部。我始终相信，人也要学会生活——以对待工作同样的认真，去追求美、精进与愉悦。

When I first came across GPT-2, I did not use it to solve problems, but to write poems. I finetuned it on classical Chinese verse and watched, with quiet amusement, as it struggled and sometimes triumphed in shaping lines of unexpected grace. The exercise began as play, yet it became a study in rhythm and restraint — a dialogue between the ancient and the modern, between human intention and mechanical chance.第一次接触 GPT-2 时，我没有用它解决问题，而是用它写诗。我用中国古典诗词对它做微调，看着它笨拙地挣扎，偶尔又写出意想不到的雅致句子，暗自觉得有趣。这本是游戏，却成了对节奏与克制的研习——古老与现代、人的意图与机器的偶然之间的一场对话。

That same impulse toward form and harmony led me to study interior design at the National Design Academy (UK). What began as curiosity turned into craft. I learned how proportion and light could make a space speak softly, how stillness could be designed. Later, I put this to practice when I designed my own house in Shanghai. Each room, each shadow, felt like a thought made visible.同样对形式与和谐的追求，让我去英国国家设计学院（National Design Academy）学习室内设计。起初只是好奇，后来变成了手艺。我学到比例与光线如何让空间轻声说话，静谧又如何被设计出来。后来，我把这些用在了自己位于上海的住宅设计上。每个房间、每一处光影，都像一个被看见的念头。

Tea, too, has been a quiet companion. I have always admired the discipline behind simplicity — the way a single cup, prepared with care, can hold centuries of tradition. I took a course in tea appreciation, became a certified advanced tea taster, and for a time created my own brand. I travelled through Yunnan, visiting remote tea gardens, tasting leaves still warm from the sun. It was not business but devotion — the pursuit of a flavor that felt like calm itself.茶，也是一位安静的伴侣。我一直欣赏简单背后的自律——一杯用心冲泡的茶，可以承载数百年的传统。我学习了品茶课程，成为认证高级品茶师，还曾一度创立过自己的茶品牌。我走遍云南，造访偏远的茶园，品尝仍带着阳光余温的茶叶。那不是生意，而是虔诚——追寻一种如同宁静本身的味道。

And then there is motion — the other half of stillness. I travel often, drawn by landscapes that remind me how vast the world is. I have wandered through Europe, North and South America, and stood among the silent ice of Antarctica. In Peru, among the ruins of Machu Picchu, I felt the strange humility of standing where civilizations once dreamed. I ski whenever I can — in Chamonix, Niseko, New Zealand and others— chasing the fleeting precision of balance on a slope that forgives no mistake. There is a discipline in that motion, a kind of meditation that happens too quickly for thought.然后是动——静的另一半。我常常出行，被那些提醒我世界有多辽阔的风景所吸引。我走过欧洲、北美和南美，也站在南极寂静的冰原之间。在秘鲁的马丘比丘遗迹中，站在曾经孕育过文明之梦的地方，我感到一种奇异的谦卑。只要有机会我就去滑雪——霞慕尼、二世古、新西兰等地——追逐在不容失误的雪坡上转瞬即逝的平衡之精准。那种律动里有一种自律，一种快得来不及思考的冥想。

In these pursuits — art and design, tea and travel, poetry and snow — I have found a rhythm to life. Not the loud rhythm of ambition, but the quieter one that comes from giving oneself fully to whatever lies before you, and finding, for a moment, that it is enough.在这些追求之中——艺术与设计、茶与旅行、诗与雪——我找到了生活的节奏。不是野心那种喧嚣的节奏，而是一种更安静的节奏：全心全意投入眼前的事，并在某个瞬间发现，这就已经足够。
