01Research focus

Robots that leave the lab: perceiving, remembering, and acting in places they have never seen.

MOD-01 · MAPPING

Spatial Perception & Mapping

Topological maps that stay robust under perceptual aliasing and a changing world — reasoning over pose hypotheses in continuous SE(3) instead of trusting a single global metric map.

CROSS · NeurIPS'26 TOPO-Bench · ICRA'26
MOD-02 · MEMORY

Long-term Robot Memory

Turning a robot's history into usable state — memory matched to the timescale of the task, so policies can act on what they have seen minutes, hours, or days ago.

Blog · The right memory World models
MOD-03 · NAVIGATION

Open-world Navigation

Map-free navigation that survives the real world — deployed across seven countries — plus social navigation among people and language-guided navigation.

GeNIE · Earth Rover '25 Arena-Bench 2.0 POLo · ICRA'24
MOD-04 · MANIPULATION

Manipulation & VLAs

Training and deploying vision-language-action models on physical robots, from task-oriented grasping to mobile base placement and uncertainty-aware trajectory diffusion.

REAL-I · ICRA'26 TOG GBPP SCOPE · CoRL'26

07Join the mission

Looking for student researchers.

I'm actively looking for motivated students to work on cutting-edge problems in robot navigation and embodied AI in general. My research has won two international robotics challenges at ICRA and two Best Paper Awards at IROS workshops. I approach research with an entrepreneurial mindset—prioritizing rapid prototyping, real-world deployment, and practical impact.

My past students have gone on to excellent opportunities, including PhD offers from CUHK and NUS, as well as collaborations with leading research groups. I emphasize hands-on mentorship, giving students real ownership of projects and fostering both technical depth and independent thinking.
