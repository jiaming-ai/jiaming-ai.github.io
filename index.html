02Research focus研究方向

Robots that leave the lab: perceiving, remembering, and acting in places they have never seen.走出实验室的机器人：在从未见过的地方感知、记忆并行动。

MOD-01 · PERCEPTION & MEMORYMOD-01 · 感知与记忆

Spatial Perception & Long-term Memory空间感知与长期记忆

Maps and memories that stay reliable as the world changes — topological mapping that relocalizes under perceptual aliasing, reasoning over pose hypotheses in continuous SE(3), and memory matched to the timescale of the task so robots can act on what they saw minutes, hours, or days ago.在世界不断变化时依然可靠的地图与记忆：在感知混淆（perceptual aliasing）下仍能重新定位的拓扑建图，在连续 SE(3) 空间中对位姿假设进行推理，以及与任务时间尺度相匹配的记忆，让机器人能够依据数分钟、数小时乃至数天前所见采取行动。

MOD-03 · MANIPULATIONMOD-03 · 操作

Manipulation操作

Learning manipulation from real-robot data and deploying it reliably on physical robots — from task-oriented grasping and mobile base placement to uncertainty-aware trajectory diffusion.从真机数据中学习操作，并将其可靠地部署到实体机器人上——涵盖面向任务的抓取、移动底盘的位置规划，以及具备不确定性感知的轨迹扩散。

Agentic Robotics智能体机器人

Robots as agents that reason, plan, and improve themselves — using world models to imagine outcomes and agentic optimization to search for better behavior, instead of relying on a single fixed policy.把机器人视为能够推理、规划并自我改进的智能体——借助世界模型想象行动后果，借助 agentic 优化搜索更好的行为，而不是依赖单一的固定策略。
