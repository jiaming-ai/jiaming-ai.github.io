02Research focus

Robots that leave the lab: perceiving, remembering, and acting in places they have never seen.

MOD-01 · PERCEPTION & MEMORY

Spatial Perception & Long-term Memory

Maps and memories that stay reliable as the world changes — topological mapping that relocalizes under perceptual aliasing, reasoning over pose hypotheses in continuous SE(3), and memory matched to the timescale of the task so robots can act on what they saw minutes, hours, or days ago.

CROSS · NeurIPS'26 TOPO-Bench · ICRA'26 Blog · The right memory
MOD-02 · NAVIGATION

Open-world Navigation

Map-free navigation that survives the real world — deployed across seven countries — plus social navigation among people and language-guided navigation.

GeNIE · Earth Rover '25 Arena-Bench 2.0 POLo · ICRA'24
MOD-03 · MANIPULATION

Manipulation

Learning manipulation from real-robot data and deploying it reliably on physical robots — from task-oriented grasping and mobile base placement to uncertainty-aware trajectory diffusion.

REAL-I · ICRA'26 TOG GBPP SCOPE · CoRL'26
MOD-04 · AGENTIC

Agentic Robotics

Robots as agents that reason, plan, and improve themselves — using world models to imagine outcomes and agentic optimization to search for better behavior, instead of relying on a single fixed policy.

07Join the mission

Looking for student researchers.

I'm actively looking for motivated students to work on cutting-edge problems in robot navigation and embodied AI in general. My research has won two international robotics challenges at ICRA and two Best Paper Awards at IROS workshops. I approach research with an entrepreneurial mindset—prioritizing rapid prototyping, real-world deployment, and practical impact.
