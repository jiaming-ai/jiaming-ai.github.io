02Research focus研究方向

Robots that leave the lab. Two lines of work: environment representations that stay reliable across places, conditions and sensors, and robot policies that stay robust in the open world.走出实验室的机器人。两条研究主线：在不同场景、条件与传感器下依然可靠的环境表示，以及在开放世界中依然鲁棒的机器人策略。

LINE-01 · REPRESENTATION主线 01 · 环境表示

Open & Robust Environment Representation开放、鲁棒的环境表示

Representations that operate across environments, conditions, and sensing modalities: topological maps that relocalize after severe change, support object search, scale up with GPS, and even work underwater.能够跨环境、跨条件、跨传感模态工作的表示：在剧烈变化后仍能重定位的拓扑地图，支撑物体搜索，借助 GPS 扩展到大尺度场景，甚至可以在水下运行。

LINE-02 · POLICIES主线 02 · 机器人策略

Robust Robot Policies in the Open World开放世界中的鲁棒机器人策略

Policies that generalize beyond the lab: map-free navigation deployed in seven countries, championship-winning VLA post-training, social navigation learned from video, and foundation-model agents that reason, act, and improve.能够泛化到实验室之外的策略：已在七个国家部署的无地图导航，夺冠的 VLA 后训练，从视频中学到的社交导航，以及能够推理、行动并自我改进的基础模型智能体。
